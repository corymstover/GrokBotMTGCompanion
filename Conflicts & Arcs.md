## Ancient: imprisoning the Eldrazi
Ugin, Sorin, and Nahiri used hedrons to lure and imprison the three Eldrazi titans on Zendikar. ✅ [Zendikar profile](https://magic.wizards.com/en/story/zendikar-plane)

## The Brothers' War
Urza and Mishra discovered power stones in the Caves of Koilos; their war devastated Dominaria and plunged it into an ice age; afterwards Urza discovered Phyrexia. ✅ [Dominaria profile](https://magic.wizards.com/en/story/dominaria-plane). Retold in *The Brothers' War* (2022) fiction: [Episode 1](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/the-brothers-war-episode-1-the-end) … [Chapter 5](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/the-brothers-war-chapter-5-exodus).

## Phyrexia vs. Dominaria & the Weatherlight
Phyrexia nearly invaded Dominaria; the planeswalking skyship *Weatherlight*, its crew, and the Legacy artifacts (including Karn) thwarted it. Urza waged a millennia-long vendetta against Yawgmoth; they perished together. Urza's Nine Titans included Tevesh Szat and Lord Windgrace. ✅ [Dominaria](https://magic.wizards.com/en/story/dominaria-plane) · [Urza](https://magic.wizards.com/en/story/urza-planeswalker) · [Tevesh Szat](https://magic.wizards.com/en/story/tevesh-szat-planeswalker) · [Windgrace](https://magic.wizards.com/en/story/windgrace-planeswalker)

## Time Rifts & the Mending
Cataclysms, many caused by Planeswalkers, made Dominaria the epicenter of temporal-planar fractures spreading across the Multiverse; powerful Planeswalkers mended them. Teferi lost his spark to the time rifts. The Mending reduced Planeswalkers' former godlike power ("before the Mending … the spark was greater"). ✅ [Dominaria](https://magic.wizards.com/en/story/dominaria-plane) · [BRO Ch. 1](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/the-brothers-war-chapter-1-stronghold) · [BRO Ep. 5](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/the-brothers-war-episode-5-as-cruel-as-necessary) · [Unkindness of Ravens](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/unkindness-ravens-2015-07-29)

## Conflux of Alara
The five shards reconverged, as Nicol Bolas planned; wars broke out as cultures clashed. ✅ [Alara profile](https://magic.wizards.com/en/story/alara-plane)

## Phyrexian conquest of Mirrodin
Karn's Mirrodin was corrupted by Phyrexian oil he unknowingly brought; Phyrexians overwhelmed the Mirrans despite Koth, Elspeth, and Venser; Venser sacrificed himself to restore Karn. ✅ [New Phyrexia profile](https://magic.wizards.com/en/story/new-phyrexia-plane)

## Eldrazi Awaken / Battle for Zendikar / Gatewatch
Bolas lured Jace and Chandra to Zendikar; the Eye of Ugin was damaged and Eldrazi spawn flooded out; Gideon followed Chandra there (Fort Keff). Later the Gatewatch formed (Oath of the Gatewatch, 2016). ✅ [The Battle of Fort Keff](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/the-battle-of-fort-keff) · [Oath of the Gatewatch](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/oath-gatewatch-2016-02-03) · [BFZ story summary](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/battle-zendikar-story-summary-2015-12-23)

## Innistrad & Emrakul
Shadows over Innistrad / Eldritch Moon: Emrakul rises on Innistrad. ✅ [Emrakul Rises](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/emrakul-rises) · [Innistrad profile](https://magic.wizards.com/en/story/innistrad-plane)

## Kaladesh revolt
Kaladesh / Aether Revolt (2016–17) revolution against the Consulate (✅ [Revolution Begins](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/revolution-begins-2017-01-02)); later the plane was officially renamed **Avishkar** after a nearly bloodless revolution overthrew the Consulate. ✅ [Avishkar profile](https://magic.wizards.com/en/story/avishkar-plane)

## Amonkhet & the God-Pharaoh
The promised God-Pharaoh (Nicol Bolas) returns in *Hour of Devastation*; Oketra is slain (per Basri Ket's profile). ✅ [Hour of Devastation](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/hour-devastation-2017-07-26) · [Basri Ket](https://magic.wizards.com/en/story/basri-ket-planeswalker)

## Tarkir: Fate Reforged
Sarkhan Vol traveled >1,000 years into the past and saved Ugin, creating a dragon-ruled present. ✅ [Tarkir profile](https://magic.wizards.com/en/story/tarkir-plane) · [PW Guide: Fate Reforged](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/planeswalkers-guide-fate-reforged-2015-01-07)

## Return to Dominaria & War of the Spark
Dominaria (2018): the Cabal under demonlord Belzenlok ([Dominaria](https://magic.wizards.com/en/story/dominaria-plane), [Return to Dominaria Ep. 1](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/return-dominaria-episode-1-2018-03-21)). War of the Spark (2019): Bolas's Elderspell on Ravnica stole Planeswalker sparks; many Planeswalkers lost their sparks and lives. ✅ [War of the Spark: Ashes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/war-spark-ravnica-ashes-2019-06-12)

## Phyrexian Invasion of the Multiverse
Dominaria United → The Brothers' War → Phyrexia: All Will Be One → March of the Machine (2022–23). Realmbreaker, the Phyrexian "World Tree", dug roots into every plane. In the endgame, Jin-Gitaxias' army attacked Elesh Norn; Elspeth became an angel; Elesh Norn died; Karn planned to use what remained of Venser's spark to pull sparks from the compleated. Nissa lost her spark. ✅ [MOM Ep. 7](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/episode-7-divine-intervention) · [MOM Ep. 9](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/episode-9-the-old-sins-of-new-phyrexia) · [MOM Ep. 10](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/episode-10-the-rhythms-of-life) · [Aetherdrift Ep. 4 (Realmbreaker)](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/aetherdrift-episode-4-little-guy-shortcuts) · [She Who Breaks the World](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/she-who-breaks-the-world). Jace held the sylex at the Invasion Tree, arguing annihilation was the only path; he then appears wounded at his mother's home on Vryn. ✅ [Epilogue 1](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/epilogue-1-the-invasion-tree)

## Jace's Echoverse (Reality Fracture)
Story ran from 2026-08-31; set released 2026-10-02. After the Phyrexian Invasion, Jace — unmade in the Meditation Realm and rebuilt as "the Theorist" — built the **Echoverse**, a copy of the Multiverse without Phyrexians, Eldrazi, or Bolas, and planned to lay it over the real Multiverse, using Arcavios's creation as the key. Ajani, Chandra, Vraska, and Garruk confront him in Hexhaven; they face their Echoverse doubles. The Echoverse collapses (Emrakul consumes it); the Theorist dies; Jace's fail-safe passes his spark to **Tam**, who planeswalks to Strixhaven and later finds Vraska and Ranna Beleren after Jace's funeral on Vryn. ✅ [PW Guide](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/planeswalkers-guide-to-reality-fracture) · [Story hub](https://magic.wizards.com/en/story) · [Ep. 10](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/reality-fracture-episode-10-happy-birthday)
