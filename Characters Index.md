## Pre-Mending / Ancient

### Nicol Bolas
- **Who:** Elder dragon Planeswalker whose plots can lie dormant for decades.
- **Status (late 2026):** ✅ At large — escaped the Meditation Realm (spark status unknown).
- **Detail:** Imprisoned by Ugin in the Meditation Realm at the end of the War of the Spark; during Tarkir: Dragonstorm his "fetters came undone" and Ugin reports "Nicol Bolas, I am afraid, is free." Ajani later tells Liliana "Bolas escaped… He used an Omenpath—we don't know whether his spark is intact." The Echoverse was built to be "free from the machinations of Nicol Bolas" (🚫 Echoverse context).
  - Killed Ugin on Tarkir in the original timeline (turning Tarkir's dragons against him) — undone by Sarkhan's time travel.
  - Trapped the Multiverse's Planeswalkers on Ravnica (War of the Spark); imprisoned afterwards.
  - Escapes during the dragonstorm crisis (2025 story).

### Ugin
- **Who:** Spirit Dragon; twin of Bolas; helped bind the Eldrazi; "reborn at least twice".
- **Status (late 2026):** ✅ Alive (weakened) — returned to Tarkir at the end of Tarkir: Dragonstorm.
- **Detail:** In TDM Ep. 7 Ugin enters Tarkir "bloodied and exhausted", burns Sarkhan with ghostfire, calms the dragonstorms and admits "I have been remiss in the keeping of my brother." Before that, Narset believed him "gone" and "incapacitated" (TDM Ep. 2).
  - Killed by Bolas in Tarkir's original timeline; saved in the past by Sarkhan Vol, which preserved the dragon tempests.
  - Imprisoned Bolas after the War of the Spark (with Jace's help, per LE Ep. 2).
  - Fought Bolas as he broke free; both vanished ("dead now, perhaps") before Ugin reappeared.
