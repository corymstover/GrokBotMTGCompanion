# Continuing maintenance — outside the finite project checklist

**Owner decision: November 2, 2026.** The finite Master Workflow checklist is
**134/134 complete**, with **zero unchecked construction tasks**. Its three
continuing responsibilities were removed from the active checkbox inventory,
not marked accomplished forever. Their exact original wording is retained in
[the archival extraction](archive/RECURRING_CHECKLIST_ITEMS_2026-11-02.md),
with stable historical IDs K105, K125, K126.

These responsibilities continue as event-driven operations, **without**
perpetual unchecked checklist boxes:

1. **Coverage inventory (formerly K105):** Review new story and card sources;
   keep missing citations, unresolved timelines and known coverage gaps visible.
2. **Official-change watch (formerly K125):** Check new Wizards stories, Oracle
   text, Commander rules and releases. Existing weekly watch is advisory;
   human review decides whether and when to ingest changes.
3. **Set/printing reconciliation (formerly K126):** On new set/printing releases,
   rebuild the Phase 1B catalog and bidirectional printing lookup, retaining
   past pinned snapshots and reporting any freshness gaps.

These duties have no final completion date and therefore do not belong in a
project completion numerator or denominator. The former 137-item historical
sequence is preserved for provenance; active items retain their original IDs
so prior evidence and SHA-bound approvals remain traceable.

The independently certified foundation milestone, owner-bounded finish-line
decision, exact card data snapshots, deck approvals and Grixis protected
material are unaffected by this checklist classification change.
