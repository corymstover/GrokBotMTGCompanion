## Closure protocol

1. Compare PR head and canonical approval records before closing approval-related PRs. Never equate an unmerged proposal with an approved deck.
2. Keep the PR URL/diff reachable; do not delete head branches that may contain the only copy of owner decisions or evidence.
3. Closing an obsolete PR is reversible and does **not** merge its code or grant owner approval.
4. Do not prune unrelated branches merely because they are old; verify merge ancestry, branch scope and retention requirements first.
