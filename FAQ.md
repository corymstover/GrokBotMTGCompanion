## Lore

### Is Jace Beleren dead?
✅ Yes, as of late 2026. He built the Echoverse as "the Theorist" and died when it collapsed (Reality Fracture Ep. 10). His spark passed to Tam. Before that he had masqueraded as "Ashiok". → [Characters Index → Jace Beleren](Characters%20Index.md#jace-beleren) · [Planeswalker Dossiers → Jace](Planeswalker%20Dossiers.md#jace-beleren) · [Reality Fracture arc](Story%20Arc%20Summaries.md#reality-fracture-2026)

### What is an Omenpath?
The term first appears in Kaldheim (2021) for the ways between realms. After the Phyrexian invasion, Omenpaths are portals between planes that let non-Planeswalkers travel. → [Omenpath & Multiverse Connections → What an Omenpath is](Omenpath%20%26%20Multiverse%20Connections.md#what-an-omenpath-is) (where their origin is still ❓: [open questions](Omenpath%20%26%20Multiverse%20Connections.md#open-questions))

### How did the Phyrexian invasion end?
The praetors are killed (Karn kills Elesh Norn himself) and the invasion fails. Zhalfir returns, swapped with New Phyrexia through the Invasion Tree, and Nissa is restored. → [Story Arc Summaries → Phyrexian Invasion saga](Story%20Arc%20Summaries.md#the-phyrexian-invasion-saga-2023) · [Master Timeline → Phyrexian Invasion](Master%20Timeline.md#phyrexian-invasion)

### Who founded the Gatewatch, and what happened to the Eldrazi titans?
Gideon, Jace, Nissa and Chandra swore their oaths on Zendikar. Ulamog and Kozilek were destroyed in leyline fire there, and Emrakul was later bound in Innistrad's silver moon. → [Story Arc Summaries → BFZ/OGW](Story%20Arc%20Summaries.md#battle-for-zendikar--oath-of-the-gatewatch-201516) · [SOI/EMN](Story%20Arc%20Summaries.md#shadows-over-innistrad--eldritch-moon-2016)

### What was the Mending?
Time rifts spread from Dominaria. Planeswalkers mended them, and post-Mending sparks are weaker. → [Master Timeline → Time Rifts & the Mending](Master%20Timeline.md#time-rifts--the-mending) · [Conflicts & Arcs → Time Rifts & the Mending](Conflicts%20%26%20Arcs.md#time-rifts--the-mending)

### Is the Echoverse canon?
The *Reality Fracture* story is official. Its Echoverse duplicates of characters are flagged 🚫 so they aren't mixed up with the originals. → [Characters Index → status at a glance](Characters%20Index.md#status-at-a-glance) · [Conflicts & Arcs → Jace's Echoverse](Conflicts%20%26%20Arcs.md#jaces-echoverse-reality-fracture)

### What does a keyword do, and is it evergreen?
Each entry quotes the Comprehensive Rules verbatim and gives a status only when an official Wizards article supports one. ### Can a token, emblem or dungeon go in my deck?
🚫 No. A token "is a marker used to represent any permanent that isn't represented by a card" (CR 111.1). Emblems are created in the command zone (CR 114.1). Dungeon cards "aren't part of a player's deck or sideboard" (CR 309.2). → [Tokens, Emblems & Dungeons](Tokens%2C%20Emblems%20%26%20Dungeons.md)

### Is an adventurer card one card or two?
One. "Each adventurer card is only one card" (CR 715.2c). → [Tokens, Emblems & Dungeons → Adventurer cards](Tokens%2C%20Emblems%20%26%20Dungeons.md#adventurer-cards-real-cards)

## Commander deck-building

### What are the basic Commander deck rules?
Exactly 100 cards including the commander (CR 903.5a). Singleton except basic lands (903.5b). 40 life (903.7). Commander tax of {2} per previous cast from the command zone (903.8). → [EDH Standards → Core rules](EDH%20Standards.md#core-rules-verified)

### Can I play a card that has a mana symbol of a color outside my commander's identity?
No. A card's color identity includes mana symbols in its **mana cost and rules text** (reminder text excluded), and every color must be in the commander's identity (CR 903.4, 903.5c). This KB applies it strictly. → [EDH Standards → Color identity](EDH%20Standards.md#color-identity-strict) · [Rules Interactions → Commander: color identity](Rules%20Interactions.md#commander-color-identity)

### How does commander damage work?
21 or more **combat** damage from the same commander over the game makes that player lose (CR 903.10a). Partner commanders are tracked separately (702.124d). → [Rules Interactions → Commander damage](Rules%20Interactions.md#commander-damage)

### Can I have two commanders?
Only with a pairing ability such as Partner, "Partner with", Friends Forever, Choose a Background or Doctor's companion. → [EDH Pairings](EDH%20Pairings.md) · [Rules Interactions → Partner & Backgrounds](Rules%20Interactions.md#partner--backgrounds)

### What are brackets and Game Changers?
Brackets are a pregame-conversation tool. A deck's minimum bracket depends on Game Changers, extra turns, mass land denial and two-card combos. → [EDH Standards → Power bracket assessment](EDH%20Standards.md#power-bracket-assessment) · [Game Changers](EDH%20Standards.md#game-changers). Every deck in [decks/](decks/) has front-matter listing its `bracket_floor` and `game_changers`.
