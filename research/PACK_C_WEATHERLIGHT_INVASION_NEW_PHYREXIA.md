## Claims
28 claims (C001–C028): 23 CANON_CONFIRMED, 2 CANON_IMPLIED, 1 CANON_DISPUTED, 1 OPEN_QUESTION.

Highlights:
- The Weatherlight, its crew and the Legacy (which included Karn) thwarted the first invasion (C001–C003).
- Urza created Karn. Karn created Mirrodin and unknowingly carried Old Phyrexia's glistening oil, which corrupted Mirrodin into New Phyrexia (C004–C007).
- Yawgmoth was a medical genius from Dominaria who became ruler of Old Phyrexia, the "Father of Machines" (C008–C009). This partly closes a Pack B gap.
- **C010 OPEN_QUESTION:** Old Phyrexia was made by an *unknown* Planeswalker.
- **C012 CANON_DISPUTED:** who brought Yawgmoth down. Each official summary gives a different answer:
  - the ONE guide: Karn and his allies
  - the 2022 Dominaria guide: Gerrard
  - the Urza profile: Urza and Yawgmoth perished together

  All three accounts are kept. They may well describe one shared final battle.
- The Coalition, Jhoira, Shanna, Jodah, Raff Capashen and Sheoldred's DMU invasion (C014–C022).
- Urza and Mishra found the stones of power in the Caves of Koilos (C023–C024), and Urza discovered Phyrexia at the end of the war (C025). These extend Pack B.
