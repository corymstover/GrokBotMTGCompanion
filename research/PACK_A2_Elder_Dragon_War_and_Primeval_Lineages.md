# Pack A2 — Elder Dragon War, survivors and Primeval lineages

**Research checkpoint:** 2026-10-30 · **Status:** source-graded historical extension to [Pack A](PACK_A_Ugin_Bolas_Elder_Dragon_Duality.md), with nine **named-card** matches against the October 29 historical printing index. **Not** an exhaustive casualty roll, artwork scene certification, current-Oracle legality report or finalized deck.

## The key survivor correction

In [*Chronicle of Bolas: Perspectives*](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/chronicle-bolas-perspectives-2018-08-08), Bolas recalls Chromium Rhuell, Arcades Sabboth, Palladia-Mors and Vaevictis Asmadi surviving. **That is his account, not a complete master roll.** The later direct Wizards biography [*The Returning Legends of Modern Horizons 2*](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/returning-legends-modern-horizons-2-2021-06-10) independently names **Piru and Chromium** as survivors and explicitly credits Bolas with instigating the Elder Dragon War. Piru's absence from Bolas's list is a concrete source-perspective discrepancy. Do not erase Piru or assert that these sources prove exactly five, six, or any other total survivors (A018).

## Verified family links, without invented siblings

- **Piru + Chromium:** editorial Wizards biography identifies them as mates and ancestors of dragon nations, **including Primeval Dragon Crosis, the Purger**. It does **not** state that Crosis is their immediate first-generation son (A019).
- **Palladia-Mors → Rith:** the official [*Dominaria United* Rith biography](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/legends-youll-find-dominaria-united-2022-09-01) explicitly describes Rith as Palladia-Mors's daughter (A023). Rith is among the five post-war Primeval Dragons (A024).
- **Dihada / Dakkon / Piru / Chromium:** Dihada's later coercion and Dakkon's battle belong in a **post-war Corondor event**, separate from Bolas's primordial campaign (A020–A022).

## Independent lore-first deck architectures (unapproved)

1. **Bolas-led duality, Grixis:** *Nicol Bolas, the Ravager // Nicol Bolas, the Arisen* as commander, with a **colorless Ugin card** in the 99 as the competing twin. Emphasizes personal rivalry, Tarkir, war and eventual imprisonment. Excludes off-identity Arcades, Palladia, Piru, Rith and other siblings; cannot honestly claim a complete original Elder Dragon family. **New proposed lore list only**—the approved Grixis Encyclopedia Bolas list stays immutable.
2. **Full ancient dynasty, five colors:** *The Ur-Dragon* is a prospective thematic commander because Wizards' Piru biography describes the original Elder Dragons as spawned by the Ur-Dragon. Five-color identity can potentially include the rival twins, elder participants, Crosis and Rith, while the commander is a **mythic origin symbol, not a documented battlefield participant**. This is the better concept for a whole-family lore deck, subject to a fresh current Oracle and rules review.

Neither architecture is a 100-card recommendation yet. Colors in the historical `data/card_index.csv` are a research starting point and **do not replace a current legality/ban verification**. Do not edit, clone into, unlock or silently refresh any of the 62 approved decks.
