- 22 pack-local IDs carry differing display names across packs; the graph keeps the first-ingested name, so exact-name bridges can miss the others.

## Recommendation

Keep data/multiverse_graph as the single relationship schema; extend packs by reusing existing local IDs (check this audit's alias list first); resolve review candidates with cited evidence, never by name similarity alone.
