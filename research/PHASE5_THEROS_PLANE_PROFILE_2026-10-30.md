# Phase 5 — Theros pantheon, poleis, and ecological perils

**Preliminary, partial, source-graded research. Not owner-approved.**

Wizards' [Theros plane profile](https://magic.wizards.com/en/story/theros-plane)
was independently read and ten short excerpts in the new `THP001`–`THP010`
claims were checked **verbatim against the live official page** with
`python3 scripts/audit_lore_pack_theros_profile.py --live`.

The ten distinct facts add source-backed coverage of:

- the broad influence of Theros's pantheon over its mortals;
- the three notable poleis named in this primer: **Meletis, Akros and Setessa**;
- minotaur raiders, giants empowered by their terrain, seaborne krakens and
  sirens as different hazards on the plane;
- Meletis's devotion to scholarship and magic, Akros's martial traditions,
  Setessa's veneration of nature, and the network of Akroan defensive outposts.

`TS037` already credits the Reverent Army with defending Meletis and was **not
replaced or duplicated**. The source-register ID `ths_plane` also remains
unchanged; the extra pack creates no second official source identity.

The compiled Theros plane-history dossier now has **six distinct directly
incident claims**, up from three (the new direct edges are `THP001`, `THP002`
and `THP010`). The initial v2.51 narrative erroneously said five; the live
generated dossier already counted six correctly. It remains highly incomplete: this plane
primer does not specify an exact calendar year, prove a hierarchy of every
god, name the monsters in individual encounters, or enumerate every city-state.
The cited city list is **not an exhaustive census**. No before/after order was
invented from undated descriptive prose.
