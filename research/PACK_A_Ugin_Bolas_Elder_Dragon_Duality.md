# Pack A — Ugin, Nicol Bolas, and the ancient Elder Dragons

**Research checkpoint:** 2026-10-30 · **Status:** first source-reviewed, claim-graded foundation; **not** a completed universal Elder Dragon War genealogy or a decklist.

This pack expands the Companion's Ugin/Bolas starter into a source-linked research record. It draws from Wizards' *Chronicle of Bolas*, the official Tarkir history, *War of the Spark* commentary, *Tarkir: Dragonstorm*, *Lorwyn Eclipsed*, and *Reality Fracture*. Source URLs, continuity grading, identity links and explicit limitations are machine-readable in [`data/lore_research/pack_a_ugin_bolas_evidence_2026-10-30.json`](../data/lore_research/pack_a_ugin_bolas_evidence_2026-10-30.json). The corresponding audit prevents narrator assertions and Echoverse material from being silently promoted to prime-universe canon.

## What we can say confidently

1. **Twin origins:** Ugin and Nicol emerge together as twins during the original dragons' hatching. Their earliest experiences establish a sharp contrast in how they interpret danger and power. Primary: [*Chronicle of Bolas: The Twins*](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/chronicle-bolas-twins-2018-06-13) (claims A001–A002).
2. **The Elder Dragon Wars need graded narrators:** In [*Chronicle of Bolas: Perspectives*](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/chronicle-bolas-perspectives-2018-08-08), Bolas recounts his planeswalking awakening and reports that Chromium Rhuell, Arcades Sabboth, Palladia-Mors and Vaevictis Asmadi survived the ancient dragon wars. The published narrative **shows Bolas giving this account**; it does not make his self-aggrandizing claims about being first or his survivor list an independently checked universal historical inventory (A003–A004, `CANON_DISPUTED`).
3. **Two different ancient conflicts must not be spliced together:** The Chronicle's [*Unwritten Now*](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/chronicle-bolas-unwritten-now-2018-08-15) describes a Meditation Realm confrontation in which Bolas believes he has destroyed Ugin, while the story shows Ugin persisting in a transformed state (A007). The separate official [*Fate Reforged* guide](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/planeswalkers-guide-fate-reforged-2015-01-07) covers a much later battle above ancient Tarkir (A005).
4. **Sarkhan changes Tarkir's history:** The [official Tarkir plane account](https://magic.wizards.com/en/story/tarkir-plane) explicitly attributes Ugin's survival and the continuity of the dragon tempests to Sarkhan Vol's travel into the past. The dragon-dominated version of Tarkir is a **revised timeline**, not proof that the earlier outcome never happened in the original timeline (A006).
5. **Bolas's imprisonment was real, but is no longer current:** Wizards' [*War of the Spark* design commentary](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/even-more-war-games-2019-05-06) describes Bolas losing his planeswalker spark and being confined under Ugin's watch. By [*Tarkir: Dragonstorm*, Episode 7](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/tarkir-dragonstorm-episode-7-return), that prison has failed, Bolas is free, and Ugin has returned to Tarkir (A008–A010).
6. **The spark question remains open:** Ajani's later account in [*Lorwyn Eclipsed*, Episode 2](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/lorwyn-eclipsed-episode-2-shake-off-slumber) expressly leaves Bolas's spark after the escape uncertain. **Do not state that he definitely has or lacks it now** (A011, `OPEN_QUESTION`).
7. **The Echoverse is not prime continuity:** The [*Reality Fracture* plane guide](https://www.magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/planeswalkers-guide-to-reality-fracture) describes an alternate Echoverse history without Bolas. That is an `ALTERNATE_CONTINUITY` statement **about Jace's constructed reality**, not an erasure of Bolas from the principal Multiverse (A012).

No numeric Ancient-era dates are asserted here. **Publication dates and in-universe event order are different data fields.** The original and revised Tarkir branches must remain distinct.

## Deckbuilding implications — research, not an approved 100-card list

The eventual *Ugin versus Nicol Bolas — Elder Dragon Duality* Commander project has a strong canon foundation: twins, rivalry, competing philosophies, Tarkir's changed history, and the Meditation Realm prison. That **does not** make every Dragon, elder or ancient artifact a confirmed ally of Ugin or follower of Bolas.

Potential card-representation research anchors include named Ugin/Bolas planeswalker and legendary cards, Tarkir-related story moments, and the 2019/2025 conflict arcs. Before an actual deck is offered:

- Verify specific Oracle card names, paper printings, legal color identity, current Commander rules and ban status.
- Grade each chosen card as **direct canon depiction**, **direct lore reference**, **lore-inspired analogue**, or **mechanics-inspired stand-in**.
- Choose a legal commander that serves the narrative, disclosing any symbolic color-identity compromise.
- Use [the Phase 1B set/printing atlas](../SET_ATLAS.md) for set origin and card history, never to infer story canon solely because a card was printed in a set.
- Preserve all 62 previously approved Grixis decklists: **this is a new independent lore-deck concept**.

## Known gaps and next research questions

- Establish a corroborated Elder Dragon War participant roster, lineages and casualties across primary sources. Bolas's list in *Perspectives* is **not** the entire answer.
- Determine which dragon kin, human factions and named characters actually sided with Ugin or Bolas at **which** period. Opposition to Bolas does not automatically establish membership in Ugin's alliance.
- Map the exact printed cards depicting each verified conflict: relevant Scryfall printing IDs, image/story-spotlight evidence, names and source-context. **No card depiction is certified in this first pack.**
- Reconcile any genuine contradictions in older historical descriptions without retroactively assuming publication order equals the in-universe chronology.
- Research Bolas's situation after the *Tarkir: Dragonstorm* escape, including destination and whether the spark remains absent. Do not treat fan speculation as canon.
