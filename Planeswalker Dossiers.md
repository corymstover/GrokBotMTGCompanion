## Jace Beleren
**Identity:** "may be the most talented mind mage in the Multiverse … a strategic genius of exceptional intelligence and insatiable curiosity" ([profile](https://magic.wizards.com/en/story/jace-beleren-planeswalker)). Home plane: [Vryn](Planes%20Index.md#vryn).
**Spark moment:** Fighting his mentor, the sphinx Alhammarret, inside his own mind, Jace damages his memories, "broke into pieces and walked" and lands on Ravnica. "He'd awakened as a planeswalker" ([Jace's Origin: Absent Minds](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/jaces-origin-absent-minds-2015-06-24)).

## Gideon Jura
**Identity:** "A powerful warrior-mage with the ability to make himself invulnerable, Gideon is fiercely loyal, unyielding, just, charismatic" ([profile](https://magic.wizards.com/en/story/gideon-jura-planeswalker)). Born Kytheon Iora on [Theros](Planes%20Index.md#planes-with-official-profiles).
