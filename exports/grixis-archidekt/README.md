## Import notes

- ⚠️ **Not tested in Archidekt itself.** The files are checked offline on every `validate_all` run. Each matches its deck page card for card. Each is 100 cards, with the commander line(s) first and tagged `[Commander]`, no duplicate lines and no blank lines inside the list, and each passes `evaluate_decklist.check` (singleton, color identity, Commander legality). Paste one deck into Archidekt and confirm the commander category before relying on it.
- ⏳ The two pending decks are exported for completeness but are not Commander-legal before their set release. On or after 2026-10-19 (scheduled follow-up: 2026-10-20), run `python3 scripts/activate_pending_grixis.py`. It refreshes the Scryfall bulk, re-checks the `mbc` cards (The Madcap Jester, Dragon's Smile, Tresserhorn's Lord, Returned) and flips both decks from ⏳ pending to active. Then run their refinement pass: `python3 scripts/grixis_refine.py --plan 6 --cycle <N> --decks The_Madcap_Jester,Tresserhorn_s_Lord__Returned`, then `rebuild_enrichment.sh`. `validate_all` fails a pending deck once its `legal_from` date has passed, so this cannot be forgotten. Lord of Tresserhorn is a different, already-legal deck (batch 3).
- Full method, every change with its written review, flagged items and homogenization findings: [Grixis Refinement Cycle](../../grixis/Grixis%20Refinement%20Cycle.md#end-of-cycle-summary-60-legal-decks).
