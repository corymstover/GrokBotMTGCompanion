## Story the deck tells

**Arc:** [The Brothers' War](../Conflicts%20%26%20Arcs.md#the-brothers-war) told from Urza's side. Urza and Mishra found the power stones in the Caves of Koilos, and their rivalry became a war that "devastated Dominaria" ([Dominaria profile](https://magic.wizards.com/en/story/dominaria-plane)). Kroog fell in **28 AR**; in **63 AR** Urza activated the sylex as Mishra's machine-self attacked; in **64 AR** "there was silence in Terisiare" ([BRO Ep. 5](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/the-brothers-war-episode-5-as-cruel-as-necessary)). The blast shattered Terisiare into an archipelago ([Planeswalker's Guide to Dominaria](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/planeswalkers-guide-dominaria-2022-08-31)).

**How the deck tells it (guidance):** you open in Tocasia's dig and on the Koilos stones, build Urza's workshops and lands, and field the war machines of Kroog and the Argivian armies. Lat-Nam's Third-Path wizards stand by as interaction. If the game goes long, Golgothian Sylex is the war's ending. Karn and the Metathran look ahead to Urza's later legacy.
