## Story the deck tells

**Character:** [Nahiri](../Planeswalker%20Dossiers.md#nahiri), the kor lithomancer of Zendikar. Six thousand years ago, with Ugin and Sorin, she trapped the Eldrazi on Zendikar; "At Ugin's direction, Nahiri constructed a massive network of stone structures called hedrons" ([BFZ Story Summary](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/battle-zendikar-story-summary-2015-12-23)). When the Eldrazi nearly escaped, "neither Sorin nor Ugin returned to help" (same source). She was later trapped in the Helvault for a millennium and took revenge on Sorin, which unleashed Emrakul on Innistrad ([Stone and Blood](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/stone-and-blood-2016-06-15)). She hunted the Lithoform Core in the Skyclaves ([ZNR Ep. 1](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/episode-1-heart-skyclave-2020-09-02)), was compleated on New Phyrexia, and survived, vowing "No Planeswalker will set foot on Zendikar ever again" ([Beyond Repair](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/beyond-repair)).

**How the deck tells it (guidance):** Kor of Zendikar carry the lithomancer's forged weapons (Nahiri's own abilities make Kor Soldiers and move Equipment). Hedrons and Ugin's tools build the prison. The Helvault package is her imprisonment and her revenge on Sorin's plane, and the Skyclave cards are the ZNR hunt. 🚫 The compleated Nahiri (ONE) is red-white, so it cannot appear in a mono-white deck; that chapter is told only in the summary.
