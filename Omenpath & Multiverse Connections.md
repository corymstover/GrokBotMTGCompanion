## What an Omenpath is
| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| The term first appears in **Kaldheim (2021)** for the ways between the realms: "Kjell had described Omenpaths as the ways between worlds, opening and closing at random like the freeze and thaw of a land bridge." | [Know Which Way the Wind Is Blowing](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/know-which-way-wind-blowing-2021-01-08) |
| On Kaldheim, individuals could open them: "Tyvar had already opened an Omenpath." | [KHM Ep. 4: Into the Demons' Realm](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/episode-4-demons-realm-2021-01-27) |
| After the Phyrexian invasion, Omenpaths are **portals between planes**, letting non-Planeswalkers travel. On Thunder Junction, "The Omenpath sat wedged between the enormous rockface, crackling with blue energy." | [OTJ Ep. 1](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/episode-1-an-offer-of-revenge) |
| They're described as triangular: "the triangular outline of the Omenpath that had delivered them to Lorwyn". | [Lorwyn Eclipsed Ep. 2](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/lorwyn-eclipsed-episode-2-shake-off-slumber) |
| New ones keep appearing: "The last Omenpath that appeared opened only to Arcavios" (Ixalan). | [LCI: Pawns](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/the-lost-caverns-of-ixalan-pawns) |
