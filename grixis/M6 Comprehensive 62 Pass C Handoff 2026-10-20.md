## Critical independent-review firewall

**A source-bound authored review is *not* independent critical review.** Its verdict, candidate ranking and comparison are susceptible to author influence and must be hidden from an assessor's initial stage. A reviewer who previously wrote the blueprints/optimizers, saw an author KEEP/PROPOSE disposition or contributed to an earlier critique **must disclose that prior exposure**; using a new label/model name does not restore independence. Do not pass an author's entire `deep_reviews/` directory to an unsealed first-pass assessor.

1. **Register critic and provenance before showing judgments.** Record name/model/version, organization or operating environment, prior involvement/exposure to the engineering analysis, source/date/time, branch/commit and reviewer-specific tool access. Check owner-authorized readiness for genuinely independent critic work; do not self-certify it.
2. **Construct a neutral per-deck first-stage intake:** exact 100-card canonical list/hash and commander faces; current official Oracle and Commander legality; rules/version dates; project-authorized exceptions; *factual* card roles and explicit material alternative card identifiers **without** author verdict, rank, predicted improvement, performance interpretation or proposed cut persuasion. The 62 tracker entries identify source hashes, but the intake must omit `deep_review_evidence_path` and any location of author conclusions until the first pass is sealed.
3. **Independent analysis, one deck at a time:** identify weakest *actual* slots, commander-specific mechanical dependencies, credible novel candidates (including outside M5), legal/card-identity/official-vs-exception issues, mana and curve, combos traced from paid zones/actions, target availability, matchup and interaction constraints, as well as counterarguments to proposed changes. Assessor must make a disposition without viewing the author's opinion.
4. **Cryptographically seal** the first-stage critic finding to immutable source SHA and review input fingerprint/commit. Capture original critic response *before* exposing prior author's recommendation. If prior exposure prevents independence, label as an advisory critique and BLOCK qualified Pass C until the independence problem is resolved.
5. **Only after sealing** compare each critic disposition and alternative cut with `data/grixis/m6/deep_reviews/<tracker_deck_id>.md` and previous M5 candidate tests. Enumerate disagreements and agreements; do not treat agreement as qualification without evidence.
6. **Adjudicate unresolved claims** through targeted effect-aware Phase B with stated rules coverage and adversarial opponent responses, not goldfish PASS proxies. Distinguish source-card legality from project-authorized exceptions and keep all canonical cards and approvals read-only.
7. **Publish evidence-bound reviewer provenance, precise per-deck disposition, blocked issues and coverage limitations** only if the genuinely independent reviewer requirements have been met. Otherwise leave formal Pass C zero or the relevant deck BLOCKED; never set M6 accepted, mark zero-improvement certificates, issue full reproducibility J bundles or declare qualified independent 62/62 from authored coverage alone.
