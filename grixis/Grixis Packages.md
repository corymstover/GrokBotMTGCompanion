## Grixis rules insights (from the source)

> Rules interactions the source author worked out and relied on. Each one is quoted with its source path and checked against the Oracle text in our card entries (quoted below). How to *use* them is guidance.

- **Trigger multipliers are additive, not multiplicative.** In the Nekusar build the source found that [Wizard's Staff](../cards/W/wi.md#wizards-staff) ("If a triggered ability of equipped creature triggers, that ability triggers an additional time.") and [Harmonic Prodigy](../cards/H/ha.md#harmonic-prodigy) add instances of *both* Nekusar triggers, while [Krang, the All-Powerful](../cards/K/kr.md#krang-the-all-powerful) ("If a player drawing a card causes a triggered ability of a permanent you control to trigger, that ability triggers an additional time.") adds one only to the draw-caused damage trigger, not to the draw-step trigger. Source: `refinement/decisions/Nekusar_the_Mindrazer_Candidate_Decisions_2026-10-15.md`.
- **Damage replacement can break exact-damage payoffs.** The same source file cuts Solphim, Mayhem Dominus because doubling Nekusar's 1 damage stops [Ghyrson Starn, Kelermorph](../cards/G/gh.md#ghyrson-starn-kelermorph) from seeing an event of exactly 1 damage. Oracle: "Three Autostubs — Whenever another source you control deals exactly 1 damage to a permanent or player, Ghyrson Starn deals 2 damage to that permanent or player."
- **Attack triggers come before combat-damage draws.** For [Cecily, Haunted Mage](../cards/C/ce.md#cecily-haunted-mage) ("Whenever Cecily, Haunted Mage attacks, you draw a card and you lose 1 life. Then if you have eleven or more cards in your hand, you may cast an instant or sorcery spell from your hand without paying its mana cost.") the source notes that a combat-damage draw such as [Robe of the Archmagi](../cards/R/ro.md#robe-of-the-archmagi) resolves *after* her attack trigger. So it cannot meet the eleven-card check on the same attack. Source: `refinement/evidence/Cecily__Bjorna__Lutri_Approval_Gate_Dossier_2026-10-13.md`.
- **"Cast from hand" clauses still work with free casts.** [Apex of Power](../cards/A/ap.md#apex-of-power) cast for free through Cecily's trigger is still cast from hand, so its hand clause applies. "Without paying its mana cost" bars alternative costs but allows additional costs (CR 118.9a, 118.9d). Same source file.
