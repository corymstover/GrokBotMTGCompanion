## Hand reviews

Decks given a commander-first hand look (guidance; principle 2: no artificial weakening).

- **[Evelyn, the Covetous](decks/evelyn-the-covetous.md#refined-list-bot-cycle-39)** (Bot cycle 41): **identity-first rebuild assessed, list left unchanged and flagged.** Evelyn's engine is "Once each turn, you may play a card from exile with a collection counter on it", so extra Vampire-enters triggers in one turn only add to the exile pool, not to plays. The list's best line is the cEDH core: Worldgorger Dragon + Animate Dead / Necromancy / Dance of the Dead, Thassa's Oracle + Tainted Pact / Demonic Consultation, and Underworld Breach + Brain Freeze. Evelyn adds one flash card-advantage play per turn from any library. Every Vampire or flicker card on her EDHREC page was checked against the list's weakest slots. Cordial Vampire, Blood Artist, Captivating Vampire, Bloodline Keeper and Dusk Legion Zealot (overall ranks 142–5,228) only fit by replacing a 1-mana combo or protection piece (Reanimate, Defense Grid, Culling the Weak, Vexing Bauble) and fail the power guard or role parity. Viscera Seer (rank 257) is the closest, but it would replace Defense Grid's protection for the combo turn. An identity-first Vampire version would clearly be weaker at the source's stated cEDH ceiling, so no swap is made. Flag: the commander contributes value, not the win line. Revisit if a cEDH-grade Vampire or exile-play payoff is printed.
- **[Marchesa, Dealer of Death](decks/marchesa-dealer-of-death.md#refined-list-bot-cycle-39)** (Bot cycle 41): **identity confirmed after a scoring fix, list left unchanged.** The 14% score was our gap, not the list's. Marchesa triggers on "Whenever you commit a crime", and the reminder text says targeting opponents, anything they control, or cards in their graveyards counts. Most of the list's cEDH interaction (targeted counterspells, bounce, removal) plus Spatial Binding, Hex Parasite, Spellskite, Tezzeret and Lapis Lazuli Talisman commit crimes. A new crimes hook now counts them. The source records this list as matching the 2026-10-11 Takeda Ryuhei tournament-winning configuration and keeps Lapis deliberately: it turns blue spells into targeted untaps, which are crimes. Considered and not made: Gitaxian Probe (a free crime cantrip, 37.4% of Marchesa decks) for Mystic Remora, Lapis Lazuli Talisman or Last Chance. It passes the guard, but each cut is a deliberate source decision (Remora: card-advantage pillar; Lapis: crime density; Last Chance: finisher), and Probe does not clearly do more. No swap.
- **[Nicol Bolas](decks/nicol-bolas.md#refined-list-bot-cycle-39)** (Bot cycle 42): **identity confirmed after a scoring fix; 1 hand-picked swap.** The 31% score was mostly our gap. Nicol's trigger is "Whenever Nicol Bolas deals damage to an opponent", and the combat hook did not cover it until this cycle. The list is already built to make him connect or deal damage: Lightning Greaves, Swiftfoot Boots, Sword of Feast and Famine, Pain for All, Chandra's Ignition, Kediss, Emberclaw Familiar. It also punishes the hand discard: Liliana's Caress, Raiders' Wake, Waste Not, the Tinybones pair, Torment of Hailfire. Pain for All, Chandra's Ignition and Kediss look generic, but each makes Nicol himself deal damage to an opponent, so they stay. One change: No Mercy (defensive) → Megrim (another discard payoff).

### Kess, Dissident Mage / Kefka, Court Mage (64% overlap)

- **Shared nonland cards:** 56 nonland cards: 8 fast-mana pieces, the cEDH free-interaction/tutor core (Fierce Guardianship, Pact of Negation, Mindbreak Trap, Deflecting Swat, Demonic/Vampiric/Mystical Tutor, Imperial Seal) and the Ad Nauseam / Tainted Pact / Thassa's Oracle / Brain Freeze win package; 22 shared cards match both commanders' Oracle hooks.
- **Verdict:** Partly homogenization. For Kess ("Once during each of your turns, you may cast an instant or sorcery spell from your graveyard") the instant/sorcery-heavy cEDH core is identity-driven: every shared spell is a second use from the graveyard. For Kefka the same spells are generic: his Oracle text ("each player discards a card" on enter/attack; "Whenever an opponent loses life during your turn, you draw that many cards") is served only by the Kefka-only cards (Waste Not, Tinybones, Bauble Burglar, Culling the Weak), not by the shared win package (Brain Freeze 22.0%, Borne Upon a Wind 17.9%, Final Fortune 11.1% on Kefka's EDHREC page).
- **Proposed identity-driven changes:** For Kefka's batch (guidance; each goes through the power guard and a written review): Final Fortune → Liliana's Caress (Kefka's discard makes each opponent lose 2, and on your turn Kefka then draws that many; 33.4% of Kefka decks), Borne Upon a Wind → Monument to Endurance (every discard from Kefka's trigger draws or makes Treasure; 37.8%), Brain Freeze → Dauthi Voidwalker (discarded cards are exiled with void counters for you to cast; 33.1%). Kess: no change; the overlap is her identity.

### Cormela, Glamour Thief / Kess, Dissident Mage (62%)

- **Shared nonland cards:** 54 nonland cards; 40 of them match both commanders' instant/sorcery hooks, 13 are fast mana plus Rhystic Study and Thassa's Oracle.
- **Verdict:** Identity-justified. Both commanders are instant/sorcery engines: Cormela's mana is "Spend this mana only to cast instant and/or sorcery spells" and she returns an instant or sorcery when she dies; Kess recasts them from the graveyard. Each keeps its own line: Cormela has storm and copy (Tendrils of Agony, Dualcaster Mage, Birgi, Seething Song) and altars, Kess has graveyard setup (Snapcaster Mage, Gifts Ungiven, Intuition, Dragon's Rage Channeler). The shared fast mana is ceiling-appropriate for two cEDH lists.
- **Proposed identity-driven changes:** None. Re-check Cormela's Rhystic Study slot in her batch only if the guard finds a stronger instant/sorcery payoff.

### Jeleva, Nephalia's Scourge / Kess, Dissident Mage (62%)

## Method

- **Commander dependence (fact-based heuristic):** a nonland, non-commander card counts if (a) its Oracle text or type line matches a *hook* taken from the commander's own Oracle text (mechanic patterns in `HOOKS`, plus creature types named in the commander's text, from CR 205.3m), or (b) its EDHREC synergy on the commander's page is ≥ 15%. Score = counted / nonland cards. Patterns are loose by design; the score ranks decks, it is not a rules statement.
- **Generic share:** cards with no commander link that appear in ≥ 16 of the 62 source lists. **Overlap:** Jaccard index of nonland card sets. **Thoracle core:** Demonic Consultation, Jace, Wielder of Mysteries, Laboratory Maniac, Tainted Pact, Thassa's Oracle. **Control core:** An Offer You Can't Refuse, Arcane Denial, Counterspell, Cyclonic Rift, Deadly Rollick, Deflecting Swat, Delay, Dovin's Veto, Fierce Guardianship, Flusterstorm, Force of Negation, Force of Will, Mana Drain, Mental Misstep, Mindbreak Trap, Mystic Remora, Negate, Pact of Negation, Rhystic Study, Snuff Out, Spell Pierce, Stern Scolding, Swan Song, Toxic Deluge.
- **Ceiling:** a deck with dependence ≥ 60% and generic share ≤ 15%, or with no qualifying swap, is flagged at/near its ceiling and left unchanged.
- **Cuts:** only generic cards (≥ 16 lists) with no Oracle hook and EDHREC synergy < 5%. Never a Game Changer, a piece of a Commander Spellbook combo in the list, or a mana source of MV ≤ 2 (speed serves every commander at its ceiling).
- **Adds:** EDHREC synergy ≥ 15% and inclusion ≥ 5.0% on the commander's page, **and** an Oracle hook to the commander; Commander-legal, inside the color identity, no new Game Changer unless the list already has 4+ and targets Bracket 4+, no mass land denial, no extra-turn card unless the list already plays one.
- **Pairing:** each add replaces a cut whose key roles (ramp, draw, removal, wipe, counter, protection, tutor; Oracle patterns) it covers, at MV at most 1 higher; interaction (removal, wipes, counterspells, protection) is only replaced by interaction at equal or lower MV. At most 5 swaps per deck per batch.
- **Power guard (Bot cycle 40):** an add must not be a clear power downgrade of its cut: commander-page inclusion at least the cut's (this commander's players choose it at least as often), overall EDHREC rank no worse than 4× the cut's or +2000, and no free spell (pitch/commander-free) replaced by a non-free one. Every guard-passing swap needs a written manual review (`data/grixis_refine_review.json`) saying why the add does more in *this* deck; unreviewed swaps are not applied and fail `validate_all`.
- **Verification:** the refined list must pass `evaluate_decklist.check` (100 cards, singleton, color identity, Commander-legal), keep every Commander Spellbook combo of the source list (re-queried), and not raise the bracket floor above the source floor or target. A failing swap is dropped.
- **Frozen decisions:** each batch's swaps are stored in `data/grixis_refine_batches.json`; `validate_all` re-verifies them on every run.
- **Limits:** EDHREC synergy measures how often decks of *this* commander play a card versus all decks of its colors: community data, not a quality judgment. The heuristic cannot see multi-card lines the Oracle patterns miss; at-ceiling decks are candidates for a hand review.
