- **Bothersome Noisemaker:** efficient amass redundancy, but Saruman, the White Hand already turns noncreature spells into larger Army growth and the list does not need another narrow cast-payoff creature.
- **My Precious / Shadowspear:** both provide excellent evasion or combat utility, but the deck already has Orcish Siegemaster, Wonder, Rogue's Passage, The Black Gate, new Great Goblin trample, and new Minas Morgul shadow. Another nonland evasion slot is redundant.
- **Moonstone, Harsh Mistress:** genuinely synergizes with Sauron's optional hand discard, but the discard is optional and the existing card-advantage/discard support is stronger across game states. No clean commander-first cut survives.
- **Smaug, Wicked Worm:** strong current card, but Treasure density is not high enough to make its secondary engine superior to existing Army/Ring pieces.
- **Chandra's Ignition / All Will Be One:** powerful counter/damage conversions, but The Great Goblin already supplies a highly thematic counter-damage payoff and the list should not drift into generic counter-burn redundancy.
- **Command Beacon / Phyrexian Tower:** defensible utility lands, but Sauron's ward makes repeated commander removal less routine and Minas Morgul/Rogue's Passage/The Black Gate are more directly identity-bearing utility lands.
- **Generic Oracle/Consult, Breach, or turbo-combo conversion:** rejected. It could raise generic Grixis ceiling but would violate the commander-first/no-forced-homogenization standard.

**Rules-critical conclusions to gate**

- Sauron's ward is a ward cost requiring the opponent targeting him to sacrifice a legendary artifact or legendary creature.
- Each opponent spell causes a separate amass Orcs 1 trigger from Sauron; countering the opponent's spell does not remove the already-triggered Sauron ability.
- Army combat damage causes the Ring to tempt; each Ring temptation then causes Sauron's optional discard-hand/draw-four trigger.
- Seven Nazgûl are legal because Nazgûl explicitly permits up to nine copies. Each Nazgûl independently triggers when the Ring tempts its controller.
- Mauhúr changes a +1/+1-counter event on an Army/Orc/Goblin from N counters to N+1, not +1 per counter.
- The Great Goblin triggers once for each event that puts one or more counters on an Army/Orc/Goblin, not once per individual counter.
- Minas Morgul's shadow counter makes the Army a Wraith in addition to its other types and gives it shadow while the counter remains; subsequent Nazgûl triggers can therefore add counters to that Army.
- Great Goblin, Foul-Hearted amasses Goblins onto the existing Army, making that Army a Goblin as well as its other Army subtypes, and grants Armies trample.
- Roaming Throne choosing Avatar causes Sauron's triggered abilities to trigger an additional time. Choosing Wraith instead causes each Nazgûl's triggered ability to trigger an additional time; these modes are not simultaneous because one creature type is chosen.
- Wonder still has valid Island support and Anger still has valid Mountain support in the revised mana base.
