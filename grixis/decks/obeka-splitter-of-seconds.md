**Obeka, Splitter of Seconds — Commander-First Blueprint**

Date: 2026-10-15  
Cycle: `global-restart-2026-10-13`  
Deck: #45 of 62

**Identity**

**Natural ceiling: Power Bracket 4 — Optimized.**

Primary identity: **Extra Upkeeps / Combat Damage / Upkeep Triggers**.

Obeka is a four-mana 2/5 Ogre Warlock with menace. Whenever she deals combat damage to a player, her controller gets that many additional upkeep steps after that combat phase. Under the official rules treatment, those upkeep steps occur in additional beginning phases whose untap and draw steps are skipped. The deck therefore treats **actual combat damage dealt by Obeka** and **additional instances of Obeka's combat-damage trigger** as its primary resource multipliers.

The commander-first sequence is:

1. resolve and protect Obeka;
2. force combat connection;
3. increase her actual combat damage and/or multiply her combat-damage trigger;
4. convert the resulting additional upkeeps into cards, Treasures, Clues, counters, tokens, life pressure, monarch value, suspend acceleration, artifact production, and alternate wins;
5. use Time Sieve when upkeep-generated artifacts support repeatable extra turns.

**Final engine priorities**

**Damage and trigger multiplication**
- **Mjölnir, Hammer of Thor** — doubles damage Obeka would deal; Obeka is worthy, so equip worthy is `{1}`.
- **Dragonfire Blade** — three-color Obeka reduces equip `{4}` to `{1}`; +2/+2 directly increases added-upkeep count while hexproof from monocolored protects the commander.
- **Hatred** — converts life into +X/+0 at instant speed; every additional point that connects becomes another upkeep.
- **Roaming Throne** — choose Ogre or Warlock; Obeka's combat-damage ability triggers an additional time.
- **Wizard's Staff** — gives Obeka prowess and causes her triggered abilities to trigger an additional time; Obeka uses normal equip `{3}` because she is a Warlock, not a Wizard.
- **Combat Research, Embercleave, Sword of Feast and Famine, Black-color Rings** and the retained equipment package continue to raise damage, create multiple damage events, or improve connection.

**Efficient upkeep engines**
- **Super Intelligence** — one mana turns every upkeep of enchanted Obeka's controller into a card draw.
- **Smaug the Magnificent** — each controller upkeep creates a Treasure; Obeka-created upkeeps therefore generate one-sided artifact acceleration, and Smaug's attack trigger converts accumulated Treasures into damage.
- **Tamiyo's Journal** — each controller upkeep investigates; extra upkeeps generate Clues, which are card draw, tutor fuel, and Time Sieve material.
- **Darksteel Reactor** — every controller upkeep can add a charge counter; twenty counters win the game. Obeka can compress many Reactor counters into a single postcombat sequence.
- Retained engines include all six Courts, As Foretold, Bonehoard Dracosaur, Creeping Bloodsucker, Dragonmaster Outcast, Extravagant Replication, Mechanized Production, Phyrexian Arena, Plargg and Nassari, Replicating Ring, Thopter Spy Network, Thassa, and Twilight Prophet.

**Suspend / time-counter leverage**
Ancestral Vision, Inspiring Refrain, Jhoira of the Ghitu, Profane Tutor, Rousing Refrain, Suspended Sentence, As Foretold, and Replicating Ring all improve when extra upkeeps rapidly advance time/counter-based engines.

**Artifact extra-turn package**
Time Sieve remains commander-appropriate because Thopter Spy Network, Smaug, Tamiyo's Journal, Replicating Ring, Mechanized Production, Court of Vantress, and other artifacts can turn Obeka-created upkeeps into expendable artifacts. The deck does not treat Sieve as an unconditional three-card combo from an empty board; board state and artifact cadence matter.

**Identity constraints**

- Do **not** convert this into generic Grixis cEDH. Project reranking and current evidence support Bracket 4, not a tournament-established cEDH ceiling.
- Do **not** add generic extra turns or Oracle/Consultation/Breach packages merely because they raise abstract power.
- Do not spend more slots on evasion once the commander already has menace plus Aqueous Form, Brotherhood Regalia, Silver Shroud Costume, Greaves, Boots, Thassa, Shizo, and Silent Arbiter.
- Prefer cards whose output scales directly with **Obeka-created upkeeps** or **Obeka's damage**.
- Keep **35 lands**; Obeka costs four and the deck retains several four-to-six-mana upkeep engines.

A later challenger emphasized Smaug / Super Intelligence / Darksteel Reactor / Tamiyo's Journal / Aggravated Assault / Blackblade Reforged.

- Tamiyo's Journal supplies both artifact generation and unrestricted tutoring from the same upkeep engine.
- Aggravated Assault + Sword of Feast and Famine is powerful but substantially more generic and did not beat a commander-native upkeep engine for the final slot.
- Blackblade Reforged is a strong damage amplifier but redundant after Mjölnir, Dragonfire Blade, Hatred, Embercleave, Combat Research, and the three scaling Rings.

**Rules-critical conclusions**

**Added upkeeps**
Obeka's trigger creates a number of additional upkeep steps equal to the combat damage she dealt to a player. Rules implementation places those upkeeps in additional beginning phases after the combat phase, with untap and draw skipped. `At the beginning of your upkeep` abilities trigger during each of them.

**Damage amount is the central multiplier**
- **Dragonfire Blade:** Obeka has three colors, so equip `{4}` costs `{1}` when targeting her. +2/+2 raises base combat damage from 2 to 4 and therefore raises the base added-upkeep count from 2 to 4 when she connects.
- **Mjölnir:** Obeka is legendary, red, and not a Villain, so she is worthy. Equip worthy `{1}` applies. The damage-doubling replacement changes the combat damage actually dealt, so Obeka counts the doubled amount.
- **Hatred:** life paid as the additional cost becomes +X/+0 until end of turn. Successful combat damage converts that power directly into added upkeeps.
- **Combat Research / Embercleave / Sword / color Rings:** retained because they raise damage, create multiple damage events, or make future hits larger.

**Trigger multiplication**
Roaming Throne and Wizard's Staff each cause the relevant triggered ability to trigger an additional time. If both apply to Obeka, one combat-damage event creates **three total Obeka trigger instances**: original + Staff + Throne. These effects are additive, not multiplicative.

Wizard's Staff also grants prowess to the equipped creature. Obeka is a Warlock, not a Wizard, so normal equip `{3}` applies.

**Double strike**
If Embercleave gives Obeka double strike and she deals combat damage in both combat-damage steps, her ability triggers separately for each damage event. Trigger multipliers apply to each event independently.

**Super Intelligence**
The Aura triggers at the beginning of the enchanted creature controller's upkeep. Enchanting Obeka means every normal or Obeka-created upkeep produces another draw while the Aura and creature remain on the battlefield.

**Smaug the Magnificent**
Smaug creates a Treasure at the beginning of each controller upkeep. Every Obeka-created upkeep therefore creates another Treasure. Smaug's attack trigger then deals damage equal to the number of Treasures controlled to any target. Unlike Generous Plunderer, Smaug does not give an opponent a Treasure as part of that upkeep engine.

**Darksteel Reactor**
Reactor is indestructible and can receive a charge counter during each controller upkeep. Obeka-created upkeeps accelerate its twenty-counter alternate win condition directly. Each resulting upkeep independently supplies another Reactor trigger.

**Tamiyo's Journal**
Journal investigates at the beginning of each controller upkeep, so extra upkeeps create extra Clues. Three Clues can be sacrificed to tutor any card. A Clue cannot be sacrificed simultaneously to Journal and Time Sieve, so artifact accounting must respect the separate costs; the deck's high upkeep count can generate enough Clues for either function as board state permits.

**Time Sieve / Thopter Spy Network**
Thopter Spy Network triggers at each controller upkeep if an artifact is controlled. Time Sieve itself is an artifact, so an established Sieve + Network board satisfies Network's intervening-if condition at normal upkeep.

If Obeka then connects for at least 4 damage, Network produces one Thopter at the normal upkeep plus four during the postcombat added upkeeps. Those five expendable artifacts can fuel Time Sieve for an extra turn while Sieve remains. On the extra turn, the same cadence can repeat if Obeka connects again and the permanents survive. This is an established-board repeatable-turn engine, not an unconditional loop from an empty battlefield.

Smaug and Tamiyo's Journal provide additional artifact cadence for Sieve, but their tokens must still be counted honestly against any other sacrifice/tutor costs.

**Arena of Glory**
If any mana produced by its exert ability is spent on Obeka's creature spell, the resulting Obeka gains haste until end of turn, enabling immediate combat and therefore immediate extra-upkeep generation.

**Purpose**

A final challenger pass was run after recovery of the existing SHA-bound approval-ready packet. The pass specifically tested four high-lift/current alternatives that were not individually discussed in the earlier decision closeout: Darksteel Reactor, Tamiyo's Journal, Robe of the Archmagi, and Blackblade Reforged. The existing candidate was not presumed correct merely because it already existed.

**Challenger results**

**Darksteel Reactor — REJECT at final slot stage**

Darksteel Reactor is highly commander-synergistic: each Obeka-created upkeep can add a charge counter, and twenty counters win the game. It is a legitimate alternate-win card and current optimized Obeka data gives it meaningful inclusion/lift.

It does not displace the finalized package because it costs four mana and produces no resource, board pressure, protection, evasion, or card advantage before reaching twenty counters. The list already retains Mechanized Production as an upkeep-accelerated alternate win while also generating artifact copies, and Time Sieve as a commander-native artifact-turn engine. Reactor is therefore a strong alternate build choice rather than a material upgrade over a finalized slot.

**Tamiyo's Journal — REJECT at final slot stage**

Tamiyo's Journal is also excellent with additional upkeeps: each upkeep investigates and three Clues can be converted into a tutor. Its Clues also feed Time Sieve.

The closest retained competitor is Generous Plunderer. Journal is cleaner and asymmetric but costs five mana and does nothing until an upkeep occurs. Plunderer costs two, can begin producing mana before Obeka is established, converts every added upkeep into Treasure immediately, and the Treasure mana is available during the postcombat sequence/main phase to deploy the deck's expensive upkeep engines. The opponent receives a tapped Treasure only if the controller chooses to make a Treasure; that downside is real but does not outweigh the two-mana setup and explosive ramp role in this exact curve.

**Robe of the Archmagi — REJECT at final slot stage**

Robe is a strong Obeka card: Obeka is a Warlock, so it equips for `{1}`, and it draws cards equal to combat damage dealt.

Combat Research retains the corresponding slot because it costs only `{U}`, immediately gives legendary Obeka +1/+1 and ward `{1}`, directly increases the number of additional upkeeps by increasing combat damage, and grants its combat-damage draw ability to Obeka herself. Because that granted ability is a triggered ability of Obeka, Wizard's Staff and a correctly typed Roaming Throne can each cause the draw trigger to trigger an additional time. Robe has the higher draw ceiling, but Combat Research is materially cheaper and simultaneously improves damage/upkeep output and protection.

**Blackblade Reforged — REJECT at final slot stage**

Blackblade can generate very large power increases in a 35-land deck and is a valid commander-damage/upkeep multiplier.

It does not beat Mjölnir, Hammer of Thor or Sword of Feast and Famine in the final configuration. Mjölnir multiplies all damage rather than adding a fixed land-count bonus, scales multiplicatively with Hatred/Rings/Combat Research/other buffs, equips worthy Obeka for `{1}`, and supplies an ETB removal mode. Sword supplies protection, +2/+2, discard pressure, and a full land untap after connection. Blackblade is therefore powerful but redundant rather than a net upgrade.

**Rilsa Rael, Kingpin — RECONFIRMED REJECT**

Rilsa remains a credible high-lift challenger because taking the initiative creates upkeep-based Undercity progression and completing a dungeon can give an attacker +5/+0, first strike, and menace. At five mana, however, she requires an additional subsystem whose control can be contested in combat and whose payoff generally matters on a later attack. The finalized damage multipliers and upkeep engines begin affecting Obeka more directly and with fewer dependencies.
