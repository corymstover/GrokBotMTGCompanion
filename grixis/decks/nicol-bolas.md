| Cut | Add | Role kept | Why (commander-specific) | Power guard + review (Bot cycle 40) |

| No Mercy | **Megrim** (hand-picked) | (no key role) | **damage to an opponent → discard (hand-picked)**: Nicol Bolas reads "Whenever Nicol Bolas deals damage to an opponent, that player discards their hand."; Megrim reads "Whenever an opponent discards a card, this enchantment deals 2 damage to that player.". > 🔎 **Hand review (Bot cycle 42): identity confirmed after a scoring fix; 1 hand-picked swap.** The 31% score was mostly our gap. Nicol's trigger is "Whenever Nicol Bolas deals damage to an opponent", and the combat hook did not cover it until this cycle. The list is already built to make him connect or deal damage: Lightning Greaves, Swiftfoot Boots, Sword of Feast and Famine, Pain for All, Chandra's Ignition, Kediss, Emberclaw Familiar. It also punishes the hand discard: Liliana's Caress, Raiders' Wake, Waste Not, the Tinybones pair, Torment of Hailfire. Pain for All, Chandra's Ignition and Kediss look generic, but each makes Nicol himself deal damage to an opponent, so they stay. One change: No Mercy (defensive) → Megrim (another discard payoff).

**Nicol Bolas — Commander-First Blueprint**

Date: 2026-10-15  
Cycle: `global-restart-2026-10-13`

**Commander identity**

Current Oracle:

- `{2}{U}{U}{B}{B}{R}{R}`
- Legendary Creature — Elder Dragon
- Flying
- At the beginning of your upkeep, sacrifice Nicol Bolas unless you pay `{U}{B}{R}`.
- Whenever Nicol Bolas deals damage to an opponent, that player discards their hand.
- 7/7

The critical rules fact is that Nicol Bolas's discard ability triggers from **damage for any reason**, not only combat damage.

**Commander-first plan**

1. **Accelerate an eight-mana, color-intensive commander.** The deck needs unusually high mana quality because casting Bolas requires UUBBRR plus two generic and maintaining him asks for UBR each upkeep.
2. **Make Bolas himself deal damage immediately or to multiple opponents.** Haste/evasion is useful, but direct commander-sourced damage is stronger because it bypasses combat and still empties a hand.
3. **Convert hand destruction into resources.** Waste Not, Geth's Grimoire, Tergrid, both Tinybones cards, Liliana's Caress, Raiders' Wake, and Sangromancer make the discard event more than one-for-one attrition.
4. **Protect the commander at low opportunity cost.** Free stack interaction, Greaves/Boots, Regalia, Cavern, and Command Beacon are justified because losing an eight-mana commander before it deals damage is catastrophic tempo.
5. **Retain a compact combat finisher.** Aggravated Assault + Sword of Feast and Famine remains a repeatable extra-combat line when a creature can connect and five lands can fund another Assault activation.

**Signature commander-sourced damage engines**

- **Dragon Tempest:** Nicol enters as a flying Dragon, gains haste, and the entering Dragon itself deals at least 1 damage to a chosen target. If an opponent is targeted, that opponent discards their hand before combat.
- **Ingris Stingerquill:** whenever Nicol attacks, Ingris causes **Nicol** to deal 1 damage to each opponent. Each opponent therefore discards their hand before blockers.
- **Kediss, Emberclaw Familiar:** after Nicol deals combat damage to one opponent, Nicol deals that much noncombat damage to every other opponent. One connection becomes table-wide hand destruction.
- **Chandra's Ignition:** Nicol deals damage equal to his power to every opponent and each other creature. At printed power this is 7 to each opponent plus a broad creature sweep, and every opponent hit discards their hand.
- **Pain for All:** enchanting Nicol immediately has Nicol deal damage equal to his power to another target; if Nicol is later dealt damage, Nicol deals that much to each opponent.
- **Quicksilver Dagger:** gives Nicol a tap ability through which Nicol deals 1 to a player and then draws a card.
- **Viridian Longbow:** durable Equipment version of a Nicol-sourced one-damage tap ability.

**Discard payoff network**

- **Waste Not:** converts each discarded card into cards, mana, or bodies.
- **Geth's Grimoire:** draws for each opponent discard.
- **Tergrid, God of Fright:** can return discarded permanent cards to your battlefield.
- **Tinybones, Bauble Burglar:** can stash discarded cards still in opponents' graveyards and let you play them later.
- **Tinybones, Trinket Thief:** draws after a discard turn and punishes empty hands with its activated ability.
- **Sangromancer:** gains 3 life per opponent card discarded, providing stabilization proportional to the commander's success.
- **Liliana's Caress / Raiders' Wake:** convert discarded cards into direct life loss.

**Tergrid / Bauble Burglar ordering**

If an opponent discards a permanent while Tergrid and Tinybones, Bauble Burglar are controlled by the same player, both triggers can exist. Their controller chooses the order. If Tergrid resolves first and moves the permanent out of the graveyard, Tinybones cannot exile that card from the graveyard; if Tinybones resolves first, Tergrid cannot later return that card. This is flexible overlap, not automatic double value.

**Natural power ceiling**

**Power Bracket 4 — Optimized.**

The commander costs eight mana, has a recurring upkeep cost, and does not itself provide a deterministic command-zone combo. The strongest realistic build is therefore high-powered optimized control/discard with explosive commander-sourced hand-wipe sequences—not a forced generic cEDH Oracle/Breach shell.

**Deliberate differentiation from #42 Nicol Bolas, the Ravager**

The project immediately follows this deck with **Nicol Bolas, the Ravager // Nicol Bolas, the Arisen**. High-ranked Bolas planeswalkers are therefore not automatically imported here. The original Nicol Bolas deck is defined by **Bolas himself dealing damage to empty hands**; the Ravager deck can own the stronger Superfriends / transform-planeswalker / Bolas-tribal identity.

Recommended Archidekt tags: **Discard / Commander Damage / Control**.

**Commander-first rules decisions**

**Training Grounds**

Ravager's transform ability is an activated ability of a creature you control while Ravager is on its creature face. Training Grounds reduces the generic portion by `{2}`, from `{4}{U}{B}{R}` to `{2}{U}{B}{R}`. It does not alter colored symbols and does not apply once Nicol is a planeswalker because the relevant creature activated ability no longer exists on that face.

**Displacer Kitten / double-faced reset**

Displacer Kitten can blink front-face Ravager after a noncreature spell, returning the card as a new object on its front face and retriggering the each-opponent discard ETB.

If Kitten blinks the transformed Arisen, the card likewise returns front-face up because a double-faced card enters front face by default unless an effect specifically instructs it to enter transformed. This is therefore an intentional reset line, not a way to blink the Arisen and keep the planeswalker face.

**Deliver Unto Evil**

The transformed commander is a **Bolas planeswalker**, so Deliver Unto Evil's enhanced condition is satisfied while the Arisen is controlled. The spell can then return all of its up-to-four legal targeted graveyard cards to hand rather than allowing an opponent to withhold two.

**Rowan's Talent / Rings of Brighthearth**

Both copy activated loyalty abilities, but the copied abilities do not repeat the loyalty cost. Rowan's Talent automatically copies the enchanted planeswalker's activated loyalty ability; Rings requires `{2}` when its trigger resolves. This lets the candidate multiply the Arisen's draw, removal, reanimation, or ultimate abilities without falsely adding/removing loyalty for the copies.

**The Chain Veil / Rings / Teferi engine**

The Chain Veil's activated ability is nonmana, so Rings may copy it for `{2}`. Official Chain Veil rules state that each additional resolution grants each planeswalker another loyalty activation that turn.

With Teferi, Temporal Archmage plus The Chain Veil plus Rings and mana permanents that can collectively produce six mana after Teferi's `-1` untaps them (for example Mana Vault + Sol Ring + a one-mana permanent/land), the deck can repeat the following engine:

1. Pay `{4}` to activate The Chain Veil and `{2}` for Rings, producing two Chain Veil resolutions.
2. Use one Teferi activation to `-1`, untapping The Chain Veil and enough mana permanents to produce six again.
3. Use another Teferi activation on `+1` to restore the loyalty spent by `-1`.
4. Spend the refreshed six mana to activate/copy The Chain Veil again.
5. Each pair of Chain Veil resolutions adds two more loyalty activations, replacing the two Teferi activations consumed by the loop.

The loop can therefore generate arbitrarily many Chain Veil resolutions / planeswalker loyalty-activation permissions while Teferi's loyalty remains stable. This is a multi-piece Bracket-4 combo engine, not a claim that any two of these cards alone are infinite.

**Sanctum Lurker**

Sanctum Lurker prevents the normal state-based action that puts planeswalkers into their owners' graveyards for having 0 loyalty. A surviving 0-loyalty planeswalker can later activate the granted `[+2]` ability when timing/activation restrictions permit, putting loyalty counters on itself as the activation cost.

**Ripples of Potential / Dreamtide Whale**

Ripples proliferates first, then may phase out any chosen permanents you control that received counters that way. Phasing does not remove the counters, giving the deck a two-mana loyalty-growth/protection play.

Dreamtide Whale can proliferate loyalty counters whenever a player casts their second spell in a turn. Proliferate can also add a time counter to the Whale itself, extending its vanishing clock when strategically useful.

**Dark Intimations**

Dark Intimations remains because it converts multiplayer sacrifice/discard into recursion and draw, and its graveyard rider gives the next **Bolas planeswalker spell** an additional loyalty counter. This rider applies to Bolas planeswalker cards cast as planeswalker spells; it is not treated as a bonus to Ravager's activated transformation, which is not casting a planeswalker spell.
