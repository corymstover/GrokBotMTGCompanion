**Cormela, Glamour Thief — Commander-First Blueprint**

**Refinement date:** 2026-10-13  
**Frozen baseline:** `GrixisFINALTXT60--2026.zip`, member `Cormela__Glamour_Thief.txt`

**Commander identity**

Cormela is a Grixis spellslinger/combo commander. Her tap ability produces {U}{B}{R} that can be spent only to cast instants and sorceries. When Cormela dies, she returns a target instant or sorcery card from your graveyard to your hand. The deck combines fast mana, spell-based tutors and draw, graveyard recursion, sacrifice/reanimation effects, and compact combo finishes.

**Baseline identity retained**

The baseline already contains the central spell-combo package: Brain Freeze, Underworld Breach, Lion's Eye Diamond, Tendrils of Agony, Ad Nauseam, Dualcaster Mage + Twinflame, Cormela sacrifice/reanimation support, and substantial protection. The refinement preserves the established win conditions and the commander-specific recursion plan.

**Auto-approved refinement**

**Cut Grapeshot; add Galvanic Iteration.** Galvanic Iteration costs {U}{R} and sets up a copy of the next instant or sorcery cast that turn; it has flashback for {1}{U}{R}. This can copy high-impact spells such as Tendrils of Agony, Brain Freeze, or Mizzix's Mastery. The copied spell is not cast, so it does not create another storm trigger, but it resolves as an additional copy of the original spell's effect. Galvanic Iteration is itself an instant, so Cormela can recover it from the graveyard when she dies.

Grapeshot is a legitimate storm payoff and can clear creatures, but the deck already has Tendrils of Agony and Brain Freeze as storm-based finishes, plus the Dualcaster Mage/Twinflame line. Galvanic Iteration increases the ceiling of those existing lines and gives Cormela another high-value instant to recur. This is a one-for-one role change, not a claim that Grapeshot is unplayable.

Project candidate ranking: #9 for Cormela; 18.3869% EDHREC inclusion and 11.454502 lift in a 3,856-deck snapshot. EDHREC's current Cormela page shows Galvanic Iteration around 18% inclusion and 11x lift. No gameplay win-rate claim is made.
