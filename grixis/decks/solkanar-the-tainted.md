**Sol'Kanar the Tainted — commander blueprint**

Date: 2026-10-15  
Cycle: `global-restart-2026-10-13`  
Deck: #53 of 62

**Commander identity**

Sol'Kanar is a five-mana U/B/R political-control commander whose end-step trigger offers four modes that may each be chosen only once for that battlefield object: card draw, table drain/lifegain, targeted damage, then exile-and-return under an opponent's control. The optimized deck should exploit both halves of that text rather than merely prevent the drawback.

Primary identity pillars:

1. **End-step mode recycling.** Thassa, Deep-Dwelling; Planar Incision; and a second repeatable blink engine can make Sol'Kanar a new object, resetting which modes have been chosen.
2. **Control-transfer politics.** Donate, Harmless Offering, Bazaar Trader, Blim, Coveted Falcon, Role Reversal, Shifting Grift, Puca's Mischief, Fateful Handoff, Sudden Substitution and related cards turn unwanted permanents into opponent liabilities or convert ownership/control asymmetry into cards and tempo.
3. **Giftable curses / losing permanents.** Demonic Pact, Archfiend of the Dross, Greed's Gambit, Illusions of Grandeur and Plague Reaver reward taking value first and transferring the liability later.
4. **Ownership recovery.** Homeward Path, Coveted Falcon and Planar Incision can recover owned creatures or Sol'Kanar when control changes become inconvenient.

**Natural power ceiling**

**Power Bracket 4 — Optimized.**

The commander supports powerful interaction, tutors, premium mana and compact gift-kill packages, but its natural game plan is slower and more board/sequence dependent than true cEDH Grixis shells. Do not convert it into generic Oracle/Breach or turbo-combo.

**Current-cycle priorities**

- Preserve the historical Thassa reset engine and all high-value transfer/cursed-gift pieces.

- Protect five-mana Sol'Kanar without self-shroud that interferes with blink targeting.
- Accelerate Sol'Kanar while keeping sufficient colored sources.
