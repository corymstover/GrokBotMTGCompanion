**Dr. Eggman — Current-Cycle Commander-First Blueprint**

**Date:** 2026-10-14  
**Cycle:** `global-restart-2026-10-13`  
**Deck:** #15 — Dr. Eggman  
**Status:** refinement blueprint complete; approval candidate not yet user-approved

**Commander identity**

Dr. Eggman is a Grixis artifact/value commander whose defining engine is his beginning-of-end-step trigger:

- draw a card;
- then each opponent faces a villainous choice: that player discards a card, or Dr. Eggman's controller may put a Construct, Robot, or Vehicle card from hand onto the battlefield.

The strongest natural build is therefore not generic Grixis artifacts and not generic discard. It is a **villainous-choice artifact-cheat deck** that makes both branches valuable:

1. maintain a dense hand of high-impact Construct / Robot / Vehicle cards so opponents dislike choosing the deployment branch;
2. punish or harvest actual discards so opponents dislike choosing discard;
3. multiply or repeat Eggman's end-step trigger;
4. prioritize artifact-enter payoffs over cast-only artifact payoffs, because Eggman puts cards onto the battlefield without casting them;
5. maintain enough direct interaction and artifact infrastructure to function when Eggman is removed.

**Primary game plan**

**Early game**
- Develop mana with Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Fellwar Stone, Mox Opal and Talismans.
- Establish durable artifact infrastructure such as Foundry Inspector, Padeem, Scrap Trawler, Master Transmuter, Mechan Assembler, Simulacrum Synthesizer, Weapons Manufacturing, or Ultron, Artificial Malevolence.
- Preserve premium Eggman targets in hand when practical rather than spending every threat conventionally.

**Midgame**
- Resolve Dr. Eggman and turn every end step into a resource fork.
- Use discard payoffs (`Liliana's Caress`, `Waste Not`, `Tinybones, Bauble Burglar`, `Geth's Grimoire`) to make real discard choices costly.
- Use `The Valeyard`, `Roaming Throne`, `Strionic Resonator`, `Spark Double`, and `Y'shtola Rhul` to increase the number or value of Eggman triggers/end steps.
- Convert deployment choices into high-impact entries such as Gearhulks, `Cityscape Leveler`, `Metalwork Colossus`, `Myr Battlesphere`, `Krang, Utrom Warlord`, `Vision, Spectral Synthezoid`, `Thunderhawk Gunship`, and the Ultron package.

**Closing game**
- Overwhelm the table through repeated free high-mana artifact deployment, copied artifact entries, Robot/Construct/Vehicle board development, and discard attrition.
- `Cyberdrive Awakener`, `Cybermen Squadron`, `Tezzeret, Master of the Bridge`, high-power Vehicles/Robots, and large artifact bodies provide combat or life-total closure.
- The deck does not need to become a generic deterministic Grixis combo shell to reach its realistic ceiling.

**Natural power ceiling**

**Bracket 4 — Optimized.**

The commander generates substantial free mana/value and scales strongly with trigger multiplication, but the strategy still depends on a five-mana commander, hand composition, end-step timing, and opponents' villainous choices. The optimized natural ceiling is powerful high-synergy Commander rather than forcing a cEDH shell that would make Eggman incidental.

**Structural priorities**

1. **Eggman-compatible targets:** keep a robust Construct / Robot / Vehicle density. The approval candidate retains 28 legal Eggman deployment targets.
2. **Trigger multiplication:** Valeyard, Roaming Throne, Strionic Resonator, Spark Double, Y'shtola.
3. **Discard conversion:** Liliana's Caress, Waste Not, Tinybones, Geth's Grimoire, plus other villainous-choice support.
4. **Artifact-enter engines:** Mechan Assembler, Simulacrum Synthesizer, Weapons Manufacturing, Ultron Artificial Malevolence, Master Transmuter support.
5. **Hand replenishment:** Eggman's own draw, Thoughtcast, Thought Monitor, Curie, One with the Machine, Geth's Grimoire.
6. **Protection / interaction:** Padeem, Lightning Greaves, Fierce Guardianship, Mana Drain, Deadly Rollick, Deflecting Swat, Cyclonic Rift, Toxic Deluge, Blasphemous Act, Chaos Warp, Withering Torment and artifact-based removal bodies.

**Key construction rule**

Do not overvalue cards that say "whenever you cast an artifact spell." Dr. Eggman's signature deployment effect **puts** the chosen card onto the battlefield; it does not cast it. Enter-the-battlefield and artifact-enter payoffs are structurally preferred when comparable.
