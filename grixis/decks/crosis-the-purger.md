**Crosis, the Purger — Commander-First Blueprint**

**Refinement date:** 2026-10-13  
**Frozen baseline:** `GrixisFINALTXT60--2026.zip`, member `Crosis__the_Purger.txt`  
**Frozen baseline SHA-256:** `bbd4acc84ff0abd5f43045bd28d9cc4a154cbfe2592923967315b9a1497c4435`

**Commander identity**

Crosis is a flying Grixis Dragon whose combat-damage trigger can let its controller pay {2}{B}, choose a color, and make the damaged player reveal their hand and discard all cards of that color. The deck is built around connecting with Crosis, repeated discard, discard-punishment payoffs, and card advantage from opponents losing cards.

**Baseline identity retained**

The list retains its discard engines and payoffs: Bottomless Pit, Dark Deal, Hymn to Tourach, Oppression, Mind Twist, Teferi's Puzzle Box, Wheel of Fortune, Windfall, Megrim, Liliana's Caress, Painful Quandary, Waste Not, Geth's Grimoire, Dream Salvage, and the Specter package. The existing protection, interaction, and fast mana remain unchanged.

**Auto-approved refinement**

**Cut Brotherhood Regalia; add Blizzard Specter.** Blizzard Specter is a four-mana blue-black flying Specter. Whenever it deals combat damage to a player, its controller chooses either to return a permanent that player controls to its owner's hand or to make that player discard a card.

The card adds another evasive threat that directly advances the discard plan while also providing tempo interaction. When it connects, Crosis can separately offer its own paid, color-based discard trigger. The Specter can also choose a bounce effect when discarding is less useful. EDHREC's Crosis Specters page reports 91% inclusion and 91% synergy in its 22-deck sample; the project candidate ranking places it #1, with 47.9074% inclusion and 209.144435 lift in a 1,123-deck snapshot.

The tradeoff is losing Brotherhood Regalia's ward {2} and limited evasion support. Crosis already flies, and Lightning Greaves remains in the list as protection/haste equipment. Blizzard Specter adds a body and a repeatable combat trigger more closely aligned with Crosis's identity.

**Baseline provenance**

The current-cycle baseline is the repository's October 13 Crosis list. Relative to the October 12 frozen source package, that list had exactly one prior-cycle change: Brotherhood Regalia was replaced by Blizzard Specter. Because the global restart made Crosis unapproved, the October 13 list was treated only as the latest authoritative baseline and every slot was re-audited rather than inherited as locked.

**Strategic identity and ceiling**

Crosis is optimized as discard-control built to make the commander connect, convert combat damage into large targeted hand destruction, and exploit discarded cards through punishment, resource generation, theft, and card advantage. The commander costs six mana and requires combat damage plus a {2}{B} payment, so the strongest realistic commander-first build is **Bracket 4 — Optimized**, not cEDH.

**Approved nonland changes**

- **Scythe Specter → Painter's Servant.** Painter is the highest-impact commander-specific upgrade: once Crosis connects, naming Painter's chosen color makes the damaged player's whole hand eligible for Crosis's discard effect.
- **Specter's Shroud → Brotherhood Regalia.** Regalia makes Crosis unblockable, supplies ward {2}, and equips a legendary creature for {1}; it advances the commander more directly than one additional discard-on-hit trigger.
- **Painful Quandary → Sword of Feast and Famine.** Sword helps Crosis connect through black/green blockers, adds discard, and can untap lands before Crosis's trigger resolves so the {2}{B} payment remains available.
- **Megrim → Bandit's Talent.** Liliana's Caress and other damage payoffs already cover the redundant Megrim role; Bandit's Talent adds its own discard event, pressure on empty-handed opponents, and scalable card advantage.
- **Dauthi Voidwalker → Tinybones, Bauble Burglar.** Tinybones converts the deck's core discard events into playable stolen cards while avoiding Voidwalker's replacement-effect tension with Tergrid and Reanimate.
- **Counterspell → Mana Drain.** Same base interaction role with a substantially higher ceiling; the stored mana can accelerate Crosis or support a protected post-counter turn.
- **Bedevil → Recoil.** Recoil answers any permanent while directly advancing the discard plan; against a player with no other cards in hand, the bounced card is forced back to the graveyard.
- **Animate Dead → Swiftfoot Boots.** Reanimate remains as the efficient graveyard-value slot. A second cheap haste/protection piece is more important to a six-mana commander whose value depends on surviving and connecting.

**Approved land changes**

- **Crosis's Catacombs → Shizo, Death's Storehouse.** Removes the Lair tempo cost and adds a black source that can grant Crosis fear.
- **Seething Landscape → Plaza of Heroes.** Replaces a tapped fetch-style land with untapped legendary fixing and an emergency protection mode for Crosis.
- **Smoldering Marsh → City of Brass.** Untapped five-color fixing removes a conditional tapped-land failure point.
- **Sunken Hollow → Cavern of Souls.** Naming Dragon fixes Crosis and makes the commander uncounterable while preserving an untapped land slot.
