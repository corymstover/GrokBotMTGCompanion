# Story Arc Summaries

> **Bot cycle 7 · issue [#34](https://github.com/corymstover/GrokBotMTGCompanion/issues/34).** Narrative summaries of major story arcs, with key beats, outcome and lasting consequences. **Every beat cites the official magic.wizards.com episode it comes from.** Quotes are verbatim; the rest paraphrases the cited episode.
> Short arc entries (sets, planes, characters) live in [Conflicts & Arcs](Conflicts%20%26%20Arcs.md). Characters' status lives in [Characters Index](Characters%20Index.md); see also [Planeswalker Dossiers](Planeswalker%20Dossiers.md) and [Omenpath & Multiverse Connections](Omenpath%20%26%20Multiverse%20Connections.md).
> **Method:** each episode's opening and closing passages were read, plus targeted passages for outcomes. Gaps are marked ❓ rather than filled in. Side stories are mostly not summarized.
> **Coverage (Bot cycle 7):** Phyrexian Invasion (ONE + MOM, with MAT consequences), MKM, OTJ, BLB, DSK, TDM. **Next:** WOE, LCI, Aetherdrift, Edge of Eternities, Lorwyn Eclipsed, Secrets of Strixhaven, Reality Fracture, and pre-2022 arcs.

### March of the Machine
| # | Key beat | Source |
|---|---|---|

| 7 | Karn, once again a prisoner of Phyrexia, is freed. Elspeth defeats Jin-Gitaxias and flies to protect Wrenn and Chandra at the tree. | [Ep. 7: Divine Intervention](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/episode-7-divine-intervention) |
| 8 | Wrenn merges with the Invasion Tree. The Phyrexian armies pour through the gate and find Zhalfir: "Zhalfir stands ready." | [Ep. 8: Wrenn and Eight](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/episode-8-wrenn-and-eight) |
| 9 | Karn kills Elesh Norn himself ("He burns the sight of Norn's corpse into his memory"), then walks through the gate to Zhalfir. | [Ep. 9: The Old Sins of New Phyrexia](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/episode-9-the-old-sins-of-new-phyrexia) |

**Lasting consequences:** Elspeth is "an angel now"; Melira is dead; Karn has given up his spark ([Ep. 10](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/episode-10-the-rhythms-of-life)). Afterwards most sparks fade: "Teferi's spark had faded … Koth's as well" ([She Who Breaks the World](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/she-who-breaks-the-world)). Nahiri closes Zendikar: "No Planeswalker will set foot on Zendikar ever again" ([Beyond Repair](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/beyond-repair)). Omenpaths then let non-Planeswalkers travel between planes; see the [Omenpath era](Master%20Timeline.md#omenpath-era-post-invasion). ❓ The in-story origin of the Omenpaths isn't explained in the fetched sources.

Her body is laid to rest, but "her ghost was with Kaya".
**Lasting consequences:** Kaya leaves Ravnica, and Proft and Etrata become partners. Niv-Mizzet intends Ravnica to be the hub of the Omenpath-connected Multiverse ([Ep. 11](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/episode-11-portents-and-omens)). ❓ Judith's fate after Massacre Girl's attack: "no one's claimed the kill" ([Ep. 10](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/episode-10-roots-of-decay)), so it's ambiguous in the text read.

"Ashiok" turns out to be Jace in disguise ([BLB Ep. 1](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/episode-1-calamity-comes-to-valley)), which ties this heist to the Echoverse thread ([Conflicts & Arcs → Jace's Echoverse](Conflicts%20%26%20Arcs.md#jaces-echoverse-reality-fracture)). ❓ The episodes read don't say what the vault holds.

| 5 | On Ravnica, Proft has an idea that uses the "piece of Duskmourn" Kaito brought out. | [Ep. 5: Don't Give In](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/episode-five-dont-give-in) |

After the escape, a moth-engraved door appears in Proft's office ([Ep. 6](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/episode-six-dont-die)), so the threat isn't over. ❓ Whether Valgavoth survived is left open in the episodes read.

## Tarkir: Dragonstorm (2025)
**Sets:** TDM · **Index entry:** [Conflicts & Arcs → TDM](Conflicts%20%26%20Arcs.md#tarkir-dragonstorm-2025) · **Status:** ✅ (main episodes; the clan side stories are not summarized)

| # | Key beat | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A winged woman with glowing eyes arrives seeking Narset: "Her name is Elspeth … if she has come seeking me on Tarkir, something terrible must have happened." | [Ep. 1: Stories and Their Bones](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/tarkir-dragonstorm-episode-1-stories-and-their-bones) |
| 2 | The Jeskai won't help Elspeth, but Narset will: "But I can." | [Ep. 2: Omens](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/tarkir-dragonstorm-episode-2-omens) |
| 3 | The grieving Sarkhan Vol is approached by Taigam: "I think I have something you want." | [Ep. 3: What the Past Devours](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/tarkir-dragonstorm-episode-3-what-the-past-devours) |
| 4 | Narset and Elspeth find Ajani, who has exiled himself in grief: "I'm afraid I must." He tells them about Sarkhan and the wild dragons. | [Ep. 4: Heart of Fire](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/tarkir-dragonstorm-episode-4-heart-of-fire) |
| 5 | Jace appears with Loot, whose mind "contains all of the Multiverse, every Omenpath that exists … He is a map of everything." Loot is kept in a cocoon of blue light; Jace says Loot "consented the very first time when we released him from the vault." Asked "What have you done, Jace?", he answers: "What I needed to." | [Ep. 5: Recursion](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/tarkir-dragonstorm-episode-5-recursion) |
| 6 | A flashback with Vraska, then the present: reality breaks "into mirrored fragments … unmaking reality". Jace "shattered like glass", and Narset flees with Loot. | [Ep. 6: How Wretched Love](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/tarkir-dragonstorm-episode-6-how-wretched-love) |

**Outcome:** The dragonstorms are reined in by Ugin ("The dragonstorms behave like whelps when I'm not there"). Jace is unmade, and Narset escapes with Loot.
**Lasting consequences:** Ugin says **Nicol Bolas is free** ([Ep. 7](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/tarkir-dragonstorm-episode-7-return)). Loot, a living map of every Omenpath, is now out of Jace's hands. The closing "blue cloak" reflection leads into the [Echoverse](Conflicts%20%26%20Arcs.md#jaces-echoverse-reality-fracture). ❓ Ep. 5 doesn't name "the vault"; that it's the Thunder Junction vault from OTJ Ep. 6 is an inference, not stated. ❓ Sarkhan's fate after the ghostfire isn't stated outright in the passage read.
