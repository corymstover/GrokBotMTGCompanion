# Story Arc Summaries

> **Bot cycles 7–8 · issue [#34](https://github.com/corymstover/GrokBotMTGCompanion/issues/34).** Narrative summaries of major story arcs, with key beats, outcome and lasting consequences. **Every beat cites the official magic.wizards.com episode it comes from.** Quotes are verbatim; the rest paraphrases the cited episode.
> Short arc entries (sets, planes, characters) live in [Conflicts & Arcs](Conflicts%20%26%20Arcs.md). Characters' status lives in [Characters Index](Characters%20Index.md); see also [Planeswalker Dossiers](Planeswalker%20Dossiers.md) and [Omenpath & Multiverse Connections](Omenpath%20%26%20Multiverse%20Connections.md).
> **Method:** each episode's opening and closing passages were read, plus targeted passages for outcomes. Gaps are marked ❓ rather than filled in. Side stories are mostly not summarized.
> **Coverage:** 19 arcs, 135 cited beats. Bot cycle 7 added Phyrexia, MKM, OTJ, BLB, DSK and TDM. Bot cycle 8 added the six major Gatewatch-era arcs (BFZ/OGW, SOI/EMN, KLD/AER, AKH/HOU, DOM, WAR) and the remaining Omenpath-era arcs (WOE, LCI, DFT, EOE, ECL, SOS, Reality Fracture). Other pre-2022 arcs (Tarkir, Ixalan, ELD → NEO, SNC, DMU, BRO) remain as sourced short entries in [Conflicts & Arcs](Conflicts%20%26%20Arcs.md).

## Battle for Zendikar & Oath of the Gatewatch (2015–16)
**Sets:** BFZ, OGW · **Index entry:** [Conflicts & Arcs → Gatewatch](Conflicts%20%26%20Arcs.md#eldrazi-awaken--battle-for-zendikar--gatewatch) · **Status:** ✅ main beats

| # | Key beat | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Jace returns to the Eye of Ugin, "when he inadvertently helped release the Eldrazi", to unravel the hedron network that "might be used to capture—or kill—the titan Ulamog". | [Revelation at the Eye](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/revelation-eye-2015-10-14) |
| 2 | Gideon's army liberates Sea Gate. Kiora brings word: "Ulamog is coming!" | [The Liberation of Sea Gate](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/liberation-sea-gate-2015-10-28) · [Hedron Alignment](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/hedron-alignment-2015-11-18) |
| 3 | Ulamog is caught in the hedron ring and seems close to defeat, "but Ob Nixilis had called Kozilek, one of the other Eldrazi titans, from beneath Zendikar's surface." | [BFZ Story Summary](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/battle-zendikar-story-summary-2015-12-23) · [The Rise of Kozilek](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/rise-kozilek-2015-12-09) |
| 4 | Chandra rescues Gideon, Jace and Nissa. The four swear oaths ("A choice—a commitment, a promise. To keep watch."), founding the Gatewatch. | [Oath of the Gatewatch](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/oath-gatewatch-2016-02-03) |
| 5 | The plan is set: "Two Eldrazi titans now roam Zendikar's surface," and the allies spread out to lure them. | [Brink of Extinction](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/brink-extinction-2016-02-10) |
| 6 | Nissa and Jace bind the titans with leylines. Jace stops Kiora's sea from hitting Nissa. With Nissa's touch, "the mana of an entire world" pours into Chandra: "The leylines themselves caught fire … enveloping the titans." Afterward the survivors "slowly realized that it was over." | [Zendikar's Last Stand](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/zendikars-last-stand-2016-02-17) |

**Outcome:** Ulamog and Kozilek are destroyed in leyline fire, at the cost of much of Zendikar ("tripping over corpses of Eldrazi creatures and Zendikari alike").
**Lasting consequences:** The Gatewatch is founded and carries through to the War of the Spark. Emrakul, absent from Zendikar, appears on Innistrad (see [SOI/EMN](#shadows-over-innistrad--eldritch-moon-2016)).

## Shadows over Innistrad & Eldritch Moon (2016)
**Sets:** SOI, EMN · **Index entry:** [Conflicts & Arcs → Innistrad & Emrakul](Conflicts%20%26%20Arcs.md#innistrad--emrakul) · **Status:** ✅ main beats; ⚠️ details of how Sorin was defeated are condensed

**Lasting consequences:** The guilds put bounties on the collaborators (Kaya to hunt Liliana, Ral to hunt Tezzeret, Vraska to hunt Dovin Baan). Bolas is imprisoned in the Meditation Realm and later escapes through an Omenpath ([LE Ep. 2](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/lorwyn-eclipsed-episode-2-shake-off-slumber)). Ajani urges a renewed Gatewatch: "If we truly wish to honor our friend Gideon…"
