### Post-Mending (Gatewatch era)
Order follows story publication (B). Alara reconverges → Eldrazi freed on Zendikar → New Phyrexia conquers Mirrodin → Innistrad (Avacyn, later Emrakul) → Theros → Tarkir (Fate Reforged) → Battle for Zendikar & the Gatewatch → Kaladesh revolt → Amonkhet/God-Pharaoh → Ixalan → Return to Dominaria → War of the Spark (Bolas's Elderspell). Sources: see [Conflicts & Arcs](Conflicts%20%26%20Arcs.md).

### Phyrexian Invasion
Dominaria United → The Brothers' War (frame story) → Phyrexia: All Will Be One → March of the Machine & Aftermath: Realmbreaker invades every plane; Elesh Norn dies; Elspeth becomes an angel; sparks lost (e.g., Nissa). ✅ [MOM Ep. 10](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/episode-10-the-rhythms-of-life) · [She Who Breaks the World](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/she-who-breaks-the-world)
