## Commander text (current Oracle) and the rules it depends on
- Lord of Tresserhorn: "When Lord of Tresserhorn enters, you lose 2 life, you sacrifice two creatures, and target opponent draws two cards. / {B}: Regenerate Lord of Tresserhorn." Correction to the brief: there is **no opponent-regeneration clause**. The regeneration ability regenerates only Lord, it is activated by Lord's controller, and it costs {B}.
- The enters ability is **one triggered ability** that targets an opponent (CR 603.2, 603.3). Ruling 2004-10-04: if you control zero or one other creature, Lord itself is sacrificed as part of the effect, and the rest of the effect still happens. The life loss is not a payment, so it can take you below 0 (ruling 2004-10-04).
- Regeneration (CR 701.19a) replaces only *destruction*. Sacrifice is not destruction (CR 701.21a), so {B} regeneration never saves Lord from his own trigger, from an edict, or from lethal -X/-X. It does save him from destroy effects and lethal damage (Blasphemous Act, combat). Terminate-style "can't be regenerated" turns the shield off (CR 701.19c). A regenerated attacking Lord is removed from combat (CR 701.19a).
- Suppression:
  - **Torpor Orb** ("Creatures entering don't cause abilities to trigger") and **Karn, Argent Defender** ("Artifacts and creatures entering the battlefield don't cause abilities to trigger") stop the trigger from ever triggering (rulings 2024-04-12 and 2026-08-21). If Karn and Lord enter at the same time, the trigger is still stopped.
  - **Dress Down** ("Creatures lose all abilities", layer 6) must already be on the battlefield as Lord enters. Lord then enters with no abilities, so there is nothing to trigger.
  - Removing a suppressor while Lord is still on the stack restores the trigger. Removing it after Lord has entered does not create a trigger after the fact.
- Counters on the stack: **Stifle** ("Counter target activated or triggered ability"), **Tale's End** and the new **Consign to Memory** ("Counter target triggered ability or colorless spell", replicate {1}) can each counter Lord's trigger once it is on the stack (CR 701.6). A countered trigger has no effect at all: no life loss, no sacrifice and no cards for the opponent. Consign's copies from replicate (CR 702.56) can counter a second trigger, for example an opponent's death or enters trigger in the same window.
- Commander damage: an ordinary unblocked 10-power Lord needs three hits to reach 21 commander damage (CR 903.10a). Uncaged Fury makes him 11/5 with double strike, which is 22 combat commander damage if both damage steps connect (CR 510.4, 702.4). Double strike without a power boost gives only 20, which is why the owner cut Temur Battle Rage on 2026-10-14.
- Infect (CR 702.90): damage to players becomes poison counters, and ten counters kill. Phyresis or Tainted Strike on a 10-power Lord is a one-hit kill against one player. Kediss's damage to each other opponent is noncombat damage dealt by Lord, so it carries infect if Lord has infect, but it is not commander damage (Kediss ruling 2020-11-10).

## Selected change 1: +Fierce Guardianship −Stubborn Denial
- Fierce Guardianship: "If you control a commander, you may cast this spell without paying its mana cost. Counter target noncreature spell." Any commander you control satisfies it (ruling 2020-04-17). Once you begin casting, opponents cannot remove the commander to stop the free cast. CR 903.3d: "controlling a commander" means a commander permanent on the battlefield, so FG is free only once Lord has resolved (or with Lord on the battlefield); while Lord is a spell on the stack FG costs {2}{U}. Both cards counter noncreature spells only. That includes an opponent's counterspell aimed at Lord on the stack, but in that window FG costs {2}{U} and Stubborn Denial is only a soft counter unless another power-4 creature is on the battlefield (Lord on the stack does not turn on ferocious). Cavern of Souls naming Zombie remains the clean answer there.
- Stubborn Denial: "Counter target noncreature spell unless its controller pays {1}. Ferocious — If you control a creature with power 4 or greater, counter that spell instead." Lord (power 10) turns on ferocious. It costs {U}, but it is a soft counter without a 4-power creature.
- **Flag:** Stubborn Denial was an owner-cycle ADD on 2026-10-14. Fierce Guardianship was author-rejected in that closure as a generic staple, but M6 Pass C Stage A reopened it and asked for this exact class of test, so it is admissible. It is a Game Changer (2 → 3).

## Selected change 2: +Consign to Memory −Feed the Swarm
- Consign to Memory ({U} instant, mh3 2024-06-14): counters target triggered ability or colorless spell; replicate {1}. It is a fourth on-stack answer to Lord's own trigger (with Stifle, Tale's End and Muddle-tutored pieces). It also answers opponents' enters, death and attack triggers and colorless spells (Sol Ring, artifacts, Eldrazi). Ruling 2024-06-07: each replicate copy is countered separately.
- Feed the Swarm ({1}{B} sorcery): destroys a creature or enchantment an opponent controls, and you lose life equal to its mana value. The life loss still happens if the target is indestructible (ruling 2024-11-08).
- **Disclosed loss:** after this swap and the Edict cut, hard enchantment removal is only Chaos Warp, plus Otawara bounce and Toxic Deluge/Blasphemous Act against creatures only. Feed the Swarm is not an owner addition. The closure audit called it a KEEP.

## Selected change 3: +Black Market Connections −Cover of Darkness
- Black Market Connections ({2}{B} enchantment): at the beginning of your first main phase, choose one or more: Treasure (lose 1), draw (lose 2), or a 3/2 colorless Shapeshifter token with changeling (lose 3). The changeling token is every creature type, including **Zombie**. That makes it (a) fodder for Lord's sacrifice, (b) the "another Zombie" Gravecrawler needs, and (c) a body that Diregraf Captain pumps.
- Cover of Darkness ({1}{B}): creatures of the chosen type have fear (CR 702.36). Lord is black, so any black or artifact creature can block him. Shizo, Death's Storehouse already gives Lord fear. Not an owner addition; the closure audit called it a KEEP.

## Selected change 4: +Ornithopter of Paradise −Fellwar Stone
- Ornithopter of Paradise ({2} Artifact Creature — Thopter 0/2, flying, {T}: add one mana of any color). It is a **creature**, so unlike Fellwar Stone it counts toward the two creatures Lord's trigger sacrifices. It is also a flying chump blocker. With Torpor Orb or Karn out it simply enters (it has no enters trigger). It has summoning sickness, so it cannot tap for mana the turn it enters (CR 302.6), while Fellwar Stone can. Fellwar Stone makes only colors an opponent's land could produce. Not an owner addition.

## Selected change 5: +Skullclamp −Sheoldred's Edict
- Skullclamp ({1} Equipment, equip {1}): equipped creature gets +1/−1; whenever it dies, draw two cards. Equipped to a toughness-1 creature (Bitterblossom's 1/1 Faerie Rogue tokens, Reassembling Skeleton 1/1, Gravecrawler 2/1, Bloodghast 2/1), the creature has toughness 0 and is put into the graveyard as a state-based action (CR 704.5f; regeneration can't replace it), and you draw two for {1}. Sacrificing a clamped creature to Lord's own trigger draws two, which offsets the opponent's two cards. Clamped to Lord, he is 11/3. That is 11 commander damage per hit, but his toughness drops to 3.
- With Torpor Orb or Karn, Skullclamp's dies trigger still works, because it is not an enters trigger.
- Not a combo claim: Skullclamp + Bitterblossom is card advantage that is bounded by tokens and mana. Spellbook lists no new combo.
- **Disclosed loss:** Sheoldred's Edict (instant; each opponent sacrifices a nontoken creature, a creature token, or a planeswalker) answers hexproof/indestructible threats. In the B3 grid, 2 of 14 threat cells are answered by Edict and by no kept spot removal. Sweepers cover those 2 at a large cost. Not an owner addition; the closure audit called it a KEEP.

## Staged / rejected rules notes
- **Temur Battle Rage** (owner-cycle CUT 2026-10-14; not reopened by Pass C): it gives double strike and trample with ferocious. The prior GrokBot draft added it back over Pain for All. That is **withdrawn as inadmissible** and kept only as a disclosed rejected configuration.
- **Soul's Fire** (Pass C, from the original author): "Target creature you control deals damage equal to its power to any target." Lord deals 10, and with infect that is 10 poison to one player. It is an instant. Pain for All is an Aura (sorcery speed): when it enters, Lord deals damage equal to his power to any other target, and whenever Lord is dealt damage he deals that much to each opponent. Blasphemous Act (13 damage) then reflects 13 to each opponent while {B} regeneration saves Lord (owner line 6).
- **Dress Down / Kediss / Phyresis / Tainted Strike** (Pass C mechanics item):
  - Dress Down's ability loss is layer 6 (CR 613.1f), and effects within a layer apply in timestamp order (CR 613.7). An Aura's timestamp is when it became attached. Phyresis attached **before** Dress Down is overridden; Phyresis attached **after** it grants infect. Tainted Strike resolving after Dress Down grants infect (ruling 2021-06-18: an ability granted after Dress Down entered is not lost).
  - Kediss is a creature, so its trigger is gone while Dress Down is on the battlefield; do not count a same-turn Kediss spread. Lord's own {B} regeneration is also gone that turn.
  - Anger's graveyard haste has the timestamp of when Anger went to the graveyard (ruling 2004-10-04), so Anger already there before Dress Down is overridden. Lightning Greaves re-equipped after Dress Down ({0}) restores haste and shroud.
  - The full 24-cell truth table is in the bounded measured tests (B5) and in `tests/test_m6_lord_of_tresserhorn_m7_mechanics.py`.
- **Distortion Strike** (Pass C, 34-land stress case): +1/+0 and can't be blocked this turn, with rebound. Rogue's Passage ({4},{T}) does the same each turn without spending a card.
- **Aqueous Form**: Aura, can't be blocked, scry on attack. It falls off if Lord changes zones; a recast Lord is unenchanted.
