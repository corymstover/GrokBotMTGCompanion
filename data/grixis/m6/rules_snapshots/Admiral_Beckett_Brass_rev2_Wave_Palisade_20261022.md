## Commander and theft eligibility

Admiral Beckett Brass is `{1}{U}{B}{R}`, legendary Human Pirate. Other Pirates we control get +1/+1. At **the beginning of our end step**, we gain control of **target nonland permanent controlled by a player who was dealt combat damage by three or more Pirates this turn**. The qualifying three Pirates need not be the same three that attacked initially; they must have **dealt combat damage to that SAME player**. Damage by non-Pirate tokens or noncombat sources does not replace the three Pirate sources. If Admiral leaves before beginning of end step, her ability does not trigger. If the trigger has gone on the stack and Admiral later leaves, normal independent triggered-ability rules apply; the target must still be legal on resolution.

## Changed spell mechanics and exclusions

1. **Cosmotronic Wave:** `{3}{R}`, sorcery, source Oracle: 1 damage to each creature opponents control, and creatures opponents control **cannot block this turn**. All eligible opposing Pirate blockers also cannot block. The prohibition lasts the current turn only. Wave does not target friendly Pirates or opposing blockers, cannot be cast during ordinary opponent combat, and does not give a Pirate haste/flash, prevent spells/removal/Fog, or itself deal player combat damage.
2. **Raise the Palisade (removed):** `{4}{U}`, sorcery, choose a creature type, return **all creatures not of that type** to owners' hands. Choosing Pirate keeps our Pirate attackers but also keeps an opponent's Pirates. Choosing another type may bounce our own qualifying Pirates. A bounced blocker remains out until replay but this is not permanent removal, and enters-the-battlefield synergies are reset.
3. **Fiery Cannonade (retained):** `{2}{R}`, instant; deals two damage to **each non-Pirate creature**. This protects our Pirate subtype board against small non-Pirate blockers/attackers at opponent combat timing. It cannot be substituted with Wave or Palisade for an instant-speed response and does not remove opposing Pirate blockers regardless of toughness.
4. **Open into Wonder (retained):** `{X}{U}{U}`, sorcery, X friendly creatures can't be blocked this turn and each gets a draw-on-player-combat-damage trigger. X=3 costs `{3}{U}{U}`=5 real mana, including **two blue**; a source counting only five generic mana is invalid. Unlike Wave, it draws up to three cards on three hits and targets the chosen friendly creatures.
5. **Bident of Thassa and The Indomitable (both retained):** Each separately draws on any one of our creatures dealing combat damage to a player, not merely on Pirates and not solely a single draw total for the turn. Kari Zev's attacking Ragavan **Monkey token** does not qualify toward three Pirates, but if it damages a player can still cause a draw from each present effect. The separate printed **Ragavan, Nimble Pilferer card** has Monkey Pirate subtype and *does* qualify if it damages as a creature.
6. **March of the Drowned (retained):** `{B}` instant? Source Oracle contains its modalities; return one target creature card or two target Pirate cards from graveyard to hand. This card supports recovery after defensive trades and was not silently traded for Captain Storm's counters. *(Verify exact type in saved source before drawing any instant-timing conclusion; no such timing conclusion is required here.)*
7. **Path of Ancestry (retained):** Enters tapped, produces any commander identity color; scry if its mana was spent to cast a creature sharing a type with the commander. Shizo's untapped black and one-legend fear activations are not a universally free replacement.
8. **Departed Deckhand, Skyship Plunderer, Broadside Bombardiers:** Exact prior candidate D source and executed P0 fixtures still apply because these printed cards were unchanged by the Wave/Palisade swap. Deckhand sacrifices if **targeted by a spell**, not by Wave's targetless effect; Skyship counters require existing kind and an actual combat-damage trigger; Broadside's boast only after its attack and actual sacrifice of another permanent.
