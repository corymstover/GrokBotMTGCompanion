## Deck ID and commanders (naming discrepancy recorded)
- The tracker deck ID `Grixistax___October_2026` is a deck name, not a commander name. The decklist's two `[Commander]` lines and the registry name the Partner pair **Kraum, Ludevic's Opus** and **Tevesh Szat, Doom of Fools**. The tracker `commander_names` field writes them as "Kraum, Ludevic's Opus // Tevesh Szat, Doom of Fools"; the " // " there only joins the pair and does not denote a double-faced card.
- CR 702.124b: Your deck must contain exactly 100 cards, including its two commanders. Both commanders begin the game in the command zone.
- CR 702.124c: A rule or effect that refers to your commander's color identity refers to the combined color identities of your two commanders (U/R + B = U/B/R).
- CR 702.124d: Except for determining the color identity of your commander, the two commanders function independently; commander tax is tracked separately (CR 903.8).

## Selected change 1: +Arcane Signet −Mox Amber (Pass C requested test)
- Arcane Signet ({2} artifact): "{T}: Add one mana of any color in your commander's color identity." Ruling (2020-11-10): "If you have two commanders, the ability adds one mana of any color in their combined color identities." Here U, B or R.
- Mox Amber ({0} legendary artifact): "{T}: Add one mana of any color among legendary creatures and planeswalkers you control." Rulings (2018-04-27): "If you control no legendary creatures or legendary planeswalkers, you can activate Mox Amber's ability, but you won't add any mana." / "If your legendary creatures and legendary planeswalkers are all colorless, ... you won't add any mana. Colorless is not a color." The 98 has legendary creatures/planeswalkers Ragavan, Nimble Pilferer, Sheoldred, the Apocalypse, Narset, Parter of Veils, Ashiok, Dream Render and Karn, the Great Creator (colorless, so Karn alone makes Amber produce nothing); Kraum costs five and Tevesh five.
- Talisman of Indulgence ({2}): "{T}: Add {C}. {T}: Add {B} or {R}. This artifact deals 1 damage to you." (No blue.) Measured: equal raw mana to Arcane Signet but no U, so it is dominated.
- Contamination's replacement ("If a land is tapped for mana, it produces {B} instead") affects lands only; Arcane Signet and Talismans still make U/R under our own Contamination (ruling 2004-10-04: "The second ability of this card is a replacement effect, not a triggered ability."). Blood Moon / Magus (CR 305.7) affect lands only, so rocks keep colours.

## Selected change 2: +Bitterblossom −Windfall
- Bitterblossom ({1}{B} kindred enchantment — Faerie): "At the beginning of your upkeep, you lose 1 life and create a 1/1 black Faerie Rogue creature token with flying." Ruling (2008-04-01): "The effect is mandatory. You'll lose 1 life even if you have only 1 life left."
- Contamination: "At the beginning of your upkeep, sacrifice this enchantment unless you sacrifice a creature." Both are our upkeep triggers; CR 603.3b lets us order them so Bitterblossom resolves first and the new Faerie pays Contamination. Contamination ruling (2004-10-04): "You choose whether to sacrifice a creature or not on resolution."
- Tevesh Szat +1 sacrifices a Faerie to draw two; Skullclamp ("Equipped creature gets +1/-1. Whenever equipped creature dies, draw two cards. Equip {1}") on a 1/1 Faerie makes it 2/0, which dies (state-based action) and draws two. Phyrexian Tower ("{T}, Sacrifice a creature: Add {B}{B}"), Culling the Weak (additional cost: sacrifice a creature), Diabolic Intent and Beseech the Mirror's bargain (sacrifice an artifact, enchantment, or token) all take a token; Beseech can also bargain Bitterblossom itself. Tangle Wire taps a Faerie; tokens do not trigger Overburden ("nontoken creature"). CR 701.21a: sacrificing moves the permanent directly to the graveyard.
- Leyline of the Void ruling (2024-09-20): "Tokens can still die while Leyline of the Void is on the battlefield." (Leyline only affects opponents' graveyards.)
- Cost: 1 life per upkeep, alongside Necropotence's life payments; recorded as a downside.
- Windfall ({2}{U} sorcery): "Each player discards their hand, then draws cards equal to the greatest number of cards a player discarded this way." Symmetric refill for opponents unless Narset, Parter of Veils or Notion Thief is out; with Necropotence on the battlefield ("Whenever you discard a card, exile that card from your graveyard") our discarded cards are exiled. Commander Spellbook: Windfall is in no included combo for this list (6 combos before and after).

## Staged, not counted: +Opposition −Static Orb (or −Magus of the Moon)
- Opposition ({2}{U}{U}): "Tap an untapped creature you control: Tap target artifact, creature, or land." Ruling (2022-12-08): "You can tap any untapped creature you control to activate Opposition's ability, even one you haven't controlled continuously since the beginning of your most recent turn." (Not a {T} cost, so CR 302.6 / 602.5a do not apply; summoning-sick Thrulls and Faeries can pay.) Ruling (2022-12-08): "Once a player announces that they're casting a spell or activating an ability, no player may take other actions until the spell or ability has been paid for."
- Static Orb ({3}): "As long as this artifact is untapped, players can't untap more than two permanents during their untap steps." CR 502.3: the active player determines which permanents untap. With Opposition, our tapped token army would also be capped at two untaps, so the two are anti-synergistic; Winter Orb ("players can't untap more than one land") does not cap creatures.
- Reason not counted: measured deployability. Bounded P(Opposition seen and castable, needing four mana with two blue-capable sources) by turn 4 is 0.0329 with no lock and 0.0034 under our own Contamination, versus Static Orb (three generic) 0.0599 and 0.0584. UU under our own Contamination or Moon is a real self-lock. Not clearly better; staged as an owner decision.

## Rejected rules notes
- Frozen Aether ({3}{U}): "Artifacts, creatures, and lands your opponents control enter tapped." Ruling (2007-02-01): "They do not enter untapped and then immediately tap." Four mana, does not stop instant-speed combo.
- Strip Mine: "{T}, Sacrifice this land: Destroy target land." Under our own Blood Moon / Magus (CR 305.7) it loses this ability and becomes a Mountain.
- Gemstone Caverns: luck counter only if "you're not the starting player" (about three games in four in four-player); kept.
- Torpor Orb ("Creatures entering don't cause abilities to trigger") also stops our own Orcish Bowmasters' enter trigger. Ruling (2024-04-12): "Torpor Orb stops a creature's own enters-the-battlefield triggered abilities as well as other triggered abilities that would trigger when a creature enters the battlefield." Overburden ("Whenever a player puts a nontoken creature onto the battlefield, that player returns a land") is such a trigger, so Overburden does not trigger while Torpor Orb is on the battlefield; the two do not stack. (This corrects an earlier working note in this pass that said Overburden still triggers.) Recorded as an existing baseline tension between two incumbents (Overburden explicitly retained by the owner), not changed.
