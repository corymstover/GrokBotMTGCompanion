## Commander text (current Oracle)
- {2}{U}{B}{R} 3/6 Legendary Creature — Human Scientist, Flying.
  - "At the beginning of your end step, draw a card. Then each opponent faces a villainous choice — That player discards a card, or you
    may put a Construct, Robot, or Vehicle card from your hand onto the battlefield."
- Identity: **stock the hand with high-impact Construct/Robot/Vehicle cards so that every opponent must either lose a real card (which
  Liliana's Caress, Waste Not, Tinybones, Geth's Grimoire and Entropic Battlecruiser punish) or let a machine in for free.**

## Rules relied on
- **701.55a**: facing a villainous choice means choosing one option and then performing it. **701.55b**: a player may choose an option
  that is impossible; an opponent with an empty hand may choose "discard a card", discards nothing, and no "whenever an opponent
  discards" ability triggers. Likewise, when we hold no Construct/Robot/Vehicle card the "you may put" option costs the opponent nothing.
  This is the deck's central counterplay; payload density in hand is therefore the commander's main lever.
- **701.55c**: The Valeyard's replacement makes each opponent face the choice an additional time, one at a time. It multiplies
  **choices, not Eggman's draw** (Pass C #15 point kept). **701.55d**: several opponents choose one at a time in APNAP order, so a later
  opponent sees the earlier result.
- **Put, not cast**: Eggman puts the card onto the battlefield, so it is not cast (601.2 describes casting). "When you cast" abilities
  do not trigger: Cityscape Leveler's cast trigger is lost (its attack trigger and 8/8 trample remain); Foundry Inspector's and Tezzeret's
  cost reductions do not apply. **603.6a**: enters-the-battlefield abilities do trigger — Possession Engine, Skysovereign, Interceptor
  Mechan, Behemoth of Vault 0 (energy), Demonic Junker, the Gearhulks, Myr Battlesphere and Thopter tokens all work when put.
- **301.7a / 702.122a**: a Vehicle has power/toughness only while it is a creature; putting Possession Engine or Skysovereign does not
  crew it. Their ETBs need no crew; attacking needs Crew 3. Possession Engine's control and "can't attack or block" last only while we
  control the Vehicle (Oracle).
- **513.1 / 514.1**: Eggman's trigger resolves in the end step, before cleanup; any excess over seven cards is discarded in cleanup.
  Eggman's own draw happens before the choices, so a freshly drawn payload is immediately eligible.
- **Trigger multipliers** (Oracle): Roaming Throne naming Human or Scientist makes Eggman's trigger (and a Spark Double copy's) trigger an
  additional time; Spark Double's copy is nonlegendary, so it survives the legend rule; Strionic Resonator copies one trigger per untap;
  Y'shtola Rhul's first-end-step clause adds one additional end step (**500.9**), in which Eggman triggers again. The mechanics test
  enumerates all 16 on/off combinations: the maximum is 9 Eggman resolutions in one turn (18 choices per opponent with The Valeyard).
  This is finite; no infinite loop is claimed.
- **Goblin Welder** (Pass C hypothesis): a two-target exchange that returns an artifact card from the graveyard directly to the
  battlefield; it never puts a card into our hand and so never adds an Eggman payload. **Liliana's Caress** triggers only on an actual
  discard (701.55b). Both are modelled in the bounded grid of `tests/test_m6_dr_eggman_m7_mechanics.py`.
- Commander Spellbook (2026-10-23): **0 included combos** in both baseline and candidate (36 and 30 "almost included"). No infinite or
  deterministic combo is claimed.
