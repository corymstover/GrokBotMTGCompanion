# Admiral Brass, Unsinkable: candidate-specific rules snapshot (2026-10-23)

Oracle source: `data/grixis/m6/oracle_snapshots/Admiral_Brass__Unsinkable_M7_rev2_Scryfall_20261023.json` (Scryfall `cards/collection`, retrieved 2026-10-23T02:30:18-05:00 for 109 names, plus one second-pass batched call; identical Oracle text to frozen snap-20261016 for cards present there). Comprehensive Rules: current published CR as of 2026-10-23. Paraphrased; cite the CR for exact wording.

1. **Brass beginning-of-combat trigger.** Targets a Pirate creature card in your graveyard when it triggers; if the target is gone on resolution, it does nothing (CR 608.2b). The returned creature has base P/T 4/4 (a characteristic-defining layer 7b effect; Admiral Beckett Brass's +1/+1 still applies on top), haste until end of turn and a finality counter.
2. **Finality counter.** Under CR 122.1h (CR of 2026-09-25), any permanent with a finality counter that would be put into a graveyard from the battlefield is exiled instead. This still applies if the permanent has lost its abilities, for example after Larcenist has turned it into a Treasure. Removing the counter first (Power Conduit, at instant speed) restores normal dying. Bounce or blink makes a new object with no counter (CR 400.7).
3. **Roaming Throne (chosen type Pirate).** Each Pirate creature trigger (including Brass, Larcenist ETB, Jhoira enters/attacks, Warkite attack, Siren's Ruse is not a Pirate trigger) triggers one additional time. Each instance chooses its own targets. Two Brass instances can't usefully both target the same single card.
4. **Timestream Navigator.** {2}{U}{U}, {T}, put on library bottom: extra turn, only with the city's blessing (ascend, 10 permanents). Brass's haste satisfies {T} the turn it returns. It goes to the library, not the graveyard, so it's one use per return.
5. **Kitesail Larcenist.** The ETB turns up to one artifact or creature per player into a Treasure for as long as Larcenist stays on the battlefield. A finality exile or bounce ends the effect.
6. **Warkite Marauder.** The attack trigger makes a defending player's creature a 0/1 with no abilities until end of turn.
7. **Jhoira, Weatherlight Corsair.** Enters/attacks: the opponent reveals until a historic permanent card; you put it onto the battlefield under your control and lose life equal to its MV.
8. **Ragavan (not in rev2 list; Monkey Pirate).** Combat damage to a player: create a Treasure and exile that player's top card, which you may cast this turn. Its payoff needs combat damage, unlike **Changeling Outcast**, which can't block and can't be blocked. Changeling applies in every zone (CR 604.3 / 702.73), so Outcast is a Pirate creature card in the graveyard and a legal Brass target.
9. **Nesting Grounds.** Moves a counter only at sorcery speed. **Power Conduit** has no sorcery restriction.
10. **Living Death / mass reanimation.** Finality creatures that die are exiled, so they are not returned.
11. **Siren's Ruse.** It exiles target creature you control and returns that card to the battlefield under its owner's control, drawing a card if it was a Pirate. The returned permanent is a new object (CR 400.7): no finality counter, no Brass 4/4 base or haste, summoning-sick, and its ETB triggers again. A creature we stole returns to its owner, so don't Ruse stolen creatures.
12. **Ripples of Undeath.** Triggers at the beginning of your precombat (first) main phase: mill three, then you may pay {1} and 3 life to put one of the milled cards into your hand. Beginning of combat comes after that main phase, so a Pirate milled that turn is a legal target when Brass's trigger is put on the stack.
13. **Pitiless Plunderer (cut).** It triggers whenever another creature you control dies. Finality-exiled Pirates don't die. Other creatures do: hard-cast creatures, tokens, stolen creatures, and Pirates reset by Conduit or Ruse.
