## Decision-changing interactions and exact wording rules

1. **Admiral Beckett Brass:** End-step trigger targets a **nonland permanent controlled by a player who was dealt combat damage by three or more Pirates this turn**. Merely attacking, noncombat Forerunner life loss, an extra damage event by one Pirate, or three separate opponents each hit by one Pirate is not enough. The commander must be on battlefield for trigger creation at beginning of endstep, though subsequently removing its source does not automatically remove an ability already on the stack. Condition and legal target still matter. The damage can come from three Pirate sources that began combat earlier in the turn than the commander was cast, but no such special case is claimed as part of the payable turn-four fixture.
2. **Cloud Pirates and Skyship Plunderer:** Both fly; flying may be blocked by a flyer or reach creature. Cloud Pirates additionally can only block flyers. Both are Pirate creature sources for distinct damage if they actually hit.
3. **Departed Deckhand:** Is a Spirit **Pirate**, not an unblockable noncreature; it is blockable by Spirits, and if targeted by a **spell** its sacrifice ability triggers. An equip **ability** is not a spell. Its activated `{3}{U}` grants the Spirit-only block restriction to **another** target creature, not itself.
4. **Skyship Plunderer and Malcolm, Alluring Scoundrel:** Skyship adds one of every **existing** kind of counter on a target permanent/player only if it hits; it cannot add a first chorus counter. Two already present chorus counters, both on-hit triggers and Skyship-first ordering can make Malcolm reach four at Malcolm trigger resolution, possibly casting the **actually discarded** card for no mana cost. Reversing trigger order reaches four too late to exploit that same Malcolm resolution. No free spell or card draw is assumed without actual sources.
5. **Broadside Bombardiers:** `Menace, haste` allows a just-entered attack; boast can be activated **only after attacking, once per turn**, and requires sacrificing **another** artifact/creature. A Treasure token's mana value is 0, yielding `2 + 0 = 2` damage; a two-mana Arcane Signet can yield 4 but consumes real acceleration. Two eligible blockers can block menace. There is no automatic third Pirate combat-damage source via a boast damage event.
6. **Forerunner of the Coalition:** ETB **searches a Pirate card to the top of library**, not hand; each **another Pirate entry** forces each opponent to lose one life. Daring Piracy's new hasty Pirate token each own combat triggers this repeatedly if Forerunner stays alive, even if the token never connects in combat. This is noncombat life loss and does not count toward Admiral's theft trigger.
7. **Blustersquall vs Open into Wonder:** Overload `{3}{U}` Blustersquall taps each opposing creature, not a persistent prevent-untap effect; Open at X=3 costs `{3}{U}{U}`, makes three selected own creatures unblockable that turn and gives actual on-hit draws. It is a sorcery, costs one more mana/one more U at X=3, and only its own targeted creatures gain the draw triggers.
8. **Merchant Raiders and The Indomitable:** Raiders can tap one target creature per Pirate entry and disallow its controller's untap for as long as Raiders remains controlled; Daring Piracy supplies repeated entries. Indomitable's whenever-a-creature-deals-player-combat-damage draw is a static ability on the artifact permanent, **not** conditional on crewing the Vehicle. Its graveyard cast permission has the separate three-tapped-Pirates/Vehicles condition.
9. **Mana and zones:** City of Brass deals one triggered damage whenever tapped, separate from a one-life activation cost of Mana Confluence. Cavern/Courtyard colored mana restrictions remain in force; Smoldering Marsh, shock lands, check lands, Path of Ancestry and Xander's Lounge have real entry conditions. No overloaded spell is charged its printed one-blue cost when overloaded.
