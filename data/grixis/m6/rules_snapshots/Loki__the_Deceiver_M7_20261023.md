## Commander text (current Oracle) and the rules it depends on
- Loki, the Deceiver: "Whenever Loki attacks, create a tapped and attacking token that's a copy of another target Villain you control, except it isn't legendary and it's an Illusion in addition to its other types. Sacrifice that token at the beginning of the next end step. / Whenever one or more Villains you control deal combat damage to a player, draw a card."
- CR 508.4: a creature put onto the battlefield attacking was never declared; "for the purposes of trigger events and effects, they never 'attacked.'" So the token's **enters** and **combat-damage** triggers work, but its **"whenever ... attacks"** triggers do not. The Master, Multiplied ruling (2023-10-13) states the same for tokens that enter attacking.
- CR 707.2 / 707.9b: the token copies printed (copiable) values; the "isn't legendary" and "Illusion" exceptions are part of the copy. A Villain type granted by Loki's Scepter is not a copiable value, so the token is a Villain only if the original printed card is (it is still a legal target while the Scepter effect lasts).
- CR 107.3m / Spark Double ruling (2019-05-03): a copy of a permanent with {X} in its cost has X = 0. X-cost Villains are therefore poor Loki targets and were excluded from the search.
- CR 608.2b: if the targeted Villain leaves the battlefield in response, Loki's trigger has an illegal target and does nothing. Lightning Greaves' shroud on the intended target makes it untargetable by Loki too (CR 702.18a), while Swiftfoot Boots' hexproof (CR 702.11b) does not stop our own targeting.
- "Whenever one or more Villains you control deal combat damage to a player" triggers once per player dealt damage, not once per Villain.
- Delayed sacrifice: the token is sacrificed at the beginning of the next end step. The Master, Multiplied ("Triggered abilities you control can't cause you to sacrifice or exile creature tokens you control") stops that sacrifice and turns off the legend rule for tokens. Sundial of the Infinite must be activated with the sacrifice trigger on the stack (activating it earlier skips the end step and the trigger happens on the next turn's end step; ruling 2011-09-22).
- Roaming Throne naming Villain makes Loki's attack trigger (and a copied Villain's enters trigger) trigger an additional time; targets are chosen separately for each instance (ruling 2023-11-10).

## Selected change 1: +Ultimo, Civilization's End −Crimson Cowl, Master of Evil
- Ultimo ({4}{B}{B} Legendary Artifact Creature — Robot Villain 6/5): "Menace / When Ultimo enters, each opponent sacrifices a creature of their choice. / {2}{B}, Discard this card: Each opponent sacrifices a creature of their choice."
- A Loki token copy of Ultimo enters (CR 603.6a), so every Loki attack makes **each opponent** sacrifice a creature; with Roaming Throne the enters trigger happens twice. The sacrifice gets around hexproof, shroud, indestructible and ward. The menace 6/5 copy usually connects, which triggers Loki's draw. The discard mode is an instant-speed edict from hand when Ultimo can't be cast. Baron Strucker, Tombstone and Urza's Incubator (naming Villain) reduce its generic cost.
- Crimson Cowl ({2}{B}): "Whenever one or more nontoken Villains you control attack a player, you create a 2/1 black Villain creature token with menace." A Loki token copy of Cowl does nothing useful: Cowl's trigger already resolved at declare attackers, and the copy itself never "attacked" (CR 508.4).

## Selected change 2: +Elektra, Daughter of the Hand −Killmonger, Ruthless Usurper
- Elektra ({2}{B}{B} Legendary Creature — Human Ninja Villain 3/3): "Sneak {1}{B}{B} ... / When Elektra enters, destroy target creature an opponent controls with power 3 or less."
- A Loki token copy of Elektra enters, so every Loki attack destroys a power-3-or-less creature (mana dorks, utility creatures and many cEDH-style commanders). With Roaming Throne it destroys two.
- Sneak (CR 702.190a/b): during the declare blockers step, cast Elektra for {1}{B}{B} by returning an unblocked creature you control to hand; she enters tapped and attacking the same player. Returning Loki's soon-to-be-sacrificed token pays the cost at no card loss. This is still a cast Villain spell, so Madame Hydra triggers and Baron Strucker / Tombstone reduce the generic part of the sneak cost.
- Killmonger ({2}{R}): "Trample / Whenever Killmonger attacks, he gets +1/+0 ... / Whenever Killmonger deals combat damage to a player, that player sacrifices an artifact of their choice and you create a Treasure token." A Loki copy keeps only the combat-damage half (the attack pump never triggers, CR 508.4) and has to connect to do anything.

## Pass C requested tests (staged, not counted)
- Strionic Resonator ({2}; "{2}, {T}: Copy target triggered ability you control. You may choose new targets for the copy."): copies Loki's attack trigger so you get a second token (CR 707.10c, new target allowed), or copies a token's enters trigger. Rulings 2018-03-16: the copy has the same source, and choices made on resolution are made separately. It costs {2} plus {2} per turn and taps.
- Swiftfoot Boots ({2}, Equip {1}): haste lets Loki attack the turn he is cast; hexproof protects him from opponents' targeted removal without stopping his own trigger's targeting. Rulings 2018-03-16: haste must last until attackers are declared, and one Boots can't give haste to two new creatures in the same turn.
- Seize the Day ({3}{R} sorcery, Flashback {2}{R}): "Untap target creature. After this main phase, there is an additional combat phase followed by an additional main phase." Cast it in the **postcombat** main phase so the untapped Loki can be **declared** again in the added combat, which triggers his attack ability a second time (CR 500.8). Cast precombat, it adds a combat in which Loki's first attack already used him, so it gives no second attack. Rulings 2018-12-07: if the target becomes illegal, no phases are added; flashback follows sorcery timing (2021-03-19).
- Black Market Connections ({2}{B}): at the beginning of your first main phase choose any of: a Treasure (lose 1 life), draw a card (lose 2), or a 3/2 changeling token (lose 3). The changeling token is a Villain, so it is a legal (vanilla) Loki target.

## Rejected rules notes
- Victor Timely, Wily Tycoon ({4}{U}): "When Victor Timely enters, you may cast target artifact, instant, or sorcery card with mana value 4 or less from your graveyard without paying its mana cost. If that spell would be put into your graveyard, exile it instead." A Loki copy recasts Demonic Tutor, Reanimate, Chaos Warp, Deadly Rollick, Toxic Deluge (its X is an additional life payment, so it can still be chosen when the spell is cast free) and similar. Staged against Kang Prime; not counted.
- Kingpin, Wilson Fisk: "Whenever you sacrifice Kingpin or another creature, create two Treasure tokens. This ability triggers only once each turn." Loki's end-step sacrifice feeds it, but The Master, Multiplied removes that sacrifice.
- Secluded Courtyard: enters untapped, but its coloured mana is only for creature spells of the chosen type (Villain) or abilities of such creatures; the deck's coloured noncreature spells (Mana Drain, Rhystic Study, Kindred Discovery, Dark Ritual, Demonic Tutor, Toxic Deluge, Chaos Warp, Jeska's Will) would get only {C}.
