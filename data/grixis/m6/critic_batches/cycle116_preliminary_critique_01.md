# Cycle 116 — Skeptical preliminary critique: Kess, Edea, Lynde

**Not formal independent Pass C/I/J.** Preregistration `data/grixis/m6/phase_b_plans/cycle116_three_decks_preregistered_01.json` preceded the authored conclusions. We interrogate actual approved lists and the frozen archived Oracle at CI before merging; P0 M5 screening scores do not imply deck improvements. No canonical card swap, approval or M6 promotion is authorized.

## Kess — HOLD Hullbreaker Horror / Orcish Bowmasters
Kess has once-per-own-turn permission to **cast an instant or sorcery from the graveyard**, with ordinary timing. This does *not* confer recursive creature casting or perpetual instant-speed access. Hullbreaker Horror gives flash, a hard-to-counter seven-mana spell-chain bounce engine; this could dominate a long control pod, but a legal repeatable low-cost spell + mana ledger has not been shown in the exact source 99. Kess's actual fast path is one-shot tutor/recast into Oracle via Consultation/Pact and Breach-LED/Grinding Station. Bowmasters is compact flash interaction against surplus opponent draw and wheels; exchanging it for a seven-drop without measured improvement to actual own-turn wins undercuts fringe-cEDH tempo. Preserve Bowmasters.

**Countercase to test:** if proven realistic Hullbreaker loops materially raise protection and win conversion versus grindy control, search a *different cut*, not necessarily Bowmasters. Unmeasured.

## Edea — HOLD Tidespout Tyrant / Deadly Rollick
Edea is five mana and steals a target creature only at **beginning of combat on your own turn**. Her reward is triggered by an enemy-owned creature **dying while you control it**, returning it under owner control and drawing a card. Tyrant triggers on your spell cast to **bounce a target permanent**; bouncing the borrowed creature back to its owner is not dying, so it does not draw via Edea and often sacrifices the attack. An eight-mana generic bounce engine doesn't double theft or improve Goblin Bombardment, Mass Mutiny, Ashnod's Altar, Thassa exile/blink or sacrifice-payoff loops by itself. Deadly Rollick, when a commander is controlled, can exile a target opposing creature without spending mana; losing that efficiently timed interaction increases the vulnerability of deploying Edea into a table with threatening creatures.

**Countercase to test:** Tyrant could be strong if there is a source-listed, repeatable, mana-positive spell loop and opponent-resistant board position. None is established in this authored source review. Preserve Rollick; do not conflate bounce with death.

## Lynde — HOLD Hullbreaker Horror / Withering Torment
A key double-role cut was hidden by generic candidate screening. Withering Torment can target a **Curse you control**, even while it is attached to an opponent, because you still control that enchantment; destroying it puts it into **your** graveyard. Lynde's delayed return brings it back attached to you at the next end step, then a later upkeep may transfer one to an opponent and draw two. This *does not* mean the return can trigger a previous upkeep, nor that generic bounce into hand satisfies the death-to-graveyard clause. Withering Torment also answers opposing enchantments, an unusually meaningful role in Grixis. Hullbreaker Horror returns a nonland permanent to hand instead of destroying it, and a seven-mana flash control threat has no direct Curse graveyard/sacrifice function.

**Countercase to test:** a slow game might favor Hullbreaker bounce, but retaining Curse-reset flexibility and actual enchantment removal appears more important. If the candidate is investigated further, test a different cut and the legal turn sequence. Preserve Withering Torment.
