- **Four** (both cards replacing Birgi or Kessig) are historical M5 items. Preliminary strategic judgment disfavors losing early tablewide opponent-life-loss throughput or Birgi's cast-time mana / Harnfel access.
- **Two** (both cards replacing Bituminous Blast) were specifically added as separately researched M6 alternative cuts and must not be falsely counted as accepted M5 swap IDs.

## Critical opportunity costs and missing decision gates

1. Removing Bituminous Blast removes a printed-cascade instance and instant-speed creature-targeted removal, distinct from the opponent-life-loss gained by delayed activated abilities. The full legal target, printed-versus-granted cascade and follow-up cost/hand availability must be measured.
2. Heartless {3}{R}{R} deals floor(half each player's life) damage to everyone on activation. It can enable an enormous Abaddon threshold but damages its controller and usually cannot tap on entry. Conditional event sizes and an unusually large X are **not** expected value, access probability or card-chain advantage.
3. Tree {3}{B}, 0/13 taps to exchange life with a *target opponent*. It does no damage, may gain life at low target totals, and removing Tree before resolution prevents the exchange (specific Wizards ruling), unlike Heartless's activated ability persistence.
4. A reproducible deckwide *effect-aware* baseline/challenger comparison with actual hand-held casts, source-backed colored mana, legal activations after summoning sickness, opponent response policies and the effects of **both** the added and cut cards remains absent. Cycle 104 modeled 744 land-and-Signet access samples across decks; it never compared these Abaddon candidates.
5. Printed cascade can hit unsupported card effects. Every declined, unsupported offer is an **unknown real value**, not zero value. Require a preregistered unknown-event budget and output `cannot_evaluate` if the budget prevents a clear effect-level conclusion.
6. Formal independent Pass C, I/J reproducibility and tested-pool certs remain unissued. Owner approval is necessary for any new canonical 100-card list; no resolution of all 98 rows can be inferred from this six-row focus.
