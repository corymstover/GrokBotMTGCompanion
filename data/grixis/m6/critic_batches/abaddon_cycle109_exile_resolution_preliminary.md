## Frozen source, reproducibility, and scope

- The [original preregistration](../phase_b_plans/abaddon_exile_resolution_01.json) was committed **before implementation** at `beb548d6dc22f23dec5f4710359f2756a05b6a00`.
- [Full deterministic receipts](../phase_b_bounded/abaddon_exile_resolution_01.json) and [compact reproducible audit](../phase_b_bounded/abaddon_exile_resolution_summary_01.json) rebuild from the canonical 99+commander Abaddon deck and the Cycle 107/108 source artifacts.
- 240 matched original baseline seeds and opening seven cards were replayed under **each of two alternative scripted opponent 3/3 target policies**. The 480 runs are not independent Commander games. All 100 original physical IDs remained uniquely conserved.
- Four spell names were source-verified against the pinned October 2026 Scryfall Oracle: **Mana Vault, Sol Ring, Demonic Tutor and Vampiric Tutor**. All seven offers retained their original names, physical IDs, and reveal counts from Cycle 107.
- Each permitted cascade hit was moved from the real physical library to exile, then cast for zero mana from **exile**, put on the stack above Bituminous Blast, resolved after four consecutive priority passes, and finally moved to the correct zone. None received Abaddon's hand-only cascade. The underlying Bituminous Blast and target-lost-versus-target-surviving interactions were preserved.

The first four comprise one physical Sol Ring, one Mana Vault, one Demonic Tutor, and one Vampiric Tutor. Demonic Tutor uses a declared deterministic target order from the real current library, shuffles and places the chosen original physical card into hand; Vampiric Tutor shuffles and places the selected card atop the library while paying two pilot life. These are real bounded zone/life changes, **not proof a tutor target is strategically optimal**.

The two mana rocks are allowed to enter untapped and then tap for respectively two/three colorless mana during the active priority window while the underlying Blast remains on the stack. The resulting five colorless mana are observed but **not used** to cast a follow-up. A mana source's output cannot be equated with useful mana spent or a win.

## Rules caveats and failure boundaries

1. **Mana Vault's actual untap/upkeep:** unlike other artifacts, tapped Mana Vault stays tapped across untap steps. A first green CI run revealed that the inherited generic turn-reset path had failed to preserve this restriction. This was corrected before merge. Its mandatory printed upkeep damage trigger still lacks priority/response handling, so the experiment flags **two unresolved upkeep events per response policy** rather than falsely resolving or ignoring them.
2. **Three major unresolved offers:** Chrome Mox needs a suitable imprint card, exiled color, and choice-value cost; Windfall depends on all players' actual hands and discard/redraw triggers; Deflecting Swat needs a legal target spell/ability and meaningful redirection. These were all **declined with potential value UNKNOWN_NOT_ZERO**.
3. **Nonland engine omission:** Beyond the four hits, most cards in the deck do not get played during the sampled own-turn access policy. Kessig/other noncreature cast triggers are not broadly evaluated; artifact activated mana can be generated without observed follow-through.
4. **Partial priority and target environment:** Only an externally scripted opposing 3/3 and a four-pass response cycle are modeled. The opponent removing it is not a real cast removal spell. No full multiplayer turns, threats, counterspells, stack strategy, combat, mulligans, tutors' strategic selection, death/prevention/replacement effects, or completed win conditions are modeled.
5. **Tiny conditional subset:** Four actual cast/resolutions among seven offered hits in 240 fixed-policy seeds cannot substantiate win-rate, strength or power bracket. Seven offers under two response policies are the same seven independent source seeds, not fourteen independent offers.
6. **Unresolved comparative value:** The Heartless Hidetsugu and Tree of Perdition challenger decks have *not* been replayed in equivalent opponent board/hands with effect-realized values; cannot infer any superiority or no-improvement verdict.
