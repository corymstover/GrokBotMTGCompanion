## Commander-first decision principle and model limitation

Abaddon grants cascade only to spells **cast from hand on your turn** with mana value <= the total life lost by opponents **that same turn**. A life-loss enabler operating before your first main-phase spell is more generally valuable than an expensive, delayed, opponent-dependent combat trigger. Abaddon's blueprint centers cheap damage/drain, cascade, exile-cast payoffs, fast mana, protection, and a compact separate Underworld Breach route. Opponent combat damage and non-damage life loss both contribute to X; neither turns exiled or escaped spells into hand-cast spells.

**All 15 swap hypotheses in this batch carry positive exploratory M5 goldfish verdicts but explicitly fail to model the ADDED card's relevant active abilities** (and often the removed card's). Hence **zero of these 15 results is independent evidence of superior gameplay**. Combo-count proxies, speculative half-life triggers and extra-turn cards are not promoted to wins. Cycle 79 `M6_M5_proxy_coverage_guard` requires a new effect-aware Phase-B comparison and named strategic judgment.

## Rules and evidence caveats

1. **Tree's life exchange counts as life loss, not damage.** A 40-life opponent exchanged with an unchanged 13-toughness Tree loses 27 life for X. Summoning sickness still prevents activating Tree's tap ability in the turn it enters under normal conditions. Tree must be on the battlefield when that ability resolves. Source: [Wizards release notes (Tree of Perdition)](https://media.wizards.com/2024/downloads/INR_Release_Notes_hzvmSd7KJG/EN_MTGINR_ReleaseNotes_20240903.pdf).
2. **Shredder's token copies enter attacking but were not declared attacking.** Their combat-damage triggers still work if they connect, but do not each generate another wave of copies. Source: [Wizards TMNT release notes](https://media.wizards.com/2026/downloads/TMT_Release_Notes_eM8Yplm3UV/u17aHk8iV1_EN_MTGTMT_ReleaseNotes_20251001.pdf).
3. **Half-life effects are not interchangeable.** Slasher, Quietus Spike and Shredder require combat damage; Grievous Wound requires damage to the enchanted player; Tree exchanges a life total directly; Hidetsugu deals rounded-down damage to *everyone*. These differences affect prevention, blocker sensitivity, thresholds, life loss and postcombat casting windows. Card text references: [Unstoppable Slasher](https://mtg.wtf/card/dsk/119/Unstoppable-Slasher), [Quietus Spike](https://mtg.wtf/card/c17/220/Quietus-Spike), [Grievous Wound](https://mtg.wtf/card/dsk/102/Grievous-Wound), [Heartless/Solphim discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/EDH/comments/12pql3y).
4. **A completed Spellbook near-miss does not guarantee the line is executable in this exact 99.** Confirm actual permanent configuration, commander required/not required, life/mana/resource constraints, priority, recurrence and victory endpoint. Neither `Grinding Station` nor any proposed half-life combo is approved on the evidence read so far.
5. **Card legality** in the M5 review packet is exploratory status based on its Birthday snapshot, not an independent current Scryfall recheck performed for all 15 adds in this memo.
