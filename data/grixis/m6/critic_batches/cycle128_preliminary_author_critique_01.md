# Cycle128 preliminary author critique — six commander-specific HOLD cases

These are actual named **authored** commander-first candidate judgments for The Valeyard, Norman Osborn, Sol'kanar the Swamp King, Garza Zol, Evelyn, and Sauron, the Dark Lord. They are not independently reviewed and do not imply 21 Phase B or Pass C completions.

- **Valeyard:** Seven-mana Hullbreaker does not multiply actual Villainous Choices or votes and demands further cast spells; losing conditional free Deadly Rollick exposes the commander.
- **Norman/Green Goblin:** Seven-mana generic spellbounce trades away early Orcish Bowmasters against draw engines in an established turbo spell-combo list.
- **Sol'kanar:** The candidate is ordinarily blue, while the two-mana black Rakdos Charm both triggers the commander and covers graveyard, artifact and tokens. Painter is a conditional color exception to be tested.
- **Garza Zol:** Kamahl may become a deathtouch pinger with Collar but his damage does not count as Garza damage; Rapid Hybridization protects the actual seven-mana Voltron payoff.
- **Evelyn:** Hullbreaker is not a Vampire, does not create collection-counter ETBs on its own and cannot change Evelyn's once-a-turn exile-play limit; Bowmasters' cheap stack-relevant timing matters.
- **Sauron, the Dark Lord:** Tyrant is eight-mana separate control without amass, Army combat or Nazgûl scaling; free Deadly Rollick protects Sauron's actual Army and commander development. Seven Nazgûl copies are covered by printed deckbuilding permission.

**Counterexamples:** Hullbreaker/Tidespout become meaningful in extended mana-rich games or when a demonstrably accessible source-listed loop pays for spells and yields an actual commander-specific strategic advantage. Kamahl may control creature boards with deathtouch equipment, but that doesn't increase Garza's counters. These should be tested rather than assumed irrelevant.
