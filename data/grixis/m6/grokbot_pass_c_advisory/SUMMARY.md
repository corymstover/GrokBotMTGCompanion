## Rules and legality issues flagged
- **Admiral_Brass__Unsinkable**: see its file. First rules note: Finality means a returned creature that dies is exiled, so each Pirate in the yard is effectively a single-use resource. Power Conduit can remove that counter (fact, Oracle).
- **Anhelo__the_Painter**: see its file. First rules note: A Little Chat has its own casualty 1. With Anhelo, two separate casualty instances should allow two sacrifices and two copies (unresolved hypothesis; verify against CR 702.153).
- **Doctor_Doom__King_of_Latveria**: see its file. First rules note: Doom's combat trigger targets a Villain. Doom himself is a Villain, so it always has a target (fact). Norman Osborn // Green Goblin is listed under its canonical name. No alias was renamed.
- **Lynde__Cheerful_Tormentor**: see its file. First rules note: On upkeep with both Thirst and Lynde triggers, Thirst's "that player" is fixed as you when it triggers. Moving Thirst first doesn't stop that damage (CR 603.2, judgment pending verification).
- **Sedris__the_Traitor_King**: see its file. First rules note: Unearth's "if it would leave the battlefield, exile it instead" also applies to blink. The card is exiled anyway and comes back as a new object (CR 702.84a; fact).
- **The_Madcap_Jester**: see its file. First rules note: Keep official legality (not_legal today) separate from any project exception. Cory or ChatGPT should confirm whether an exception covers MBC cards (fact).
- **Tresserhorn_s_Lord__Returned**: see its file. First rules note: Indestructible doesn't stop the ETB sacrifice. If it's sacrificed, its own 'whenever you sacrifice a creature' trigger still draws, as a leaves-the-battlefield look-back (CR 603.10a; judgment, fact-based).
- **Legality pending (official vs project):** Scryfall live (2026-10-20) lists The Madcap Jester and Dragon's Smile (The_Madcap_Jester), plus Tresserhorn's Lord, Returned (commander of Tresserhorn_s_Lord__Returned), as Commander **not_legal**. They're Mystery Booster Commander printings dated 2026-10-19, not 2026-11-13 future releases. Cory or ChatGPT must confirm whether a project exception covers them. No other deck in #43–#62 relies on a non-legal card. The 2026-11-13 dates are only reprint printings of legal cards (shocklands).
- Obeka, Brute Chronologist: Glorious End's 'your next end step' loss follows you, and 'next end step' sacrifice/exile effects are only delayed by ending the turn.
- Sedris: Sundial of the Infinite doesn't keep unearthed creatures.
- Project exceptions (Lutri companion; Trade Secrets and Yawgmoth's Bargain; 2026-11-13 future-release cards) are preserved and unchanged.
