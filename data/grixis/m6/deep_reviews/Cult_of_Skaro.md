## 2. Commander, protected identity and natural ceiling
Cult of Skaro is `{1}{U}{B}{R}` 4/4 legendary artifact creature — Dalek. **Whenever it ATTACKS**, choose at random on the stack: **Thay** +1/+1 to EACH artifact creature you control; **Caan** draw 2; **Sec** create 3/3 black Dalek artifact creature token with menace; **Jast** each opponent loses 4 life. It does not need to connect. At a three-opponent table Jast is 12 aggregate life loss but not 12 to any one player. Random mode means a trigger copy repeats the chosen outcome, whereas a newly triggered instance independently chooses its mode. No card in the baseline gives a guaranteed Jast every combat.
**Protected strategic signature:** repeatedly declare Cult and genuine nonlegendary Cult copies as attackers; amplify separate attack triggers / legal trigger copies; Doctor Who/Dalek/Cyberman artifact army uses Thay and Sec; recoup lost mana via artifact acceleration and Sword; modest interaction is necessary for survivability. Preserve approved **Bracket 4 — Optimized**, **Artifacts / Extra Combats / Copies**. Do not escalate to generic Demonic Tutor/Breach cEDH or dismantle the Dalek core for unrelated high-scoring creatures.
Critical weakness: multiple four-to-six-mana payoff investments (Helm 4+5, Brudiclad 6, Full Throttle 6, The Master 6, Spyder 5) need a surviving board and at least one protected attack. An untapped blocker does not stop initial random trigger but can deny **Sword's combat-damage trigger** and kill Cult after triggering.

**Synthesis and slot pressure.** The 34 lands include 3 U, 3 B, 3 R basics; fetchable triome/duals/shocks; **three vulnerable colored artifact lands** (Great Furnace, Seat of the Synod, Vault of Whispers); Urza's Saga as a *temporary* colorless land; Academy Ruins and Inventors' Fair primarily colorless, and Arena of Glory as a red/Cult-haste source. The curve is top-heavy but backed by Sol Ring, Mana Vault, Mox Opal (metalcraft conditional), Arcane Signet, Dimir Signet, Fellwar Stone and three Talismans plus reducers Shuri, Sculptor, Mechanaut. Commander `1UBR` requires at least three distinct colored pips; generic colorless rocks do not alone guarantee the turn-three/four cast. Urza's Saga's third-chapter sacrifice can exacerbate low land counts. No precise probability or colored-source turn metric was computed here; do not claim benchmark speed based on card count alone.

**Advantages**: actual named-multiplier density (Resonator, Scientist, Throne, Helm, Shuri, Sakashima, Spark Double, Metamorph, Auton, Irenicus, Nanogene); native scalable Dalek/Cyberman bodies; two strong artifact creature card engines (Research Thief, Thought Monitor) plus Omnitool, Spyder, Padeem; efficient protective stack spells. **Weaknesses**: few hard artifact tutors (Inventors' Fair finds 1 artifact; Saga finds mana-value 0 or 1 artifacts only); high mana to deploy some engines; artifact wipes threaten 3 artifact lands as well as board; not all clones have haste; no combat-damage assurance for Sword; recursion limited to Academy Ruins/Emry and other card-specific routes. **Likely weakest flexible slots in some matchups**: Biotransference when opponents deny subsequent artifact spellcasting or four-mana double-black setup is unreliable (but Necron tokens per later artifact spell are valuable); five-mana Canoptek Spyder when most growth is TOKEN artifacts; six-mana Brudiclad when few copyable tokens or immediate pressure; conditional five-mana Missy; low-impact Dalek Drone on high-powered tables. Each has a real identity/engine cost. **Do not auto-cut** Adaptive Omnitool, Full Throttle, Great Train Heist, Fellwar Stone, Dalek Squadron, Ashad or Genesis simply because M5 selected them as heuristic cuts.

**Strongest alternative cut reasoning.** Biotransference is a costly, double-black four-mana candidate cut, but its actual Oracle payoff is substantial here: each subsequent artifact SPELL CAST makes a 2/2 Necron artifact creature token at 1 life, while your creature spells/cards and battlefield creatures also become artifacts. That directly strengthens Cult's Thay and Sec swarm strategy. Cutting it for Karlach is consequently much less attractive than a simple role label suggests. Canoptek Spyder is 5MV and does not draw on token entries, making it a plausible cut for a token-heavy metagame, but losing the *draw-per-nontoken-artifact-creature-ETB* line harms Ashad copies and genuine artifact-creature development.
