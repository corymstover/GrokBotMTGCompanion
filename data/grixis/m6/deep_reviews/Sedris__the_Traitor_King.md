## Commander identity and natural ceiling

Sedris costs `{3}{U}{B}{R}` and grants each creature CARD in your graveyard the activated ability **unearth {2}{B}**, which returns that card to battlefield **only at sorcery speed**, gives haste and schedules exile at beginning of next end step. If the unearthed permanent would leave battlefield, it is **exiled instead**. Sedris turns mill/discard into a toolbox of hasty creature ETBs/attack effects; it does **not** cast creatures, cannot unearth artifacts/enchantments and cannot be cheated into repeat graveyard returns by sacrificing an unearthed body. Casting Sedris itself needs six mana and all three colors; grave setup can precede cast via Looting/Entomb/Buried Alive/Unmarked Grave/Gifts/Ripples.

**Protected identity:** land/drain/counter into six-mana commander, low-priced {2}{B} activated ETB threats, graveyard setup, Panharmonicon, cost-reducing Convergence, timed rescue Obeka/Sundial/Teferi's Veil/Deadeye, River Kelpie / Twilight Diviner engine, high-impact Archon/Terror/Grave Titan/Primordial, backup fair reanimation and zero-mana protection. The Deadeye Navigator + Peregrine Drake land-untap combo is a **creature-based** line with functional tie to Sedris and natural ETB finishers; does not justify replacing the deck with generic Artifact/Breach/Oracle turbo. Convergence lowers unearth's TWO generic, down to B (minimum one mana); never removes B. The highest plausible ceiling is a strong optimized grave-engine deck, with GY exile/hate, six-drop timing and 31-land draws as clear brakes. Source bracket4 is credible, **not proof** of turn-specific tournament wins.

### Structural risk and the real cut hierarchy

**Mana:** 31 lands (10 repeatable nonland sources, 10 source-model fast-mana items, 6 fetches, 3 unconditionally tapped/6 conditional tapped lands), no MDFCs, no rituals. Source U17/B19/R15 on land; U22/B24/R20 including repeatable nonlands. Unconditional by-T4 four-drop no-mulligan probability only **0.470937**, T5 fifth drop **0.303191**; all-three-land-color *presence* T4 **0.737265**. These are not Sedris cast odds, which require six mana+three colors and often extra 2B that turn. Colored fixes (three Signets, three Talismans, Arcane, Command Tower, City, Mana Confluence) offset accelerated CCC rocks, but late land starvation remains a genuine weakness. Fetching tapped surveil duals fuels graveyard yet can postpone Sedris by one whole turn. Strongest additional ramp temptation would displace **slow high-end creature** rather than Cut Jeska's Will or grave tutor; test deficit empirically before altering locked source.

**Role tension:** commander+Convergence costs 6 + 2 to deploy enchantment, before grave activations; Obeka 4, Sundial1, Veil2, Deadeye6 and Panharmonicon4 all ask for setup turns. High ceiling cannot assume ALL pieces available. Archon, Primordial, Wurm and Grave Titan produce immediate value with a single 2B unearth; Jin-Gitaxias still draws seven at first end step if unearthed in main phase: draw trigger and unearth exile trigger occur together and can be ordered to resolve draw first; his long-lived opposing hand-size pressure nevertheless needs rescue. Sheoldred must be rescued before the NEXT opponent upkeep to obtain her opponent-sacrifice trigger, making her the comparatively weaker one-use target. Etali has an attack trigger and haste helps; Ten-mana Jin is a Commander-identity concession only if preserved with Veil/Obeka/Sundial. Sneak Attack complements unearth by exploiting hand-held high-MV creature rather than grave-held one. Corpse Connoisseur and Vile Entomber are unearthable grave tutors and protected; cutting them for cheap clone/bounce combo cards often collapses engine access. Metamorphosis Fanatic and Twilight Diviner are newer reanimation/clone tools to preserve, not just generic curve slots.

**Weakest contingent slots with counterarguments:** Jin-Gitaxias (turn boundary), Sheoldred Whispering One (future upkeep), high-MV Sepulchral Primordial (opponent grave dependency), Etali (combat interaction), Chainer Nightmare Adept (requires separate discard resource), River Kelpie (slow standalone draw), Panharmonicon (four mana no board impact) and Likeness Looter (activation setup). But each has real upside in different pods; cutting Archon/Terror/Vile/Unmarked Grave/Remora for a speculative combo **is not a low-opportunity-cost cut**. Graveyard interaction is the most pronounced strategic hole: one opposing Rest in Peace/Leyline of the Void/Rakdos Charm can shut down Sedris, and Feed the Swarm/Rift/counters are the limited answers. Fast tapout six-cost Sedris makes Fierce/Force/Swat essential. Wipes Deluge/Living Death can hurt own pieces, but without Deluge a fast opposing creature-combo board is harder to stop.

## Oracle, legality and rules

**Source confirmed** via Oct16 existing interaction review (six groups) and Oct15 canonical full gate: unearthed creature is *not cast* and a Stifle-style ability counter can target unearth activation or the delayed exile trigger. If you **end the turn with exile trigger ALREADY ON STACK**, that delayed trigger is removed permanently; ending the turn before trigger would occur does not permanently save anything. **Obeka requires target player's consent** to actually end turn; if intended target declines, rescue fails. **Sundial** only activates on your own turn, needs 1+tap, and cannot answer a spell unless priority obtained and it is untapped. The unearth replacement for future exits persists even when the end-step trigger was removed; sacrificing with Ashnod's Altar/Goblin Bombardment means exile, not reusable graveyard loop. **Teferi's Veil** phases ATTACKING creatures at end of combat (after attacking/after damage); nonattacking creatures never phase out from this card. **Deadeye** blinks by exiling itself/paired creature then returning as new object, so unearth replacement does not stop the already-exile step; return is fresh permanent that no longer has the old unearth delayed exiling tracking. **Twilight Diviner** is one trigger per turn for one or more other creatures entering if entered or were cast from graveyard, creates one copy token; token is NOT unearthed and copied ETB triggers as appropriate. ([Twilight Diviner Oracle](https://mtg.wtf/card/ecl/122/Twilight-Diviner)). **Convergence** cannot reduce unearth B further because all remaining payment is colored; the archival attestation's discussion of Embalmer's Tools is irrelevant (Tools not in approved list).

**New targeted independent check:** Wizards' [Lost Caverns of Ixalan notes](https://media.wizards.com/2023/downloads/LCI_Release_Notes_kUj28nYwbydD/EN_MTGLCI_ReleaseNotes_20231107.pdf) confirm Enigma Jewel enters tapped, taps CC spend **only on activated abilities**, and craft front pays **{8}{U}** while exiling FOUR eligible nonland permanents and/or cards from battlefield/graveyard (tokens can pay eligible craft requirements but yield no exiled card abilities). Reversed Locus has copied activated abilities but each card-derived activation only once each turn, with expensive transformational setup. This is not generic ramp for casting six-mana Sedris; can pay unearth **generic portion** only, not B, and is near-blank if Convergence already reduced unearth to B. M5 P0 pass cannot establish upgrade. (Front craft displayed as {8}{U} in current Oracle sources, and legendary color identity U; source release-note version differs on craft cost—fresh Scryfall should be arbiter if challenged, but difference not strategic.) ([Wizards LCI release notes](https://media.wizards.com/2023/downloads/LCI_Release_Notes_kUj28nYwbydD/EN_MTGLCI_ReleaseNotes_20231107.pdf); [Enigma Jewel Oracle](https://www.mtg.wtf/card/lci/362a/The-Enigma-Jewel)).

**Combo correction vs seeded blueprint:** Deadeye+Drake creates repeatable Drake blinks and surplus land mana if five lands tapping for at least **three mana including U** so you can pay blink 1U and come out positive. To win with that *specific two-card* engine you still need an actual converter. **Terror of the Peaks in play** triggers on every returning Drake, causing repeated targeted damage equal to Drake's power, a real payoff if opponents do not disrupt it. **Goblin Bombardment alone is NOT a deterministic infinite-damage outlet merely because Drake+Deadeye yields infinite mana**: Drake blink does not create infinite separate creatures to sacrifice, sacrificing the paired Drake loses it (and if unearthed it exiles), so the one-time sacrifice damage does not repeat. Without Terror/other payable mana outlet, report **infinite mana/ETBs**, not an automatic win. This nuance is important in independent Pass C.

- **Phyrexian Delver** would be a strong fifth/sixth reanimation target because it returns another creature from grave when unearthed, but requires one another valuable high-end creature to be cut; possible **Sheoldred** substitute if repeat turns rarely occur, untested.

- **Embalmer's Tools** cannot further reduce the {B} left after Convergence, and would do nothing for the colored pip itself.
- **Gravebreaker Lamia** searches one card into yard, but its cost reduction concerns *spells cast* from graveyard and cannot reduce unearth activated cost; Corpse Connoisseur/Vile already do the setup.
- **Diluvian Primordial** and **Ancient Brass Dragon** make high game-state dependent payoff but Primordial/Archon have deterministic ETB; Dragon needs combat damage and unearth dies endstep.
- **Wonder** gives evasive team while in graveyard if you control Island, but this Sedris deck mainly wins via ETB effects and has few combat-connect conditions; would be much more natural in Sauron Army deck.
- **Hela, Mistress of Darkness** is future release in canonical 2026 project context, no special Sedris authorization. No unofficial exception.
- **Generic Breach/Oracle** turbo asks to use resource graveyard as escape/exile feed rather than Sedris unearth fuel, directly violates commander-first constraint.

## Conclusions and unresolved evidence

**Final authored decision `KEEP` exact 100.** Strongest P0 theoretical improvement `The Enigma Jewel` is sharply downgraded after current-Oracle restricted-mana analysis; cannot pay B on reduced unearth and steals graveyard bodies during expensive craft. P1 `Cathartic Reunion` is a one-use discard outlet that cannot replace the repeatable Remora's permission fuel in high-spell tables; P1 Fellwar/Relic/Big Score variants replacing Jeska's Will lack credible net acceleration evidence. Most plausible off-packet future Sedris improvement: **Phyrexian Delver** vs a delayed-upkeep high-end such as Sheoldred if real table recordings establish repeated end-step rescue unavailable—*unapproved*, not a recommendation to edit. Strong adversarial counterargument: lower curve/fewer expensive permanents makes Sedris earlier and more reliable. Rebuttal: cutting essential grave tutors and ETB payoffs for 2/3-mana generic value cards reduces the reason to play Sedris; the real tension is 31 lands vs ten rocks and must be evaluated with an accurate joint mana/activation cast model. No unsupported claim of exact overall win rate.

**Limits and further tests:** M5 paired tests 5 pass/5 fail/34 inconclusive/67 null are **not** actual Commander win probabilities and usually do not simulate unearth, graveyard permission, Obeka timing, multiplayer, interaction, opponent graveyards, or zero-to-six mana resource cost. This review performed **45 finite cost/timing/stack probes** and audited **111** paired hypotheses (including rows with no fully reproducible combo) using existing Oct15/16 Oracle receipts plus targeted official/current Enigma Jewel/Twilight checks. It **did not run a new full simulation or CI itself**, and does not claim a new October20 complete legality sync. A candidate whose exact card oracle/effect was unverified here remains **hold/no evidence**, not falsely mechanically disproved. Unsettled quantitative decision: whether 31 lands with 10 repeatable rocks gives adequate colored+six mana in the presence of taplands and interaction; follow-up requires real turn-level source-paid opening hands, not an automatic packet adoption.
