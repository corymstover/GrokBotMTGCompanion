## Commander identity and natural ceiling
Edward `{2}{U}{B}{R}` 5/5 legendary Human Assassin Pirate, two distinct payoffs: (1) at beginning of YOUR end step, make **ONE Treasure for each qualifying permanent you currently control that is TAPPED AND either Assassin, Pirate, or Vehicle**. A permanent with multiple listed types counts ONCE. Untapped uncrewed Vehicles are not counted, an artifact that ceased to exist before end step isn't counted, tapped Treasures themselves aren't any listed subtype. Crew a Vehicle with Assassins/Pirates before combat: crewed qualifying creatures get tapped; when Vehicle attacks it taps, counts too if it survives. (2) whenever controlled Vehicle deals combat DAMAGE to a PLAYER, look at that player's top library card, exile it face-down and **PLAY** for as long exiled: can play LAND if land plays available, unlike Ramirez's CAST; must still pay costs/use normal timing where applicable. Does not automatically steal face-up extra cards or cast them for free.
**Deck-specific identity:** Assassin's Creed IV Pirate captain leading Assassins/Pirates and commandeered ships; attack and crew generate tapped-state Treasure; Vehicle combat raids grant playable enemy cards; artifact output fuels Time Sieve and ongoing draw. **Natural ceiling Bracket 4 — Optimized**, tags suggested **Assassins / Pirates / Vehicles**, not an unrelated Grixis consultation deck. Has strong fast mana and several combos but depends on combat, creature density/crew, survivability and actual board interaction.
**Weaknesses:** five-mana three-color commander, creature and artifact wipes, early uncrewed Vehicle hands, creature crew can't also attack unless separately untapped, enemy blockers, five-artifact requirement each Sieve repetition, Fateful Discovery FORCED-draw deckout; primary combos can work without Edward, so deck must demonstrably center Edward's tapped-count and stolen-card axis rather than only support independent combo.

## Oracle, legality and rules
1. **Edward text/current Commander legality:** `{2}{U}{B}{R}`, 5/5, Assassin Pirate; tapped qualifying permanent counts **once regardless multiple types**, end-step trigger checks only those CURRENTLY present/tapped when resolves, not previously tapped creatures sacrificed/removed. Opponent Vehicle hit exile card may be **PLAYED**, including legal land play (remaining land drop, main-phase empty stack), for as long as exiled. Loss of Edward later does not undo the already created permission. Only VEHICLES connecting trigger second ability, attacking Pirates alone don't.
2. **Crew** taps any number creatures total power at least crew requirement; cannot tap creatures to BOTH crew and attack unless untap before declare attackers. CREWED vehicle becomes creature until EOT, can't attack unless controlled since turn began or haste. Vehicle becomes tapped by ATTACK, not by paying CREW itself: crewing taps the crew CREATURES, leaves Vehicle UNtapped until attacks/other tap effect. Tap more than required legally; Locomotive allows crew1 activated only ONCE each turn, but one payment can tap five or more creatures.
3. **Locomotive** on attack creates Treasure for EACH creature that actually crewed it THIS TURN; if five Pirates overcrew (assuming sufficient power), create five Treasures immediately on its attack trigger; they may be sacrificed for Time Sieve with appropriate timing, but need Sieve untapped. At end step if five Pirates still TAPPED and attacking Locomotive also tapped/present, Edward counts SIX qualifying permanents, not five, yielding SIX additional Treasure (or 12 with Throne duplicate). If one Pirate untapped/dies before end-step resolution, recount actual tapped board; Locomotive not eligible as attacker if just cast with summoning sickness.
4. **Time Sieve** actual mana cost `{U}{B}`; activated `{T}`, sacrifice FIVE artifacts to take extra turn. To *repeat* indefinitely it must remain on battlefield; sacrificing Sieve as one of five can buy ONE extra turn but destroys repeat engine. **RECTIFIED ENGINEERING BLUEPRINT ERROR:** its stated Urza's Saga tutor route is **FALSE**. Saga chapter III fetches artifact card with exact printed mana cost `{0}` or `{1}`, NOT mana value0/1, and Sieve `{U}{B}` is ineligible. Real library tutor routes are Imperial Seal, Vampiric Tutor, or other broad tutors if present (not Saga). Saga can fetch Sol Ring, Mana Vault, Lotus Petal, Chrome Mox, Mox Diamond, Mox Opal if their literal costs 0 or1, but sacrifices itself after Chapter III.
5. **Edward + Sieve extra-turn conditions:** At own end step keep Edward, untapped Sieve, FIVE qualifying tapped permanents. Edward creates five Treasure; after ability resolves tap Sieve and sacrifice five TREASURES (not Sieve), queue next turn. On next extra turn untap creatures/Sieve, tap/attack/crew five qualifying permanents again, reach end step for new five Treasures; repeats if opponents can't remove/disrupt bodies and library/upkeep risks don't cause other termination. Merely controlling five untapped Pirates does NOT make end-step tokens.
