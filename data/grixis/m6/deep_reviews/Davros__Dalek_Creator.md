## Commander identity and natural ceiling
Davros costs `{1}{U}{B}{R}`, legendary artifact creature — Alien Scientist, has menace. **At beginning of YOUR end step** it triggers, whether or not opponents have lost life. On resolution if **at least ONE opponent lost 3+ life this turn**, make ONE 3/3 black Dalek artifact creature token with menace. THEN every qualifying opponent faces choice `you draw a card` OR `that player discards a card`. No target; life loss count is individually tracked per player and **is not aggregate**. One opponent qualifying creates just ONE token, not one per opponent. An opponent at 2 life loss qualifies after an appropriately stacked Wound Reflection/Perforating trigger adds more loss before Davros resolution. Lost life can be from damage or direct life loss (not from their total net life change).
**Identity protected:** own-turn three-life qualification across all three opponents; the exact-choice tree and its discard punishments; Davros artifact trigger/choice multipliers; Dalek/Cyberman/menace army; real castable colored ramp and interaction. **Natural ceiling: Bracket 4 — Optimized** / Archidekt tags Daleks / Villainous Choice / Group Slug, not cEDH combo. Delayed engine and opponent choices limit natural throughput.
**Fail states:** own-turn failure to qualify opponents, lack of third colored pip despite two-colorless Ancient Tomb / Urza's Saga, Dave removed before end step, blank draw-vs-discard punishment, Dalks swept/creature removal, too many 5–6 mana theme payoffs in initial hand, other players willing to discard because no damage follow-through.

**Structural synthesis.** The 34 lands contain unusually strong three-color fixing with City of Brass, Mana Confluence, fetchable dual/shocks, Command Tower, Orchard, Plaza and a tapped typed triome. Their reliability offsets colorless Ancient Tomb/Geier Reach/Urza's Saga, but UBR casting must be tracked source-paid, not assumed from number of lands. Urza's Saga eventually sacrifices and cannot be treated as a persistent fourth land. Mana acceleration: Sol Ring, Mox Opal **conditional metalcraft**, Arcane Signet, Fellwar Stone, three Talismans, Ancient Tomb, Jeska's Will, Enthusiastic Mechanaut, Mana Drain next-main burst; curve top-heavy enough that replacing Fellwar or Opal with a 6MV black sorcery delays the commander in relevant hands.
**Card-access:** Davros qualified choices, Geth's Grimoire and Waste Not discard draws, Sygg's own condition, Court's monarch, Cult's random Caan, Demonic Tutor, Jeska's Will, Tinybones conditional and some Doctor Who engines. Not every card guarantees cards; opponents may choose discard rather than allow Davros draws, and Geth's Grimoire specifically closes that evasion.
**Interaction:** Counterspell, Mana Drain, Swan Song, Fierce Guardianship, Deflecting Swat, Deadly Rollick, Snuff Out, Delete, Cyber Conversion, Feed the Swarm, Cyclonic Rift, Vandalblast, Toxic Deluge, Blasphemous Act, Their Name Is Death, Exterminate!; broad protection without assuming 100% coverage of artifact sweepers. **Protection:** Padeem for artifact Davros, Greaves/Boots, Plaza of Heroes, free/paid counters. **Recursion:** Death in Heaven / Missy and other conditional Cyberman/artifact routes; no unrestricted recurring artifact-tutor loop. **Win paths:** repeated Dalek menace attacks with Raptor, group loss/discard via Entropic and Court and Wound Reflection, and late Torment of Hailfire choices.
**Most context-dependent occupants:** six-mana Wound Reflection is potentially slow but turns 2-loss end-step into 4 and doubles ongoing clock; Sycorax Commander and Weeping Angel weaker raw creature efficiency but unique Doctor Who synergies; high-X Doomsday Confluence and Genesis can clog opening hands but belong to Dalek identity; Entropic needs station crew time and high artifact infrastructure but is the principal 3-life punish. ## Oracle, legality and rules
1. **Davros trigger time/qualification:** Beginning of YOUR end step unconditional event; qualify by life lost *this turn* at trigger resolution. Opponent A lost 3, B lost2, C lost0: make ONE Dalek, ask only A to choose. If an independent source creates 2 more life loss to B while Davros awaits stack, then B also chooses if that event resolves first. A single Davros trigger never makes 3 Daleks from three qualified opponents.
2. **Choice vs discard:** If A chooses discard (has zero cards) it is instructed to discard but cannot; it does not automatically convert to "you draw". Davros gives the opponent the choice, not "unless discard". This is important for Grimoire/Waste Not: they only trigger when a card ACTUALLY gets discarded. Even with Battlecruiser 1+, an empty-handed opponent choosing discard may avoid that Battlecruiser life loss.
3. **Geth's Grimoire** gives draw per actual discarded card; it is a separate trigger and opponent can't prevent that by choosing discard over Davros draw if they actually discard. **Entropic Battlecruiser** has 1+ Station: whenever opponent discards a CARD, THEY lose 3; 8+ becomes relevant flying deathtouch 3/10 attacker with forced discard-or-lose3. It doesn't cause discard merely by being stationed; station setup uses creature activation at sorcery speed. Its trigger doesn't turn discard into damage; it causes direct life loss.
4. **Court of Ambition** on your upkeep: one opponent can take 3 life loss immediately or discard a card instead. With Battlecruiser 1+ and opponent HAS a card, either choice causes 3 opponent loss (discard then Battlecruiser). **If opponent has NO card and chooses discard, they might avoid both**, because Court uses "unless they discard"? This depends on exact Court wording: Oracle says "each opponent loses 3 life unless they discard a card" (see [Court](https://mtg.wtf/card/cmr/117/Court-of-Ambition)); if opponent unable to discard, the loss is NOT avoided; verify this exact wording before making no-cards claim. In ordinary nonempty-hand cases threshold holds. Court's upkeep happens BEFORE you can newly station Battlecruiser that turn, so it must ALREADY be stationed to support the choice branch.
5. **Geier Reach Sanitarium** costs `{2},{T}` to have every player draw then discard one. With active stationed Battlecruiser during your main phase, EACH opponent who discards 1 subsequently loses 3, qualifying even if only 0 earlier. With no Battlecruiser, discard is not life loss and doesn't qualify by itself.
6. **Wound Reflection** triggers beginning of EACH end step, independently of Davros. You own both; stack Davros BOTTOM and Wound TOP, Wound resolves first. Opponent lost 2 earlier this turn; Wound makes them lose 2 more (now total 4), then Davros checks and qualifies. If you stack Davros TOP and resolve at 2 they fail first, Wound later cannot change resolved Davros trigger. Wound *does not care when it entered* relative to original loss; checks all loss this turn.
7. **Perforating Artist** has Raid: if you attacked this turn, at beginning of your end step each opponent chooses sacrifice a nonland permanent, discard or lose 3. It is a triggered ability with condition checked at end step start, so **if not attacked, no trigger to order**. If you stack it above Davros and they choose lose 3, they qualify; if discard with Station1 Battlecruiser already active and discarded real card, triggers 3 life loss; sacrifice branch can leave them under threshold.
8. **Sygg** unlike unconditional Davros has an **intervening-if** trigger for whether an opponent already lost 3+ as the end step begins; qualifying only after Wound or Artist resolves in same end step does NOT retroactively make Sygg trigger if none qualified at beginning.
9. **Roaming Throne** enters naming Alien or Scientist (Davros has both creature types); its trigger multiplier creates two distinct Davros end-step triggers each potentially producing a Dalek/choice. **Scientist Supreme** copies Davros ability on stack by paying 2 life (your life, not opponent) on your turn once; **Strionic Resonator** `{2},{T}` copies an existing targeted triggered ability on stack. **The Valeyard** causes EACH villainous CHOICE an additional time with independent choices but not an additional Davros token; Auton Soldier is a nonlegendary artifact Davros copy and triggers itself; myriad Auton tokens are gone by end step without external retention.
10. **Unlicensed Disintegration** deals exact 3 only when legal creature target remains at resolution and artifact still controlled; it damages *that creature's controller*, not all three. If it targets indestructible legal creature damage still happens; illegal target means spell fizzles completely. Enemy damage prevention can stop qualification. Instant can be cast on turn other than our own but then NOT contribute to OUR-turn Davros check.
11. **Ingenious Artillerist** is on "one or more artifacts ENTER" not "cast artifact": simultaneous three artifacts -> 3 group damage in ONE trigger; separate one-at-a-time ETBs each yield 1 group damage (three ETBs total 3 damage). Four 3/3 Dalek tokens from multiple Davros triggers only enter during end-step RESOLUTION after those instances qualify; Artillerist ping can potentially affect LATER Davros trigger still on stack, but not already resolved one. It is a nonartifact Human and does not intrinsically grow Dalek army.
12. **Boltwave** `{R}` sorcery exactly 3 damage to each opponent; **Maestros Charm** `{U}{B}{R}` instant middle mode each opponent loses3 (no targeting), not damage. **Loyal Subordinate** combat-start 3 each with Davros controlled, does NOT require attacking. **Labyrinth Raptor** causes a creature with menace becoming blocked to force that defending player to sacrifice a creature blocking it; trigger may remove blockers but does not make it automatically unblocked. **Torment of Hailfire** isn't guaranteed 3 loss on any single repetition if opponents satisfy sacrifice/discard.
13. **Official legality:** October 14 SHA-bound 95-card check PASS, zero project exceptions. Focused outsider cards Unlicensed Disintegration and Ingenious Artillerist official Commander legal, in UBR identity, backed by linked Oracle. Unknown late-release candidate names from M5 remain unverified and not approved or automatically eligible. No invented current blanket legality scan.

## Finite sequencing and adversarial scenarios
**A — clean three-player threshold on own turn.** Our main phase `R` Boltwave, deal 3 to opponents A, B, C (not prevented). Davros on field by beginning of our end step: end-step trigger, make exactly ONE Dalek, then A/B/C each independently elect card draw for us OR discard a card. With Throne named Alien on battlefield before step, two Davros triggers, each making a Dalek and three separate player decisions; with Valeyard, each qualifying player's each Davros instance produces TWO choices but still one token per Davros instance.
**B — Court/Entropic conditional branch.** Active 1+ station from previous turn; Court triggers our upkeep, opponent A discards actual land card, Battlecruiser triggers and that player loses3; opponent B chooses 3 life loss outright; C discards actual card and takes3 from Battlecruiser. Davros end step all three qualify, even if no additional damage this turn. Without active Battlecruiser, A/C did NOT take life loss; do not count them automatically. Opponent can destroy Battlecruiser after Court choices before discard triggers resolve, but triggers already on stack still resolve independently when originating permanent gone.
**C — Sanctuary exact sequence.** Our second main with stationed Battlecruiser, have colorless+2 mana and untapped Geier Reach. Activate Sanitarium for `2,T`; everyone draws then discards one, opponents A/B/C each actually discard a card; three Battlecruiser triggers, each loses3, qualify Davros. If opponent has replacement/exile discard effect, whether a CARD discarded must be checked. No new draw engine assumed.
**D — end-step Wound stack.** Opponent A lost2, B lost1, C zero in our turn, Wound and Davros trigger simultaneously at beginning of our end step. Put Davros below Wound; resolve Wound first, A loses2 (total4), B loses1 (total2), C loses0. Davros creates one token, asks ONLY A; B still below3.
