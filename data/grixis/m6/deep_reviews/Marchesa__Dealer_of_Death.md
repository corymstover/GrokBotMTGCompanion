## Commander identity and natural ceiling

Marchesa, Dealer of Death is **{U}{B}{R} 3/4 Human Rogue**, and is **not** Marchesa, the Black Rose. She triggers **whenever YOU commit a crime**, allowing a payment of **{1}** to look at the top two cards of your library, **put one into hand, other into graveyard**. No per-turn cap. **This is NOT a card draw** for “whenever you draw” triggers. Crime occurs as you CAST a spell, ACTIVATE an ability or put an owned TRIGGERED ability on the stack targeting an opponent, opposing spell/ability/permanent or card in their graveyard; mere selection of an opponent without the word “target” is insufficient. The crime exists at initial target choice even if source is countered later or target changes; maximum one crime per SPELL/ABILITY even with four targets; storm copies not CAST don't individually create crimes. Marchesa's trigger goes above the target spell/ability and can resolve before it, provided {1} available and opponents don't remove/stop Marchesa in the prior window.

**What makes Marchesa different from normal turbo Grixis:** Every necessary interactive counterspell/bounce/removal targeting enemy material may be transformed into a **paid mini-Impulse + graveyard bin**, selecting fuel for `Underworld Breach`. She further has relatively unique recurring crime sources: `Spatial Binding` repeated one-life activations, `Hex Parasite` X=0 (2 life or B per activation), `Spellskite` ability targeting opponent-controlled spell/ability ON STACK, blue-spell `Lapis Lazuli Talisman` triggered target without even paying its optional untap, `Forbidden Orchard`'s opponent-targeted Treasure? **Spirit token**, not Treasure, plus original `Urborg`, `Tolaria`, `Hammerheim` legendary targeted land abilities and `Talon Gates` ETB. They are intentionally selected to add incremental crime triggers with minimal generic spell-slot cost.

**Win engines and ceiling:** Three compact legitimate packages: `Thassa's Oracle`+ `Tainted Pact` (no duplicate names; exile deck in response to ETB trigger); `Underworld Breach`+`Lion's Eye Diamond`+`Brain Freeze` (paid escape fuel/priority loops, storm finisher); and `Aphetto Alchemist`+`Hawkeye's Bow` (Bow's **nontargeting** damage to each opponent when equipped untapping Alchemist T-activates itself). Sources classify as **Bracket 5 — cEDH** with October tournament placement evidence, many zero-mana rocks/rituals/free counters, and premium card selection. Unlike some commander-first decks, Marchesa isn't *required* for those wins: commander identity is an efficiency/control/selection *engine*, not the combo outlet. In this exception the cEDH natural ceiling is real, but maximizing raw crime-themed cards at cost of live answer/combo windows **would** weaken her chosen tournament-tested commander-first strategy. Protect one-mana crime generator density, pilot skill sequencing and 29-land fast mana rather than forcing 5-mana crime payoffs or an unrelated tap engine.

**Real vulnerabilities:** 29 lands and many sources that fail to cast UBR on time (Tomb, Urza's Saga, Ancient Temple? NONE, Great Hall conditional, Gemstone Caverns luck, Mox Amber if no legend), possible commander removal before crimes can be monetized; free-counter resource tax (blue cards), graveyard exile breaking Breach but not Oracle/Bow lines, countering Thassa's Oracle trigger, `Cursed Totem` disabling Alchemist/Hex/Spellskite, `Rule of Law` disrupting Breach and protection on same turn; Ad Nauseam life total pressure. On the other hand, each noncompliance resistance cost must be considered vs practical real tournament cEDH threats.

## Oracle, legality and rules

**Authority/date distinction:** The **2026-10-15 canonical approval** passed full Commander legality, U/B/R identity, one-of-each count, zero project exceptions and the recorded rules/combo gate; Oct16 engineering Scryfall snapshot likewise reports compliance. **2026-10-12 Wizards Banned & Restricted notice** did not change Commander legality (its changes here were for **Competitive Brawl**, a distinct format); the next scheduled notice at the time is November30. On October19 publicly spot-verified the *specific* Marchesa Oracle/2024 crime rulings; Bow, Waterskin, Spatial Binding, Lapis, Talon Gates, Tezzeret, Wan, Valley, Magda, Ghost Vacuum, Scrabbling Claws, Glasses, Great Hall, Tolaria, Hammerheim, Urborg and current Commander status. No claim to have issued a **new live bulk 100-card** Scryfall audit in this engineering-only review, and every potential addition needs its own current printing-level official Commander legality check before approval. The named cards inspected are reported legal, and no unexpected current ban is raised.

1. **Crime timing and exact targets, not “spell resolves”:** Under crime CR **700.13**, committing a crime occurs while casting a spell, activating an ability, or putting a triggered ability **you control** on the stack that initially targets (i) an opponent, (ii) a permanent, spell or ability *controlled* by an opponent, or (iii) a CARD in an opponent's graveyard. Merely affecting opposing players globally or copying a spell without casting it does not trigger an additional crime. `Mana Drain` targeting opponent's spell is a crime; `Orcish Bowmasters` targeted pings are; `Hawkeye's Bow` deals 1 to each opponent but has **NO TARGET**, therefore its infinite-damage triggers are **not crimes**. Copy Enchantment spell does not target a source merely because it copies. Changing targets later does not undo a crime or turn a previously noncrime spell into a retroactive crime. A single spell or ability counts only once even targeting multiple opponents/permanents. Marchesa is 3/4 UBR and has **no once-per-turn cap**; each separate crime trigger may pay {1} on resolution to see TWO, choose one HAND, the other GY. This is not a “draw” action (CR121.5). Against one-card library, you put that one card into hand; against empty library Marchesa won't create another card.
2. **Targeting the same opposing object again:** `Spatial Binding {U}{B}` grants **“Pay 1 life: Until your next upkeep, target permanent can't phase out.”** You can legally activate it **repeatedly** on the same opposing permanent even if its effect already applies. Pay 1 life each activation; Marchesa separately needs {1} for each mini-Impulse. At 3 spare generic mana and sufficient life, three valid activations supply up to 3 hand selections and 3 cards to own GY (assuming library≥6), NOT six drawn cards or entirely free card flow. Marchesa need only exist when crime is committed; an already generated trigger survives creature removal unless countered.
3. **Hex Parasite and Spellskite exact payments:** Hex Parasite `{X}{B/P}: Remove up to X counters from target permanent` at X=0 **can still target** an opponent permanent with no counters and pay 2 life for B phyrexian instead of B; no actual counters need be present. Spellskite `{U/P}: Change a target of target spell or ability to this creature` can target an **opponent-controlled spell or ability on stack** for U or 2 life even if there is no legal target to redirect, so causes crime despite doing nothing on resolution. Spellskite cannot trigger crimes with an empty stack/no legal opposing spell or ability to *target*, and targeting your own spell/ability never commits a crime. Each Marchesa value trigger still costs {1}. These repeatable targeted abilities do not create inherently free infinite cards.
4. **Lapis Lazuli Talisman—important NO DOUBLE-DIP rule:** Artifact costs {2}; `Whenever a player casts a blue spell, you may pay {3}. If you do, untap target permanent.` This is a **triggered ability with a TARGET** selected when placed on stack, so choosing an opponent-controlled permanent commits a crime **before** optional {3} decision resolves. Declining {3} leaves the crime and Marchesa trigger intact. However **one Lapis trigger cannot both target an opponent's permanent for crime AND untap your own Bender's Waterskin**. If you target your own Waterskin to pay {3} and untap it, that trigger **isn't a crime**; if you target their permanent and pay {3}, it untaps **their** permanent. This distinction qualifies the Oct15 rationale that treats Lapis + Waterskin as a coupled crime/mana system: Waterskin's useful contribution is mostly its mana availability on each opponent turn to pay Marchesa {1}, or to pay {3} on a DIFFERENT self-targeting trigger. If no enemy permanent exists, Lapis can target an opposing land (permanent) for crime, even if untapping it is pointless.
5. **Forbidden Orchard and land abilities:** `Forbidden Orchard` mana ability generates mana and separately triggers "**target opponent creates a Spirit token**"; as that triggered ability goes on stack it commits crime and Marchesa offers {1} selection. Spend the Orchard's floating colored mana as colorless requirement if useful; the opponent eventually gets 1/1 Spirit token, NOT Treasure. `Hammerheim` tap-target creature loses landwalk and `Urborg` (actual old Legendary LAND, not `Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth`) tap-target creature loses first strike/swampwalk can target opposing creature WITHOUT those abilities: still crime but land is tapped in lieu of producing red or black. `Tolaria` taps to target creature losing banding, **only during an UPKEEP step** (not combat/main), so its crime potential is conditional; all three also tap for one colored mana instead if preferred. `Talon Gates of Madara` can enter from hand for {4} as special land ability and its ETB "**up to one target creature phases out**" can cause one crime if opposing creature is targeted; choosing zero doesn't. Phasing doesn't retrigger ETB on return.
6. **Magda, Tezzeret, Wan and Blue Spirit? NONE:** `Magda, the Hoardmaster` creates ONE **tapped** Treasure for the FIRST crime each turn, triggers once a turn even if multiple Marchesa crimes that turn and produces no mana from that token until untap (unless another untap effect). Tezzeret, Cruel Captain is {3} **colorless legendary planeswalker**: the 0 loyalty ability untaps **target artifact or creature** (if that target is opposing, crime), -3 searches artifact with MANA VALUE≤1 to HAND including Bow {R}, LED {0}, Petal {0}; `Urza's Saga` instead searches artifact with **printed mana cost exactly {0} OR {1}**, so **cannot** tutor Bow whose printed cost is {R}, despite Bow's MV1. Displacer Kitten blink of Tezzeret via a noncreature cast gives a NEW planeswalker object eligible to activate loyalty again but still only own main phase empty stack absent another permission. `Wan Shi Tong` {X}{U}{U} enters with X counters/draw floor(X/2), then gains counters/draw whenever an opponent searches THEIR library; not whenever YOU search theirs with Praetor's Grasp, and no target/crime on Wan's own search trigger. No “Blue Spirit” card is present; avoid unrelated Mai/Zuko cross-deck assumptions.
7. **Mox conditional and Great Hall:** `Mox Amber` makes colors among **colored** legendary creatures/planeswalkers you control: Marchesa in play UBR; `Magda` R, `Birgi` R, `Wan` U; Tezzeret is COLORLESS and cannot alone make colored mana through Amber. `Mox Opal` requires metalcraft 3 artifact permanents; `Mox Diamond` requires discard real land from hand on ETB or must be sacrificed (29-land build isn't guaranteed spare); `Chrome Mox` imprints a nonartifact nonland colored card; `Gemstone Caverns` only gets luck counter/colored pregame with specified beginning player/hand condition. `Great Hall` tap colorless at baseline; pay {1},tap gets TWO MANA any combination of colors **only to CAST legendary spells** (Marchesa/ Magda/ Birgi/ Wan/ Tezzeret), not Tainted Pact, Oracle, Marchesa crime {1} activation payment, or Waterskin abilities. `Spire of Industry` gives colored only while you control an artifact AND pay 1 life; by itself it's C, so 29 land proxies are not guaranteed UBR.
8. **Hawkeye's Bow + Aphetto payable infinite:** Bow {R}, equip {1} at **sorcery timing** on `Aphetto Alchemist` {1}{U} creature; Alchemist needs no summoning sickness or haste (has been under your control since your latest turn began), then tap it to activate its **{T}: Untap target artifact or creature**, targeting ITSELF. It taps as cost; Bow's **nontargeting** triggered ability deals **one damage to each opponent** and resolves before Alchemist's untap ability; Alchemist untaps upon resolving; repeat without additional mana to deal enough to all three opponents. Source: Commander Spellbook `https://commanderspellbook.com/combo/4091-7786/`; source Oct15 approved combo. It does **not** trigger Marchesa by self-targeting or by Bow damage, and opposing creature removal while the untap activation is on stack stops continued loop; protection needs proper priority. Tezzeret −3 can search Hawkeye Bow due MV1, Saga cannot due printed mana cost {R}.
9. **Oracle / Tainted Pact timing:** Have `Thassa's Oracle` {U}{U} cast and resolve so its **ETB triggered win check is on stack**; respond to the trigger with `Tainted Pact {1}{B}` exile cards from own library repeatedly, electing not to take a card. Since ALL 99 approved library names are singleton (zero basics), no two sequential cards ever have same name; after library empties, Oracle trigger checks library size 0 against devotion blue (normally at least Oracle's 2 if alive) and wins. Removing Oracle AFTER library emptied need not stop win because 0-card library condition can pass at devotion0; better interaction is counter **Oracle spell**, `Stifle` its triggered ability, or counter Pact before library empties; forcing a draw with no cards causes loss as state-based action before trigger resolves. Pact doesn't itself target, so Marchesa gets NO crime from it. A hypothetical deck adding duplicate basics would break unconditional full exile; this list has no basics and is singleton, so approved line is sound. Source Spellbook `https://commanderspellbook.com/combo/1295-3093/`.
10. **Breach / LED / Brain Freeze payment and caveat:** `Underworld Breach {1}{R}` enchantment gives nonland graveyard spells escape by paying original spell costs **plus exiling three OTHER GY cards for each cast**, and is sacrificed at next end step. `Lion's Eye Diamond {0}` enters as artifact spell, sac/discard entire hand to produce **three mana of one chosen color**; activate only with priority under LED rules, not while trying to pay for another spell as a mana ability. `Brain Freeze {1}{U}` mills 3 targeted cards and has storm; its original spell can be a crime ONLY if targeting opponent; to generate GY fuel it often targets SELF (no crime), while COPIES created by storm aren't cast and do not by themselves commit crimes. With enough initial GY and paid U to cast Brain Freeze, recurring LED + Brain Freeze via escape can grow storm and self mill/fund loop. **Merely milling opponents completely is NOT an immediate game win** until they must draw a card (or another effect wins); finish may require an eventual `Thassa's Oracle` or a library-loss sequence, an actually available spell/priority window, or the table conceding to deterministic lock. Do not assert three cards alone from any arbitrary empty GY win immediately. Graveyard exile, `Rule of Law`, `Drannith Magistrate` and `Counterbalance` are relevant response risks.
11. **Free counter/timing distinctions:** `Force of Negation` free exile+pitch condition specifically **on an opponent's turn**, unlike `Force of Will` pitch any turn; `Pact of Negation` is free now but {3}{U}{U} next upkeep or you lose; `Mindbreak Trap` free if an opponent cast 3+ spells this turn, exiles targets, does not protect against triggered/activated abilities; `Mental Misstep` only MV1, `Minor Misstep` only MV0/1. `Pyroblast` and `Red Elemental Blast` have differing legality of blue-target selection; track exact mode wording and cannot treat both as unconditional red counters. `Chain of Vapor` controller of bounced permanent may sacrifice a land to copy and choose new target, which can rebound onto Marchesa. `Borne Upon a Wind` and `Valley Floodcaller` grant noncreature spell flash timing but do not bypass mana costs or let **Oracle creature** be flashed by Floodcaller's noncreature permission. **B — productive Spatial Binding crime is 1 mana and 1 life PER selection, not zero.** Begin with Marchesa on battlefield, `Spatial Binding {U}{B}` active, one opposing permanent targetable and 3 untapped lands/mana sources able to produce {3}; enough library cards. Activate twice more, each time pay another life and another {1}, even if original opposing permanent already cannot phase out; result after three resolvable triggers is **three hand cards, three GY cards and 3 life lost**, no actual draw triggers and no need for Spatial effects to be useful. **C — Lapis/Bender trigger, with correction of misleading “untap itself + crime” assumption.** At player B's main phase, Marchesa, `Lapis Lazuli Talisman` and `Bender's Waterskin` are controlled by us; Waterskin is untapped from B's untap and holds one colored mana. Marchesa trigger goes above Lapis; tap Waterskin for one mana to pay Marchesa's {1}, look at two and select hand/GY. Lapis resolves: **decline {3}**, leaving opponent's permanent untouched. That is an entirely legitimate paid-one crime triggered by the opponent's spell. **If we choose our OWN Waterskin as Lapis target** instead, we can pay {3} to untap it on Lapis resolution but have not committed a crime, and this same trigger doesn't yield Marchesa cards. Opponent can cast no blue spells, kill Lapis or Marchesa, and shrink value drastically. Cut Lapis for Ghost Vacuum yields more guaranteed precision graveyard hate **when a card exists** but loses every-blue-spell crime reaction; neither dominates all pod conditions.

**D — timing of repeatable graveyard crimes versus real Breach prevention (Vacuum / Claws / Glasses).** Opponent has `Underworld Breach` on battlefield and `Lion's Eye Diamond` in GY, intending to cast it via escape.
