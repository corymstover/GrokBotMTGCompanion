## Commander identity and natural ceiling

**Norman Osborn front**: 2-mana {1}{U}, 1/1 unblockable Human Scientist Villain. When deals combat damage **to a player** connives: draw card, discard card, if nonland discarded +1/+1 counter. Sorcery-speed **{1}{U}{B}{R}** transform, *does not exile/reenter*, into **Green Goblin**: 3/3 flying menace UBR legendary Goblin Human Villain; graveyard-cast spells cost generic **{2} less**, and nonland cards in GY have **Mayhem equal to their own mana cost**, playable ONLY if they were discarded this turn and spell timing is legal. This deck's signature value is zero/cheap discard outlets into immediate graveyard casting, huge compressed spells and Breach-style graveyard transitions. Mayhem specifically **not** free infinite permission for cards that entered GY by mill/sacrifice on prior turns. Unblockable front offers reliable filtering before transforming; choosing to transform forfeits additional Norman-specific connive hits until recast.

**Power ceiling: Bracket 5 — cEDH** based on canonical approval's prior 121 documented competitive entries, exact 2026 deck profile and dense zero-mana acceleration, tutors, stack defense, Ad Nauseam, Oracle/Consultation/Pact and Underworld Breach/LED/Brain Freeze. A competitive optimization can use normal cEDH combos **when natural commander-specific Mayhem/discard engines remain protected**; pure generic goodstuff with three irrelevant card slots is not adequate. Commander-free win packages are permitted as convergent wins but should not become a reason to cut Goblin-specific engine. Distinguish actual 2026 metagame-dependent results from archived tournament count: **no fresh reproducible current win-rate simulation or event-based aggregate was done here**.

**Identity-specific tension:** Opponents may remove Norman in response to transform? Transformation is sorcery speed but uses an activated ability, opponent can respond; ability has no tapping cost. Once transformed, Mayhem works for each nonland card **discarded during that turn** while in graveyard and available under legal timing. Borne Upon a Wind/Valley Floodcaller enable sorcery Mayhem on opponents' turns, but those pieces use slots. Yawgmoth's Will graveyard-exile replacement blocks new discards entering GY on its turn; Necropotence rejected because discard-trigger attempts to exile them. The archive's compact Oracle/Consultation lines do not prove the commander is required for a win, and it must not claim so.

## Mana, win paths and realistic interactions

**Mana:** Raw October16 static profile: 23 lands, U/B/R nominal land sources 14/13/13, repeatable-inclusive U/B/R sources 21/20/20, six-plus colored zero-cost rocks conditional on imprint/metalcraft/legend presence/opponent lands. Source model's T2 two land-drop probability ~66.7% under no-mulligan sampling and T4 four lands ~23%, so meaningful fast Norman deployment requires cheap sources and careful mulligans. These are **not turn-two commander cast probability**. Normal live cEDH London mulligans and free resources make raw land-drop proxy a weak statistic. No effect-aware win rate inferred.

**Color facts:** Norman {1}{U} can be cast with Ancient Tomb+Island or Sol Ring+U. Transform {1}{U}{B}{R} cannot be paid solely with colorless from Mana Vault/Grim Monolith, or Cavern's chosen-color mana restricted to casting Human **creature spells**. Mox Amber while Norman is blue only makes U; transformed Goblin is UBR so Amber makes ONE color of choice among U,B,R. City of Traitors sacrifices when playing another land, creating potential mid-combo land loss. Lotus Petal/LED mana vanishes after use, no guaranteed next-turn resources. Gemstone Caverns' pregame exile is **not** discard for Mayhem.

**Win paths:** (a) Thassa's Oracle ETB plus Consultation (B) or Tainted Pact (1B) exiles library when configured with no duplicates; opponents can remove Oracle or counter trigger/spell, so sequence/stack management matters. (b) Underworld Breach + LED + Brain Freeze storm turns, escaping LED with exile three **other** graveyard cards each iteration, generating U/B/R resources, milling self/others; must manage graveyard fuel vs exile and spell/stack interaction. (c) Underworld Breach + Grinding Station + Mox Amber self-mills, mana requires front/back Norman or other legend, **not automatically a resource-free infinite from empty graveyard**. (d) Goblin Mayhem lines for Big Score, Dark Deal, Breakthrough, Wheel, Unstable Experiment accelerate selection/draw and feed endgame without necessarily being a standalone deterministic win. (e) Ad Nauseam as draw-to-combo, needs life and spot protection. Retaining these maintains real Bracket 5 while preserving identity.

**Decision:** ZERO unconditional material replacements. **HOLD entire approved list**. ## Oracle, legality and rules

1. **Commander two-name equivalence RESOLVED** from actual alternate-name printing source, no change to mechanical UBR card identity. Officially printed Goben/Fleem and Norman/Green Goblin are equivalent *Through the Omenpaths* and *Marvel Spider-Man* versions. Source evidence above; engineering status conflict tag is documentary and not proof of different Oracle.
2. **Norman is a transformable modal double-faced card under 2026 comprehensive rules**; the card flips on battlefield, it is **the same permanent**, NOT exiled and returned like Nicol Bolas the Ravager. +1/+1 counters and Auras on it continue through transformation subject to legality. The back face's mana value and colors differ (3/3 UBR); command zone/default cast uses front face 1U. New code must not infer MV0 or separate Green Goblin card in command zone.
3. **Mayhem**: A nonland card gets alternative mayhem cost equal to its **printed mana cost** while in graveyard. Eligible only if **discarded this turn**. Cast timing unchanged: instant can be cast in response, sorcery normally only main phase empty stack; Borne Upon a Wind/Valley Floodcaller extend spells' permissions. Mill/self-sacrifice or exile not discard. If discarded before transforming and still in GY that turn, once Goblin appears it may qualify.
4. **Goblin generic reduction** `{2}` for all spells actually **cast from graveyard**, including mayhem, escape and separate flashback, never colored pips or alternative costs stacked. Artist's Talent level2 reduces noncreature spells another {1} generic; Big Score `3R` → `1R` under Goblin or `R` with Talent, but its additional discard is mandatory. Dark Deal `2B` → B; Unstable Experiment `1U` → U.
5. **Necropotence** exile trigger happens after a discard, it can use GY while trigger on stack for permitted instant or flashes; cannot normally cast a Mayhem sorcery with trigger pending, and subsequent exile kills access. Yawgmoth's Will is stronger conflict for **new** cards: replacement effect exiles cards instead of going to GY. Prior old GY graveyard-access works until spell moves.
6. **Ghostly Pilferer** discards a card as activation COST (zero mana) to become unblockable; triggers mayhem. This is not normal loot draw; can't wait until after blocks and become unblocked.
7. **Artist's Talent** looting triggers on noncreature spell casts and discards before drawing by its Oracle. Even if discard happens during trigger resolution, cannot cast a mayhem sorcery mid-resolution; after full stack empties on own turn works.
8. **Lion's Eye Diamond** zero-cost with discard entire hand as cost and three mana **one chosen color**, usable at instant timing; cannot use mana while partially casting spell. Dropping hand gives Mayhem eligibility same turn only if Goblin in play/returned and card still GY, then can cast appropriate spell at legal time. Sacrifice/LED/Breach separately cost real exile fuel.
9. **Front/Back Mox Amber**: front Norman only BLUE from creature color; transformed Goblin UBR makes Amber produce ONE of U,B,R. Green Goblin's mana cost is its current face cost when on battlefield and contributes blue black red; never three mana.
10. **Tainted Pact** 100 name-singleton avoids repeated-name forced stop; consultation naming a missing card can exile deck if no cards named that remain, but if Thassa's Oracle is already in hand that card does not exist in remaining library. On ETB with empty library, Oracle can win provided trigger resolves and devotion condition evaluated, even with Oracle removed during resolution? Oracle trigger uses LKI devotion? Rules check; don't assume it automatically wins under removal.
11. **Underworld Breach** escaped spells pay normal mana plus exile three OTHER graveyard cards each casting; Green Goblin reduces generic for those casts but **Breach does not require Goblin**. Recasting LED feeds mana, Brain Freeze needs sufficient storm copies/target/self-mill/escape fuel and opponents cannot interrupt.
12. **Breach + Grinding Station + Mox Amber** station activation sacrifices Amber as cost mill 3, re-escape Amber for 0 and exile 3 GY cards, artifact ETB untap; can replenish GY mill equal to exile cards only under actual resources and need other legend for Amber U. Does not guarantee win from empty GY and blank opponent interaction.
13. **Cavern of Souls Human** colored mana protects *casting Norman creature spell* only. Transform is ability, not casting second face, no Cavern 'uncounterable Goblin spell'. Defense Grid taxes opponent answers on our turn but other players may use free spells that still owe 3.
14. **Gemstone Caverns pregame exile** is not discarding and cannot enable Mayhem. `Simian Spirit Guide` exiling itself also not a discard.
15. **Will of the Jeskai** commander-present as spell is CAST may choose both the wheel-to-five and instant/sorcery GY flashback until end of turn; this creates permissions distinct from Mayhem, and a flashback cast from GY receives Goblin generic discount but still exiles card instead of graveyard when leaves stack. Normal sorcery timing remains; don't call its modes instant-speed from Goblin alone. [Oracle](https://mtg.wtf/card/tdc/40/Will-of-the-Jeskai).
16. **Monument to Endurance** at most one of each of three modes can be chosen PER TURN; fourth discard trigger has no effect. Since a Big Score may trigger extra card discard on cast, draw/treasure modes can help once but don't yield infinite Treasure with LED alone. [Oracle](https://mtg.wtf/card/dft/237/Monument-to-Endurance).
17. **Yawgmoth's Will** lets cast graveyard but replacement exiles any card going to GY from anywhere for that turn, hampering additional Mayhem feed; remains valid separate high-power replay turn.
18. **Official legality and exception:** certified in source Oct15/16 Scryfall full-list and WotC banned list checkpoints; no special project ban exceptions here, no new 2026-10-19 exhaustive 100-card current legality scan claimed. Preserve approval. Unverified later Oracle/ban change must be reported before any approved modifications.
