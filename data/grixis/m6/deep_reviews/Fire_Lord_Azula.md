## Commander identity and natural ceiling
**Fire Lord Azula** is **`{1}{U}{B}{R}` (FOUR mana)**, 4/4 Human Noble, **firebending 2** (WHEN ATTACKS, make `{R}{R}` restricted UNTIL END OF COMBAT), and **when YOU CAST ANY SPELL WHILE Azula is ATTACKING, COPY that SPELL**, with new targets for copy. Copy of permanent spell becomes token on resolution. Copies created by this trigger are NOT cast, and do not themselves cause spell-cast triggers or storm count, though magecraft/copy triggers see them. She must legally ATTACK to switch on: must have controlled since turn began or haste. Once removed from combat or the combat phase ends no more Azula copying; her attack trigger can resolve even if opponent blocks, because damage is not required.
**Approved power ceiling:** **Bracket 5 — cEDH**, tags **Combat-Flash / Spell Copy / Combo**. Protected identity: Azula spell-copy DURING ATTACK, combat-only mana, access to sorceries via Quicken/Borne/Floodcaller/High Fae/Emergence, immediate haste via Arena/Expedite/Wisps, paid-card-cost reuse and commander-specific Narset's Reversal+Frantic Search and Breach+Frantic Search loops. Compact Oracle/Pact and LED/Breach/Brain Freeze are fallback noncommander finishes, not rationale for converting to same generic Grixis shell.
**Natural vulnerabilities:** aggressive four-color-symbol mana (1UBR), commander dies precombat to removal or can't attack through summoning sickness, 26 land/conditional 0-cost accelerants, firebending red cannot pay UU or B tutors, multiple interactions duplicated without multiple legal stack targets, Ad Nauseam life and draw losses, and cross-interference from Samut split-second protection with direct casts from own spells.

### Mana, resources, curve and control sufficiency
**26 distinct lands**; no basic (all fetches find nonbasic typed dual/shocks). Ten fetchland-like early access sources, City of Brass/Mana Confluence/Command Tower and conditional Starting Town. `Arena of Glory` only untapped on entry if **Mountain subtype already controlled** (Badlands/Steam Vents/Volcanic etc) and costs R to exert for RR; haste applies IF that mana cast commander, not from ordinary tap R. Exert delays next untap. `Cavern of Souls` name Human for uncounterable commander, but chosen-type colored mana only CAST creature of that type and cannot pay Beseech/Frantic Search or extra spells. `Emergence Zone` sacrifices LAND for one-turn flash, no additional land drop. Zero basic lands leave resilience to Blood Moon/Back to Basics poor; 26 land and sacrifice fetches add vulnerability.
Fast nonland mana: Chrome Mox, Diamond, Metalcraft Opal, Petal, LED (discard all hand), Mana Vault, Grim Monolith, Sol Ring, 3 Talismans, Arcane Signet, Fellwar Stone, rituals Dark/Cabal/Rite/Simian and Birgi. 4MV Azula may cast early with ideal draw but one red firebending trigger doesn't pay UU Reversal; double-blue and BBB Beseech are actual source requirements. **No sampled percent of turn-two/three Azula or simulated mulligan** claimed. Mana curve skew: 26 lands/74 nonlands; large 5MV Ad Nauseam/Wan Shi, 4MV High Fae, Big Score, Beseech, 3MV Rhystic/Necropotence etc. Many blue cheap interactions; protect the commander while attacking from kill/bounce/stax.
Cards and payoff: Remora, Rhystic, Necropotence, Ad Naus, Faerie, Wan Shi, wheels/loot/draw; copied `Big Score` consumes one extra-cost discard yet both draw2/make two treasure, `Demand Answers` one extra cost discard/sac ARTIFACT then each draws2; `Frantic Search` copies untap lands twice while combat mana remains until combat ends. `Snap` copies may have different legal targets and untap2 lands each; can't assume a target survives or counter fails. Interaction: free Fierce/Force/Pact/Trap/Misstep, one-mana Song/Serenade/Offer, Fluster, blue blasts, red blasts, Redirect, bounce, Stifle absent; few hard creature removal spells but creature tax/stax bounce is main way to clear problems.
Finish lines: attacking Azula+Narset's Reversal+Frantic Search conditional repeated spells and mana (Commander Spellbook source); Azula+Breach+Frantic Search finite-to-library repeated drawing/mana subject to grave fuel; Breach+LED+Brain Freeze regular; Oracle+Consultation/Tainted Pact compact. Core combat copy plus flash/hasty sources wins through interaction; not necessary to force an extra generic eight-mana artifact to justify B5.

## Oracle, legality and rules
1. **Official Azula (Wizards Avatar release notes)**: `{1}{U}{B}{R}`, firebending on ATTACK adds RR **until end of combat**; copy trigger while ATTACKING for **any SPELL** cast, including instant, sorcery, creature, enchantment and artifact with flash permission. Original **was CAST once**; copy is NOT cast. X and extra COST PAID to original are copied; but copies cannot pay a new additional cost. If target original spell gets countered BEFORE Azula trigger resolves, trigger can still create its spell copy using last-known details, unless forbidden by target structure. If Azula removed AFTER its trigger went on stack, ability still resolves. No automatic copied spell cast triggers.
2. **Haste/flash sequencing**: default summoning-sick Azula cannot attack on entry. `Crimson Wisps`/`Expedite` on Azula before declaration give haste+draw ONE, not two (Azula wasn't attacking on their cast). `Arena of Glory` {R},tap EXERT for RR can grant haste to creature spell that spent its mana, demands pre-existing Mountain for untapped entry; ordinary R tap does not give haste. `Quicken` grants next sorcery as flash, but need to cast Quicken before that sorcery in COMBAT to obtain copy. `High Fae Trickster` explicitly grants as-though-flash to spells, `Emergence Zone` sacrifice grants flash for whole turn, `Borne Upon a Wind` similarly limited to turn.
3. **Copy-dependent payout**: `Big Score` cast for 3R with discard additional cost once, Azula trigger copy and original each draw2 and create 2 TREASURE -> 4 cards and 4 Treasures, if both resolve. `Demand Answers` 1R discard card OR sac artifact as cost once, copy+original each draw2=4 cards with **zero innate Treasures**, never say Demand makes Treasure. `Frantic Search` original+copy each draw2 discard2 untap up to3 lands, but a targeted effect such as Snap may fizzle if all targets illegal, so can't guarantee lands untap.
4. **Bargain from Azula**: `Beseech the Mirror` {1}{B}{B}{B} bargain extra cost paid ORIGINAL -> copy also bargained, two potential searches and ≤4MV casts if timed in combat with flash and Azula attacking. Each resulting spell is CAST and may cause NEW Azula copies if while she remains attacking. However **if Samut, Tyrant of Naktamun is under your control**, each instant/sorcery including Beseech has SPLIT SECOND while on stack, which FORBIDS players from casting spells—even as instructed by Beseech DURING its own resolution. The tutor search/put into hand still occurs; bargained free-cast part is **blocked** on both original and copy while each is a split-second spell on stack. This is a material, previously unreported internal nonbo, not an official illegality issue. Relevant 702.61a and Wizards Samut ruling.
5. **Samut other lines**: `Samut` grants split second on instants/sorceries. `Azula` TRIGGERED ability still triggers and puts copy on stack; copy as created NOT CAST, allowed despite split second. However while Beseech still on stack you can't CAST new spells, so the protection also constrains combo responses until it resolves. The 2MV Samut strengthens blue counter wars but can interfere with casting from resolution effects such as Beseech/cascade; tradeoffs require owner if change.
6. **Narset's Reversal/Frantic Search**: Azula attacking, no summoning sickness, Frantic Search 2U in hand, Narset's Reversal UU in hand and at most 3 lands which can produce >=UUU. Declare attack, firebending RR to pay generic on Frantic; resolve copy Search draw2 discard2 untap up to3; then cast Reversal UU targeting ORIGINAL Search, Azula copies Reversal targeting ORIGINAL REVERSAL; copied Reversal copies ORIGINAL Reversal (targeting original Search) and returns original Reversal to hand; copied Reversal of Reversal copies Search and returns original Search to hand; Search copy loots and untaps land for another round. Under normal rules iterative repeated card draw must stop before decking unless win is identified; target can be disrupted, removal before spell cast or counter attacks stops line. Commander Spellbook corroborates. **Azula is essential to this precise line**, unlike backup Oracle.
7. **Breach+Frantic Search**: Underworld Breach already in play, Azula attacking, 2U Search cast from grave via ESCAPE requires three other GY cards (if from grave) and full mana. Each cast Azula doubles land untaps/draw-discard; discarded cards potentially replenish escape fodder and recursion, but grave hate or no three fodder stops. Commander Spellbook lists line; don't describe as unconditional infinite library because finite game.
8. **Breach/LED/Brain Freeze**: normal cEDH independent backup uses Breach escape of LED {0} (sac/discard hand to 3 colored mana) + Brain Freeze copies and self-mill to replenish three other grave exile cards per escape. Opposing grave hate and early resource count matter; copies not cast by storm, Azula's copy may enhance mills when attacking.
9. **Oracle + Consultation/Pact**: `Thassa's Oracle` UU creature entry triggers devotion/library check; respond before it resolves with B Demonic Consultation naming card absent or Tainted Pact exiling full 100 unique library. No basics duplicates; WotC legal check dated Oct14. Copies of Tainted Pact by Azula if she attacks are redundant if entire deck already exiled; careless cast may lose desired selected hand card before Oracle resolve, and copying Oracle as a nonlegendary spell? Oracle is not legendary so token copy is another creature with its own ETB if cast during combat at flash speed.
10. **Split-second interaction/adversarial**: Samut's split second protection DOES NOT prevent TRIGGERED copy abilities, including Azula and spells with magecraft/copy; it DOES prevent cast from Beseech resolution and opponent casting normal counters while split-second spell on stack. Once that Beseech finished resolution, players can cast before next on-stack object resolves. If an opposing removal spell was already on stack below your split-second protection, spell remains there and resumes after spell leaves.
11. **Target and redirect**: `Redirect Lightning` {R} as ADDITIONAL COST pay 5 life OR pay {2}, targets a SPELL OR ABILITY with **ONE TARGET** (not arbitrary spell with two), changes legal target when resolves, only if alternative legal. Azula copy inherits extra-cost status, but targets must be legal. `An Offer` counters noncreature only, `Strix Serenade` counters artifact/enchant/creature only, `Swan Song` counters instant/sorcery/enchant only—three spells with distinct roles, so don't count 3 unconditional U counters. `Force of Negation` free blue pitch ONLY if opponent's turn, not your own attacking combat.
12. **Spell timing and permanent copies**: While attacking, with flash cast `Mystic Remora` or `Rhystic Study` -> Azula creates spell copy resulting TOKEN enchantment. With `Thassa's Oracle` cast as creature with flash while Azula attacking, copy resolves as token Oracle and ETBs, then original enters later: two triggers with potential different current library count; Azula won't count resulting copies as extra CAST for storm.
13. **Legality**: official Commander full locked 100/100 gate PASS 2026-10-14, UBR, no exceptions. External candidates `Turnabout`, `Spellseeker`, `Storm-Kiln Artist`, `Dramatic Reversal`, `Lightning Greaves` are U/R/colorless with legal Commander text in referenced current card sources; a hypothetical modified 100-card list would need independent exact legality/Oracle approval before user swap.

## Finite sequencing and adversarial scenarios
**A—attack activation source.** Azula cast for 1UBR this turn without haste, controller has 3 untapped lands and Dark Ritual: cannot declare her attacker, cannot firebend, and casting Dark Ritual main phase does NOT copy. If `Expedite` before combat targets Azula paying R, gives haste and draws ONE, not two; she attacks and RR can then pay red/generic instants during attack. Enemy removal in response to Expedition prevents target from gaining haste.

In countercase preexisting Volcanic Island (Island Mountain), Arena enters UNTAPPED; pay {R},tap exert for RR, spend one RR toward Azula 1UBR (R slot), gives haste and can attack same turn. Remaining U,B and any needed generic must be actually paid from other sources. Exerted Arena misses next normal untap.
**C—Big Score one discard.** Azula attacks (haste or under control since turn started), two red firebending and genuine mana for Big Score 3R, discard ONE card as additional cost, put copy and original on stack; both resolve -> draw FOUR total cards and FOUR Treasure, not pay two discards and not eight Treasures. Opponent Counterspell on one copy stops that one's draw/Treasure, other survives.
**D—Demand Answers zero Treasure.** Same attack, cast Demand Answers 1R with one card discarded or one ARTIFACT sacrificed as additional cost, Azula trigger copies it; two spells draw FOUR cards total after resolving, make ZERO Treasure from their own text. If choose sacrifice rock already tapped, loss of future colored mana may slow follow-up.

Azula trigger creates copy carrying bargain payment status; when COPIED Beseech resolves, search permitted, but attempt to cast target <=4MV from library is not permitted while Beseech copy with split second ON STACK; copied Beseech then goes away. Original bargained Beseech resolves similarly; search yes, free cast NO. Without Samut (or if removed before the spell is cast), both tutor+free-cast permissions work and found spells cast while Azula still attacking are additionally copied by Azula. The later resolving tutor determines the FINAL top card (you do NOT draw two cards). ## Conclusions and unresolved evidence
**KEEP approved/locked 100 cards unchanged.** This is a genuine full-deck verdict, not initial M5 top-one HOLD. The tested material issues are:
1. **Samut/Beseech nonbo: MATERIAL** and rules-grounded. Should enter independent Pass C checklist because Samut can neuter bargained cast-portion of double Beseech while increasing protection of instant/sorcery elsewhere; this may drive a future user-facing `Samut→Spellseeker` or `Samut→Lightning Greaves` proposal, but present evidence lacks controlled net cEDH evaluation. Do not treat old gate PASS as exhaustive strategic certification.
2. **Turnabout** is a real redundant commander-dependent Narset Reversal land-untap combo over Repeal/Big Score, but Frantic Search already occupies cheaper looping role and provides actual looting, so not a proven upgrade.
3. **Dramatic Reversal** may capitalize on dense fast artifacts, but many opening hands lack enough artifact mana and cut costs matter.
4. **Storm-Kiln/Spellseeker** provide commander-specific synergy or cEDH tutors, but replacing Big Score / flash or stack protection has real losses.
5. **26 lands/no basics** is a deliberate cEDH turbo mana risk, not automatically illegal/underpowered; no mulligan-rate model justifies changing it.

**Strongest dissent from HOLD:** the Samut/Beseech contradiction is a concrete lost part of a high-impact copied tutor, rather than merely a theoretical value tradeoff. Counterpoint: without Samut, opponent 1U counter interaction can remove Azula's storm turn; Samut's split second may be more valuable on most OTHER casts, so a direct rules conflict does not prove net negative in matches. Recommend targeted scenario/meta-based comparison, then user approval if material improvement established.
