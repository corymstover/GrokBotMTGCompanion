## Commander identity and natural ceiling

Nekusar is {2}{U}{B}{R}, 2/4 Zombie Wizard. Beginning of EVERY player's draw step gives that player an EXTRA card; whenever an OPPONENT draws, Nekusar deals exactly 1 damage to that opponent. His draw trigger is separate from his damage trigger. Real draws matter; hand shuffles that do not draw are not damage. Strategy: wheels into opponent-draw punishment, stack Wizard/draw-trigger multipliers and keep protection for lethal wheels. Natural ceiling **Bracket 4 Optimized**: five-mana commander, high-cost payoffs and opponents refilled by wheels prevent claims of generic cEDH consistency. Win through wheels/Peer, Breach re-casting wheel with Jeska's Will, or optional Niv/Curiosity; no homogenizing into Oracle turbo.

Identity protection: prioritize true opponent draws, draw punishers, Nekusar Wizard triggers, exact-one-damage Ghyrson, cheap shields and self-life safety. A known internal hazard: Krang doubles your own Spiteful Visions/Phyrexian Tyranny draw-trigger penalties; careless wheels can kill their caster. `Ghyrson` needs exactly **one DAMAGE**, not loss of life; Solphim's former damage doubling conflicted with it.

## Whole-deck structural, curve, mana, protection and identity analysis

**Mana and exact colored constraints.** Approved 35-land mana evidence: **9 fetchlands; six basics (2 Island, 2 Mountain, 2 Swamp); 7 repeatable nonland mana sources, 8 fast-mana flags** (some are burst, not sustain), 1 ritual; conditional tapped lands 6 and unconditional tapped lands 2, utility lands 4. There are **eight spells with double-blue**, four double-red and three double-black by mana model, but **Niv-Mizzet, Parun needs UUU RRR** on one spell. Nekusar's own {2}{U}{B}{R} can be accelerated by Ancient Tomb, Sol Ring or Mana Vault only when separate UBR access exists. Underworld Dreams BBB, Peer 4BBB, Wheel RR, Niv 3U+3R pressure colored sources. **Source turn proxies are NOT cast success rates.** No-mulligan turn5 `all_three_land_source_presence` **0.805874**, strict five-land-drop proxy **0.425075**; turn4 strict four-land-drop proxy **0.592219**. Different denominators and assumptions; do NOT multiply to invent actual Nekusar cast probability. Fast mana and conditional turn-on sequencing matter. Recommendation: the mana base is fundamentally strong, but test color and life budget together; don't solve a five-mana-commander problem by swapping a cheap counter for a rock unless a real advantage survives opponent interaction.

**Win packages and costs.** About a dozen distinct wheel/large-draw effects; discard wheels feed **Waste Not, Liliana's Caress, Bloodchief Ascension** (when online) and also refill opponents. **Winds of Change, Molten Psyche, Echo of Eons, Teferi's Puzzle Box** shuffle or put hands elsewhere, so **do not discard** into Waste Not/Caress, and Echo shuffles own graveyard away, interfering with Breach setup. Reusable draw-step feeders (Kami, Anvil, Spiteful, Fevered Visions, Stormfist, Box) improve incremental inevitability but may accelerate opponents into interaction and threaten your life. **Wheel and Deal** is uniquely asymmetrical: each opponent discards/draws 7; you draw only one. **Wheel of Misfortune** lets opponents decline to wheel by selecting a minimum number, so it is not guaranteed 21 opposing draws. **Peer** kills one target only when target post-half-life plus other damage is covered; cannot assume it independently ends table.

**Protection & recovery.** Cheap responses include An Offer, Arcane Denial, Swan Song, Mana Drain, Force of Will, Force of Negation and conditional-free Fierce Guardianship; Swat protects a targeted spell/commander, Cyclonic Rift is a one-sided bounce reset, Chaos Warp broad permanent handling, Toxic Deluge sweeps attackers at life cost. This is significant interaction density for a five-mana commander. **Force of Negation** pitches free only on opponents' turns; it must be paid normally on controller's main-phase wheel. **Fierce Guardianship** is free only while controlling Nekusar; killing Nekusar in response can change the cost of a *subsequent* counter. An Offer gives an opponent two Treasures; Arcane Denial grants an opponent delayed draws that may hurt under Nekusar but supplies answers. Removing one cheap counter for generic mana may increase successful early commander deployments but reduce protected finishing opportunities.

**Recursion / inevitable finish.** `Underworld Breach + Wheel of Fortune + Jeska's Will` is a **real** three-piece wheel recursion if there are at least six other graveyard cards initially, six additional between own hand/grave, >=6 opposing hand and 2R available; two escaped spells each exile three *other* graveyard cards, Wheel's discard replenishes fuel and refills opponent hand. It can be stopped by graveyard exile in response, countering Breach/Will/Wheel, disrupting opposing hand before Will, or lethal self-damage. No `Lion's Eye Diamond` or `Brain Freeze` in the approved 100. `Curiosity` on Nekusar is excellent *card flow*, not self-referential infinite: your newly drawn card is YOUR draw, so Nekusar does not damage opponents from that draw. `Curiosity` on Niv is an **optional loop** of your draw -> Niv's 1 damage to opponent -> Curiosity may draw, stopped by choice/interaction/library count. The natural deck ceiling should be represented by these on-theme lines, not assumed alternative infinite combos.

**Self-damage red team / strategic priority.** If `Spiteful Visions` and `Phyrexian Tyranny` are out under your control alongside `Krang`, drawing **7** yourself from Wheel creates **14 Spiteful damage** plus **14 Tyranny pay-{2}-or-lose-2 triggers**; without payments that is **28 additional life loss**, total **42 life** in the absence of Ghyrson, Sheoldred, payments or other effects. **With Ghyrson Starn present**, each Spiteful Visions one-damage event to yourself also triggers Ghyrson to deal two additional damage **to you**. Krang creates two Spiteful damage triggers per draw, but does not double Ghyrson's damage-caused trigger. Seven own draws thus generate **42 Spiteful/Ghyrson self-damage plus up to 28 Phyrexian Tyranny life loss (70 total)** before payments, life gain or trigger sequencing. Exact Oracle: https://mtg.wtf/card/40k/124/Ghyrson-Starn-Kelermorph and https://mtg.wtf/card/c13/233/Spiteful-Visions . Do not mislabel Tyranny life loss as damage (Ghyrson doesn't affect it). On a 40-life starting point, these unresolved penalties are lethal, but triggers go on the stack and controller may order own lifegain and opponent punishment strategically; if the table dies first you can win before self-damage, provided opponents cannot respond. This is a **real cost, not automatic always-on loss**. Sheoldred's healing from own draws can counterbalance it, and paying {2} per Tyranny trigger when able helps. In aggressive pods, `Phyrexian Tyranny` may be a genuine weakest-slot target despite punisher redundancy.

**Counterarguments / weak slots:** Compared with premium wheel, counter and multipliers, the worst situational rates are `Fevered Visions` (four-mana delayed conditional two damage, symmetric cards), `Stormfist Crusader` (opponent benefits without immediate wheel), `Kami of the Crescent Moon` (help opponents T2 before punisher), `Magus of the Wheel` and `Jace's Archivist` (creatures wait through summoning sickness), `Reliquary Tower` (colorless source, only relevant with oversized hand), `Phyrexian Tyranny` (self-mutilation alongside Krang), `Consecrated Sphinx` (six mana, controller deck-out/self-punishment exposure). **Their strong case** is genuine commander-first draw density/backup through commander removal and inexpensive repeated access; none is categorically dead. Weakest-slot ordering is contingent on speed and opponent composition: against aggressive creatures, slow group-hug is lowest; against counterspells, protection is precious; against attrition, Sphinx/BMC-style value wins longer games.

**Anti-homogenization:** Keep Ghyrson/Krang/Staff/Prodigy and draw punishers ahead of copying generic Grixis control; restrict tutor/fast-mana swaps to proving earlier **protected** wheel lethal (not merely “land plus rock by turn four”). Theoretical `Power Artifact + Grim Monolith` colorless infinity or `Mind Over Matter` untap engines do not by themselves cause opposing draws and are not legitimate reasons to displace `Underworld Breach` until effect-aware comparative proof.

## Oracle, Commander legality, and rules review

**Verification provenance.** Canonical October 15, 2026 approval-ready source and the engineering October 16 Scryfall Oracle / October 15 rulings / September 25 Comprehensive Rules nine-interaction review were reopened. Wizards' official [TMNT release notes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/teenage-mutant-ninja-turtles-release-notes) confirm Krang's additional-instance rule; the [Ghyrson Oracle and release notes](https://media.wizards.com/2022/downloads/40K_Release_Notes/EN_40K%20Commander%20Release%20Notes_20220830.pdf) confirm it responds to exactly one damage by any other source you control to a permanent or **player**, including its controller. Day's Undoing Oracle [verified](https://mtg.wtf/card/cmm/85/Days-Undoing). No unexpected off-color card or official ban was identified in the approved October 15 full-list legality checkpoint; none of the authorized project exceptions apply to Nekusar. This is NOT a newly performed Scryfall Oracle re-query on all 100 cards today.

**Commander trigger timing.** Nekusar's start-of-draw-step ability triggers after the turn-based draw action. The turn-based draw itself cannot be replaced by the extra triggered draw: the player draws normally, then the extra trigger resolves separately. Opponent drawing a card for any reason triggers Nekusar's one-damage ability, including the usual draw and Nekusar's extra draw. Your draws are **not** damaged by Nekusar (but can trigger Spiteful Visions, Phyrexian Tyranny and Sheoldred).

**Wizard's Staff / Harmonic Prodigy / Krang.** Staff equips Zombie **Wizard** Nekusar for {1}. Staff and Prodigy each add one instance to BOTH Nekusar triggers. Krang adds one to draw-**caused** triggered abilities of your permanents only: it augments opponent-draw damage, NOT start-of-draw-step trigger. With all three, Nekusar's step trigger occurs **three** times, and each opponent actual drawn card causes **four** separate Nekusar damage triggers (base + Staff + Prodigy + Krang). An ordinary opponent draw step thus yields up to four actual draws (normal plus three extra) and sixteen Nekusar one-damage triggers (before Ghyrson), if nothing prevents/replaces a draw. This is additive, not doubling multipliers repeatedly; it is fragile to instant-speed removal of Nekusar, Staff/Prodigy or Krang before triggers exist.

**Ghyrson's self-damage is important and source risk was understated.** Ghyrson says whenever another source **you control** deals exactly one damage to a permanent or player, Ghyrson deals two more to the same object. You own and control Spiteful Visions, and it deals one damage **to you** for each own draw. Each instance therefore triggers Ghyrson for two more **to you**. With Krang but without Staff/Prodigy affecting Spiteful, there are two Spiteful 1-damage triggers per own draw and one Ghyrson 2-damage trigger per 1-damage event. Each own draw potentially totals **6 damage** from Spiteful+Ghyrson and **4 life loss** from doubled Phyrexian Tyranny if neither payment is made. Krang does NOT double Ghyrson's triggered ability: Ghyrson triggered because *damage* was dealt, not directly by drawing. There are separate intervening triggered abilities/priority windows; total for seven own draws may reach **42 damage + 28 life loss = 70 life lost**. With Sheoldred, own draws add life-gain triggers (which Krang also adds), but the order and whether life gain resolves before lethal damage matter; do not net life gain automatically. Without Krang, each own draw with Ghyrson/Visions/Tyranny yields 3 damage + potential 2 life loss, or **35** combined from seven cards.

**Damage vs life loss.** Nekusar, Fate Unraveler, Underworld Dreams and Spiteful each deal damage where the relevant Oracle says so; Ghyrson needs an exactly-one **damage event**, not a one-point life-loss decrement or a player **losing 2 life**. Liliana's Caress, Phyrexian Tyranny and Sheoldred's opponent-draw clause use life loss and are not enhanced by Ghyrson. Ghyrson's own extra two is not a recursive trigger because it is that same source and is not exactly one.

**Actual draws vs discards vs shuffles.** Wheel of Fortune, Windfall, Wheel and Deal, Reforge the Soul, Dark Deal, Burning Inquiry and Magus/Archivist cause DISCARD (condition/source count varies) followed by actual draws, so Caress and Waste Not trigger from opposing discards. Echo of Eons, Winds of Change, Molten Psyche and Teferi's Puzzle Box move/shuffle hands without discarding; they trigger draw damage, not Caress/Waste Not from hand movement. Echo specifically shuffles graveyard into library, weakening Breach setup. Molten Psyche's metalcraft damage counts all opponent cards drawn **that turn**, including before it resolves, if you control three artifacts at the relevant check. The source's original wheel categorization and Commander Spellbook are consistent with this distinction.

**Day's Undoing is a false positive for on-turn wheel damage.** It makes everyone draw seven, but if cast on YOUR turn, the same resolving spell ends the turn and exiles pending triggers from the stack / skips the time when draw-trigger punishers would be put on the stack. It is therefore a risky substitute for Consecrated Sphinx in this list despite M5 `pass` (unless cast on another player's turn through an additional permission, which is not the normal sorcery use). This was not a fresh candidate put into the canonical deck.

**Curiosity and Niv.** Curiosity enchants Nekusar: his damaging a player lets you draw optionally; your draw does NOT make Nekusar damage again, so not infinite. Enchant `Niv-Mizzet, Parun`: when you draw, Niv deals one to an opponent; Curiosity may draw; repeat until stop, opponent dead or deck exhausted. Opponents may respond to each trigger with removal or damage prevention. If you have Ghyrson, he can add two more damage to Niv's one-ping and speed kills but still doesn't create another Curiosity trigger from Ghyrson damage unless Curiosity was attached to Ghyrson rather than Niv; the loop's new draws arise from **enchanted Niv's** damage only.

**Peer into the Abyss.** Target opponent loses half life rounded up and draws half library rounded up; this can kill that player from simultaneous/staggered damage triggers, but it is NOT an unconditional all-three-opponent win, and large target-library size makes the finishing threshold important. Sheoldred life-loss and Orcish Bowmasters have their own specific draw-trigger restrictions.

**Breach + Wheel + Jeska's Will:** Underworld Breach grants Wheel and Will escape for original mana plus exiling **three other graveyard cards each**; Will uses the hand size of the targeted opponent for red mana. Wheels discard and draw seven to refill graveyard and the opponent's hand. This can sustain iteration when actual sufficient other cards/mana remain, and Nekusar/punisher deals opponent damage; it is *not* a mana-free loop. Opposing graveyard hate can stop future casts, spell counter can interrupt the chain, and your own Spiteful/Tyranny/Ghyrson effects can kill you mid-loop. No need to add LED/Brain Freeze to assert this conditional commander-native finish.

**Interaction constraints:** Force of Negation alternative free cast only during opponent's turn; Fierce Guardianship free only while controlling Nekusar; Deflecting Swat changes legal targets only and cannot redirect an overloaded no-target spell. Protection is essential, and none can be assumed to work after an opposing split-second response leaves no priority window. Toxic Deluge reduces your own Nekusar, Sheoldred and Ghyrson stats as well as enemy board.

## Finite, source-paid, adversarial scenarios

**Method:** These are newly authored **bounded deterministic rules traces**, not games run, Monte Carlo data, or effect-aware simulator results. All sources/choices are stated. They deliberately challenge (a) keeping the 100, (b) an apparent positive candidate, and (c) the strongest structural weakness. Table is four Commander players starting at 40 life unless a different life total is specified. Priority is available between discrete trigger resolutions; hand and mana resources matter.

**A — actual turn-three five-mana Nekusar, not the proxy.** Initial opening hand `Command Tower`, `Blood Crypt`, `Island`, `Sol Ring`, `Arcane Signet`, `Nekusar` in command zone and another spell. Turn1 play Command Tower, tap it for {C} to cast Sol Ring {1}. Turn2 play Blood Crypt untapped paying 2 life; tap Sol Ring for {C}{C}, cast Arcane Signet {2}. Turn3 play Island. Available: Sol Ring {C}{C}, Tower U, Blood Crypt B, Signet R; pay Nekusar {2}{U}{B}{R}, leaving Island untapped U. You can hold a `Swan Song` with Island if in hand; `An Offer You Can't Refuse` similarly. Without Blood Crypt untapped or Signet's red, raw five mana does not cast five-mana **UBR** Nekusar. A second colorless rock replacing Offer may accelerate raw mana but lose the only live protection.

**B — partial stacks when Nekusar dies.** Same board; opponent untaps, draws normal card, Nekusar's one-damage trigger created; opponent casts `Swords to Plowshares` on Nekusar in response. Even if Nekusar leaves, his existing damage trigger still deals 1 when it resolves. Nekusar's extra draw-step trigger already on stack still makes the opponent draw another card, but **no new Nekusar damage trigger** from that second draw if Nekusar is no longer on battlefield. Comparing Sphinx or a cheap wheel against a counter must include these lost future triggers, not just early hand sizes.

**C — Staff, Prodigy and Krang exact additive count.** Opponent starts draw step with Nekusar equipped with Wizard's Staff, Harmonic Prodigy and Krang controlled by Nekusar player. Normal turn-based draw triggers four Nekusar damage instances (base + Staff + Prodigy + Krang). Step-beginning triggers Nekusar extra-draw ability three times (base + Staff + Prodigy); if all resolve and remain on battlefield, opponent draws **four total cards** and each causes **four 1-damage triggers**: 16 Nekusar damage. With Ghyrson also controlled and surviving, each 1 event yields a Ghyrson 2 to same opponent: potential **48 damage** from four actual draws, before other engines. If opponent removes Nekusar before the extra draws resolve, the three existing draw-step triggers still draw cards, but new damage triggers stop. This is NOT “double four times” or “four more cards from Krang.”

**D — self-inflicted 70 from legal wheel.** You have `Krang`, `Spiteful Visions`, `Phyrexian Tyranny` and `Ghyrson`, at 40 life, opponents at 40. You cast `Wheel of Fortune` for **{2}{R}**, each player discards and draws seven. YOUR draws create **two Spiteful 1-damage triggers each** and two Tyranny triggers each. If they all resolve and you elect not to pay any of the 14 Tyranny {2} payments, every Spiteful damage triggers Ghyrson for 2 more to **you**. Seven cards × (2 Spiteful + 4 Ghyrson + 4 Tyranny life loss) = **70 total self-life reduction**. Opponents also draw 7 and have their own punishment triggers; as controller of those triggers, you can order stacks to try killing opponents **before** your own damage resolves. If all opponents die while you live, the game ends; do not assume a fixed damage ordering or automatic survival. If you only have 40 life and no healing, allowing the whole self-stack to resolve loses. `Sheoldred` adds your own +2-life draw triggers, Krang doubles them; stack order/priority and cards spent to pay Tyranny can change survival. `Ophidian Eye` replacing Tyranny removes its 28 own life-loss risk while retaining card access when attached to Nekusar, but introduces Aura fragility and removes a damaging opponent-draw punisher. **No universal improvement proven.**

**E — controlled discard versus hand shuffle.** Board Nekusar, Waste Not and Liliana's Caress, opponents' hands have 5, 6, 7 cards, respectively. Cast `Windfall` for {2}{U} while your hand has 3 cards; everyone discards, then draws **7** (maximum discarded among players). Opponents discard 5+6+7=**18** cards: Liliana's Caress causes 18 × 2 = **36 life lost distributed** according to each discard, and Waste Not triggers once per card by type. Each opponent then draws 7: Nekusar causes 7 damage to each opponent (subject to responses), 21 total opponent draws. `Winds of Change` for {R} with the same initial hands SHUFFLES cards away and re-draws old hand sizes, so opponents draw 5+6+7=18 total, causing 18 Nekusar pings, but **ZERO** Liliana's Caress or Waste Not triggers from those hand movements. If the controller needs discard-powered resources for Breach, Windfall is superior despite extra {2}. Opponents can respond to Windfall by killing Nekusar, diminishing damage but not the spell's card access.

**F — Timetwister versus Day's Undoing.** Board Nekusar and Ghyrson, six mana available, main phase. `Timetwister` costs {2}{U}: everyone shuffles hand/graveyard into library and draws seven. Assuming opponents each draw seven, Nekusar yields **7×3 = 21 damage to each** with Ghyrson if both survive until draw triggers resolve. `Day's Undoing` costs the same and draws seven but also **ends your turn during resolution**; the newly triggered Nekusar/Ghyrson effects do not get to resolve that turn. So an M5 favorable simulator evaluation for Undoing cannot substitute for actual damage mechanics. Timetwister is the credible contender, not Undoing, but Timetwister shuffles Breach fuel and may still leave opponents alive with seven new cards.

**G — first and second wheel with metalcraft.** You control Nekusar, Arcane Signet, Sol Ring and Chrome Mox (three artifacts). You cast `Winds of Change` for {R}; each opponent with 5 cards shuffles/redraws 5 and receives 5 Nekusar damage if Nekusar survives. Later that turn cast `Molten Psyche` for {1}{R}{R}, with opponents now each having 5. Each shuffles/redraws 5, causes 5 more Nekusar damage; with metalcraft still three artifacts, Molten Psyche also deals each opponent **10 damage**, counting 5 first wheel draws PLUS 5 later wheel draws this turn (plus any other drawn cards this turn). Total with two spells and Nekusar: **5+5+10=20 damage per opponent** if all draws, damage triggers and metalcraft survive. A `Bane of Progress` removing artifacts before Psyche will remove metalcraft even though ordinary spell draws still happen; `Vandalblast` also destroys mana artifacts and strips metalcraft.

**H — short and long wheel value, Sphinx and anti-decking.** You control Nekusar and Consecrated Sphinx, opponents each draw 7 during Wheel: **21 opponent card draws**; Sphinx independently offers up to **42 optional cards** drawn by you (2 per opponent draw), which can deck you if you blindly choose all or kill you under own draw punishers. You may decline many Sphinx triggers, and opposing removal can stop future draw events triggering it. A one-shot `Compulsive Research` would cost less but would not create this scale; `Alhammarret's Archive` doubles your own draws but does not give draw opportunities on opponents' cards. `Ophidian Eye` plus Nekusar gives up to 21 optional draws from Nekusar damage events, cheaper but commander-dependent and Aura removal-risk. These are real tradeoffs, not a simple Sphinx-is-expensive cut.


**I — Breach/Jeska's Will/Wheel paid recursion.** `Underworld Breach` battlefield, `Jeska's Will` and `Wheel of Fortune` in graveyard; at least six other grave cards plus another six among hand/grave, opponent holding six cards, and {2}{R} available. Escape Will for {2}{R} and three other grave cards, generate at least six red, then escape Wheel for {2}{R} (from those red) and three other grave cards. Wheel discards and redraws seven, refilling opponent hand and your graveyard; three red remain for the next Will. Repeat only while source resources persist. Hate on graveyard, counterspells, insufficient hand size or self-damage stops an alleged deterministic win. Source: https://commanderspellbook.com/combo/1368-1878-2706/ .

**J — Peer one-opponent kill.** Target A at 40 life with 52 cards in library using paid {4}{B}{B}{B}. `Peer into the Abyss` reduces A to 20 and draws 26; with Nekusar and Ghyrson surviving, 26 one-damage Nekusar triggers plus 26 Ghyrson two-damage triggers deal up to **78** additional damage to A. Opponents B/C aren't hit by Peer itself. If B controls `Notion Thief`, much of A's draw is replaced by B drawing instead, redirecting Nekusar punishment away from A; stack interaction can also stop Peer.

**K — protection versus generic acceleration.** Against an opponent `Underworld Breach` threat, `Bojuka Bog` exiles their graveyard on ETB but enters tapped, giving up early mana tempo. `Swan Song` can counter their Breach if U held open. Replacing `An Offer You Can't Refuse` with a Dimir Signet may improve some Nekusar openings but removes the one-mana counter available while tapping nearly out for a lethal wheel. `Fellwar Stone` also depends on opponents' colors. The proxy's `pass` for rocks does not demonstrate a better *protected* kill.

**L — Deluge at low life.** You are at 14 life with Nekusar 2/4, Ghyrson 3/2 and Krang plus Spiteful on board. Opponent has four 3/3 attacking tokens. Cast `Toxic Deluge` for {2}{B}, X=3 and pay 3 life: all 3/3 attackers and Ghyrson die, while Nekusar survives as 2/1 until end of turn. You are at 11 life and cannot safely cast self-damaging wheels without protection/healing, even though Deluge efficiently prevents 12 impending combat damage. Replacing Deluge with `Worldfire` destroys this survival line.

## Conclusions and unresolved evidence

**Decision: `KEEP` the exact approved 100 unchanged.** The approved Nekusar deck has 35 lands, 97 unique names, six repeating basic slots, proven UBR identity/Oct15 legality checkpoint, 9 fetchlands, efficient actual wheels, independently redundant draw punishers, commander-specific Wizard/caused-by-draw multipliers, cheap protected wheel interaction and source-verified conditional Breach/Will/Wheel plus Curiosity/Niv lines. It reasonably reaches **Bracket 4 — Optimized** on its own damage-wheel plan. No tested challenger proves a material net gain after properly modeling wheel-draw triggers, discard versus shuffle, Ghyrson exact-one damage, Krang self-damage and opposing responses.

**Most important new finding is a risk, not a proved card change:** Ghyrson **also** adds two damage to the caster whenever `Spiteful Visions` deals exactly one to that caster. With Krang, Spiteful and Tyranny, a wheel causing seven of your own draws can create **42 damage + up to 28 life loss = 70** absent life gain/payments/priority-based opponent kills. The historical interaction note explicitly flagged Tyranny and Spiteful through Krang but did not separately enumerate Ghyrson's self-damage. This is strategically material and should appear in independent Pass C as a challenge. It does not automatically imply that cutting a card is optimal: opponents can be killed first, Sheoldred can gain life, damage triggers may be answered, and Spiteful itself also increases opponent damage.

**Most credible prospective add/cut choices (not applied):** `Ophidian Eye → Phyrexian Tyranny` if frequent self-kills; `Timetwister → Fevered Visions` if slow group draws lose faster tables; a 2-mana colored rock → a slow group-hug effect, **not** An Offer, if protected turn-four Nekusar is measurably better. Side candidates: `Ophidian Eye` versus `Consecrated Sphinx` for curve, `Power Artifact` with Grim Monolith can produce unbounded colorless, but offers no direct opponent draw/damage and cutting Breach destroys an on-theme wheel loop. `Day's Undoing` is rejected for own-turn end-turn timing, despite its nominal favorable proxy. `Solphim` is not to be restored blindly, because it prevents Ghyrson from witnessing exactly one Nekusar damage.

**Strongest counterargument to KEEP:** Phyrexian Tyranny and Spiteful together with Krang/Ghyrson expose you to self-lethal wheels, and `Fevered Visions` may be an inefficient delayed group-hug slot. An additional cheap card-access Aura or wheel could raise the probability of winning before opponent interaction. **Strongest counterargument to swapping:** an Aura requires live Nekusar/Niv, reduces independent punishment density and is 2-for-1 vulnerable; removing slow group-feed weakens inevitability through commander removal; exchanging a counter for ramp makes a protected lethal turn harder. The current packet's 25 proxy `pass` verdicts lack real multiplayer effect modelling of those dependencies.

**Required future A/B evidence, not invented here:** mulligan-aware four-player simulations with legitimate UBR payment, actual active-player draw-step ordering, all draw and Ghyrson triggers, own life budget, appropriate counters and opponent responses. Test at minimum early turbo/combo, creature aggro, and attrition opponents. For each challenger compare **turn to protected Nekusar, paid wheels, opposing cards drawn, life remaining after wheels, commander removal/recast, number of actual finishers and true victory**, with full uncertainty. The existing M5 69 candidate hypotheses (25 pass, 25 no difference, 11 inconclusive, 8 fail; 54 combo flags) and 280 Oct17 EDHREC vectors are hypothesis resources, **not winning-game evidence**. Twelve authored cases A–L here are bounded rules reasoning, not played games. Current Oct19 complete Oracle/banned-card sweep **not performed**; formal baseline verification was Oct15/16. Unverified novel-candidate Oracle text must be checked before any approval proposal.
