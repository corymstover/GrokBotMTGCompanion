## Commander identity and natural ceiling

Lord is **1UBR, legendary 10/4 Zombie with {B}: regenerate** and an enters trigger that makes its controller **lose 2 life, sacrifice two creatures, and target opponent draw two cards**. Regeneration does not defeat the sacrifice requirement; absent two expendable *other* creatures, Lord can be one of the sacrifices. The 10-power body is the defining asymmetric advantage, and harsh self-inflicted ETB is the buildaround tax. A real Lord-first deck therefore (a) handles or profits from that ETB, (b) gets the commander through defenders and protects its vulnerability to 4 toughness and exile, (c) converts 10 power into near-immediate poison, repeat table poison, commander lethal, or mass damage, and (d) carries Zombie sacrifice recursion as a plan B. **Target bracket: 4 Optimized, not generic 5/cEDH**. Opponent blockers, cheap stack removal, taxed mana, slow setup for infect + haste/evasion, and cards needing multiple pieces limit the natural ceiling.

**Identity guardrails:** `Torpor Orb`, `Karn, Argent Defender`, `Dress Down`, `Stifle`, `Tale's End` are not interchangeable with generic counters; they specifically solve the Lord trigger. `Tainted Strike`, `Phyresis`, `Uncaged Fury`, `Kediss`, `Chandra's Ignition` specifically exploit 10 printed power. `Gravecrawler` is enabled by Lord's Zombie type. Avoid replacing that package with Thassa's Oracle + Consultation, Breach/LED storm, or excessive aristocrats engine padding. Correct 10-power threshold: unmodified Lord deals 20 with double strike, so **a +1-power effect is essential** to one-combat commander-damage lethal; `Uncaged Fury` supplies both.

### Deck-level structure

- **Mana:** 35 lands, eight inexpensive rocks (`Sol Ring`, `Arcane Signet`, `Dimir Signet`, `Rakdos Signet`, `Fellwar Stone`, three Talismans), and `Nightscape Familiar`. Lands contain five fetches, strong typed duals, at least two practically colorless utility spots (`High Market`, `Rogue's Passage`), a deliberately tapped `Bojuka Bog` and `Xander's Lounge`, and conditional multiplayer lands. Source `data/grixis/mana_base/lord-of-tresserhorn.json` counts 35 land / 8 nonland repeatable sources, color source potential B23/R17/U18 on lands, B29/R22/U23 including repeatable nonlands. **Counts are not cast probabilities.** That source's naive 11-card fourth-turn view reports B presence .954565, R .888913, U .903815, all-three-color *presence proxy* .798563 and strict fourth land drop .592219. The weaker *red* and *blue* pips on a UBR four-drop matter: don't cut `Talisman of Creativity` for a high-MV sacrifice body or claim four lands are almost guaranteed.
- **Card access:** `Demonic Tutor`, `Vampiric Tutor`, `Muddle the Mixture` transmute (MV precisely 2), `Mystic Remora`, `Undead Augur`, `Wilhelt`, `Dress Down`, and selective channel/cycle. Access is lower-density than generic optimized Grixis combo; that is intentional but remains an attrition weakness. `Muddle` does not find 1-MV `Tainted Strike`, 3-MV `Uncaged Fury` or 5-MV `Chandra's Ignition`.
- **Interaction and shield:** `Chaos Warp`, `Feed the Swarm`, `Terminate`, `Deadly Rollick`, `Sheoldred's Edict`, `Toxic Deluge`, `Blasphemous Act`, `Stifle`, `Tale's End`, `Muddle`, `Stubborn Denial`, `Deflecting Swat`, `Not of This World`, `Slip Out the Back`. Creature removal is adequate; true broad stack answers against forced board resets and graveyard hate are less numerous. Two sweepers can erase indispensable fodder before Lord's ETB.
- **Protected engine and conversion:** suppression 5; acceleration 8 conventional rocks + Familiar; evasion/haste in Regalia, Boots/Greaves, Shizo, Passage, Cover, Anger, Wonder, Arena; finishers Tainted Strike/Phyresis, Kediss, Fury, Ignition, Pain for All; sacrifice recursion via Gravecrawler/Skeleton/Bloodghast/Nether Traitor and 5+ outlets. Sub-package cards overlap enough to function beyond a single fragile combo.
- **Actual liabilities:** over-reliance on targeting own Lord (`Greaves` creates real self-shroud), needing two pre-ETB creatures if suppression absent, potential `Karn`/Orb anti-synergy with artifact or creature ETB triggers, colorless evasion land's four-mana tax, `Headless Rider`/Wilhelt taking turns to pay back, `Sheoldred's Edict` poor into token boards, `Cover` fear defeated by black/artifact blockers. No claim that these must all be cut.
- **Highest cut scrutiny, not automatic cuts:** `Cover of Darkness` (two-mana early evasion but partial), `Headless Rider` (3-mana delayed support), `Sheoldred's Edict` (flexible low-mana answer but sacrifice chosen by opponent), `Rogue's Passage` (colorless and 4-mana evasion), and `Wilhelt` (4-mana board engine). **Strongest retains:** five ETB suppression pieces; Tainted Strike/Phyresis/Kediss/Fury; Regalia; Signet/Talisman color fixers; direct removal `Feed the Swarm`; Gravecrawler/Altar/outlets; both tutors.

**Decision:** `Key to the City` → `Cover of Darkness` is the **one serious nonmandatory proposal**, dependent on playstyle and testing. This is not claimed to outperform baseline with proven probability. In creature-light tables with black/artifact blockers or repeated interaction, Key's truly unblockable line and discard enablers have meaningful marginal utility. In Zombie-wide games, Cover evades for all Zombies without consuming cards or tap, works even when Lord has shroud, and does not risk empty-hand activation failure. Therefore an unconditional yes would be overclaiming: mark `PROPOSE_SWAP_FOR_CORY_APPROVAL` as an **optional tactical upgrade**, preserving the approved list until explicit owner choice. The alternative cut `Headless Rider` costs another sacrifice source and creates inconsistent Lord-trigger fodder; `Sheoldred's Edict` costs unique interaction. Both inferior defaults to Cover as cut.

## Oracle, legality and rules

**Official vs project:** approved source October 14 verification PASS (100 singleton with basic land exception, UBR identity and official Commander legality); no project exception applies. Engineering Oracle bulk 2026-10-16 reported `compliant`; October 12 Wizards banned-and-restricted announcement changed other formats but **not Commander**. Targeted October 19 current Oracle/release research: `Karn, Argent Defender` {2} static prevents enters-caused abilities from triggering, officially Commander legal and released 2026-10-02; `Key to the City` {2} artifact activated tap+discard for unblockable and untap {2} optional card draw, Commander legal; `Nightscape Familiar` reduces blue/red spells by **one generic total** even if both blue and red, legal; `Pain for All` {2}R Aura trigger deals power as damage to another target and reflects damage to each opponent. See receipts.

**Precise mechanics:** (1) `Torpor Orb`/`Karn` suppress Lord ETB only if present at entry. `Dress Down` must resolve first; it has flash, but casting it **in response to Lord's existing triggered ability** does not undo that trigger. `Stifle`/`Tale's End` counter the existing ability while it's on the stack. (2) The ETB has a target opponent; the sacrificing and draw occur on resolution, with target constraints; two other creatures must exist then to save Lord if it is not suppressed. (3) If Lord has infect, 10 damage to a player produces 10 poison, so `Phyresis` combat is lethal on an unblocked hit. `Tainted Strike` makes power 11; its buff lasts this turn, no poison effect after the turn. (4) `Kediss` triggering after Lord combat damage makes **Lord** deal that much *noncombat* damage to each other opponent; while infect lasts, it produces poison, not commander-damage accumulation on those other players. Removal of Lord in response to Kediss can break the expected infect on resolution: if Lord is no longer on battlefield, use last known information about source, including whether it had infect, as appropriate; never assume all effects ignore removal windows. (5) `Chandra's Ignition` targets Lord *when cast*; if Lord absent as illegal target on resolution, no damage. With `Phyresis` 10 poison each opponent, with `Tainted Strike` 11. (6) `Uncaged Fury` +1/+1 double strike produces 11+11 commander combat damage against a single unblocked opponent if Lord survives both damage steps. No infect requirement. (7) `Key to the City` can tap the same turn it enters (noncreature artifact), discarded Anger/Wonder turn on only if relevant Mountain/Island controlled, unblockable must resolve **before blockers declared**, cannot retroactively unblock. Its card-draw trigger occurs as it untaps, placed on stack at beginning of upkeep, optional {2}; it does not draw immediately on discard. Greaves' shroud prevents targeting Lord with Key; `Cover of Darkness` itself does not target the commander. (8) `Rooftop Storm` alters Zombie casting costs, but not commander tax. Gravecrawler still requires another Zombie. (9) `Nightscape Familiar` reduces Lord from 1UBR to UBR, NOT UB or other impossible over-reduction; source generic one only.

**Nonbos:** Karn/Orb suppress ETB triggers including `Shambling Ghast`? Shambling Ghast's death trigger is *not* an ETB and works; `Jadar` end step also works; `Bitterblossom` upkeep works; `Bloodghast` landfall return still works since a **land** enters, not a creature. `Headless Rider` triggers on deaths, not entries. `Lightning Greaves` shroud blocks own `Tainted Strike`, `Chandra's Ignition`, `Uncaged Fury`, `Pain for All`, `Slip Out the Back`, and `Key` if added. `Dress Down` on Lord will make his printed regeneration unavailable until it wears off. `Toxic Deluge` -X/-X does not destroy; regeneration cannot save the 10/4 when enough toughness reduction applies.

## Finite sequencing and adversarial scenarios

1. **Fast clean combat line, specific costs:** with B/R/U + 1 generic available, `Torpor Orb` already deployed for 2, Lord costs 1UBR, equips `Brotherhood Regalia` for {1} since Lord is legendary (artifact was played previously for {2}), and plays `Tainted Strike` for B **before combat damage**. If Lord has haste from Anger/Boots/Arena or survived a turn, one unblocked combat deals 11 poison. Minimum spell/equip spending for this turn is **4+1+1=6 mana** if Regalia and Orb are predeployed; plus haste cost if not preenabled. Do not report turn-three poison without matching colors, setup and attack permissions.
2. **Kediss table spread, interactable:** control Lord, Kediss (1R), and Phyresis (1B) with one opponent available to receive combat damage. Lord deals 10 infect combat damage, first player loses to state-based poison; Kediss trigger deals 10 noncombat infect to remaining opponents if resolving intact. Blocking by an artifact/black creature under Cover, Teferi's Protection-style prevention or response removal can stop/limit spread. `Tainted Strike` alternative gives 11 for B, but spell can be countered before combat.
3. **No suppression, no fodder:** cast Lord for 1UBR onto empty creature board. On ETB Lord goes on stack, opponent gets chance to interact; absent suppression Lord sacrifices himself as part of satisfying its own two-sacrifice effect and opponent draws two. This is **not** a safe vanilla 4-mana 10/4. If two fodder tokens already available, sacrifice those and pay 2 life + opponent two cards; potentially profitable if haste+kill assembled, otherwise opponent net advantage remains.
4. **Warren Soultrader finite cost proof:** with a separately controlled Zombie, `Gravecrawler` in graveyard, `Warren Soultrader` and `Zulaport Cutthroat`, sacrifice Gravecrawler and pay 1 life to generate a Treasure. Zulaport drains every opponent 1 and gains controller 1. Spend Treasure for B to cast Gravecrawler; repeat each iteration with life net 0 and opponents -1. Each loop requires priority to resolve, triggers and creature still available; opponents can exile Gravecrawler in graveyard in response to casting (before cost paid), counter cast, remove Soultrader/outlet, or otherwise disrupt. With `Blood Artist` instead of Zulaport one chosen opponent loses 1 and you gain 1 each time, enough to work through opponents sequentially, not simultaneous table drain.
5. **Rooftop alternative finite cost:** Lord (a Zombie) + `Rooftop Storm` ({5}{U}) + `Gravecrawler` and `Goblin Bombardment` (1R). While Lord survives and the pieces are available, sacrifice Gravecrawler to Bombardment for 1 damage, then cast Gravecrawler for {0} from graveyard, repeat. It is a win only against players not protected from targeted damage / graveyard lock, not a guarantee at an arbitrary table. `Torpor Orb` does not turn off Gravecrawler's graveyard cast restriction or Bombardment activation.
6. **Key versus Cover, explicit blockers:** enemy controls a black creature and an artifact creature, both untapped. With Cover naming Zombie, fear is *not* sufficient against either. With Key {2} artifact untapped, a card in hand and Lord not shrouded, tap/discard to target Lord before declare blockers: now it cannot be blocked, even by those creatures. Conversely if enemy controls only red/green blockers and hand empty while Key is equipped? Key isn't Equipment and cannot activate with no card; Cover makes all your Zombies effectively unblocked, no cost. This is genuine environment-dependent evidence, not sample-measured performance.
7. **Key creates graveyard value but with timing:** hand holds `Anger`, battlefield Mountain, untapped Key. Before Lord combat, discard Anger as Key's activation cost targeting Lord; Anger's continuous haste ability immediately active from graveyard as soon as discard completed, and when ability resolves Lord becomes unblockable. If Lord is on field and just entered this turn, haste is ready prior to attackers. This requires Key to be under control, Lord to survive, and one Mountain; Key alone doesn't supply Anger's haste.
8. **Counterargument to proposed swap:** table with Lord in Lightning Greaves and `Cover of Darkness`, opponents controlling nonblack/nonartifact blockers and you have zero disposable cards in hand. Cover's non-targeting tribal fear makes Lord attack past blockers now. Key would require removing Greaves or can't target, AND a card to discard. This is the strongest concrete downside, and enough to make recommendation optional rather than mandatory.
9. **Torpor Orb / Tribute Mage failure:** Orb in play, Mage is cast for 2U as an ETB tutor, but its ETB ability never triggers; this exact deck can do that to itself by design. `Vampiric Tutor` for B works with Orb and Karn, finds any card, even missing infect/protection; a goldfish `pass` cannot invalidate this.
10. **Turn-four mana condition:** need one generic, U, B, R. Source no-mulligan 11-seen simulation gives only .592219 strict probability of four natural land drops and .798563 per-color presence proxy (ignores some restrictions and land-entry timing). These figures are separate marginals: **multiplying or equating them to cast probability is invalid**. Early rocks, tapped lands and fetch target choices change outcome; no turn-by-turn calibrated win rate claimed.
11. **Pain for All/Blasphemous Act:** Pain attached to Lord (3 mana already paid), Lord infected, Blasphemous Act deals 13 to Lord. Pain last trigger exists even if Lord suffers lethal damage, and may deal 13 infect to each opponent using last known information, absent prevention/counterplay. If a regeneration shield is active, Lord can remain; if an opponent removes Pain/Lord *before* Act resolves, the combo can fail. This is a risky multiple-piece five-plus mana line, not a baseline goldfish kill metric.
12. **Hate scenarios:** Rest in Peace effects shut down Gravecrawler recursion/Anger/Wonder; Ghostly Prison taxes Lord attack and Key unblockability **does not waive attack taxes**; Silent Arbiter-type restriction caps attackers; ward/hexproof target spell response can strand `Ignition` and `Phyresis`; board wipe on precombat strips haste/attack, and regeneration does not stop exile/toughness reductions. `Feed the Swarm` and `Chaos Warp` solve some, not all.
13. **Resource substitution:** removing one `Talisman of Creativity` for M5 `Spawnsire of Ulamog` cuts both red and blue fixing, raises curve eight, and makes early UBR cast less reliable; its separate M5 sim `fail` also does not model all effects, but commander-identity reasoning already rejects it.
14. **EDHREC association limits:** `Assault Strobe` very high Lord lift can reflect any 10-power commander and plays double strike, but 20 unbuffed commander damage is not an immediate one-hit win; `Cover` is less raw unconditional than `Key` in black/artifact meta. The decision uses actual combat arithmetic over inclusion rank.
