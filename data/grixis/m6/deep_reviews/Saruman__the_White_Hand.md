### Newly identified Oracle/documentation defects, kept separate from canonical status

1. Engineering `grixis/interactions/saruman-the-white-hand.md` calls `Blood for the Blood God!` **MV8**. The true Oracle printed mana cost is **{8}{B}{B}{R}, MV11**, so Saruman amasses ELEVEN on cast, NOT eight; it draws eight, deals eight damage and can reduce generic cost by number of creatures died. Independent live [mtg.wtf current Oracle](https://mtg.wtf/card/40k/108/Blood-for-the-Blood-God), [other independent catalog](https://mtgcardsearch.org/card/blood-for-the-blood-god). This is a **material existing source rules annotation error**, not a cardlist/hash/legality error. Do not count engine's 7 verified rows as all factually correct without this exception. Correct in this authored artifact; request later narrowly scoped engineering documentation fix if warranted, without rerunning pipeline.
2. Source `Saruman_the_White_Hand_Rules_Attestation.json` incorrectly says `The Black Gate` targets a creature AND player. Actual Oracle **targets only creature**, chooses eligible highest-life/tied player **at resolution**. [Wizards Tales of Middle-earth release notes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/the-lord-of-the-rings-tales-of-middle-earth-release-notes). Existing engineering interaction review flags this known documentary issue.
3. `Volcanic Salvo` reduction is based on **TOTAL POWER** of creatures you control WHEN casting (not highest single power, not damage). Saruman's amass12 trigger is put onto stack AFTER full cast; its counters cannot lower THAT same Salvo's locked cost. Fixed minimum RR. [Volcanic Salvo source with rulings](https://mtg.wtf/card/m21/172/Volcanic-Salvo). This sequencing limits overly optimistic early cheap-salvo claims.

## Commander identity, core lines and realistic ceiling

Saruman `{1}{U}{B}{R}` 2/5 Avatar Wizard. **Whenever you CAST a NONCREATURE spell, amass Orcs X where X is that spell's MANA VALUE ON STACK**; separately Goblins and Orcs you control have ward {2}. Commander identity = cast expensive-on-card, inexpensive-to-pay noncreature spells to build ONE very large Orc Army (or choose alternate Army), capitalize on actual counter-placement triggers and finish with Army damage/combat. MV and spells CAST not copies; real copies cast with Mizzix's Mastery trigger, spell copies placed on stack do NOT. This is not generic Grixis goodstuff. He costs four with 3 exact UBR pips and needs to survive the first high-MV action.

**Identity packages:** discounted high-MV cards Volcanic Salvo MV12 (existing creature total-power-dependent generic reduction), Blood for the Blood God! MV11 (death-dependent reduction), Blasphemous Act MV9 (creature-density reduction), Treasure Cruise/Dig Through Time MV8 (delve), Force of Will MV5 and Deadly Rollick MV4/Fierce/Swat MV3 under alternate free costs; X spells Assault on Osgiliath, Shatterskull Smashing, Summons of Saruman; amass-on-resolution layering Orcish Medicine, Foray, Rage, Swarming of Moria, Widespread Brutality; trigger-multipliers Harmonic Prodigy, Wizard's Staff, Veyran for instant/sorcery casts; Mauhúr adds ONE per placement; The Great Goblin and All Will Be One punish each placement. Protect/reroute an Army with The Ozolith, Orcish Medicine, The Black Gate evasion, Soul's Fire and Chandra's Ignition; wipe/Fall of Cair Andros rebuild.

**Natural power ceiling** Bracket **4 — Optimized**, with setup/commander dependency, potential 2-card damage finishes but NOT established tournament cEDH. High-cost discounted spells demand external creatures, graveyards, opposing board or deaths to truly be low-paid. Academy mono-shield game would not translate to guaranteed opponents failing responses; Army removal/exile/bounce/counter-of-cast also matters. We do not force Oracle/Consult/Breach infinite in every encyclopedia entry.

## Oracle/rules/legality — decision-relevant exact mechanics

1. Saruman only triggers for spell **CAST** that is **NONCREATURE**. Opponents' spells and copied spells created directly on stack do not trigger; Mizzix Mastery copies CARDS exiled then instructs CAST, and those CAST copies DO trigger.
2. MV reduction: Volcanic Salvo printed 10RR => 12 always on stack despite total power generic cost reduce; Blood for Blood God! printed 8BBR => **MV11**, not eight; Blasphemous Act 8R=>9; Cruise 7U=>8; Dig 6UU=>8; Force Will 3UU=>5; Deadly Rollick 3B=>4; Deflecting Swat 2R=>3; Fierce Guardianship 2U=>3, regardless alternate payment. `Mana Geyser` 3RR is MV5.
3. Volcanic Salvo cost calc uses creatures' aggregate power **before** Saruman cast trigger, cost locked, reduction only generic; still RR minimum. Can target UP TO two **distinct creature/planeswalker targets** only, not players and cannot target same creature twice to deal 12.
4. Blood for Blood God! cost reduce for EACH creature died this turn, generic min0, pay BBR, then discards hand/draw8/deals8 each opponent/exiles itself. Even if 8 die, total min BBR not free. Must cast while creature-death count is already known, not assuming its own ability kills creatures.
5. `X` spells: value of X contributes to MV **while on stack** for Assault/Summons/Shatterskull; if casting an X spell WITHOUT PAYING MANA COST (e.g. Summons bonus card), X=0, its own amass X or X-scaled effect may shrink.
6. `Harmonic Prodigy` and equipped `Wizard's Staff` trigger Saruman extra for NONCREATURE casts; Veyran triggers extra only when cast is instant/sorcery that triggers Saruman. Multiple are additive: Saruman+Prodigy+Staff+Veyran with instant MV5 → **4 distinct amass5 triggers** before source spell resolves; for enchantment/artifact noncreature casts only base+Prodigy+Staff =>3, Veyran not applicable.
7. `Mauhúr` adds one more +1/+1 counter per placement EVENT even if original puts twelve, not double; `All Will Be One` deals number of actual counters per event and `The Great Goblin` deals2 once per event, even if 12 counters.
8. `Bothersome Noisemaker` amasses Goblins1 separately on noncreature cast. These are additional counters on existing Army, which becomes Goblin and Orc, still one creature, not necessarily two Armies. Trigger order controlled for own simultaneous triggers to maximize source payoffs.
9. `Widespread Brutality` Saruman+4 trigger resolves before spell's amass Zombies2, then Army deals power to each NON-Army creature, so Army survives even if Saruman as non-Army creature dies. `Blasphemous Act` deals13 to Army and commander, both usually die, but Fall of Cair Andros will rebuild from excess on opponents.
10. `Soul's Fire` 2R source Army power after Saruman+3 resolves; **does not SACRIFICE**. `Kazuul's Fury` sacrifices Army as additional cast COST before Saruman triggers; no late growth from that same spell on sacrificed Army. `Chandra's Ignition` can use enormous Army as power source and hit every opponent but usually kills Saruman as a different creature.
11. `Fall of Cair Andros` triggers per opposing creature receiving *excess NONCOMBAT* damage; excess = dealt minus lethal threshold, not raw total13, after toughness/damage already marked. Works with Act/Widespread and some Ignition/Salvo, stack resolves after damage/SBAs; each resulting amass is separate counter-placement and can trigger Great Goblin and All Will Be One.
12. `The Black Gate` chooses eligible highest-life tied player when ability RESOLVES and **targets only creature**. It does not evade all three opponents automatically; protects one attack lane and may conflict when targeted player already dead/ life ranking changes.
13. `Ringsight` Ring tempts first, Ring-bearer chosen becomes legendary, then tutor card SHARING COLOR with any legendary creature controlled. With Saruman UBR on field effectively most colored spells, not colorless Sol Ring or colorless land; without a suitable legend tutor restricted.
14. `The Ozolith` obtains counters after Army leaves battlefield, supports transferring counters to attacking OWN creature at start combat; does not itself restore removed Army instantly or protect against `-X/-X`.
15. `MDFC` land BACKS played as lands do not trigger Saruman. Spell front CAST triggers; Kazuul's Fury additional sacrifice cost, Shatterskull's X contributes to MV, Sea Gate's 7 needs UUU and can be land when necessary, Sink into Stupor is cheap flexible interaction.
16. `Orcish Medicine` may grant indestructible for destroy/damage but not exile/bounce or -X/-X; trigger Saruman before its resolution. `Sauron Dark Lord` enormous creature backup not itself noncreature, so Saruman doesn't trigger by casting him.
17. All official Commander color/legality snapshot from Oct15/16 PASS UBR; Hobbit 2026 added cards also currently individually show Commander legal in external database. Project exceptions NONE, no alteration of canonical approvals. Any owner swap requires current all-100/Oracle/legality full recertification, not implied by this read-only review.
