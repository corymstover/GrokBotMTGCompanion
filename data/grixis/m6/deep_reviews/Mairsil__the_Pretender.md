## Commander identity and natural ceiling

**Mairsil's actual identity:** `Mairsil, the Pretender` {1}{U}{B}{R}, 4/4 Human Wizard. On each entry, may **exile one creature or artifact CARD from own hand or graveyard** with a **cage counter**. Mairsil continuously has activated abilities of all OWN caged cards in exile; each gained ability instance once each turn. The cage itself persists between zone changes; a blink/recast/new printed-copy Mairsil gets new ETB and fresh acquired instances. Unlike conventional reanimation, moving payloads from GY to BATTLEFIELD is often a waste: the desired zone is cage exile, not battle. A card that merely triggers or gives a static effect cannot be acquired through cage; support creatures/lands/enchantments remain useful **outside** cage.

**Game plan:** Accelerate to {1}UBR through 32 lands plus rocks, tutor/draw/discard costly cage payloads to GY, establish haste (Anger+Mountain, Lightning Greaves) or Thousand-Year Elixir for T abilities, keep Mairsil alive, cage draw-seven/untap/exile/Disc/ping effects, and use Quicksilver Elemental plus Gilded Lotus and a self-untap payload to refresh activated instances for repeatable mana, with Walking Ballista as clean colorless outlet. `Tree of Perdition` + `Hateflayer` is a commander-specific deterministic **one-opponent** kill (base 4 toughness/power), not a table-wide wipe, and must be repeated with fresh instances/objects/turns to beat a full multiplayer pod. Copy/reset via Pack Rat, Rings, Essence Flux, Displacer Kitten and Spark Double is crucial to avoid spending 6 or more mana on recasts.

**Protected core:** (1) GY seed `Entomb` / `Buried Alive` / `Unmarked Grave` / `Intuition` / `Faithless Looting`; (2) efficient cage payload `Gilded Lotus`, `Quicksilver Elemental`, `Horseshoe Crab`/`Morphling`/`Staff`, `Tree`/`Hateflayer`, `Asmodeus`, `Aetherling`; (3) haste/untap `Anger` /`Greaves` /`Elixir` /`Minamo`; (4) repeatable cage resets and copying; (5) low-cost stack protection; (6) mana reliable enough to cast UBR commander AND pay BBB/UU tap/draw in an interactive 3-opponent pod.

**Natural power ceiling:** source **Power Bracket 4 — Optimized**; multiple real lethal lines, tutor density and efficient protection, but must maintain Mairsil in play, access particular exile payloads, avoid GY replacement and execute more than one 1/opponent activation. Upgrading the mana base is intended to improve this natural strategy's realistic castability, not to force generic Thassa's Oracle/Consultation cEDH.

## Whole-list structural and weakest-slot analysis

**Mana sources:** 32 lands (3 basics, **3 fetchlands**, shock/original duals, 3 pathways, several enters-conditional check/battle lands, one guaranteed ETB tapped Xander's Lounge, and four notable utility lands Minamo/Otawara/Sokenzan/Takenuma); 14 repeatable nonland mana-source entries in engine Step-C and **13 “fast mana” card flags** (classification includes mana rocks, not all unconditionally online turn one); `Lotus Petal` is one-shot. Colored sources counted independently: lands **B17/U18/R18**, lands+repeatable nonlands **B26/U28/R27**; these count possible output not jointly payable, and some lands produce one fixed mode, Signets need input, Vault/Tomb lack colors, Petal single-use and Mox Diamond needs spare land. Raw colored pip demand of 59 pip equivalents **U28 (47.46%), B22 (37.29%), R9 (15.25%)**, with U/B double-pip payload activations and triple-black Asmodeus bottleneck; red least demanded after commander cast. The land package has **10 conditional ETB tapped sources** and 1 always-tapped source, so replacing a T1/T2 slow land with a fetch is a **real commander-access topic**, not cosmetic homogenization.

**Source-bound no-mulligan static proxies, NOT cast probabilities:** by turn four at eleven seen cards **76.5851%** all three land-colors present somewhere; **50.1949%** have four natural land drops (strict). They cannot be multiplied into an accurate Mairsil cast probability; neither jointly simulates tapped land timing, fetch target availability, mulligans, fast-mana imprint/discard or existing artifacts and Anger. 4-mana commander tax after removal means {3}{U}{B}{R} first recast, then {5}{U}{B}{R}. Three fetches/three basics leave room for 1–2 additional fetches without wiping every typed target; check duals already include Badlands/Underground Sea/Volcanic Island and Blood Crypt/Watery Grave/Steam Vents/Xander's Lounge.

**Actual setup clock:** T1 cheap rock/tutor with U/B/R, T2 Signet/Ramp plus Entomb/payload; T3 Mairsil possible with rock/Vault/Tomb and UBR, but an ETB-tapped Haunted Ridge/Shipwreck Marsh loses the spell; T4 Mairsil normally chooses ONE cage payload, cannot tap from summoning sickness without haste/Elixir; turn after establish shield plus another cage via Flux/Pack Rat/Kitten. Ramping more but deleting cage pieces or cheap interaction leaves a Grixis shell with no commander engine; *moving land tempo while holding 32 slots* is the natural untapped-speed challenge.

**Card economy and defense:** Rhystic, Remora, caged Arcanis draw-three and Asmodeus draw-seven, Frantic Search/Faithless Looting and tutors prevent attrition; caged Cephalid Inkshrouder gives shroud but prevents your own targeted flux/Minamo until end step, and Greaves shroud can similarly block self-blink while attached. Protection An Offer/Force/Guardianship/Swan Song, Swat, Aetherling blink, Endling undying, Disk+blinking and spot creature exile/kill (Shauku/Minion) are strong *while engine survives*. Exile graveyard **before ETB cage** prevents loading target, but caged exiled cards remain in exile even if Rest in Peace later appears: new milling/looting targets still get exiled instead of GY. `Drannith Magistrate` blocking command-zone casting, counterspells and `Imprisoned in the Moon`/ `Darksteel Mutation` stripping printed abilities are especially dangerous. `Toxic Deluge` wipes Mairsil without protection; Disk kills almost all your own artifact ramp, so combo with Aetherling and judge tempo.

## Oracle, legality and rules

**Independent identity baseline**: Current source-deck commander Mairsil {1}{U}{B}{R}, 4/4, has *ETB* to cage one artifact or creature card owned by you from hand or graveyard and a *static ability* granting activated abilities of your cards in exile with cage counters; **each gained activated ability can be activated only once each turn**. Recent current public Oracle confirms Mairsil Commander legal; source complete October 14 deck audit was 100 distinct singleton UBR legal, with no project exceptions; Oct16 engine registry independently records 100-card official compliance. **We have not rerun a live bulk Oracle+ban-list audit against every card today**. New proposed Marsh Flats/Misty Rainforest October 19 spot-check: each officially Commander legal, colorless identity and eligible typed-dual targets. Canonical state lock unchanged.

1. **Precisely what can enter cage:** the card exiled must be an **artifact or creature CARD** in your HAND or GRAVEYARD, not an instant/sorcery/enchantment or land. `Entomb` and `Unmarked Grave` place cards in GY then the next Mairsil ETB can cage, but `Unmarked Grave` searches only **nonlegendary** card (so legendary `Shauku`, `Arcanis`, `Asmodeus` NOT findable with it). In addition, once `Rest in Peace` is active, relevant put-into-graveyard effects send payloads to exile *without cage counters*, making them unusable with Mairsil; it doesn't retroactively remove caged exiled cards. Each Mairsil ETB creates one cage trigger; a tutor loading three from `Buried Alive` is three future options, NOT all caged instantly.
2. **Only activated abilities transfer; intrinsic costs/conditions remain:** `Asmodeus`'s **static Binding Contract replacement** doesn't transfer, so caged {B}{B}{B}: draw 7 is real seven cards, not exiled face-down draws. Its other linked {B} return ability doesn't retrieve Asmodeus-exiled cards on Mairsil because missing original linkage. `Shauku`'s original upkeep life loss/attack restriction does not transfer, but its caged T **exile a creature and place +1/+1 counter on Mairsil** does. `Minion of Leshrac`'s T destroy creature OR land transfers, while its original untap/upkeep penalty doesn't. `Venser`'s ETB bounce, `Hullbreaker Horror`'s cast-bounce and `Conjurer's Closet`'s beginning-end-step blink are **triggered**, so Mairsil does NOT gain those as caged abilities.
3. **Once per instance / per object / per turn:** merely untapping Mairsil with Minamo, Seal, Crab or Staff does **not** refresh a once-used individual granted ability. Caged `Quicksilver Elemental` grants `{U}: This creature gains all activated abilities of target creature until end of turn`; Mairsil must TARGET himself while legal, and gains **fresh instances** of all his current acquired abilities. QE itself can be copied again through a new instance, permitting looping. `Quicksilver Elemental`'s other blue-mana-as-any-color permission is a **static ability and is NOT acquired through cage**, so BBB Asmodeus still needs actual B. Mairsil ability use is independent per Mairsil object; `Essence Flux`, `Displacer Kitten`, Aetherling end-step return, and Spark Double as a separate permanent let new objects use fresh caged abilities.
4. **Marvin disagreement / provenance:** The October 14 approval verification explicitly stated Marvin could repeatedly use inherited ability without the one-use limit. This is not now accepted: engineering October 16 correction under **CR 113.10a** treats “you may activate each of those abilities only once each turn” as **activation instructions becoming PART of each granted ability**; Marvin gains each inherited ability and its limitation. Hence, with caged Gilded Lotus + Horseshoe Crab, **Marvin is NOT itself an infinite-mana engine**; it is an extra parallel once-per-turn tap/draw/removal source. The source interaction document mairsil-10 remains dated “uncertain” and should not silently overrule the later engineering correction. This is a **high-confidence CR-derived interpretation, not an official bespoke Wizards ruling**. Owner-approved deck still has Quicksilver Elemental and several proven loops, so no deck reconstruction was required.
5. **Haste, summoning sickness and own shroud are decisive:** Any caged `{T}` or `{Q}` activated ability needs Mairsil to have been controlled continuously since your turn began, or gain actual haste via `Anger` in GY + Mountain-typed land or `Lightning Greaves` equipped. `Thousand-Year Elixir` permits T/Q abilities immediately but does NOT let Mairsil attack (not real haste). Greaves gives shroud, **which prevents targeting Mairsil with your own Quicksilver Elemental self-target ability, Essence Flux, Minamo, Patriar's Seal and Displacer Kitten**, not just hostile removal. To run QE's self-refresh loop, MOVE Greaves onto another creature via equip 0 at sorcery speed or rely on Anger/Elixir; if shroud from caged Cephalid Inkshrouder is already active until EOT, you can't legally target Mairsil with Quicksilver self-copy that turn. `Spark Double` enters as copy *without targeting*, unaffected by Mairsil's shroud choice.
6. **Aetherling + Disk timing / commander exile choice:** After caged Disk `{1},{T}: destroy all artifacts, creatures, enchantments` activates, retain priority to pay {U} for caged Aetherling to exile Mairsil then return at beginning of next end step. The Disk activation survives source leaving and wipes all artifacts including your mana rocks, all creatures, all enchantments; only Mairsil is protected by his exile. **If you choose to move exiled Mairsil to command zone** on state-based action under Commander rule, the Aetherling delayed return doesn't bring it back; let it remain in exile until end step. If Mairsil is killed before Aetherling activated, no such defense. The returned commander is a NEW object with another cage trigger; haste must be restored for new tap/Q uses.
7. **Pack Rat + Rings / legend rule:** caged Pack Rat yields `{2}{B}, Discard a card: make a token copy of this creature`, where “this creature” is Mairsil. Discarded creature/artifact card is in GY as an ACTIVATION COST by time the token ETB cage trigger can resolve, so it can cage that discarded card if still there. Each token is legendary unless another effect changes that; state-based legend rule makes you keep ONE, but its ETB cage trigger still resolves even for a sacrificed legend copy. `Rings of Brighthearth` triggers from the original *nonmana* Pack Rat activation; pay {2} **when Rings triggered ability resolves**, get an additional token/cage trigger without paying a second discard. Not two persistent legendary Mairsils; retaining a token sacrifices original Mairsil and could change command-zone tax next recast. `Training Grounds` discounts Pack Rat's **generic activation cost** {2}{B} down to {B}, but cannot discount Rings' optional trigger payment {2}. A copied activated ability was not itself activated and doesn't consume the once-use allowance for the original granted ability.
8. **Rings + Aphetto Alchemist + Gilded Lotus standalone:** With external Alchemist that can tap, external untapped Lotus and Rings on battlefield, tap Lotus for three, tap Alchemist to untap Lotus, and pay Rings triggered {2} to copy the ALchemist nonmana untap targeting Alchemist itself. Resolve copy to untap Alchemist, then original untaps Lotus (target choices can be ordered correctly); tap Lotus repeatedly to generate net +1 colored mana per iteration after paying Rings 2. This doesn't require cage Mairsil, so it's a valid backup rather than central proof all combos need Mairsil. Rings does not copy Lotus's *mana* ability, and `Training Grounds` does not reduce the Rings triggered fee.
9. **Tree/Hateflayer one-opponent kill and copy layers:** On 4/4 Mairsil with **Tree of Perdition** and **Hateflayer** caged, target opponent with Tree's {T} exchange life total with Mairsil toughness: opponent goes to **4**, Mairsil toughness becomes that opponent's previous life (often 40). Then pay **{2}{R}**, activate Hateflayer's **{Q} untap** to deal damage equal to Mairsil **power 4** to opponent, reducing 4 to zero absent prevention/lifelink/life replacement. The {Q} ability needs haste / control since turn start. This kills **one opponent** per once-use pair without ability refresh, not automatically all players. If Mairsil's toughness exceeds power at swap, opponent may survive and need P/T-shift from caged Torchling/Endling/Morphling. If opponent removes Mairsil before exchange resolves, exchange fails; if target life total cannot change normally, don't assume 4 lethal.
10. **Staff/Quicksilver/Gilded Lotus infinite paid trace and outlets:** Cage Gilded Lotus (T: add three SAME-color mana), Horseshoe Crab (U: untap self), QE (U: copy target creature's activated abilities), establish haste/no shroud, Mairsil untapped. Tap acquired Lotus ability → UUU; spend U untap via Crab → UU remaining; spend U on QE targeting unshrouded Mairsil → U remaining, get fresh Lotus/Crab/QE instances; repeat net **at least +U** per cycle. The new QE instance keeps the engine going. Since caged QE's static permission to spend U as other colors did **not** transfer, choose actual B for caged Asmodeus BBB or use blue infinite on spells that accept U/colorless. `Walking Ballista` cast with very large X for mana can ping opponents (its damage ability removes physical counters); when only CAGED with no counters on Mairsil, its {remove counter}: ping is unusable unless counter added, e.g. Shauku/Endling/Spark Double copy. A high-mana pool is not itself a win if outlet isn't accessible.
11. **Existing few sources of rules risk:** `Eater of the Dead` caged zero-cost untaps only IF Mairsil is tapped when its ability resolves and there is a target creature CARD in a graveyard that can be exiled; legal targets and prior ability instance limit apply. `Omen Hawker` produces C+U **ONLY for paying ACTIVATED ability costs**, cannot pay spell costs or Rings triggered optional {2}; `Patriar's Seal` is {3} artifact whose external {1},{T} can untap legendary Mairsil. Caging Seal would give a tap-cost untap-self ability, meaning tapping Mairsil just to untap it, not a net tap engine; keep Seal on FIELD. `Wishclaw Talisman` as cage is ineffective because Mairsil has no wish counters and the ability must remove one as cost; cast the artifact instead and obey only-your-turn activation limit.

These are documented rather than recycled as fresh verified facts.

If before Tree resolves opponent exiles Mairsil, the exchange fails; if after exchange opponent gives protection/prevents damage or removes Mairsil before Hateflayer resolves, don't claim guaranteed lethal. Each acquired activation spent once; in a four-player game two remaining opponents are NOT automatically dead. `Spark Double` printed copy with +1/+1 makes 5/5, swaps opponent to **5** then 5 power damage if both abilities available—count current stats, don't assume always four.

**F — Pack Rat / Rings makes multiple ETB cage triggers, not permanent 3-Mairsil army.** Caged Pack Rat, `Rings of Brighthearth` external on field, Mairsil with enough mana. Activate Pack Rat cost **{2}{B} + discard a card** (or {B} under Training Grounds). Rings triggers, pay **{2} unrestricted** on resolution and copy it; copied activation creates a Mairsil token with its own ETB cage trigger; legend rule immediately forces only one legendary Mairsil kept. Original ability then resolves creating ANOTHER legendary token and cage trigger, with another legend choice. Both triggers can cage one artifact/creature each from hand/GY when resolving; the card discarded as cost is a valid candidate if it remained in GY. **Neither token remains alongside the original without a nonlegendary-changing effect**, and keeping token over original may send original card to command zone. If an opponent exiles discarded payload from GY before its trigger, cannot cage that one; choose a different valid card or no card.

**G — actual distinction between Null Rod and Cursed Totem.** With `Null Rod` or `Stony Silence` on board, artifacts like **external** Sol Ring, Rings, Wishclaw, Patriar's Seal and Marvin (which is an ARTIFACT CREATURE) cannot activate. However original Mairsil is a **nonartifact creature**, and abilities it got from cards caged in EXILE now belong to that creature: `Null Rod` does **NOT** suppress caged Gilded Lotus or Eater of the Dead abilities on Mairsil. In contrast `Cursed Totem` prohibits activated abilities of **creatures**, including mana abilities and all caged Mairsil activations; `Linvala, Keeper of Silence` is similar when controlled by an opponent. This is a major commander-first hate-resilience distinction and a reason generic artifact-combo goldfish data cannot represent actual table success. `Chaos Warp`, counters and `Cyclonic Rift` protect against the lock; `Deadly Rollick` answers creature Linvala/Ouphe but **not** Cursed Totem. Removing Mairsil's entire cage engine with Totem is far more crippling than making the external rocks dormant.

**H — blink speed comparison: Ghostly Flicker vs Essence Flux.** You control Mairsil and the opponent casts targeted exile at it while you have **one U untapped**: baseline `Essence Flux {U}` can blink Mairsil, dodging target and immediately providing next cage trigger/new once-use instances. Challenger `Ghostly Flicker {2}{U}` in the same hand cannot be paid; no protection. In a more developed state with **three available mana**, Mairsil and `Gilded Lotus` external on field, Ghostly Flicker can exile/return both Mairsil and Lotus: Lotus reenters **untapped**, Mairsil triggers another cage, and previously spent Lotus T may be reused. That's a real higher-ceiling play but at extra 2 generic and lacking flexible one-U shielding. Cutting Ballista to get this line sacrifices victory when unlimited U is produced. Thus evidence isn't enough to adopt the P1 “pass” as a guaranteed improvement.

**I — asymmetric interaction and GY-hate target.** Opponent turn two `Rest in Peace`: GY-seed `Entomb`/looting stops feeding Mairsil and `Anger` cannot stay in GY, but Mairsil can still **cage from hand directly**, and previously caged exile cards continue to confer abilities if Mairsil remains on battlefield. Another opponent destroys external `Rings` / `Patriar's Seal`, but creature Mairsil retains already caged *artifact activated abilities* in exile; no need to actually cast those artifacts. Opponent plays `Cursed Totem` instead: Mairsil's entire acquired activation plan is OFF and the best immediate answer is `Chaos Warp` or a counter if held. New fetchlands improve speed of holding U/R/B interaction but don't add extra counterspells; must preserve `An Offer`, `Force`, `Fierce Guardianship`, `Swan Song`.

**J — colored activation vs generic fast-mana delusion.** A hand of `Ancient Tomb`, `Mana Vault`, `Lotus Petal`, `Mox Diamond`, `Omen Hawker` plus one land and payload may have “lots of mana” on paper, but Mairsil UBR still requires actual U/B/R; Diamond sacrifices itself without land discard; Hawker makes restricted mana usable **only for activated abilities**, not casting Mairsil; Vault/Tomb are colorless; Petal is one-shot. `Asmodeus` drawn from seven via caged BBB demands actual B regardless of caged Quicksilver's static mana reallocation not transferring. That is why the mana audit's **0.765851 all-three-color presence proxy** is not cast probability and why an untapped typed-dual fetch is materially commander-specific.
