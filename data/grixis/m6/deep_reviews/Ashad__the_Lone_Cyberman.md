## Commander identity and natural ceiling

Ashad, the Lone Cyberman is a Grixis Legendary ARTIFACT creature. The FIRST **nonlegendary artifact spell you cast each turn** has **casualty 2**. Optional additional sacrifice of power≥2 creature AS CAST generates a COPY of that spell; for artifact permanent spells, each resolved spell copy becomes an artifact TOKEN, not another cast. On ANY other creature sacrificed, Ashad gains a +1/+1 counter. Priority isn't generic artifact combo alone; it is to make one or more copyable artifact spells EACH PLAYER TURN via Shimmer Myr, satisfy 2-power fodder cheaply, and convert copied artifact tokens into attack power, ETB advantage, sacrificial mana, ping/drain or recursive loops.

Natural ceiling is approximately **Bracket 4 optimized**, conditional on 30-land/fast-artifact mana, copy target availability, commander survival and artifact hate. Artifact spells/cheap treasures can explode and Urza/Worldwalker Helm offers genuine high-ceiling combo, but a 4–5 mana commander with 2+ casualty and first nonlegendary artifact constraint isn't automatically top-tier cEDH. Single artifact removal/wipe can have devastating scale because rocks, tokens, engines and commander overlap in artifact type.

**2+ power fodder acquisition:** Poetic Ingenuity's artifact-cast trigger at most once per turn creates 3/1 Dinosaur, valuable next cast/turn but **cannot retroactively be sacrificed to casualty of the artifact spell that created it**. Biotransference on artifact casts yields 2/2 Necron tokens (not once per turn) at life cost; Solemn Simulacrum 2/2 is both eligible fodder and excellent copy target; Foundry Inspector, Enthusiastic Mechanaut, Thought Monitor, Wurmcoil, Urza Constructs (power artifact-count dependent), Zombie Cybermen and Brudiclad tokens add redundancy; Thopter Spy Network makes 1/1s which need growth for casualty. Cyberdrive Awakener temporarily animates noncreature artifacts into base 4/4s, opening surprising fodder at cost of losing valuable mana/engines if sacrificed. Losing 2+ creature BEFORE your first nonlegendary artifact spell stops commander copier even with Twinning Staff on board.

**Copy payoff:** Casting Ichor Wellspring as your first nonlegendary artifact spell with independent power-2 fodder gives original and one token copy, EACH enters to draw 1, and each can later be sacrificed to KCI to draw from its graveyard trigger (token does enter graveyard from battlefield before disappearing). With Twinning Staff there are TWO token spell-copies plus original = three Wellsprings. Solemn Simulacrum similarly yields duplicate basic-land searches and death draws, but tapped basics and finite available basics matter. Copy Wurmcoil to get multiple 6/6 lifelink/deathtouch and paired 3/3 artifact Wurms when die. Copy a nine-mana Portal to Phyrexia to get independent opponent sacrifice-three ETBs and upkeep reanimations; huge ceiling with large cost/timing. Copies of artifacts are not CAST; Jhoira historic draw, Poetic's token trigger and Ashad's first-spell gate do not recur from copy resolutions.

**Death/recursion/drain:** KCI sacrifices ARTIFACT for CC; Ashnod's Altar sacrifices CREATURE for CC; Phyrexian Altar sacrifices CREATURE for ONE any color. None interchangeable when noncreature artifact or colored mana is required. Disciple of the Vault sees artifacts put from battlefield into grave, Reckless Fireweaver sees artifact ENTER, Ingenious Artillerist counts batch entrances, Marionette Master sees your artifacts go grave and drains based on its POWER, Mayhem Devil sees any permanent sacrifice. Scrap Trawler returns **lower-MV ARTIFACT CARD** to hand when artifact you control dies; cannot return artifact tokens that cease existing. Emry recasts an artifact from graveyard, Welder exchanges artifact on BF for artifact in grave, Academy Ruins places grave artifact on top. Exile-based mass removal/graveyard hate bypasses these pathways.

**Engine protection/interaction:** Padeem grants own artifacts hexproof (includes artifact commander), Greaves protects equipped creature at shroud cost, free Fierce/Swat, Mana Drain, Arcane Denial, Chaos Warp/Cyclonic Rift/Delete/Cyber Conversion offer response. There's substantial weakness to artifact-wide effects e.g Vandalblast, collector stax stopping activated artifact mana and torpor effects stopping ETBs, plus 30-land opening risk. Countering Ashad or removing fodder before spell payment shuts down copy engine; after casualty paid, destroying Ashad or sacrificed creature doesn't retroactively cancel copy trigger.

**Rules tests and necessary interaction distinctions:**
1. **Ashad** grants casualty 2 to the **first nonlegendary artifact spell you CAST on EACH TURN**, not a legendary artifact, artifact land played as land, or nonartifact spell. Its own legendary card doesn't qualify. Biotransference makes NONlegendary creature spells also artifacts, expanding legal set, but doesn't remove legendary supertype.
2. Casualty sacrifices a CREATURE of power ≥2 as additional CAST cost; copied artifact spell permanent resolves as TOKEN. One sacrifice per casualty (not infinite), only one first eligible artifact gets grant that turn; opponent cannot respond between paying cost and having cast.
3. Twinning Staff FIRST ability is general SPELL-copy replacement, works on ARTIFACT PERMANENT spell copies. If Ashad copies it once, Staff produces TWO copies instead. The ACTIVATED 7-tap ability is limited to instant/sorcery only; don't conflate.
4. **Poetic Ingenuity** triggers only once per turn on artifact CAST (whether creature or not), creates a 3/1 Dinosaur but not until AFTER that spell was cast and casualty already paid, so can't pay same spell casualty. **Biotransference** creates 2/2 Necrons from artifact CAST on each eligible cast, but still after cast, and loses 1 life each.
5. **Ichor Wellspring** draws on artifact ETB and on its becoming put into graveyard from BF; token copied Wellspring *does* hit grave when sacrificed before ceasing to exist and can trigger draw. If exiled/bounced, no grave trigger. No 2-power body on Wellspring itself.
6. **Solemn** first cast with casualty needs a prior ≥2-power body; copying creates separate 2/2 token with ETB basic land search; two/three bodies ETB means that many tapped basics if available. On death tokens draw but cannot themselves return as graveyard CARDS to Scrap Trawler/Emry.
7. **Iron Man, Bleeding Edge** copies artifact spell once per turn and makes copy nonlegendary; Iron Man himself is LEGENDARY and not target of Ashad casualty unless loses legendary. Copy effects and Twinning Staff can increase nonlegendary token artifacts on future casts; original cost paid, copies are not casts.
8. **Cybermen Squadron** makes nonlegendary ARTIFACT CREATURES myriad; attack copies are tokens tapped and attacking other opponents and exiled end combat. They weren't cast and no Ashad casualty on token creation. Ashad itself is legendary, Squadron's "nonlegendary" condition doesn't grant Ashad myriad.
9. **KCI vs Altars**: KCI accepts ANY ARTIFACT for 2C, Ashnod's accepts creature for 2C, Phyrexian accepts creature for one colored. Ashad grows only for sacrificing ANOTHER creature, NOT an Ichor Wellspring, Map, Treasure or artifact land. Disciple sees artifact going grave, Marionette only YOUR artifact grave events, Mayhem Devil every sacrifice; Reckless Fireweaver on artifact ENTRY.
10. **Worldwalker Helm** replacement creates extra Map token when creating one+ artifact token, with two Helm effects each contributes another Map (replacement interactions tracked, no automatic self-contained infinite without original token copier and mana). Helm activated {1}{U}+tap copies ARTIFACT TOKEN (not nontoken original). At least one token Helm required to self-copy.
11. **Urza + Helm + Artificer Class**: Artificer Class must be LEVEL THREE, its start-of-end-step trigger target ORIGINAL Worldwalker Helm and then create token Helm plus Map. Original Helm and Map can be tapped by Urza's ability to provide UU; pay 1U + tap the **new untapped token Helm** (or original if still untapped) to copy a token Helm. Two+ replacement effects give additional Maps. Future newly created token Helms supply untapped activation sources; Urza taps 2 Map tokens to pay 1U each repeat, surplus Map taps generate U mana. Result: arbitrarily many untapped artifact tokens and blue mana, then Urza {5} repeatedly to exile/play library. **Costs and response windows:** Artificer levels (1U then 5U at sorcery timing) fully paid prior, begin endstep trigger must legally resolve, Urza and Helm must survive, first token must exist, and original/available Helm for activations. This is a conditional three-card loop, not 'automatically infinite from all three resolving once' without level/timing.
12. **Three Steps Ahead** replacement combo setup: With Urza + Helm on BF, cast Three Steps Ahead by paying **{3}{U}** using copy mode targeting original Helm. On resolution create token Helm, then Helm replacement adds Map. As above, tapping artifacts via Urza and activating token Helm repeats; no Artificer Class or end-step wait. Opponents can counter instant/remove Urza/Helm before tokens enter. Not Ashad casualty-eligible (instant).
13. **Their Name Is Death** destroys NONARTIFACT creatures, spares artifact Ashad/most army but also opponent artifact creatures and any of your nonartifact token fodder; Biotransference turns all your creatures artifact, protects your nonartifact creatures but can be destroyed/exiled itself. Artifact creatures becoming 4/4 via Cyberdrive still suffer summoning sickness based on how long permanent has been under your control.
14. **Urza's Saga** isn't artifact (enchantment land) and played as land; third chapter search limited to {0} or {1} MANA COST artifact, not arbitrary 0–1 MV e.g no-cost suspend, and Saga sacrifice is part of chapter process.
15. **Wurmcoil** original and copied token are legitimate two creatures with lifelink/deathtouch; copy token death creates two 3/3 artifact token bodies that can be sac to KCI, trigger entry and death lines. Portal originals and copies each ETB independently, opponents choose what to sacrifice on resolution (not target three chosen). No immunity to instant artifact hate.

## Finite sequencing and adversarial scenarios

**A. Cheap copies and genuine sacrifice costs.** You control Ashad and a spare power-2 creature (e.g Poetic Dino from PRIOR cast). Cast Ichor Wellspring as first nonlegendary artifact spell this turn for {2} and sacrifice Dino for casualty additional cost. Ashad gets +1/+1 counter triggered from sacrifice; casualty trigger when cast creates one copy of Wellspring on stack. It resolves to artifact TOKEN (ETB draw), original resolves (ETB draw): two draws. If KCI present, sacrifice token copy for 2C, its put-in-grave trigger draws again while token ceases, Disciple/Marionette/Mayhem payoffs each see relevant events. You don't get Poetic Dino from this spell UNTIL after already having paid casualty cost, so token from same first cast cannot be the sacrifice.

**B. Staff creates THREE Wellsprings, not two.** With Twinning Staff also on battlefield, same first cast and casualty creates TWO spell copies not one, yielding 2 token Wellsprings plus original. Three independent ETB draw triggers (3 cards total), and each can be KCI-sacrificed for 2C and its death draw. But only the actual Dino sacrifice grows Ashad; KCI sacrificing noncreature Wellsprings won't grow him.

**C. Flash on opponents turns.** With Shimmer Myr, spare 2/2 body on battlefield and untapped appropriate mana, first nonlegendary artifact spell during Opponent A turn gets casualty, and another first artifact during Opponent B turn can also get it IF a distinct power2 body remains. Unwinding Clock untaps artifacts on both opponents' untap steps and can replenish mana, but does NOT create fodder from nothing or untap nonartifacts. If Myr removed before cast, ordinary artifacts cannot be cast at instant speed absent other permission.

**D. Urza loop exact bounded bootstrap.** Prerequisite Urza + Helm + Artificer Class LEVEL THREE, next own endstep with Helm untapped; endstep Class copies Helm, yielding one token Helm and one Map. With Urza, tap ORIGINAL Helm and Map for two blue (tapping these for Urza doesn't use their own tap-symbol activated abilities, doesn't have summoning sickness issue for token maps). Pay 1U with two blue and TAP the TOKEN Helm to create a token copy of it. There are two Helm replacements on BF before this token creation, so produce new token Helm plus TWO Maps. New token Helm is untapped; tap two of fresh Maps via Urza for UU, pay 1U and tap new token Helm to repeat. After each loop, you gain at least one additional untapped Map as original Helm count rises. Eventually spare Maps tap via Urza produce arbitrary U; no immediate red/black production is implied. Cast/play from Urza {5} with available mana, honor spells' color identity only if they are cast without paying mana cost; do not label as unconditional win without a damage/board converter and opponents unable to disrupt. No third-party estimated win rate asserted.

**E. Replacement with Three Steps Ahead scenario.** Have Urza + Helm and {3}{U} available DURING OPPONENT turn. Cast Three Steps Ahead selecting +{3} copy mode targeting original Helm, get token Helm+Map, begin same iterative loop if opponents pass priority. This cuts Artificer Class' 3+ level mana plus endstep delay, but the instant consumes a card and is less persistent than Class for future turns. If artifact token under Helm target removed in response to activation, Helm ability may fizzle; if opponent removes Urza, the loop loses free U taps.

**F. Adversarial artifact hate.** Vandalblast overloaded hits Ashad, altars, rocks, artifact token Helm and copies, and cannot be saved solely by Padeem hexproof (overload does NOT target); Deflecting Swat cannot retarget a mass sweeper without targets. Collector Ouphe/Null Rod effects shut off activated artifact mana and Helm activated copy, but Urza's ability is on a CREATURE and doesn't itself cease being an activated ability of nonartifact Urza; even with Urza mana permission, Helm copy activation remains blocked. Stony Silence outside opponent's color identity still present threat. Torpor Orb prevents copied Wellspring/Portal ETB, but not their cast or death triggers. Graveyard exile impairs Emry/Scrap/Welder, but first-cast copying from hand remains alive. These limits prevent assuming locked inevitability.
