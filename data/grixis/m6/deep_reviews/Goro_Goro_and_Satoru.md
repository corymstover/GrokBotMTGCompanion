## Commander identity and natural ceiling
Goro-Goro and Satoru is **{U}{B}{R}** 3/4 Goblin Human. Whenever ONE OR MORE controlled creatures that **ENTERED THIS TURN** deal **combat damage to a player**, make ONE 5/5 flying red Dragon Spirit. This groups hits **per damaged player per combat-damage step**: two fresh Ninjas hitting A simultaneously make one Dragon; hitting A and B creates two; fresh double-strikers can trigger in first-strike and regular steps separately. Creatures can enter tapped/attacking from ninjutsu or sneak and qualify on subsequent player damage, but do NOT generate attack-declaration triggers. Old Slither Blade hitting without reentering this turn doesn't qualify by itself.
Activated **{1}{R}** grants haste ONLY to bodies currently controlled as it resolves; Dragon born afterward doesn't inherit haste. Continuous **Samut, Hazoret's Champion** or **Dragon Tempest** does grant new Dragons haste, allowing extra-combat/Aura chain. Dragon ETB multiplies value with Terror of the Peaks and Tempest direct damage.
**Natural ceiling Bracket 4 — Optimized**; identity: ninjutsu/sneak fresh attackers, repeat attack/at-beginning combat tokens, flying Dragon army, doublestrike, repeated combat and Breath of Fury. Capped by blockers, creature wipes, fragile haste source, and UBR opening. No unrelated Consultation/Thoracle turbo conversion; approved exact Archidekt tags remain whatever canonical source records rather than asserting new tags.

### Integrated curve, mana, threat, card flow, answers and finish
**33 lands/colored source test.** Goro costs UBR, so Ancient Tomb and Sol Ring give NO printed-pip discount; rainbow Tower/City/Confluence, typed fetchable duals, 3 Talismans, Signet, Fellwar help. Crumbling/Xander tri-lands tapped; Shipwreck/Stormcarved slow, Drowned/Sulfur Falls type-check, Fabled Passage early tapped. Two Islands/one Swamp/one Mountain resist nonbasic punishment. Replacing Crumbling with Sokenzan gains red utility but loses U/B on triple-colored commander; a gain must be proven with sampled source-paid hands. UU Counterspell/Drain, RR Breath and repeated {1}{R} still compete in combat.
**Threat density.** Cheap evasive Changeling Outcast, Slither Blade, Triton Shorestalker create unblocked ninjutsu windows but need haste when newly cast to trigger immediately; Ninja of Deep Hours, Moon-Circuit, Mistblade, Prosperous, Ingenious, Fallen Shinobi, Nashi, Mist-Syndicate, Sakashima Student, Yuriko, Thousand-Faced Shadow and both Satorus convert eligible unblock into new body and value. Ragavan Dash, Gingerbrute/Phoenix Chick attack with haste. Kaito Bane can ninjutsu in as creature with loyalty. Commander combat yields Dragons, but opponent creature-heavy blockers lower fresh hit probability.
**Repeatable body generators.** Daring Piracy at BEGINNING each combat yields temporary hasty menace Pirate, Urabrask's Forge hasty Horror, Loyal Apprentice contingent on commander yields flying hasty Thopter, Fire Navy Trebuchet declares attack to yield 2/1 flying attacking Boulder. Determined Iteration requires token to populate first. Raphael Sneak gives attacking creatures double strike and two damage steps. Dragon Tempest/Samut are core **dynamic/continuous** haste, unlike Goro's snapshot. Breath of Fury wants successful player hit, legal DIFFERENT target creature to inherit Aura and new haste each extra-combat Dragon. Karlach and Great Train Heist create finite extra combats; Aggravated Assault needs 3RR each use.
**Draw and resource conversion.** Bident4/Enduring Curiosity4/Grazilaxx3/Waterbender Ascension2; Ninjas/Face-Breaker/Grim Hireling add cards and Treasure. Relative weakness: several expensive on-hit draw payoffs overlap; in stalled creature boards they give no cards, while an early counter may have been needed. But replacing every draw source with more Ninjas can make deck lose after sweep.
**Interaction.** Offer, Arcane Denial, Counterspell, Mana Drain, Fierce, Deflecting, Swan protect commitment; Deadly Rollick, Withering Torment, Terminate, Chaos Warp, Rift, Vandalblast remove blockers/hate; Toxic Deluge is symmetrical and kills dragons when X>=5. Lightning Greaves shroud may prevent attaching your Breath to wearer. Recursion limited: ninjutsu bounce/recast, Chick GY replay, and generating fresh creatures, not generic reanimator package. Opponent combat/flyer density remains ceiling.
**Winning:** sustained multiple 5/5 flying tokens, double-strike Dragon triggers, direct damage for every Dragon via Terror/Tempest, Breath combat chain if connected hasty replacements exist, and finite/conditional extra-combat bursts. A single 1/1 ninja with haste often supplies more commander damage-engine setup than a random 6MV Dragon.

## Oracle, legality and rules
1. Goro "one or more" groups each player and each damage step separately; entering this turn prerequisite; not combat damage to planeswalker. Haste ability snapshots existing bodies, does not give future Dragons haste. [Goro](https://mtg.wtf/card/moc/448/Goro-Goro-and-Satoru).
2. Breath of Fury {2}{R}{R} attached creature player-hit SAC first then attach Aura to ANOTHER controlled creature; if no legal other, no untap/extra combat, Aura goes to grave. If Goro triggers on same fresh hit, order Goro trigger ABOVE Breath to create Dragon first. Newly created Dragon needs current haste and a clear route to connect in the next combat. [Breath](https://mtg.wtf/card/c16/121/Breath-of-Fury).
3. Yuriko's COMMANDER NINJUTSU {U}{B} works from HAND in the 99; it also works from command zone if Yuriko were commander, but in this deck Goro is commander. Ninjutsu creature enters tapped ATTACKING, NOT declared attacker, and qualifies Goro for combat-player hit. [Official WotC 2018 release notes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/commander-2018-edition-release-notes-2018-07-27).
4. Raphael Sneak {1}{R}{R} cast during own declare blockers by returning an unblocked attacker, enters ATTACKING; grants all attacking creatures double strike, thus fresh attacker hitting A in first strike and normal produces TWO distinct Goro trigger events. If blocked and blocker dies in first strike, stays blocked without trample. [Raphael](https://mtg.wtf/card/tmt/297/Raphael-the-Nightwatcher).
5. Fire Navy Trebuchet attack-declared trigger creates 2/1 flying Ballistic Boulder tapped/attacking, then sacrificed at next end step; token was NOT declared attacker, but entered this turn and can hit Goro. Phoenix Chick DOES attack alone, cannot BLOCK; GY return to battlefield tapped/attacking requires declaring THREE+ attackers and paying RR.
