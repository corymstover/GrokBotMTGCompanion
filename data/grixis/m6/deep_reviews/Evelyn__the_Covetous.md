## Commander identity and natural ceiling
Evelyn is a **FIVE**-mana `{2}{U/B}{B}{B/R}` 2/5 Vampire Rogue with FLASH. Whenever she **or another Vampire you control ENTERS**, exile top card of each player's library with COLLECTION counter. Her static ability permits **ONE play TOTAL per TURN from exile with a collection counter placed by an ability YOU controlled**, with any-color mana spent to CAST it; it does **not** automatically bypass timing, give additional land drops, make costs free, or let each Evelyn copy play once independently.
**Important natural ceiling choice:** Canonical October14 approval explicitly adopted **Bracket 5 — cEDH / fringe-competitive** (not B4 tribal). Worldgorger Dragon + reanimation Auras is Evelyn-specific: repeated permanent enters generate unbounded mana and **repeated Evelyn ENTER exiles cards from all libraries** until a valid graveyard reanimation exit breaks the mandatory loop. Oracle/Pact and Breach/LED/Brain Freeze are backup cEDH plans. This deck is not a Vampire tribal shell; aside from commander, **no other Vampire creature remains**. That's a measurable commander-specific weakness, not a reason to unilaterally revise locked canonical version. The defense is that generic Vampire reentry engines cost too much tempo versus validated cEDH competitiveness, while Evelyn flash, Leyline/Borne Upon a Wind, Party Thrasher, Saw in Half, Valley Floodcaller and looping Evelyn create real identity-specific use.
**Causal weakness:** Evelyn is often not required for independent Oracle/Consultation or Breach finish, so one may win through a generic Grixis package. Mark commander-role dependence **SIGNIFICANT but not CENTRAL** in those routes; actual Evelyn-centered proof must be repeated collection exiles from WGD or several profitable opponent-turn collected-card plays, not just high game-win rate. 26 lands + many fast mana and 5MV commander -> high mulligan/colored source stress and life-loss Ad Nauseam/Necro risks. cEDH bracket is natural ceiling already approved, not permission to homogenize without evidence.

### Role synthesis / weakness exposure
**26 lands** (26 names) is deliberately turbo with an unusually high zero-mana package. Fetchable Grixis original duals/shocks, two rainbow lands, Gemstone Caverns pregame, Tomb/City of Traitors burst, Thundering Falls guaranteed tap, conditional Blazemire/Mistrise/Mount Doom. Only Island and Swamp one each; costly lands exiled/dumped to Mox Diamond and rituals make Evelyn's hybrid `{U/B}`+`B`+`{B/R}` difficult if colored mix is wrong. `Tainted Pact` full self-exile REQUIRES distinct names across 100 and has that property now. Replacing one card with same-name second basic or duplicate destroys guarantee.
**Ramp / payability**: Chrome Mox, Diamond, Opal (metalcraft), Petal, LED, Mana Vault, Sol Ring, Talisman U/B and B/R, Signet/Fellwar, Tomb/Traitors, Dark/Cabal/Culling/Rite ritual, Simian Spirit Guide. LED DISCARD HAND on activation, so cannot casually assume full protected hand and mana together. **Cards**: Mystic Remora/Rhystic/Necropotence/Ad Nauseam, Faerie, Wan Shi Tong, Windfall/Notion Thief/Bowmasters; Necro EXILES discards and skips draw step so need consciously acquire WGD grave fuel separately.
**Protection and interaction**: eleven-plus free/cheap counter options Force variants, Pact, Misstep, Trap, Flusterstorm, Swan, Offer, Pyro/REB; Ability counter Stifle; bounce Chain of Vapor, opposing tutor theft/hate Agent/Dauthi; own Defense Grid can unintentionally obstruct casting collected spells on OPPONENT turns. Vexing Bauble counters free spells **symmetrically**, including crucial own ZERO LED escape; sacrifice Bauble before combo after retaining mana.
**Assembly/recursion**: Entomb/Unmarked Grave/Faithless/Ripples find grave Dragon; Animate Dead, Dance, Necromancy are actual loop Auras; Reanimate for utility but on its own not same WGD blink engine; tutors to hand/top, Breach escape. **Wins**: (1) WGD+Aura with Evelyn on field, legal exit, collection exile and eventual Oracle from exile; (2) Thoracle+Consultation/Pact independently; (3) Underworld Breach/LED/Brain Freeze bounded by grave fodder and library state. Need exact library-exile and legal timing on collected spell; no invented "guaranteed WGD into Oracle" from a forced no-exit loop.

## Oracle, legality and rules
1. [Evelyn text](https://mtg.wtf/card/snc/184/Evelyn-the-Covetous) `{2}{U/B}{B}{B/R}` **MV 5**, not three; once each turn one collection-counter card exiled by YOUR ability may be played, *not one per exiled card or per Evelyn copy*. Exiled LAND is PLAYED only on your main phase with land drop available, unless a separately valid effect allows different timing (ordinary "as though flash" generally grants casting timing to spells, not a new land-play permission). Opponent-turn play of collected SORCERY/CREATURE requires Leyline/Borne Upon a Wind/Valley Floodcaller or other timing permission and priority.
2. **WGD Aura loop**: WGD reanimated with Animate Dead/Dance/Necromancy enters, ETB exiles OTHER permanents you control (including Aura and Evelyn if present); Aura leaves causing WGD to die, its leaves trigger returns exiled permanents, including Aura enchanting WGD from grave, restarts. Lands/rocks reenter and may tap for mana between iterations. Evelyn returning as Vampire triggers collection exile. If NO legal alternate reanimation target or other break, looping may be MANDATORY and game drawn, not "infinite mana = automatic win". Need another creature in graveyard or outside action to redirect Aura and finish, then cast Oracle from exile at a LEGAL window if exiled and collection permission unused this turn. Casting Oracle in own main after break can win with empty library; no instant-speed exemption invented.
3. **Saw in Half important correction to older current-cycle text**: Spell only creates two copies if target is destroyed and actually DIES, not if replacement to exile or indestructible. Current Commander rule `903.9a` moves a dead commander to grave FIRST and owner may then move to command zone as SBA; **choosing command zone AFTER the death does not erase the fact Evelyn died** and DOES NOT inherently stop Saw's tokens. Older wording about "commander replacement moves directly to command zone instead of grave" is obsolete/inapplicable for usual deaths. With two token Evelyn copies entering simultaneously and original gone, each sees self+other Vampire entering = 4 exile triggers; legend rule requires only ONE token surviving unless exception, but pending triggers persist. Copies do not themselves count as physical commanders for commander damage/tax.
4. **Vexing Bauble** counters ANY spell if NO mana actually SPENT to cast, even your own spells: LED/Lotus Petal/Mox zero mana spells and free-pitched Force/Pact/Mindbreak/Probe. If a genuine additional mana cost/tax was paid, Bauble may not trigger; don't overgeneralize "mana value0 = counter"—actual amount spent is test. Sac Bauble by `{1},{T}` to draw before combo, mana/priority available; if it is summoning sick? NONCREATURE artifact may tap immediately, yes.
5. **Breach/LED/Brain Freeze**: Underworld Breach escape for each card costs mana cost AND exiles THREE OTHER grave cards; LED 0 mana CAST from GY, sacrificed for mana and hand discard; Brain Freeze 1U repeated storm self-mill supplies cards for escape. A six-fodder initial snapshot, adequate blue mana and spell sequencing matter. Your own Bauble counters ESCAPED LED because no mana spent and defeats engine until sacrificed; opponent grave exile halts it; milling entire table isn't itself win if opponent can respond/replacement shuffle.
6. **Oracle/Consult and Pact**: Oracle U U ETB devotion/library compare resolves after respond with B instant Consultation to exile deck naming card not present, assuming no enemy denial. `Tainted Pact` exile all library with all **100 distinct names**; if duplicates entered later by edit, line can stop on duplicate and is no longer deterministic. Opponents can Stifle Oracle ETB or counter Oracle.
7. **Once each turn vs copies/flash:** two Evelyns briefly entering from Saw does not increase you to two collection card plays in one turn, even if both produce triggers; flash enables Evelyn or instants normally, but her PERMISSION does not give EVERY exiled card flash. Leyline / Borne / Valley help opponent-turn cast of noninstant but Defense Grid taxes your own spells on opponents' turns, an explicit internal tension.
