## Commander identity and natural ceiling

**Marchesa, the Black Rose** costs {1}{U}{B}{R}, is a Human Wizard with **dethrone**, grants other creatures you control dethrone, and returns any creature you control that DIED with a +1/+1 counter to battlefield under your control **at the beginning of the next end step**. Counter must be present as it dies; merely gaining one after dying is impossible. Marchesa must be on battlefield as that death occurs for the death trigger to exist; the delayed return then survives Marchesa leaving. A Marchesa with her own counter also triggers on her death. Dethrone triggers on attacking the player with most/tied life, not their planeswalker; counters occur before blocks. Marchesa's primary gameplay is **protecting sacrifice/ETB creature resources**, not a generic Grixis spellslinger win. Sacrifice engines (Ashnod's/Phyrexian Altars, Bombardment, Dementia, Seer, Feeder, Yahenni, Tower, Yawgmoth), counter sources (Unspeakable Symbol, Forge, Gev, Mimic, Thran Vigil, Uncivil Unrest, Cauldron, Danny, dethrone), persist/undying bodies (Redcap, Glen, Clique, Kelpie, Flayer, Mikaeus) and death/ETB payoffs (Blood Artist, Zulaport, Syr Konrad, Gary, Flayer, Mayhem Devil, River Kelpie, Satoru, Twilight) form a coherent recursion economy.

**Natural ceiling Bracket4:** Compact persist/undying infinites and premium tutors/interaction are real, but the battlefield, commander-return end-step and counter-placement dependence are genuine weaknesses. Source blueprint honestly notes **48 Commander Spellbook enumerated infinite lines do not require Marchesa**; they are usually persist reset+free outlet combos. They are **not** 48 independent strategic win plans, and shouldn't be used to inflate to cEDH or imply commander required for the kill. Marchesa improves resilience, repetition and value while backup loops finish. The exact 21 Oct15 source changes are ALREADY incorporated in this approved deck; do not re-propose Sneak Attack, Sage, Gev, Spark Double, Danny, Cauldron, Satoru or Altar as new additions.

## Mana, development, draw, interaction and vulnerabilities

**Mana inventory:** 34 lands, 6 fetchlands, 6 repeatable nonland mana sources (Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Fellwar Stone, three Talismans), no ritual or one-shot mana inventory; 4 utility lands, 1 unconditional ETB tapped land (Xander's Lounge), six conditionally tapped land flags. Source colored land access potential **B20 / U20 / R16**, unconditional **B19 / U19 / R15**; including repeatable nonland **B24 / U24 / R20** (not simultaneous usable resources). Spell pip demand **B44 (65.67%) / U13 (19.4%) / R10 (14.93%)** and 8 double-black spells vs 2 double-blue spells; BBB Grave Pact and Mikaeus are hard colored commitments. Turn3 10-card no-mulligan proxy: **74.1852%** “all UBR potential land source presence” and **73.6993%** strict 3 land drops; neither means Marchesa T3 is castable (requires four total mana, one of each color, with tapped-land/rocks constraints). Turn4 strict 4 land drops **56.2728%** without ramp. Life payments on Unspeakable Symbol, City, Confluence, painlands, Yawgmoth and Deluge compete with combat life totals and ability to dethrone. `Forge of Heroes` and `High Market` are colorless until utility; `Phyrexian Tower` produces BB by sacrificing creature but may cost a board resource.

**Game plan:** T1 black sac outlet or cheap counter enabler, T2 rock/Strix/engine, T3 draw/counter source, T4 Marchesa with held-up protection or immediate Forge/Unspeakable Symbol counter, then sacrifice countered ETB creatures to reuse at next end step and assemble a repeatable persist reset + outlet + drain/Redcap payoff. Against opponents with higher life, dethrone supplies counters before combat damage; against opponents who lower life intentionally, no reliable dethrone trigger, so independent counter sources are essential. Attack is NOT required to combo, but timing around end step matters.

**Protection / interaction:** An Offer, Fierce Guardianship, Flare of Denial, Chaos Warp, Cyclonic Rift, Feed the Swarm, Otawara, Blasphemous Act, Toxic Deluge, Fleshbag, Glen Elendra Archmage; Grave Pact is repeatable sacrifice control. The deck has only a small permission suite vs turbo Grixis, and several answers are sorcery speed; unlike Marchesa, Dealer of Death, this commander is NOT optimized as cEDH counterspell turbo. `Lightning Greaves` shroud can block our own targeted Unspeakable Symbol/Forge if equipped; equip0 move it before placing counters if timing permits. No graveyard-return protection against Rest in Peace/Leyline/targeted exile; persist, Marchesa and reanimation all need card to visit/return from GY, whereas `Agatha's Soul Cauldron` exiles creatures for ability sharing (can conflict with later reanimation). `Skullclamp`, Satoru, Kelpie, Danny, Haruspex, Braids and tutors cover access, but each has board/setup constraints and excessive draw in a mandatory infinite loop can lose to empty library.

**Opportunity-cost pressure:** 5+ mana engines Flayer, Syr Konrad, Gary, Mikaeus, Uncivil Unrest, River Kelpie and 4-mana Grave Pact can clog a 34-land draw. `Twilight Diviner` once each turn is only good if graveyard returns already happen; `Braids` asks for a disposable permanent at own end step; `Grim Haruspex` risks mandatory overdraw on loop; `Sneak Attack` is high-output but needs creature IN HAND and a counter before end-step sacrifice; `The Ozolith` conserves counters but doesn't independently put a counter on a freshly returned creature until beginning of combat. `Iron Apprentice` modular only moves to artifact creature, so its use as a universal counter donor requires other wording (the card's “when dies” counter-moving ability if present) and should not be assumed without Oracle. `Uncivil Unrest` five mana may be slow but is a mass entry-counter reset and modified-damage closer, not generic anthem. `Gray Merchant` has an actual Mikaeus/Yawgmoth engine role and should not be dismissed as an overcosted aristocrat alone.

## Oracle, legality and rules

**Oracle provenance:** Current authoritative source gate Oct15 and engineering Oct16 bulk Scryfall + 11 `marchbr-01..11` verified rulings; current Oct19 fresh 100-card Oracle lookup **not run**. The official Commander legality checkpoint is dated, and any future newly proposed card needs current independent verification before user approval. Canonical status UBR/no exception remains unchanged.

1. **Delayed end-step, zone:** Marchesa checks controlled creature with +1/+1 counter *as it dies* while her ability exists. It returns CARD from GY at beginning of NEXT end step, not instantly; dying during an end step misses that step's beginning and returns next turn's end step. If it leaves GY before delayed trigger resolves, no return even if back in GY later. Countered Marchesa herself can return, but command-zone replacement choice prevents it if moved to command zone; graveyard exile is a live answer. Opponent can exile target GY card before delayed trigger, or remove Marchesa BEFORE death trigger happens. A creature stolen from opponent that dies under your control returns **under your control** if Marchesa saw countered death, unless GY movement/replacement prevents it.
2. **Dethrone:** Attacking most-life/tied player grants one counter to each attacking creature with dethrone; **Marchesa herself** also has dethrone and gives other creatures dethrone, but granting dethrone to Marchesa again? Oracle says other creatures, so no doubling for her. It doesn't trigger attacking a planeswalker or battle. `Forge of Heroes` can target newly-entered Marchesa this turn to counter without combat, but is colorless otherwise; `Unspeakable Symbol` pays **3 life per +1 counter** instant-speed. `The Ozolith` stores counters when countered creature LEAVES, moves them to another creature at beginning of combat only; the returning creature itself does not retain its previous counters automatically.
3. **Persist reset:** Persist returns creature with -1/-1 counter if no -1/-1 counter at death; +1/+1 and -1/-1 counters annihilate in pairs via state-based action, allowing next death to trigger persist again. `Gev` requires an opponent lost life THIS turn for +1 counter placement; `Metallic Mimic` must choose correct creature type; `Uncivil Unrest` gives riot choose +1 counter; `Thran Vigil` only triggers when card leaves YOUR graveyard DURING YOUR turn (can't sustain opponent-turn loop). `Sage of Fables` counter for Wizards entering; `Spark Double` enters as new copy with extra counter each persist reentry. These effects work without Marchesa once persist loop established; the commander makes normal creature trades resilient.
4. **Redcap self-kill vs damage:** `Murderous Redcap` ETB damage equal power can target **itself**, kill itself after counter-reset state if power≥toughness; in some states `Mikaeus` +1/+1 bonus changes its power/toughness and self-damage still meets toughness only if base equal, yes Redcap 2/2 becomes 3/3. With `Gev` after an opponent lost life, Redcap enters with +1, ETB damage 3 to itself 3/3, dies; persist returns with -1 counter + next +1 counter cancel, ETB damage2 to itself2/2, repeat. This loop alone damages **Redcap**, not opponents; requires Blood Artist/Zulaport/Syr Konrad/Mayhem Devil/Flayer/other death/entry payoff to win. Alternatively free sac outlet lets ETB Redcap target opponents directly each loop, yielding lethal. `Uncivil Unrest` doubles modified source damage, can change self-kill sequence, verify exact power and damage when applying.
5. **Sacrifice and priority:** Sac outlets Ashnod, Phyrexian, Dementia, Bombardment, Viscera, Carrion, Yahenni are repeatable with no tap cost; Yawgmoth sacrifices **another creature**, pays 1 life, puts -1/-1 on up to one target and draws (can't use as fully free infinite at low life); `Warren Soultrader` also pays life and sacrifices ANOTHER creature. `Phyrexian Tower` taps and sac for BB, once per untap; `High Market` taps and sac for 1 life gain, once per untap. `Flare of Denial` alternate cost sacrifice nontoken BLUE creature, e.g. Glen Elendra or Strix, which may later Marchesa-return if countered. Sacrifice as COST cannot be responded to after cost paid, but opponents can remove commander or counter source in response to prior spells/abilities.
6. **Spark Double:** As it enters copies persist creature (e.g. Redcap) and adds a +1 counter to itself. On sacrifice, copied persist triggers (assuming no -1 counter), card returns from GY as new Spark Double copying Redcap anew and gets new +1 counter, cancelling -1 counter; free outlet loops and Redcap ETB damage can hit opponent. With `Ashnod's Altar` each loop nets CC, Redcap ETB damage from Spark Double and unlimited death/ETB. Must have actual persist creature on battlefield to copy at each reentry; if original target gone, choose another creature or Spark enters non-copy with no persist, breaking loop.
7. **Flayer + Sage + Ashnod:** Flayer of Hatebound has undying; sacrifice it to Ashnod for CC, return with +1 counter, ETB triggers its damage to target based on entering creature power; pay {2} to Sage to REMOVE +1 counter and draw card, rearm undying, repeat. The draw is mandatory when paying Sage; cannot “hold priority” to avoid eventual drawing empty library if resolving unbounded loop; **choose finite number of iterations sufficient to kill opponents before decking**, or have replacement/other outlet. Sage's Wizard entry counter doesn't apply to Flayer (Devil), Sage's paid counter-removal does. This is a source-paid loop, not Marchesa-dependent.
8. **Mikaeus and Gray Merchant:** Mikaeus gives undying to other **non-Human** creatures; Gray Merchant is Zombie (non-Human), gets undying, Marchesa/Yawgmoth are Human and do NOT. With Yawgmoth {1 life}, sac another creature, -1 counter target, draw, alternate undying/persist reset lines need actual second qualifying body and enough life/resources; `Gray Merchant` ETB drains each opponent equal to black devotion and gains life, enabling paid Yawgmoth activations. Do not claim a three-card “Mikaeus + Yawgmoth + Gray Merchant” infinite without the **additional qualifying nontoken non-Human creature**, proper target and alternating counter state.
9. **Sneak Attack end step:** R put creature card from HAND onto battlefield with haste; beginning next end step sacrifice it. If creature has +1 counter and Marchesa saw it die to Sneak Attack sacrifice trigger DURING end step, Marchesa delayed return won't trigger until **next end step (next player's end step)**, not immediately during same end step. The creature returns permanently then, unless removed from GY in response. If sacrificed before end step begins, return can occur that same turn end step; Sneak Attack's delayed sacrifice effect will try to sacrifice the original object but not its newly returned version. This makes active counter source valuable.
10. **Satoru, Danny, Twilight, Cauldron:** `Satoru` draws on nontoken creatures entering without cast; Marchesa/persist/undying returns qualify, tokens don't. `Danny Pink` draws when counters placed on creature FIRST TIME each turn, entering with counter counts; a returned creature is a new object with fresh first-time condition. `Twilight Diviner` creates token copy of qualifying creature entering from GY **once each turn**; tokens lack counters, and a token dying can't be returned as a card. `Agatha's Soul Cauldron` exiles **creature card** from GY to share ACTIVATED abilities with countered creatures; cannot share Ashnod's Altar (artifact, not creature card), triggered/continuous abilities, or abilities of a card not exiled by it. This is graveyard hate AND counters but conflicts with reanimating that exact creature.
11. **Payoffs and control:** `Blood Artist` target PLAYER when any creature dies (can target any player), `Zulaport Cutthroat` EACH opponent for own creature deaths; `Mayhem Devil` whenever ANY player sacrifices PERMANENT, so fetchland and Treasure sacs count, `Grave Pact` each opponent sacrifices creature on your own creature deaths, doesn't target and won't bypass “can't sacrifice”. `Flayer` graveyard-entry damage source is entering creature, not necessarily Flayer. `Blasphemous Act` deals 13 and `Toxic Deluge` gives -X/-X until EOT: Marchesa return during same end step may still face lingering Deluge debuff if died before end step and returns before cleanup, a serious self-wipe hazard. Prefer sacrifice or return on NEXT turn end step for Deluge.

## Finite sequencing and adversarial scenarios

These are **authored bounded scenarios** with source-paid costs and priority, NOT new goldfish runs or actual four-player matches. The 129 M5 trials are effect-incomplete, and the reported 260 EDHREC vectors are descriptive, not causal win-rate evidence.

**A — Commander protected without combat, against a board wipe.** Opening line: T1 `Swamp`→`Viscera Seer`; T2 `Command Tower`→`Arcane Signet` ({2}); T3 `Badlands` gives four available mana (three lands + Signet), cast Marchesa {1}{U}{B}{R} via Swamp B, Tower U, Badlands R, Signet generic. No mana remains for `Unspeakable Symbol` placement unless another resource, but `Forge of Heroes` could instead be the T3 land replacing Badlands ONLY if colored UBR already available from Swamp/Tower/Signet, and its tap is held for commander-counter activation after Marchesa enters (the generic cost must come from elsewhere). Next turn opponent targets Marchesa with destroy; if Marchesa has no counter, it does NOT return; if Forge/Unspeakable Symbol placed one in response before death, Marchesa death trigger makes end-step return. **Tradeoff:** `Fellwar Stone` or any 2-mana rock cut for Spawning Pit makes T3 UBR+generic less likely; outlet redundancy isn't worth losing early commander cast in this opening.

**B — Dethrone and controlled end-step recursion.** T4 Marchesa on board, `Baleful Strix` with no counter, `Viscera Seer`. Opponent A has 40 life, B has 34, C has 36. Attack **A** with Strix: Marchesa grants dethrone, trigger puts +1/+1 counter on Strix BEFORE blockers; if attack B instead, no dethrone and no automatic Marchesa return. After combat sacrifice countered Strix to Seer for scry1; Marchesa trigger sets delayed return beginning of upcoming end step. At end step Strix returns (fresh object with no counter) and draws a card. Opponent `Swords to Plowshares` in response to Seer's sacrifice activation **can't exile Strix already sacrificed**; opponent can exile Strix while it is on battlefield before sacrifice or use graveyard exile before delayed return. Against high-life control opponents, this is a real commander-native value line.

**C — Sneak Attack end-step edge and meaningful competing cut.** `Sneak Attack` active, Marchesa and `Unspeakable Symbol` in play, R mana and 3 life available. During own main, pay R to Sneak `Gray Merchant` from hand, gain life from ETB drain, pay 3 life to Symbol to give Gary +1 counter, then choose: (1) sacrifice Gary to Seer **before end step**, Marchesa schedules return at beginning of THIS turn's end step, triggering Gary ETB again; Sneak's end-step sacrifice of old object does not affect returned new object. (2) wait for Sneak's beginning-of-end-step sacrifice; Marchesa sees countered death but its delayed return waits until the **next player's end step**. In both cases opponents can exile Gary in GY before return. Cutting Sneak Attack for `Repercussion` or generic wheel removes unique haste + ETB + delayed recurrence, even if the other enchantment is powerful.

**D — Payable Redcap/Gev loop, opponent damage isn't automatic.** Have `Gev, Scaled Scorch` and `Murderous Redcap` available, and an opponent lost at least 1 life this turn (e.g. `Mayhem Devil` ping after a fetch sacrifice). Cast Redcap 2BB. Gev puts +1/+1 counter on Redcap as it enters; Redcap ETB damage equal power (3) can target **Redcap itself**, killing it; its persist returns it with -1/-1 counter and Gev counter +1 cancel via state-based actions. Reentered Redcap ETB damages itself 2, repeats. This is unlimited death/entry count but **not unlimited damage to opponents** unless Blood Artist, Zulaport, Mayhem Devil or another drain/payoff is present; with `Goblin Bombardment` free sac outlet, instead have Redcap ETB target opponent and sacrifice it to Bombardment to reset, generating lethal. Enemy kills Gev while Redcap death trigger on stack: future returns may retain -1 counter and stop. `First Day of Class` gives one-turn counter/haste alternative, but Gev persists over turns and triggers with opponent life loss; replacing Danny's repeated counter-draw for a one-shot is not proven superior.

**E — Wizard persist control loop isn't free permission.** `Glen Elendra Archmage` on board with `Sage of Fables` (Wizard entry counter) and opponent casts noncreature spell. Pay {U} and sacrifice Glen to counter spell; Glen had +1 counter, so its persist **doesn't trigger?** Persist only checks **-1/-1 counters**, so it DOES trigger and returns with -1/-1, Sage adds +1 on entry, cancel. Glen ready to activate again if {U} remains; the {U} must be paid **per countered spell**, and Glen is a new creature but its activated ability has no tap cost, so summoning sickness irrelevant. Marchesa death trigger also set up delayed return when Glen died with +1 counter, but persist already moved card out of graveyard, so delayed return won't double it. If Sage removed before Glen persist resolves, returned Glen keeps -1 counter and subsequent persist stops unless another reset. `Counterbalance` would be generic but current Glen/Sage is unusually commander-aligned repeatable stack defense.

**F — Spark Double/Redcap/Altar actual payable loop.** Have `Murderous Redcap` battlefield, `Ashnod's Altar` battlefield and cast `Spark Double {3}{U}` choosing Redcap as it enters. Spark copies Redcap, gets +1 counter, its ETB deals damage to opponent. Sacrifice Spark-Redcap to Ashnod for CC; persist returns the Spark Double card with -1 counter; as it enters it copies original Redcap again, adds +1 counter, +1 and -1 cancel, Redcap ETB pings opponent again. Each cycle adds CC and damage, assuming original Redcap still on battlefield to copy. If original Redcap is removed between iterations and there is no other persist creature, Spark's reentry cannot copy Redcap and stops. This line does not require Marchesa and is honest about her commander-independent backup wins. `Walking Ballista` could use unlimited colorless, but replacing `Mayhem Devil` with Ballista gives a redundant finisher instead of early every-sac ping; only a measured faster win could justify it.

**G — Mikaeus/Yawgmoth/Gary cost and missing fourth body.** On battlefield `Mikaeus, the Unhallowed`, `Yawgmoth, Thran Physician`, `Gray Merchant of Asphodel` and **another qualifying non-Human nontoken creature** (e.g. `Murderous Redcap`, if not disabled by counters). Mikaeus gives Gary undying. Yawgmoth ability requires **pay 1 life and sacrifice another creature**; alternate Gary and the second creature as resources while putting -1/-1 counter on target as needed so the undying counters can be removed/cancelled. Gary's ETB repeatedly drains each opponent for black devotion and gains enough life to fund paid activations in a real multiplayer board. Remove second body, or exile Gary on a death trigger, and no self-contained automatic three-card infinite is established. Cutting Gary for a generic six-mana Kokusho is not obviously better because Gary pays life and ETB with black devotion while Kokusho requires dying and drains fixed five.

**H — Flayer + Sage + Ashnod finite kill and library limit.** `Flayer of the Hatebound` no +1 counter, `Ashnod's Altar`, `Sage of Fables` on board; sac Flayer to Ashnod→CC, undying returns Flayer with +1 counter and its triggered damage to opponent, spend CC on Sage's {2} remove +1 counter and draw one card, then repeat. If each opponent has 40 life, take **finite** number of repetitions to deal lethal before drawing beyond library size; since each Sage activation draws, can't declare an unbounded loop with no deck-loss risk. Opponent graveyard exile in response to undying stops the return. `Flayer` expensive but is an actual source-paid finisher; replace it only after preserving another low-card kill.

**I — board wipe and delayed-return timing.** With Marchesa, countered Baleful Strix and countered Glen Elendra Archmage, cast `Blasphemous Act` for R if creature count high enough. Act deals13, all three may die; Marchesa sees creatures with +1 counters die **simultaneously**, triggers for each including itself if it had a counter; they return at next end step if remain in graveyards. `Toxic Deluge` for X=4 grants -4/-4 **until end of turn**; Marchesa-returned small creatures at this same end step may immediately die again from the still-active -4/-4, often without +1 counter, and won't recur. Thus “Deluge is a free one-sided wipe with Marchesa” is FALSE without correct end-step sequencing. Against `Rest in Peace`, death cards are exiled instead of graveyard and cannot return; both wipes become much less asymmetrical. `Living Death` replacement adds mass symmetric reanimation but gives opponents creatures too, not a universal upgrade.

**J — Village Rites vs Braids real resource throughput.** You have Marchesa, countered `Baleful Strix` and `Braids, Arisen Nightmare` on board at own end step. Sacrifice Strix to Braids, each opponent must sacrifice same type or lose2 and you draw; if they decline and cannot match, you may draw up to three cards and drain 2 each, then Marchesa delayed return **next player's end step** (because Braids sacrifices after beginning of this end step). `Village Rites` instead costs {B} at any instant window, sacrifices Strix as additional cost and draws **exactly two cards**, Marchesa return at appropriate next end step, but loses the repeated every-turn pressure and potential three draws. Against immediate stack interaction, Village Rites's instant timing can be better; against attrition Braids dominates. The source v1.1 `pass` for Village Rites didn't model Braids at all, so cannot settle matchup.

**K — opponent adaptation and metagame ceiling.** Three opponents: graveyard-hate player with `Rest in Peace`, combo player with `Thassa's Oracle`, creature deck with `Toxic Deluge`. Marchesa's delayed recursion and all persist/undying loops collapse under Rest in Peace; `Feed the Swarm` can destroy the enchantment at sorcery timing if available, `Chaos Warp` instant, `An Offer` counter on stack. Adding another sac outlet `Bloodflow Connoisseur` doesn't answer it, and `Gravecrawler` worsens dependency. Versus Oracle on stack, `Flare of Denial` can sacrifice blue nontoken Strix/Glen at no mana, `Fierce Guardianship` free if Marchesa present, `An Offer` one U for noncreature? Oracle is CREATURE spell, so **An Offer CANNOT counter Oracle spell**; Flare can counter it, Glen counters only noncreature, Fierce only noncreature. To stop Oracle ETB, this list has no `Stifle`—must counter creature spell with Flare or disrupt library/trigger through other means. This real weakness is a ceiling reason, but swapping to cEDH generic stack package would undermine optimized Bracket4 plan unless owner asks.

## Conclusions and unresolved evidence

**Final authored disposition: `KEEP` the exact 100 approved cards, with meaningful matchup-conditional alternatives.** This is a new independent substantive commander-first review, not mere repetition of the October15 “zero new material improvements” closeout. The 34-land black-heavy mana plan, repeatable counter and sacrifice redundancy, four real persist/undying recursion bodies, death/ETB draw and drain density, and creature-based tutor/stack protection form a coherent Bracket4 deck. No challenger among the 129 M5 swaps or offranking candidates has a documented payable, opponent-interactive, matchup-adjusted improvement over its best real alternative cut. The only goldfish `pass` (Village Rites for Braids) is **a known effect-model asymmetry**, not proof.

**Strongest counterargument to KEEP:** `Braids` triggers only at own end step and may wait one extra opponent end step to return sacrificed creatures; instant `Village Rites` is 1 mana, draws two now and protects a creature from exile removal if sac is paid first. `First Day of Class` can turn an otherwise delayed counter-reset into an immediate one-turn kill and is cheaper than Danny; `Walking Ballista` can monetize infinite colorless and improve outlet redundancy; `Kokusho` adds big flying/drain at death. In faster combo-heavy tables, the current 4–6 mana permanents may lose before first end step. These are genuine questions for measured meta trials.

**Strongest counterargument to swapping:** Replacing `Braids` with Rites removes a repeatable sacrifice+multiplayer card draw tax, replacing `Danny` with First Day loses every-turn draw and counter placement, replacing `Mayhem Devil` with Ballista loses cheap any-permanent sacrifice payoff, and replacing `Cauldron` with Gravecrawler loses counter placement/ability sharing/precision graveyard exile. High-value existing combos are **commander-independent**, but Marchesa is essential to the board-resilience game plan, and deleting counter sources to add a 10th sacrifice outlet can make that identity worse.

**Testing limits:** No new actual opponent-aware simulation or physical game trials. M5 129 paired abstract goldfish screens often model one or both cards only as castable and omit delayed Marchesa trigger, persist/undying, +1/-1 cancellation, Sneak timing, opponent graveyard hate, colored mana and instant priority. EDHREC 260 inclusion vectors are not wins. Scenarios A–K are manual finite state reasoning, not empirical samples; a future registered test should compare Village Rites/Braids, First Day/Danny, Ballista/Mayhem, Gravecrawler/Cauldron and Kokusho/Syr across 4-player removal/hate/turbo conditions, track real castable turn and actual Marchesa returns/draw, and disclose simulator coverage before claiming win percentages. The Oct15 source official Commander legality gate and Oct16 Oracle compliance are not newly live-verified Oct19 for all 100, so new rules changes after checkpoint would require fresh check; no specific conflict surfaced here.
