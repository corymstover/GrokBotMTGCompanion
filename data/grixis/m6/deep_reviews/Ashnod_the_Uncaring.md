## Commander identity and natural ceiling

Ashnod grants an extra copy of each qualifying NONMANA activated ability of artifact or creature that sacrifices one or more permanents AS COST. Tokens qualify as sacrifice payments. No once-turn restriction. Sacrificed input paid ONCE and triggered copy yields extra benefit. Prime commander-dependent activations: Dockside Chef, Trading Post two sacrifice modes, Mind Stone, Moonsilver Key, Repurposing Bay, Time Sieve, Bolas's Citadel, Clan Crafter, Spellbombs/Clues. Mana sacrifices to Ashnod's Altar, KCI and Lotus Petal do NOT count. Noncommander combos supplement natural optimized Bracket 4 ceiling, not substitute for distinctive copying. The five-mana commander and artifact-hate exposure limit realistic certainty.

## Oracle, legality and rules

### Discrete source-verified rulings

1. Ashnod's Oracle: each activation of an ARTIFACT or CREATURE ability that is NONMANA and for which one or more PERMANENTS are SACRIFICED AS AN ACTIVATION COST creates one optional copy. Cost is paid once. Tokens may be sacrificed. NO once-per-turn limit. Earlier historical restrictions were wrong (fresh 2026-10-14 source correction).
2. Trading Post has FOUR modes but only the **sacrifice a creature / return target artifact card from GY to HAND** and **sacrifice an artifact / draw 1** modes qualify, because the discard and pay-1-life costs are not sacrifices. Claim that Ashnod doubles all four modes is false. Sacrificing one creature can return TWO separate grave artifacts to hand if two legal targets remain.
3. Ashnod's Altar (sac creature for CC), Krark-Clan Ironworks (sac artifact for CC), Lotus Petal (sac for chosen color) are MANA ACTIVATIONS; all explicitly excluded. Sac triggers by Mayhem Devil, Crime Novelist, Marionette Apprentice and Disciple of the Vault do not become copied Ashnod abilities; they are TRIGGERED effects.
4. Goblin Welder taps and CHOOSES targets as cost/target step, then if both legal the artifact is sacrificed and grave artifact returned SIMULTANEOUSLY during RESOLUTION. No activation-cost sacrifice, so Ashnod NEVER directly copies Welder. Goblin Engineer, Scrap Welder and Repurposing Bay pay SAC in their activation cost and qualify.
5. Repurposing Bay is sorcery-speed {2} tap SAC another artifact with printed/current MV=X as COST. Original and copied ability each independently search for an ARTIFACT CARD with mana value EXACTLY X+1, not arbitrary pieces or unequal successive values. A 0-cost artifact token can seek MV1 twice only if two qualifying artifacts remain.
6. Time Sieve T+sac EXACTLY FIVE artifacts inserts one extra turn from original and one from copy; Rings of Brighthearth paying another 2 can produce third activation copy. Taking those turns does not intrinsically make another five artifacts. No mana/sac cost is duplicated when Ashnod or Rings copies an ability.
7. Bolas's Citadel tap and SAC ten NONLAND PERMANENTS as activation cost: Ashnod adds one copy, so all opponents lose ten life twice (20 total). Paying life to cast topdeck spells remains unrelated. Citadel may get tapped for activation in response to removal if it's untapped, and costs are paid before opponent responses.
8. Altar of Dementia sacrificing a creature mills equal to that creature's POWER twice and may choose new target player. Executioner's Capsule sacrifices itself to destroy up to TWO distinct NONBLACK target creatures if two legal targets chosen on activation/copy; additional target need not have been legal on the original? It must be legal for copy when created. Pyrite Spellbomb's damage is two per resolution (four total), but both damage abilities can target different creatures/opponents.
9. Mishra's Bauble sacrifices itself as nonmana cost; Ashnod duplicates the delayed draw-at-NEXT-turn-upkeep, not immediate draw. Dockside Chef accepts any artifact or creature sacrifice plus 1B and duplicates DRAW, no added bodies. Forensic Gadgeteer discounts generic ARTIFACT activation costs down to a minimum of 1 mana; clue tokens have their own qualifying activated sacrifice-draw.
10. Treasure Vault is an ARTIFACT LAND and its XX+tap+SAC activation is NONMANA, so Ashnod can copy X Treasure generation to yield two batches at the SAME X chosen. Buried Ruin is a land but not an artifact/creature by default, therefore fails commander source requirement. Urza's Saga is an Enchantment Land and not an artifact by default.
11. Rings of Brighthearth is a separate triggered effect triggered by activating nonmana ability, permits paying 2 for ONE MORE activation copy. On Ashnod-eligible activation, resolving Rings and commander-copy triggers may produce 3 total activation resolutions from one paid-sacrifice cost.
12. Retrofitter Foundry costs/qualifying modes: pay1+T+SAC a Servo to create a Thopter (two with Ashnod), T+SAC a Thopter to make a 4/4 Construct (two with Ashnod); normal 2+T to make Servo, and 3 to untap, do NOT sacrifice and are not doubled.
13. Walking Ballista counter-removal is NOT sacrifice of permanent and is not commander copied, but a genuine outlet for unbounded COLORLESS mana via 4-for-one +1/+1 counters and remove-counter one damage per counter. It retains role even when its direct Ashnod synergy is nil.
14. Creature DEATH includes artifact token hitting grave from BF and can trigger Scorpion, Marionette, etc., though tokens then cease existing and cannot be returned as CARDS by Scrap Trawler, Retriever, Deathmantle. Exile, bounce, replacement effects bypass such dying triggers and break some combos.

## Finite sequencing and adversarial scenarios

### A. Cheap resource compression: Chef versus ranked rivals

Control Ashnod, Dockside Chef and a Treasure plus 1B available. Activate Chef, pay 1B and sacrifice Treasure ONCE: trigger commander to copy the nonmana activation. Resolve copy and original to DRAW TWO cards. Mayhem Devil separately pings one target on the ONE actual sacrifice; it is not triggered a second time merely from copying Chef. Compare Detective's Satchel: its Thopter-making tap activation requires you have sacrificed earlier in turn but pays no sacrifice cost itself, and Ashnod does not copy that Thopter activation.

### B. Expensive but genuinely doubled: Time Sieve and Bay

Control untapped Time Sieve, Ashnod, exactly five expendable artifacts. Tap Sieve and sac all five during ACTIVATION, immediately commit five permanents. Two extra turns are created as Ashnod copy and original resolve. You cannot take unlimited turns unless you can make five more artifacts EACH subsequent turn, plus untap Sieve. With Repurposing Bay and a sacrificial 2-MV artifact, pay2 tap/sac artifact; two independent searches for EXACTLY MV3 ARTIFACT each, subject to both existing in library. Costs aren't paid a second time; neither ability can search MV4 on second resolution.

### C. Myr Retriever + Scrap Trawler + Altar + reducer (real initial zones)

Commander Spellbook prerequisite: Scrap Trawler in YOUR GRAVEYARD, Myr Retriever ON BATTLEFIELD, Ashnod's Altar and Etherium Sculptor on battlefield. Start 0C float. Sac Myr Retriever using Altar MANA ability => +2C; Retriever triggered ability returns OTHER artifact, the Trawler, from grave to hand. Cast Trawler for cost 2 (3 reduced by Sculptor); sacrifice Trawler to Altar => +2C; Trawler's death trigger targets Retriever MV2 (strictly <3) in grave to return to hand; cast Retriever for 1 (2 reduced by Sculptor), leave 1C. Repeat: each 4C generated by 2 creature sacs pays 3C for two casts and nets 1C. Infinite arbitrary finite repeats, artifact ETB/death and creature-sacrifice events, and potentially unbounded COLORLESS mana. This loop does NOT depend on Ashnod copying the Altar mana ability (it cannot). If Trawler starts ON BATTLEFIELD with no alternate recursion piece set up, don't claim instantaneous loop; move pieces to required grave/board positions first.

### D. Marionette Apprentice + Nim Deathmantle + Ashnod's Altar

With Altar and Deathmantle present and Marionette Apprentice on battlefield, choose FABRICATE to create a 1/1 Servo artifact token as Apprentice enters. Sac Servo to Altar +2C while Apprentice on BF => trigger Apprentice causing EACH opponent lose1. Sac Apprentice to Altar +2C => Deathmantle non-token death trigger; pay4 to return Apprentice to BF and attach Deathmantle; its ETB fabricates a fresh Servo. Repeat. Starts and ends with 0C float, so loop is mana-NEUTRAL but produces infinite as many finite chosen iterations of ETBs/own artifact deaths, which kill via Apprentice. Ashnod's Altar mana activations are NOT doubled by commander. Opponent grave-exiling Apprentice as response to Deathmantle trigger ends loop.

### E. Dross Scorpion + Retrofitter Foundry + Ashnod's Altar

Control Dross Scorpion, untapped Retrofitter Foundry, Altar, initially two generic mana. Activate Foundry pay2 tap to make 1/1 Servo artifact CREATURE. Sac Servo to Altar (mana ability) => gain 2C and Scorpion triggers from artifact creature death to untap Foundry. Spend those 2C again. Every iteration costs/returns 2C, hence NET ZERO mana, but arbitrary repeated artifact creature ETB and death (Reckless Fireweaver, Marionette Apprentice or Mayhem Devil converts to win). Foundry's basic 2+tap make Servo has NO sacrifice cost and is NOT copied by Ashnod; Scorpion's untap is TRIGGERED not activated.

### F. Triple-layer Rings and Ashnod: a single cost

Control Ashnod, Rings of Brighthearth, an untapped Executioner's Capsule, 1B for Capsule, 2 additional mana for Rings and TWO distinct enemy nonblack creatures on battlefield. Activate Capsule targeting first, pay 1B tap and sacrifice it. Ashnod trigger copies target-destroy ability and may retarget to second. Rings triggers from activation, pay2 to create additional copy and choose target (may be same remaining target if legal). Three total ability objects resolve but can destroy at most TWO distinct creatures if only two available; if target already left BF, later copied effect may fizzle. This is three activations resolved only as many legal targets. Does not sacrifice a second/third Capsule.

### G. Adversarial hate, tempo and replacement

An opponent's overloaded Vandalblast destroys most of artifact infrastructure in one stroke; does not target and isn't stopped by Padeem-type hexproof. Null Rod or Collector Ouphe shut off many artifact activated costs and mana while leaving Ashnod's creature ability but no useful qualifying artifact activators; Chaos Warp/Rift and counterspell must defend before combo. Rest in Peace/Scavenging Ooze can exile Retriever/Trawler/Apprentice grave card before recovery, breaking grave loops even when Ashnod remains. Blowing up Ashnod in response AFTER activation doesn't remove pre-existing commander trigger/copies on stack; removing before your next qualifying activation does. Timing of tap availability, priority and legal target count on copies prevents false deterministic outcomes.

## Conclusions and unresolved evidence

**STRATEGIC HOLD.** Approved deck already concentrates on qualifying sacrifice-COST activations and provides intact engine/counterplay. The 95 approved-card Oracle rows are Commander-legal UBR and the full 100 is valid.
