## Commander identity and natural ceiling

Ashnod grants an extra copy of each qualifying NONMANA activated ability of artifact or creature that sacrifices one or more permanents AS COST. Tokens qualify as sacrifice payments. No once-turn restriction. Sacrificed input paid ONCE and triggered copy yields extra benefit. Prime commander-dependent activations: Dockside Chef, Trading Post two sacrifice modes, Mind Stone, Moonsilver Key, Repurposing Bay, Time Sieve, Bolas's Citadel, Clan Crafter, Spellbombs/Clues. Mana sacrifices to Ashnod's Altar, KCI and Lotus Petal do NOT count. Noncommander combos supplement natural optimized Bracket 4 ceiling, not substitute for distinctive copying. The five-mana commander and artifact-hate exposure limit realistic certainty.
