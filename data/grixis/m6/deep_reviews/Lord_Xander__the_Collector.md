## Commander identity and natural ceiling

Xander is a 7-mana 6/6 Vampire Demon Noble. Enter: **target opponent discards half cards in hand rounded DOWN**. Declared attack: **defending player mills half their library rounded DOWN**. Dies: **target opponent sacrifices half NONLAND permanents they control rounded DOWN**. Each trigger affects one specific opponent/defender rather than automatically the table. Attack trigger occurs at declaration even if Xander is subsequently blocked or killed; copies entering attacking **do not** trigger attack. Empty/one-card hand, one-card library, and one nonland permanent each round to **zero** for relevant halves, so repeated halving hits diminishing returns.

**Protected signature:** cheat/recur a commander after **actually reaching a legal zone**, stack triggered abilities, make true copies, exploit legend-rule death triggers and repeated attack declarations. Payoffs `Bruvac`, `Riverchurn Monument`, `Fraying Sanity`, `Cruel Calculations`, `Tergrid`, `Roaming Throne`; cast/copy `Auton Soldier`, `Blade of Selves`, `Phantasmal Image`, `Helm of the Host`, `Saw in Half`, `Hall of Echoes`, `Sakashima`, `Molten Duplication`, `Strionic Resonator`. Combo backup (`Breach`+LED+Brain Freeze and Guildmage+Mindcrank) is allowed but never a reason to strip Lord's distinctive halving package. **Natural ceiling Bracket 4 — Optimized**; the seven-cost commander and need for connected attack/reanimation delimit realistic ceiling despite unusually strong mana and permission.

**NEW CRITICAL RULES FINDING:** `Persist` says **target NONLEGENDARY creature card**; it cannot reanimate the legendary Lord Xander even if he is in the graveyard. `Entomb` and `Gamble` search **library**, cannot find the commander in the command zone. Thus current Oct14 approved blueprint incorrectly counts Persist as one of Lord's five reanimation routes and Entomb as a direct Xander graveyard tutor. These are not Commander legality violations and do not unlock approved list, but materially narrow access plan. Legal Persist targets include `Auton Soldier`, `Phantasmal Image`, `Spark Double`, and `Roaming Throne`; if original Lord already exists, an Auton or Image can still generate triggers. `Feign Death` requires Xander on battlefield, a death this turn, and choosing to leave him in GY for return. Costed hardcast {4}{U}{B}{R} first, then +2 for each past command-zone cast (9, 11 etc). Do not call any of these turn-one commander-to-graveyard tutors.

### Whole-deck synthesis

- **33 lands** in a seven-mana strategy is viable only because of premium burst acceleration: `Sol Ring`, `Mana Vault`, `Grim Monolith`, `Ancient Tomb`, `Lotus Petal`, `Lion's Eye Diamond`, `Dark Ritual`, `Jeska's Will`, 3 Talismans and `Arcane Signet`. Some of those require graveyard setup, a commander already controlled or discard of hand; do not count all as unconditional seven-mana access. Historical static `data/grixis/mana_base/lord-xander-the-collector.json`: B19/R16/U17 land sources, B22/R19/U20 with repeatable rocks, 33 lands, 7 repeatable nonland mana sources, six fetches, 3 unconditional taplands, 7 conditional taplands. The four-turn no-mulligan strict 4-land-drop proxy is .532594, not an Xander cast probability. 7-mana Xander cast-state must respect UBR pips, `Hall of the Bandit Lord` tap/life, `Hall of Echoes` colorless slot, and Urza's Saga land disappearance.
- **Card access** combines tutors `Demonic Tutor`, `Vampiric Tutor`, `Imperial Seal`, `Mystical Tutor`, `Gamble`, `Entomb`, backed by `Mystic Remora`, `Rhystic Study`, `The One Ring`, `Brainstorm`, `Wheel of Fortune`, `Cruel Calculations` and `Jeska's Will`. **Not all tutors access commander in command zone**; `Entomb` and `Gamble` find support cards in library only.
- **Interaction/defense:** `Fierce Guardianship`, `Force of Will`, `Force of Negation`, `Pact of Negation`, `Swan Song`, `An Offer You Can't Refuse`, `Mana Drain`, `Deflecting Swat`, `Deadly Rollick`, `Cyclonic Rift`, `Chaos Warp`, `Toxic Deluge`, `Opposition Agent`, `Orcish Bowmasters`. Many free spells have real conditions; `Force of Negation` cannot be pitched free on own turn, `Fierce Guardianship` and `Swat` are not free when Lord is only in command zone.
- **Clone and trigger multiplicity:** Strong native leverage from 11 slots. ETB, attack and death are different windows: image legend-rule burst is high-rate but destroyed by active no-legend effect; nonlegendary clones live longer but lack free deaths; myriad tokens do NOT trigger attack and EXILE rather than die at end of combat. `Hall of Echoes` also disables beneficial legend-rule death burst *that turn*, despite being a great attack-copy land; `Sakashima` has the same anti-synergy while present.
- **Real cut scrutiny:** `Persist` is the major target mismatch; `Karlach` high-cost extra-combat enabler; `Hall of the Bandit Lord` tapped colorless/hurt-life haste cost; `Urza's Saga` early colorless mana, `Bojuka Bog` tapped black; `Underworld Breach` powerful but commander-independent; `Notion Thief` and `Orcish Bowmasters` strong general goodstuff; `Cruel Calculations` can overdraw. **Retain floor priority:** mana engine, UBR colored sources, early tutors, free shields, critical Xander copies, `Bruvac` and mill finishing, death payoffs and discard/sac conversion.

## Oracle, legality and rules

**Exact source evidence:** `Persist` Oracle "Return target nonlegendary creature card from your graveyard to the battlefield with a -1/-1 counter on it", `Lord Xander` Legendary, so invalid target, *including a legendary card in graveyard*; relevant 2026 printing https://mtg.wtf/card/ecc/83/Persist . `Entomb` searches your library, not command zone. Commander put into GY actually goes there; as SBA owner can move it to command zone, or leave it there so `Reanimate`/`Animate Dead`/`Necromancy`/`Goryo` can resolve (Wizards: https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/commander-masters-release-notes and https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/commander-legends-release-notes-2020-11-06). `Seize the Day` {3}R with {2}R flashback, legal Commander: https://mtg.wtf/card/msc/166/Seize-the-Day . `The Master of Lake-town` loss-to-mill text and legality: https://mtg.wtf/card/hob/297/The-Master-of-Lake-town . October 2026 `Hall of Echoes` Oracle and Rulings 2026-08-21: https://mtg.wtf/card/pfra/179p/Hall-of-Echoes .

**Finite rules caveats:** `Phantasmal Image` becomes LEGENDARY Xander when entering, and original Xander remains, so after entry before triggers stack a legend SBA causes you to choose one to keep, other dies; its own ETB trigger and dies trigger are on stack and controller chooses order. If Hall of Echoes or Sakashima prevents legend rule, **the extra death never happens via this mechanism**. `Saw in Half` destroys original Xander (which can trigger dies), creates 2 smaller legendary token copies, each ETB, one dies due legend rule absent prevention; if original was indestructible and doesn't die, Saw's conditional "if that creature dies this way" matters. `Helm of the Host` creates a NONlegendary token at beginning combat that enters and gains haste, so it can be declared as attacker and gets attack trigger; Blade of Selves tokens enter tapped and attacking, so their attack triggers do not occur. `Auton Soldier` is nonlegendary copied Xander and gives original myriad; its generated tokens exile end-combat with **no dies trigger**. `Hall of Echoes` does not enter when it becomes Xander, so no ETB. It can only be declared attacking if controlled since turn started or given haste, *untapped*, and activation 5 mana occurred before declaration.

**Milling arithmetic:** Xander library 41 -> floor(41/2)=20, then with Bruvac mill 40 => **1 left**, not empty. Library 40 -> floor20 -> mill40 => empty; loss happens only when next attempted draw from empty library, or life-loss combo; not immediate state-based loss solely due empty library. `Maddening Cacophony` kicked half-UP avoids parity gap with Bruvac. `Fraying Sanity` later end step counts cards actually library -> GY, not exile or shuffled-away cards. `Riverchurn Monument` needs 2UU and tap, once-per-permanent exhaust, opponent pre-existing GY counts; it does not mill directly based on Xander's *current library* size independent of graveyard.

**Spell and combat conditions:** `Force of Negation` blue pitch free only on opponents' turns; cannot protect own Xander copied kill for free. `Pact of Negation` 3UU following upkeep cannot be ignored. `Sword of Feast and Famine` land untap requires dealt combat damage; `Aggravated Assault` activates only at sorcery speed in main phase and needs 3RR repeatedly. `Karlach` attack must occur during first combat with an ally and untaps creatures later, but merely being on battlefield does not make Lord attack. `Roaming Throne` doubles Xander triggers by choosing Vampire/Demon/Noble, not opponent's abilities. `Underworld Breach` + LED + Brain Freeze requires 1U per Freeze, zero-mana LED escapes needing 3 graveyard cards, enough library/graveyard to exile 3 for each recast, and actual increasing storm count. This review does not invent infinite loops in a blank graveyard.

## Finite sequencing and adversarial scenarios

1. **Starting Xander in CZ, Entomb in hand**: cast Entomb for B, search library. Lord Xander isn't there. It can find an actual 99-card graveyard piece or `Underworld Breach` fuel, but cannot set up {B} `Reanimate` on your commander. Any projected turn-two Xander via Entomb/Reanimate from CZ is **false**.
2. **Persist invalid target, GY**: original Xander actually died, remains legendary Xander CARD in GY. `Persist` for 1B cannot even choose that card as target. It can choose nonlegendary `Auton Soldier` in GY instead; Auton ETB may copy an existing Xander and trigger half-hand discard, but if no Lord present it cannot itself copy Lord. This is a serious timing cost, not 'Persist works with Lord' as direct recursion.
3. **Valid Necromancy on dead Lord**: Xander cast normally for {4}{U}{B}{R}; opponent kills it. Lord enters GY, owner leaves there at SBA. Pay {2}{B} for Necromancy targeting it; Lord enters, triggers target opponent discard half; Necromancy makes it lose -1/-0? (Necromancy does NOT reduce P/T) and permanent retains attack ability. Opponent can remove aura, causing Xander to be sacrificed. Does not work from CZ or through grave hate.
4. **Goryo tempo alternative**: if Xander already in graveyard, 1B instant Goryo's Vengeance targets it, returns with haste. Attack this turn -> defending player mills floor half. Exile delayed trigger next end step causes NO death trigger. If `Altar of Dementia` is already on board, sacrifice Xander before end step: mills 6 targeted + Xander death trigger forces half nonland sacrifices; owner may leave card in GY again. Opponents can respond before activated sacrifice by removing Xander or exiling graveyard before vengeance resolves.
5. **Legend clone math with Xander**: Xander on battlefield, cast Image for 1U as copy. Image enters as Lord, both legendary. Legend SBA: keep original; copied Image dies. 1 ETB discard-half trigger and 1 dies sacrifice-half trigger go stack; two effects choose target opponent independently. With 7-card hand, discard floor(7/2)=3; with 7 nonland permanents, sacrifice 3. If no-legend Hall/Sakashima present, Image doesn't die and only ETB occurs.
6. **Myriad Xander line**: equipped original Xander attacks opponent A. Original's attack mills A half at declaration, Blade creates one tapped attacking legendary Xander token for each other opponent B,C; both token ETBs make target(s) discard half hand, no token 'attacks' trigger. SBA legend rule removes tokens if original retained, token deaths each sacrifice half nonland; they are not exiled by end combat because already died.
7. **Auton Soldier line**: Auton enters copy Xander as NONlegendary with myriad, produces initial half-hand ETB. Following turn original Auton-Xander attacks A and itself triggers Xander half-library; myriad copies tapped attacking B,C also ETB discard half but DO NOT Xander attack. End combat tokens EXILE, so no Xander 'dies' sacrifice trigger.
8. **Helm vs Blade**: original Xander controlled with Helm equipped (4 to cast,5 equip), begin combat copy hasty nonlegendary Xander enters (discards half hand) and can be declared attacking this combat (mills half opponent library), unlike Blade token. Cost is 9 + existing Xander, so not an "early free trigger".
9. **Bruvac parity**: opponent library 41, Xander attack base mills 20, Bruvac doubles to 40, leaving one. Milling is not instant death if no draw attempted; does not fully defeat opponent absent subsequent draw, second mill or Guildmage drain. Against library 40 it mills 40 exactly, yet player remains alive until draw obligation. A reshuffle titan graveyard trigger can change state if not countered/exiled.
10. **Cruel Calculations**: target library-to-GY count 34 (Xander trigger and another effect). Cast {2}{U} draws 34, not 34 just because library changed size; if Rest in Peace replacement exiled cards instead, counted cards to GY may be zero. Large draw risks self-deck and opens response to Notion Thief.
11. **Sword Assault payable loop**: Xander attacks with Sword equipped, attack halves player A's library regardless of blockers. Only if Sword creature *deals combat damage to a player* do lands untap; postcombat main {3}{R}{R} Assault untaps creatures and grants additional combat and main. Must control lands producing at least 5 and recast activation each main; if blockers absorb damage, no untap and loop breaks.
12. **Seize vs Karlach**: Xander survives first attack. At postcombat main pay {3}{R} Seize targeting Xander -> untap and add combat/another main after current main; Xander declares attack again, milling half remaining library (e.g. 40->20->10). Later flashback {2}{R} from graveyard during a later main grants another. Karlach's extra combat requires Karlach itself attacking with at least one other creature in FIRST combat on that turn; at five mana creature setup. Seize is one-shot targeting Xander, which Greaves shroud blocks; Karlach may be better when deck already has reliable double attackers or an instant Xander turn.
13. **Gamble/Necromancy risk**: you cannot search Xander from CZ with Gamble. You can search `Necromancy` support or `Command Beacon` if included; after putting the card in hand, random discard can lose it. No assumption that any discard leads to desired commander GY.
14. **Wheel resource adversarial**: with Notion Thief absent, Wheel makes each opponent discard and draw 7, potentially refilling the very opponent Xander was draining. With Thief in play, replacements affect their draws; but enemy removal in response exposes full 7-card refill. M5 `Windfall` 'pass' result doesn't capture this.
15. **Free counter response**: Xander enters/attacks on YOUR turn, opponent casts exile removal. `Force of Negation` cannot be cast for its alternate no-mana pitch cost then, only by paying 1UU; `Force of Will` can pitch a blue card and life on either turn; `Fierce Guardianship` may cast free because Xander present. `Pact` free now but 3UU debt next upkeep can lose.
16. **Dimir Guildmage/Mindcrank**: Guildmage first ability activated for 1UB and resolves for turn; seed opponent GY card (e.g. via Xander declared attack) causes lose 1, Mindcrank mills 1, triggers Guildmage, repeats while opponent has library/life and permanents remain. If only limited cards left, loop stops with graveyard exhausted; it doesn't always guarantee kill from high life on tiny library.
17. **Graveyard hate scenario**: Rest in Peace or opponent Bojuka Bog after Lord dies/exile effect blocks `Reanimate`, `Necromancy`, `Goryo`, `Feign Death` if commander doesn't remain GY, and Breach. Best baseline answer is hard-cast via rocks, or preserve protection, not claim an easy 2-mana reanimation.
18. **Copy counterplay**: Krosan Grip/equipment removal before activation of Helm, Sword or Blade disrupts attack multiplier; removal of Xander before resolving targeting `Saw in Half` fails all conditional tokens; against Roaming Throne killing it before Lord attack triggers removes doubling, while killing after triggers already on stack does not undouble those triggers. Timing explicit.
19. **No-legend anti-synergy**: Hall of Echoes has been activated copy Xander (5 mana), so no legend rule for own permanents that turn. Play Phantasmal Image Xander for 1U and expect death trigger? No; both persist until something actually dies. Hall grants an extra attack ability if not tapped and was controlled long enough. Net impact depends on which Xander half is desired, not simply 'Hall always upgrades clones'.
20. **M5 supposedly positive swap**: replace Lotus Petal with Commander's Sphere because the simulator logged a `pass`; opening turn Petal provides a single chosen missing U/B/R immediately at zero mana, whereas Sphere requires three mana first and taps thereafter. Sorcery-speed M5 model does not represent haste, opponent interaction, source timing; no commander-dependent causal inference.
21. **Opponent three-player target distribution**: Xander attacks A and triggers A mill; his ETB discarded B, and his death trigger targets C. A single Xander life can pressure all three individually, but no single trigger targets all. Mill-only finishers need player-by-player plan; claims of one-attack whole-table mill without independent effects are wrong.
