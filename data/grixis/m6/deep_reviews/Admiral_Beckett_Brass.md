## Commander identity and natural ceiling

Admiral Beckett Brass is a four-mana Grixis Human Pirate; its end-step trigger steals target nonland permanent controlled by a player who was dealt **combat damage by three or more distinct Pirate creatures that turn**. Count individual Pirate damage sources, not damage points or number of opponents. Those three Pirates must have damaged the SAME player during the current turn, possibly in different combats; Beckett itself can be a Pirate attacker. The +1/+1 anthem affects OTHER Pirates only. On successful theft, control change has no printed expiration.

Core strategy is early evasive Pirate development, token generation, removing blockers, combat damage into Treasure/cards, repeated end-step permanent theft. Current shell is Pirate-combat-dependent, not a general steal-everything Grixis control deck. Compact recursion, protection, and burst mana make a focused Bracket 4 optimized ceiling plausible; the commander cannot be assumed cEDH simply because tutors and free stack interaction are present. Remove commander or Pirate board and its main win engine genuinely disappears.

**Connection package:** Changeling Outcast and Storm Fleet Sprinter are unblockable; Spyglass Siren, Spectral Sailor, Stormtamer, both Malcolms, Zephyr Singer, Freebooter, Larcenist, Zara, Warkite, Francisco fly; Kari Zev/Fathom Fleet menace; Open into Wonder can make precisely three Pirates unblockable at **XUU (X=3 costs five)**. Cover of Darkness gives fear, which black or artifact blockers can still block. Daring Piracy creates hasty menace Pirate before attackers are declared. Fathom Fleet Captain makes a Pirate token while attacking but NOT stated tapped and attacking; the token normally connects a future combat.

**Engine and conversion:** Roaming Throne choosing Pirate is itself a Pirate and causes Beckett's creature triggered theft to trigger twice, permitting TWO different permanent theft targets if qualified. It does not double Double Down enchantment. Double Down copies Pirate spell casts; nontoken Pirate copies resolve as token creatures, but legendary Pirate copies may be lost to legend rule. Coercive Recruiter grants temporarily stolen enemy creatures Pirate status, haste, untaps them, so they can become qualifying hits. Don Andres grants opponent-owned creatures you control Pirate status, menace and deathtouch. Edward Kenway generates Treasure for tapped Pirates end step; Kindred Discovery draws on Pirate attack/entry, Indomitable and Bident on combat hits. Zephyr Singer convoke taps creatures before its flying-counter ETB, so tapped convokers often cannot swing that turn; counters assist FUTURE combats.

**Interaction/resilience:** Mana Drain, Counterspell, Fierce Guardianship, Deflecting Swat, Siren Stormtamer protect the connection; Chaos Warp, Cyclonic Rift, Feed the Swarm and Kitesail Larcenist clear key blockers/locks. Fiery Cannonade hits non-Pirates, Raise the Palisade bounces non-Pirates, Toxic Deluge can kill your own army. March of the Drowned / Admiral Brass Unsinkable recover Pirates but with restrictions. Three hits can be stopped by one timely blocker, Fog, removal or end-step sacrifice of the intended permanent. The deck has few unconditional postwipe rebuild tools.

**Proof guards:** Three DIFFERENT Pirate creatures damaging the SAME player, not three damage from one creature/double strike or one Pirate per different opponent; damage is COMBAT and this TURN. Roaming Throne choosing Pirate is itself Pirate and duplicates *other Pirate creature* Beckett trigger (not enchantments). Copies of legendary Pirates created by Double Down trigger legend rule and cannot all survive. Kari Zev's ephemeral Ragavan token is Monkey-only while Ragavan Nimble Pilferer printed card is Monkey Pirate. Fathom Fleet generated Pirate does not enter attacking. Daring Piracy token hasty and created start combat can attack this turn even though exiled next end step. Zephyr convokers tapped before combat cannot attack without untap/haste; its flying counters are permanent. The Indomitable Vehicle non-Pirate needs three tapped Pirates/Vehicles to cast from grave *and normal 2UU mana/timing*, cannot count by merely having attacked last turn. Port Razer's extra combat trigger only when it hits a player, and it cannot attack same player again that turn; other Pirates may. Cover of Darkness fear is not unconditional unblockable. Coercive Recruiter grants type+haste only until end turn, allowing a one-turn stolen creature to qualify. Countering or destroying commander BEFORE beginning of end step prevents theft trigger; removing it AFTER trigger goes on stack doesn't erase already-on-stack trigger. Theft targets eligible opponent permanents and they must still be legal on resolution.

## Finite sequencing and adversarial scenarios

**Scenario 1 (double theft):** Beckett + Roaming Throne choosing Pirate + two other Pirates are present and all THREE connect with opponent A this turn. At beginning end step Beckett triggers twice. Target two distinct nonland permanents controlled by A, steal each when its own trigger resolves if it remains legal. If only two Pirates connected, no Beckett theft; if only one theft target, the second trigger has no additional legal target unless another qualifying target exists (the same one could be chosen twice, but would usually become an illegal target of one trigger after the other resolves). Roaming Throne does not make two Pirates count as three.

**Scenario 2 (tokens):** Your Changeling Outcast and Spyglass Siren are ready, Daring Piracy creates a haste Pirate before declare attackers, all 3 deal combat damage to same opponent -> end-step steal. If flying Siren is blocked, two successful sources are insufficient. Daring token's scheduled end-step exile does not undo damage history.

**Scenario 3 (mana):** With three other untapped mana-producing lands and Necropolis as fourth land drop, Necropolis tapped means 3 available mana this turn. With City of Brass in same position, total 4 mana and one self-damage upon City tap; a four-MV UBR Beckett may be cast assuming other sources cover all pips. Probability of drawing the changed singleton among ten seen without replacement = 10/99 ≈ 10.10%, NOT a measured win-rate/cast-on-turn gain.

**Scenario 4 (convoke):** Three attack-ready Pirates tapped to convoke a Zephyr Singer before combat gain flying counters but cannot attack that turn without untapping, undercutting immediate 3-hit trigger; casting postcombat or using summoning-sick bodies to convoke may be preferable.

**Scenario 5 (antagonist):** A Fog effect, removal of a third Pirate before combat damage, artifact/black blocker vs Cover fear, Rule of Law restricting multiple Pirate casts, opposing board wipe, and end-step sacrificing a targeted permanent can respectively stop same-player 3 hits or deny an actual steal. Do not treat M5 goldfish pass proxies as measured live-turn success. No multiplayer effect-accurate test, source-replay comparison, or colored-mana simulation conducted; improvement claim is source-Oracle-tempo analysis only.
