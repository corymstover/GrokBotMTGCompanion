## Commander identity and natural ceiling

**Commander Oracle:** Original `Tetsuo Umezawa`, {U}{B}{R} Human Archer, **can't be the target of Aura SPELLS** but can be targeted by Equipment equip, AURA *abilities* and moved Auras; has **{U}{B}{B}{R}, {T}: destroy target TAPPED OR BLOCKING creature**. Cost has **NO generic component**. On paying you require U + TWO B + R, plus Tetsuo untapped and able to pay tap ability (sick unless haste/elixir). A tapped attacker qualifies, and an UNtapped blocker still qualifies; an already-tapped blocker also qualifies. Any target ceasing to be tapped/blocking before resolution may become illegal; hexproof or protection also restricts targeting. **Tetsuo cannot target planeswalker, untapped nonblocking creature, or opponent**.

The identity is **create legal tap/block conditions** (Flood, Puppeteer, Rathi Trapper, Blustersquall, Frozen Aether, forced combat and goad); **pay repeated four colored mana** for surgical destruction; **untap or give haste** (Minamo, Magewright, Elixir, Aphetto, Fatestitcher, Seal, Retreat, Sting, Boots/Greaves); and **extract cards/damage from attacks/taps** (Verity Circle, Arrows, Firkraag/Karazikar/Grenzo, political pressure). This is not `Tetsuo, Imperial Champion` (equipment/spells attack damage); don't homogenize into Grixis generic combo even if it raises raw power. The real natural ceiling is political control with high colored-mana cost and opponent-creature dependence. Copy activations with Bracers/Rings, but unless one of the combos additionally creates colored mana, an unlimited Tetsuo untap does not equal unlimited UBBR activations.

**Commander-protected sources:** low MV colored Signets/Talismans; Sting/Seal and at least six independent untap pieces; reusable Flood/Puppeteer and board-wide Blustersquall target-maker; forced combat/political commander-specific Firkraag, Karazikar, Red Death, Kardur, Fumiko; Tetsuo and Royal Assassin; cheap counters and instant removal so the three-drop survives while mana reserved. **Do NOT cut Aphetto Alchemist for unrelated self-target mill, lose Sting, or remove Talisman for a five/six-mana mana rock** based on effect-blind simulator.

### Mana/curve/target access, weak slots and protected cuts

Raw source Stage C: 35 lands + 1 modal flexible land, six source-model repeated basic groups (the three basic land names); **5 fetches** Bloodstained/Flooded/Marsh/Misty/Polluted; mana audit `data/grixis/mana_base/tetsuo-umezawa.json` reports fetchlands **5**, but observed actual approved list also includes `Scalding Tarn`? Reconciled enumeration above: no Scalding Tarn in approved TXT, thus five. 5 conditionally tapped /2 always tapped lands, 11 repeatable nonland sources, 11 fast-mana model labels + one ritual. Land source B18/R15/U17; incl rocks B25/R23/U24, all measured as **source presence** not actual cast odds. Five basic Islands, five Mountains and SIX Swamps support repeat activations B B. No-mulligan turn3 at least three natural land drops and all-three-color land-source presence should not be multiplied as independent nor interpreted as commander cast probability; source commander on turn3 all-three-color presence **0.659**, but 3 real mana simultaneously & untapped are separately required. Maze of Ith does NOT tap for mana; Xander's Lounge and Bojuka Bog are always tapped. The MDFC enters tapped as land, so 36 functional land-capable does not mean 36 untapped colored lands. Mana Vault rejected as source candidate because its CCC produces no U/B/R for commander cast OR activation, and extra rocks must not displace two-mana Talismans.

**Removal versus setup:** Source Tetsuo activation and Royal Assassin require legal tap/block state. Tap-makers count ~Flood, Puppeteer, Rathi Trapper, Hylda Crown, Blustersquall, Retreat, Karazikar; Goad/forced attacks count ~Disrupt, Fumiko, Kardur, Geode, Red Death, Bloodthirsty Blade, Shiny Impetus, Firkraag and Grenzo. But goad does not physically tap when cast; opponent may have vigilance, pay attack taxes, be unable to attack, or remove creature before declaring attack. Verity Circle only draws when an opponent creature becomes tapped **NOT while being declared an attacker**, so goad ATTACK taps are not draw triggers. Borrowing 100,000 Arrows at sorcery timing can draw many after opponents have left tapped creatures, but opponents usually untap before your next main; strongest with your own tap effects DURING your turn. Deluge/Damnation/Act potentially remove all own untap creatures, so emergency only. Singletons Bedevil, Terminate, Withering Torment, Chaos Warp address nontapped creature/enchantment/planeswalker/artifact problems the commander misses.

**Relative weak slots:** Geode Rager six mana landfall goad, Fumiko four mana attack-forcing with no on-enter effect, Frozen Aether four mana stax/tapped entrants, Verity Circle three mana delayed draw, Borrowing Arrows conditional sorcery, Propaganda protection not target maker, Urabrask five mana expensive haste/ETB tapped, Red Death gives opponent card whenever using its target-making mana producing ability, Firkraag and Karazikar expensive political threats, and Xantcha life-pressure slower. Their retained rationale is identity-relevant redundancy/attacker tempo/draw. Hylda's Crown costs 3 then can tap one creature on your turn for FREE generic activation (needs `{T}`), and can later sac for drawn cards per opposing tapped creature; losing it to Wheel removes a commander-specific target maker. Gilded Lotus is not obviously superior to **Red Death** (UR 2-mana repeatable goad and R generation while giving card) or early **Talisman of Creativity** (two-mana immediate UR fixing). Strongest benign cut candidate in a fast noncreature pod: Xantcha or redundant Fumiko; but trading native table pressure for generic top-end changes the deck's identity and nothing in M5 reliably quantifies improvement.

**Insufficient deterministic win proof:** Bracers+Aphetto Alchemist alone lets you repeatedly untap Alchemist plus another artifact/creature, but not automatically gain mana. Add a **tappable artifact mana source** (Arcane Signet, Dimir/Izzet/Rakdos Signet with input, Talismans, or Sol Ring depending colored capacity) and the pair can create unlimited mana as long as appropriate source/activation costs allow positive production. For instance, with Arcane Signet tapped/untapped profitably, each Alchemist activation resets Alchemist and Signet to produce one U/B/R mana per cycle; can generate arbitrary colored mana. With Tetsuo and one repeatable tap-target/untap line, can destroy every legal tapped/blocking opposing creature, but WITHOUT ability to make untapped creatures legal targets, can't kill every board creature. No compact intrinsic table kill in source list; artifact combo is still on-theme repeat-use tool, not a reason to convert generic Thoracle.

### Head-to-head alternatives, including more sensible cuts

1. **Gilded Lotus P0** replacing Red Death or Talisman is not acceptable merely because a model marks the three-mana tap as a repeatable source. True positive-case opening: you already have five mana and THREE missing black pips across Tetsuo ability plus counters, Lotus on next turn can add BBB; source actual Tetsuo needs UBBR, so Lotus tap BBB still lacks U and R from lands. If one extra mana artifact could be supported, the most reasonable *other* cut is six-mana `Geode Rager` when landfall goad rarely triggers or expensive `Urabrask the Hidden` in lower-creature pods. Those alternatives lose commander-native target density/haste and are NOT approved.
2. **Wheel of Fortune P1** vs Crown is an outsized identity loss. Testing Wheel against `Borrowing 100,000 Arrows` could be fairer where opponents rarely remain tapped until sorcery speed, or `Geode Rager` where slow; but Wheel gives each player seven and can supply opponent answers to Tetsuo. Under a predictable goad-heavy table, Crown draws multiple cards and supplies tap targets, whereas a red wheel has zero repeatable tap function. `KEEP`, not a new proposal.
3. **Cephalid Aristocrat P1** vs Alchemist has no relevant card advantage: getting targeted causes YOU to mill2, and the list lacks graveyard recursion. Alternative swapping Xantcha is less catastrophic but still a five-mana mill-only creature without commander synergy.
4. **Chromatic Orrery** is the hardest off-core ramp case because its spend-as-any-color static bypasses mana color limitation while it stays; however its 7-mana entry is expensive for a three-mana commander and loses earlier UR colored development if Talisman cut. Testing it vs Urabrask or Fumiko is more defensible, but still four-mana Tetsuo activation repeatedly needs draw/target creation. Source packet null/inconclusive is not proof.
5. **Rimewind Taskmage**, **Enigma Jewel**, and **Magosi** are mechanically constrained even before comparing abstract model metrics: without four snow permanents Taskmage is blank; Jewel cannot pay UBBR with restricted colorless; Magosi's extra turn explicitly requires forfeiting a preceding turn. No viable approved cut exists without a new supported plan.

**No more ranked card automatically rises to `PROPOSE_SWAP_FOR_CORY_APPROVAL`** under this test model. Unmodeled artifacts, even with high raw score, are not proof of better command-specific win conversion.

### Off-ranked/previously rejected serious alternatives

- **Opposition:** UUU enchant tap any creature you control to tap opponent artifact/creature/land, enormously strong if board full but creature-light 3-drop controller with many noncreature spells can't reliably fuel it. Flood/Puppeteer have cleaner and less board-dependent targeted commander utility. Alternative to Fumiko would cost blue pips and limit color structure.
- **Norritt:** 3B blue-untap + target attack goad old source trick, but Puppeteer at 2U can tap OR untap any creature while Norritt only untaps BLUE creatures; Tetsuo UBR qualifies blue. Cost/fragility high.
- **Thornbite Staff:** untaps equipped creature when another creature dies, potentially with Tetsuo first kill; equip costs, need UBBR for every activation and source of initial tapped target. Once loop online it can grind, but Sting/Seal/Minamo target the same bottleneck more smoothly without per-kill event condition.
- **Tamiyo, the Moon Sage:** five mana UU planeswalker tap target permanent and keep tapped plus card draw from tapped boards; expensive, fragile, less removal than one-mana Blustersquall/cheap Crown.
- **War's Toll:** forced attacks/land stax, symmetric political risk and generic anti-fun stax without extra Tetsuo colored mana. Budget of payoffs already tight.
- **Ghoulish Impetus / Parasitic Impetus:** single-target goad Aura with stat/treasure incidental, but Bloodthirsty Blade, Shiny Impetus and board-wide Disrupt already give balanced redundancy; not a material upgrade without a named cut.
- **No Mercy:** enemies hitting you destroys their creature AFTER damage, defensive not proactive tapped target manufacturing; commander wants active tap or blocking targets.
- **Mana Vault:** one-mana CCC advances expensive generic permanents but never helps pay `{U}{B}{R}` commander or `{U}{B}{B}{R}` ability. Fast ramp not equivalent to native repeated removal.

## Oracle, legality and rules

Source 2026-10-15 official 100-card legality and color identity verified, 2026-10-16 Scryfall Oracle + 2026-09-25 WotC CR five interaction groups, plus card-specific targeted checks cited above. **All significant mechanics below are SOURCE-BOUND, not from M5 simulation.** No new full 2026-10-20 global legality refresh, any unexpected new ban/Oracle drift should be reviewed before owner changes.

**Targets and activation:** Tetsuo requires tapped OR blocking (independent) on announcement AND resolution; removing tap with untap invalidates a tapped nonblocking target, while untapped blocker remains eligible until its blocking status ends. Hexproof/shroud/protection from ability source stop targeting even when tapped. Only Aura SPELLS cannot target Tetsuo; Equipment equip, Planar Incision targeting and other targeted abilities may. `Frozen Aether` forces new opposing creatures to enter tapped (including ones with vigilance), `Blustersquall` overload has no target and taps all opposing creatures (can hit hexproof), but then Tetsuo STILL must individually target them and cannot hit hexproof. Goad means must attack if able, and must attack player other than goader if able, but if vigilance they remain untapped after attacking and aren't blocking, so no automatic Tetsuo legal target; before declare attackers they may be untapped.

**Repeatable untap:** `Sting` untaps equipped Tetsuo at beginning of EACH combat, every player's turn; this is not a free new UBBR payment and occurs BEFORE attacks, so you may need a previously tapped creature, Flood, blocking target from prior combat not now available, or instant tap effect after attackers. `Patriar's Seal` taps to produce color mana OR taps with 1 generic to untap Tetsuo, not both same tap. `Minamo` U,tap untaps Tetsuo as LEGENDARY. `Retreat` gets two separate landfalls for fetch then fetched land; on each can tap opposing creature or untap Tetsuo, but search land must actually enter. `Lightning Greaves` shroud protects commander but also prohibits Minamo/Puppeteer targeting until un-equipped; `Swiftfoot Boots` hexproof still permits controller own target. `Thousand-Year Elixir` lets new creature use tap abilities and itself can untap once, but it doesn't give haste for attacks. `Urabrask` gives haste and forces opponent creatures entered tapped.

**Copies and infinite line:** Equip `Illusionist's Bracers` onto Tetsuo, one UBBR tap activation can be copied for second legal target; `Rings of Brighthearth` pay2 on same activation adds another copy, so up to THREE removal resolutions from one Tetsuo tap payment (provided three legal targets, not automatically when only one). Equipping Bracers on **Aphetto Alchemist**: tap Alchemist choosing SELF, put Bracers copy above original and choose untap another artifact/creature (such as Arcane Signet); copy untaps rock, original untaps Alchemist, permitting repetition. Tapping Arcane Signet between cycles produces one mana of any Grixis color per repeat if Alchemist has been under control since beginning of turn (or has haste/elixir). Merely these two cards create repeatable untaps but **not** mana without a tappable productive source. Repeated Tetsuo kills require UBBR available AND Tetsuo reuntapped, AND targets tapped/blocking; even arbitrary mana not sufficient alone if targets never meet conditions. No fabricated infinite damage/guaranteed win.

**Goad and draw:** `Red Death` {T} goads opposing creature then allows opponent to draw1 and gives you R on resolve; unlike land mana ability it targets and USES STACK, can be responded to or fizzle if creature removed. `Verity Circle` only triggers when an opponent creature becomes tapped **without attacking**, so tapped on goaded attack DOESN'T produce draw; Flood/Puppeteer/Crown and Karazikar's attack trigger (tapping blocker) can create qualifying events. `Borrowing 100,000 Arrows` counts target opponent TAPPED creatures at sorcery resolution; doesn't tap them or count all three opponents. `Hylda's Crown` {1},tap target creature for 1 generic cheaper during YOUR turn => tap-only on your turn, instant target manufacturing without spending colored mana after 3-mana setup, and 3 sacrifice to draw number of tapped OPPONENT creatures. `Karazikar` attack at a player taps one target their creature (its player is the player attacked), goads it; when opponents attack OTHER opponents both parties may draw/lose1 depending trigger. `Red Death` opponent draws cards (and you get R) only if target remains legal; cannot ignore gift to enemies in resource-limited table.

**MDFC/tempo:** Sink into Stupor spell face returns TARGET spell to owner HAND, not counter to graveyard, and/or returns nonland permanent to hand; land face Soporific Springs is a tapped blue source that cannot be cast as instant when played as land. Important if opponent re-casts a spell. Full 100 audit still singleton with six Swamps and five each Islands/Mountains. No defined project exception.
