## Commander identity and natural ceiling
**Garza**: `{4}{U}{B}{R}` = **SEVEN** mana 5/5 **FLYING, HASTE**, Vampire Noble. Whenever a creature **dealt damage by Garza during that turn** DIES, she gets +1/+1 counter. Doesn't require lethal damage itself or same moment; does require Garza be source and creature actually DIE (graveyard, not exile replacement). Whenever Garza deals **COMBAT DAMAGE TO PLAYER** may draw ONE. Damaging a planeswalker, dealing NONCOMBAT to an opponent, or Sword of Fire and Ice dealing its own damage do NOT trigger her combat draw. Combat Garza flying/haste already has evasive/fast attacks without boots.
**Natural ceiling:** B4 — Optimized, tags **Voltron / Commander Damage / Deathtouch-Control**. Keep Garza as the DAMAGE SOURCE. Thorns: Collar/Quietus deathtouch + Staff/Chandra/Nova/Fall/Soul's Fire deal her damage to opposing creatures and grow her; counters retained Ozolith; equipment protects/evasion and boosts 21 commander damage; alternate infect via Exoskeleton+Kediss; Sword/Assault or Reaver/Assault extra combats. Opponent removal/edicts and escalating commander tax genuinely cap bracket.
**Anti-homogenization:** don't add unrelated Oracle/Consult cEDH or random Vampire tribal counters; generic 6MV creature value doesn't express Garza's damage-source text. A card improves this deck when it increases *Garza sourced* damage to creatures, connects reliably, grants meaningful protection/evasion, improves 7MV source-paid arrival, or finishes through her.

### Mana, curve, value, protection and wins
**38 distinct lands**, including colorless Tomb/Command Beacon/Rogue's Passage, conditional Cavern/Plaza of Heroes and early typed duals/rainbows, fetches, black and blue painlands and 3 single basics. Bojuka Bog and Xander's Lounge tapped, lands with activations (Rogue's Passage 4,T, Shizo B,T) consume mana needed to hold protection or equip. Seven-mana commander needs **4UBR**, and re-cast after one commander death normally **9MV**. Rocks Sol Ring, Mana Vault, Grim Monolith, Arcane Signet, Fellwar Stone, 3 Signets, 3 Talismans, Ancient Tomb and Jeska's Will 2R burst; Talisman colors not generic source interchangeable when playing UU counter +RR Nova/Aggravated. Command Beacon converts next Garza cast to FROM HAND at printed cost; does not count as command zone casting. No exact 7MV colored-source opening hand Monte Carlo claimed.
**Damage engine**: Staff {2},tap ping kills with Collar/Quietus deathtouch; on creature death Staff UNTAPS Garza, then two MORE generic must be paid per next ping. Garza herself gets one +1 counter per damaged creature that actually died. Chandra's Ignition 3RR Garza-sourced damage to each other creature and opponent; Nova Flame {X}{2}{R}{R} FIRST puts X counters on Garza and deals Garza CURRENT power to each OTHER CREATURE (NO opponent damage); both cost and target matter. Fall of Hammer and Soul's Fire give targeted damage; can target creature and profit counter or opponent and profit Curiosity, but only combat hit yields Garza draw. Scythe can steal killed creature then MOVE itself away from Garza, so it is burst conversion not stable permanent stat boost.
**Cards/interaction**: Garza combat may draw1, Curiosity can draw on ANY Garza damage to opponent including her Kediss splash, Robe of Archmagi draws equal combat damage but EQUIP4 to Vampire Noble (not 1), Mask draws2/discard1, Sword of Fire/Ice separate artifact-source ping & card; Rhystic Study and tutors protect against draw-starvation. Free Fierce/Swat/Deadly with Garza, UU hard counters, cheap Offer/Song? only Offer among list, Chaos Warp, Feed, Pongify, Rapid Hybrid, Terminate, Sheoldred Edict and Deluge. If opponent removes Garza before Ignition resolves, targeted spell fizzes; protection can't assume boots solve every edict. One-creature Voltron can still be stopped by fog, opponent untargeted exile, sacrifice or 2+ consecutive removal.
**Protection/evasion**: Mithril Coat indestructible from destroy, Swiftfoot hexproof & haste (haste redundant but hexproof valued), Brotherhood Regalia ward+unblockable, Commander's Plate protects from WHITE/GREEN only (UBR can still remove), Slip phased creature absent combat that turn, Rogue/Shizo/Trailblazer evasion and flying intrinsic. High-cost equipment piles make pre-combat time/mana a limiting factor; not enough to simply count equipment copies.
**Kill packages**: 21 same-commander **combat** damage to each player; Hatred instant can give +16 power to native 5/5 for 21 damage at 16 LIFE paid if enough life and no damage prevention; Jeska triple 8/8 from Commander's Plate => 24 commander combat damage; Exoskeleton gives infect and with Garza >=10 effective damage, Kediss transfers that amount of GARZA-sourced NONCOMBAT infect to other opponents so 10 poison each (not commander damage). `Kediss` triggers on a successful combat hit only; spreading Curiosity draws is a separate reward. Extra combat `Assault` repeated only when hit with Sword SoFF untaps enough lands for 3RR, or Reaver Cleaver creates at least five usable Treasure from actual successful combat damage.
**Curve risk**: only Garza as critical high-threat, equipment and sweep sorceries often demand 2–5 mana per piece AFTER 7-mana commander. Doubling her power without evasion doesn't beat a 1/1 flier; handing opponents a 3/3 token via Pongify can stall some combat lines. Meaningful cuts should target redundant high-investment equipment with similar roles while retaining protection/land count, not source-paid draw/protection just for a different expensive creature.

## Oracle, legality and rules
1. **Garza** officially 4UBR 5/5 haste flying: whenever CREATURE dealt damage by **Garza herself** THIS TURN dies, +1/+1 counter Garza, not on every creature death; dies battlefield→GY requires no grave exile replacement. Combat damage to player (not any damage) MAY draw1.
2. **Thornbite Staff** equip4; tap activated ability costs `{2},T`: Garza deals 1 to any target; when ANY creature dies Staff's equipped-creature granted trigger UNTAPS Garza. Deathtouch from Collar/Spike means a point from Garza to creature is lethal; after each kill need an ADDITIONAL TWO mana to activate Staff again. No arbitrary infinite ping unless another real mana engine pays two each time. Staff granted untap also sees **other players'** creature deaths, not only victim of last ping.
3. **Nova Flame** official 2026 Marvel notes: `{X}{2}{R}{R}`, X +1/+1 counter on your targeted Garza BEFORE Garza deals her now-current POWER to each other CREATURE, not to opponents. X=0 valid for 4 mana board wipe with deathtouch. If target Garza illegal upon resolution, no counters/no damage. Contrast `Chandra's Ignition` {3}{R}{R} also deals Garza power to EACH OPPONENT and each other creature, yielding noncombat opponent damage that can trigger Curiosity. The same single Nova Flame action may kill many creatures and Garza receives a SEPARATE +1 trigger for each it actually damaged that dies.

## Conclusions and unresolved evidence
**KEEP exact 100 locked cards.** The strongest alternatives explored are cheap pinger/deathtouch redundancy (**Viridian Longbow**, **Gorgon's Head**, **Rune of Mortality**) and one high-mana repeat tap damage Aura (**Burning Anger**). They enhance Garza's own damage text but replacing Scythe/Quietus/Robe has genuine costs: stealing killed creatures, halving life, drawing 5+ cards on a direct hit. **No unsupported ranking implies those cuts are now mandatory.** Important original-role findings:
- Garza 7MV is not "fast cEDH" even with Mana Vault/Monolith; B4 is appropriate to her natural ceiling.
- Thornbite Staff is pay-two-per-ping, not mana-free, and death untap from ANY creature can give exceptional timing.
- Scythe actually MOVES off Garza when recursion works, so it is not a stable power/equip engine on a board wipe.
- Robe of Archmagi costs EQUIP4 for a Vampire Noble, not the equip1 alternate; it is one credible high-investment flex slot against fast-removal metagames.
- Kediss spread is Garza-sourced NONCOMBAT (infect yes, commander damage no), and Curiosity recognizes the spread.
**Strongest counterargument to KEEP:** `Viridian Longbow` cheaper per ping could dramatically outperform the expensive Scythe capture slot in pods where deathtouch kills and +1 counters decide games more than opponent creature recursion, but the lack of untap and loss of +2 power/capture makes matchup-dependent rather than obvious. Need paired tests before asking Cory approval.
