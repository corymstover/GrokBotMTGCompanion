## Commander identity and natural ceiling
Doctor Doom, King of Latveria is a `{1}{U}{B}{R}` **3/3 legendary creature — Human Noble Villain**. Oracle: (1) whenever YOU DISCARD **ONE OR MORE land cards** in a discrete event, EACH opponent loses 2 life; (2) at the beginning of combat ON YOUR TURN, **TARGET VILLAIN you control** gains menace and connives. Doom may target HIMSELF because he is a Villain. Each discard event is counted once, not each discarded land in it; Faithless Looting, Frantic Search, Wheel, Windfall may dump multiple lands in one event and still cause only one Doom trigger. Multiple separately resolved connives/activated discard costs are more efficient.
**Protect:** self-discarded lands as repeated resource ammo; *separate* events, not hand size lost; Villain+connive core with proper combat targets; Moonstone/Containment/Crucible/Dakmor/Shadow as distinct reuse constraints; Doom-copy engines and fast enough mana/protection. **Natural ceiling Bracket 4 Optimized**, tags **Land Discard / Villains / Connive**. Natural alternate finisher a source-paid, library-bounded Dakmor loop rather than unrelated Consultation/Oracle cEDH shell.

### Mana, curve and role pressure
**38 lands is purposeful**, not automatically too many: Doom's discard outlets consume them as ammunition. Only **Island, Mountain, Swamp one EACH** among 38 lands; fetched duals, checklands and pain lands must solve 1UBR, while RRR Seismic Assault is distinctly harder. `Villainous Hideout` supplies colorless by default, with colored ANY restricted only to **casting Villain spells or activating abilities of Villain sources**; thus it can pay part of Doom's Villain casting cost but NOT generically pay red for Tectonic Reformation's **granted cycling ability** unless activation is of a Villain source (it isn't). `Ancient Tomb` is colorless, and `Dakmor Salvage` tapped black is a real tempo cost. `Sunken Hollow` with only three basic-land cards enters tapped frequently unless conditions actually met. This is a source-count caution, not a measured colored-access probability.
**Ramp:** Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Fellwar, three Talismans, Ancient Tomb, conditional Black Market Connections and Jeska's Will; Treasure from Big Score/Unexpected Windfall and Badgersaur. Bone Miser BB upon discarding land is real but cannot pay U for Trade Routes. **Engines:** 1-2 MV Trade Routes / Tectonic Reformation / Psychic Frog; turn 4 Doom; 4MV Moonstone, Surly Badgersaur, Chameleon and Spark Double; 5MV Bone Miser / Archfiend; many four-plus-mana characters compete before hand is stocked with land fuel. **Card advantage:** Looting, Frantic Search, cycling lands, Change of Fortune, Wheel/Windfall, Black Market Connections, Monument to Endurance, Green Goblin/connive suite. **Protection/removal:** Fierce Guardianship, Deflecting Swat, Swan Song, Chaos Warp, Feed the Swarm, Cyclonic Rift, Toxic Deluge, Vandalblast and selective creature kills via Lethal Scheme; graveyard hate, enchantment removal to Reformation and Doom creature removal remain credible disruption. **Recovery:** Crucible replays lands in grave ONLY as land drops, Moonstone exiles discarded card for play with timing, Containment Construct similarly but shorter time, Shadow only returns qualifying actual grave cards, Dakmor only dredges from grave; overlap is deliberate but some pieces conflict with preserving Dakmor for dredge.
**Tension points:** bulk wheels do not multiply Doom triggers; multiple Villain top-ends may not add enough separate land-discard events; heavy colorless utility lands restrict fast 1UBR/Seismic RRR. **Credible potential flex slots (not asserted bad):** one high-CMC Marvel Villain such as Loki or Iron Monger if its in-game connive/payoff rate underperforms, or `Demand Answers` if hands often lack spare land; **Doom Reigns Supreme is only {1}{B}, a cheap high-identity Plan rather than expensive filler**; do NOT strip core `Trade Routes`, `Tectonic Reformation`, `Psychic Frog`, `Dakmor`, `Badgersaur`, `Chameleon`, `Roaming Throne`, or `Villainous Hideout` without source-aware counterfactuals.

## Oracle, legality and rules
1. **Doom source:** [card Oracle](https://mtg.wtf/card/msc/6/Doctor-Doom-King-of-Latveria) 3/3 Human Noble Villain, `{1}{U}{B}{R}`, official legal Commander. Trigger `one or more` for one discard event; multi-land simultaneous effects only ONE, separate activations even within same turn each one. Doom's combat connive target is a Villain including himself. No Villain controlled -> no legal target (but Doom himself counts if present); if target illegal on resolution, no connive / no discard.
2. **Connive:** Draw ONE then discard ONE; if nonland discarded place +1/+1 on conniver. Land discard triggers Doom but does NOT place connive +1/+1 (unless other card independently places it). [Wizards 2026 Marvel release notes](https://media.wizards.com/2026/downloads/MSH_Release_Notes_N6hMZoes90/zfGb4BJ8Bf_EN_MTGMSH_ReleaseNotes_20260513.pdf).
3. **Villainous Hideout:** verified **LAND** from Wizards Marvel release notes and [card text](https://mtg.wtf/card/msh/276/Villainous-Hideout). `T: C` OR any color only for casting VILLAIN spell or activating VILLAIN source ability; `3,T` connives TARGET Villain at sorcery speed, draws then discards. Critical audit count + color restriction; card is not just a generic unrestricted tri-land.
4. **Dakmor line:** Tectonic Reformation on BF grants land in HAND `cycling R`; Surly Badgersaur on BF; Doom under controller; Dakmor in hand; pay actual R, discard Dakmor for cycling cost -> Doom triggers table loss2, Badgersaur makes Treasure. With Dakmor in GY on resolving cycling draw, **replace that draw with dredge2** (mill EXACTLY 2; return Dakmor to hand), then spend Treasure for R and repeat. This is `near-infinite` in [Commander Spellbook](https://commanderspellbook.com/combo/779-2546-4467/) only while library has >=2 cards, dredge still legally available, permanents survive and opponent doesn't break loop. With 40 life and 3 opponents, twenty separate events each doing 2 loss to EACH is lethal, *provided library has enough cards for at least 19 dredges after first cycle* and no prevention/life gain. It does not inherently generate infinite mana or stay active without library to dredge. Removing/replacing Badgersaur before it triggers breaks one-for-one Treasure funding; graveyard exile of Dakmor breaks dredge; other draw effects used after loop may be deck-out risk. If Doom is absent, same engine mills without killing opponents.
5. **Monument to Endurance:** [Oracle/ruling](https://mtg.wtf/card/dft/237/Monument-to-Endurance): every CARD discarded triggers an ability, choose one mode **not yet chosen this TURN** among draw, Treasure, each opponent loses3; after THREE have resolved choosing different modes, later triggers that turn are removed from stack with no effect. Never an unlimited damage/mana resource in Dakmor loop. The available trigger count from bulk discard CAN exceed Doom's single trigger.
6. **Crucible / Moonstone / Construct:** Crucible plays land from GY under regular land drop/timing, not cycle from grave. Moonstone and Construct may exile discarded Dakmor for short-term playing privileges, which prevents it being in grave for dredge. Neither grants a second additional land play. Shadow of the Grave returns only cards that were put in grave from HAND during turn and that remain in GY, not exiled by other cards.
7. **Seismic Assault:** `{R}{R}{R}` to cast; discard a LAND as separate cost of each `0` activation, deal2 to legal target on resolution. Doom's drain trigger independent of whether Assault's 2 damage is countered if land discard occurred. In a four-player table discard 3 lands in three distinct activations with Doom => each opponent loses 6 (18 aggregate); single mass discard 3 lands => each loses 2 (6 aggregate).
8. **Roaming Throne** choose Villain (or other Doom creature type) on entering -> each own Doom triggered ability triggers additionally, **one event creates TWO triggers**; neither changes number of discarded lands. Spark Double nonlegendary Doom copy and Chameleon keeps distinct name allowing two separate permanents/triggers. Chameleon copy needs exact retained-name confirmation (source-based blueprint), and cast/copy timing can differ.
9. **Bone Miser:** Land discarded gives `{B}{B}` via trigger; Tra de Routes `{U}` discard-cost activation needs genuinely U available even if BB created from prior discard. With Bone Miser and Badgersaur separately you may have BB and Treasure, spend Treasure as U for Trade Routes; not automatic if no Badgersaur.
10. **Wizard/effect behavior:** Lethal Scheme convoked by multiple creatures causes EACH of them to connive as discrete actions, so each discarded land may create separate Doom triggers; target requirement for Lethal Scheme still matters. `Change of Fortune` self-refill depends on prior discard count this turn. `Cool but Rude` level2 counts each card discarded; `Glorious Purpose` puts plan counters for connive and cashes out at six—legitimate alternate Villain plan.
11. **Opposition:** Counter `Tectonic Reformation`, instant removal of Badgersaur, grave hate on Dakmor, kill Doom, remove `Seismic Assault`, and nonbasic hate attacking the 38-land color web all break payoff or loop. Lack of Wards on Doom makes free protection important; Doom's life-loss ability isn't damage and cannot be redirected as damage.

## Finite sequencing and adversarial scenarios
**A — own combat selection.** Doom, Villainous Hideout, an Island and a BR dual on board; target Doom with triggered ability at BEGINNING COMBAT, before attackers. He gains menace, draws one/discards a land. Doom land trigger adds each opponent lose2 on resolve. If Doom removed in response to combat trigger and it has become illegal target, ability fails, no draw or discard; if it already resolved, removing Doom doesn't reverse life lost by existing Doom trigger.
**B — mass discard versus independent activations.** One `Faithless Looting` draws2 then discards 2 lands simultaneously: one Doom trigger =>2 to each opponent. One `Seismic Assault` activation discards 1 land, opponent has chance to respond, then a SECOND activation discards another: Doom triggers twice, 4 loss to each; Seismic's selected damage separately deals 2 to target each if ability resolves and target still legal. Doom effect uses non-targeting each-opponent life loss and doesn't require Assault target legal.
**C — source-paid Dakmor six cycles.** Baseline mana + Doom, Surly, Reformation in play, Dakmor hand, library with >=12 cards. Spend R from red source, discard Dakmor to cycle (Doom2 to each, Badgersaur Treasure). Cycling draw dredge2, return Dakmor. Sac Treasure for R, cycle again. Repeat total SIX cycles: library loses 12 cards, each opponent loses 12 life from Doom (assuming no prevention); six Treasures created AND consumed, NO net infinite stored Treasure, no extra land drops, no claim of 100% win. Opponent exile Dakmor at draw replacement window / response to cycle breaks line. With 19+ cycles and all normal conditions, starting 40 life can be drained, but no finite deck guarantees that library reserve.
**D — Monument counterexample.** Three cycling/discard events with Monument active select Treasure, draw, opponents lose3 (any order). Fourth to sixth discard in SAME turn still triggers but no legal unchosen mode and will be removed with no effect. Meanwhile Doom can trigger on all six SEPARATE land events (each opponent -12). Do not multiply Monument six times or treat self-replenishing Treasure.
**E — Bone Miser and Trade Routes mana.** Blue land and two land cards in hand; cast Trade Routes earlier. Pay U, discard first land: Doom2, Bone Miser BB, draw1. Cannot pay U to discard second merely with produced BB. If a Talisman UR untapped, tap for U (lose1) to pay second activation; Doom2 more, then BB additional. Assess actual U source count for claims of repeating.
**F — removal and grave hate.** Opponent holds instant exile against Dakmor in grave plus Swords for Badgersaur. In response to cycling/dredge sequencing, removing Badgersaur before LAND discard means no Treasure and only initial R invested; Doom drain may still occur if Doom lives. Exiling Dakmor before cycling draw resolves makes dredge option unavailable and draws normal replacement instead. Moonstone/Construct MUST NOT exile Dakmor when aiming to dredge it.
**G — power ceiling against aggro and artifact hate.** Turn 2 Signet with 2 blue-red lands and Ancient Tomb makes generic mana but not necessarily all UBR; Doom cannot enter until remaining colored pips solved. If opponent removes Reformation, deck can still use Frog/Trade Routes, but forced land discard dwindles without card access. If opponent exiles grave, Crucible and Moonstone value decreases and combo lost; wheel refill gives one doom trigger with lands discarded, but enough cards for next cycle.
**H — actual Villain-specific line.** Villainous Hideout can cast Doom (Villain) with any colored mana, but same colored tap cannot pay Reformation's R cycling activation because land card being cycled is not a Villain source. Hideout's `3,T` connive targeting Doom is sorcery-speed: draw1 then choose land to discard, Doom triggers. With Roaming Throne naming Villain, both Doom triggers fire; Hideout does not inherently untap for another free activation. Cards labeled 'any mana color' require source restriction checks.
**I — ranked cut adversary.** Opponent deploys graveyard hate or a stax enchantment. Swapping `Feed the Swarm` for `Tidespout Tyrant` would delete black enchantment removal and replace it with 8MV creature that may never resolve through hate. Swapping `Trade Routes` for `Necropotence` deprives each-U repeated land-discard replacement and prevents normal draw step dredges while enchantment lives, even if raw card access improves. Lower score cannot override exact resource requirements.

## Conclusions and unresolved evidence
**KEEP** entire approved baseline, no auto swap. True strongest challengers: cheap repeatable discard via **Putrid Imp** as Frog redundancy, more connive via **Ledger Shredder** when second spell cast triggers across turns, and possible five-mana **Living Laser** temporary token swarm after prior discards, whose Oracle is verified but net performance/cut remains unsupported. The most tempting cut is a 4–6MV Villain without enough commander-enabling throughput, but removing Marvel characters erodes identity if cheap outsider adds only overlapping utility. The unchanged list's primary win is land-as-resource commander drain plus Villain/connive and conditional finite Dakmor combo, not a generic Grixis discard shell.
**Strongest counterargument:** At 38 lands and a high five-mana Villain count, the inexpensive `Putrid Imp` may be a substantive speed upgrade. It is *not* proven without credible replacement slot: Frog already repeats free discards while supplying draw/evasion, Putrid Imp consumes hand cards without replacement and cannot receive Doom's target connive. Ledger Shredder is promising multiplayer card quality but its trigger is opponent action dependent and non-Villain. We retain **evidence-required proposal only**, no claim of global/local optimum.
