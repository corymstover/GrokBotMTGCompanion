## Commander identity and natural ceiling

**Missy** is {3}{U}{B}{R}, legendary 4/5 Time Lord Rogue. **Whenever ANOTHER NONARTIFACT CREATURE dies** (any player's), its card returns to battlefield under your control **face down and tapped**, as a **2/2 nameless colorless Cyberman ARTIFACT creature**. Face-down artifact state is crucial: sacrificing it again while still face down **does not** trigger Missy. Turn it face UP (via its morph/disguise/megAmorph cost or Ixidor/Etrata/Skirk/Ugin) to restore printed nonartifact type, then the next death recycles again. Flipping is not leaving/re-entering; no fresh ETB on flip. Returning a tapped Cyberman is compatible with SAC costs that do not require tap; `Phyrexian Tower` itself taps and cannot be looped without untap effect. Tokens that die cease to exist and cannot return. Opponent commander can move to command zone. If Missy dies alongside another nonartifact creature her death trigger looks back and returns that other creature, but never Missy herself.

At **your beginning end step**, each opponent chooses either every artifact creature you currently control deals one damage to that opponent, OR you draw a card and chaos ensues. Opponents can choose the no-effect damage option if you control zero artifact creatures; they can choose to grant you cards to avoid high damage. `The Valeyard` doubles number of choices, not your control of opponent selections; no current dedicated chaos payoff. This villainous choice is flavorful **and strategically weaker than guaranteed damage or draw**; pressure depends on timing and board count. Cyberman theft and nonartifact-only sweepers create board advantage; the deterministic win package uses MISSY plus reveal-color morph plus a zero-mana sacrifice outlet, with Blood Artist/Zulaport/Mayhem Devil/Fireweaver/Pyrotechnic Performer additional payoff layers.

**Protected identity:** repeated opponent-creature acquisition and Cyberman assembly; 3 reveal-morph bodies (Ruthless Ripper, Dragon's Eye Savants, Horde Ambusher); six functionally distinct sacrifice outlets (5 no-tap free outlets and Phyrexian Tower one per untap); turn-face-up utility interaction, asymmetric nonartifact wipes, Valeyard villainous-choice multiplier and Doctor Who Cyberman flavor. Anti-goal: do not replace with generic Grixis Breach/Oracle or unrelated artifact sacrifice combo; no claim of cEDH speed merely because the three-piece loops are unbounded. **Natural ceiling Bracket 4 — Optimized**: commander costs **six** with three colored pips and recursive combo requires a face-up morph, outlet, reveal card and no opposing graveyard replacement/exile. The blueprint records **17** Spellbook combo lines with Missy present. Those named combinations do not mean 17 independent win-condition families; many are permutations of 3 reveal bodies and sacrifice outlets.

**Commander deployment reality:** A generic {C}{C}{C} from `Mana Vault` or {C}{C} from Ancient Tomb/Sol Ring makes six mana possible faster, but still must have U/B/R independently; `Inventors' Fair` only colorless, `Phyrexian Tower` can pay black at creature-sacrifice cost and sacrifices at most once per untap. Conditional lands `Luxury Suite`, `Morphic Pool`, `Training Center`, `Exotic Orchard` and the check/slow lands need an actual turn/multiplayer setting. The 3 fetchlands can select typed original duals, shocks, Sunken Hollow or Lounge depending target availability. Artifact support `Arcane Signet`, Fellwar Stone and three Talismans underpins early UBR; replacing Fellwar Stone with a speculative payoff or Otawara with Temple of the False God would degrade an already demanding six-mana engine.

**Development roles:** 3 reveal morphs, 5 persistent mana-free sac outlets (Altar of Dementia, Ashnod's Altar, Goblin Bombardment, Phyrexian Altar, Viscera Seer), one Phyrexian Tower land-sac outlet, 4 direct death payoffs (Blood Artist, Zulaport, Mayhem Devil, Reckless Fireweaver), face-up payoff cards (Aphetto Runecaster, Panoptic Projektor, Pyrotechnic Performer, Ugin's Mastery), and 3 cheap unconstrained tutors (Demonic, Diabolic Intent, Vampiric). If Missy is removed, most loop bodies become fair morph cards, especially Dragon's Eye Savants. This is the *main* commander-necessity risk, not a reason to substitute a generic cEDH win.

**Interact with board state correctly:** `Delete` / `Their Name Is Death` spare artifact Cybermen and kill opposing NONARTIFACT creatures; `Toxic Deluge` kills all qualifying creatures including Missy/Cybermen, although Missy's last-known-information death triggers can still recur other nonartifact cards. `Cyber Conversion` turns a creature face down without a death: it does **not** itself trigger Missy or steal opponent's permanent. `Cyclonic Rift` bounces instead of killing, so no theft. `Terminate` and `Feed the Swarm` are destroy-based and can convert opposing nonartifact creatures but cannot hit indestructible or replacement-shielded targets. `Swan Song` only counters enchantment/instant/sorcery; `An Offer` counters noncreature spell, creating opponent Treasures, and cannot protect Missy from a creature-spell counterfight. `Boltbender`/`Willbender` can redirect single-target effects, not Wraths; `Kadena's Silencer` counters abilities, not spells.

**Real opportunity-cost vulnerabilities:** Cybermen Squadron is **7 mana**, and its myriad copies are **exiled at end of combat**, so it does **not increase artifact creature count when Missy's end-step choice resolves**. The source blueprint's loose “Squadron scales the villainous-choice damage” is only true via persistent nonlegendary artifact creatures or other longer-term effects, **not via transient myriad tokens**. Squadron still magnifies combat, attacks multiple opponents and triggers `Reckless Fireweaver` on each artifact token entry. `Cybership` costs six, needs crew 4 and combat damage to a player to produce stolen Cybermen (much slower than Missy death loops). `The Cyber-Controller`, `Death in Heaven`, `They Came from the Pipes` and `Ugin's Mastery` provide iconic theft/Cyberman/face-down identity but compete with six-mana Missy for the curve. `They Came from the Pipes` has **mandatory** draw for every face-down creature entering, a self-deck threat in arbitrarily repeated loops; `Grim Haruspex` death draw is also mandatory for *another* nontoken friendly creature, not for itself. These deserve adversarial testing, not generic “all value always good.”

## Oracle, legality, rules and paid combo accounting

**Oracle/rules provenance:** `grixis/interactions/missy.md` enumerates nine independently previously checked source interactions from Scryfall Oracle bulk dated **2026-10-16**, rulings dated **2026-10-15**, Magic Comprehensive Rules dated **2026-09-25**; current public [Wizards Doctor Who release notes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/magic-the-gathering-doctor-who-release-notes), [Missy](https://mtg.wtf/card/who/1137/Missy), [Cybermen Squadron](https://mtg.dragonshield.com/card/who_1048_CybermenSquadron), [Blasting Station combo](https://edhrec.com/combos/grixis/413-1794-4424), [Pipes](https://mtg.wtf/card/dsc/45/They-Came-from-the-Pipes) and [The Master](https://mtg.wtf/card/who/1134/The-Master-Gallifreys-End) were additionally consulted this review. The Oct12 B&R announcement concerned Competitive Brawl, not tabletop Commander; approved Oct15 100-card official legality and color-identity gate remains authoritative historical audit. Incoming Blasting Station is colorless and publicly Commander-legal per card database context; before actual swap approval/finalization rerun the up-to-date all-100 Oracle, banned-list and singleton/color gate on exact proposed final candidate SHA. No exceptional project banned cards are present or proposed.

**Missy's own die trigger vs state:** The word “another” means Missy **does not** return herself. Missy triggering sees nonartifact creatures immediately before they die, even when she dies simultaneously: she may return other nontoken creature cards. Tokens cannot come back after reaching GY and ceasing to exist; opposing commander owner may choose command zone before trigger resolves. If sacrificed body is an **artifact** creature at death (Cyberman), Missy does NOT trigger, even if its underlying printed side is nonartifact. If `Rest in Peace` replaces graveyard transition with exile, “dies” doesn't occur and trigger fails entirely; with `Dauthi Voidwalker` type replacement, check replacement precisely. If an opponent removes the creature card from GY while Missy trigger waits to resolve, it cannot come back. If opponents get priority after sac activation and before Missy death trigger resolves, graveyard exile can interrupt. Returning Cyberman tapped does not block sacrifice as cost to Altar/Bombardment/Seer, which have no tap cost.

**Face up requires a legal method:** Turning a morph face up is a **special action that doesn't use stack**, but only when its controller has priority and can reveal specified colored card in HAND. `Ruthless Ripper` reveal black, `Dragon's Eye Savants` reveal blue, `Horde Ambusher` reveal red. The same physical card in hand may be shown every loop; no card discard, reveal card remains hand. A tapped face-down Cyberman can still turn face up. You cannot re-flip a face-up creature without it being face down again. `Panoptic Projektor` discount applies to CASTING future face-down spells, not flipping Cybermen already on battlefield; flip triggers get doubled. `Ugin's Mastery` sees face-down creature spell on stack as a colorless creature **cast**, not a Missy face-down return placed directly on field.

**Paid free-reveal loop, concrete:** With Missy, face-up **Ruthless Ripper**, `Goblin Bombardment`, and a black card in hand, pay Bombardment's cost by sacrificing Ripper; Ripper goes GY, Missy triggers. Missy's trigger resolves putting SAME card onto battlefield face down, tapped, Cyberman artifact. With priority, reveal black card and turn that face-down creature up by paying morph's NONMANA reveal requirement, it becomes face-up nonartifact Ripper, whose turn-up triggered ability causes **target player to lose 2 life**. Resolve as appropriate, activate Bombardment again: each complete iteration = **one Bombardment damage to any target + Ripper's targeted 2 life loss + a creature death + a face-down artifact-creature ETB**. Opponent with instant graveyard exile can respond to Missy trigger, and `Stifle` can counter trigger. A finite 20 iterations = 20 Bombardment damage + up to 40 life loss to legal targeted opponent if all resolved; this is **not** automatically 60 to EACH opponent unless correctly distributed.

**Dragon's Eye Savants** same free reveal blue mechanism; sacrifice it to Bombardment = 1 damage/loop, and no inherent face-up life-loss trigger. With `Altar of Dementia` instead, Savants printed power **0** when sacrificed face up, therefore zero cards milled without other buffs. With `Ruthless Ripper` (printed power1) or `Horde Ambusher` (positive power), Dementia mills per iteration; each Ripper iteration also loses 2 life target opponent. With `Ashnod's Altar`, each reveal-loop sacrifice yields **two colorless**; with Phyrexian Altar, **one colored mana** of your choice. `Viscera Seer` generates one scry per death, not a direct kill absent additional payoff. `Reckless Fireweaver` deals 1 to each opponent whenever each Cyberman ARTIFACT comes back, so even Savants+Seer plus Fireweaver produces lethal table drain; `Blood Artist` target-player drain occurs on CREATURE death; `Zulaport Cutthroat` drains each opponent when your creature dies; `Mayhem Devil` sees sacrifice.

**Blasting Station line:** Cost of Station {3} generic (cast previously); activation is `{T}, Sacrifice a creature: deal 1 damage to any target`. When Ripper dies to pay the activation, Missy returns it as tapped Cyberman, which **enters** and triggers Station's untap. Ripper then flips by revealing black; its face-up ability causes target player to lose two; can reactivate Station when untapped for next iteration. Commander Spellbook via [EDHREC's combo record](https://edhrec.com/combos/grixis/413-1794-4424) explicitly documents this conditional route. If Station was tapped but another creature previously enters, its trigger can untap; initial setup must satisfy tap cost. If opponent removes Station in response to Missy ETB/untap, later repeat stops, even though damage ability already on stack still resolves. In the face of `Stony Silence` / `Null Rod` artifacts' nonmana activated abilities are shut off (Station and artifact Altars as applicable), but Goblin Bombardment (enchantment) and Viscera Seer (creature) remain separate outlets. Added Station increases artifact-hate exposure versus an enchantment sac outlet, a genuine downside.

**Important existing source interactions:**
- **Ixidor + Pitiless Plunderer + Ashnod's Altar + Missy:** Face-up nontoken nonartifact friendly creature sacrificed to Ashnod for {C}{C}; Plunderer (another friendly creature death) gives Treasure {T},sac for U; Missy returns the creature face down tapped. Ixidor pays `{2}{U}` using both colorless plus Treasure U to flip it up. Repeat under intact objects; this is **mana-neutral**, not a claim of net infinite mana, but generates unbounded deaths/ETBs/treasure casts? Treasure **sacrifices** (not casts), repeated artifacts enter, allowing Fireweaver/Mayhem Devil payoffs. Does not work if sacrificed creature is an artifact when it dies.
- **Grim Haruspex + Phyrexian Altar + Missy:** Haruspex dies face-up nonartifact; Altar produces B, Missy returns face down; morph pay B to turn up. Net **zero mana** with deaths/ETBs, and Haruspex's “another” trigger **does not draw on its own death**. Other payoff permanents needed for victory.
- **The Valeyard:** When each opponent faces Missy's choice, they must face it again. At each choice opponent may choose damage OR your draw independently. At zero artifact creatures opponents can avoid giving draws. “Chaos ensues” does NOTHING in a game without chaos-triggered abilities/planar effects.
- **Squadron:** Nonlegendary artifact creatures have myriad, including Squadron. In four-player free-for-all an attacking applicable artifact creature makes copies attacking each of the two OTHER opponents; the copies are **exiled at end of combat**, not at end step. They can enable combat and artifact ETB damage while present. They cannot be counted at Missy's end-step choice after normal combat. If `Time Sieve` were added, exiling in end of combat leaves a window *during combat* to sacrifice temporary tokens to Sieve; that is a different high-setup route, not evidence current list has infinite turns.
- **The Master, Gallifrey's End:** Other nontoken artifact creatures you control dying (including a nontoken *face-down Cyberman*) trigger its ability; you may EXILE card from GY then select an opponent with most life. That opponent chooses 4 life lost or create token copying the exiled card. If it chooses the latter, that permanent is copied from **face-up characteristics of the exiled card** as appropriate, not automatically a new tapped Cyberman. Missy cannot itself return the nontoken artifact body that died, making Master's separate choice actual role coverage.
- **Cybership:** {6} Vehicle with **crew 4**; combat damage to player steals top two **cards** onto your battlefield face down as Cyberman artifacts. Crew requires tapping creatures with combined power≥4, so simply casting Cybership does not attack without crewing, summoning-sickness exceptions handled by artifact creature rules. The stolen cards don't need to be printed creatures to be face-down Cybermen; turning noncreature permanent face up requires a valid method (Etrata's ability may exile/cast if cannot legally turn it up).
- **They Came from the Pipes:** `{4}{U}` enchantment ETB **manifest dread twice**; whenever face-down creature enters under your control, **draw one**, including EACH Missy loop return. A player cannot normally choose to stop its mandatory trigger. If a loop requires 50 repetitions but you have 20 cards left, 21st trigger forces draw from empty library and loses game before you have finished 50 unless winning trigger is already placed/resolves first. `Panoptic Projektor` doubles turn-FACE-UP triggers, **not** Pipes face-DOWN entry trigger.
- **Death in Heaven:** Saga I/II mills then EXILES GY for target player; chapter III returns creatures previously exiled **with that same Saga** as face-down Cybermen; this is not an ordinary mass destroy, does not cause deaths from opponent battlefield by itself, and can conflict with your own GY loop setup if choosing yourself.
- **Bane of the Living:** face-up -X/-X affects all creatures; you must ensure Missy survives or apply last-known-information same-wipe trigger rule, and avoid killing your freshly returned artifact Cybermen if X≥2. `Delete` deals X to nonartifact creatures AND each player, so calculate actual player life and possible simultaneous commander death.
