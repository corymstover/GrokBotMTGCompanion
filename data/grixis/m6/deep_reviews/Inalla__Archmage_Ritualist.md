## Commander identity and natural ceiling
**Inalla costs `{2}{U}{B}{R}`, 4/5 Human Wizard** but optimized Inalla is overwhelmingly a COMMAND-ZONE deck. Eminence says whenever **ANOTHER NONTOKEN Wizard** enters under your control, if Inalla is on BF or in CZ, MAY PAY **{1}** on resolution to make ONE token copy of that Wizard, which gains HASTE and is exiled at beginning next end step. Copy doesn't trigger another Inalla Eminence (token excluded), but its own ETB does fire and the original Wizard trigger and Eminence can be STACKED to allow tutor, sacrifice, reanimate and token copy while the pending ability remains. Pay {1} PER eligible trigger; cannot arbitrarily pay {3} on one to make 3 copies. A creature cast but countered on the stack never enters. Actual Wizard need not have been cast to cause trigger: Reanimate/Persist/Unearth/Apprentice Necromancer count.
**Five untapped Wizards** as the ACTUAL cost of Inalla's separate battlefield activated ability makes target player lose **7 life**; it operates ONLY when Inalla is on the battlefield. Five creatures newly controlled CAN be tapped to pay this cost even without haste because it doesn't have a creature's tap symbol in its cost, though hasty copied Wizards can also do so. Inalla must be cast for {2}{U}{B}{R} (+ tax if applicable) after sufficient colored Treasure; Eminence alone in CZ cannot drain. For each of three opponents ordinarily at 40 life need six 7-loss activations per opponent absent prior life losses.
**Natural ceiling BRACKET 5 — cEDH**, owner-approved 2026. Top priority the **Spellseeker → Culling/Unearth/Burnt Offering/Finale/Entomb/Reanimate/Scholar/Shallow Grave** command-zone-assisted one-card setup, and independent **Bloodline Necromancer + Ruthless Technomancer** treasure-copy recursion. Oracle/Consultation/Pact and Breach/LED/Brain Freeze are resilient backups, not reasons to homogenize into Kess generic control. Casting Inalla early is usually unnecessary and costs 5 mana vs proactive Spellseeker 2U plus black fuel. The 2026-10-14 source documents full 49-slot cEDH rebuild and original tournament corroboration; no independent new controlled win rate inferred.

## Oracle, legality and rules
1. [Inalla Oracle](https://mtg.wtf/card/c17/38/Inalla-Archmage-Ritualist): Eminence only when in CZ or battlefield, **another NON-TOKEN Wizard** enters. May pay {1} ONCE as ability resolves. Token ETB still triggers its OWN abilities, but token cannot retrigger Eminence. Token gets haste, exile next end step. Tap five untapped Wizards only while actual Inalla on BF, no {T} on any single creature, so summoning sick Wizards are legal cost bodies.
2. [Spellseeker](https://mtg.wtf/card/bbd/41/Spellseeker) searches **instant or sorcery card MV≤2**, not enchantments or Scholar creature; token tutor and original tutor each fire when respective object enters, controllable order with pending Eminence; Culling the Weak sacrifice is a casting ADDITIONAL COST so Wizard dies while Inalla pending trigger remains and token can still be created.
3. [Scholar of the Ages](https://mtg.wtf/card/m20/74/Scholar-of-the-Ages) returns up to TWO targeted instant/sorcery grave cards, only if those targets remain legal when its trigger resolves. Shallow Grave grave-top creature matters; `Burnt Offering` sac Scholar MV7 yields SEVEN B/R, `Shallow Grave` costs1B, loop has positive mana even after optional Inalla payment1, provided grave order and targets maintained. [Spellbook Scholar/Burnt/Shallow](https://edhrec.com/combos/grixis/813-1181-2171) combo independent of Inalla yields mana/storm but not naturally the five-Wizard finisher without commander copies.
4. [Bloodline+Ruthless+Inalla](https://commanderspellbook.com/combo/1869-2721-3719/): bloodline enters for4B and RETURNS Technomancer from GY; resolve Technomancer ETB sac original Bloodline power3 ->3 Treasures; pay1 on Technomancer Eminence to create token, its ETB sacs original Technomancer power2 ->2 Treasure; resolve original pending Bloodline Eminence by paying1, token Bloodline returns original from GY; repeat. ## Conclusions and unresolved evidence
**DECISION KEEP the exact approved 100, with test candidates clearly distinguished.** The current source's 49-slot rebuild legitimately makes Inalla cEDH commander-first, with two different Inalla-dependent endgames and independent backups; this deep pass does not independently find an unambiguous net-upgrade cut. Best challenge is **Micromancer or additional reanimation over five-mana Kefka**, which could exchange an inefficient fair ETB/Ad Naus hit for more tutor consistency.
