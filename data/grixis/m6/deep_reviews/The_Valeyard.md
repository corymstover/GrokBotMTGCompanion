## Commander identity and natural ceiling

The Valeyard `{2}{U}{B}{R}` has two narrow static capabilities: each opponent faces an actual **villainous choice one additional time**, individually resolving the entire selected option before the next choice, and the Valeyard controller **may vote an additional time while voting**. These are not generic spell-copy triggers: cards must truly use 'villainous choice' or a rules-recognized *vote*. Copying Valeyard through Spark Double, Quantum Misalignment, Sakashima and Auton Soldier stacks the modifiers **additively**. One Valeyard: each opposition-facing choice twice; two: three times; three: four times. Your extra votes per copy grow linearly. The controller chooses how to cast their extra vote(s), subject to the voting card's mode and when votes are cast.

**Distinctive win condition:** combine repeated opponent-facing villainous decisions (Davros, Dr. Eggman, Genesis, The Master, Daleks) with real vote cards (Messenger Jays, Plea for Power, Expropriate, Capital Punishment, Coercive Portal, Tyrant's Choice, all three Paths, secret council spells). The deck seeks advantage through opponent agency skewed by extra votes, unavoidable repeated penalties, card flow and villain threats. High investment per permanent is a limitation: a five-mana commander with no native card draw/combat ability will often accomplish nothing until another qualifying vote/choice resolves. Prevent replacing his organic political control with generic free-spell turbo, self-mill or Tyrant/Hullbreaker combo packages.

**Realistic ceiling:** source **Power Bracket 4 — Optimized**. The list's numerous Game Changers such as Force of Will, Fierce Guardianship, Demonic Tutor, Vampiric Tutor, Cyclonic Rift, Rhystic Study, Expropriate and others plus a nine-mana Council's dilemma finisher justify the floor, but there is no demonstrated reproducible tournament cEDH fast kill. Valeyard is a high-dependency static multiplier, not an independent combo enabler or guaranteed two-turn clock.

- **36 lands** (29 nonbasics, seven basics [Island 2/Mountain 2/Swamp 3]), **nine fetchlands**, and 10 repeatable nonland mana sources plus Dark Ritual as a one-shot. - Source static report: potential land colored sources U 22 / B 21 / R 21; unconditional U20/B19/R19; land plus repeatable nonland U29/B28/R28. These counts are *source-presence proxies*, NOT independent guaranteed turn-by-turn simultaneous colored mana; conditional land entries and color choice constraints are accounted separately. Commander needs exactly **{2}{U}{B}{R}**, minimum five available mana and all three colored pips; after one command-zone recast seven mana, then nine.
- Intended lines: early T1 Mystic Remora/Brainstorm or Sol Ring; T2 colored Signets/Talismans/Fellwar Stone or reactive vote card (Tyrant's Choice/Split Decision); T3–5 cast Valeyard when protection or already playable villainous-choice/vote is expected; follow with clone/extra vote then capital-punishment/choice payoff. In blind creature pods and combo pods a reactive counter remains necessary. Attempting early colorless Tyrant instead of Deadly Rollick is not a free ramp improvement.
- Source no-opponent simulator is goldfish only. It cannot count opponent *actual voting*, decline/split decisions, true extra vote stacking, attacker survival, or effect of adding an eight-mana creature while losing zero-mana removal. Therefore its candidate 'pass' is not a demonstrated commander-first victory improvement.
- **Force of Will blue-pitch integrity:** 31 blue-identity nonland names other than commander include four colorless Signets/Talismans and Force itself. Exactly **26 other truly blue nonland cards** remain available in library as potential alternative-cost exiles. Source Oct15 claim 26 is correct; a multicolor card is pitchable if it is actually blue, and U-symbol activated-mana cost alone doesn't make a colorless rock blue. The Valeyard in command zone itself is not hand fuel. Alternative cost additionally pays one life; countering ordinary spells does not stop activated vote permissions.

### Principal opposing evaluations and alternate cuts

#### Hullbreaker Horror or Tidespout Tyrant → Deadly Rollick
Eight-mana bounce commanders are broadly powerful and can create locks if repeated low-cost artifact/spell loops truly close. Source packet flags external Spellbook completions but does not give exact 100-card, payable mana, target/priority sequences. Deadly Rollick answers threatening creatures for zero once 2UBR Valeyard is active; a nine-ish generic creature takes a whole turn and contributes zero choices/votes. Alternative cuts Kess, a high-MV Path, or generic tutor all lose resilience or choice access. HOLD KEEP.

#### Ioreth of the Healing House or Cephalid Illusionist → Dr. Eggman
Ioreth untaps legendaries that actually tap, but The Valeyard is a static-ability commander with no tap symbol. Actual artifact/land tapping interactions need exact Oracle and turn cycle, not synergy-by-type. Cephalid Illusionist can support unrelated milling loops but no organic voting. Dr. Eggman triggers native Villainous Choice and counts toward the 13-source requirement; losing it makes The Valeyard itself less relevant. KEEP.

#### Shadowblood Ridge / Darkwater Catacombs → Flooded Strand
EDHREC inclusion of filter lands is higher than Flooded Strand, but on an optimized premium three-color base this is not comparative mana quality. Flooded Strand can fetch Island-typed Steam Vents, Watery Grave, Underground Sea, Volcanic Island and Xander's Lounge (including black and red indirectly), and basic Island. Filters cost an input mana and can be nonfunctional alone. The evidence is not a color-sequencing win. KEEP.

#### Panoptic Mirror → Auton Soldier or Rakdos Signet
Imprint a high-leverage vote/choice sorcery at five-mana artifact plus imprint cost and later upkeep can be strong if left online, but unlike a Valeyard copy it does not multiply every choice or each vote. Auton Soldier is a six-mana repeat copy native to commander and makes combat-time myriads; Rakdos Signet advances five-mana colored casting. Panoptic Mirror also opens generic Expropriate/extra-turn abuse, undermining direct identity. KEEP.

#### Underworld Breach → Genesis of the Daleks or Mystic Remora
Graveyard value is reasonable and Kess already recasts one spell from grave each of your turns. Underworld Breach requires stocked graveyard and adequate fuel to replay six- or nine-mana spell; it provides no voting by itself. Genesis produces multiple villainous-choice chapters; Remora protects early when five-mana commander not yet active. KEEP.

#### Dualcaster Mage / Naru Meha / Kiki-Jiki → Dr. Eggman or Kess
Each is a known combo shell component, but has high required partner/zone/priorities. Dualcaster and Naru copy spell on stack, which may replicate a vote spell once but cannot copy Valeyard's *static replacement* ability; Kiki-Jiki targets only nonlegendary creatures and Valeyard is legendary. Removing Eggman strips actual choice source and Kess rescues choice/vote spells without forcing combo kills. KEEP.

#### Repercussion / Toralf / Fall of Cair Andros → native vote/draw/exile
Blasphemous Act creates appealing damage math against creature-dense boards, but life totals/toughness and own creatures determine whether it is dangerous, lethal or self-harming. Repercussion is symmetric and not a vote. None corrects high-MV Valeyard timing or supplies missing choice effects. Actual early Mystic Remora/free exile beats speculative removal conversion. KEEP.

#### Omarthis, Mirror-Mad, Cephalid/Crackdown alt lines
Mechanical near-misses are artifacts of combinatorial Spellbook search. The deck lacks a validated repeatable free target/untap and direct kill conversion; free-engine assumptions, priority or mana omissions are not allowed. These cards neither replace Valeyard's political plan nor protect it. HOLD baseline.

## Oracle, legality and rules

1. **Actual definition:** CR 701.55a/c distinguishes villainous choice decisions and replacement-produced extra sequential decisions. Valeyard applies only when an **opponent** faces that keyword, not when you face one; one Valeyard adds exactly ONE extra choice, not doubles itself recursively. Two independent replacements are additive, not multiplicative.
2. **Actual voting:** CR 701.38a/c/d: extra vote permission only matters if instruction uses *vote*. Prisoner's Dilemma and Seize the Spotlight merely ask choices, not eligible vote spells. Secret council requires actual simultaneous recorded votes and the extra Valeyard vote(s) are written down at the same voting occasion. Each Council's dilemma vote resolves independently, unlike majority-based will of council.
3. **Messenger Jays:** Each feather/quill vote generates the corresponding effect; the extra Valeyard vote is independently assigned, not an arbitrary spell copy. Can choose two identical outcomes or split, based on who needs cards/counters.
4. **Expropriate:** At nine mana, the controller votes on Time or Money first; each additional Valeyard vote can choose Time (additional extra turn) or Money (permanent acquisition), while each opponent still votes. This is a huge payoff **only after actually casting and resolving nine-mana sorcery**; no automatic infinite loop.
5. **Grudge Keeper:** Opponent loses two life if they voted for an option you voted for none of; when you split extra votes among both options, each opponent potentially matches one and avoids the drain. Voting twice the same option may be correct despite lower apparent vote variety. Model of Unity often rewards a different shared-vote pattern.
6. **Illusion of Choice:** One-blue instant modifies actual voters' selections; can set opponent choices and controller's extra vote consistently, but doesn't create a voting event and cannot make a generic choice card into a vote.
7. **Sakashima / Spark Double:** Nonlegendary Spark copy survives original Valeyard. Sakashima can copy Valeyard while remaining legendary but its static legend-waiver prevents state-based sacrifice until it leaves; if Sakashima leaves, normal legend rule re-checks the permanents. Cards cannot be incorrectly treated as multiple legendary Valeyards without exception.
8. **Quantum Misalignment:** Five mana creates a nonlegendary copied Valeyard; rebound next upkeep can make another if suitable controlled target remains and the spell is cast from exile. A second copy is not available in the *same* vote as its first sorcery-resolution merely because rebound exists.
9. **Auton Soldier:** A nonlegendary copy plus myriad token copies enter attacking in your attack step, then the tokens are exiled at end of combat. These temporary static vote/choice multipliers only help if real choice/vote is initiated **during combat**; they do not persist to the next postcombat main phase for Expropriate.
10. **Panoptic Mirror candidate:** Exile/imprint a qualifying instant or sorcery from hand for mana value paid, then may copy and cast it during upkeep for free. This is repeated cast advantage if intact and requires target spell legality, but does not make Valeyard replacement effect a triggered activation or replace a real vote by itself.
11. **Dualcaster / Naru/Kiki candidate:** To create repeated copies one must pay actual mana and satisfy spells/targets, and Kiki-Jiki has `nonlegendary creature you control` restriction; cannot casually copy legendary Valeyard. A generic Spellbook near-miss is not a constructed fully legal deterministic loop for this exact list.
12. **Color and fetch:** Original Windswept Heath was dead without typed Plains/Forest and removed; Arid Mesa finds basic Mountain and Mountain-typed duals. Flooded Strand does **not** demand white cards/white identity for UBR use; it fetches Island-typed Watery Grave, Steam Vents, Underground Sea, Volcanic Island and Xander's Lounge. A filter-land substitution cannot be justified by 'zero EDHREC usage of Flooded Strand'.
13. **Casting priority and protection:** Opponent can remove Valeyard or a Valeyard copy before you cast a choice/vote if priority is passed. Static additional votes/choices require surviving to relevant voting/choice event. Countering a choice spell on stack prevents the event entirely. No guarantee every choice hurts opponents if their alternatives can avoid the main penalty.
14. **Official status:** approved Oct15 and engineering Oct16 snapshot officially Commander-legal, no special exception; no new full external bulk scan performed in this authored pass. Historical attestation is cited as historical, not fresh oct20 oracle certification.
