## Commander identity and natural ceiling

Original **Nicol Bolas** costs `{2}{U}{U}{B}{B}{R}{R}` (8 total), is a 7/7 flier, requires paying `{U}{B}{R}` at the beginning of **your** upkeep or sacrificing him, and causes an opponent to discard their **entire hand whenever Nicol deals damage to that opponent**, not just combat damage. The commander's own damage events are the defining currency; damage from a **spell/equipment** rather than from Nicol does **not** trigger his discard ability. Repeated damage to an already-empty opponent has low incremental discard value, so sources that hit all opponents and protection against the first counter/removal spell matter much more than a generic double-strike amplifier.

**Protected identity:** Make original Bolas the direct damage source via Ingris, Kediss, Dragon Tempest, Chandra's Ignition, Pain for All, Quicksilver Dagger and Viridian Longbow; turn those discards into control/resources through Waste Not, Geth's Grimoire, Tergrid, Tinybones, Caress/Raiders and life stabilization. Ensure strong colored acceleration and shields for the 8-mana/UBR-upkeep burden. Preserve selective flavor (Ravager body, The Eldest Reborn), but **do not migrate** to the next encyclopedia commander Nicol Bolas, the Ravager's transform/Superfriends identity. Do not default to Thassa's Oracle/Breach as a substitute win condition.

**Power:** Bracket **4 — Optimized, low-end of bracket** due to expensive commander, three pairs of colored pips, recurring upkeep, limited early board and vulnerability to removal. Sword/Assault can win without commander, but relying on it as sole plan would fail identity. Not supported as realistic turbo-cEDH. The optimization target is earlier meaningful Bolas-sourced discard and survival, not arbitrary generic combo density.

## Mana and curve audit

**Land count:** 37, including two basic Islands, one basic Mountain, one basic Swamp, nine fetches (Arid Mesa, Bloodstained Mire, Flooded Strand, Marsh Flats, Misty Rainforest, Polluted Delta, Scalding Tarn, Verdant Catacombs, Wooded Foothills), three original duals, three shocks, Xander's Lounge, 3 Commander utility lands plus other fixing and Hall. 4 basics total; high nonbasic dependence is acceptable for tuned three-color deck but hostile Blood Moon/Back to Basics tables are hazardous. Two lands (*Bojuka Bog, Xander's Lounge*) are always tapped; Hall is also tapped, and several have conditional untap or color access; the archived static source calls out three unconditional tapped lands plus seven conditional tapped lands.

**Mana source distinctions:** Sol Ring/Ancient Tomb/Mana Vault/Grim Monolith pay generic, not `UUBBRR` and not `UBR` next upkeep. Mox Diamond requires **discarding a land**; Chrome Mox **exiles** a nonartifact nonland card and usually produces exactly one color; both reduce available cards. Signets require `{1}` to filter into two colors. Talismans and Arcane Signet help supply precise pips. Cabal/Dark Ritual supply black only; Seething Song red only; Nightscape Familiar reduces generic portions of blue/red **spells**, not the 6 fixed colored pips. Nine fetches with typed duals make single-color reliability good, but a fetch for the wrong typed pair can strand the **second** required pip.

Without a new context-aware effect simulator, no invented turn-4 win rates.

**Payable arithmetic counterexample:** Five lands producing U/U/B/B/R (5 colored) plus Sol Ring (2 colorless) plus Grim Monolith (3 colorless) yields 10 total but **only one R**: Nicol cannot be cast at any resource count absent a second red pip. Six lands yielding U,U,B,B,R,R plus a Sol Ring tap (2) pay exactly `{2}{U}{U}{B}{B}{R}{R}` and require reserving or producing UBR at next upkeep. Command-zone recast after one prior cast is 10 total including UUBBRR, not 8. **Command Beacon** puts commander into hand at land-sacrifice cost, so a cast from hand pays its 8 printed cost and does not incur command tax, but still requires the precise colored pips.

**Acceleration evaluation:** Removing an additional land for a fourth ritual looks aggressive: 37 already makes Mox Diamond discard less free; 9 fetches help smooth, but 8-mana commander should not be treated like a 3-mana turbo deck. Gilded Lotus offers triple colored mana for sustained upkeep but costs 5 and can clog the commander turn; Relic of Sauron for 4 has strong UBR tap fixing but consumes a whole development turn; Commander's Sphere at 3 is slower than Cabal Ritual's tactical burst. Avoid blanket replacement of essential colors and interaction with higher-MV artifacts.

## Oracle, legality and rules

1. **Nicol source and upkeep:** Any damage Nicol itself deals to opponent, combat or noncombat, triggers discard; his upkeep sacrifice trigger occurs at **beginning of owner's upkeep** and requires UBR on resolution. Responding with removal beforehand is viable; holding only Sol Ring and Grim generic mana does **not** save him. [Approved Oct15 verification](https://github.com/corymstover/grixis-commander-encyclopediaGPT/blob/main/refinement/verification/Nicol_Bolas_Approval_Ready_Verification_2026-10-15.md).
2. **Ingris attack:** Nicol is already in play, Ingris (BRR) is on battlefield, attack is declared while Nicol has haste or is no longer summoning sick. The Ingris triggered ability makes **Nicol** deal 1 to each of three opponents **before blockers**. If hands have 6/4/3 cards at resolution, 13 cards are discarded, triggering each applicable source separately; with Caress, life loss 12/8/6 respectively, NOT combat commander damage. Opponents may use instant-speed spells in response, prevent source damage or remove Nicol **before attackers** are declared. [Ingris Oracle](https://mtg.wtf/card/fra/409/Ingris-Stingerquill).
3. **Kediss:** Nicol hits player A for 7 **combat** damage, Kediss trigger makes Nicol deal 7 **noncombat** to B and C. One hand wipe each (A via combat and B/C via secondary trigger); secondary 7 is **not commander combat damage** for 21-point rule. Requires initial combat damage; chump blocking or fog can stop original hit. It does not spread A's discard effect; it causes separate new damage events.
4. **Dragon Tempest:** Nicol Dragon ETB can trigger Tempest to grant flying creature haste and deal at least one damage **from the entering Dragon** to any target; targeted opponent discards if damage is dealt. Haste only becomes helpful while he is on battlefield; the trigger does not reduce his 8-mana cast cost. ETB removal and target legality are real windows.
5. **Chandra's Ignition:** Cast `{3}{R}{R}` in main phase, targeting **your Nicol**. On resolution Nicol deals power (normally 7) to each other creature (including **your** Ingris, Kediss, Sangromancer) and each opponent. Damage = source Nicol -> separate discard triggers if not prevented; spell fizzles if Nicol is no longer legal target. Important self-sweep weakens own support and may feed enemy death triggers. Nicol itself is not in 'each other creature'. Explicitly reject claimed clean one-sided creature sweep.
6. **Pain for All:** `{2}{R}` Aura enchants Nicol; when it enters, enchanted creature deals power damage to **any other target**; Nicol is source. If Nicol later takes damage, aura causes Nicol to deal that much to each opponent even if damage was lethal, using LKI as needed. No target gets hit if its target becomes illegal on ETB trigger; paying to enchant and immediate removal before resolution is an exposure. [Pain for All Oracle/rulings](https://mtg.wtf/card/eoe/337/Pain-for-All).
7. **Dagger/Longbow:** Quicksilver Dagger grants tap: Nicol pings one target **player/planeswalker** and you draw; Longbow grants tap: 1 to any target. Both require Nicol to be able to tap despite summoning sickness, typically haste from Dragon Tempest/Greaves or prior turn; don't conflate Equipment's own spell cast with creature damage. Use Aura before Lightning Greaves makes Nicol shrouded; Swiftfoot gives hexproof against opponents instead.
8. **Curiosity and Geth:** One 1-damage event to each of three opponents through Ingris can trigger Curiosity **three times** for 3 optional cards, not 13 times for each discarded card; Geth's Grimoire can draw once for **each** of 13 opponent cards discarded if on board. Opponents can respond to all discard-trigger stacks and hand size may require cleanup discards; do not assume 13 cards can be held permanently.
9. **Tergrid/Tinybones:** both can trigger on the same opponent-discarded permanent; active controller orders triggers, but moving that card to battlefield first prevents graveyard exile for Bauble; exile first prevents Tergrid return. Can't gain/exile the same card twice. Discarded nonpermanents don't satisfy Tergrid's permanent clause.
10. **Oppression + shield:** With Oppression out, casting Force/Counterspell or Fierce Guardianship requires discarding another card when the cast-trigger resolves, before interaction spell resolves. If your last card is the answer, you can still cast it then discard zero; if you had protected enabler in hand, protecting your 8-drop may force it away. Enemy empty hands make Oppression less effective. **Self-discard tax** is a serious incumbent weakness.
11. **No Mercy:** Oracle triggers whenever **any creature** deals damage **to you** (including, in rare states, one you control), destroys that creature; not 'when opponent attacks', not protection for Nicol. Removing all opposing cards via hand wipe can leave No Mercy redundant if board is already empty. [No Mercy Oracle/ruling](https://mtg.wtf/card/dmr/95/No-Mercy).
12. **Sword/Assault:** Equipped attacker connects; Sword untaps all **your lands**, opponent discards one if able. With an **additional main phase** and five land-produced usable mana, pay `{3}{R}{R}` to Assault, untap all creatures that attacked this turn and create extra combat and main phase. Needs creature to survive, path to damage, land quantity/usable RR, and correct main-phase timing on **each** iteration; five raw colorless lands are insufficient to pay RR. Before first successful connection, no land refunds and no loop. Can be infinite combats **conditional** on opponent blocker/removal and continued damage. Sword and Assault do not independently require Nicol, so secondary win only.
13. **Hellkite Courser cast-vs-enter:** Its effect **puts** Nicol into battlefield without casting, so Cavern and casting reducers don't apply; return at **next end step**, before upcoming Nicol upkeep, unless other effects change zone. Unlike Geode Golem, no commander tax is charged for putting onto battlefield. If Bolas is damaged via Dragon Tempest or attacks (has haste), trigger is legitimate. [Wizards Commander Legends release notes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/commander-legends-release-notes-2020-11-06).
14. **Geode Golem cast tax:** An unblocked/trampling 5/3 Golem connecting can **cast** commander without paying printed mana cost **during trigger resolution**; **commander tax remains mandatory** because it is an additional cost. The trigger can be wasted if opponent removes Golem before combat connection. Geode's damage is **not** Nicol damage. [Wizards Commander Masters release notes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/commander-masters-release-notes).
15. **Mithril Coat:** Flash in response to **destroy** spell, entry trigger needs target legendary creature you control; opponent can remove Nicol in response to attach trigger and leave coat unattached. Indestructible cannot pay Bolas upkeep UBR or save him from sacrifice. [Coat Oracle/rulings](https://mtg.wtf/card/ltr/379/Mithril-Coat).
16. **Burning Anger:** Enchanted creature, not Aura, is source; tap damage equal to creature power when ability resolves. Power 7 can force one opponent discard, but `{4}{R}` additional setup plus summoning sickness is expensive; no automatic win. [Burning Anger Oracle/rulings](https://mtg.wtf/card/m15/133/Burning-Anger).
17. **Power Artifact:** Grim Monolith is `{2}`, `{T}: {C}{C}{C}`, `{4}: untap`. Power Artifact lowers the **activated untap ability** cost by 2 (to `{2}`), so a C1 net loop exists with one tap/untap cycle; it yields only colorless and never inherently satisfies UUBBRR. Mana Vault **does not** use equivalent activated untap cost. Reject 'infinite colored mana' claims.
18. **Survival and control race:** Forcing empty hands on turn 7 does not answer already-established creatures, graveyards, commanders on board or permanents with activated abilities. Toxic Deluge and Rift are necessary but cost a turn and can remove own Bolas (Deluge). Timing discard before opponents' draw steps grants eventual repopulation; repeated damage or a fast finisher is necessary.
