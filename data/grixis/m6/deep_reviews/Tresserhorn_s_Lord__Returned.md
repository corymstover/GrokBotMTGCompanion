## Commander identity and natural ceiling

**Tresserhorn's Lord, Returned** `{2}{U}{B}{R}` 10/4 Zombie Noble with trample and indestructible. Unlike the other Lord of Tresserhorn deck, Returned wants three creatures in play when its ETB trigger RESOLVES, sacrifices those three, pays 3 life, gifts a targeted opponent three cards, and **draws one card for every sacrificed creature**. Without full payment, sacrifices itself and draws one card. A single-purpose token package before turn five is mandatory, not optional cute synergy. Once kept, free sacrifice outlets turn disposable bodies into draw; combo loops with Gravecrawler and The Locust God close via cheap drain or Bombardment, while the 10-power body can attack as a credible 3-hit commander-damage clock if it connects.

**Power bracket:** Source author's natural **Bracket 4 — Optimized**, while engineered automatic *floor* says Bracket3 because of only two Game Changers. These are distinct claims: expected deck capability vs mandatory Game Changer floor; do not silently equate. 21 source-matched Spellbook lines, one two-card infinite with commander context; in practice conditional on fodder, life, board, shuffle/deck size, mana, and target/payoff. Commander is a five-mana heavily colored 3-creature+3-life+gift-3-cards investment, so not a reliable tournament cEDH shell. Respect no-homogenization.

## Mana, early game and sacrifice economics

- **33 front-face land cards** including basic counts; a 34th land-capable choice on the *back* of Boggart Trawler. Land utility High Market, Kher Keep, Shizo, Phyrexian Tower and Cavern of Souls all have timing and color costs, not 5 guaranteed sources of any-color mana. Static source proxy: B20, R16, U16 land sources; repeatable nonland-inclusive B24/R20/U20; reported six repeatable nonland mana sources and one Dark Ritual. Big colored spell skew B49/R12/U5 raw pip equivalents, but exact commander requires *one each* UBR by 5 mana. Reported no-mulligan proxy 78.7% all colors at baseline five resources does not equal casting Tresserhorn and feeding its three-body ETB.
- **Critical deployment constraint:** casting costs `{2}{U}{B}{R}` but with ETB retained has additional sacrifice *at resolution*, 3 life and opponent draws 3. Requires 3 sacrificial creatures besides Tresserhorn available then; if opponent removes creatures/commander before ETB resolution, results differ and can leave the commander to sacrifice itself. Multimodal BMC/Weaponcraft Enthusiast/Mogg War Marshal/Chittering Witch/Tempt with Vengeance supply bodies at different clocks; tokens delayed until end step like Jadar cannot guarantee full three at casting time.
- T1 setup: B→Bitterblossom, Reanimate/Viscera Seer if relevant, one-mana interaction; Sol Ring into outlets/three-cost producer. T2: Mogg War Marshal (two Goblins, then death replacement), Arcane Signet/Fellwar/Talisman or Jadar (one body next end step). T3: Weaponcraft Enthusiast fabricate **two Servos** (3 bodies total), Ophiomancer plus prior producers, BMC, or Mahadi/defensive stack. T4–5: only cast Tresserhorn when three bodies can be paid and three life safely spared, with opponent drawing 3 and some protection if possible. Consider whether delaying Commander for a 2MV counter is worth preserving engine.
- **Resource paradox:** The Lord draws THREE when its three-creature ETB paid. Plumb the Forbidden sacrificing N as casting cost produces *N commander-draw triggers* plus its N+1 spell/copy draws = **2N+1** cards if all resolve; Plumb costs N+1 life across spell and copies. It can self-deck and its sacrifice costs cannot be reversed by countering it. Every Gravecrawler/Locust sacrifice draws a card, so repeatable events need an explicit win condition before the library empties. Mahadi's end-step Treasures based on deaths can convert these sacrifices to future mana; replacing him with one Signet is not categorically superior.
- The Commander being 10/4 naturally requires **three unblocked hits** for 21 commander combat damage at 10 per hit, before buffs; Infantry Shield and Wand of Orcus create a second distinct line (body production + combat), and My Precious provides evasion/protection, but this is a contingency rather than primary sacrifice-to-draw win.

### Ten principal challenged mechanisms and replacement tests

#### Signets vs Mahadi: strongest P1 challenge
Rakdos, Dimir and Izzet Signet cost 2 and filter mana, which can genuinely increase U/B/R commander cast speed when the hand lacks color fixing. The source model marks all three PASS, but treats Mahadi as a one-shot creature and does not value his end-step Treasures from THREE creatures sacrificed for Tresserhorn's entry. Mahadi cannot produce these until end step and requires bodies, so the early rock has real tempo upside; however Mahadi then produces colored recast resources AND is the body sacrificed to another outlet if necessary. The existing 33 front-face lands plus Boggart land back and 6 repeatable rocks make early colored pressure a real weakness. No 300-sample goldfish comparison incorporating three-body setup and opponent draw supports an indisputable cut. Hold KEEP at this specific slot, flag signet vs slower flex as future targeted alternative.

#### Mayhem Devil: 58 candidate attempts to displace actual sacrifice-to-damage
Mayhem Devil sees sacrifices of noncreature Treasures/fetchlands and opposing permanents as well as your creature ETB payments, providing cheap instant triggers and on-board interaction. Dualcaster/Naru spell-copy engines, newer sacrifice-only triggers and tribal payoffs may be strong but do not necessarily turn all three ETB sacrifices into useful early damage/removal. Most candidates require a different conditional loop. The 58 replacement proposals here reveal screening dependence on a single labeled 'weak' card, not 58 independent actual weaknesses.

#### My Precious: combat identity vs generic self-sacrifice
The commander is naturally 10/4 with trample and indestructible and can win with two unblocked commander-damage hits (10 per hit means three normal hits, unless other pump gets it to 11+, so do NOT call it two-hit at base power). My Precious/Allure helps connect and protect while its Adventure uses sacrificing a creature as cost, giving a commander draw. Not of This World can cost zero in response to target effects because power 10≥7. Substituting another altar/body for My Precious trades away a distinct legitimate 10-power pressure line. Equip/Adventure mana and timing must be budgeted.

#### Braids, Black Market Connections, and draw saturation
Tresserhorn's own sacrifice draw works only while he survives the whole ETB gate or is sacrificed to trigger one draw; on turns before that, BMC/Braids/Village Rites/Deadly Dispute/Skullclamp form the setup. Additional Grim Haruspex, Species Specialist and 6MV Liliana provide redundant conditional draws but displace early independent draw. Pay N fodder for Plumb yields 2N+1 cards from Tresserhorn + all spell copies if it stays on field, but draws don't make bodies by themselves.

#### Blasphemous Act vs setup creatures
Cheap in large tables and sometimes the only quick global board reset before Tresserhorn is affordable. Replacing it with token makers/Tombstone Stairwell/Liliana does not stop enemy boards immediately. Blasphemous Edict already in deck is an *actual sacrifice* sweeper but can compel sacrificing Tresserhorn if fewer than 13 others; his indestructibility does not save him. These are complementary panic levers and an explicit risk, not 'upgrade with more on-theme creatures'.

#### Not of This World and targeting protection
The base 10-power commander makes Not of This World {0} against a spell or ability targeting him, important since casting him demands UBR, five mana, three other creatures, three life and gifting an opponent cards. Malevolent Hermit/other generic protection can sometimes stop broad noncreature effects, but at the proposed cut they rarely protect a target at zero mana while retaining sacrifice fodder. No assumption that this counter saves from non-target mass-sacrifice.

#### Free sac outlets / Rooftop Storm / Gravecrawler setup
Tresserhorn himself is a Zombie, so Crawler + Phyrexian Altar makes a B-neutral recurring sacrifice loop while the Lord is present. Every sacrifice causes mandatory draw; you can stop the OPTIONAL sacrifice actions, but resolving already queued draws can lose if library empties. Locust God + free outlet uses commander draw→Insect→sac→draw until stop, likewise not literally infinite draw from a finite library. Extra outlet-only options are redundant until a concrete protection/life-decking benefit is established.

#### Blasphemous Edict, indestructibility and the 3-sac ETB
The Lord's ETB instruction is all-or-nothing on resolution: sacrifice three creatures + 3 life + target opponent drawing 3 or sacrifice the Lord. Paying the three-creature part produces three draw triggers if the Lord stays; even if the Lord himself must be sacrificed his draw trigger still occurs by leaves-the-battlefield lookback rules. An opposing exile spell targeting him before the ETB resolves can prevent the requirement staying useful. Paying three life and gifting an opponent three cards are actual costs to efficiency, not cosmetic.

#### Future-release legality contradiction and authority reconciliation
**Critical: As of 2026-10-20, this new-to-Magic MBC #52 commander is NOT YET officially Commander-legal.** The updated Wizards primary announcement `https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/whats-inside-mystery-booster-commander-edition` states Commander legality begins **November 13, 2026**; official MBC release notes' earlier general legality sentence and source 2026-10-15 verification improperly inferred legality in August without reflecting updated schedule. The engineering rules page and exception registry properly mark pending release and project-authorized planned decks; canonical approval and lock STILL valid as project-authorized future-release deck. Do not silently edit canonical registry/approval or call this officially legal. After the announced date, engineering activation and a fresh actual legality check are necessary before calling it active.

#### Increased originality relative to Lord of Tresserhorn
The encyclopedia separately treats original Lord of Tresserhorn as a drawback-suppression Voltron/Infect deck; Returned embraces the ETB to draw three and fuel Locust God/Gravecrawler aristocrats, with optional 10-power combat. Adding Torpor Orb, global ETB shutoff or reorienting to pure infect/Voltron would make the two decks indistinguishable and negate Returned's core.

- **Flare of Malice:** Efficient edict as free spell by sacrificing nontoken black creature, which triggers Tresserhorn draw; original 19-change source rejected due to existing removal density and sacrifice of valuable setup body.
- **Tainted Strike:** 10-power infect one-hit pressure seems tantalizing, but shifts toward original Lord of Tresserhorn deck and competes with core sacrifice-draw; not an automatic thematic slot.
- **Torpor Orb / Karn, Argent Defender:** Suppresses Tresserhorn's three-sacrifice ETB rather than exploiting it; may preserve 10-power creature but erases commander-native early three sacrifice draw, fundamentally wrong identity.
- **Field of the Dead:** High-land threshold too slow for 33 front-face lands, replacing a colored source worsens 5MV three-color commander cast; no material early three-body advantage.
- **The Meathook Massacre:** Real on-theme wipe and drain, but four/taller mana and red-team originally preferred cheaper Bastion fodder and multiple other wipes/drains; strong metagame fork, not mandatory swap.

- **Potential off-pool basic land upgrade:** Lands are already untapped premium but only 33 front-face + back Boggart and high life usage. Any extra land/ramp change must have precise real weakest cut and owner reopening, not a mechanical Signet replacement of Mahadi.

## Oracle, legality and rules

1. **Not a cast cost:** sacrifice-three, pay-3-life and target opponent draw-3 happen on entering-trigger resolution. To keep commander you must do ALL three, not partial. If you can't, sacrifice Tresserhorn. His own sacrifice triggers his last draw ability even leaving alongside other creatures (official MBC ruling).
2. **Three sacrifices mean three draw triggers:** when three creatures sacrificed in a single resolution Tresserhorn sees each; the draws happen after ETB ability finishes and go on stack separately; removal/other triggered effects can respond. Sacrifice of Food/Treasure alone is not a creature and won't draw.
3. **Must have target opponent:** the ETB targets an opponent for three cards. If every target becomes illegal before resolution it does not resolve, rather than magically collecting three creatures; single legal opponent usually remains at multiplayer table. This is game-dependent and cannot be ignored in lifegain/card-gifting calculations.
4. **Gravecrawler:** can be cast from your graveyard if you control ANOTHER Zombie, and Tresserhorn is one while on battlefield. Phyrexian Altar sac Gravecrawler makes {B}, pays next `{B}` cast. Every death triggers commander draw, which can deck you without a win conversion.
5. **The Locust God:** every drawn card makes one hasty 1/1 Insect; sac Insect to a free outlet triggers commander draw again; if the outlet is Goblin Bombardment, every cycle does one target damage. The loop requires an initial sac/draw, free outlet and both creatures on battlefield, and the library is finite. Stop optional sacrifices before losing to draw from empty library.
6. **Warren Soultrader:** sacrifice-another-creature costs one LIFE per activation, makes Treasure. Crawler loops pay their own B with Treasure but run out of life unless Blood Artist, Bastion or Zulaport returns at least a life per iteration. Marionette Apprentice damages opponents but does NOT directly restore your life.
7. **Plumb the Forbidden:** sacrificing N creatures is additional casting cost, Commander draws N on sacrifice; original spell draws one/loses one life and creates N copies, each draws one/loses one life. Total 2N+1 drawn and N+1 life lost if all resolve; no post-counter undo of sacrifice costs.
8. **Boggart Trawler // Boggart Bog:** modal double-faced land back can be played as land, not both creature and land simultaneously. A static mana estimator counts 33 front-face lands; independent deckbuilding 'land-capable' count 34. No hidden 101st card.
9. **Blasphemous Edict and indestructibility:** sacrificed creatures ignore indestructible. If a player controls fewer than thirteen others, the 10/4 Lord may have to be sacrificed with them; its draw still triggers. This is an **acknowledged retained antisynergy**, not a legality violation.
10. **Nightscape Familiar:** reduces only the generic part of a BLUE or RED spell including Tresserhorn `{2}`→`{1}`, cannot reduce its exact U/B/R pips.
11. **Not of This World:** can be zero-cost because Tresserhorn power 10, counters a spell/ability targeting a permanent you control. Not a broad counter to untargeted mass sacrifice; does not protect if commander already left.
12. **Stubborn Denial:** ferocious hard counter available with Lord (power 10) on board, but precommander it is 'counter unless pay 1' noncreature only. Card not equivalent to an ordinary universal counter when Fled.
13. **My Precious // Allure of Power:** adventure is cast and resolves before Equipment can be cast later from exile; adventure's creature sacrifice is extra cost and draws only while Tresserhorn exists; equipment must be equipped to confer hexproof/unblockable as source reviewed.
14. **Infantry Shield:** mobilize number based on 10 power yields Warrior tokens when applied, then delayed sacrifices create commander draw triggers later if it survives. Counts and triggers must observe equip/action text, not free attack-time ten cards without timing.
15. **Wand of Orcus:** trample + deathtouch can assign minimal lethal damage to creatures then excess to player for Zombie output; equipment and block timing matter, not guaranteed direct damage.
16. **Cavern of Souls:** choose Zombie for the commander if present, protection applies to creature SPELL (counterability) paid with colored ability; it does not prevent ETB being countered by Stifle or the commander being removed on resolution.
17. **Bitterblossom / Ophiomancer:** 1/1 flyers each your upkeep with one life, 1/1 Snake at each upkeep only while none controlled: token production timing cannot be counted as '3 ready right now' immediately after one cast.
18. **Endrek Sahr:** Thrulls depend on creature mana value cast, but seven Thrulls makes him sacrifice himself on trigger; token flood can meet the commander ETB if sequencing safe and Tresserhorn not already on battlefield.
19. **Living Death:** sacrifice old board as part of resolution before graveyard returns (with exile grave setup); Commander sacrificed this way triggers its draw, then leaves; indestructible no defense. Distinguish from destroy wrath Blasphemous Act.
20. **No unsanctioned official legality:** as-of date 2026-10-20 Wizards updated legality states MBC new cards **not legal Commander until Nov13**, not currently legal despite original uncorrected release notes and Oct15 canonical verification. Project authorized future-release deck retains its approved/locked status; do not call it currently officially legal. Recheck activation on/after the date, do not silently overwrite canonical authority.
