## Commander identity and natural ceiling

Mishra costs {2}{U}{B}{R}, 5-mana Human Artificer; **at beginning of combat on YOUR turn**, target **noncreature artifact you control**, make a token copy named **Mishra's Warform** that is a **4/4 Construct artifact creature** in addition to other types, gains haste until end of turn and is sacrificed at beginning of next end step. You need Mishra to survive until your beginning-of-combat trigger, a legal noncreature artifact target, and for expensive activations the right available mana; opponent may remove target in response and make trigger fizzle. Copy preserves printed mana cost/MV, ETB and activated abilities, but not tapped status/counters. Name override can interact with legend rule if two copied legendary artifacts produce same named legendary Warforms; original and Warform have different names.

The deck is a **Warform ETB/death/value conversion and artifact-copy engine**, not generic Breach/Oracle cEDH: Ichor Wellspring and Prized Statue turn each combat into cards/Treasures; Lich's Relic, Mightstone/Weakstone, Spine and Portal create removal; Coveted Jewel draws and generates mana; Mindslaver turns Warform into repeatable turn control; Gonti's Aether Heart + copied trigger/populate provides extra-turn lines; Worldwalker Helm + Urza + Thousand-Year Elixir produces recursive artifact tokens/blue mana. Arcum, Welder, Engineer, Transmute Artifact, Repurposing Bay, KCI and Smelting Vat **convert** expendable Warforms into enduring material. Nine of 19 documented blueprint Spellbook combinations include Mishra, all reported infinite-turn lines are commander-native, unlike many persist-only decks. This high commander dependency and five-mana combat bottleneck support **high Bracket4** rather than manufacturing a Bracket5 rating through unrelated Thoracle/Breach.

## Mana, curve, interaction and identity protection

**Source model** (not empirical cast probability): 34 lands, six fetchlands, 13 fast-mana flags, 16 repeatable nonland mana sources, one one-shot mana source, 4 utility lands, 1 unconditional tapped land, 6 conditional tapped flags. Potential colored land sources **U18/B17/R15**; land+repeatable rocks **U27/B24/R22**, which are not independent guaranteed mana. Colored spell pip demand **U29 (69.05%) / R8 (19.05%) / B5 (11.90%)**; five double-U spells, none double B/R. Turn3 strict three land drop proxy **73.6993%**, all UBR potential land presence **68.961%**; turn4 strict four land drop proxy **56.2728%**, not turn4 Mishra cast probability. Actual commander requires FIVE mana including UBR; Chrome Mox/Mox Diamond/Opal, Vault, Tomb and Sol Ring can accelerate but cost cards/metalcraft/untap/life. `Mishra's Workshop` adds 3 **only for artifact spells**; cannot cast Mishra or pay Worldwalker/Mindslaver activations. `Urza's Saga` and artifact lands add critical artifact density but increase vulnerability to mass artifact destruction. Xander's Lounge tapped and fetchable; `Inventors' Fair`, `Academy Ruins`, `Urza's Saga`, `Mishra's Workshop` are valuable utility/colorless slots but can strand UU/UBR. The deck is blue-intensive **despite Grixis commander**; replacing Flooded Strand with Darkwater Catacombs or Shadowblood Ridge may hurt typed-dual selection.

**Interaction and defense:** Force of Will, Mana Drain, Fierce Guardianship, An Offer, Deflecting Swat, Chaos Warp, Cyclonic Rift, Toxic Deluge, Nihil Spellbomb, Otawara and Lich's Relic Warforms. `Padeem` protects artifacts with hexproof but doesn't protect Mishra creature from removal or prevent board wipes. `Mystic Remora` and `Rhystic Study` fuel interaction while waiting to untap with Mishra; `The One Ring` can refill hand but a Warform copy did NOT enter from being cast and gives no protection. `Arcum` and `Whir` access artifact threats, `Gamble` and `Imperial Seal` cost tempo/card quality. Artifact-hate `Vandalblast` overload, `Null Rod`, `Stony Silence`, `Collector Ouphe` and exile effects attack this deck on different axes; bounce/Chaos Warp/stack defense are necessary, and KCI/Urza mana abilities can bypass some activation restrictions only if their precise wording/rules allow.

**Role balance / cut pressure:** `Cryogen Relic`, `Lich's Relic`, `Coveted Jewel`, `Portal to Phyrexia`, `Spine of Ish Sah`, `Mindslaver` are high-value copy targets but multiple expensive artifacts can clog hand without Welder/Transmuter/Workshop. `Smelting Vat` and `Repurposing Bay` are activation/cost and mana-value gated; `Simulacrum Synthesizer` demands artifact spell casting (Warform **created**, not cast), so its synergy is with the artifact spell shell, not direct Warform triggers. `Aetherflux Reservoir` Warform is a 4/4 that copies spellcast/life-gain text, but Warform creation is NOT casting a spell and should not be credited as spell storm. `Brudiclad` costs 6 and needs tokens, but can convert multiple tokens to Warforms at combat. `Esoteric Duplicator` costs resources and deferred next-end-step trigger. `Thousand-Year Elixir` is poor alone but indispensable for specific Helm copies without haste. `Uthros Research Craft` can be stationed by Mishra in precombat main phase and still trigger Mishra's combat ability, but tapping Mishra can expose the commander to removal and consume potential artifact tap effects.

## Oracle, legality and rules

**Evidence provenance:** Engineering `grixis/interactions/mishra-eminent-one.md` reports nine rules interactions checked against Scryfall Oracle bulk **2026-10-16**, Scryfall rulings **2026-10-15** and CR **2026-09-25**. Canonical `refinement/verification/Mishra_Eminent_One_Approval_Ready_Verification_2026-10-15.md` records the approved candidate's 100-card count, UBR identity and official Commander legality (no exceptions). This is a **dated checkpoint**, not a fresh Oct19 legality requery.

1. **Mishra targets a noncreature artifact you control** when beginning-of-combat trigger is put on stack. If opponent destroys that artifact or turns it into a creature before resolution, no Warform; killing Mishra after his ability triggers does not remove the trigger. Warform copies original copiable artifact values, except name becomes Mishra's Warform, 4/4 Construct artifact creature in addition to other types; it gains haste for the turn and is sacrificed at beginning of next end step. Copied mana cost/MV is original's, **not** 4 from power/toughness. Warform is a *created token*, not a cast spell; it does not trigger Synthesizer's artifact-cast payoff, Reservoir's spell-cast trigger or Research Craft's artifact-cast draw.
2. **Gonti's Aether Heart:** Original Heart plus Warform each trigger when Warform artifact enters: two energy each = four. Need **eight energy** to exile a Heart for an extra turn; `Determined Iteration` and Mishra trigger together at combat. Stack Iteration first, Mishra above; Mishra creates Heart Warform; Iteration populates it (selection on resolution, not targeted) to create another Heart Warform. Two *legendary* Warforms with same name cannot remain together, but artifact entry triggers still provide energy; arrange priority to exile one after enough triggers resolve, and keep the original Heart for subsequent turns. `Worldwalker Helm` creates Maps, also triggering energy. The repeat requires Mishra, Heart, multiplier and available target next extra turn; it is not automatically live with only two cards.
3. **Worldwalker Helm + Urza + Thousand-Year Elixir:** Mishra copies original Helm, Helm replacement makes additional Map. Warform Helm activation **{1}{U}, {T}** creates token copy of an artifact token; it can copy itself. Urza's ability taps artifacts for U, Elixir allows newly created creature Warform copies to activate tap abilities as though they had haste. Copies of Warform retain copiable name/4/4 but **not** the temporary haste granted by Mishra; they are not all subject to Mishra's original end-step sacrifice. Each activation still costs {1}{U}; actual loop needs enough untapped artifact mana resources and newly generated Maps to fund it.
4. **Mindslaver Warform:** Haste lets copied Mindslaver activate {4}, {T}, sacrifice to control target player's next turn; original remains. `Mishra's Workshop` mana is restricted to casting artifact spells and cannot pay this activation. ## Finite sequencing and adversarial scenarios

The following are **manual bounded source-paid scenarios**, not measured games or new simulation. M5 flags 74 candidate cases as mechanics-not-modelable.

**A — early commander, Chrome Mox versus Mind Stone.** Opening `Command Tower`, `Ancient Tomb`, `Blood Crypt`, `Chrome Mox`, `Ichor Wellspring`, `Fierce Guardianship`, `Rhystic Study`. T1 Tower, Chrome Mox imprint blue nonartifact Study. T2 Tomb casts Wellspring for 2 and draws 1. T3 Blood Crypt untapped (pay 2 life), available Tower R, Crypt B, Chrome Mox U, Tomb CC = **{2}{U}{B}{R}**, Mishra casts and combat Warform Wellspring ETB draws one. Replace Chrome Mox with `Mind Stone`: T2 Tomb can cast Stone, T3 Tower+Crypt+Tomb+Stone yields five mana but only Tower/Crypt colored, **no simultaneous UBR**, so no T3 Mishra unless the Wellspring draw found a different colored source. Real bottleneck is **three distinct pips**, not raw mana. If enemy removes Mishra before combat no Warform; if after trigger on stack the Warform still arrives unless artifact target becomes illegal.

**B — KCI versus rock under removal.** With Mishra, original Ichor Wellspring and `Krark-Clan Ironworks`, combat creates hasty Warform Wellspring; ETB draw one. Sac Warform to KCI as mana ability for CC and draw from copied death trigger. If opponent casts Swords to Plowshares targeting Warform, sacrificing it in response pays KCI cost, removes legal target and still draws. `Mind Stone` replacing KCI can draw by sacrificing itself for {1}, but cannot sacrifice Warform or produce this two-card engine; the M5 `pass` fails to account for that loss.

**C — Heart plus Determined Iteration.** Mishra, original `Gonti's Aether Heart`, `Determined Iteration`, zero energy. At beginning combat put Iteration trigger below Mishra trigger. Mishra copies original Heart as legendary Warform; original Heart and Warform Heart each give two energy on Warform ETB, total **four**. Iteration populates Warform Heart, producing another legendary Warform. The original Heart, first Warform and new Warform each trigger on artifact entry (up to six energy); legend rule removes one Warform, but its ETB triggers remain on stack. With at least eight energy, exile surviving Warform as activation cost for extra turn; original Heart remains for next turn. If opponent removes original Heart before Mishra resolves, trigger loses target; if opponent removes Mishra before next combat, no repeat.

**D — Helm/Urza/Elixir costs.** Mishra copies original `Worldwalker Helm`; original Helm replacement makes a Map as Warform enters. `Urza, Lord High Artificer` can tap Helm/Map for U, paying Warform Helm's **{1}{U}, {T}** to create a copy of artifact token Warform. New copy lacks Mishra-granted haste, but `Thousand-Year Elixir` permits its tap activation, and Helm creates another Map. Each iteration requires real {1}{U}; use newly made Maps and Urza tap-mana, not imaginary free activations. Removing Elixir stops newly made Warforms from immediately tapping; removing Urza stops renewable blue mana. `Sculpting Steel` does not substitute for haste permission.

**E — Mindslaver Workshop trap.** A hasty Warform copy of `Mindslaver` may pay **{4}**, tap, sacrifice to control an opponent's next turn. Original Mindslaver remains. `Mishra's Workshop` provides {C}{C}{C} **only to cast artifact spells**, not to pay Mindslaver's activated cost. Workshop plus one Island cannot activate the Warform, despite displaying four mana symbols across lands. Enemy can remove original Mindslaver in response to Mishra targeting it, fizzling the copy; once Warform's ability is activated and paid, killing original doesn't undo control.

**F — Repurposing Bay and MV.** Mishra copies `Ichor Wellspring` (MV2), draws one. In postcombat main use `Repurposing Bay` at its allowed activation timing and pay its printed activation cost, sacrifice Warform, draw one from death, and tutor **MV3** artifact if Bay's activation conditions are met. The token is 4/4 but **MV2**, not MV4; cannot use that Warform to directly search Portal to Phyrexia MV9. `Transmute Artifact` also uses copied mana value, and its sacrifice occurs on resolution.

**G — Ring versus Jewel.** Mishra copies `The One Ring`: token was not cast, so no protection; token has zero burden counters before first tap, tapping puts first counter and draws **one**. It does not inherit original Ring's counters. Warform `Coveted Jewel` draws **three** on ETB and can tap for three mana due haste, but is a six-mana artifact and original Jewel can be stolen on opposing combat damage. Jewel's immediate value favors Warform combat; Ring original protects on casting and accumulates draws in long games.


**H — Panharmonicon versus Iteration.** With Mishra, Wellspring and `Panharmonicon`, a Wellspring Warform enters, draws **two** from doubled ETB, then one on death: **three** cards. With `Determined Iteration`, Mishra Warform ETB draws one; populate creates another Wellspring Warform ETB draw one; both are sacrificed at next end step and draw once each: **four** cards, if they survive. Iteration also provides two bodies for KCI/Arcum and populates Heart for extra turns. Replacing it with a generic ETB doubler is not strictly superior.

**I — graveyard/activation hate.** Against `Null Rod`, activated nonmana abilities of artifacts are shut off: Mindslaver, Helm, Top and many artifact engines stop, but Mishra's triggered Warform ETB still occurs. `Collector Ouphe` can be removed by `Toxic Deluge` or `Chaos Warp` before committing to combo. Against overloaded `Vandalblast`, Padeem's artifact hexproof does not protect the artifact board because overload does not target. `Deflecting Swat` cannot redirect a spell that has no targets. Adding a generic mana rock is not protection against Rod or Vandalblast.

**J — Gamble versus Fabricate.** With five cards in hand before resolving `Gamble`, searching for Gonti's Heart results in six cards before random discard: chance Heart is discarded is **one in six** assuming all six equally likely. `Fabricate` finds Heart without random discard but costs 2U sorcery instead of R, often delaying Mishra. `Goblin Welder` or Emry can reuse a discarded artifact only if their own costs, targets and tap restrictions are met. Fabricate improves certainty in slower tables; Gamble improves tempo and finds nonartifacts. No universal replacement demonstrated.

**K — adversarial stack and natural ceiling.** Opponent A has `Force of Will`, B has `Vandalblast`, C threatens `Thassa's Oracle`. If Mishra is countered, `Fierce Guardianship` isn't free unless commander already controlled, so pay 2U or use `Force of Will`/Mana Drain. With Mishra in play, Fierce can counter overloaded Vandalblast; Swat cannot redirect its no-target overload. `An Offer You Can't Refuse` and Fierce Guardianship cannot counter Oracle (creature spell), whereas Force of Will/Mana Drain can. These defenses exist but do not justify describing this five-mana combat engine as cEDH-speed.
