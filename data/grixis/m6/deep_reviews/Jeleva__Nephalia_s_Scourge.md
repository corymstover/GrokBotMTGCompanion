## Commander identity and natural ceiling
**Jeleva `{1}{U}{B}{R}` (four-mana 1/3 flying Vampire Wizard)**: on entering each player exiles TOP X cards of own library where X is mana ACTUALLY SPENT casting **this** Jeleva; first normal paid cast X=4, first recast with 2 tax X=6, no mana spent if creature PUT onto BF without being CAST X=0. Cannot voluntarily overpay; cost reduction can reduce X. Exiles include every player's cards, but current Jeleva can cast ONE exiled instant/sorcery on each declared ATTACK via triggered permission without paying mana cost, before blockers. A fresh/blinked Jeleva is a NEW object linked to a NEW exile batch and cannot cast OLD cohort (those remain exiled otherwise). Trigger cast may use your or opponent's exiled instant/sorcery; any such card must be CAST while attack trigger resolves, ordinary sorcery timing overridden, other restrictions and mandatory additional costs persist. Alternative casting costs (overload) cannot be paid in lieu of free cost, X in printed cost defaults 0.
**Unique core:** TOP STAGING (Brainstorm/Scroll Rack/Sensei Top, Mystical/Vampiric/Imperial/Personal Tutor, Scheming Symmetry, Long-Term Plans) → pay/CAST Jeleva and exile a known huge spell → give HASTE (Greaves/Courier/Arena) → attack and cast Peer into the Abyss, Mind's Desire, Mnemonic Betrayal, Doomsday, Yawgmoth's Will or an opponent spell. Harmonic Prodigy, while Jeleva Wizard triggers, doubles both ETB (two sequential X-card exile sets from every player) and ATTACK cast triggers (choose up to two DISTINCT available exiled spells, one each ability). `Borne Upon a Wind` is useful for non-Jeleva timing, NOT needed merely to cast sorcery from Jeleva attack.
**Natural ceiling owner-approved BRACKET 5 — fringe cEDH**. October14 source references nonzero 2026 tournament representation but no demonstrated top-tier large-event conversion; avoid implying top-competitive results. Compact Oracle+Consultation/Pact/Doomsday and Breach+LED+Brain Freeze are legitimate backups but must NOT replace staged Jeleva exile package, or 62 decks homogenize. Limits: commander 4MV 1/3 fragile, summoning sickness, life cost from Ad Naus, 27 lands/fast rocks, high spells stranded in HAND and contingent top-of-library exposure to fetch/mill/shuffle/opponents.

### Mana, curve, protection, interaction and win conditions
**27 land, all unique.** UBR source three colored symbols on Jeleva 1UBR: Tomb/Sol Ring/Vault/Monolith offer GENERIC only; need island/swamp/red dual/fixing. Arena of Glory must see existing Mountain subtype to ENTER untapped, and must exert with {R},tap for {R}{R} and spend generated mana on CASTING Jeleva to grant haste; ordinary R tap cannot. Gemstone Caverns conditional opening counter, Opal three artifact metalcraft, Mox Diamond consumes scarce land, Traitors sacrifices to next land play. Control of a named spell on top before Jeleva ETB is a race vs own draw, enemy instant library change and Jeleva removal. First cast only 4 cards/library; harmonic duplicate sees next sequential four, not twice same cards.
**Ramp:** Petal, Chrome/Opal/Diamond, Mana Vault/Grim/Sol Ring, Signet/Fellwar/2 Talismans, Dark/Cabal/Rite/Simian, Ancient Tomb/Traitors, Jeska's Will mana under own turn or free cast. LED discards full hand so must commit Jeleva/stack/protection first, and its Breach recursion not free without grave fodder.
**Card access:** Mystic/Rhystic/Necro/Ad Naus, wheels, Ponder/Preordain/Probe, instant Vamp/Mystical/Long-Term Plans/Brainstorm, Personal/Imperial/Scheming sorcery tops, Scroll Rack/Top self stage, tutor to hand Demonic/Gamble/Wishclaw. `Long-Term Plans` is exceptionally commander-positive: third from top lies within first paid cast X4; after X reduced below3 it can fail, and it can tutor NONinstant card which Jeleva won't cast from exile, so choose instant/sorcery consciously. Unlike Kess, Jeleva doesn't grant cast-from-exile for other effects indiscriminately, only its own current exiles.
**Interaction & defense:** free Fierce/Swat now actually work if Jeleva controlled and can cover her attack (contrast Inalla CZ); Negation free ONLY on opposing turn not normal own attack. Force Will, Pact, Fluster/Misstep/Swan/Offer, Mindbreak Trap, red blasts, Chain/Flood bounce, Opposition Agent search-hate, Hexing defensive. With 1/3 body an opponent removal spell before Jeleva declares attacker prevents attack cast, and removal while attack trigger already on stack does not erase independent trigger but may change associated exile access? ability uses last-known identity, prior exiles still associated with that object as it existed, pending trigger can generally resolve; legal casts depend specific conditions. Greaves shroud stops targeting Jeleva but not its own attack/ETB.
**Wins and payoffs:** Jeleva staged high spell as fastest identity win; Mind's Desire storm copies free cards but requires prior CAST count; Peer half-library/half-life draw, high 7MV Ad Naus hit and opponent Bowmasters risk; Mnemonic Betrayal requires paying downstream opponent GY spells despite its own free Jeleva cast; Doomsday must assemble five cards and loses half life with normal restrictions; Yawgmoth's Will grants grave CAST at normal costs/one turn and exiles used cards. Companion-free Oracle/Pact/Consult and Breach/LED/Freeze backup. Jeleva recasting later increases X (unless cost reductions) but requires more mana/commander tax and reduces probability of protected immediate haste attack.
**Higher-cost cards:** Peer7/Mind's Desire6 produce exceptional value at zero attack-mana but painful from Ad Nauseam; compare cutting a high payoff against lowering Jeleva identity itself. `Borne Upon a Wind` 1U and `Harmonic Prodigy` 1R are not interchangeable: former helps cast ordinary spells at flash timing, latter increases Jeleva triggers only if predeployed.

## Oracle, legality and rules
1. [Jeleva official rulings](https://mtg.wtf/card/c13/194/Jeleva-Nephalias-Scourge): ETB actual MANA SPENT CASTING, including tax, first normal cast4, second paid6, blink or reanimate without cast => X0; cast with cost reduction counts lower paid mana. Associated exiled cards belong to object instance, not all copies/casts named Jeleva; new object can't access previous exiles even if still exiled.
2. Jeleva attack triggered permission CASTS while ability resolves BEFORE blockers, ignores type-based sorcery timing; chosen card cannot be recast after first use; must pay any MANDATORY additional cost, may pay kicker optional cost, can't pay alternative OVERLOAD cost, printed X must be ZERO without paying mana cost. Card controlled by opponent may be cast by you if exiled with current Jeleva and after resolution goes to owner grave if normal.
3. Harmonic Prodigy causes Jeleva Wizard ETB trigger twice for X each (top4 then next top4 in first paid case), attack trigger twice to cast one spell each if available; cannot cast same card a second time after it left exile. Prodigy must exist when Jeleva-trigger conditions happen, can't retroactively double ETB if cast later.
4. `Arena of Glory` untapped only if pre-existing Mountain subtype; pay {R},tap EXERT gets RR, use ONE on CAST Jeleva for HASTE. If it enters tapped no same-turn source haste; exert misses next untap. [Wizards MH3 notes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/modern-horizons-3-release-notes).
5. `Long-Term Plans` searches any card THIRD from top, Jeleva first four exiles top4, so staged card accessible if instant/sorcery and no top change in response. `Scheming Symmetry` two chosen players top cards both captured when Jeleva exiles top≥1 of EACH; opponents can react via instant draws/shuffles before Jeleva ETB.
6. `Brainstorm` puts two cards from HAND on top, `Scroll Rack` swaps hand with top deck and puts exiled hand chosen order to TOP after draw, `Sensei's Divining Top` sequences top3. Topping an exiled opponent card to Jeleva is NOT automatically allowed; only own tutors stage own and Symmetry opponents.
