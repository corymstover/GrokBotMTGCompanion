## Commander identity and natural ceiling

**Identity:** Grixis resource-denial stax, not generic Kraum/Tevesh turbo. Tevesh (4B, starting loyalty 4) creates two 0/1 Thrulls with +2 and converts another creature/planeswalker into two cards (+1; three if sacrificed commander). Thrulls are asymmetric feed for Contamination, Skullclamp, Culling the Weak, Diabolic Intent, Phyrexian Tower, and Tangle Wire. Kraum (3UR flying haste 4/4) draws when **an opponent casts their second spell each turn**: one trigger per opponent per turn, and stax taxes may prevent that threshold. Kraum is a secondary clock, not a reliable unlimited engine; don't overstate his commander-dependence when opponents stop casting.

Natural ceiling is **Bracket 5, rogue/fringe cEDH** per approved decision file, not proof of current tournament conversion. Protect early artifact resources and prison density, use Tevesh Thrulls to break parity, preserve win paths via Helm + Leyline of the Void/Dauthi Voidwalker and Breach + LED + Brain Freeze. The finish packages are **commander-independent**: this is an honest identity cost, offset by commander-specific denial parity. Antigoals: generic Oracle-Consultation package, hard one-spell caps (Kraum/Breach antisynergy), rebuilding as turbo Ad Nauseam, gratuitous mill payoffs. No commander swap or canonical edits.

**Structural reconciliation.** 26 lands, exactly 3 basics (Island/Mountain/Swamp), diverse fetches and original ABUR duals/shocks: powerful untaxed fixing but dramatically sensitive to Blood Moon/Magus and Back to Basics if basics not fetched before lock. One plain Island and one Mountain are thin under blue-heavy counterspell/Breach turns. The mana package has Sol Ring, Vault, Monolith, 3 zero-Mox, Chrome Mox, Lotus Petal, LED and 2 Talismans; metalcraft, legendary presence, discarded land and mana-color restrictions must not be conflated with guaranteed opening mana. Tevesh and Kraum both cost five, so this low-land list is explicitly dependent on burst artifacts, not a standard casual ramp curve. 19 mapped denial cards (including Helm partner Leyline), 16–17 stack/removal effects, 13 search/draw tools, 3 burst rituals, multiple combo/pressure roles. Role labels do not imply 19 independent hard locks; redundant pieces overlap and self-tax. Primary defects: colored-mana self-lock, creature pressure on Tevesh, inability to cast Breach chains under own Chalice/Damping Sphere/Trinisphere, artifact-wipe sensitivity, weak second-spell draw frequency when prison succeeds, costly 4–5 MV threats in fast pods.

## Oracle, legality and rules

**Partner color:** Kraum UR + Tevesh B = UBR; 2 commanders count within 100, and the official deck has no intentional ban exception. Existing approved gate attests Oct14 full Commander legality; fresh external 100-name Oracle recheck remains out of scope, and this review does not falsely label it newly validated.

**Tevesh and feeds:** Skullclamp makes a 0/1 Thrull 1/0, state-based death draws two. Sacrifice to Contamination is at start of own upkeep, before a new +2 that turn; opponents killing both Thrulls can force Contamination away. Tevesh cannot activate loyalty on opponent turns without a special effect. `Phyrexian Tower` may turn a Thrull into BB but is itself shut into a Mountain by Blood Moon. `Culling the Weak` sacrifices at additional cost, so cannot be countered into undoing the sacrifice. Tevesh +1 gets three only if sacrificed creature or planeswalker **was a commander**, not automatically from any Thrull.

**Helm line:** Helm 4 generic cast, then activation X=1 plus tap with opponent target; Leyline/Voidwalker replacement prevents opponent's milled cards from hitting graveyard so neither stop condition occurs and targeted library is exiled. This **does not immediately win** without a required draw from empty library or an additional effect; removal of replacement before resolution defeats assumed all-library result. Commander Spellbook independently documents Voidwalker/Helm at https://commanderspellbook.com/combo/2581-5065/ . For Leyline/Helm see https://edhrec.com/combos/helm-of-obedience . Leyline affects opponents only, allowing own Breach GY fuel.

**Breach:** Escape still pays printed mana **and exiles three other graveyard cards**; LED must discard whole hand to generate three of one color. Brain Freeze storm copies are copied, not cast. A loop is not infinite without repeated payable costs, grave cards and cards to cast. Chalice X=0 counters cast LEDs/Petals, Trinisphere prices nominally free spells at three (if untapped), Damping Sphere taxes each later spell. Sequence/prison release required before trying to Breach.

**Other self-tax:** Overburden checks nontoken creatures, so Thrulls bypass; Torpor Orb prevents ETB triggers of creatures (including own Bowmasters) but not Overburden's triggered enchantment ability. Kraum's second-spell trigger remains possible under Damping Sphere but opponents can choose not to cast again. Karn -2 wishes from outside game cannot be treated as Commander sideboard tutor absent a table-specific special rule. Karn + stax can stop Wishclaw activation after donating to opponent; check timing. Blood Moon removes activated abilities of own nonbasic lands. `Tainted Pact` may dig past every singleton but this list does not play Oracle. `Necropotence` hand discards are exiled, reducing Breach fuel.

## Finite sequencing and adversarial scenarios

**Manual, conditional rules sequences — no measured win-rate, goldfish distribution, full-source paid simulator or opponent AI.**

1. **Turn one acceleration:** land + Sol Ring + Winter Orb may be payable, but Orb itself doesn't fix UR/BB for both five-mana commanders; with Chalice X=0 Mox/LED casts self-counter.
2. **Tevesh setup:** Turn 2/3 Tevesh (4B) requires enough fast mana; activate +2 for two Thrulls. If opponent removes both before Contamination upkeep, sacrifice cannot be paid, enchantment sacrificed; don't assume permanent lock.
3. **Tevesh draw:** Next own turn +1 sacrifice a Thrull draw two; or +2 two Thrulls; cannot do both normally. With Skullclamp, each equipped Thrull dies to state-based actions and draws two, mana to equip required.
4. **Artifact wipe:** Opponent removes Mox/Sol Ring/Orb; remaining 26-land base plus own Back to Basics can strand lock pieces and UR requirements. Persistent enchantments are not automatically a win.
5. **Blood Moon:** fetch Swamp and Island before Moon to preserve BB/U pips; Moon also overrides Tower, Command Tower and color access from nonbasics, but artifact rocks still cast colored mana if on battlefield.
6. **Kraum under Damping Sphere:** opponent casts spell A, then pays surcharge and casts B, Kraum draws one. An opponent who casts only A gives Kraum zero cards. Chains would further reduce own draws.
7. **Helm on turn N:** Lay 4-mana Helm and Voidwalker (BB) or Leyline (2BB unless opening). Activate X=1; targeted player can remove the replacement while Helm on stack; then stop after mill count/creature, not assume exile all.
8. **Breach taxed:** Cast 1R Breach, escape LED and Brain Freeze paying their costs + three other GY cards each; with active Damping Sphere, later spells increasingly costly; with Trinisphere 0-cost LED requires 3; own Chalice X=0 counters LED.
9. **Karn versus artifact pods:** Karn shuts activated abilities of **opponents'** artifacts; own Mox works. His outside-game wish is not a reliable sanctioned sideboard line.
10. **Station challenger:** Station + Breach/LED recursion can generate extra self-mill under a payable board, but loses Tangle Wire's taxing phase. Against turn-two opposing mana creatures, Tevesh+Wire can buy more time than Station, and Torpor Orb only stops relevant ETB abilities.
11. **Signet challenger:** On turn 1 land+0-mana Chrome Mox, casting a 2-mana Signet potentially uses entire available first-turn mana, whereas 0-mana Amber does not fix absent commander but can be deployed earlier for future metalcraft. Need source-paid opening-hand comparisons to choose.
12. **Counter-war:** Force of Negation free only during opponent turn, Fierce Guardianship/Deadly Rollick only free with either commander controlled; with Trinisphere on board, even nominally free protection still costs at least 3, unless special rules override.
