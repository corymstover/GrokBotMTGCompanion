## Commander identity and natural ceiling

Anhelo, the Painter costs **{U}{B}{R}**, is a 1/3 Vampire Assassin with deathtouch, and gives the **first instant or sorcery spell you cast EACH TURN** **casualty 2**. Sacrificing a power ≥2 creature as an optional ADDITIONAL spell-casting cost creates one copy when casualty trigger resolves; copy is NOT cast. Identity must prioritize **recurring cheap power-2 fodder on each turn cycle**, instant-speed impactful spells, copied card advantage/tutors/removal/extra turns, and magecraft payoffs that trigger on COPY rather than only CAST. With Anhelo removed, generic Grixis storm+copy spells still function but only weakly justify 2/2 Zombie/Fractal/manifest infrastructure; commander is essential.

**Natural ceiling:** optimized Bracket 4 spike potential from 3-MV commander, free interaction, copy triggers and real Dualcaster/Twinflame/Reiterate wins, but needs fodder, spell-cast sequencing and mana across opponents' turns. Some independent compact loops could approach high-power/cEDH in lucky hands, yet no tested sustained cEDH matchup proof; preserve spell-copy-and-sacrifice identity rather than replacing with Oracle/Consultation goodstuff.

**Card access:** Dig Through Time casualty is a deep two-resolution library selection at one delve-paid original cost; each copy yields cards and fuels spell triggers. Vampiric/Mystical Tutor as instant topdeck searches **do not become two extra cards on being copied** — final shuffle/top placement often undoes first placement. **Interaction:** premium Counterspell/Mana Drain/An Offer, Deadly Rollick/Fierce Guardianship/Swat/Chaos Warp/Rift/Feed/Toxic Deluge and Ral/Narset's Reversal provide considerable protection but do not all benefit from casualty copies: after the first copy counters the only targeted spell, remaining identical targeted copies may fizzle if no other spell targets exist. Copying An Offer can also hand opposing Treasure value, a meaningful failure. The 35-land engine can pass mana on opponent turns to use Anhelo again if fodder remains. Lack of targeted GY hate is a real metagame slot concern.

**Copy versus cast:** Anhelo's first-spell grant and printed casualty are not the same as Twinning Staff's replacement; Staff adds one copy every time something would copy a spell one or more times. Veyran doubles certain *permanent* triggered magecraft abilities (Archmage, Ral, Deekah, Storm-Kiln, Talrand cast trigger) but doesn't directly make casualty a second cast or double the spell's own casualty trigger. Talrand, Stitcher, Cryptic Pursuit and Judith check CAST, while Archmage/Deekah/Ral/Storm-Kiln check CAST OR COPY. Poppet Factory back face disables Stitcher's token-generation while it is transformed but gives base 3/3 and removes decayed abilities from existing tokens.

**Timing and combo boundaries:**
1. Commander casualties triggered on **first instant or sorcery CAST each individual player's turn**, not first spell of any card type. Copy is NOT cast and won't consume a cast limit. A second instant after the first (even if first was countered) does NOT get Anhelo's grant. Casting one on opponent turn grants casualty again if eligible.
2. Sacrifice is an **additional casting cost**, not triggered sacrifice on resolution. Once cast, opponent cannot destroy the chosen creature "in response" to stop already-paid casualty. If creature must be power≥2, verify actual power AS additional cost paid (not printed base alone).
3. A Little Chat has PRINTED casualty 1 and receives separate casualty 2 from Anhelo; pay either/both with appropriate **distinct** bodies, get one copy per paid casualty (plus Staff extra copy when applicable); not one sacrifice covering both costs.
4. Magecraft Archmage/Deekah/Storm-Kiln/Ral respond to casts AND copies; Talrand/Poppet/Cryptic/Judith trigger only CAST (Cryptic requires FROM HAND). Veyran doubles abilities of a PERMANENT from casts/copies, not the spell-stack casualty trigger itself.
5. Copying a single counter target may create one counter success and later copies with no legal target. An Offer supplies opponents Treasures; do not count copies as multiple protection spells unless separate legal target spells. Two copies of an un-targeted tutor that puts searched card INTO HAND (Demonic Tutor) produce real separate value; two Vampiric/Mystical topdeck searches shuffle/reposition one on top and normally no two-card value without an intervening draw.
6. Dualcaster Mage + Twinflame: Start Twinflame (1R) targeting ONE other own creature; flash Dualcaster (1RR) with original pending; Dualcaster ETB copies Twinflame targeting Dualcaster itself. A hasty Dualcaster token enters, ETB can copy pending original Twinflame targeting latest token, repeat arbitrary finite times before letting original resolve. Attacking with tokens requires combat availability, not through protection; opponents can remove/counter at several windows. **No Anhelo casualty payment required.**
7. Reiterate {1}{R}{R} + buyback {3} = **6 mana per recast**; with original Mana Geyser on stack yielding **N red where N = total opponent TAPPED lands** at each resolution, N≥6 can sustain repetitive copy-cast cycles if you can initially pay 6, N≥7 produces unbounded red. If opponent untaps lands (not normally until turn change) count may change. Jeska's Will's mana-mode payoff similarly requires N≥7 *opponent-hand cards* and same modes chosen on original while commander controlled. A claimed unbounded line with fewer resources is invalid.
8. Narset's Reversal copies targeted spell and returns original to owner HAND; Staff adds copies but does not make a free, recurring self-cast of Narset's Reversal. **No demonstrated arbitrary infinite solely from Narset's Reversal + Twinning Staff**; older blueprint's broad infinite description is not independently certified here. Reiterate/stack context may enable finite chains and requires individual proof; don't count infinite without payable steps.
9. Primal Amulet only gains charge counters on inst/sorc CAST; after four transforms into Wellspring. Its colored mana can duplicate later cast spell when SPENT, not copies or current spell that caused flip. Transform to land doesn't use your land drop.
10. Army of the Damned 13 tapped 2/2 Zombies per resolution; copied 26, tapped Zombies may still be sacrificed for casualty of a LATER spell/turn; can't pay original casting cost retroactively.
11. Galvanic Iteration first cast may itself get casualty, duplicate delayed copy next inst/sorc; next spell CAST even if it's your second won't get Anhelo casualty, but Iteration may still copy it. Original and copy spell triggers must be kept separate.
12. Ral/Storm-Kiln Artist may convert valid copy loops to ping/Treasure; infinite claims need a real repeated cast/stack/mana loop, not number of combo cards in deck.

## Finite sequencing and adversarial scenarios

**A — first-turn casualty threshold (no magic token in the past):** Have Anhelo, Jadar and no decayed token at your beginning of end step. Jadar creates 2/2 decayed there; next opponent turn cast Startle as first instant, sacrifice Zombie as casualty COST. Startle copied, target creature (two legal choices or one existing target) gets -2/-0 as appropriate; in the ordinary no-removal case get TWO 2/2 Zombies, draw TWO cards, plus cast/copy triggers from Archmage/Ral/Storm-Kiln if on BF. If opponent removes only target creature before all spell resolutions, those targeted Startles fizzle and produce no tokens/draw. If start with NO fodder and cast Startle first, Anhelo's casualty cannot use a token made by Startle itself, so only original resolves and later token can be used on another player's turn.

**B — separate double-casualty:** Anhelo on battlefield, two 2/2 Zombie tokens from prior spells, A Little Chat in hand first instant this turn. As cast, sacrifice first Zombie to printed casualty 1, second to added casualty 2. If both costs paid, one original + TWO copy resolutions = examine top two, choose one to hand, bottom one, repeated THREE times; this is three cards acquired, not six from top six. With Twinning Staff, each casualty copy event is increased 1, total original + FOUR copies = FIVE separate resolutions and five cards if library sufficient. Triggering Ghoulish Procession is NOT guaranteed from these token sacrifices.

**C — copy-value versus copy-fizzle:** Anhelo + single 2/2 fodder, opponent puts exactly one noncreature spell on stack. Cast An Offer You Can't Refuse (first instant turn) targeting it, sacrifice 2/2, produce copy. If copy counters opposing spell, original has no legal target and doesn't resolve or gift another two Treasures. This is NOT a value-double; with two distinct spells on stack, legal retargeting could make it remove two but give Treasures to corresponding controllers. Replacing with Counterspell similar duplicate target issue but no Treasure drawback. On YOUR turn cast Demonic Tutor first sorcery with casualty to get two tutor-to-hand resolutions, much better independent value.

**D — Plumb quantified vs fodder:** Anhelo + THREE distinct 2/2 Zombies on board (plus optional Archmage Emeritus). Cast Plumb as first instant this turn, sacrificing TWO zombies for Plumb own added cost and the third separately for Anhelo casualty. Resolve original + two Plumb copies + one casualty copy = FOUR cards drawn and four life lost if no intervention; if Archmage survived, cast/copy triggers add four EXTRA drawn cards. If you sacrificed Archmage to pay instead, its magecraft cannot trigger while it's gone. With Twinning Staff, copy effects yield original + THREE from Plumb copying-two + TWO from casualty = SIX draw/life. Each copy does NOT cost additional creatures, but your board now has zero zombies, so next-turn Anhelo casualty is not guaranteed. Opponent anti-draw effects or insufficient library can be fatal. This line proves synergy, not net structural superiority.

**E — Reiterate exact mana:** Opponents collectively have seven tapped lands and no untap interaction, you have initial 6 red available, Mana Geyser spell on stack. Cast Reiterate for base 1RR and buyback 3, targeting Geyser, pay 6R. Reiterate resolves, returns to your hand via buyback, copied Mana Geyser resolves to produce 7R, original Mana Geyser remains on stack. Repeat paying 6R out of 7R and generating 7R while original waits. Each iteration nets 1R and 1 cast plus copy triggers; Ral can ping, Storm-Kiln produces Treasures. With six tapped opponent lands only, repeat is mana-neutral and still makes repeated CAST/COPY events if no reaction; no infinite RED mana. With five, cannot self-sustain without an additional resource. Countering original Geyser or Reiterate at an appropriate priority window disrupts.

**F — hostile matchups:** Soul-Guide Lantern/Nihil Spellbomb can exile Breach fuel and Kess/Ascendancy targets but do not negate already cast spell copy trigger; a boardwipe killing your three 2/2 tokens before casting next instant leaves you with no casualty 2. Rule of Law restricts FIRST inst/sorc each turn but casualty copies aren't cast, so Anhelo still gets a legal copy when fodder available; Teferi-style flash restriction reduces opponent-turn instants. Graveyard hate alone doesn't stop an instant copied from hand; commander removal does prevent future grants but existing spell already on stack retains its casualty ability. Copying Time Warp into extra turns has political/opponent priority windows; no claim of guaranteed deterministic win.

**What was and was not tested:** Current authorized source, 94 card Oracle cards, aggregate 100, nine repeated basic copies, combo mana arithmetic and stack order, prioritized candidate fit. Not yet tested with a full opponent-response Forge simulator, matched mana-source cast-rate experiments or actual 4-player outcomes. M5 266+29 rankings are projection work, not gameplay proof. ## Conclusions and unresolved evidence

**STRATEGIC HOLD** on current 100 approved cards; the strongest new challenger is Plumb the Forbidden as a commander-specific turn-cycle card-draw/magecraft amplifier, but its multi-creature cost and life loss oppose sustained casualty-2 resource availability and no material, lower-cost cut was established. Original Jadar/Cryptic/Deekah/Startle suite is coherent and source-approved. The previously described "Narset's Reversal + Twinning Staff infinite" is **not independently demonstrated**, a documentation correction / unresolved combo proof issue, not a reason to change deck. Topdeck-tutor copy value is also limited by shuffle sequencing. Neither issue alters the current 100-card legality or permits an unauthorized edit.
