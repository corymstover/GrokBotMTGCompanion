## Commander identity and natural ceiling

Sauron {5}UBR, 9/9 legendary Avatar Horror with trample. **WHEN CAST** trigger goes on stack ABOVE Sauron and RESOLVES independently, does `amass Orcs 5; mill five; then return a creature card from your graveyard to the battlefield` in that order. This effect **does not target** the creature: you choose among creature cards available during resolution, including those just milled. Opponents may respond to trigger itself or exile graveyard BEFORE it begins, but **cannot interrupt BETWEEN its mill five and reanimation**. Countering 8-mana commander SPELL does NOT remove cast trigger; countering activated/triggered ability (Stifle-like) or replacing GY arrival can. Commander {5}UBR requires 5 generic + U B R; no miracle low CMC fantasy. Secondary `Whenever a commander an opponent controls dies, the Ring tempts you` generates Ring/Nazgûl backup and is influenced by opponents' choices about commander going to CZ.

**Personal identity**: colossal Sauron CAST reanimation trigger and secondary thematic Ring/Nazgûl, backed by exact cheap GY creature selection, ETB chaining into Fanatic/Archon, multiple reanimate Auras/sorceries, Ancient Brass Dragon/Jin/Valgavoth enemy threats, Wonder/Anger keyword support, fast mana to cast Sauron sooner, and control to protect boss. **Natural ceiling Bracket 4 Optimized**, not force generic cEDH Oracle+Breach package. The commander can be countered and STILL reanimate, so eight mana sometimes buys two threats, but absent fast mana/hand resources its clock remains slow.

**Structural stresses**: 32 lands and only six fetches; 2B/4 mana accelerators still leave exact UBR; very large creature top-end is poor naturally cast, poor GY access from unfiltered draws; engine has 4 Nazgûl and Ring cards competing with reanimate-target slots but intentionally retained for flavor; `Jin-Gitaxias`, `Sheoldred`, `Ancient Brass Dragon` do not immediately reanimate another creature and may be removable before accruing value. Board sweeps and graveyard hate end high-cost efforts. Play meaningful answers rather than eliminate all flavorful cards.

**Strongest candidate counterargument:** Intuition 2U immediately tutors exactly 3 selected, two grave; losing Forbidden Alchemy's unconditional look4 hand selection of *ANY* spell, land, draw or shield can strand a hand with three legendary monsters but NO Reanimate. Piles can be designed to make every branch work only with other setup. Hence no guarantee of exact two-card win from 2U. Its impact needs opening-hand/turn probability simulation and opponent-target choices. No canonical swap applied.

**Weakest incumbent challenge**: Grave Researcher 2B body with upkeep slow and require 3 creatures already GY then prepared B copy, much worse than direct B Reanimate. ## Oracle, legality and rules

1. **Sauron cast trigger** independently resolves even if Sauron spell countered. Order amass5, mill5, return ONE creature card from own GY; return instruction is **non-targeting**, so choice made during resolution, no response between mill and return. Stifle effects can counter trigger, Leyline/Rest in Peace can starve GY.
2. **Commander zones**: Beacon moves Sauron from command zone to hand, cast from hand avoids +2 per prior command-zone cast and does not increase future tax; **still cast triggers**. Sneak Attack or Reanimate Sauron as permanent does not cause cast trigger.
3. **Nazgûl** nine name copies permitted under card-specific text in Commander (4 currently). Their ETB tempts; multiple Nazgûl every temptation adds a counter to **each Wraith** you control, multiplicative across board but removal/Anger/Wonder limits.
4. **Sauron opponent-commander death** trigger occurs if opposing commander DIES (field to GY), not if exiled, bounced, and not merely cast; controller may move to command zone via state-based action after its death without retroactively eliminating death.
5. **Metamorphosis Fanatic** ETB up to one TARGET creature in our GY with lifelink counter, immediately can chain after Sauron trigger and targets accessible upon Fanatic entering. Miracle 1B only if first card drawn that turn and revealed timely; when reanimated from grave no Miracle cost is relevant.
6. **Grave Researcher // Reanimate** 2026 printed preparation card front2B creature, first own upkeep surveil1 then if >=3 creature cards in GY becomes PREPARED; that creates a copy of B Reanimate spell IN EXILE, cast when sorcery timing is legal, unprepare on casting. It is **one creature card** in graveyard, never an extra B Reanimate card in hand/GY. Sources official [Secrets of Strixhaven notes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/secrets-of-strixhaven-release-notes).
7. **Persist** can reanimate NONLEGENDARY only, legal on Fanatic/Archon/Ancient Brass/Voracious Fell Beast, NOT Sauron/Valgavoth/Jin/Sheoldred. `Reanimate` B and Auras lack that restriction but other life/attachment risks.
8. **Dance of Dead** enters attached tapped creature, gives +1/+1, special can't-untap-unless-upkeep paid 1B, Aura leaves -> controller sacrifices creature. If Aura becomes illegal, target creature lost.
9. **Necromancy** flash mode special timing sacrifices enchantment at beginning NEXT CLEANUP step, not the normal next-end-step sacrifice; Aura leaving loses creature. Don't misrepresent as Obeka's end-step suppression.
10. **Living Death** step1 exile all creature CARDS from every GY, step2 everyone sacs battlefield creatures, step3 return those exiled in step1; tokens (e.g. Orc Army) never in first step and cannot return. **If Sauron was on battlefield he dies in step2 and doesn't return**; if a Sauron card was ALREADY in GY during step1 and owner leaves it there (commander may opt command zone), it could return—qualifies earlier shorthand “Sauron doesn't return.”
11. **Phyrexian Tower** tap/sac creature mana ability can stock GY BEFORE Living Death begins resolving, cannot sacrifice Sauron or target mid-resolution. `Victimize` sac creature as casting cost and reanimates two targeted tapped creatures.
12. **Anger/Wonder** from GY only active with subtype Mountain/Island respectively on a controlled land. Original dual/shocks typed to qualify; Mountain and Island physical one each. Haste and flying apply immediately after Sauron's reanimation if condition met.
13. **Mouth of Sauron** ETB targets player (can target self), mills3 THEN counts instant/sorcery cards in THAT player's GY for Orc amass; useful selfmill but expensive creature which is not itself a game finish.
14. **Valgavoth** 9/9 wards opponent sacrifice3 nonland permanents, replacement exiles certain cards heading to opponents' graveyards, keeps its exiled cards for casting on your turn by paying LIFE equal MV; cannot assume can cast free on opponents turns or use opponent GY normally.
15. **Too Greedily, Too Deep** 5BR sorcery reanimates ANY graveyard creature then that creature damages EACH OTHER CREATURE equal to its POWER. Reanimating 9-power Valgavoth can kill own Sauron9 toughness/Army unless protected; not the same as mass graveyard reanimation and often competes with cheaper Entomb/Reanimate.
16. **Intuition** 2U instant with TARGET opponent deciding one of 3 cards to hand, two to GY, not a tutor directly to graveyard of all three, cannot guarantee chosen best target if opponent chooses which remains. **Forbidden Alchemy** top FOUR look, choose one to hand, three GY, flashback6B; no opponent chooses. Both legal blue Commander spells.
17. **Command Tower/Beacon/Mount Doom**: Mount Doom last mode SACRIFICES **legendary artifact** (e.g. One Ring) alongside Mount Doom, cannot pay by sacrificing creature Sauron; known prior Saruman deep review mistakenly described 'including Saruman'. Do not reuse that interaction; separate targeted engineering erratum recommended.
18. **Official legality snapshot**: complete source Oct15 + engineering Oct16 bulk UBR PASS, four Nazgûl authorized card rule, no project legality exception. New Intuition is Commander legal source verified, no deck change until explicit owner approval then full 100 new gate.
