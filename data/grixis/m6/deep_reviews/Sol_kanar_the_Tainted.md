## Commander identity and natural ceiling

Sol'Kanar is **{2}{U}{B}{R} 5/5**, beginning OWN END STEP choose exactly one of FOUR *not already chosen for that battlefield object*: draw 1; each opponent loses 2 / you gain 2; 3 damage to up to one OTHER creature/planeswalker; exile and RETURN himself under an opponent's control. **Not an ordered Saga**: you can choose any still-available mode, including dangerous fourth before others, and choices are recorded when trigger goes on stack, even if countered. Changing CONTROL without leaving battlefield does not reset mode history. **Blink returns a fresh object,** resetting all four; fourth mode itself naturally blinks into new opponent-controlled object. Whoever currently controls new object chooses future modes on that controller's own end step; opponent then draws/drains on THEIR behalf.

Two linked identity packages: **end-step mode-reset/recovery** (Thassa, Conjurer's Closet, Planar Incision, Homeward Path, Coveted Falcon); **cursed gifts / political transfers** (Pact/Gambit/Coil/Archfiend/Illusions/Plague Reaver/Audience, Donate/Offering/Blim/Falcon/Bazaar/Role/Puca/Sudden). The full natural win is sequence-dependent: extract safe value, give a permanent while its controller will suffer, protect transfer from stack/board responses, recover your creature as needed. The compact Demonic Pact plus gift is NOT a turn-two guaranteed kill: Pact needs THREE YOUR upkeeps selecting safe modes before donor grants it, then opponent upkeep (unless control/extra turns alter sequence). Coil is immediately lethal to current controller with EMPTY graveyard, but only once you have transferred and emptied it legally; the opponent can interact with one or both steps. Bracket4 realistic, not cEDH Grixis turbo. This deck is deliberately NOT the other Sol'kanar's black-spell/swampwalk deck.

**Protected design:** 5 mana UBR fixing, repeated end-step blinks, unique Homeward Path and Planar Incision recovery, hand/board/resource donation suite, at least five true liabilities, stack answers especially Ertai/Fierce/Force/Swat, flexible grave exile for Coil, and Goblin Bombardment as sacrifice emergency outlet. Don't add generic 8-mana bounce monsters or Oracle/Consult/Breach solely to inflate abstract scores. **Native failure mode:** selecting mode4 or donating curse at wrong time gives advantage to opponent / returns lethal permanent when a holder leaves; control and ownership are different rules.

### Whole-deck structural interpretation

**Mana:** 35 lands, no MDFC, six fetches, nine repeatable nonland sources and ten model-fast-mana sources plus Dark Ritual. Source-colors on lands B20/R17/U21, with repeatable rocks B25/R22/U26; pip demand (model) B35.1% R23.0% U41.9%, much more **blue-heavy** than original Sol'kanar's black base. Conditional tapped 10/35 lands, unconditional tapped2, so fetched shocks/original duals and tapped triome sequencing matter. Turn2 two land drops .895, turn3 three .759, turn4 four .592, turn5 five .425 (source no-mulligan proxy); all required land-source color presence by turn5 .837, **not** actual Sol'Kanar cast probability (five total mana, UBR all simultaneously, taplands and conditional opponent lands not jointly simulated). First commander tax means 7 mana, second 9; effects returning to hand and re-casting may avoid command-zone tax but still need five mana. Mana Vault/Sol Ring burst generic only; Signets/Talismans fix UBR, Forbidden Orchard costs a 1/1 opposing Spirit as tempo concession, Homeward Path colorless is non-negotiable utility.

**Resource and interaction:** access from Vampiric/Demonic Tutors, Baleful Strix, Humble Defector, Coveted Falcon, Fateful Handoff; protection Force of Will blue pitch source audit **28 other blue nonland cards**, Fierce Guardianship, Swat, Swan Song, Mana Drain, Ertai. Creature removal/board wipes Withering Torment, Deadly Rollick, Chaos Warp, Rift, Blasphemous Act, Toxic Deluge, Banditry, Vandalblast. Counterspells cannot rescue a donated curse **after** its lethal state trigger is already on stack except by stopping that trigger or the control change; tax/pillowfort from Propaganda and role swaps buys time. One spell alone, even a seven-mana Audience, doesn't immediately remove all opponents; multiple upkeep rounds and removal windows remain.

**Actual fragile/opportunity cost slots:** `Spiteful Banditry` {X}{R}{R} is a scalable sweep/once-each-turn Treasure generator (NOT mana rock); if room for ramp maybe compare this slot but against creature-based politics it's protection. `Humble Defector` generates two cards but gifts own creature, needs turn/summoning sickness; strong if Homeward Path. `Assault Suit` four mana + equip and may give opponent use of Sol's modes, its non-sacrifice static can protect Archfiend holder. `Visions of Duplicity` reshuffles two opponent creatures but cannot directly donate your curse. `Reins of Power` 2UU temporarily swaps creatures, a play-dependent defensive slot. `Perplexing Chimera` depends on opponent casts and selection; five-mana creature to trade for spell but thematic. `Kain`, `Khârn`, `Plague Reaver` are table/interpersonal control pressure, weaker when opponent won't attack or accepts gifts; do not unilaterally turn into generic card draw. `Greed's Gambit` is profoundly swingy: ETB for YOUR draw3/life6/Bats then recurring END-STEP self-punishment if you fail to hand off. `Archfiend` low-oil grant to opponent can become lethal next upkeep but not if sent away with four counters early. `Immortal Coil` may kill YOU before donation if you have no graveyard cards, so setup matters. The source's Oct15 "zero new material improvements" is prior evidence, not automatic final answer to present review.

## Oracle, legality and rules

**Four modes and stack.** Trigger occurs at beginning of OWN end step; choosing unused mode is part of putting ability on stack. With controller also owning Thassa/Closet triggers at same beginning end step, stack `BLINK BELOW, Sol MODE ABOVE` to resolve beneficial mode then blink, leaving new first-three choices for next end step. If only mode4 remains, choose fourth, put BLINK ABOVE that mode4 trigger, resolve blink before fourth; old object is gone, its pending trigger won't move fresh object to opponent. A failed/countered mode nevertheless counts as chosen for THAT object; once all four chosen, a fifth trigger has no legal modes and is removed from stack rather than letting you choose a first mode again. Planar Incision is an INSTANT targeting artifact/creature regardless control and returns it under **owner** control, so it can retrieve already donated Sol as a new object and reset modes (while Thassa/Closet cannot target enemy-controlled Sol). Homeward Path returns OWN CREATURES only, no new object and no enchantment/artifact return; if previously given away by Sol mode4, that mode itself produced a new object with cleared modes before Homeward Path retrieves it.

**Demonic Pact:** four choices are persistent for that physical permanent through control change; modes chosen during trigger placement, even if resolution fails. A normal two-card Donate line requires Pact already selected three safer modes across three previous OWNER upkeeps and Donate {2}{U} legal permanent transfer before recipient upkeep. On recipient next upkeep, only "You lose the game" can be chosen; if it resolves and no lose-game protection, recipient leaves. If they destroy Pact, counter transfer, or use Angel's Grace, no guaranteed win. Even a Pact that has already selected ALL four can persist without automatically losing again; its triggered ability has no legal unused mode, per [Wizards Demonic Pact release notes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/foundations-release-notes). A Pact returned from recipient defeat has *same selected modes* and may present future risk if still has unused lethal mode, rather than treating control change as blink.

**Immortal Coil:** artifact {2}{B}{B} triggers controller LOSE GAME whenever THEIR graveyard empty; unlike prevention, filling grave after triggered won't cancel pending loss. Donate/Harmless Offering/Bazaar Trader/Fateful Handoff can transfer Coil, then Rakdos Charm {B}{R} target that holder's grave, or Bojuka Bog ETB exile that grave; trigger resolves if not countered (and will retrigger while empty if countered). Coil prevents damage to its current controller via graveyard exile, so a simple combat swing doesn't reliably kill holder. If you cast Coil while YOUR graveyard already empty before sending it, you trigger own loss. `Fateful Handoff` draws FOUR from Coil mana value 4 then hands it over (mana value count, not four mana spent necessarily). Permission and priority windows remain.

**Known M1 flags, explicitly resolved as documentation vs dependency:**
1. `Greed's Gambit` recurring penalty is at **BEGINNING OF CONTROLLER'S END STEP**, NOT upkeep. Its cast ETB gave original caster draw3/gain6/three Bats. Transferring after ETB makes opponent take EACH end-step discard1, lose2, sac1. Removing Gambit while opponent controls it makes THAT opponent receive LTB discard3/lose6/sac3, but if opponent holding it LEAVES game first and ownership returns before leaving battlefield, penalty can later become yours. Source attestation's "upkeep" is a documentation error; verified [current Oracle](https://mtg.wtf/card/big/38/Greeds-Gambit).
2. **Returned cursed objects when victim leaves** (CR800.4a): a simple continuous control-change effect giving opponent your owned Pact/Coil/Archfiend/Gambit ENDS when that opponent leaves, and object often returns under its original owner's control rather than vanishing; old histories, oil counters and control-specific triggers remain. Don't knock one target out first if own remaining game would inherit active fatal liability. `Captive Audience` and `Xantcha` instead enter UNDER OPPONENT'S CONTROL as default, so when that player leaves those owned-by-you objects are EXILED (rather than automatically returning to you), per Oct16 source M1. This is a real multiplayer sequence dependency, not an illegality or unapproved new exception. [CR800.4a](https://www.mtgtools.io/rules/800).

**Target precision, donor restrictions:** Bazaar Trader only can gift its target artifact/creature/land you control, not enchantment Pact/Gambit/Illusions; `Donate`/`Harmless Offering` give permanent enchant as well, but different target wording and sorcery speed; `Fateful Handoff` target artifact/creature you control, draws before handoff; `Sudden Substitution` needs BOTH a spell on stack and a creature, so cannot be cast simply to give away Pact outside a spell; `Role Reversal` requires legal two permanents that share type. `Coveted Falcon` disguised for {3} then flipped by paying {1}{U} (special action) gifts any number of targeted YOUR permanents to one opponent and draws one per actual gift, and ATTACK trigger takes one permanent OWNED but not controlled back; a control exchange alone doesn't reset Sol modes. `Jon Irenicus` gifted creatures can't be sacrificed immediately, complicating targeted opponent's escape from oil counters, but Jon's end-step timing needs a valid owned creature and actual gift target. `Assault Suit` changes creature control ONLY temporarily with equip/static no-sac rule; it doesn't reset mode history and cannot force opponent to choose desired mode.

**Secondary curse precision:** `Illusions of Grandeur` enters under your control gains20 then gift transfers cumulative upkeep and leaves-the-battlefield life LOSS20 to current controller; opponents may simply pay cumulative upkeep while delaying liability. `Archfiend of the Dross` oil count carries through control transfer and each current-controller upkeep removes one, possible lose-game when reaching zero; donating with four counters is not same as donating with one. `Captive Audience` directly enters under opponent control with first punitive mode selected at THEIR upkeep (one each end step? no: Audience triggers at upkeep); no generic Donate needed. `Xantcha` similarly enters under opponent control and anyone can pay {3} to make its controller lose2/draw1 for the activating player, not lose for original caster always. `Spiteful Banditry` {X}{R}{R} deals X to EACH creature on ETB and provides ONE Treasure when one or more enemy-controlled creatures die on a turn, at most once each turn, confirmed [Oracle](https://mtg.wtf/card/ltr/149/Spiteful-Banditry). The M5 mana-positive rock comparisons ignore this dual sweep/treasure utility.
