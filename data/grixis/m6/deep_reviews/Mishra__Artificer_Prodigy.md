## Commander identity and natural ceiling

**Mishra, Artificer Prodigy ({1}{U}{B}{R}, 4/4 Human Artificer)** triggers WHENEVER you **CAST an artifact SPELL**: optional search your own **graveyard, hand and/or library** for a card with the **same name as that spell**, put it straight onto battlefield and shuffle if library searched. **In 100-card singleton, the named card is typically the actual cast card on STACK**, not in any permitted search zone; therefore Mishra normally finds nothing unless another spell/trigger moves that artifact card from stack to **graveyard** (counter) or **library** (Possibility Storm) *before* Mishra's trigger resolves. Countering your artifact can therefore become a cheaper/resilient way to resolve it for yourself while countering opposing nonartifact spells normally. Asymmetric play is real only if Mishra stays on battlefield, the relevant named card reaches eligible zone, and the trigger is sequenced correctly. Pure artifact goodstuff without hostile symmetrical triggers does not exploit him.

**Protected seven-axis artifact parity core:** `Blood Funnel` (your noncreature spells cost {2} less, but auto-counter without creature sacrifice), `Nether Void` (tax {3} or counter every spell), `Nullstone Gargoyle` (counter first noncreature spell of turn), `Chalice of the Void` (match MV), `Void Mirror` (zero colored mana spent), `Vexing Bauble` (zero TOTAL mana spent), `Planar Chaos` (lose coin flip). `Possibility Storm` tucks original cast-from-hand artifact into library and free-casts a replacement before Mishra retrieves its original, supporting more unfair locks when Mirror/Bauble counter replacement. `Encroaching Mycosynth` makes own nonland permanent spells artifact too, expanding commander triggers. `Mycosynth Lattice` does NOT do that; it changes permanents, NOT spells on stack.

**Natural high ceiling:** Historical **Bracket 5 — fringe cEDH, commander-centric artifact stax-combo**. Mishra paradoxically thrives under his own spell-denial permanents and can convert them into effective artifact casting discount, while `Mystic Forge`/Top/Reservoir, `Bolas's Citadel`/Top/Reservoir, KCI/Trawler/recursion, Karn/Lattice and Lattice/Vandalblast win/lock through artifacts. He is **NOT a generic deterministic early Grixis Consultation list**; dependence on a four-mana commander, particular stax object, color fixing and protection limits consistency relative to established tournament commanders. The effective goal is strongest realistic own identity, not swapping in Oracle/Consult or Breach/LED to declare universal cEDH.

**Identity asymmetry caveat:** Mirror/Bauble and Chalice can disable Mishra's own cheap artifacts or free cast loops WITHOUT Mishra. Blood Funnel, Nether Void, Gargoyle, Planar Chaos, Possibility Storm can counter/tuck your own **nonartifact tutors/counters/wipes**—and Mishra can't return those, even if they are powerful. `Mishra's Workshop` makes {C}{C}{C} solely for **CASTING artifact spells**, not 1UBR Mishra or triggered Nether Void tax, so naive 29-land “17 mana rocks” counts badly overstate commander castability. Protect the commander first, choose lock deployment after mana/counter readiness and pick stax to match actual opponent strategy.

## Oracle, legality and rules

**Provenance and availability:** October15 canonical approval-ready verification and October16 independent engineering Oracle/rulings/Comprehensive Rules (effective September25) supply full list legal/UBR checkpoints; `grixis/interactions/mishra-artificer-prodigy.md` has **8 verified cases and 2 significant anti-synergy flags**, the latter not silently dismissed. Targeted current public Oracle/legal readings on **October19**: Mishra `https://mtg.wtf/card/tsp/243/Mishra-Artificer-Prodigy`; The Enigma Jewel `https://mtg.wtf/card/lci/362a/The-Enigma-Jewel` and Locus `https://mtg.wtf/card/lci/362b/Locus-of-Enlightenment`; Uthros `https://mtg.wtf/card/eoc/27/Uthros-Research-Craft`; plus specific artifact lock Oracle notes and rulings in `grixis/interactions/mishra-artificer-prodigy.md#mishraap-01..10`. Official Commander legality of commander and Enigma candidate verified on publicly read pages. **The entire approved 100 was NOT freshly bulk-audited on Oct19**; its official legal and identity PASS is dated Oct15/16. There is no project-authorized legality exception or companion, and no unexpected flagged card emerged.

1. **A copied cast trigger is not the same as a normal second spell.** Mishra triggers for each artifact spell **CAST by controller** while Mishra is on battlefield. Once trigger exists, removing Mishra doesn't automatically counter it. In singleton, one exact named card is ordinarily the artifact spell on **stack**, where Mishra cannot look; with no other copy, Mishra's trigger usually searches/finds nothing. When a separate counter/tuck ability you control resolves **before** the trigger, it moves original card to Mishra's graveyard/library search zones, and the trigger can put it straight onto battlefield. The card PUT is **not cast**, hence no fresh Mishra/Jhoira/Uthros/Aetherflux cast trigger from the recovery itself. An opponent can wait for Mishra trigger to resolve with the original spell still on stack, then counter the original: Mishra will find nothing. Protect trigger order and priority, don't pretend Mishra makes all artifact spells universally uncounterable.
2. **Same-player stack ordering for self-counters:** With Mishra plus own `Blood Funnel` and you cast a qualifying NONCREATURE ARTIFACT, both triggered abilities belong to YOU. You choose their relative order on stack (Mishra trigger FIRST, Blood Funnel ABOVE), let Blood Funnel counter the spell when you decline to sacrifice a creature; the cast card moves to GY, then Mishra trigger searches GY and puts it onto battlefield. Blood Funnel's noncreature-spell COST reduction {2} occurred at casting, and recovery skips paying additional cost. `Nether Void` similarly counters unless {3} is paid (tax is triggered, not a spell cost; Workshop restricted mana cannot pay it), `Nullstone Gargoyle` counters first noncreature spell of EACH turn, `Void Mirror` counters if no COLORED mana spent, `Vexing Bauble` if NO mana spent, `Chalice` if mana value equals charge-counter count and `Planar Chaos` if coin flip loses. Each independent counter trigger is controlled by its own source controller, not simply yours if someone else owns the stax permanent; trigger APNAP ordering may differ in an opponent-controlled stax pod.
3. **Different zero/mana tests:** `Vexing Bauble`: check whether literally **any mana was spent** casting spell, NOT printed mana value; Top reduced to {0}, Citadel Top cast for LIFE, and free spell off Possibility Storm all generally meet no-mana condition. `Void Mirror`: check whether any **colored** mana was spent, so a fully colorless-mana cast Ring/KCI/Top may be countered even with substantial **generic** mana spent. If generic artifact cost is paid with a blue mana from a Talisman, Void Mirror doesn't counter that cast; this can preserve `The One Ring` CAST protection rather than recovering it via Mishra. `Chalice` looks at **spell's mana value**, which reductions never change; a Top {1} cost reduced to free is still MV1, and Mox Amber MV0. Zero-cost artifacts played as lands (Great Furnace / Seat / Vault) are **NOT spells**, so playing them doesn't trigger Mishra, Chalice or Reservoir.
4. **Chalice “X=0 when put” and commander life:** A `Chalice of the Void` cast for X=1 normally costs {2} and enters with one charge counter IF resolves. If countered by your own stax and Mishra recovers the card into battlefield, it wasn't cast into the battlefield so X is treated as 0; it enters with **zero** charge counters and counters MV0 spells, including your own Chrome Mox/Diamond/Opal/Amber while Mishra absent. You can decline optional Mishra search if you prefer it in GY and another means to recover it. `Chalice` doesn't counter a spell already on the stack before it entered, because ability triggers on future casts only.
5. **Possibility Storm repositions an original artifact:** With `Possibility Storm {3}{R}{R}` controlled and a cast artifact from HAND, stack Mishra trigger below Storm trigger. Storm **EXILES** original, reveals until another card sharing a card type, lets caster CAST it free, and puts all exiled cards including original back onto **bottom of library in random order**. When the ORIGINAL Mishra trigger finally resolves, its originally named card is in library and can be brought onto battlefield **if still there**. The free replacement artifact is a distinct CAST that triggers Mishra, and if it is subsequently countered by `Vexing Bauble` / `Void Mirror`, its own Mishra trigger may return that card from graveyard with proper ordering. Opponents lack Mishra recovery and often have their replacements countered; **NOT a universal lock**: spells cast from other zones avoid Storm, additional COSTS spent as mana can evade Bauble/possibly Void Mirror, uncounterable spells survive and opponents may interact with triggers.
6. **Your stax counters NONARTIFACT protection too:** Under `Blood Funnel`, `Demonic Tutor`, `Force of Will`/ `Mana Drain`, `Swan Song`, `Toxic Deluge`, `Vandalblast` and `Cyclonic Rift` are noncreature and may be countered if no creature sacrificed. Under `Nether Void`, every spell (including your hand-cast counterspell) needs {3} additional on triggered tax or loses. `Nullstone Gargoyle` often eats your first noncreature spell of turn, whether artifact or instant. `Planar Chaos` 50% counter possibility applies to your WIN attempt and free protection. Possibility Storm turns sorcery from HAND into randomly chosen SORCERY spell, which cannot choose overload as alternative cost when cast without paying its mana cost. Consequently `Mycosynth Lattice` + overloaded `Vandalblast` needs Storm absent or Vandalblast cast from different zone, and stack taxes satisfied, before claiming victory.
7. **Mycosynth Lattice versus Encroaching Mycosynth, and Karn:** `Mycosynth Lattice {6}` makes **all permanents** artifacts and all cards not on battlefield/spells colorless, and lets mana be spent as though any color; it **does not turn all spells into artifacts** or trigger Mishra for any creature/sorcery by itself. `Encroaching Mycosynth {3}{U}` specifically makes YOUR NONLAND PERMANENT spells artifacts plus other types, including your creature, planeswalker and enchantment SPELLS, enabling Mishra triggers for those and exposing own permanents to artifact hate. With `Karn, the Great Creator` on field, Lattice makes enemy lands artifacts and Karn's static prevents opponents activating abilities of their artifacts, including activated LAND mana abilities; available floating mana, already cast spells and effects not using activated abilities aren't retroactively stopped. In ordinary Commander, Karn's wish -2 cannot access a nonexistent sideboard from outside game, and never count it as an extra 101st card.
8. **KCI/Trawler recursion isn't blanket infinite:** `Krark-Clan Ironworks` sacrifices an artifact for {C}{C}, a mana ability, and can fuel artifact casting from own hand/GY. `Scrap Trawler` must choose an artifact card in your graveyard with **strictly lower mana value** than the artifact that went to graveyard; `Myr Retriever` and `Junk Diver` return ANOTHER artifact card from GY when they die. With appropriate interlocked pieces and enough generic mana discounts (e.g. `Etherium Sculptor` for Top/other artifact costs), these can generate repeated artifact casts, sacrificed death triggers and net colorless mana. The exact current Spellbook lines are source documented, but **KCI+Trawler+Retriever alone is not declared unbounded** without additional artifact recursion/discount and correct target existence/cast payability. Removing KCI for Jewel eliminates the principal artifact sacrifice and enters-leaves-GY mechanism, even if a proxy reported a “pass.”
9. **Top/Forge/Reality Chip/Citadel paid loops:** `Sensei's Divining Top` tap to draw a card and put itself on top of library. `Mystic Forge` allows casting artifacts/colorless spells from library top; `The Reality Chip` only grants top-of-library **play/cast** while correctly reconfigured and attached to creature; `Cloud Key`, `Etherium Sculptor`, `Foundry Inspector`, `Enthusiastic Mechanaut` reduce Top by its generic {1}, letting free recast when TOP is on library. Then each Top cast triggers `Aetherflux Reservoir` escalating life; once 50 life actually available, spend 50 to deal 50 damage to one opponent/target, repeat only after restoring >50 with further casts. `Bolas's Citadel` alternative casts Top from top paying **1 LIFE** instead of its {1} mana cost; Reservoir life gains can exceed this cost after casts accrue. If `Void Mirror` or `Vexing Bauble` counters a zero-mana Top spell, **Mishra's later trigger** recovers that exact Top from GY into battlefield, allowing continued tap-draw loop; when Mishra absent it stops. If Top is sole remaining library card, activating its tap-draw from top needs another card to draw and may lose from empty draw—do not assume zero-card library supports loop. `Aetherflux` still triggers on casts of countered artifacts, but Reservoir itself must already be on field.
10. **The One Ring without CAST:** `The One Ring`'s protection-from-everything is granted by an **if you cast it** ETB condition. If an artifact Ring spell is countered under Void Mirror and Mishra puts same card from GY directly onto battlefield, it **was not cast as that entering Ring permanent**, so ETB protection trigger won't apply. Its tap-draw and burden counters can still work. A normal Ring CAST that uses colored mana among generic cost can avoid Void Mirror and enters as cast, granting protection; do not rely on Mishra rescue to replicate all ETB benefits. `Emry` lets cast Ring from GY once for turn if tapped/able; that CAN generate protection on its normal resolution. `Mishra's Workshop` makes CCC restricted to casting artifact spells and cannot pay for Ring's activated draw after cast.
11. **Uthros Research Craft card flow:** {2}{U} Artifact — Spacecraft. Station is a sorcery-speed activation that taps an untapped friendly creature to add station counters equal to its power; a **power-4 Mishra** supplies four in one activation. At 3+ counters, an ARTIFACT CAST triggers **draw one card and add charge counter**, and that trigger resolves before original spell (including spells countered by own artifacts). `Brainstorm` replacement pays {U} for a one-shot draw-three/top-two (not pure +3 cards), whereas Uthros card advantage can scale into multiple artifact casts. Needs a creature and station window and survives less well versus artifact hate; no blanket claim it is better in all T1/T2 draws.
12. **Enigma Jewel actual Oracle, craft ceiling and payability:** Candidate front is **{U} legendary artifact, enters tapped**, {T} adds {C}{C} restricted to paying **ACTIVATION costs**—including its own craft—but **NEVER** to CAST Mishra/Top/Vexing Bauble/other spells, pay Nether Void triggered {3}, or pay any spell's alternative cost. Front craft costs **{8}{U}** and exiles four or more OTHER nonland permanents you control and/or cards in own graveyard that have ACTIVATED abilities, return transformed Locus under owner's control, sorcery speed; very resource intensive. Locus has only activated abilities of exiled craft-material cards and can activate EACH instance only once each turn, copying a nonmana ability when activated, but not copying triggered/static/cast effects. A `Mox Amber` cost {0} taps for COLORED mana only if you have a LEGENDARY colored creature/planeswalker in play (Mishra himself covers all UBR); it does not enter tapped and enables CAST of those colored spells. The two are NOT equivalent first-four-turn mana sources, regardless of “mana source” proxy labels. The Enigma Jewel is officially Commander-legal and U color identity; absence from deck doesn't constitute legality blocker.
13. **Additional zone/copy/color pitfalls:** `Mox Opal` only produces color once metalcraft (3 artifacts) is online; `Mox Diamond` discards a LAND to survive ETB; `Chrome Mox` imprints nonartifact colored nonland (narrower than generic artifact-heavy source). `Mox Amber` requires colored legend: Mishra/Jhoira/Emry/Urza meet relevant colors, whereas `Karn` and `Tezzeret, Cruel Captain` COLORLESS don't enable it alone. `Mishra's Workshop` restricted CCC cast artifact spells only. `Tezzeret, Cruel Captain` -3 tutors artifact with MV≤1 to HAND (including Top/Bauble/Mox/Sol Ring, NOT Aetherflux), while `Urza's Saga` third chapter tutors artifact with printed MANA COST {0} or {1}, NOT merely every MV≤1 card (though chosen examples work). `Transmute Artifact` demands sacrificing artifact, finding artifact, and paying any positive mana value difference or new artifact ends in GY; source stack and UU matter.
14. **Planar Chaos and opponent targets:** Planar Chaos's **every spell cast** random coin-flip counter trigger is controlled by its controller, including own nonartifact defensive spell. If you cast an artifact with Mishra and Planar Chaos both controlled, put chaos above Mishra, lose flip → artifact is countered to own GY → Mishra recover; win flip → artifact still on stack when Mishra triggers so no named card in searchable zone, then resolves ordinarily. If opponent counters the spell AFTER Mishra trigger resolved and while it remained on stack, you normally get no recovery. Planar Chaos's upkeep coin-flip destruction makes the lock nonpermanent, can stop own tutor; resilience cannot be treated as guaranteed.

**A — Actual turn-two Mishra and why Jewel is not Amber.** T1 `Command Tower` taps to cast `Sol Ring` for {1}; Ring taps for {C}{C} to cast `Dimir Signet` {2}. T2 play `Badlands`.
