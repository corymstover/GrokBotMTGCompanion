## Commander identity and natural ceiling

Wick is a four-mana 2/4 Rat Warlock `{3}{B}`; whenever it or another Rat you control enters, it makes one 1/1 Snail if you have none, or places +1/+1 counter on an existing Snail. Activated ability is exactly `{U}{B}{R}, sacrifice a Snail: Wick deals damage equal to that creature's *last-known power* to EACH opponent, then you draw that many cards`. Thus its power ceiling is building Rats and Snail power and assembling a single paid Snail shot or a free-sac token-type-change loop, not generic Rat swarm beatdown. A counterspell/hero piece can defend it, but costs UBR each direct activation.

**Distinct packages:** A (12+ low-cost Rat entries, multi-body Song of Totentanz); B (3 Snail-preserving Rat type adders), C (5+ free sacrifice outlets including Ashnod/Phyrexian Altars, Goblin Bombardment, Carrion Feeder, Altar of Dementia, Viscera Seer), D (Snail growth Blade of Bloodchief/Bulk Up/Lich's Relic/Ozolith), E (Marrow-Gnawer/Intruder Alarm repeating Rats), F (Impact Tremors/Mirkwood Bats/Zulaport and Tainted Strike as actual deterministic finishing outputs), G (one- and two-mana counters/redirection). UBR activation color is the genuine gating resource even though casting Wick costs black only.

**Natural optimized ceiling:** Source **Power Bracket 4 — Optimized**, five Game Changers, 13 baseline documented full-deck Spellbook combinations (one two-card infinite classification in engine), with many commander-native 3-card combinations. Not an independently measured cEDH list due to layered board/enchantment dependence, color activation and token timing. Do not add generic Oracle/Consult/Breach shell at expense of Wick trigger structure.

## Mana, early setup and opportunity cost

- 35 dedicated lands (36 if Boggart land back used), 4 fetchlands, basic Island x2/Mountain x1/Swamp x1. Engineered source-presence proxy land U19/B20/R19, plus six repeatable nonland mana sources U23/B24/R23. 7 'fast mana' sources in heuristic, one Dark Ritual, conditional tapped 9; counts not simultaneous unrestricted untapped mana. Cavern restricted Rat colored creature spell only; cannot pay Wick's UBR activation. Swarmyard colorless and can regenerate Wick (Rat) but no UBR.
- Wick costs `{3}{B}` (not UBR) and can enter around turn 3 with acceleration. A separate UBR payment for each Snail sacrifice is necessary unless free outlet combo/Plunderer/Phyrexian Altar supplies the mana. Controller cannot count a no-color Sol Ring/Ancient Tomb as blue or red. Colored mana in turn of combo materially affects which engines matter more.
- Openings: T1 tutor/Seer/Blade/Sol Ring; T2 Pack Rat, Rat King Verminister, token maker, Talisman/Fellwar; T3 Arcane Adaptation, Impact Tremors, Rat engine/Altar; T4 Wick where protected and Rat ETB available; subsequent turns accumulate Snail power or type-change/free-outlet combo. The line is not deterministic when Wick removed on stack, color missing or Rat maker is a four-mana threat.
- Counters/growth: Blade stores counters on equipped Snail, Ozolith stores counters when it leaves but moves only at begin combat. Bulk Up can double Snail damage/draw; Tainted Strike transforms WICK's damage to infect so 10 damage=10 poison per opponent if not prevented. Wick's draw of many cards can deck pilot and is not optional once activation resolves; plan payoff/life totals carefully.

### Strongest disputes, paid timing and explicit risk tests

#### Four proxy-PASS challengers are genuine resource tradeoffs
Deadly Dispute or Dimir Signet over **Ashnod's Altar**, Dimir Signet or Rakdos Signet over **Pitiless Plunderer** were the only source P1 successes. Rocks improve earliest colored access, but Altar has ongoing creature sacrifice and native infinite Snail setup with type-changing, and Plunderer makes one Treasure for every dying Snail. The M5 goldfish labels Altar a generic mana rock or Plunderer a one-shot, undercounting loops. No upgrade clears the cut cost, though a Signet vs true extra 4+ drop remains viable targeted postowner hypothesis.

#### Roaming Throne against small Rats (68 candidates)
Roaming Throne chooses Rat as it enters, becomes a Rat itself, and doubles Wick's Rat-entry triggers as a triggered ability of another Rat creature. More one-mana Rats add one trigger apiece but do not double every later Rat entry. Throne does NOT double Wick's activated UBR ability. Its four-mana setup is real, yet it contributes Rat density and magnifies boardwide tokens. Retain over Typhoid Rats, Burglar Rat, Rat Colony, Azure Beastbinder and unsupported combo pieces.

#### Wick's native Snail→Rat free-outlet loop and interrupt windows
With Arcane Adaptation/Leyline of Transformation/Maskwood Nexus, a Snail also counts as Rat without losing Snail. The trigger checks whether a Snail is controlled *when it resolves*. Sacrifice an existing Snail to a free outlet while a Wick Rat-entry trigger is on stack; on resolution it creates Rat Snail, which triggers Wick; sacrifice again before new trigger resolves. This produces arbitrary *finite* ETB/death/sacrifice events and, with Ashnod's Altar, colorless mana; with Phyrexian Altar, any color; with Mirkwood Bats/Impact Tremors/Zulaport/Bombardment, a kill. Requires correct timing and no opponent interruption. Conspiracy removes Snail type and breaks native ability.

#### Wick's paid UBR damage vs free outlets
Sacrificing a Snail to Wick's own `{U}{B}{R}` activated ability uses its last-known power to deal damage to EACH opponent and to draw that many cards; one Wick activation alone is not automatically infinitely repeatable because it requires a new Snail plus three colored mana each time. Infinite free-sac Rat-typed Snail chains through outlets differ from a one-shot Wick activation. Tainted Strike must give WICK infect at ability resolution (or Wick's last known characteristics if it leaves after activation); at 10-power Snail, each opponent takes 10 poison unless prevented.

#### Marrow-Gnawer / Intruder Alarm finite specification
Marrow-Gnawer has a tap ability and requires sacrificing a Rat as cost. Intruder Alarm untaps all creatures whenever a creature ETBs (including opponent creatures) and prevents normal untaps. With adequate Rat count and surviving Gnawer, created Rat tokens untap Gnawer and increase the Rat count so the generation cycle repeats; source reports unlimited Rat tokens. Do not count one initial Rat as sufficient for a positive growth loop or assume opponents cannot exploit the symmetric Alarm.

#### Pitiless Plunderer as practical UBR activation bankroll
Sacrifice a 1/1 Snail under a type changer and Plunderer makes a Treasure when that creature dies. A single death pays at most one of the three UBR activation pips unless a sequence stores several Treasures; it nonetheless converts Wick's free-outlet engine into deterministic mana and supports the native commander damage. Signet is better T2 but loses high-throughput late mana at *that* cut.

#### Rat-specific growth spells vs tribal clutter
Song of Totentanz is XR for X Rats plus haste; each Rat ETB grows the Snail, and the base effect scales down to early hands. Source explicitly replaced symmetric seven-mana Plague of Vermin with Song during red-team. Rat Colony, Ink-Eyes, Nashi, and old Rat lords can be meaningful elsewhere but usually require combat/ninjutsu or fail to create multiple Rats per spell; keeping fast token makers maximizes Wick's natural throughput.

#### Power counters and draw/decking cap
Blade of the Bloodchief on Snail creates +1/+1 counters from deaths, The Ozolith can preserve counters when Snail leaves, Bulk Up/Relic grow it for a single Wick activation. Sacrificing a 50-power Snail makes Wick deal 50 to each opponent and also draw 50: if too few cards remain in library, the draw is lethal to the pilot on state-based check. Ozolith counter movement is beginning-of-combat, not any-time instants. Some counter-synergy payoffs fail because Snail is replaced each time.

#### Protection vs 3MV Rat acceleration and actual 4MV commander
Commander itself is 3B, so early cost asks B, but ability UBR demands one of each afterward. Cavern of Souls Rat-colored creature mana may cast Wick but cannot pay its noncreature activated cost; Pathways pick one face, sometimes weaken activation; generic 2MV rocks and Mana Confluence cover more cases. Cyclonic Rift/Mana Drain/Fierce/Swat protect against actual stax and opponent combo, while swapping for a niche Rat or self-copy line may improve goldfish only.

#### Board wipe / token-pod and symmetric effects
Toxic Deluge and Blasphemous Act can erase own Rat/Throne board, so sequence after Rat-trigger setup or in response to threatening wins, but without real sweepers opposing wide boards overwhelm Wick before one-shot Snail conversion. Rat-specific blockers or Crypt Rats cannot always play the same wipe role. Intruder Alarm may untap enemy token decks, a real symmetry risk requiring careful deployment.


### On-theme off-ranking alternatives

- **Panharmonicon:** Tempting extra ETB triggers but four mana and no Rat body, narrower than Roaming Throne with ward and Rat-type selection; earlier author rejected.
- **Conspiracy:** Unlike the three retained type changers, overwrites Snail's types, destroying ability to sacrifice the token as a Snail. A concrete anti-combo.
- **Three Tree City:** In Rat swarm makes large tribal mana, but colorless at low board size and three specific UBR activated pips are more urgent.
- **Thornbite Staff / Kiki-Jiki / Splinter Twin:** Copy/untap package needs target, actor ability, mana and summoning sickness. Kiki cannot target legendary Wick; projected arbitrary card-combos do not establish a safe cut.
- **Rat Colony exceptions:** Card could legally include several copies, but project baseline is singleton 99 names and changes cannot be automatically added. Every copy is merely one Rat entry unless additional effects; change would need owner reopening.
- **Mirkwood Bats vs Blood Artist:** Bats triggers on tokens entering or being sacrificed and is key to native Rat-Snail loops; already approved. A generic artist payoff can drain on deaths but lacks token creation double hit.
- **The Ozolith / Snail exile:** Ozolith only catches counters when creature leaves and moves counters at combat; exiled or bounced Snail does not keep old Snail identity, but counters may still be stored. This is mitigation, not protection from losing the engine itself.
- **Generic Oracle/Consult/Breach:** Could artificially boost standalone speed but would displace the need for Wick, three type changers and Rat-to-Snail payoffs. Contradicts commander-first power ceiling.
- **New Rat releases:** The 192 screened names include newer cards with uncertain paid lines; no auto claim of Oracle proof or currently legal until they become actual swap candidates. No full Oct20 Oracle bulk refresh was performed.

## Oracle, legality and rules

1. Wick casting is `{3}{B}`, activation `{U}{B}{R}`. The sacrifice is a COST, happens immediately; An already-activated Wick ability resolves even if Wick leaves the battlefield; last-known information establishes its source and relevant characteristics, and the sacrificed Snail's last-known power determines damage and card draw.
2. Wick Rat-entry trigger checks existence of Snail when it resolves. If an existing Snail is sacrificed in response and none remains, a new Snail is created (otherwise it counters a Snail). One trigger per separate Rat entering, including each Rat from Song.
3. Snail must REMAIN a Snail for Wick activation. Arcane Adaptation/Leyline of Transformation/Maskwood Nexus add Rat while preserving Snail, enabling a new Rat-Snail token ETB to itself create the next trigger. Conspiracy naming Rat overwrites Snail and breaks loop.
4. Free outlet loop is Rat-Snail ETB/sacrifice and repeats when a Wick trigger is on stack. Ashnod's Altar generates CC each sacrifice; Phyrexian Altar one arbitrary colored mana; neither is a free UBR Wick activation every loop without mana accounting.
5. Rat-Snail ETB + Impact Tremors deals 1 per token entry, Bats on creation AND sacrifice, Zulaport on death, Bombardment on sacrifice damage, Altar of Dementia mills by power. The loop can produce any chosen finite number, but opponents can interact at priority windows and replacement effects may stop it.
6. Tainted Strike must resolve targeting WICK before his damage ability resolves; because Wick is damage source, its noncombat damage to each player is infect. Snail power 10 deals ten poison counters to each player if not prevented; Snail having infect alone wouldn't achieve this.
7. The sacrificed Snail's power is measured at last existence; Blade of Bloodchief counters and Bulk Up buffs apply then, but subsequent Ozolith movement does not retroactively alter previous damage.
8. The Ozolith can retain counters on Snail leaving and can move at beginning of COMBAT, not instant activation any time. Counters are on new creature only if transfer resolves.
9. Roaming Throne entering as Rat under selected type triggers Wick and doubles triggered abilities of other Rat creatures. It does NOT double Wick's activated UBR sacrifice ability, nor does every one-damage spell become a Rat ability.
10. Marrow-Gnawer activates by tapping and sacrificing a Rat; Intruder Alarm creature entry untaps all creatures but also prevents normal untap and affects opponents. Sufficient Rat count and time since control are required for a growing loop.
11. Cavern of Souls can make Rat creature SPELLS uncounterable with its restricted colored mana, but Wick's activated ability is not a spell and cannot spend that conditional color.
12. Mirkwood Bats makes damage on token creation/sacrifice, Impact Tremors on friendly creature entry, Zulaport Cutthroat on your creature dying. They have different trigger frequencies and do not all trigger on each land/Treasure sacrifice.
13. Boggart Trawler creature front is a creature spell, Boggart Bog land back is played as land. One physical card, 35 front land plus one flex, not 37 cards.
14. Rat Colony's any-number deckbuilding permission does not force inclusion, and no duplicate exists in this approved list. The singleton/basic audit still passes.
15. No new official Commander ban/identity issue discovered beyond historical Oct15–16 verified card list; no exhaustive new Oct20 Oracle snapshot run. Any actual new approved swaps would require individual current Oracle and full 100-copy verification.
