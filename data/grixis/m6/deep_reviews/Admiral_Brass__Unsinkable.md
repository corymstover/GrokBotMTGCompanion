## Commander identity and natural ceiling

Admiral Brass, Unsinkable ({2}{U}{B}{R}), a Human Pirate, mills four on entering and at the **beginning of each combat on your turn** may return a targeted **Pirate creature card** from *your graveyard* as a **base 4/4**, with **finality counter** and **haste until end of turn**. This is an unusually specific tempo-reanimation engine: plan to Entomb/Buried Alive/loot high-impact Pirates, deploy Brass, then recur one (or more if valid trigger copy) at start of combat and immediately attack/use ETB. Other creature types cannot be returned by Brass, and the finality counter usually **exiles rather than letting it die and re-enter grave**. The deck succeeds when EACH Brass reanimation has meaningful immediate impact.

Commander necessity: baseline already supports high-end 7-MV Angrath's Marauders without paying seven (becomes 4/4 haste but retains damage doubling), Amphin Mutineer removal ETB, Coercive Recruiter attack/steal, Hostage Taker ETB removal, Port Razer combat chains, Zara attack-steal, Corsair Captain/Forerunner ETBs and Skeleton Crew leave-grave payoffs. **Power Conduit** is an unusually commander-specific tool: remove finality counter as the tap activation COST and place a charge/+1 counter; recovered Pirate can now die normally and be replayed later. Backup Reanimate/Animate Dead/Victimize/Living Death/Patriarch's Bidding supply redundancy but cannot replace Brass's natural beginning-combat haste and 4/4 conversion.

Realistic ceiling: strongly focused **Bracket 4 optimized** with high burst/steal from hasty reanimated Pirates, constrained by five-MV commander, grave filling, graveyard shutdown and tap-ability tempo. Free stack counters and tutors increase power but do not make a Pirate-dependent, combat-centric reanimator automatically cEDH.

Creature/Treasure ramp (Malcolm, Kenway, Lannery, Gemcutter, Pitiless, Ramirez) depends on board, ETB or dying. No exact turn-five UBR paid-cast probability has been computed; 35 lands is not an outcome statistic.

**Grave setup/selection:** one-mana Entomb is strongest *exact Pirate* setup before combat; Buried Alive 3 cards creates two premium Pirates + Wonder split; Faithless Looting and Mary Read's tap draw/discard seed the grave; Tunnel-Grinder selectively discards and draws N+1 without refilling opponents; Brass ETB itself mills four. Wonder in GY + Island gives army flying, but Wonder is NOT a Pirate and cannot be returned by Brass itself. Grave hate or replacement exile attacks both Brass and mass recursion. Mary Read's discard triggers Treasure only if discarded **Island/Pirate/Vehicle card**; discarded Wonder is none of these, so Wonder puts flying online but gives no Treasure.

**Repeatability/timing:** Brass returns ONE targeted card per begin-combat trigger. Roaming Throne choosing Pirate doubles Brass's trigger (and ETB mill trigger), but if only one Pirate card in the grave both triggers cannot reanimate that same card twice; after one resolves and moves it, other target becomes illegal. Power Conduit is tap-limited without untap, one finality counter per activation, and its counter-removal is a cost, so can happen while answering instant-speed removal by activating in response. Removing finality counters doesn't itself cause death or recast; requires another sacrifice/kill/exchange. Living Death exiles currently graveyarded creatures *before* sacrifices: creatures sacrificed after exiling, including finality Pirates exiled instead of dying, are not among the creatures it returns.

**Interaction:** Deadly Rollick, Counterspell, Mana Drain, Fierce Guardianship, Deflecting Swat, Chaos Warp, Cyclonic Rift, Feed the Swarm, Siren Stormtamer and various Pirate ETB answer suite; some conditional on commander staying in play. Toxic Deluge clears creatures but also self-Pirates; selective grave hate may nullify value on trigger placement. Target counter/sacrifice still meaningful even through protection. Mass recursion has symmetric costs: Patriarch's Bidding and Living Death help opponents' graveyards too. Deck has good creature interaction and treasure but lacks enchantment grave hate offswitch abundance; can't rely on a counter after hate resolves.

## Oracle, legality and rules

Baseline retrospective PASS Oct 16 with exact source raw `056f...` and recorded historic override `e66...` both clearly distinguished. All approved names resolved, Commander legal, within B/R/U, 100 exact and only basics repeat. Official WotC banned announcement Oct 12 contains no Commander changes for these cards (https://www.magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/banned-and-restricted-october-12-2026). Current added candidate Hex Parasite confirmed official Commander legal, one color identity Black from Phyrexian mana, and Oracle activated ability **{X}{B/P}: Remove up to X counters from target permanent, +1/+0 for each** (https://mtg.wtf/card/nph/137/Hex-Parasite). This is a different effect from Conduit's **TAP, remove single counter as COST; put +1/+1 on creature or charge on artifact**. One is a chosen target permanent on RESOLUTION, the other removes cost pre-response; abilities must be paid/targeted at proper timings.

**Rulings explicit:**
1. Brass ETB mill four; with prior Roaming Throne naming Pirate, ETB mill triggers twice, eight cards milled through two resolving triggers. End-step damage alone never triggers reanimation.
2. Brass beginning-of-combat trigger **requires a legal target Pirate creature card already in YOUR graveyard as the trigger is put on stack**. An Entomb cast in response AFTER trigger is on stack cannot retroactively provide a target; cast during main phase before beginning combat instead.
3. Roaming Throne naming Pirate duplicates reanimation trigger, allowing two DIFFERENT eligible Pirate creature cards to return if both are grave targets at trigger time. Two triggers may target the same card, but first moving it generally makes the other fizzle; don't claim guaranteed double reanimation with one card.
4. Reanimated Pirate remains actual printed creature types, gains BASE 4/4 and haste; other printed abilities persist; it receives a finality counter. The counter replaces WOULD DIE with exile, so Pitiless Plunderer does **NOT** trigger from that finality creature being destroyed/sacrificed. Counter removal before death lets it die normally, but does NOT move it immediately.
5. Power Conduit removes finality as an activation cost after the creature is already returned, and can aim its +1/+1 counter at that or other legal creature; removing counter is paid even if opponent removes its chosen target before ability resolves. Conduit does NOT itself grant a free or repeated untap.
6. Living Death handles creatures exiled from graveyard BEFORE sacrificing in-play creatures; finality in-play Pirates sacrificed are exiled instead and won't return; even regular creatures sacrificed in the middle cannot come back through that same Living Death.
7. Patriarch's Bidding permits each player to choose a creature type and benefits opponents too. Creature cards chosen and returned simultaneously trigger Skeleton Crew only ONE time for a group leaving at once, not N times for N creature cards. Roaming Throne choosing Pirate may cause that Skeleton Crew triggered ability to trigger twice.
8. Wonder in grave with Island controlled gives flyers; not a Pirate, cannot be brought back by Brass's trigger. Return Wonder with generic Reanimate would turn OFF its grave-dependent global flying. Distinguish Island **land subtype** from generic producing blue mana.
9. Brass's Tunnel-Grinder triggers on END step if descended; entering itself and discarding cards only qualifies if at least one **PERMANENT CARD** went to a graveyard that turn, not a sorcery/instant-only discard or token death. Three qualifying end-step counters transform into Tecutlan; Tecutlan gives discover on casting permanent spells USING ITS MANA. Brass's reanimation puts Pirates directly into play, DOES NOT cast them and will not trigger Tecutlan's discover.
10. Mary Read's {T} draw-discard is playable immediately when reanimated with haste; Island/Pirate/Vehicle discard creates tapped Treasure, not the drawn card itself and not Wonder discard. Returned Mary can attack and tap only with legal timing; tapping before attack usually forfeits attacking without untap.
11. Port Razer's hit creates another combat and untaps your creatures; Razer cannot attack a player already attacked during that turn. With three opponents, normal repetition limited unless it becomes a new object. The value remains massive but not unconditional infinite.
12. Type-specific combo claims do not generalize to generic creatures: Roaming Throne itself becomes a Pirate (not an eligible Pirate card in GRAVE unless type-changing effect applies there); Skeleton Crew printed Skeleton Pirate and Commander printed Human Pirate.

## Finite sequencing and adversarial scenarios

**Worked scenario A: stack and counter removal.** At beginning of combat control Brass and untapped Power Conduit; Amphin Mutineer is in your graveyard. Brass trigger goes on stack, target Amphin, resolves. Amphin ETB targets opponent's non-Salamander creature, exile it and opponent gets 4/3 Salamander. Amphin now base 4/4 haste with finality. Before declaring attackers activate Power Conduit: TAP it and remove Amphin's finality counter as cost, choose +1/+1 and target Amphin. When ability resolves Amphin gets +1/+1 counter, attacks as 5/5 haste; if opponent removes Amphin before counter-placement, cost was already paid so finality remains gone and death puts Amphin back in GY, ready for a future Brass combat (unless opponent EXILES it). Sequence requires a legal creature target and unprevented reanimation; this is a finite repeat-engine proof, not infinite.

**Worked scenario B: double-trigger and grave depletion.** Brass/Throne(Pirate) controlled. Own grave holds Angrath's Marauders and Coercive Recruiter; BOTH eligible on begin combat, Brass trigger doubles, target each separately. In usual absence of responses, two 4/4 haste Pirates return with finality; Marauders doubles damage dealt by controlled sources and Recruiter ETB temporarily takes enemy creature and grants Pirate haste. Cannot remove both finality counters with single Conduit tap absent untap. If opponents remove Brass before beginning combat, zero trigger; if after trigger on stack, queued triggers resolve if targets legal despite Brass being absent, unless required effects depend on later Brass control (they do not). Return both requires distinct graveyard cards and no graveyard restriction.

**Worked scenario C: Wonder/selection.** Entomb Wonder on previous end step, control an Island; Wonder in your grave makes Pirates fly. Buried Alive in own precombat main can choose Port Razer, Amphin and Wonder if still in library; if Wonder already in grave choose other Pirate. At start combat Brass can target Port Razer, return 4/4 haste flyer and potentially hit for extra combat. If target gets eaten by instant grave-hate precombat, Brass may trigger with no target. Do not say Brass returns Wonder.

**Adversarial scenario D: hate and rescue.** Rest in Peace replacement exiles milled/discarded cards instead of grave and exiles dead Pirates, turns off Brass targets and Power Conduit reuse. Grafdigger's Cage prevents creature cards from grave entering battlefield; Brass's triggering ability can be placed but cannot return its targeted card while Cage applies. Existing removal must answer these hate permanents first. Under Rule of Law, only one spell per turn may limit graveyard setup but Brass trigger itself isn't a cast and can continue if already stocked. These cases show why merely adding a second finality-remover has diminishing value.

**Adversarial scenario E: baseline alternative**. Opponent protects against combat via Fog or blockers: Port Razer cannot produce extra combat, but reanimated Amphin and Corsair ETB still provide value even without damage. Anti-grave effects target upstream pipeline; more aggression alone cannot solve it. Mass grave reanimation may let opponents return better creatures and is not a guaranteed rescue.
