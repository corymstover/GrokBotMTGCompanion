## Commander identity and natural ceiling

**Power Bracket 5 — cEDH** (commander-specific evidenced by Oct14 tournament-source research and [October 17 2026 real tournament top-8 Kefka](https://metagame.info/en-us/mtg/tournaments/commander-cedh-octubre-hamelin-games-top-8-2026-10-17), not a forced goodstuff label). Archidekt tags **Discard / Trigger Copy / Turbo**.
**Front face:** `{2}{U}{B}{R}` 4/5 Human Wizard. Each ENTER/ATTACK is one trigger; each player (including YOU) discards ONE card if able, THEN YOU draw **ONE for each DISTINCT CARD TYPE among all cards discarded by all players by that one trigger**, not one per opponent, not one per card. Creature, artifact, instant, sorcery, enchantment, land, battle, planeswalker, kindred are card types. Supertype "legendary" isn't. An artifact creature discarded contributes TWO card types. When four players discard four creature cards, draw only **one**. Empty hands reduce both pressure and draw. You can set up graveyard for Anger/Breach/delirium by own discard.
**Transform:** `{8}` generic, sorcery-speed activated ability each opponent CHOOSES a permanent to sacrifice, then Kefka transforms to 5/7 flying Avatar Wizard. They make choices in order, then all sacrifice simultaneously; same permanent cannot be sacrificed twice. It's expensive at actual cEDH speed, and back requires opponent lose life on OUR turn to generate proportional draws; returning a discard effect that merely discards with no loss doesn't trigger back. The cEDH slot economy must earn front-face ETB/attack and compact win support first, without pretending Ruler is absent.
**Protected commander-first spine:** Anger discarded from Kefka's own ETB + Mountain/Steam Vents/Badlands/Volcanic Island; Arena of Glory cast haste; Gogo (no legend conflict), Harmonic Prodigy, Displacer Kitten, Essence Flux, Phantasmal Image, Phyrexian Metamorph, Saw in Half, Molten Duplication create more Kefka trigger pressure; Waste Not/Tinybones leverage actual discards. Independent compact kills Thassa's Oracle + Consultation/Pact, Underworld Breach + LED/Brain Freeze are allowed in genuine cEDH but should not replace Kefka with interchangeable Grixis shell.
**Natural ceiling and vulnerability:** Five colored-pip requirements `2UBR`, 28 lands plus fast artifacts/rituals and multiple fetch/land variants. Some stage-dependent cards (`Demonic Counsel`, `Mox Opal`, `Culling the Weak`, `Jeska's Will`, `Necropotence`) create tension with early opponent-hand compression or grave development. Strong table/turn interaction makes bracket 5 realistic, but not a guaranteed five-mana commander on turn2.

## Oracle, legality and rules
1. **Front type union:** A and B discard creatures, C discards land, we discard Anger creature => discarded types Creature+Land only, **draw2** despite FOUR cards. If player discards artifact creature, union adds Artifact too. Land cards discarded from own Kefka triggers count to that trigger. Discard with empty hand produces no card and no type; separate Harmonic triggers calculate separately.
2. **Harmonic Prodigy:** Other Wizard you control Kefka entering or attacking triggers ADDITIONAL time, rather than doubling each DRAW. If first resolution gives 3 distinct types then second after opponents have fewer cards gives 1/0 types, total 4/3 not 6 guaranteed. Kefka remains Wizard on BACK; life-loss draw triggered separately twice each with Prodigy, if Prodigy still there.
3. **Gogo:** Gogo's *already-triggered* beginning-of-combat copy ability resolves into "Gogo-named Kefka" and grants BOTH Gogo+original haste/+2 attack, but newly gained Kefka "whenever attacks" has not occurred yet. In declare attackers if both attack, **two** Kefka attack triggers, no new ETB trigger; legend rule sees different names, both live. If opposing instant removes original after Gogo's trigger target but before resolution, Gogo copy won't happen.
4. **Anger:** One Kefka ETB triggers, you choose Anger as YOUR discarded creature. In grave at trigger resolution, assuming a Mountain (Badlands/Steam Vents/Volcanic Island/Blood Crypt counts via land type) controlled, your Kefka gains haste and can attack this turn. `Necropotence` replacement exiling cards discarded by YOU can prevent Anger reaching grave; if Anger already there, fine. `Arena of Glory` haste via its special mana also needs actual correct cost/spend.
5. **Clones/legends:** `Phantasmal Image` or `Phyrexian Metamorph` copying Kefka on entry each generate a Kefka ENTER trigger, THEN state-based legend rule moves one conflicting same-name legendary from battlefield; existing trigger still resolves even if source gone. `Gogo` name exception avoids this rule; `Molten Duplication` enters token Kefka, ETB trigger then legend picks surviving hasty token or original. `Saw in Half` destroys Kefka, makes TWO entering Kefka token copies if actually destroyed, creates two ETB triggers, legend leaves one. Under CURRENT rules a TOKEN COPY of transforming double-faced Kefka is a **transforming double-faced token**, so can transform if it later pays 8; regular single-face `Phantasmal Image`/`Metamorph` or Gogo copied characteristics CAN'T transform. Cited current Lorwyn release notes and March of the Machine update; do not repeat obsolete pre-2023 "all tokens can't transform" rule.
6. **Displacer Kitten:** Noncreature SPELL cast while Kitten alive triggers blink own nonland, Kefka leaves and reenters before Kitty trigger resolves? Stack last-in-first-out; after it resolves Kefka ETB trigger fires, can cause more discards, but each cast is not automatically another noncreature spell. Blinking YOUR commander under your control after exit returns as new object, does not cost commander tax. Reanimate needs Kefka in graveyard (rather than having chosen command zone).
7. **Beseech the Mirror:** 1BBB, bargain sacrifice artifact/enchantment/token as **additional cost** to Beseech, then tutor a card, if bargain was paid may CAST MV <=4 searched card for free DURING resolution. `Thassa's Oracle` MV2 can be cast but must have library empty / Consultation response ready for Oracle trigger, and spell cast during resolution is not same as instant window for free cast; `Underworld Breach` MV2 works. Without bargain no free cast. `Valley Floodcaller` grants timing (noncreature flash), not free color or sacrifice materials.
8. **Demonic Counsel:** Without delirium (>=4 DIFFERENT card types among your graveyard), searches for DEMON card; approved 99 contains no Demon-type card, so often only shuffle. Kefka front self-discard one card per trigger does not automatically yield 4 types; instant ritual + fetch land + creature + artifact sacrificed/placed into grave can suffice, if actually all grave. The Oct17 Top8 also uses Counsel, so this is a risk to manage, not automatic proof to cut.

For opponent life-loss ON YOUR TURN (damage or life loss), each trigger draws equal that single opponent actual life lost; if opponent takes 3 from Bowmasters at one event, draw3, if 3 opponents lose one each separately, draw3 total. This is not "draw whenever opponent discards" unless another effect causes life loss. Do not confuse opponent paying life on their own turn.

## Finite sequencing and adversarial scenarios
**1. Zero opposing hand types:** Kefka ETB at four-person table; 3 opponents have empty hands, our own hand discards a Creature. Only Creature among discarded -> draw exactly ONE. Waste Not sees no opponent cards, Tinybones receives no opponent discards. Distinct from "3 discard triggers automatically".
**2. Mixed card-type union:** A discards Land, B a Creature, C an Artifact creature, us an Instant. Union Land/Creature/Artifact/Instant = **4**, though only 4 cards; Kefka draw4, with Harmonic Prodigy extra independent discard event could draw fewer on second pass if hands emptied.
**3. Real haste:** We cast 2UBR Kefka with City of Brass + Badlands Mountain + Underground Sea + Sol Ring for generic2; choose Anger in our hand to discard to ETB (still have hand because cast Kefka); after resolution Anger in GY and Badlands Mountain -> haste, attack now if combat still upcoming. With Prodigy, additional trigger may discard something else, not automatically more types.
**4. Gogo priority:** Control Kefka and Gogo, neither has haste yet. At beginning combat Gogo trigger targets Kefka. If resolves, Gogo copies Kefka except name, +2/haste each; declare both as attackers -> TWO separate Kefka attack triggers. An opponent can kill Gogo or target Kefka before resolved copy so only a single trigger remains; Gogo can't retroactively generate Kefka ETB.
**5. Legend paradox:** Kefka in play cast Phantasmal Image choosing to enter copying Kefka -> triggered ETB gets put on stack after image enters and state-based legendary action chooses one. Even if Image is grave when trigger resolves, each player discards and you draw types normally. Saw in Half on original Kefka, original destroyed, two KEFKA double-faced copies enter, TWO ETBs trigger, one survives legend; commander choice for destroyed original doesn't invalidate those copies.
**6. Counsel dead mode:** Opening cards fetchland, Chrome Mox imprint nonland, Sol Ring, Demonic Counsel, land, Force, combo piece. Turn2 own grave may contain only fetchLAND, maybe instant if cast, because Chrome Mox IMPRINT is EXILE not grave, Sol Ring stays battlefield; if <4 GY types, Counsel only searches a DEMON (none in approved library). Comparing hypothetical Dragon's Rage Channeler one-mana surveil after noncreature casts may fill additional types, but consumes tempo/card and can surveil unwanted win pieces. This is strongest strategic tension.
**7. Paid Thoracle line:** Own main cast `Thassa's Oracle` paying UU; when ETB trigger on stack, cast `Demonic Consultation` B naming nonexistent card, entire rest library exile; opponent gets priority to counter Consultation/Oracle trigger or kill Oracle to lower devotion, and we need enough blue devotion at resolution. If Oracle is last blue source and removed, still no-library (0<=0) wins if Oracle trigger exists and resolves, but thwart through Stifle/counter/Teferi etc. Actual spell targets/responses control result.
**8. Breach fuel:** Grave holds LED, Brain Freeze, 6 other cards. Cast Underworld Breach 1R, escape LED by exiling3 other GY cards, cast Brain Freeze 1U escaping for 3 other GY cards; use it to mill SELF with storm copies enough to replenish grave, must recast real spells and pay mana each. If 0 eligible extra cards before first escape, combo not online; cannot count hypothetical unbounded loop.
**9. Post-Kefka Jeska's Will:** A opponent begins hand5, Kefka two triggered discard events reduce to3. Casting Jeska's Will targeted at that player gives three R mana vs initial five, unless choose extra top-three mode with commander controlled. This makes Kefka's disruptive main plan sometimes anti-synergistic with specific turbo mana source; choose target/timing consciously.
**10. Transform is expensive:** Opponents each with expendable Treasure tokens, our 8 generic available at sorcery speed, transform; each sacrifices ONE CHOSEN permanent, often expendable tokens, then back draws only when opp LOSE LIFE on our turns. Ordinary Kefka first face board pressure may have outperformed spending eight now; if transform is the win line, confirm a way to trigger actual life loss e.g. combat/Bowmasters. No mass permanent deletion guaranteed.
**11. Modern opponent-top8 counterexample:** Actual Oct17 tournament comparator uses 26 lands and many independent interaction/draw choices; raw land-count benchmark alone suggests higher no-land risk but doesn't imply bad competitive deck, due to fast mana/mulligan selection. A control experiment should pair identical 100 starting hands/mulligan conditions and opponent pressure across (a) approved Kefka, (b) Channeler replacing Tinybones, (c) Faithless replacing Mnemonic Betrayal, (d) Talisman Indulgence replacing Culling, and (e) Opposition Agent replacing Counsel. Track turn1/2 delirium card types, 2UBR payable Kefka turn, Anger activation, meaningful Kefka triggers, protected combo windows, and wasted tutor spells. No sample data invented.
