## Commander identity and natural ceiling

`The Madcap Jester` is a four-mana {1}{U}{B}{R} 3/2 Clown Wizard. **Whenever Jester ATTACKS**, each opponent exiles top card of their library (even if attack deals zero combat damage). **Whenever a spell or ability YOU CONTROL CAUSES one or more cards to be exiled from an OPPONENT'S LIBRARY**, the Jester gives you permission to PLAY those cards **until end of your next turn**, with mana of any type allowed for casting; normal casting restrictions, mana costs, land-drop limits, and maintaining access to the exiled object still apply. Cards exiled from **your own** library, and opponents exiling cards via their own independent spells, do not meet trigger. One cause/action exiling multiple cards yields a single access trigger covering its eligible cards; several separate causes create separate triggers. If Jester isn't on battlefield when the exile event happens, its permission is not created retroactively. Permission granted and resolved generally persists if Jester is later removed before you play the exiled cards.

**Native plan to preserve:** U/X/BBB sorcery and activated library exiling (Extract, Sadistic Sacrament, Bitter Ordeal, Tasha's Hideous Laughter, Inside Information, Jester's Cap/Scepter, Ashiok, Psychic Surgery, Circu, Ensnared by the Mara and Jester ATTACK); cast-from-exile throughput and payoff (Bilbo discount, Dragon's Smile once-turn zero, Don Andres Treasures, Gonti, Prosper Treasures, Party Thrasher, Passionate Archaeologist damage from commander, Nalfeshnee copies, Wild-Magic Sorcerer first-exile-cast cascade, Laughing Jasper, Plargg, Sire, Ragavan); early haste/permission (Lightning Greaves, cheap tutors/rituals); interaction that keeps fragile 3/2 live; and compact secondary Oracle/Consult/Pact or Breach/LED/Petal/Brain Freeze wins. The native engine takes OPPONENT resource as cards playable until next turn, but doesn't automatically put those spells into your hand, cast off-turn sorceries, or permit paying unfamiliar colored requirements without ordinary mana restrictions (mana of any type solves color symbols only).

**No forced homogenization:** Replacing the unique exile effects / stolen-spell payoffs with more zero-to-two-mana generic Grixis staples might increase average turbo speed but undermine what makes Jester worth choosing over Kess/RogSi/Inalla. Conversely, high-MV 4–7-cost value payoffs slow this list compared with fully focused tournament turbo; the source Bracket5 is plausible from compact protected win packages but shouldn't be presented as measured cEDH conversion. Strongest real trade-off: amount of Exile Theft shown vs probability of protected early Oracle/Breach kill. Prior owner accepted this deliberate balance.

If opponents rarely cast black, Ritual/Petal can still turbo, but sacrificing LED requires discarding your hand and can scuttle protected Oracle unless combo assembled in graveyard.

**Structural engines and stress:** Opponent library-exile source density ~11 primary enablers, alongside commander attack/Greaves. Some live exile effects expensive (Cap six mana incl activation; Dragon's Smile seven; Sire six; Nalfeshnee six; Plargg five) and can be removal magnets; best true optional cuts among slow candidates are **Sire of Stagnation, Plargg and Nassari, Nalfeshnee, Jester's Cap, Gonti and Don Andres** only if swap increases objective early commander exile tempo without crushing stolen-spell payoffs. Their defense is each doing something unusual: Sire draws two on opponent land+exiles top2; Cap searches chosen opponent cards, steals via Jester and hates library combo; Nalfeshnee copies exile casts; Plargg produces library access even when opponents refuse to cooperate. Generic +1-mana rocks over those is not automatically commander-first improvement; measure distribution of opening hands where Jester on T2/3 with protection against capacity to exploit stolen cards.

**Card velocity/protection:** Instant-like free/cheap Force/Negation/Fierce/Pact/Swat/Drain/Swan/Rollick versus draw Remora, Rhystic, Necropotence, extra theft, tutors Demonic/Vampiric/Imperial/Gamble/Beseech/Mystical. Pact of Negation has mandatory next-upkeep 3UU or lose, so a T1 combo attempt without subsequent mana/timing can self-destruct. Mox Opal metalcraft isn't automatic with only one artifact; needs THREE including itself. Preserving Opal over Grinding Station has real turn1 color impact; the best alt Station cut would instead challenge a slow cEDH-unproven top-end, if owner authorizes reconsideration. ### Strongest P0 and competing alternatives

**Hullbreaker Horror against Deadly Rollick:** Horror is **seven mana** (not eight). Its flash and spellcast-bounce engine can dominate long games, especially beside cheap artifacts, but doesn't itself exile an opponent's library and cannot replace Rollick's *free* response to creature-combo when Jester is tapped out. A much fairer alternative would be to challenge `Sire of Stagnation` or `Nalfeshnee` (expensive Jester-specific exile/draw/copy), not delete cheap protection. That alternate cut sacrifices commander identity and still requires a real matchup/turn chronology. No supported approval request.

**Grinding Station against Mox Opal or Dragon's Smile:** Station is cheap and can sacrifice artifacts to selfmill for Breach; it requires targets, sacrificial artifacts and tap/untap events, not an automatic loop. Against opponent cards, its ordinary mill sends them to grave, so "Jester draws everything" requires the Dauthi Voidwalker replacement interaction, marked **uncertain** by existing Oct16 rules audit. If owner later wants to test Station, a 5–7 mana slow payoff cut (not free colored Opal or project-unique Dragon's Smile) would be the coherent comparator. This project intentionally reserves many exile payoffs, so no automatic swap.

**The positive P1 mana-score cluster:** Two-mana Fellwar Stone, UR Talisman, two-color Signets and generic Thran Dynamo naturally accumulate more mana over 5–8 turns than one-shot `Lotus Petal` or `Rite of Flame`. Their *simulator pass* doesn't prove a better turn2/3 Jester with interaction or a faster protected Oracle/Breach win. `Thieving Varmint` cannot pay for Jester because it produces mana usable ONLY for spells you DON'T own, another mana-model blind spot. In a slower table, a repeatable rock might compete with a top-end value creature, but among the proposed cuts Petal/Rite are critical turbo sources.

**The false P1 Oracle cuts:** `Mizzix`, `Crackdown Construct`, `Cephalid Illusionist/Aristocrat`, `Ambassador Laquatus` do not reproduce Oracle+Consultation/Tainted Pact and Breach's Oracle finish. Their abstract pass is not a valid win-rate upgrade. `Wheel` over Remora and `Reiterate` over Pact of Negation likewise remove one-mana draw or zero-mana protected endgame without a commander-native replacement. `Breaking // Entering`, `Glimpse` and slow self-mill creatures don't directly exile opponent-library cards; invoking the uncertain Dauthi interpretation as an assured Jester win is forbidden.

**Alt-cut research only:** `Sire of Stagnation` (6 MV), `Plargg and Nassari` (5 MV), `Nalfeshnee` (6 MV), `Gonti` (4 MV) and `Jester's Cap` (six effective mana incl activation) are the true slow/conditional competitors against Fellwar/Talisman/Horror/Station, not Oracle/free counter/Opal/Petal. Each currently serves an opponent-library theft or exiled-spell payoff role. ## Oracle, legality and rules

**Commander latest targeted official notes:** Wizards [MBC release notes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/mystery-booster-commander-edition-release-notes) contain `The Madcap Jester` printed text and explicit normal TIMING/payment ruling. Those notes do NOT affirm full official pre-release Commander legality; Wizards [legality announcement](https://www.magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/whats-inside-mystery-booster-commander-edition) still sets **November 13, 2026**. Official card images/database sites may mark cards legal in anticipation; follow Wizards' date.

**Direct exiles:** A Jester attack ability YOU control causes up to one exiled card from each opponent, one grouped event, and access through end NEXT turn even when controller/opponent manually performs physical exile. With Ashiok - or Extract/Sacrament/Cap/Tasha/Ensnared/Circu/Psychic Surgery - if *your* spell or ability exiles opposing library, permission covers those exiled cards through next turn. Ensnared villainous choice: one card may be cast free now OR top 4 exiled and hit for total MV; Jester's normal-cost permission may give access to all library-exiled cards independently, including those not free cast now. Correct spells cast from exile trigger Don Andres, Prosper, Passionate Archaeologist and Nalfeshnee, each under their own conditions. Jester's permission has normal land one-per-turn limit, no fast-land permission, no casting enemy sorceries during other player's turn absent flash, and stolen colors may be paid using mana of any type but the cost still exists.

**Inside Information is two NONCOMBINABLE cost permissions:** XBB library exile first resolves. Its built-in THIS-TURN permission requires pay LIFE equal to exiled spell mana value rather than its mana cost, while Jester gives independent through-next-turn permission to pay actual mana using any type; Dragon's Smile offers once-per-your-turn pay{0} alternative cost. You CHOOSE one applicable alternative cost. For a big exiled spell with life3 and MV6, choosing Inside Information's pay life6 or Jester normal mana6 or Dragon's Smile zero on YOUR turn changes source-paid reality; there is no "pay life and zero" or zero at opponent's instant speed if ordinary casting illegal.

**Dauthi replacement is explicitly UNCERTAIN, not approved deterministic Jester engine:** When `Brain Freeze` causes opponent to mill, `Dauthi Voidwalker` replacement exiles those library cards into exile with void counters instead of into their graveyard. The rules plausibly attribute modified event to controlled Brain Freeze spell and thus Jester's cause trigger; **2026-10-16 source interaction jester-02 is marked UNCERTAIN** because new Jester official rulings do not specifically establish the `causes... to be exiled` attribution after opponent library-to-grave replacement. Thus MAY trigger Jester but do NOT count this as proven in making cut decisions, M6 pass, or official infinite line. Independently Dauthi gravehate + one-time cast void-exiled card remains useful. If a stolen card was cast using Jester permission, it changes zones; its later Dauthi exile after leaving grave does NOT inherit original one-use Jester permission, no repeat Petal exploit.

**Scepter face-down exiled cards:** You can LOOK at the targeted opponent's face-down cards because Scepter permits it; official source interaction jester-03 treats them as playable via Jester if exiled from OPPONENT library and still in exile; casting turns card up and usual mana/timing applies. If Scepter spends one of those cards for its counter effect, it leaves exile and Jester permission for that object ends.

**Consultation/Pact are OWN-library self-exile:** They cannot provide Jester stolen-library access. Oracle ETB trigger goes to stack, hold priority and cast Consultation naming card absent from remaining library to empty; empty deck + Oracle resolve checks devotion, wins even with zero devotion (no failed draw involved). For `Tainted Pact` all 100 physical names distinct, so it can continue reveal/exile until own library empty and not be forced to stop on a duplicate; player chooses not to put one into hand on way, ensuring depletion; Oracle must remain in play/ETB trigger valid and uncountered to win. Opponent can counter Oracle, Stifle ETB trigger, exile grave for Breach, or restrict spells with Rule of Law; plan has real stack vulnerability.

**Breach resource line (payable, not a magic "infinite" label):** Underworld Breach 1R on field, LED and Brain Freeze in grave or ready to discard, at least 3 expendable grave for LED escape and further 3 for Brain Freeze, mana from LED UUU. Cast/escape LED0, it enters; activate sacrifice/discard-hand for UUU, escape Brain Freeze for 1U + exile THREE OTHER grave cards, with LED previously CAST this turn Brain Freeze storm creates at least ONE copy, target yourself for at least 6 mill. After Brain Freeze original resolves it goes to grave, LED grave and selfmill cards refill future escapes; later storm grows as repeated LED/Freeze casts are performed. Initially requires more noncombo grave fuel if both LED/Freeze start in grave; opponent priority/fast grave exile, counters, library depletion, creature attacks interfere. If mill opponent with Dauthi, replacement MAY exile and Jester MAY grant access, **not needed** for source Breach victory. Milling opponent deck empty is not immediate loss until forced draw or further action; finish via Oracle if own deck empty or empty opponent on draw.
**Exile triggers timing:** Psychic Surgery triggers when opponent SHUFFLES (not each search if shuffle bypassed), Jester gain access by player-caused library exile; Sadistic Sacrament kicker exiles 15 cards at high 10 total mana versus 3 normally. Jester's first attacker ability exiles before blocking; no combat damage required. The color identity of spells STOLEN from opponent doesn't affect legal deck inclusion because stolen aren't deck cards, and any type of mana can pay for colored pips but still need sufficient generic/total.
