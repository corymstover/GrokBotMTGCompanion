## Commander identity and natural ceiling

Thraximundar `{4}{U}{B}{R}` is a 6/6 haste Zombie Assassin that causes *defending player* to sacrifice one creature of their choice when it attacks, before blockers, and may grow +1/+1 for **every creature sacrificed by any player** (including sacrifices as costs). The authentic build composes a seven-mana commander-damage clock from (1) opponent-facing edicts, (2) repeatable own Zombie bodies and free sac outlets, (3) loop drain/damage, (4) reliable colored ramp and evasion so Thrax actually attacks, (5) targeted protection against costly recasts. Creature destruction from a wrath does NOT constitute 'sacrifice', nor do sacrificed planeswalkers/artifacts unless they are creatures.

**Identity boundary:** Thrax acts as the **other Zombie** to enable Gravecrawler from graveyard and becomes the physical +1/+1 counter payoff of sacrifice loops; its attack edict pumps itself through its second trigger. Rooftop Storm can make its printed Zombie mana cost 0 while commander tax still applies on casts from the command zone. Cultist/Shizo can close commander damage with flying/fear and +3/+3 before counters, but a seven-mana threat remains vulnerable to exile, instant removal, bounce and commander tax. High Bracket 4 natural ceiling; not an independently verified cEDH deterministic speed deck. Generic tutor/Oracle/Hullbreaker lock upgrades that remove edict engines harm identity.

### Material challenged alternatives and actual replacement risks

#### Relic of Legends → Black Market Connections, P0 agreement
The **strongest** independent-optimizer/challenger disagreement is real: a 3MV rock taps for any color and can tap any untapped legendary you control to produce mana, with Mahadi, Braids, Liliana? Liliana is a planeswalker so cannot tap her as a creature, and Thrax is not on board pre-seven mana. The before-commander legends available (Mahadi, Braids, Wilhelt, Grimgrin, etc.) can sometimes be tapped without summoning sickness due to Relic's own ability (the untapped creature as cost); but those require casting other cards first. Tapping Thrax after it enters competes with immediate hasty attacks unless sequencing around combat; tapped commander cannot attack. BMC provides a choice of Treasure, card and Changeling 3/2 (Zombie) each turn with controlled life expenditure, enabling tutors, edicts and Crawler. Current deck has many two-mana signets/talismans already. Relic's potential infinites need exact untap-reentry board/cost accounting. No verified upgrade at BMC cut. KEEP.

#### Deadly Dispute / Commander's Sphere / Chromatic Lantern / Relic of Sauron / Thran Dynamo / Crowded Crypt → BMC
Each supplies ramp or draw by a different route, and some are individually good. Dispute is only single spell, requires sacrifice and uses two mana; Sphere and Lantern cost three, tapping for one; Relic of Sauron four and Dynamo four yields colorless; Crypt three plus delay. BMC begins making optional draw, Treasure and a Changeling fodder during the controller's first precombat main phase after its cast and is not a creature that ordinary wipes destroy. Seven-mana Thrax rewards fast mana but BMC is both ramp *and* drain/card/Zombie fuel, and the baseline has 36 lands, Ancient Tomb, Mana Vault, Sol Ring and a 2MV rock package. Keep until paid opponent-aware timing proves otherwise.

#### Viscera Seer / Woe Strider / Blasting Station / Spawning Pit / sacrifice-outlet replacements
More free outlets are powerful but the 100 already has Phyrexian/Ashnod's/Altar of Dementia, Carrion Feeder, Goblin Bombardment, Warren Soultrader, Grimgrin, Yawgmoth and Phyrexian Tower. An outlet alone does not create recursive creature or win payoff. The relevant cuts remove BMC's early engine or Tergrid's theft; simple redundancy is not a commander-specific net win. Blasting Station has a real loop with a recurring creature that enters each cycle, but requires resolving and untapping at priority windows, and is not a better sacrifice-to-damage role than cheaper Bombardment at the named cut.

#### Roaming Throne → Mahadi
Choosing Zombie can duplicate **triggered** abilities of other Zombie creatures you control, including Thrax's sacrifice-counter and attacking edict triggers, making a strong commander-forward addition rather than pure generic goodstuff. Note: Thrax gets two triggers per creature sacrificed, not an additional sacrifice from the opponent automatically; doubling his attack trigger does ask two sacrifices. Roaming Throne is itself an artifact creature and one MV higher than Mahadi. It doesn't duplicate Grave Pact/Dictate (enchantments), or improve turn-three resources. Losing Mahadi's Treasure from each dying creature (including edicted opponents) weakens the mana engine for recasts and sacrificed Zombie loops. This is the most serious *off-P0* challenger and deserves future paired testing against an actually optional higher-cost card, not an automatic cut. KEEP in current locked list.

#### Bontu's Monument → Talisman of Creativity
A meaningful direct Gravecrawler payoff: cost reduction applies to black creature spells and opponent loss/life gain fires when you CAST creature spells, yielding a deterministic payoff with Rooftop Storm/Altar. But it does not make mana nor satisfy the R and U pips of {4}{U}{B}{R}; the specific cut removes cheap U/R fixing. Existing Blood Artist, Zulaport, Diregraf, Mayhem Devil and Goblin Bombardment already close recursive creature wins. KEEP named cut; flag as interesting independent alt-payoff only.

#### Aetherflux Reservoir / Heartless Summoning / Rooftop Storm, creature loops
Aetherflux rewards sequences of many casts and is a legitimate outside-the-commander loop win piece, but removes Tergrid opponent-sacrifice payoff at screened cut. Heartless Summoning gives -1/-1 to Gravecrawler (2/1 → 1/0), shutting down its ordinary battlefield sacrifice loop because it dies via state-based actions *without* sacrifice; also kills one-toughness utility, a material antisynergy. The raw Spellbook completion number omits these physical changes in play. KEEP.

#### Tombstone Stairwell → Toxic Deluge
Stairwell creates huge recurring Skeleton churn each upkeep proportional to graveyards, a strong multiplayer sacrifice/death amplifier with Mayhem Devil/Grave Pact/artist. It gives the same potential to opponents and requires many creature cards in graveyards, and the 100 has no repeatable sorcery-speed 3MV unconditional board wipe after losing Toxic Deluge. It is not a one-for-one replacement for Deluge against wide tables before commander. KEEP; evaluate only with safer separate cut in a changed meta.

#### Night of Souls' Betrayal / Kaervek, the Spiteful / Engineered Plague
Static global -1/-1 disrupts Gravecrawler (2/1 → 1/0), especially bad with Thrax on board wanting to SACRIFICE rather than simply have creatures die to state-based actions. A token sweeper can be useful against opponents but changes primary loop availability; Toxic Deluge lets the pilot control X and sequence around reanimation. NOT an equivalent replacement. KEEP.

#### Sheoldred, Whispering One replacements
Sheoldred costs seven, same as Thrax, so she is a plausible flex target in extremely fast pods. However the existing list explicitly wants slow inevitable opponent-upkeep *sacrifice* (pump Thrax) plus recurring own creature reanimation (reuse Fleshbag/Winter/Plaguecrafter). The substitute names—Yahenni, Relentless Dead, Elemental Mastery, Garrison Excavator, etc.—each add only one package. Some may be faster but none at the proposed cut has verified a robust replacement for BOTH roles. KEEP while acknowledging an alternate metagame might prefer a cheaper direct support card.

#### Tergrid replacements
Tergrid is high-salience and vulnerable to removal, but it **steals permanents opponents actually sacrifice**, creating a strong commander-native reward for Thrax attacks and Grave Pact/Vona/Flare. Replacing it with generic self-mill, Hullbreaker bounce, Tidespout, Aetherflux, Pashalik (Goblin-specific) or another artifact sacrifice outlet changes the strategic payoff away from opponent sacrifices. Opponent choice/commander pressure are underrepresented in source goldfish, so do not use those pass markers to demote it.

#### Seven-mana Thrax, cultivation and Cultist hazard
Two-colorless Ancient Tomb, Mana Vault, Cof﻿fers-Urborg, Sol Ring plus cheap rocks are critical to cast {4}{U}{B}{R}. Once Thrax attacks, his forced sacrifice resolves in declare attackers before blocking and his own counter ability triggers. With Cultist he starts at 9/9 flying/deathtouch/ward 3 life, but each upkeep requires **sacrifice a creature**, and if he is your only creature, you must sacrifice your commander. This is a material real drawback; the deck's Gravecrawler/Skeleton/Changeling tokens and Wilhelt fodder support it, but the opening sequence cannot count the +3/+3 as 'free'. No candidate list swap fixes this better than the retained plan while preserving all support roles.


### Off-ranking and pre-approval red-team challenges

- **Ophiomancer:** Creates a reliable Snake each upkeep to feed Pact/Dictate/Cultist; very credible absent stronger fodder, but the current recursion bodies already make deterministic kills and the screened 'weak' BMC is another body source. Targeted re-test rather than forced insertion.
- **Lagomos, Hand of Hatred:** Early haste Elemental sacrifice at end step and conditional tutor after enough deaths. Distinct strong engine, but extra red pip and once-per-own-turn body, no proven upgrade over present tutors/edicts.
- **Soul Shatter / Make an Example:** One-sided or multiplayer edicts are on-theme, but actual Vona's Hunger/Flare/Grave Pact already make opponents sacrifice and no weak incumbent replaces one.
- **Blasphemous Edict:** High-ceiling board mass-sacrifice, but can force own commander to sacrifice; unlike Toxic Deluge its own-board removal not controllable on chosen X.
- **The Ozolith:** Counters transferred away from Thrax when he leaves battlefield are compelling, but it protects accumulated power rather than protecting commander from removal or enabling a fresh attack; cut cost high.
- **Geralf, Visionary Stitcher:** Zombie token sacrifice and flying support can grant evasion, but Cultist and Shizo already open commander damage, and Yawgmoth is better draw/outlet.
- **Butcher of Malakir:** Seven-mana body duplicates Grave Pact/Dictate, fails early commander deployment; not an improvement at named slot.
- **Gisa, Glorious Resurrector:** Strong opponents' death-to-own creatures transformation but exiles opponents' creatures as replacement; can conflict with grave recursion politics and more direct Thrax sac benefit.
- **Tarrian's Soulcleaver:** Excellent commander counters-from-death equipment alternative, but loses Cultist's ward, flying, and deathtouch; no stronger evasive damage line established.
- **Raw Oracle/Consult fast win packages:** Explicitly violate commander-first role and force stronger bracket without making Thrax's own attack and sacrifices matter; out of scope absent user directive.

## Oracle, legality and rules

1. **Attacker/defending player:** Declare Thrax attacking one defending player; attack trigger goes to stack, that player sacrifices a creature of their choice on resolution BEFORE blockers are declared. Thrax itself gains +1/+1 for that sacrifice if on battlefield when triggered and counter trigger resolves. You cannot choose their creature unless another effect says you do.
2. **Universal trigger:** Thraximundar triggers when *any player* sacrifices a **creature**, including paying Phyrexian Tower, Diabolic Intent, Flare of Malice's nontoken-black alternative cost or Cultist upkeep. It does not trigger on Vandalblast destroying artifacts, on an ordinary 'destroy creature' wipe, on sacrificing only planeswalkers for Plaguecrafter/Flare, or on noncreature Treasure sacrifice for mana.
3. **Carrion Feeder/Altars and Zombie condition:** Gravecrawler can be CAST from grave only if controller controls **another Zombie**. Thrax (when on battlefield) qualifies, also Carrion Feeder, Diregraf Captain, Wilhelt, Grimgrin, Fleshbag (if held), Warren Soultrader and BMC Changeling token. Gravecrawler cannot be cast if no Zombie remains. 'Can't block' limitation remains.
4. **Altar recursion costs:** Phyrexian Altar sac Gravecrawler generates B which pays 1B? Gravecrawler's cost is **{B}**, so same B recasts once; the 2-card-with-a-Zombie recurring cast/death/sacrifice is unbounded. Ashnod's Altar produces only CC, so by itself cannot pay Gravecrawler's black cost without a source/engine.
5. **Rooftop Storm:** pay zero instead of printed Zombie creature spell mana, not an alternative mana value; **commander tax still added** when Thrax cast from command zone. Saccing Gravecrawler to Carrion Feeder/Bombardment for 0 and recasting with another Zombie alive yields unbounded triggered events, potentially immediately lethal with payoffs.
6. **Skeleton-Plunderer-Phyrexian:** sacrifice Skeleton to Phyrexian Altar for one mana of any color, Plunderer creates Treasure when another creature you control dies; Treasure yields another mana, pay 1B to return Skeleton **tapped**. The Skeleton's own return is an activated ability from grave, not a cast, but sacrifice is repeatable; life/colored mana must be tracked.
7. **Skeleton-Plunderer-Ashnod:** each Skeleton sacrifice yields CC plus Treasure via Plunderer; spend C plus Treasure for {1}{B} return, leaving one net colorless mana per cycle. Sacrificed Treasure itself is a noncreature artifact, not Thrax counter, but repeated Skeleton sacrifice triggers Thrax if present.
8. **Warren Soultrader** costs one life to sacrifice another creature and make Treasure; the Gravecrawler loop needs the generated Treasure for {B}, and if unbounded iterations without a drain/lifegain component it can kill YOU. Blood Artist or Zulaport recoups life in-loop and damages opponents as a real payoff.
9. **Cultist hazard:** Cultist of Absolute +3/+3, flying, deathtouch, ward life, and mandatory upkeep SACRIFICE A CREATURE. If Thrax is your only creature then you must sacrifice Thrax, not decline the trigger. Token fodder from BMC/Wilhelt/Gravecrawler mitigates but doesn't guarantee safe upkeep; no source claim of risk-free permanent buff.
10. **Call to the Grave:** opponent each upkeep must sacrifice a non-Zombie if able, Thrax Zombie spared. It can force your own non-Zombie support to die, including Blood Artist/Plunderer/Yawgmoth, so protection timing matters.
11. **Winter:** optional own creature or planeswalker sacrifice when Winter ETBs leads each opponent to sacrifice a creature. The initial sacrifice if creature grows Thrax, opponent creatures each further trigger; sacrificing planeswalker does not. Winter's ability is not a generic permanent-free wipe.
12. **Flare/Plaguecrafter:** Flare of Malice forces highest mana value creature or planeswalker sacrifice by each opponent; only creature sacrifices count. Plaguecrafter may lead discard or planeswalker rather than creature sacrifice if boards are empty.
13. **Toxic Deluge versus all-sac:** -X/-X kills indestructible creatures via toughness zero when sufficient, but is destruction/state-based graveyard movement not sacrifice, gives Thrax zero counters. Necrotic Hex/Vona's Hunger are actual sacrifice effects; different role for speed versus counter generation.
14. **Shizo vs Greaves:** Fear stops most nonblack, nonartifact blockers. Lightning Greaves grants shroud and can prevent YOU targeting Thrax with Shizo's activated fear ability until Greaves moved. Boots hexproof does not block your own targeting; attack planning differs.
15. **Cabal/Urborg and Command Beacon:** Coffers taps while paying 2 for B per Swamp, Urborg works on opponents' lands too. Command Beacon sacrificed to move commander to HAND; hand-cast doesn't add tax; subsequent command zone casts count only previous command-zone casts.
16. **Tergrid:** only opponent **sacrificed non-token permanents** that go to opponent graveyards are available for theft if Tergrid is on battlefield, and owner choices/stack interactions may alter outcomes. Not every edict or wipe guarantees a creature stolen.
17. **Roaming Throne:** does not double *static* or sacrifice costs, and doubling Thrax's triggered +1/+1 and attack-edict abilities depends on appropriate selected creature type and its other-creature condition; it cannot copy Grave Pact or Dictate enchantment triggers merely by Zombie selection.
18. **Bontu's Monument:** reduces black creature spells and triggers when you cast creatures, strong on Gravecrawler repeated CAST loop; cannot produce colored mana as a two-mana Talisman and is not self-funding without the cast/mana enablers.
19. **Heartless Summoning:** Gravecrawler is base 2/1, -1/-1 continuous effect makes it 1/0 and it dies state-based before you can activate sacrifice outlets. Creature dying is NOT being sacrificed, so does not directly trigger Thrax or refund Phyrexian Altar. This invalidates naïve completion if Crawler is needed on battlefield to be sacrificed.
20. **Official status and exception boundary:** Canonical Oct15 source and Oct16 static Scryfall verification reported official Commander legality PASS and no project exception; no new October 20 full current banlist run performed, so any future newly changed legality must be separately verified rather than silently certified.
