## 2. Commander-first strategy and ceiling

**Identity to preserve:** Lynde (1UBR 2/4 deathtouch) repeatedly recycles **Curses from battlefield into their owner's graveyard**, returns them attached to their owner at the **next end step**, then **at the beginning of YOUR upkeep** moves one Curse attached to you to an opponent and draws two if moved. Every part matters: own graveyard, from battlefield, delayed return, your upkeep, one attached Curse per trigger, no target on move. Merely discarding/milling a Curse cannot trigger the return. This is not generic enchantress, storm, Grixis control or Oracle-Consult.

**Win texture:** incremental redirected-Curse damage/attrition (Curse of Thirst, Torment of Scarabs, Cruel Reality, Maddening Hex, Restless Dead pressure), opponent-targeted political resource incentives, copied enchantments, persistent constellation drains (Grim Guardian, Wicked Visitor), and enough premium permission/removal to survive the slow clock. **Bracket 4 — Optimized, at the low end** is the source's realistic ceiling: quality interaction and tutors are high; no proven compact in-list Curse-native game-ending loop. Lower-power pods may face oppressive attrition, while fast combo pods can simply outrun the slow Curse clocks.

**Protected sub-engines:** (a) low-cost Curse reset `Despotic Scepter` + `Claws of Gix` / `Braids` / `Perilous Research` / `Demon of Fate's Design`; (b) Paradox Haze / The Ninth Doctor / Obeka, Splitter of Seconds extra upkeep creation; (c) self-enchanted Curse of Misfortunes fetching further Curses before Lynde redistributes them; (d) Curse of Bloodletting combat-damage amplification of Obeka; (e) Curse-copy variants; (f) broad answers and 35-land blue/black/red reliability. Weak slots may exist; changes must improve these mechanics on cost, timing, resilience or clock, rather than merely create another commander-agnostic two-card combo.

### Exact-trigger traps and adversarial checks

1. `Curse of Misfortunes`'s Oracle is "**At the beginning of YOUR upkeep**..." (the **Aura controller's** upkeep) regardless of who it enchants. When it enchants **you**, both the Curse tutor and Lynde upkeep trigger occur; as controller, order Lynde below the tutor on the stack, tutor resolves first, then move a Curse. When attached to an opponent, it still triggers on YOUR upkeep and tutors a Curse **onto that opponent**, not during their upkeep. Accordingly, self-placement is optional tactical access to use Lynde's draw, not a universal prerequisite for repeated enemy attachment.
2. A newly returned Curse on you at the preceding end step that says "**At the beginning of enchanted player's upkeep**" independently triggers at YOUR upkeep. If `Cruel Reality` and Lynde both trigger, resolving Lynde first and moving Cruel Reality **does not remove its already-triggered effect**. Likewise Torment of Scarabs; `Curse of Thirst` checks Curse count when its trigger resolves. Reset timing should avoid self-lethal upkeeps.
3. Scepter taps to destroy **target permanent you own**, not necessarily control; a Curse you own enchanting an enemy is a valid target. Claws and Perilous Research require a permanent **you control**; you still control Aura Curses enchanting opponents. Scepter has no mana activation cost, Claws costs {1} per use, Perilous Research costs {1}{U} and a permanent after drawing two.
4. At the beginning of an end step, Braids triggers; its sacrifice occurs as that trigger resolves *after* the beginning of that step. Lynde's new delayed return therefore cannot trigger for the already-passed start of this end step; the next end step is normally the next player's, not immediately this one. With 4-player turns, normally returns at next player's end step before your next upkeep. If an effect causes a new end step sooner, use that actual next end step.
5. Additional upkeep does not provide a natural draw or untap. `Paradox Haze` adds a further upkeep to enchanted player's beginning phase. `The Ninth Doctor` requires the specified untap-step condition to trigger, and doesn't endlessly retrigger itself in extra upkeeps. Obeka must **deal combat damage to a player** and adds that many additional upkeep steps after the phase; Obeka damage boosted by a Curse of Bloodletting on that **damaged player** increases steps; no built-in unblockability.
6. Lynde's move does **not target** an opponent (can place Curse on a hexproof player); casting a Curse normally targets as an Aura spell. Copies of Curse of Echoes' spells are **not cast**, so won't trigger spell-cast triggers. `Maddening Hex` damages its currently enchanted spellcasting player and then attaches randomly to another opponent; it is not a repeatable deterministic controller-chosen allocation.
7. `Curse of Fool's Wisdom` on yourself triggers life loss **and** life gain when you draw; equal exchange is life neutral if no effect changes it. `Curse of Unbinding` still mills the enchanted player but its Aura **controller** gets the creature. `Curse of Surveillance` Aura controller chooses eligible draw targets. None is a license to ignore graveyard loss, timing or stack responses.

## 4. Structural and source-reliability analysis

- **Mana:** 35/99 lands, six fetchlands, three original duals (Badlands/Underground Sea/Volcanic Island), three shocks, Command Tower/City of Brass/Mana Confluence, six printed mana rocks plus Sol Ring (seven). Oracle-aware static source audit: **19 B, 20 U, 18 R** land source labels (including conditional); plus repeatable rocks **24 B/24 U/22 R** labels. Explicitly **NOT** guaranteed independent simultaneous sources. Commander `{1}{U}{B}{R}`, first recast six mana. Black has the greatest raw colored-pip demand: **32 of 68 pips (47.06%)**, vs 22 U and 14 R. `Nightscape Familiar` only reduces payable *generic* amounts. Filter Signets demand an activation mana, do not work as standalone sources. Bojuka Bog and Xander's Lounge enter tapped; some multiplayer lands can also enter tapped. Basic quantity check Island 4 / Mountain 3 / Swamp 3, enough fetch targets after early duals are gone.
- **Bounded static source proxies (not win rates):** no-mulligan cards-seen/turn-4 proxy estimates **79.9237%** all three colors present among available *land* sources, but separately only **59.2219%** hit strict four natural land drops by eleven seen cards, and these are **not multiplied** to produce a commander cast probability. Rocks, mulligans, sequencing, tapped timing and opponent decisions are not simulated jointly. This is why a 4-mana commander can't be simply assumed on T4.
- **Curve/development:** Accelerates chiefly via Ring, Tomb and seven two-mana rocks, not Lotus Petal/Vault sprint; curve has expensive Curses (Cruel Reality seven; multiple five/six) and engines (Doomwake, Demon, Bitterheart). Mana priorities: t1/t2 Curse or rock; t3 Lynde/engine only with acceleration; t4 Lynde at best with U/B/R; then token/control, reset and upkeep multiplication. Extra-upkeep engines often cost their entire turn (Haze 3; Doctor 3; Obeka 4) and may create *no* extra draw if no Curse is currently attached to you.
- **Interaction:** Mana Drain, Swan Song, Fierce Guardianship; Deflecting Swat redirects targeted spells; Deadly Rollick, Withering Torment, Chaos Warp answer spot threats; Toxic Deluge, Damnation, Blasphemous Act clear creatures; Cyclonic Rift protects from unfavorable assembled board. **Against Rest in Peace or Leyline of the Void**, the Curse never reaches your graveyard normally, shutting off Lynde's death trigger; hard counters and enchantment removal, not extra upkeep, are the answers. Against **Farewell** or exile board wipes, delayed Curse recycling does not work. `Propaganda` protects against aggro but cannot stop noncombat combo. One `Bojuka Bog` checks opposing graveyards but enters tapped.
- **Card flow:** Lynde draw-two is conditional on a Curse being on you when the upkeep ability resolves AND moving it; Rhystic Study, Reaper, Entity Tracker, Grim Guardian/Visitor payoffs, Curse of Verbosity, surveillance, Jeska's Will and three topdeck/universal tutors offer real backups. Tutor count isn't a substitute for a recursion outlet. `Curse of Misfortunes` is uniquely scalable; if removed or exiled early, one-for-one Curse deployment becomes slow.
- **Self-punishment vs attrition:** biggest structural risk is multiple harmful Curses returned together. Self-attached Bloodletting amplifies all incoming damage; Death's Hold reduces own creatures; Echoes lets others copy your spells. Opponents can aim removal at **Lynde before** your own Curse hits graveyard to deny her trigger; once delayed ability created, killing Lynde does not stop a previously triggered delayed return. Tactical threshold: never sac multiple harmful Curse engines without a realistic plan for the next upkeep.
- **Threat to homogenization:** the 22 M5 packet rows include Hullbreaker Horror, Vilis, Tidespout Tyrant, Heartless Hidetsugu, Reiterate, Rakdos Joins Up, Bladewing, Second Chance, Mirror-Mad Phantasm—most gain a combo-proxy score by replacing a deck-specific piece with a standalone unrelated package. This is not a natural Lynde optimization.

## 6. Decision-relevant finite/payable game-state comparisons

These are **deterministic hand/board thought experiments with actual payment and timing**, NOT sampled Monte Carlo results, measured win percentages, live opponent games, or spellbook-certified combo completions. One sample each illustrates why a card can dominate narrowly yet still have lower floor.

**A — baseline with self-Curse Misfortunes (turn 5-6 control midgame):** You control Lynde and `Curse of Misfortunes` attached to yourself; pay 4B previously over past turn to cast Misfortunes, not assumed free. At beginning of your next upkeep stack the tutor to resolve before Lynde, fetch `Curse of Vengeance` if unique into play attached to yourself, then Lynde moves that one to an opponent and draws exactly **two**. Misfortunes stays self-attached; next upkeep can find a different Curse. If Haze {2}{U} already attached to you, after first upkeep an additional upkeep repeats: distinct Curse fetch, one move, two more drawn (**four** total across two upkeeps), absent interaction. A Wish-style shortcut can't summon curses that are not in library. **Counterplay:** counter or exile Misfortunes before upkeep, remove Lynde before upkeep, stack-response remove candidate Curse before move resolves; Enchant removal and graveyard exile disrupt continuation. If opponent instead casts Krosan Grip on Haze during earlier main phase, extra upkeep never materializes.

**B — compare Resonator vs Wicked Visitor for single vs multiple self-Curses:** Cost Resonator {2} on your earlier turn; on upkeep need **{2} + tap** to copy one upkeep-triggered ability. With only `Curse of Misfortunes` attached to you and no tutor success, first copy may move it for two cards; original cannot find a second Curse on you and draws nothing. With Misfortunes + Curse of Vengeance attached to you, copied Lynde trigger can move Vengeance and draw two; original could also move Misfortunes and draw two, but you lose future self-Curse tutor. Better copy the `Misfortunes` trigger instead (requires distinct names, enough Curses in deck), get TWO Curses attached to you, then Lynde can move only ONE that upkeep unless extra upkeep exists. This is raw trigger doubling but may **bank a painful Curse on self**. Visitor instead costs {1}{B} and drains on each enchantment entry without further {2} recurring tax. **Result:** Resonator raises ceiling in rich assembled states, lower immediate floor; not automatically a swap.

**C — compare Beseech vs Perilous Research / Visitor:** You control untapped four mana including **BBB** (e.g. Swamp, Badlands, Underground Sea, UBR source), plus an owned Curse on an opponent and Lynde in play. Cast Beseech `{1}{B}{B}{B}`, bargain-sac that **controlled Aura** as additional cost; Lynde triggers on a Curse hitting your GY but delayed return **next end step**. Search and, if bargained, cast `Paradox Haze` MV3 with no mana cost (legally attach to you). Net: exchange a Curse board attachment temporarily for Haze; at next end step that Curse reattaches to you, which at **following** upkeep can move to opponent and draw two. **Counterplay:** counter Beseech after bargain paid; you sacrificed Curse nonetheless; Lynde's trigger already exists and return remains barring exile. Exile the Curse from graveyard before end step to defeat return. **Baseline** instead `Perilous Research {1}{U}`, instant draw two then sac an Aura; enough if U present, no BBB, but does not tutor Haze. `Wicked Visitor` instead simply drains on Aura ETB; cannot fetch. **Result:** Beseech is strong at ideal colored resources but not demonstrated better across average hands than incumbent.

**D — Obeka/Bloodletting damage:** When `Curse of Bloodletting` enchants target opponent and `Obeka, Splitter of Seconds` (2/5 menace) deals its normal 2 combat damage to that opponent unblocked, Bloodletting doubles that actual damage to **4**; Obeka triggers for **4** new upkeep steps after the combat phase. Each new upkeep triggers Lynde, but she draws **zero** unless an eligible Curse is attached to you on resolution. If only one Curse is on you, at most one of the four Lynde triggers moves it absent new supply, for **two cards total**, not eight; Curse of Misfortunes can generate supply if already attached to you, and other triggers may be spent. Opponent blocks Obeka or removes Bloodletting before damage: no doubled outcome. This is potent setup-dependent leverage, not infinite. **No manual self-loop** has been established.

**E — hate and table response:** Opponent deploys `Rest in Peace` ahead of a sacrifice sequence, replacing graveyard entry with exile: Lynde does not trigger, reducing reset outlets to card sacrifice costs. Meanwhile a creature-combo player threatens win t4 and three other players hold up answers: `Withering Torment` at 2B instant removes Rest in Peace/another enchantment (pending protection) or a creature; replacing it with Hullbreaker Horror (7 mana) cannot cast on the timeline. This adversarial case is **decisive** against the source leading M5 proposal. At a stalled combat table with Curse of Thirst already on target opponent, heavier Curse density and safe reset access win on commander mechanics while a generic eight-mana creature risks removal.

**F — no-simulator evidence inflation:** M5's reported `test_pass` for Hullbreaker Horror, Vilis, Hidetsugu, Tidespout and Izzet Signet came from 300-sample exploratory `goldfish_mc`; those rows explicitly say swapped effects were `no effect modelled beyond being a castable card` and that Spellbook near-miss combo matches counted towards win-turn proxy, with **no opponents/combat/politics**. Other 22 rows: 2 `fail`, 13 `inconclusive`, 2 `no_detectable_difference` and 5 `pass`. These are **historical reported tests**, not rerun, not predictive win rates and not adjudicators of Curse upkeep recursion. No effect-aware paired match was run in this review because available simulator doesn't model Lynde's controller/owner/attachment/resolved triggers and manual game states cannot substitute for a statistical experiment. **Next experimental test, if asked:** effect-aware four-player 1,024 matched seed hands per candidate, mulligans, four-turn mana color timing, 2+ Curses attached to self, t4 removal response, RiP/Farewell, and turns-to-lethal when opponent casts 1/2 spells and creature ETBs; preregister exact outcome and effects before claiming upgrade.

## 7. Red-team / strongest counterarguments

**Against KEEP:** Visitor is merely one life per enchantment entering, and Nightscape Familiar discounts the generic portion of only some casts. More copies of Misfortunes and Lynde via Resonator can create explosive cards/board pressure, and Beseech bargain converts a low-impact Curse into Haze while guaranteeing the Curse returns. The baseline's three counterspells are also a relatively thin anti-combo suite by cEDH standards. An effect-aware test might show the additional tutor/copy function consistently outperforms a one-life drain. **This is a legitimate outstanding strategic possibility**, not an assertion of zero potential improvement.

**Against replacing Visitor:** Visitor costs two, adds guaranteed incremental threat on **every enchantment ETB**, including Lynde's delayed return, and doesn't require three black pips or two more mana each upkeep. Swapping it reduces the already slow but actually connected attrition clock. Keeping Nightscape Familiar saves generic on multiple cheap blue/red spells under mana constraints. **Beseech** offers no guarantee of Curse presence, can be countered after bargain-sac paid, and is stranded with BBRU split lands instead of BBB. **Resonator** copying Lynde can force transfer of the core self-enchanted Misfortunes, or copy a tutor to stack self-harm Curses. Losing actual anti-hate removal for a creature-based 7–8 mana generic combo is clearly worse against exile hate and a combo race.

**Adversarial stop criterion:** No source-verified, payable, commander-first and realistic-early-game swap beats a clearly specified incumbent across at least baseline engine value, colored tempo, disruption resilience and natural Curse win clock. Thus **KEEP** is an authored conservative call, *not* a globally proved optimum, not an exhaustive mathematical local-optimality certificate.
