## Commander identity and natural ceiling
Dr. Eggman is **`{2}{U}{B}{R}` 3/6 flying Human Scientist** (five mana, NOT four). At beginning of YOUR end step you **draw one**; THEN each opponent in turn order faces the actual *villainous choice*: opponent may **discard a card** OR you **may put a Construct, Robot, or Vehicle CARD from your HAND directly onto battlefield**. The player may legally pick an option whose action is impossible: empty-handed opponents may choose discard for zero actual cards and prevent a free machine; you can have no eligible cards and opponents can elect the deployment branch with no action. No discard occurred in the empty-hand case, so Caress/Waste Not/Grimoire do not trigger. Opponents decide after seeing previous opponents' choices/deployments.
**Protected identity:** A Sonic Doctor building machines with free high-impact Construct/Robot/Vehicle entries, *actually* taxing discards, repeating end steps/choice triggers, preserving board via artifact redundancy. ETB/artifact-enters abilities are primary: ability **PUTS** and never CASTS, so casting reductions / cast triggers don't automatically gain value. No generic Grixis combo override.
**Natural ceiling:** **Bracket 4 — Optimized**, tags **Villainous Choice / Robots / Artifact Cheat**. Five-mana commander, high-cost artifacts stranded in hand when opponents discard (especially when already empty), delayed end-step timing, only 31 lands, and artifact wipes place real constraints. Very strong only if both branches remain punishing and there is protected access to good targets.

### Mana, role sufficiency and natural fail states
**Land package: 31 copies only.** 3 artifact lands Great Furnace/Seat of the Synod/Vault of Whispers count as both colored sources and artifacts for Opal; Urza's Saga is a **temporary colorless land** sacrificed after chapter III; Inventors' Fair and Academy Ruins are mostly colorless; Ancient Tomb only generic; Spire of Industry requires actual controlled artifact to create UBR. Two basics EACH (Island/Mountain/Swamp), Rainbow City of Brass/Mana Confluence/Command Tower and typed premium duals/shocks help UBR but red/black/blue three pips and later double-blue Y'shtola/Mana Drain cannot be assumed. 31 lands plus rocks can cast commander early with ideal hands, but opening hands without a two-mana fixer or sufficient land drops may strand Eggman and 6–11 mana artifacts. This is the **largest structural exposure**; before claiming bracket speed run 31-vs-32/33-land exact-source mulligan test, but do not cut an approved target automatically.
**Mana and ramp:** Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Fellwar Stone, three Talismans, conditional Mox Opal, Ancient Tomb, Urza's Saga, Foundry Inspector (generic artifact cast reduction ONLY) plus artifact engines. Most high-cost targets are cheat-eligible but Eggman is five MV and opponent can discard instead; absent Eggman pay the high rates. **Card advantage:** Eggman each end step draw, Thoughtcast/Monitor, One with the Machine (needs high MV artifact), Geth's Grimoire (actual discard), rare Thopter Fabricator token on second draw, discard-trigger value in Waste Not/Tinybones. **Removal/interactions** Mana Drain/Fierce/Deflecting/Deadly, Chaos Warp, Withering Torment, Cyclonic Rift, Toxic Deluge, Blasphemous Act, Their Name Is Death, Noxious Gearhulk, Demonic Junker. **Protection** Greaves for nonartifact Eggman, Cryptothrall protects ARTIFACT bodies rather than Eggman, Padeem artifact hexproof (Eggman also not artifact), counters stop sweeper if timely. **Recursion and assembly:** Academy Ruins, Scrap Trawler, Scrapyard Recombiner to HAND, Forgemaster and Transmuter artifact entry, Fabricate hand tutoring.
**Engine density:** Approved prior blueprint counted 28 Eggman-compatible Constructs, Robots and Vehicles. That's a 2026-10-14 sourced typed-card assessment, distinct from my fresh **name/count** parse. Type, not merely being an artifact, qualifies: `Roaming Throne` (Golem) does NOT qualify but doubles triggers when SCIENTIST chosen; `Spark Double` (untyped Eggman clone) cannot itself be Eggman-put. **Specific blind spots:** `Cityscape Leveler` only destroys on CAST or ATTACK not when put during end step; `Cyberdrive Awakener` artifact 4/4 animation lasts only until end of turn, useless for next-turn alpha if entered at our END step; `Depthshaker Titan` ETB makes some 3/3 temporary artifact creatures which are SACRIFICED at next end step, and there is typically no combat left when Eggman puts it; `Coalstoke Gearhulk` resurrects a hasty MV<=4 creature which can be exiled next end step before it ever attacks (Y'shtola additional same-turn end step can accelerate expiry). In contrast **Myr Battlesphere, Noxious Gearhulk, Demonic Junker, Thought Monitor, Oildeep Gearhulk, Combustible Gearhulk** yield immediate ETB value when put in.
**Actual weakest situational slots** in some tables: six-mana Blitzwing is end-step-triggered and unable to trigger on Eggman's very end step if cheated; Cyberdrive Awakener 6MV and temporary animation similarly badly aligns with own-end-step cheat (though 4/4 creatures/anthem valuable if HARDCAST main phase or on following turn); Depthshaker Titan may trade away machines at next end step rather than defend; high-cost Vehicles need crew. **Counterweight:** each is a genuine Robot/Construct/Vehicle with signature mechanical value. Their retention is supported by broad threat density, so a selective cut must improve BOTH actual fork branches without gutting the robots.

## Oracle, legality and rules
1. **Eggman** current Oracle `{2}{U}{B}{R}`, 3/6 flying Scientist, end-step trigger draw then **each opponent** villainous choice. A choice is NOT an "unless" cost; CR 701.55b allows impossible options, e.g. empty discard or no eligible machine. The Valeyard makes a player take each villainous choice **twice** sequentially, and each opponent follows turn order; actual discarded CARDS alone trigger Caress/Geth/Waste Not/Battlecruiser.
2. **Roaming Throne** name Scientist, creates extra **separate Eggman end-step TRIGGER** — another draw and new choice series (may differ). It is a Golem, not hand-deploy eligible via Eggman. **Spark Double** copies Eggman as nonlegendary and has its OWN end-step trigger; no legend rule conflict. **Strionic Resonator** `{2},{T}` copies full on-stack triggered ability; source need not still be on battlefield for copy to resolve. No fresh end step from copying trigger.
3. **Y'shtola Rhul** `{4}{U}{U}` at beginning of first end step exiles TARGET creature you control and returns it under **its OWNER** control; if target illegal as ability resolves, ability fails and NO extra end step. First end-step creates second end step, triggering each eggman that IS present at start of that second step; doesn't create a new main/combat. Blinking an opponent-owned stolen creature returns it to its OWNER rather than to us; target your own Eggman, Y'shtola or another own permanent when safe.
4. **Cityscape Leveler** `{8}` Construct; cast-or-attack destruction, no ETB; putting into battlefield via Eggman skips cast destruction; must WAIT for next declared attack (no haste automatically) to get first destruction. Contrast **Demonic Junker** {6}{B} Vehicle targeted creature destruction WHEN ENTERS, **Noxious Gearhulk** ETB destroy creature and gain life, **Oildeep Gearhulk** ETB forced targeted hand discard then that player draws, **Coalstoke Gearhulk** ETB temporary resurrection. Targeted ETB trigger can be disrupted by target removal and legal payment/resource details.
5. **Depthshaker Titan** 5RR Robot; ETB noncreature artifacts become 3/3 artifact creatures, sacrifice them at beginning NEXT end step; grants other artifact creatures melee/trample/haste continuously. If Eggman puts it DURING first end step, ETB targets create 3/3 bodies but they CANNOT ATTACK this turn (no combat), and they can be sacrificed at start of next actual end step (e.g. Y'shtola's extra end step immediately). Artifact lands animated this way risk SACRIFICE, a severe resource trap. Cyberdrive Awakener ETB animates until EOT instead, cleanup resets before next combat; permanent flying antenna survives.
6. **Coalstoke Gearhulk** 1BBRR when ENTERS puts target MV<=4 creature card from any grave under you with finality counter, grants menace, deathtouch, haste, and exile at beginning of NEXT end step. Cheat it during Eggman's end step means no combat left for that temporary haste creature; Y'shtola extra same-turn step can exile it before you get another turn. **Oildeep Gearhulk** UUBB ETB chosen discard from targeted hand then draw, feeding real discard payoffs and replenishing opponent cards.

Our ordinary draw step = first, Eggman own end step draw = second, creates 1/1 flying Thopter token. But when it enters from Eggman's end-step choice AFTER draw, it did not see the earlier second draw this turn, so it generates no retroactive Thopter. A separate Eggman trigger/extra end step can draw again, but then it may be the third or fourth draw — not "second" unless exact count applies.

Counter spells still require legal target on stack, especially during Eggman end step. Strong but not universally improved.

## Finite sequencing and adversarial scenarios
**A. Core fork empty-hand:** On turn 5 Eggman on battlefield, 3 opponents A,B,C have 0 cards in hand. Own end step draw1, in turn order all three choose discard; each discards NOTHING, no machine put in, no Caress/Waste Not/Grimoire. Any claim three free Constructs guaranteed is false. Even with The Valeyard each can choose impossibility twice.
**B. Full hands, only one threat:** Eggman own end step after drawing, our hand holds Myr Battlesphere and no other eligible card. A chooses deployment, Battlesphere enters and makes four Myr; B,C are free to choose deployment as their decision even though we no longer have an eligible machine, so neither produces one. Alternatively they choose discard real cards feeding Grimoire. Counting three potential puts is incorrect without three eligible hand cards.
**C. Trigger-copy stack:** Before end step control Eggman and Roaming Throne named Scientist. At step begin Eggman ability triggers TWO distinct times. Pay 2,T to Strionic Resonator target one on stack -> third Eggman instance resolves; **THREE draws and up to NINE opponent choices** at 3 opponents (less if effect countered), note later instances can draw fresh eligible cards. If The Valeyard also in play, each actual choice is offered TWICE (18 individual decisions), no guarantee of 18 discards nor 18 machines.
**D. Synthesizer value:** Eggman resolves choice placing Myr Battlesphere from hand under you. Battlesphere ETB creates 4 Myr. Simulacrum Synthesizer sees nontoken artifact mana value7 enter and makes artifact-count-scaled Construct. If Ultron also on board, its nontoken entry ability may pay2 create TOKEN copy of Battlesphere, token copy ETBs to create another 4 Myr; token Battlesphere does NOT itself retrigger Ultron. Need actual 2 untapped mana available at own end step; opp can remove Synthesizer/Ultron before choice.
**E. Cityscape versus Junker:** Opponent has an immediate must-destroy attacker. Eggman choice puts Leveler 8/8 onto battlefield at end step, **no destruction** because not cast, summoning sickness prevents immediate attack until next turn absent haste. If instead put Demonic Junker Vehicle from hand, its ETB can destroy up to one target creature from EACH player's board now; it can also include a friendly target if chosen, and crew will be needed later to attack. Legal targets/removal responses still matter.
**F. Y'shtola removal/gains:** At beginning of first end step Eggman and Y'shtola trigger; Y'shtola targets our Eggman, blink resolves -> new Eggman object under our control, extra end step scheduled. Old Eggman end-step trigger that was already on stack still resolves even though source left. At beginning second end step new Eggman triggers and draws/choices again; Y'shtola also triggers there (but no third end step because NOT first), still must target. Opponent removes Y'shtola's targeted creature in response to FIRST trigger -> trigger fizzles entirely, NO extra end step. Note Y'shtola ability returning stolen creature under original OWNER is a nonbo.
**G. Temporary animation:** Eggman ends step put Cyberdrive Awakener, ETB animates current nontoken artifacts into 4/4 until EOT; no subsequent combat on ordinary turn so effect expires before they swing, though flying anthem for artifact creatures persists. Putting Depthshaker during end step animates noncreature artifact lands into 3/3, with sacrifice at next end step; with Y'shtola extra end step you sacrifice those lands SAME TURN, a serious poor line. Avoid choosing artifact lands as ETB targets.
**H. Mox hand and early commander:** Hand with Academy Ruins, Ancient Tomb, Inventors' Fair, Sol Ring, Mox Opal, Eggman and eight-drop has large generic mana, but no UBR: Opal not active absent three artifacts, Eggman NOT castable until blue, black, red actually present. A single untapped dual in later draw may still not supply all pips. Never count conditional Opal as 0MV guaranteed rainbow.
**I. Challengers:** Scrawling Crawler put under Eggman during end step (if opponent chooses) has no this-turn upkeep trigger; on next our upkeep it makes all draw one and opponent each lose1 from drawing, so each will have at least one card to elect discard at next Eggman choice unless they already spent it. Opponents get free cards and may remove our engine before step. Torrential Gearhulk put in end step can target and cast an existing Chaos Warp instant in grave free if legal target, but Mana Drain grave with EMPTY stack of spells cannot be cast. Contrast preexisting Coalstoke: even if it revives 2MV creature with haste after end step, it cannot attack until next turn if delayed exile allows.

## Conclusions and unresolved evidence
**Final original-author recommendation: KEEP exact approved locked 100 pending further evidence.** This is a substantive conditional hold, not a rank-one "no upgrades" assertion. Most credible *real* experiments: `Scrawling Crawler` (eligible cheap Construct, fixes empty-hand fork at cost of feeding opponents), `Torrential Gearhulk` (strong ETB instant-from-GY, but own-end-step timing limits instant targets), `Imposter Mech` (cheap eligible opponent ETB-copy but crew burden) and selective 32nd land (31 is low and five-mana commander). Each has at least two serious alternative cuts and preserves the machine-first identity. Do not auto-apply.
**Strongest counterargument:** In multiplayer, a 3-mana Scrawling Crawler can nearly guarantee cards in opponents' hands at next own end step and chip life, making discard-or-put more punishing than six-mana Blitzwing whose damage ability misses initial Eggman-cheated end step. However Crawler draws EACH opponent a free card on your upkeep and could provide removal/combos; Eggman opponents can still choose deployment while you have no legal target, and Crawler's own 3/2 is fragile. Need actual game-evidence rather than theme intuition. `Depthshaker` and `Cyberdrive` are particularly poor as **end-step cheat** payoffs but remain strong if hardcast precombat or already controlled, so not automatic cuts.
