## Commander identity and natural ceiling
**Gwendlyn Di Corci** costs `{U}{B}{B}{R}` = **4 MV** (not generic-red-black-blue one-per-color), 3/5 legendary Human Rogue. **{T}: TARGET PLAYER discards ONE card at RANDOM. Activate only DURING YOUR TURN.** Ability has tap symbol, so unless haste/pseudo-haste present, newly cast commander cannot activate; untapping Gwendlyn and adding blue via Pemmin's Aura produce repeated random discards in *one own turn*. It cannot be activated on an opponent's turn even if untapped or given flash. If targeted player has no hand, ability cannot cause actual discard and gives no "discarded a card" triggers; priority/wasteful activation is not a substitute.
**Commander-first engine:** Bracers duplicates activation without extra tap and may retarget a second player; Stone/Seal/Minamo/Elixir/Pemmin provide untaps, Lightning Greaves/Elixir prompt activation; random discard forces inconvenient cards instead of choice. Wheels then REFILL hands for Gwendlyn to empty again; hand-discard, draw/life punishment, Chains/Notion/Agent disruption and opponent permanent capture via Tergrid. The compact Underworld Breach/LED/Wheel loop is BACKUP, not generic cEDH plan to replace commander.
**Natural ceiling:** **Bracket 4 — Optimized**, tags centered on Random Discard / Wheel Control / Activated Abilities; cannot realistically claim bracket5 cEDH for a one-card-per-tap four-mana commander without turbo homogenization. Weaknesses: soft lock symmetric effects; wheel opponents a fresh seven cards (they may untap/cast), one target at a time vs 3 opponents, creature removal/tax, few haste setups, hands quickly empty so payoffs go dead without deliberate reloading.

### Mana/ramp/curve, card economy and defensive structure
**35 lands** 29 names, dual/fetch/pain/UBR fixing alongside 5 Swamps, 2 Islands, 2 Mountains; additional basic Swamps support BBB Underworld Dreams and other black punishers. Ancient Tomb helps 4MV total but makes C only, Gwendlyn needs **U B B R**, so cannot assume early general just from Tomb/Sol Ring. Crumbling Necropolis, Xander's Lounge and Bojuka Bog enter tapped; conditional Drowned Catacomb and opposing-colored Orchard. Minamo U source with `{U},T` untap legendary commander is exceptional native utility but can only untap when Gwendlyn is legal target (own Pemmin shroud turns off Minamo until end-turn). 2MV Signet, three other rock sources, rituals/Petal/LED/Mox funds tempo but 35 land limits mana flood; no controlled turn4 casting % measured.
**Speed/activation**: Gwendlyn tapped only on own turn, first activation if eligible. Illusionist's Bracers 2MV+equip3 (5 initial mana), copy on each activation; Pemmin's Aura {1}{U}{U} + U each untap, shroud mode can block friendly untaps; Minamo activated U tap; Magewright Stone and Patriar Seal require 1 mana tap, Elixir 3 mana pseudo-haste and separate activated untap. Equip/tap protection mana competes with wheeling and interaction. Not all untappers give unlimited activation without pay/resource.
**Draw/discard scaling**: Geth's Grimoire draws EACH opponent card discarded; Tinybones Pocket Nuisance pings once per PLAYER's one-or-more discard EVENT (including your own), Liliana's Caress pings each discarded CARD by opponent, Waste Not outputs by discarded CARD TYPE; Tinybones Trinket Thief draws if at least one opponent discarded on previous turn, Raven Man end-step creates at most one Bird from qualifying discarded activity. `Bloodchief Ascension` tracks cards going to opponent GY once active, so Dauthi's grave exile interferes with that as well as Tergrid. Several draw-denial effects paradoxically prevent your own draw-based Sheoldred/Underworld Dreams/O-Bowmasters punish triggers when opponent card draws replaced.
**Interaction:** Offer, Force/Force of Negation, Mana Drain, Swan, Fierce, Swat, Cyclonic Rift, Withering Torment, Deluge, Opposition Agent/Dauthi; strong against stack/search and cards but removing wheel or cheap counters for one more untap may increase opposing win turns. Bracket4 shell good at long-game resource denial, but high-density 3–5MV stax permanents can die to sweeper.
**Ending games:** commander's repeated random discard + Caress/Waste Not/Tinybones/Trinket, Tergrid theft if permanents in grave, ongoing Sheoldred/Underworld Dreams/Bowmasters damage when draws happen, Bloodchief active grave triggers, Oppression/Chains/Pit hellbent lock with any pressure, optional Wheel+LED+Breach finite library-bound line. Windfall/Timetwister can refill enemy hands, so sequencing Notion Thief/Chains can be vital but also conflicts with chosen payoffs.

## Oracle, legality and rules
1. **Gwendlyn** {U}{B}{B}{R} 3/5; {T} target PLAYER one RANDOM card discard; activation ONLY during YOUR turn even with vigilance/haste/untap. Normal creature tap sickness applies unless Greaves or Thousand-Year Elixir pseudo haste. [Gwendlyn](https://mtg.wtf/card/leg/228/Gwendlyn-Di-Corci).

Bracers must be equipped (equip3 sorcery timing); moving onto Alchemist means Gwendlyn ability no longer copied, but Alchemist copied untap can target Gwendlyn and Alchemist to create repetition. [Bracers](https://mtg.wtf/card/rvr/260/Illusionists-Bracers), [Aphetto](https://mtg.wtf/card/ons/64/Aphetto-Alchemist). Do not double untap cost assumptions.
3. **Aphetto loop exact**: with READY Alchemist+Bracers equipped to him, Gwendlyn ready/usable on your own turn, tap Alchemist to untap Gwendlyn, Bracers creates copy choosing Alchemist as other target, both resolve, Alchemist may be tapped again, and Gwendlyn can tap between cycles. Each iteration target opponent may discard RANDOM card if any. Opponent can respond to Alchemist targeting before resolve; moving Bracers from Gwendlyn cost equip3 and leaves Gwendlyn without copy. More Alchemist untaps than enemy hand cards DO NOT produce extra discarded-card triggers.
4. **Pemmin shroud**: blue mana per untap; another blue activation gives enchanted Gwendlyn shroud for turn, preventing spells/abilities (including YOUR Minamo/Seal/Stone) from targeting her thereafter. If already attached before shroud, Pemmin remains; its intrinsic untap ability can still operate on enchanted creature because doesn't TARGET.
5. **Pocket Nuisance** triggers when ANY PLAYER discards one-or-more cards as single discard event and deals **ONE damage to each opponent per event**, including on your own discard. For a single Wheel with four players each discarding nonempty hands, four separate discard events may trigger once per player; it does NOT trigger 7 times because someone discarded 7 cards. `Liliana's Caress` trigger EACH individual card discarded by opponent; Geth Grimoire for each opponent card, even when grave replacement exiles. [Pocket Nuisance](https://magicmeta.net/cards/tinybones-pocket-nuisance).
6. **Dauthi/Tergrid anti-synergy**: when opponent discards permanent, Dauthi replacement EXILES with void counter instead of grave; discard still occurred for Waste Not/Caress/Grimoire, but `Tergrid` may NOT put that exiled card onto battlefield because its oracle requires from a GRAVEYARD. A physical grave card re-entering GY later isn't same zone object for pending trigger. Likewise Ascension grave-card payoff may be suppressed. [Dauthi rulings](https://mauldron.com/cards/f1c2dbe2-fbe0-4058-bdf1-91d1b1832786), [Tergrid](https://mtg.wtf/card/khm/307a/Tergrid-God-of-Fright).
7. **Chains/Notion replacement**: Chains replaces DRAW other than first normal draw-step draw with discard one then draw, or mill one if can't discard (subject chain-replacement wording); Notion Thief replaces opponent draw outside first draw-step draw with draw by you instead. Multiple replacement effects on the same event require correct affected-player choice and event sequence; NEVER assert "Wheel lets me draw all twenty-one while Chains always makes opponents discard" without determining order. Notion moving draws from opponents may also reduce Bowmasters/Underworld Dreams/Sheoldred direct opponent-draw punishment. [MTG judge discussion](https://apps.magicjudges.org/forum/topic/53388/).
8. **Timetwister vs wheel**: Timetwister SHUFFLES hands/GYs to libraries then draws7; cards shuffled away are NOT DISCARDed and therefore don't trigger Liliana/Caress/Waste Not/Grimoire, though draw effects can punish if draws happen. Wheel of Fortune DISCARDs actual hands then draws7 and supports discard triggers; Windfall discards/draws contingent max hand size; Dark Deal draws one fewer, Wheel of Misfortune chooses numbers and wheel participation, not guaranteed universal discard.
9. **Breach/LED/Wheel**: LED sacrifice+entire hand discard yields three of ONE color, escape LED costs exile three other GY cards, Wheel 2R similarly escape exile3 other. Prerequisite adequate cards in GY/hand and resolving Wheel; after wheel fully resolves may cast escaped LED at sorcery legal timing, cannot cast it DURING Wheel resolution. Repeated wheels eventually deck out without a win outlet or shuffle; grave hate/Chains/Notion/Dauthi and own Opposition can change. Source Spellbook combo `1368-2706-3518`, not guaranteed from zero grave.
10. **Bottomless Pit and Oppression** are symmetric, can force controller discard during own combo. `Underworld Dreams` and Bowmasters see actual card DRAW, not just attempted draw replaced by Chains/Thief, and no extra value after empty opponent hand except refill wheels.
11. **Official legality**: canonical Oct14 SHA gate 100/94 UBR legal PASS, zero exceptions; no future/proxy exception. New candidates Aphetto, Bracers, Freed are UBR/colorless legal from current Oracle; any hypothetical new 100-card version needs new exact-SHA gate plus owner approval.

## Conclusions and unresolved evidence
**KEEP approved locked list unchanged** pending targeted real evidence. The most credible new commander-first improvement is **Aphetto Alchemist** when wearing existing Illusionist's Bracers, for repeatable two-way untapping, a synergy underweighted by old "summoning sickness" argument. It is better as raw discard throughput but setup is 2MV Alchemist + 2MV Bracers + equip3 + Gwendlyn4; removes Notion Thief/Agent/Grimoire/Stone value and is fragile. In optimized B4 tables the wheels/draw-denial layer could outperform Alchemist in important games. Also **Dauthi Voidwalker vs Tergrid** is real conflict of two otherwise strong cards; sequencing them or prioritizing one versus another can matter, but doesn't automatically warrant a cut. Symmetric Oppression/Chains and draw-denial/Sheoldred tension are another tactical constraint.
**Strongest dissent:** The commander's defining random-discard ability is low-rate unless repeatable; Alchemist+Bracers likely generates higher identity-central throughput than one additional 4MV draw-value card. But once opponents are hellbent, activation does nothing and an Alchemist board without Wheel or Notion can lose to opponent topdeck. Evidence currently insufficient for source-verified favorable swap. Preserve B4 ceiling and ALL 62 canonical approvals.
**Unresolved:** no new 94-name Scryfall full sweep, no measured head-to-head multiplayer sessions, independent GrokBot Pass C pending. No canonical modifications, project exceptions, milestone acceptance or owner approval altered; M6.4 historical 62/62 remains separate.
