## Commander's protected identity and actual natural ceiling

The 100 is an intentional high-ceiling **artifact-cast, self-counter-recovery, prison and recursion** deck. Mishra's same-name artifact cast trigger ordinarily finds nothing in singleton while the original spell is on stack; self-counter triggers (`Blood Funnel`, `Nether Void`, `Chalice`, `Void Mirror`, `Vexing Bauble`, `Planar Chaos`, `Nullstone Gargoyle`) can put the original into graveyard *before Mishra searches*, allowing it onto battlefield. `Possibility Storm` can tuck the original from a hand cast onto library bottom and Mishra can recover it. The power comes from sequencing symmetric restrictions asymmetrically while assembling Top/Forge/Reservoir, KCI/Retriever/Trawler and Karn/Lattice closures.

Natural ceiling remains the historically documented **Bracket 5 — fringe commander-centric artifact stax/combo**, a strategic classification rather than newly measured competitive placement. It is fast and oppressive with its right pieces, but is dependent on a four-mana UBR commander, colored fixing with only 29 lands, selective counters, and a high-priority opponent removal response window. Do **not** transform it into generic Grixis consultation/turbo combo simply because those packages have higher average speed in other shells.

## Actual response windows and adverse games

- **Before recovery:** cast an artifact and put the owned self-counter trigger above Mishra's, but opponent may remove Mishra before cast, counter the trigger, exile the original artifact from the graveyard between trigger resolutions, or wait until Mishra's trigger has resolved to counter an uncountered original spell. Mishra's ability does not retrieve a card from exile or give protection without a triggered ability waiting.
- **Against artifact hate:** artifact lands, fast rocks, Forge, KCI and mana engines share a vulnerability to sweepers or shutoffs. A cheap Petal helps first development but does not increase resilience to a later mass artifact answer. Engineer/Welder resurrection of nine-mana Gargoyle is vulnerable to graveyard exile, tapped/sick Welder and target removal.
- **Against stack combo:** Planar Chaos and Nullstone may counter an opponent's crucial spell, but can also block the pilot's Force effects; Bauble/Mirror turn zero-mana protection and newly suggested Scepter copies into liabilities. Piloting the prison in the wrong order or without Mishra changes the actual game outcome. An early protection cast is often more consequential than one artifact cast.
- **Under failed commander access:** `Badlands` + `Island` + `Ancient Tomb` gives colors U plus B *or* R and sufficient generic, but not all UBR concurrently. Petal supplies the missing color and makes turn-three Mishra payable in that exact hand. Replacing Gargoyle loses a possible cheated first-spell-lock later. These are **manual reproducible examples**, not statistically representative results.
