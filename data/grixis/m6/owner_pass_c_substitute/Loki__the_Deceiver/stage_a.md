## Commander-first identity

Loki triggers **when declared as an attacker**, creating a **tapped-and-attacking** Illusion copy of **another target Villain you control**, except the copy is not legendary; **sacrifice that specific token at the beginning of the next end step**. Its second ability draws a card whenever one or more of your Villains deal combat damage to a player: the trigger is per **player hit**, not per individual Villain hitting that same player. Loki is four mana, not hasty on its own. The deck is therefore not generic Grixis control/clone goodstuff: it is **Villain typal combat, targetable copy value and delayed-token preservation**.

The exact 100 provides many intrinsic Villains (Baron Strucker, Black Cat, Carnage, Chameleon, Doctor Doom variants, Doctor Octopus, Kang variants, Mysterio, Stilt-Man, Ultron and others) alongside tribal support (Kindred Discovery, Patchwork Banner, Urza's Incubator, Cover of Darkness, Path of Ancestry, Roaming Throne). The existing **The Master, Multiplied** and **Sundial of the Infinite** directly answer Loki's end-step sacrifice clause; one is a six-mana creature vulnerable to removal, the other costs mana/activation timing and consumes the turn after the end-step trigger is on the stack. Lightning Greaves/Swiftfoot Boots accelerate a safe Loki attack, while Cover of Darkness helps Villains connect and draw. 34 lands plus Sol Ring, Signet, 3 Talismans, Dark Ritual, Soul Stone and several typal cost/restricted sources support a midcurve creature-combat game.

The natural optimized ceiling is tentatively **Bracket 4 (optimized combat/copy midrange)**, not a demonstrated B5 tournament ceiling. This is a provisional commander-identity judgment, not a reason to append generic Breach/Thoracle loops.

## Critical trigger/copy/sequencing audit

1. **Declared attacking versus entering attacking:** Loki's new token was not declared as attacker. Its own `whenever this attacks` or copied creature's *attack* trigger does **not** trigger on that entry, although copied `when this enters` triggers do. Source: WotC Marvel Loki ruling and WotC Doctor Who myriad rulings. E.g. a Loki token copied from **Kang Prime** may trigger Kang's *enters* mode, **not** another *attacks* mode. Similarly, copying **Crimson Cowl** or an attack-only payoff is not equivalent to declaring another attacker. No recursive automatic Loki-trigger chain is present.
2. **Token lifetime:** The copies normally die to the delayed end-step *sacrifice*, even if tapped and attacking. **The Master, Multiplied** says triggered abilities you control cannot force sacrifices/exile of your creature tokens, so its presence as this delayed trigger resolves preserves them. **Sundial of the Infinite** must end the turn **while the sacrifice trigger is on the stack**, during your own end step; ending the turn before that trigger occurs merely moves the next-end-step deadline to a later one. With either save absent, the token goes away.
3. **Token recursion:** `Thunderbolts Conspiracy` returns a dead Villain card/permanent with a finality counter, but a dead **creature token** ceases to exist before an ordinary return-from-graveyard effect can recover it. It is not another permanent token-retention engine; it helps nontoken Villains survive one death.
4. **Valuable copies:** `Titan of Littjara` (choose Villain as it enters and draw for other creatures of matching type when it enters/attacks) can generate an **ETB draw** from Loki's copied Titan, not an attack-trigger draw. `Tombstone, Career Criminal` has an ETB Villain recursion ability that does trigger in a copied token; copying Stilt-Man can multiply combat-damage theft triggers *if copies connect*. `Kindred Discovery` naming Villain triggers for an arriving Villain token, but does not get an attack trigger from that token's entering attacking. ETB resolution, removal and target legality matter.
5. **Loki's Scepter** can steal an enemy creature and make it a Villain *until end of turn*, providing a temporary legal Loki-copy target precombat. But creature-type added by this effect is **not copied** as a copiable characteristic: if the stolen creature was not originally a Villain, its Illusion copy does not automatically remain a Villain. That impacts Kindred Discovery and Loki's combat-draw condition. The token is still nonlegendary and an Illusion.
6. **Legends:** Loki's attacking copy specifically is nonlegendary, so copying a legendary Villain does not itself trigger the legend rule. `Spark Double` can also copy nonlegendary while enhancing stats. `Hall of Echoes` is a *land* that can spend {5} to become a creature copy until end of turn; it grants temporary legend-rule exemption, but is colorless land utility, not guaranteed early UBR mana. If it becomes Loki and was controlled since turn start, attacking may be possible depending on current creature status and timing; do not assume an untapped land that entered this turn may attack.

## Mana, vulnerability and weakest conditional slots

34 lands and 8 basics materially reduce one-land openers versus low-land cEDH lists, but colored access still depends on untapped U/B/R; `Villainous Hideout` produces unrestricted {C} but its colored mana is restricted to **Villain spells/abilities**, not generic counterspells or Loki's equip costs. `Cavern of Souls` named Villain improves creature casting but not blue interaction. `Oscorp Industries` enters tapped, `Hall of Echoes` colorless, and 4-mana Loki needs to survive a combat turn before making its own token.

**Conditional slots:** (a) `Sundial of the Infinite` is a narrow token-saver/turn-shortener that may be dead if Loki cannot attack; retaining it is warranted when The Master costs six and is removable. (b) `Swiftfoot Boots` is the second protective footwear alongside Greaves; haste/hexproof redundancy is vital to four-mana attack commander but may compete with dedicated copy-output. (c) `Hall of Echoes` supplies colorless mana and only copies at five, while `Villainous Hideout` has colored-use restrictions; replacing a utility land may compromise land count/fixing. (d) Four- to six-mana board value pieces such as `Kindred Discovery`, `Titan of Littjara` and The Master are expensive, yet directly magnify Loki's Villain identity, so dismissing them as generic curve cuts would be shallow.

**Opponent counterplay:** instant removal of Loki before declare attackers (no trigger), removal of target Villain in response to attack trigger (no valid copy on resolution), removal of The Master before sacrifice resolves, removal/ability counter of Sundial, Fog/combat tricks shutting off damage draw, and exile/sweepers resetting token army. `Cover of Darkness` grants fear but does not create trample or protect against board clears. Anti-tribal removal can cut multiple engines at once.

## Adversarial challenger and strongest rebuttal

**Provisional candidate to challenge:** **+ Strionic Resonator / − Swiftfoot Boots**, not endorsed as an automatic swap. Strionic Resonator {2}, {T} for {2} copies one triggered ability you control, including Loki's *already triggered* attack-copy ability or combat-damage draw; its copied trigger may choose a new legal Villain target. Against slow midrange tables, a second independently chosen attacking Illusion (or extra cards on connection) may scale commander-specific value. The cut tests whether keeping Lightning Greaves, Deflecting Swat and other protection makes **two sets of boots** redundant.

**Strongest counterargument:** Resonator must be drawn and deployed, use two mana **during combat after Loki's attack trigger**, survive artifact removal and have a targetable Villain. Meanwhile losing Swiftfoot Boots lowers the chances of a protected or haste-enabled Loki attack if Greaves is absent, and Resonator adds no immediate bodies without Loki + another Villain. Removing the cut card may worsen every game where Loki is attacked/countered before triggering. In tempo-sensitive pods this could be an unmistakable downgrade. More directly, **Roaming Throne already doubles appropriate Loki triggers** after choosing a shared creature type, giving redundancy in the approved list.

**Competing cards/cuts (unranked research, not decisions):** `Shared Animosity` (Villain combat finishing) vs a secondary protection or slow generic utility slot; `Helm of the Host` (another nonlegendary Loki copy before declaring attackers, but substantial cast/equip cost); retaining both protection pieces and just accepting fewer triggers may be best. Alternative cuts Sundial or an expensive top-end Villain should be compared only with specific play-pattern evidence; Sundial is the deck's second independent delayed-token preservation path and should not be casually treated as a dead card.

**Provisional disposition: NEEDS TESTING**, not PROPOSE UPGRADE: the commander-specific Resonator/Boots swap is plausible but not proven, and material **copy-lifetime / timing interactions** deserve effect-aware rules traces.
