## Commander-first finding and verified interaction details

**Bracket 4 — Optimized**, matching the source's natural, commander-dependent ceiling. Obeka's real combat damage to a **player** creates that many postcombat upkeep-only beginning phases (not whole turns, no free untap/draw). This list converts her successful connections into extra upkeep triggers across six monarch Courts, suspend/time counters, Rings, Smaug/Journal/Network artifact streams, Reactor charge progress, and Sieve/Mechanized Production alternate finishes. It also invests in menace-compatible evasion and protection, haste, combat damage modifiers and triggered-ability multiplication. Substituting generic cEDH shells would undermine this carefully individual strategy.

Material rules receipts: Wizards [OTJ release notes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/outlaws-of-thunder-junction-release-notes) and [update bulletin](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/outlaws-of-thunder-junction-update-bulletin) for extra-phase insertion; [Marvel release notes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/marvel-super-heroes-release-notes) for Mjölnir; [Hobbit release notes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/the-hobbit-release-notes) and [Wizard's Staff Oracle](https://mtg.wtf/card/hob/59/Wizards-Staff) for additional triggered instances; [Wilds of Eldraine notes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/wilds-of-eldraine-release-notes) for Court conditional modes; [Manufactor Oracle](https://mtg.wtf/card/mh2/219/Academy-Manufactor); [Rising Oracle](https://mtg.wtf/card/ltr/145/Rising-of-the-Day). If Obeka at power 4 deals 4 player combat damage, one trigger grants four extra upkeeps; Mjölnir doubling actual damage makes eight upkeeps from the event, and if both Staff and appropriate Roaming Throne apply, **three** instances of that eight-upkeep trigger create 24 additional steps. The two extra-trigger effects do not by themselves multiply 2×2. Embercleave's double strike generates distinct damage events if Obeka connects in both steps. Paradox Haze triggers once for the enchanted player's **first** upkeep, not for each Obeka-created upkeep. As Foretold is once per **turn**, not once per upkeep.

A specific earlier advisory wording needs correction: the presence of **monarch is not required for a Court's beginning-of-upkeep ability to trigger**. Some outcomes differ if monarch is held on resolution. This distinction changes opponent interaction analysis; the prior reconciliation's overall strategic result survives that narrower correction.

Opponent counterplay and critical windows:
- Before damage, spot removal, fogs, prevention or legal two-creature menace blocks stop Obeka triggers; haste by itself does not.
- After player damage and trigger creation, removing Obeka does not erase her already-on-stack trigger; countering the trigger or removing payoff permanents before subsequent upkeep steps can reduce conversion.
- With multiple monarch Courts, table combat can steal monarchy between turns, dropping Ire/Locthwain/Vantress value even if extra upkeeps still arrive.
- Time Sieve consumes five concrete artifacts and taps once; created extra **upkeeps do not untap it**. A claimed repeatable turn chain must show an actual next turn or other untap source plus adequate new tokens.
- Hatred is paid for with life before the hit; counterspell/removal still costs the life and does not buy extra upkeeps. Heavy Phyrexian Arena/Super Intelligence draw in many upkeeps may exhaust life/deck resources.
- Creature/equipment removal, enchantment/exile effects and commander tax challenge the wide engine. Indestructible Reactor can still be exiled/bounced.
