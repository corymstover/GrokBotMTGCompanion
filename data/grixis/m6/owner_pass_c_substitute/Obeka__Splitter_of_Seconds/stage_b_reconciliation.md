## What Stage A genuinely challenged

The separately sealed Stage A identified **+Rising of the Day / -Darksteel Reactor**, alternate cut **Jhoira of the Ghitu**, as a *new, untested* effort to secure earlier 3-power Obeka contact. Neither the exact card **Rising of the Day** nor this pair occurs in the historical authored review or reconciliation. That matters because the historic verdicts addressed different questions: reject Passage for Swamp, and investigate Manufactor for Haze. A valid rejection of Rogue's Passage is **not** an analysis or dismissal of Rising. Conversely, original author did conduct exhaustive card-role analysis and found unusually strong Reactor/Jhoira fit, so the fresh proposal has a substantial burden.

**A fair fixed-hand setup challenge (illustrative, not a played/tested game)**:
- On turn three, three mana can cast Rising; four mana on turn four can cast Obeka with haste. If unblocked, Rising boosts Obeka from power 2→3, adding three postcombat upkeeps already on turn four. But if no upkeep-triggered permanents or suspended cards have been installed by then, those upkeeps yield **almost nothing**: the commander itself has no automatic draw/untap. Rising competes directly with an early Court of Cunning, Paradox Haze, As Foretold, Jhoira, or a mana/defense setup at that same curve position.
- If turn two Creeping Bloodsucker (or suspended card) already exists, turn-three Rising/turn-four Obeka instead triggers three real extra drain/progression cycles, raising the benefit. Damage may be blocked or prevented despite haste, so this is conditional.
- Reactor occupies a different four-mana role: it gains counters at all ordinary upkeeps and can convert bursts of N Obeka upkeeps into an alternate win at 20 counters. It is indestructible but not unanswerable, and requires significant setup/survival. Removing it trades inevitability for an earlier, more vulnerable contact attempt. Jhoira likewise is a real four-counter suspend outlet, not just a generic weak 3-drop. These costs prevent automatic upgrade classification.

**Rising verification:** current Oracle display `{2}{R}`, red enchantment, “Creatures you control have haste. Legendary creatures you control get +1/+0”; officially Commander-legal. https://mtg.wtf/card/ltr/145/Rising-of-the-Day. Specific timing interactions are straightforward but the **net winning impact is not measured**. This proposal must be compared not only to Reactor/Jhoira, but to an **unchanged baseline** on hands already containing Arena of Glory, Greaves, Swiftfoot Boots, or Ring of Valkas; cost/payoff overlap strongly suppresses its marginal value.

1. **Full agreement on engine identity and card math.** Original review independently supports Stage A's 35 lands / 100 quantities / 96 distinct names, exact SHA, seven basic copies, 2/5 menace Obeka, extra beginning phases without untap/draw, Court/upkeep/suspend artifact package, and Bracket 4 — Optimized natural ceiling. 2. **Reject exact GrokBot land swap for grounded reasons.** Rogue's Passage pays `{4},{T}`, effectively requiring Passage plus four additional mana on the combat turn, adds no U/B/R source, and the cut Swamp lowers an already two-copy basic Swamp count to one. The approved list has multiple access modes Aqueous Form, Brotherhood Regalia, Thassa `{1}{U}` unblockable, Shizo fear and Silent Arbiter+menace. This creates substantial redundant evasion for a colored-source cost. The caveat is a *slow, mana-rich pod* after all other evasion is removed; not proof of baseline upgrade. Keep original Swamp and the 35-land structure.
3. **Unresolved distinct former author proposal.** Academy Manufactor is a `{3}` 1/3 replacement effect: EACH time Journal would produce a Clue or Smaug a Treasure, create one each Clue/Food/Treasure instead. With both generators active that is **six artifacts per upkeep**; one alone yields three. This can power Time Sieve with five artifacts while preserving extra material and can accelerate Mechanized Production's same-name win threshold. But losing Paradox Haze sacrifices a relatively resilient extra upkeep **even without Obeka**; Manufactor is a fragile 1/3 that fails without another token source. The combo is a *conditional multi-card finite engine*, not a guaranteed infinite turn or proven upgrade. Reconciliation explicitly left it awaiting testing. First-party original review enumerates 24 paid scenarios, which establish mechanics but **not** frequencies/relative outcomes.
4. **Specific GrokBot wording mistake:** advisory says “Monarch Court triggers need you to be monarch at the time (fact).” This is incorrect if meant literally: the Courts' **beginning-of-upkeep triggers occur regardless of who is monarch**; their *stronger outcomes* may conditionally check that status, often on resolution. For example Court of Locthwain still exiles a card even without monarchy, and Court of Vantress then provides a distinct non-monarch mode. Retain exact WotC wording, https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/wilds-of-eldraine-release-notes. The reconciliation elsewhere accurately describes the weaker versus stronger modes. This correction doesn't itself require a swap.
