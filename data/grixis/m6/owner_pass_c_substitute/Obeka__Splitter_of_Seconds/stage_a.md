## Commander-specific identity and natural ceiling

Obeka is a {1}{U}{B}{R} 2/5 legendary Ogre Warlock with menace. Each time she **deals combat damage to a player**, her trigger adds **that many individual upkeep steps** after the combat phase. Each extra upkeep is in a new beginning phase with untap/draw skipped; it is not an extra turn and does not grant fresh land plays or main-phase resources. The commander rewards: (a) reliably connecting, (b) increasing *actual* combat damage, (c) installing upkeep-triggered payoffs before combat, then (d) protecting a repeatable engine and the monarch when Courts are involved. Its natural high ceiling is an explosive optimized combat/upkeep-combo shell; calling it a deterministic cEDH win-rate deck is not justified by these inputs.

The approved 100 is unusually coherent rather than generic spellslinger. Combat access, scaling damage, and triggered-ability multipliers feed **six Courts** (Ambition, Cunning, Embereth, Ire, Locthwain, Vantress), Phyrexian Arena, Creeping Bloodsucker, Bonehoard Dracosaur, Twilight Prophet, Dragonmaster Outcast, Smaug the Magnificent, Tamiyo's Journal, Thopter Spy Network, Replicating Ring and Darksteel Reactor. Suspend/time tools (Ancestral Vision, Profane Tutor, Inspiring/Rousing Refrain, Suspended Sentence, Jhoira of the Ghitu, As Foretold) benefit from additional upkeeps. Smaug/Journal/Network produce artifacts toward Time Sieve; Mechanized Production and Court of Vantress present alternative artifact-copy win paths. Courts require keeping/reclaiming **monarch** for several top-end modes, a different dependency from simply retaining Obeka.

## Mana, pressure and real dependencies

**35 lands** includes seven basics, nine fetchlands, multiple unrestricted three-color/five-color and U/B/R dual sources, plus Ancient Tomb. The four-mana commander needs all U/B/R by deployment; U/B/R are heavily supported by fetchable dual/shock/triome targets, but Ancient Tomb itself supplies no colors, several Courts demand double colored mana, and mana-fixing artifacts are still exposure to artifact hate. The recurring two-to-four-mana setup cards compete with casting Obeka on turn four. The seven rocks include Replicating Ring, which is slow before it receives enough night counters. This is a **structural** assessment, not an independently simulated color-source percentage or measured opening-hand rate.

There is an inherent **combat latency**: Obeka entering without haste cannot connect immediately; she can be removed before declaring attackers, blocked by two or more creatures despite menace, neutralized by pillow-fort taxes/fogs, or intercepted before combat damage. Existing counterweights include Lightning Greaves, Swiftfoot Boots, Ring of Valkas, Arena of Glory, Shizo, Aqueous Form, Thassa, Brotherhood Regalia, Silent Arbiter, and protective equipment. Silent Arbiter's one-blocker limit makes menace effectively un-blockable when Obeka is the one permitted attacker, absent effects that ignore/replace blocking legality. Damage multipliers require both connection and timing; Hatred paid from life is especially vulnerable to instant-speed removal before damage.

## Rules and interaction traces — verified versus conditional

1. **Extra-upkeep insertion**: the first-party *Outlaws of Thunder Junction* release notes and CR update explain the extra beginning phases after combat, skipping untap/draw; all relevant `at the beginning of your upkeep` abilities trigger separately. No phantom extra draws or untaps; abilities can be responded to in each upkeep: https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/outlaws-of-thunder-junction-release-notes and https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/outlaws-of-thunder-junction-update-bulletin.
2. **Damage amount is not trigger count**: unmodified Obeka dealing 2 combat damage yields **one** trigger for **two** upkeeps. Mjölnir, Hammer of Thor doubles actual damage the equipped creature would deal (2 to 4, thus four upkeep steps from the damage event), not the number of events. Double strike from Embercleave can create two damage events and two triggers if she actually damages a player in both steps; blockers/removal and available targets matter. The Mjölnir rules and `equip worthy` restriction are covered by Wizards' Marvel release notes: https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/marvel-super-heroes-release-notes. Obeka is a red legendary non-Villain creature under printed characteristics and hence a plausible worthy equip recipient; confirm current characteristics for an exact rules packet.
3. **Ability multiplicity**: Wizard's Staff doubles an equipped creature's triggered ability; Roaming Throne choosing **Warlock** or **Ogre** adds another instance of Obeka's combat-damage trigger. Two distinct additional-trigger effects are **additive**, not an assumed exponential copy loop: for one qualifying trigger, two such effects yield three instances, barring other effects. Wizard's Staff equips Obeka for generic equip {3}, since she is *not* a Wizard; `equip Wizard {1}` is unavailable for Obeka. This is not a copy effect and each resolving trigger creates its own batch of extra upkeeps. Source: https://mtg.wtf/card/hob/59/Wizards-Staff and the Wizards Hobbit release notes https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/the-hobbit-release-notes. Exact ordering of multiple inserted phases is governed by WotC's insertion rule, not assumed simultaneous triggering.
4. **Paradox Haze** is not an evergreen multiplier of each postcombat upkeep. It adds an upkeep after the enchanted player's **first upkeep each turn**, normally a precombat upkeep; it does not automatically double every Obeka-created upkeep.
5. **Monarch/copies**: Court of Locthwain gives continuing permission to play its exiled cards, while a monarch-gated free-cast permission from each upkeep still obeys normal spell timing absent other permission; it is not unconditionally `cast everything now`. Court of Vantress, if monarch, creates a *token copy* of another targeted enchantment or artifact; when not monarch it can become a copy itself. Both are especially strong only with chosen copy targets and monarch security. Source: https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/wilds-of-eldraine-release-notes.
6. **Artifact threshold paths**: Smaug creates **one Treasure on each of your upkeeps**, and Tamiyo's Journal can make Clues; they are not immediately identical names for Mechanized Production's eight-of-a-name condition. Mechanized Production needs an enchantable artifact and surviving legal attachment; Time Sieve consumes five actual artifacts per activation and costs a tap. The number of extra upkeeps alone does not establish an infinite-turn loop. Smaug text from https://mtg.wtf/card/hob/229/Smaug-the-Magnificent.
7. **Suspend**: extra upkeeps remove one time counter per applicable suspended spell trigger. Casting a spell when its last counter is removed uses the explicit suspend trigger, including spells that are sorceries in an upkeep; do not treat this as ordinary sorcery-speed permission. Jhoira must first exile the spell using her activated ability. `As Foretold` gains counters rapidly but its free spell use is limited **once each turn**, not once per extra upkeep.
8. **Conditional draw**: Super Intelligence is a {U} Aura giving a draw at the upkeep of *enchanted creature's controller*. Enchanting Obeka pays off in every created upkeep so long as attached; if moved to an enemy creature it helps that controller instead. Reference https://mtg.wtf/card/msh/77/Super-Intelligence. Phyrexian Arena additionally costs 1 life per draw. Twilight Prophet only fires its value trigger with city's blessing, and repeated life/payment effects can become hazardous when many upkeeps occur.

## Adversarial slots, hypotheses and candidate cuts

- **Commander access/haste hypothesis**: `Rising of the Day` (3 mana, creatures you control haste; legendary creatures +1/+0) could increase the number and potency of same-turn Obeka connections and serve as redundant haste. The current deck already has haste via Arena of Glory, Greaves, Boots and Ring of Valkas and already has efficient +power sources; the question is whether the extra on-time connective percentage earns a three-mana non-upkeep slot. The strongest possible **cut candidate to stress-test is Darksteel Reactor**: its indestructible upkeep-count alternate win is thematic but requires reaching 20 counters, has little immediate impact if Obeka fails to connect, and can be exiled/bounced. Cutting it would remove a genuinely commander-specific win condition. **Alternative cut**: Jhoira of the Ghitu, a two-mana activation plus a four-time-counter delay and small creature body but one of the genuine suspend/time engines. Neither cut is endorsed without comparing real timelines and matchup value.
- **Triggered redundancy hypothesis**: `Strionic Resonator` {2} artifact with {2}, tap to copy an on-stack Obeka or payoff triggered ability offers a mana-priced extra trigger but duplicates substantial value from Wizard's Staff/Roaming Throne. It may be win-more or needlessly demanding during postcombat mana development; candidate cuts would again involve a presently useful protection/upkeep slot, not free space.
- **Evasion hypothesis**: `Trailblazer's Boots` or `Key to the City` might outperform one of the existing repeatable protection/evasion pieces in high-blocker pods. Both sacrifice some of the existing integrated power-growth/ward package. `Whispersilk Cloak`'s shroud prevents further targeted buffs/equipment attachment changes and is not automatically an upgrade.
- **Treasure alternate-win hypothesis**: `Revel in Riches` can leverage Treasure generation and extra upkeep checks, but needs its exact ten-Treasure threshold and opponent creature deaths; replacing Reactor or an established Court trades one conditional alternate win for another, not self-evident improvement.
- **Naturally defensible slots**: the three Rings gain +1/+1 counters on relevant upkeep and furnish haste/hexproof/regeneration roles; Combat Research is cheap extra damage/draw/ward on a legendary commander, not a generic filler. Silent Arbiter is exceptionally aligned with menace, and the six Courts are load-bearing. Do not cut such slots merely to increase staple density.

### Opponent windows and table states worth targeting

1. **No-haste turn four**: compare Obeka cast with/without Rising of the Day already on board and with a setup Court in hand, accounting for whether the enchantment pushes other pieces back a turn.
2. **Obeka removed at declare attackers or with her damage trigger on stack**: a trigger already on stack exists independently of its source, but damage prevented/removed before connection creates no trigger. An extra-upkeep engine and Court may still work in ordinary upkeeps; some payoff placements are dead before the next connection.
3. **Monarch lost across table**: retain/displace court effects and compare Court Locthwain/Vantress/Ire payoffs at weaker non-monarch modes; assess whether protecting a small body to retain monarch is more important than one further upkeep.
4. **Equipment destroyed/exiled or commander bounced**: Wizard's Staff/hammer/double-strike damage lines lose multiplicative performance, colored mana for commander tax becomes the choke point.
5. **Counterargument to Rising of the Day**: haste after paying 3 mana can be redundant in precisely the hands where Obeka has haste already, never provides immediate card material, and removes an independently game-winning Reactor/Jhoira engine; it may decrease power despite solving a salient failure state. The accepted list's protection, token production and alternate finishers could be better than another aura/enchantment devoted to commander attack.
