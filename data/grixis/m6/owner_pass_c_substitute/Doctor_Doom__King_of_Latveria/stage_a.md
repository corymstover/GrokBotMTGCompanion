## Commander-first architecture and realistic ceiling

The commander is **Villain combat connive plus repeatable land-discard group drain**. It wants enough lands in hand to convert each *separate* discard into a two-life multiplayer tax, Villains to receive recurring menace/connive, and cards that transform discarded lands into value rather than losing real mana development. This is a differentiated land-cycling/connive pressure deck with Marvel supervillain presentation, not generic Grixis midrange. An optimized high-power Bracket 4 is a defensible working ceiling, **not** a measured bracket rating or evidence of cEDH access.

- **Discard enablers:** `Tectonic Reformation` grants cycling `{R}` to each land in hand; `Trade Routes` turns a land in hand into a `{1}` discard/draw activation and can return battlefield lands for reuse; `Seismic Assault` discards lands as a damage cost; `Faithless Looting`, `Frantic Search`, `Desolate Lighthouse`, `Geier Reach Sanitarium`, `Currency Converter`, `Cool but Rude` and connive effects diversify one-card events. `Wheel of Fortune`, `Windfall`, and `Change of Fortune` provide burst resets, but a single action discarding multiple lands makes Doom trigger **once** for that action, not once per land.
- **Villain network:** `Baron Strucker, HYDRA Overlord` reduces Villain cost and enables an extra conditional connive; `Prowler, Clawed Thief` rewards subsequent Villain entries; `Leader, Super-Genius` magnifies connive; `Iron Monger, Sadistic Tycoon` converts connive into Villain counters; `Kang, Temporal Tyrant` attacks/connives and rewards second draws; `M.O.D.O.K.`, `Norman Osborn // Green Goblin`, `Green Goblin, Nemesis`, `Green Goblin, Revenant`, `Loki, the Deceiver`, and `Doctor Doom` support the identity. `Glorious Purpose` rewards actual connive events, not discards generically.
- **Additional conversion:** `Bone Miser` turns discarded lands into black mana (not red); `Surly Badgersaur` makes a Treasure for each discarded land; `Green Goblin, Nemesis` likewise makes **tapped** Treasure for a land discard, so it cannot immediately pay a fresh cycling cost; `Monument to Endurance`, `Cool but Rude` level 2, `Archfiend of Ifnir`, `Containment Construct`, and `Moonstone, Harsh Mistress` generate discard value. `Containment Construct`/`Moonstone` exile effects must be declined for a land intended to be dredged from the graveyard.
- **Copy/pressure:** `Chameleon, Master of Disguise` can copy a controlled Doctor Doom while keeping Chameleon's own name, and `Spark Double` creates a nonlegendary clone. These retain independently triggered group-drain effects; `Roaming Throne` can choose a relevant printed creature type (e.g. Villain) to double qualifying Doom triggers. `Loki` copies after the start-of-combat trigger window, so an attacking copy of Doom does **not** retroactively receive its own beginning-of-combat trigger.
- **Interaction:** `Cyclonic Rift`, `Toxic Deluge`, `Chaos Warp`, `Vandalblast`, `Feed the Swarm`, protection in `Fierce Guardianship`, `Deflecting Swat`, `Swan Song` and efficient tutors help assemble/support the engine. Graveyard hate, commander removal before combat, enchantment removal, and pressure against self-discarding hands remain substantive counterplay.

## Critical built-in finite combo — rules inspection, not playtesting

**Doom + `Tectonic Reformation` + `Surly Badgersaur` + `Dakmor Salvage` in hand** plausibly forms a self-funding *finite* sequence:

1. Activate Dakmor's Reformation-granted `{R}` cycling, discarding it to pay the activation cost. Doom sees one land-card discard and its triggered ability makes each opponent lose 2 when it resolves. Badgersaur separately triggers to create an **untapped** Treasure. Both triggers are placed above the cycling activation, and can resolve before the cycle's draw.
2. Replace the cycling draw with Dakmor Salvage's **dredge 2** while the land is now in the graveyard, milling two cards and returning Dakmor to hand. Use the Treasure for `{R}` on the next cycle, and repeat with a new discard event.
3. One cycle costs a red and produces one replacement Treasure, so net red expenditure after initial access can be zero. Every successful iteration mills **2** and drains each opponent **2**. Twenty repetitions can deal 40 life loss per opponent **only if** the library has at least 40 cards available to dredge, the permanent engine survives, and no opponent interrupts. The line cannot repeat indefinitely using only these pieces, because dredge becomes unavailable with fewer than two library cards and no automatic reshuffle is assured.

This is a priority targeted rules/game-state test, not a certified deterministic win: check stack/priority, trigger ordering, dredge replacement legality and library depletion on a real simulator or independently reproducible tabletop trace. Opponents can remove Surly, Doom or Reformation, exile Dakmor from the graveyard while its cycling activation is pending, or counter an activated/triggered ability with the appropriate narrow interaction. Ordinary Counterspell does not counter cycling as a spell. Additional cards such as `Bone Miser` and `Cool but Rude` can raise output but are not required for the hypothesized core.

## Genuine challenger and alternatives — no swap approval

**Test `+Glint-Horn Buccaneer / -Green Goblin, Revenant` against unchanged canonical.** Glint-Horn is `{1}{R}{R}`, haste, and separately deals **1 damage to each opponent whenever you discard any card**, including a land discarded to Doom's trigger or a nonland connive. While attacking it can pay `{1}{R}` and discard a card to draw, giving an extra discard engine. It shifts some output away from needing lands in hand, comes down earlier than a five-mana attacker, and can amplify each cycle in the Dakmor/Reformation loop.

But Buccaneer is a **Pirate, not a Villain**: it misses Baron Strucker cost reduction, Prowler ETB connive, Iron Monger counters, Glorious Purpose when it connives via effects targeting Villains, and the intended Marvel Villain identity. `Green Goblin, Revenant` is a thematic Villain, flies with deathtouch, and on attack can discard and draw cards equal to the cards you discarded that turn, a potentially major late-turn reload. The Goblin's greater mana/timing exposure makes it a plausible marginal cut, not an objectively weak card. That is the strongest case for **KEEP**. Another possible high-curve cut is `Ultron, Unlimited`, but sacrificing that unique engine without effect-specific testing risks worsening the board contingency. Do **not** call this an approved improvement absent matched evidence.
