## Real convergence and divergence

1. **Identity and baseline agreement:** All records support the 38-land (not GrokBot's 35), `1UBR` Human Noble Villain commander, optimized Bracket 4, individual land-discard events, repeatable connive/lands-in-hand/reuse infrastructure and the protected Marvel Villain core. Stage A reached these conclusions without opening earlier verdicts; it independently identified the same finite Dakmor/Badgersaur/Reformation kill route already identified by the comprehensive review and reconciliation. **This combo is corroboration, not a novel discovery.**
2. **Legality flag resolved:** GrokBot's `2026-12-18` was a later **promotional printing** date, not Doctor Doom's original legal card debut. Wizards lists Doctor Doom as the headliner of **Doom Prevails** and specifies Marvel Super Heroes Commander release **June 26, 2026** (`https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/marvel-super-heroes-commander-decklists`). The October 14 owner gate had it legal. There is **no future-release project exception** for Doom and no newly identified legality blocker. This is a prior advisory source-selection mistake, not an excuse to modify the canonical list.
3. **Stage A independently challenged the earlier KEEP with a specific non-Villain:** `+Glint-Horn Buccaneer/-Green Goblin, Revenant`. Glint-Horn's `1RR` haste and 1-to-each-per-discard trigger could add damage even with nonland connive and make Dakmor cycles each cause an extra life-point of damage, but it is **not a Villain** and competes for red. The earlier October owner candidate screen **did consider Glint-Horn** broadly and declined it; the previous M6 comprehensive analysis prioritized `Putrid Imp` and `Ledger Shredder` tests instead and did not provide matched, exact-replacement evidence for the Revenant cut. So Stage A's hypothesis is neither an automatic new insight nor discredited by a documentary KEEP; it is a **low-priority contested marginal test**, behind mechanically consequential uncertainties and stronger candidate disagreements elsewhere in the 62-deck queue.
4. **The Stage A strongest KEEP counterargument strengthened by post-seal sources:** Wizards' *Spider-Man Release Notes* confirm `Green Goblin, Revenant` is `{3}{B}{R}`, flying/deathtouch, and on every attack **discards one card then draws one for each card discarded that turn** (`https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/spider-man-release-notes`). This can refill heavily after a sequence of separate discarded lands, enabling more Doom attacks/connives without abandoning the Villain card type or a featured character. The hypothetical Glint-Horn damage alone does not demonstrate its superiority over this unique late-game refill. Its discard-while-attacking activated ability has its own mana cost and removal exposure. Treat exact matchup as real but **not yet worth an immediate new owner proposal**.
5. **Other plausible challenger cuts in prior reviews:** `Iron Monger, Sadistic Tycoon`, `Loki, the Deceiver` and `Demand Answers` were candidate flex slots for `Putrid Imp` / `Ledger Shredder`; `Living Laser` alternative has a five-mana attack-token plan. All sever some degree of Villain/connive/land-discard identity and were previously *considered*, not quantitatively tested. An outsider's cheap activation may improve throughput only if hand fuel, other engines and survival permit; `Psychic Frog` already repeats no-mana discards. Do not re-open all clean KEEP decks for an inconclusive outsider ranking.
6. **Stage A precision corrected after sealing:** `Villainous Hideout` counts among the 38 lands but its rainbow mode is **restricted to Villain spells or abilities of Villain sources**. It cannot pay `{R}` for the cycling ability granted to a **land** by Reformation, or general non-Villain costs. This sharper source limitation was in previous reconciliation/author audit but the neutral Stage A only identified generic conditional-source risk. `Monument to Endurance` limits each selected mode to once a turn, so repeating the Dakmor cycle does not gain unlimited Treasure/damage from Monument even though Doom's and Glint-Horn's qualifying triggers repeat. Do not amend sealed A to conceal that added precision.

## Effect-aware bounded rules trace and opponent counterplay

This is **documentary deterministic interaction checking**, not a playtested win rate, and does not by itself satisfy the effect-aware Phase B or local-optimum certificate:

- **Payable case D1, 1 iteration:** You control Doom (`1UBR` already paid), Surly (`3R` already paid) and Tectonic Reformation (`1R` already paid). Dakmor is **in hand** and a usable red is available. Activate Dakmor cycling `{R}`, discarding Dakmor as a cost. Doom's trigger puts **2 life loss for each opponent** on the stack; Surly's independent land-discard trigger creates **one usable Treasure** before cycling draws. On the cycling draw apply Dakmor's dredge 2 while it is in grave: mill exactly two cards and return Dakmor to hand. Tap-sac Treasure for red, ready to cycle again. Order between Doom and Surly triggers is controlled by their controller; each resolves before cycling activation absent interference.
- **D2, 20 iterations / finite bounds:** Each success removes 2 cards from your library via dredge and removes 2 life from each opponent through Doom. Starting with at least **40** cards in library and all components intact allows the *proposed* 20 cycles (40 per-opponent life loss and 40 cards milled), with no self-refresh beyond the generated Treasure. This is not an infinite loop; with 39 cards or fewer remaining, the 20th dredge cannot be used normally. If an opponent gains life, prevents life loss through appropriate effects, removes Doom, or breaks treasure/refuel/yard access, the lethal conclusion changes. Doom loss is **life loss, not damage**, and triggers individually rather than a single stack-wide guaranteed kill.
- **D3, interruption:** Upon Dakmor being discarded as activation cost, an opponent receives priority and may exile it from the graveyard in response before cycling resolves; then the cycling draw cannot dredge that same card. An opponent can also kill Surly before its land-discard trigger even exists, remove it in advance, or remove Reformation before the next cycling iteration. Once Badgersaur's triggered ability is already on stack, killing Badgersaur ordinarily does **not** undo that trigger; the current one Treasure can still be created. Destroying a source while a source-trigger is already on stack does not retroactively erase the trigger. `Stifle`-style interaction can counter a triggered or activated ability; generic spell Counterspell cannot counter cycling as it is not a spell. Exiling the discarded land with `Currency Converter`, `Containment Construct` or `Moonstone` (when chosen) obstructs the dredge return.
- **D4, separate-card vs grouped event:** One hand-discard of three lands from a wheel makes **one** Doom 2-life trigger (and three Surly treasure triggers, because Surly says whenever you discard **a** land card). Cycling three lands separately makes **three** Doom triggers for 6 life lost per opponent. A triggered death/land-discard payoff must not be inflated by counting land cards when its printed grammar says *one or more*.
- **D5, conditional Buccaneer comparison:** With identical Doom, cycling and a discarded Dakmor, if Buccaneer is additionally controlled, its separate trigger deals each opponent **1 damage** per card discarded. Together with Doom's 2 life loss that is **3 life total decrease per opponent** in an ordinary no-prevention case, though prevention of damage can affect only the Buccaneer point. At 20 successful iterations, this is 40 Doom life loss + 20 Buccaneer damage, not 60 *damage*. The proposed swap cuts Revenant, which might have provided a large refill but cannot be assessed from this fixed board state. Buccaneer does not supply the red Treasure; Surly still required for self-funding.
