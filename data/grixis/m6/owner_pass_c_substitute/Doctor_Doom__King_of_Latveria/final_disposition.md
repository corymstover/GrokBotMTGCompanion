## Commander-first merits and limitations

- Doom's own **Villain combat connive** and **2 life loss to every opponent per separate land-card discard event** are the primary engine. High-quality land-discard activations (`Tectonic Reformation`, `Trade Routes`, `Seismic Assault`, `Psychic Frog`), 38 land cards as ammunition, Villain tribal connive (`Baron Strucker`, `Leader`, `Prowler`, `Iron Monger`, `Green Goblin`, `Kang`), land value (`Bone Miser`, `Surly Badgersaur`), copy redundancy (`Chameleon`, `Spark Double`, `Roaming Throne`), and interaction/tutors work coherently. This is not a homogenized Grixis cEDH shell.
- **Rules/source VERIFIED:** Doom triggers once per event discarding one or more lands, not per land in a wheel; self-target connive is legal while Doom is a Villain, but an illegal target on resolution means no draw/discard; a discarded **land** on connive creates **no +1/+1 connive counter**. `Villainous Hideout` is the 38th land, but cannot fund arbitrary red cycling; `Green Goblin, Nemesis` creates tapped Treasure, unlike untapped Badgersaur Treasure. `Monument to Endurance` chooses each option at most once per turn. `Loki` combat-created copies miss that turn's already-passed beginning-of-combat trigger. Relevant current Wizards rulings verified; no global deck-wide Oracle recertification claimed.
- **Finite combo:** Doom + Reformation + Badgersaur + Dakmor in hand can cycle Dakmor for red; Doom group-drains 2, Badgersaur makes Treasure, dredge 2 returns Dakmor on cycle draw, Treasure funds next cycle. Twenty successful iterations require at least forty available library cards for dredge, plus all pieces and opponent noninterference. This is **not infinite** and not an empirically established win condition in sampled pods. Yard exile, removed permanents, insufficient library, prevention/life-gain effects or disrupted triggers are realistic response lines. Stage B records payable 1/20-iteration traces and a graveyard-interruption counterexample; these are rule reasoning rather than simulated match outcomes.
- **Colored-source/curve weakness:** `{1}{U}{B}{R}` commander with many strong dual/fetch/pain sources but one Island/Mountain/Swamp and restricted/colorless utility; `{R}{R}{R}` for Seismic competes with repeated red cycling and multiple four/five-mana Villains. The historical 38-land count is deliberate. No goldfish or colored-source statistical result is claimed.
