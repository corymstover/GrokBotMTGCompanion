## Commander identity and challenger
Thraximundar is seven-mana 6/6 hasty Zombie Assassin forcing defending player to sacrifice a creature on attack, then accumulating +1/+1 counters whenever any player sacrifices a creature. Deck combines repeated enemy edicts (Grave Pact/Dictate/Plaguecrafter/Fleshbag/Accursed Marauder), our sacrifice and aristocrat payouts (Yawgmoth/Altars/Mayhem Devil/Blood Artist), Zombie recursion (Gravecrawler/Wilhelt/Rooftop Storm) and commander damage via Shizo, Cultist and equipment. Historic ceiling Bracket **4 — Optimized**, Archidekt tags **Sacrifice / Zombie Aristocrats / Commander Damage**. These must not be replaced with generic tutor-only Grixis combo.

Sealed challenger **+Ophiomancer/-Necrotic Hex** is not yet proved better: Ophiomancer offers conditional Snake creation at each upkeep and cheaper sacrifice fodder; Hex costs six and is one-shot but combines opponent-facing mass creature sacrifice and Zombie-token generation in a commander-specific counter burst. Opposing board width, ally sac outlet availability, own upkeep schedule and commander survival govern effectiveness.
