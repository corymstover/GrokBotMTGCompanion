## Commander-first thesis
Thraximundar is a costly hasty Zombie Assassin whose attack makes defending player sacrifice a creature and whose +1/+1 counters scale with all players sacrificing creatures. Its distinctive line blends *forced enemy sacrifices* (Fleshbag Marauder, Plaguecrafter, Accursed Marauder, Innocent Blood, Sheoldred's Edict, Vona's Hunger, Grave Pact, Dictate of Erebos, Call to the Grave, Sheoldred) with own sacrifice outlets/value (Phyrexian/Ashnod's Altar, Goblin Bombardment, Yawgmoth, Carrion Feeder, Braids, Skullclamp) and Zombie recursion (Gravecrawler, Rooftop Storm, Wilhelt, Diregraf Captain) to create both commander-damage and aristocrats finishers. Thraximundar's attack trigger cares about defending player, NOT each enemy by default; sacrifices caused elsewhere can still grow him. Mana curve and triple-colored commander casting require actual sources before evaluating on-turn combat. His 7-mana cost and commander tax are natural timing limits; building pure Grixis turbo combo would abandon identity.

## Neutral challenger and countercase
**+Ophiomancer / -Necrotic Hex** as test hypothesis, NOT a committed swap. Ophiomancer reliably creates Snake bodies at each upkeep when you have no Snakes; these can feed own altars, Braids, Skullclamp and Grave Pact triggers, improving continuous sacrifice fodder and defensive survival. Countercase: Necrotic Hex is a six-MV spell forcing each player to sacrifice six creatures and supplies Zombies, a spectacular mass board-control/counter increase for Thraximundar at the cost of more mana and dependence on opposing board depth. Crucial Oracle uncertainty: Necrotic Hex sacrifice count and whether own Zombies are created before or after enemy sacrifices require verification; no invented calculation of exact counters. Another possible cut Archfiend of Depravity threatens opponents' creature width but is a high-value cap even if not a sacrifice event; do not replace blindly. Ophiomancer is not a Zombie, and Snake availability could compete with Gravecrawler Zombie-recursion condition if it reduces Zombie density.

## Rule/interaction probes and answer windows
Gravecrawler can cast from graveyard only while another Zombie is controlled (Thraximundar counts if present; Diregraf/Wilhelt may count). Infinite sacrifice loops through Rooftop Storm/Gravecrawler/Phyrexian Altar require payability and loop legality; Phyrexian Altar adds a colored mana when creature sacrificed, can fund Gravecrawler if legal Zombie on field with no mana-cost Rooftop Storm (but whether graveyard cast permits alternate cost needs exact Oracle). Pitiless Plunderer/Gravecrawler loops require recurring mana and actual death triggers. Ophiomancer makes Snakes conditional once per upkeep, not a recurring token at each sacrifice. Opponent may exile the graveyard, use sacrifice-proof token spam, remove commander before attack, or block with disposable creatures. Double-check exact targeting/timing and protection effects. Necrotic Hex should not be credited with unconditional six counters when few legal creature sacrifices occur.
