## Commander-first strategic assessment

Lynde is a four-mana 2/4 deathtouch Grixis Curse-recursion commander. When a controlled Curse goes from battlefield to your graveyard, the delayed return arrives at the **next beginning of an end step**, initially **attached to you**. Each of **your** upkeep triggers can move **one** of your self-attached Curses to an opponent and draw two. The approved shell makes losses profitable with Claws of Gix, Despotic Scepter, Braids, Perilous Research and Ashiok's Reaper, expands curses with Misfortunes, copies them with enchantment copiers, and uses Haze, Ninth Doctor and Obeka to create more **conditional** personal upkeeps. Opponent-focused Curses, Thirst, Bloodletting, Cruel Reality and aristocrat/control elements create an attrition game with political aggression rather than generic Grixis combo.

## Verified and unresolved mechanics / counterplay

1. **Own graveyard, not hand:** discarded/milled/exiled Curse does **not** trigger Lynde. Destroy/sacrifice an owned, controlled Curse while Lynde is on battlefield to generate return; if it goes into graveyard, delayed return resolves at the next *future* end-step beginning and usually attaches **to Lynde's controller**. An enchantment wiped after Lynde has left will not generate her trigger.
2. **Self-Curse danger** is real, not absolute: at your upkeep, already-triggered Curses stay on stack after Lynde moves them. For Curse of Thirst, the **enchanted-player identity at trigger is fixed** but the **number of Curses on that player is read at resolution**. A solo returned Thirst moved first can therefore resolve for **zero damage**; with other self Curses, damage can remain and self Bloodletting may double it. Cruel Reality/Torment of Scarabs triggers do not vanish merely because the Aura moves. Don't assume instant harmless transfer of a pile.
3. **Misfortunes correction:** Curse of Misfortunes triggers on the Aura controller's upkeep, even when attached to opponent. When self-attached, stack its trigger above Lynde to place a new Curse on self, then Lynde can transfer that Curse and draw2; on the opponent, it tutors directly onto that opponent at **your** upkeep. This corrected a mistaken phrase in sealed neutral Stage A without retroactive modification.
4. **Extra upkeep prerequisites:** Paradox Haze on yourself grants an extra upkeep; Ninth Doctor requires actual untap in your untap step; Obeka requires actual combat damage (2 normally, potentially4 under Bloodletting on damaged player). Extra upkeep **does not** include untap/draw, and Lynde draws only if a still-self-attached Curse transfers that step. Extra player-upkeeps don't inherently trigger Curses that wait for an opponent's upkeep.
5. **Braids timing:** Braids causes sacrifice *after* your end step started; Lynde's delayed return consequently waits for the **next end step (typically next player's)**, not earlier that same end step. Claws/Despotic used in prior main phase can return by current end step. Opponent can exile Curse in graveyard during the interval.
6. **Accursed Witch double face:** Infectious Curse goes to your graveyard after sacrifice, can satisfy Lynde if she was there, but CR 712.14 default returns the DFC **front face Accursed Witch** as a creature; no instant recycled Infectious Curse attachment. The exact interrupted copy-Aura cases with Copy Enchantment/Mirrormade/Estrid have to use last-known Curse status and legal attaching choices, not auto-return all cards as Curses.
7. **Curse of Surveillance** allows the Aura controller, not enchanted player, to select any number of other players to draw, including solely self. **Curse of Obsession** gives enchanted player two additional cards at the draw step *before* endstep discard; they can use instant-speed access in between. **Curse of Echoes** gives other opponents as well as controller potential spell copies; **Curse of Chaos** is cheaper and attack-driven but grants rummage to attackers. These are real political costs, not proven add/cut.
