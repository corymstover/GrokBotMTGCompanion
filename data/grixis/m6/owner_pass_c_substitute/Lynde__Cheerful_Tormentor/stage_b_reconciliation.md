## Paid temporal board cases, not measured outcomes

**A. Self-return before endstep, one Thirst:** Lynde and Curse of Thirst attached to opponent; with Claws of Gix pay {1} and sacrifice Thirst **before your end step begins**, recovers to your field attached to you at own end-step beginning. Next your upkeep Lynde and Thirst both trigger. Stack Lynde on top, attach Thirst to opponent and draw2; Thirst trigger still resolves targeted at its originally enchanted player **you**, but counts zero self-Curses at resolution if there are none remaining. This is **zero Thirst damage**, not automatic self-lethal.
  
**B. Same state plus Bloodletting self:** if Bloodletting remains attached to you after moving Thirst, Thirst can count one self-Curse and deal one damage that Bloodletting doubles to two. If you move Bloodletting instead, Thirst remains and can deal one without Bloodletting's replacement. More self-Curses can raise damage; one transfer per upkeep creates a genuine bottleneck. Counterplay includes killing Lynde before its upkeep trigger and grave hate before return.

**C. Braids at beginning of end step:** Braids trigger goes on stack at step start; when it resolves you sacrifice a Curse. Lynde's newly created delayed ability cannot turn back time to the already-passed beginning; the return occurs at **next available end step** (normally next player's), not the same endstep. Another opponent's end step is usually before your next upkeep, so practical loss may be smaller than one full turn. If Lynde is removed *before* the Curse is sacrificed, no delayed trigger.

**D. Misfortunes self-placement:** `Curse of Misfortunes` attached to you, Lynde controlled. At your upkeep both trigger. Put Lynde below Misfortunes so tutor resolves first, find **distinct-named** Curse attached to yourself, then Lynde transfers it to an opponent and draws2. If Paradox Haze also grants a second upkeep, another search/move may occur if deck has distinct Curse targets. Misfortunes attached to opponent still tutors **on your upkeep** directly onto that opponent, without needing self-attach; Lynde still requires a self-attached Curse to draw.

**E. Curse of Echoes vs Chaos:** with 5 mana paid to Echoes on opponent A, if A casts an instant, every other player including Lynde's controller may copy that spell, potentially helping Lynde **and** opponents B/C. Curse of Chaos at {2}{R} on A instead triggers when A is attacked, once per attacking player each combat; that attacker can discard then draw one. Echoes stronger in frequent valuable A-spells but highly political; Chaos cheaper but gives opponents rummage rather than stopping combo. No counted removal turn or win-rate advantage for either. Neither is an authorized switch.

**F. Ninth Doctor/Obeka conditional count:** Ninth Doctor already untapped at beginning of untap step does not untap and grants **no** extra upkeep. Obeka 2/5 must actually hit player for2 (or4 when Bloodletting doubles damage to that opponent) for 2/4 extra upkeep steps. Four upkeeps do **not** mean draw eight with only one Curse on self; Lynde can transfer only one until further Curses are supplied by Misfortunes or other effects.

Each case is a manual source/cost/priority trace, not seed-based simulation, sampled opponents, Phase B certification or proof of swap superiority. Reuse authored six cases and current source checks. Prioritize new Phase B tests for (1) self-harm in one/two/three Curse stacks, trigger controller/enchanted identity and transfer ordering, (2) DFC/copy states, (3) life/race of Curse pressure under opponents' enchantment and graveyard hate, and only then (4) Chaos/Echoes plus Resonator/Beseech alternatives if costed states show real need.
