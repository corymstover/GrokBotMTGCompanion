## Rules corrections and mechanical reconciliation

1. **Material legacy assumption refuted:** original deep review identifies an erroneous older blueprint statement that `Urza's Saga` finds `Time Sieve`. It cannot: Saga's last chapter selects an artifact with printed mana cost exactly `{0}` or `{1}`, while Sieve has mana cost `{U}{B}`. This finding is preserved as a rules warning and is **not new to JLK**. Tutors such as Imperial Seal and Vampiric Tutor can find Sieve subject to their own timing/draw requirements. Do not reuse bad Saga tutor-path probability as certification evidence.
2. **Fateful Discovery is compulsory:** the 5MV enchantment's artifact-entry draw triggers cannot simply be declined. With Edward producing two Treasures and Academy Manufactor converting each to Food, Clue and Treasure, six artifact entries cause six draws, including if Time Sieve sacrifices five tokens while draw triggers are already on stack. If the library contains only five cards and all six draws resolve, the controller attempts to draw from empty and loses; tokens sacrificed after entry do not cancel draws. Card velocity and deck exhaustion are both relevant.
3. **Mirkwood Bats is a credible unexamined alternative, not a verified improvement:** its creation/sacrifice trigger causes each opponent to lose one life for EACH token created or sacrificed. In the same bounded two-qualifier / Edward + Manufactor + Sieve state, six token creations and five token sacrifices produce eleven Bats triggers for eleven life loss to each opponent, if Bats survives and the sequence resolves. If the Sieve tokens are sacrificed with Bats absent or removed before triggers occur, the promised drain is absent; removal after a trigger is on the stack normally does not erase it. The Sieve line can be repeated only with real tapped qualifying permanents every turn, Time Sieve surviving/untapping, payable access, and opponents not ending the loop. This enumeration is **a finite effect trace, not a gameplay outcome or repeatable lethal certification**.
4. **Subtypes/timing matter:** Bat contributes neither Pirate/Assassin tapped-count density nor Vehicle player-hit theft, while Discovery draws through any artifact entry. Alternative cutting `Adrestia` sacrifices a low-crew ship and thematic connection. Replacing Discovery removes a potentially vital draw/filtering engine at the same time it removes forced overdraw. Edward's own five mana, triple color, and fragile crews remain performance gates. The original review's separate finite Malcolm + Glint-Horn Buccaneer line requires at least two opponents for self-funding and available cards/attacks; it cannot be used as proof the Bat is redundant in every board state.
