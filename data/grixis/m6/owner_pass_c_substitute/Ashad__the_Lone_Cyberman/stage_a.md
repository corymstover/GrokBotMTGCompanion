## Commander mechanics and natural ceiling

Ashad is {1}{U}{B}{R}, legendary artifact creature — Cyberman 3/3. The FIRST **nonlegendary artifact spell** cast on EACH turn gets **casualty 2**. We may sacrifice another creature of power 2+ as a casting additional cost, triggering an artifact-spell COPY; an artifact permanent-spell copy resolves as an artifact TOKEN, retaining that artifact's ETB/static effects but not creating an additional CAST. Each OTHER creature sacrificed puts a +1/+1 counter on Ashad through his independent triggered ability. Casualty can activate again on a different opponent turn if Shimmer Myr enables artifact flash, or through artifact spells that inherently have flash; there is still only one first nonlegendary artifact spell per turn. Copying a LEGENDARY artifact spell is not enabled by Ashad, and a nonartifact enchantment/instant does not qualify.

This is a scarce, high-impact artifact replication and sacrifice-aristocrats engine, not just artifact goodstuff. Wurmcoil Engine, Portal to Phyrexia, Simulacrum Synthesizer, Ichor Wellspring, Noxious Gearhulk, Thought Monitor, Cyberdrive Awakener, Cybermen Squadron, Auton Soldier, Sculpting Steel and Phyrexian Metamorph are meaningful COPYABLE nonlegendary artifact spells with on-entry/value or future sacrifice synergies. Biotransference can make other nonlegendary CREATURE SPELLS artifact spells in all relevant zones, expanding casualty candidates, but does not override legendary status. Twinning Staff's replacement adds one additional copy whenever casualty would create a spell copy: one original plus two artifact-token spell copies (if paid). Copy-artifact tokens trigger artifact-enter effects such as Reckless Fireweaver/Ingenious Artillerist, but do not count as a second cast for Jhoira, Weatherlight Captain or Uthros Research Craft's spell-cast draw trigger.

Multiple ways to sacrifice artifact creatures (Ashnod's Altar, Phyrexian Altar, Krark-Clan Ironworks; some sacrifice ANY artifacts, some creatures), recur artifacts (Emry, Goblin Welder, Scrap Trawler, Academy Ruins, Esoteric Duplicator), produce artifact tokens and convert death/entry (Marionette Master, Disciple of the Vault, Mayhem Devil, Reckless Fireweaver, Pitiless Plunderer) create a coherent optimized Bracket 4 artifact aristocrats/replication game plan. Conditional loops may spike higher but need actual proven resource/cost/trigger pathways, not a claim of automatic cEDH. Preserve Cybermen and artifact-copy identity.

## Neutral adversarial hypotheses and counterarguments

**Principal named candidate, prior administrative exposure declared:** +Three Steps Ahead / -Artificer Class. Three Steps Ahead is a versatile U spree INSTANT: U+1U to counter target spell; U+3 to create a token copy of a target artifact or creature we control; U+2 to draw two then discard one; multiple modes cost more and resolve in printed order. At four mana, copy mode can duplicate an existing significant artifact without requiring power2 casualty fodder, Ashad on battlefield or a first artifact spell. It is NOT an artifact spell and does not itself trigger Ashad, yet supplies immediate protection/selection. Artificer Class costs 1U and discounts the first artifact spell each turn by one generic mana; Level2 costs 1U to reveal/tutor an artifact from library; Level3 costs 5U and then creates a token copy of a target artifact at EACH of our end steps. Once upgraded, persistent copy benefits can exceed one-shot instant cloning and early discounts can help on multiple turn cycles, especially with Shimmer Myr; ten total mana cast/level2/level3 paid across turns is real and vulnerable to enchantment removal. A fast or interaction-heavy pod may prefer a four-mana instant that copies Portal, Synthesizer or Urza engine (when present), while slow attrition with reliable mana may reward the Class. Stage A judgment: NEEDS TESTING, not a proven +/-. Must compare meaningful alternative cuts such as Thopter Spy Network or Unwinding Clock if Class's repeated discount/tutor is valuable; both have genuine roles (thopter/card draw, all-artifact untaps during others' turns).

**Independent pressure point:** Is Worldwalker Helm's Map-token replacement generated when a casualty-copy of an artifact SPELL becomes a token permanent, or only when an EFFECT creates artifact tokens? Relevant because copying a permanent spell becomes a token when it resolves; not every token appearance is equivalent to "create a token". WotC Worldwalker wording says if you would CREATE artifact tokens, make an additional Map; its activated ability {1}{U},{T} explicitly CREATES a copy of a targeted artifact TOKEN we control. The latter certainly creates a token and can be targeted after token exists; do not assert the former replacement applies to Ashad casualty without a rule receipt on copy-of-permanent-spell token creation. It likely matters more to value counts than to a hard win condition, but do not manufacture a Map per casualty copy.

**Alternative challenger idea:** +Three Steps Ahead/-Unwinding Clock may preserve Artificer discounts but lose repeated artifacts untapping during each opponent's untap step. Clock's utility scales with Urza, artifact mana and sacrifice/token activation, so it is not a free replacement; especially with Shimmer Myr it unlocks more opposing-turn activity. +Three Steps Ahead/-Thopter Spy Network would lose early upkeep 1/1 Thopters, combat-damage draw, and artifact counts, though those 1/1 tokens cannot themselves pay casualty 2. Distinguish actual redundant slot from on-theme role.

Other conditional weak slots: high-MV Portal to Phyrexia if naturally drawn with no mana; large Cyberdrive Awakener/Cybermen Squadron; repeated copies of Unwinding Clock or Signets rather than impactful ETBs may provide little incremental advantage. ## Rules timing, stack, interruption scenarios

- Casualty copies first nonlegendary artifact SPELL, not the permanent already in play and not ANY subsequent spell on same turn. Sacrifice 2+ power creature while paying cast costs; Ashad's +1/+1 triggered ability from sacrifice is separate and uses stack, can resolve before the original/copy; the sacrifice cannot be undone by removal after costs paid.
- Copied artifact spell becomes token when it resolves; it is neither cast nor a card to use via Fabricate in deck. Countering the original artifact spell does not retroactively remove an already resolved copy trigger/copy, though countering casualty trigger or copy may stop that portion. If target/cost illegal during spell cast, no casualty.
- Twinning Staff acts once per copy event, adding one copy; it doesn't grant additional casualty payments. Biotransference makes creature spells artifact type while on stack but not nonlegendary by itself. Legendary creatures/Artifacts remain not eligible under Ashad's first-spell wording.
- Ashad has no built-in mana or tokens, so a hand with Ashad and Portal but no power2 fodder cannot cast a doubled Portal even if it reaches nine mana. Worldwalker Helm {1}{U},{T} ability needs a target artifact token; cannot target an ordinary nontoken artifact with that ability. Copies of artifact token from such ability retain characteristics and ETBs.
- Artificer Class Level3 can duplicate a token/nontoken target artifact controlled at end-step and may continue each of our end steps; in contrast Three Steps Ahead's artifact/creature target is chosen as spell is cast, can become illegal before resolution. Holding up 4 mana may be hard in a 30-land opening, though modes counter/draw are flexible. Instant target removal is a real adversarial branch.
- Portals copied can make opponents sacrifice creatures repeatedly on ETB, but if their creatures are gone, second ETB may have diminishing returns. Legendary-rule handling applies if Three Steps Ahead clones legendary target (unless separate exception), so "copy Urza and keep two" is not automatically possible.
