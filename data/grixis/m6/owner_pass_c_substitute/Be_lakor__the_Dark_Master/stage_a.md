## Commander-first strategic identity and ceiling

This is not generic Rakdos/Grixis goodstuff. Be'lakor is an upper-curve **Demon ETB direct-damage and card-draw engine**. Establish Be'lakor, then cheat, blink, copy or cast large Demon bodies; their printed power becomes directed removal/opponent damage without needing combat. Be'lakor itself draws on entry at real life cost; Demon density matters. Its high realistic ceiling is a very explosive optimized Bracket 4 tribal/ETB win engine, with spikes into devastating mass-copy sequences, but six-mana Grixis commander plus another costly Demon imposes serious setup windows. Not a demonstrated Bracket 5 competitive metagame claim.

**Coherent engine groups:**
1. High-power Demons: Valgavoth, Terror Eater; Vilis, Broker of Blood; Bloodthirster; Great Unclean One; Archfiend of Despair/Depravity; Lord of Change; Orcus, Prince of Undeath; Rakdos variants; Rune-Scarred Demon; Burning-Rune Demon; Doom Whisperer; Balor; Gyruda, Doom of Depths; Chaos Defiler, and others. Distinct ETB/card/attack effects complement direct damage.
2. Bypass hefty mana: Sneak Attack, Reanimate, Whip of Erebos, Patriarch's Bidding, Rakdos, Lord of Riots, Heartless Summoning, Herald of Slaanesh, Urza's Incubator, Dark Ritual and artifact rocks. Reanimation and temporary Sneak Attack Demon entries trigger Be'lakor, but require actual commander and legal targets/available mana.
3. Compound creature entries/triggers: Molten Echoes, Reflections of Littjara, Rite of Replication, Spark Double, Panharmonicon, Roaming Throne, Ghostly Flicker, Warstorm Surge, Kindred Discovery. They emphasize DEMON power and ETB rather than default spell-combo homogenization.
4. Distinct tribal win pressure: Liliana's Contract checks four Demons with **different names** at upkeep; Be'lakor pings opponents and blockers; Bloodletter of Aclazotz and Archfiend of Despair multiply opponent life loss in different ways. Whip of Erebos grants lifelink to creatures and can convert Demon-triggered damage to life recovery (requires proper source/last-known information).
5. Stack/board protection: Fierce Guardianship, Deflecting Swat, Mana Drain, Deadly Rollick, Cyclonic Rift, Chaos Warp, Bedevil, Toxic Deluge, Blasphemous Act, Feed the Swarm. Actual wipe coverage exists, but a six-mana commander is still vulnerable to countering and removal before next Demon.

## Mana curve, colored sources, and life budget

36 lands and a rich dual/fetch suite mitigate six-mana 3UBR and many five-to-nine-mana Demons. Ancient Tomb is fast but supplies no required colors. Bojuka Bog, Crumbling Necropolis, Path of Ancestry and Xander's Lounge may enter tapped; Cavern of Souls can cast Demons uncounterably when choosing Demon, but its tribal colored mana restriction does not fix arbitrary interaction spells. Seven or so rocks/discount sources and reanimation/cheat are significant, yet `Vilis` at 5BBB, `Valgavoth`, `Bloodthirster`, and `Archfiend of Despair` in the same draws create potential hand congestion if Be'lakor is answered. `Unholy Annex // Ritual Chamber` is a Room; its Demon-related trigger/cost must not be guessed based on the other half. Quantitative mana-source odds or turn-five commander probability **not measured**.

Be'lakor may draw several but **loses that much life**. Panharmonicon and Roaming Throne can double its ETB trigger, raising both card volume AND life costs, especially with Vilis or repeated blinks; drawing through own library and losing life is a genuine risk. Against lifeloss/replacement and burn tables, one should not treat the ETB as free refueling. Copying Be'lakor might trigger more ETB draws at a time when life is low.

## Material rules adversarial check: entering multiple legendary Demons

A high-upside but easily misrepresented line is **kicked Rite of Replication targeting Be'lakor itself**:
- The spell makes five tokens that enter together as legendary Be'lakor Demon copies (unless a separate effect alters legend status). Original Be'lakor on battlefield sees five **other Demon** entries, causing five Lord of Torment triggers.
- Each of five incoming copies has Lord of Torment as it enters and can see its **other four simultaneous** Demon entrants, provisionally another 20 triggers. Five incoming copies each trigger own Prince of Chaos on ETB; their abilities survive later legend-rule cleanup. **These trigger counts are rules inference requiring source-bound exact board/state audit at Stage B/targeted testing**, not a claimed measured combo win or fresh Scryfall Oracle verification.
- State-based legend rule is checked before any player receives priority to put/resolve these triggered abilities; choose one Be'lakor permanent to keep (not six live Be'lakors). When later Prince of Chaos triggers resolve, each checks the then-current Demon number, **not** the transient six during token entry. The entering copy as damage source may be gone, requiring correct last-known-information handling of power. Targets are chosen as triggered abilities go onto the stack and opponent responses apply.
- `Spark Double` instead can enter as a nonlegendary Be'lakor, remaining alongside original and triggering its own Prince + original Lord; this is an intentionally different copy route. `Molten Echoes` creating a legendary token copy of Be'lakor and `Reflections of Littjara` copying a Demon spell may hit the legend rule; distinguish tokens, entering, casting, and dying.
- If `Rite of Replication`'s single target becomes illegal before resolution, do **not** assume five tokens or their triggers occur. Anti-ETB effects such as Torpor Orb can suppress the whole mechanism. No official independent exact simulated state trace yet.

This is worth formal mechanical adjudication because misunderstanding simultaneous ETBs/legend rule could materially change the deck's actual terminal lines. Do not report all potential triggered damage as guaranteed; each targeted trigger has a legal target/priority window and source characteristics can change.

## Conditional cuts and unranked challengers

- **Warstorm Surge** costs {5}{R} and overlaps Be'lakor's power-based ETB damage. Con: another six-drop in a high-end curve, and it lacks Demon specificity. Countercase: it works for **all** your entering creatures even without a Be'lakor, supplies powerful redundancy through commander removal, and multiplies effects when present. Not a free cut.
- **Reflections of Littjara** {4}{U} may fail if Demon hand is exhausted or clone legendary bodies collide; countercase: copying Demon *spells* for another Demon entering is core identity and the spell copy's ETB effects can trigger even if legendary duplicates do not survive.
- **Rakdos, the Showstopper / other six-plus Demons** can be broad/random/conditional; countercase Be'lakor directly converts their high power and flavor to damage, so raw efficiency and Demon subtype must be considered together.
- **Unholy Annex // Ritual Chamber** or Liliana's Contract can draw or impose life-tax and alternate-victory conditions that don't directly fire Demon damage each turn; countercase each supports Demon token/hand flow and/or tribal victory; no generic-purpose removal.
- **Displacer Kitten** (unranked absent challenger): {3}{U} non-Demon blink of nonland permanent you control on casting a noncreature spell. Repeated Be'lakor blink grants extra Prince trigger/draw and can reset another Demon to trigger Torment if commander stays; four mana less than Warstorm Surge, but requires continuing noncreature spells, repeated life payment and exposes commander to reentry timing/removal. It's not Demon and its controller must already have suitable noncreature spell density. **Possible exact test pair** `+Displacer Kitten / -Warstorm Surge`, NOT a proposed swap or demonstrated upgrade.
- **Conjurer's Closet** {5} (absent) repeatable end-step blink of one owned creature, resilient incremental Demon ETBs, but five mana and non-Demon board piece; **Helm of the Host** {4} cast +{5} equip another absent repeat-copy engine, extremely slow, especially with six-mana commander. Test only if evidence shows enough games reaching that window.
- **Cheap Demon selection/protection** alternatives might beat yet another high-CMC threat, but unranked Stage A cannot assert specific unverified Oracle as an upgrade.

## Opponent counterplay and strongest counterargument

Remove/counter Be'lakor before another Demon ETB; answer the targeted Rite creature in response, stop tokens from entering, use Torpor Orb-type ETB hate, counter mass reanimation, hold instant-speed exile for graveyard/Demon recursion, and pressure life total to constrain Prince of Chaos draw. Board sweeps reset high-priced Demon bodies; an exile sweeper can strand Patriarch's Bidding/reanimation. Conversely waiting until after a Torment trigger exists does not necessarily stop that trigger when its source dies, so priority must be specific.

Strongest anti-KEEP argument: the **Displacer Kitten / Warstorm Surge** curve improvement could turn each inexpensive noncreature spell into a Demon-ETB trigger instead of paying six for duplicate damage, and more precise Rite legend-rule knowledge might reveal an exploitable two-card finisher worth increasing tutor/protection density. Counterargument: Kitten isn't a Demon, doesn't by itself create a second Demon ETB on blinking Be'lakor, increases repeat life loss, and Surge is commander-independent finish redundancy. A normal token-copy Demon entering while Be'lakor remains is already a lethal vector; no indisputably weak incumbent found.
