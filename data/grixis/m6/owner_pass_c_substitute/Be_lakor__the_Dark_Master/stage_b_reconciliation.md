## Fresh challenger and real preservation cost

The neutral-stage exact *UNAPPROVED* pairing was `+Displacer Kitten / -Warstorm Surge`. Kitten costs {3}{U}, can flicker a chosen nonland permanent when a noncreature spell is **cast**, and may repeatedly refresh a Demon entering while Be'lakor is on board. It is NOT a Demon, never itself causes Lord to trigger solely by entering, doesn't automatically make a Demon token, and blinking Be'lakor alone normally yields Prince card draw/**life loss**, not Lord damage (because he doesn't see himself as "another" Demon). Real damage requires blink of *another* Demon while Be'lakor is present or an additional Be'lakor-like Lord. Spell density, colored mana and protecting engine are preconditions.

Warstorm Surge is six mana (expensive in high curve) but deals entering **creature** power as independent damage even if Be'lakor is absent or countered; it creates redundant closing routes during a commander-tax game, and combines with Be'lakor on Demon entries. Cutting it for a non-Demon four-mana trigger-dependent blink body may decrease resilience when Be'lakor is removed. The source already has Ghostly Flicker, Spark Double, Rite, Molten Echoes and Reflections: a raw ceiling gain from one more repeat blink is not automatically superior to source diversity. Without verified identical-start sequencing or a current meta-specific commander uptime failure, **the exact proposed pair has no proven positive delta** and is rejected for immediate owner consideration.

The earlier original rival `Sakashima of a Thousand Faces` for a high-curve Demon is a more stable second Lord, but is another non-Demon 4MV setup card competing with Spark Double, clone suite and 25 printed-Demon density. The original review already rejected high-risk `Abyssal Persecutor / Sower of Discord`, `Ancestral Statue / Roaming Throne`, and other low-identity rank swaps. No unaddressed mandatory improvement discovered; do not autocut Warstorm or add Kitten based solely on lower mana value.
