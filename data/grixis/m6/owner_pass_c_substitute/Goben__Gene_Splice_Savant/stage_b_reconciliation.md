## 3. A consequential mechanical contradiction discovered during this controlled comparison

The Oct16 generated interaction artifact claims "9 verified/0 flags," but at least TWO of its claims are **incompatible with official card/casting rules** and have consequential gameplay and card-evaluation impact. These are post-seal findings, not Stage A source inputs.

**A. Modal DFC casting from command zone, Cavern interaction.**
- Incorrect source claim in `grixis/interactions/norman-osborn.md#norman-01`: "Everywhere else, including the command zone, only Norman's front face counts. A recast always comes back as Norman, and you pay to transform again." The first sentence describes ordinary zone characteristics; the second is **NOT a correct conclusion about which face may be cast**. Oct19 original authored review similarly suggests the command-zone/default cast *uses the front*, and its Cavern evaluation says Cavern protects only casting Norman.
- Controlling current WotC *Comprehensive Rules 2026-09-25*, `https://media.wizards.com/2026/downloads/MagicCompRules%2020260925.pdf`, **CR 712.11b–c**: a player casting a *modal* double-faced card chooses which face to cast before putting it on stack, and that chosen face determines casting legality; **CR 903.8** gives permission to cast one's commander from command zone, with command-tax additional cost. WotC *Spider-Man Release Notes* `https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/spider-man-release-notes` identifies Norman/Green Goblin as a modal DFC with both permanent faces, front {1}{U}, back {1}{U}{B}{R}.
- Correct practical trace: with untouched command zone and adequate {1}{U}{B}{R}, choose Green Goblin as commander spell and cast back face directly for **four** mana; do not need to spend {1}{U} on Norman nor another {1}{U}{B}{R} on a transform first. If one prior command-zone casting occurred, the additional commander tax applies to either chosen face. `Cavern of Souls` naming **Human** may fund colored mana for either Norman (Human Scientist Villain) **or Green Goblin (Goblin Human Villain)** and make that cast creature spell uncounterable if its mana was spent, subject to full current card text. It does not itself protect Norman's activated transform. This corrects a source-backed false Cavern weakness and is hugely relevant to commander access, fast mana and protective sequencing.
- **Do not confuse zone characteristics with cast-face choice:** in the command zone only the front face supplies normal card characteristics, but the casting procedure lets the controller choose modal back. If later source rules or card text disallow a face through some separate restriction, evaluate that restriction separately; no such blanket exclusion exists here.

**B. Lion's Eye Diamond activation *during* spell casting.**
- Incorrect source claim in `grixis/interactions/norman-osborn.md#norman-04`: `Lion's Eye Diamond` "is a mana ability usable only at instant timing. **It can be activated mid-cast, so you can discard your hand while paying for a spell**..." That latter sentence violates its printed activation restriction. The former sentence does not imply the latter.
- Primary WotC *Modern Horizons 2 Release Notes* `https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/modern-horizons-2-release-notes-2021-06-04` explicitly analogizes Diamond Lion to Lion's Eye Diamond: an instant-timing-only mana ability **cannot be activated while another spell is being cast**. It can be activated before casting, or after putting a fully paid spell on stack while holding/receiving priority; it does not supply mana within that casting procedure. If LED was activated first, its hand-discard cost happens before trying to cast from graveyard; Green Goblin then gives same-turn discard-based mayhem if the card is eligible and priority/timing/mana permit. Breach LED loops also require real escape fuel; not all LED sequences automatically work with a spell already in hand.
- This changes line-order diagrams, admissible starting mana for Mayhem, the value of exact colored rocks and opponent response windows. Do not promote any prior "verified" interaction certificate relying on mid-cast LED until amended and rechecked.
