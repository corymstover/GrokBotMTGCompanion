## 2. Commander identity and power ceiling

Norman front {1}{U} unblockable connives **on dealing combat damage to a player**. Green Goblin back {1}{U}{B}{R} is a flying menace 3/3 with two central, commander-specific effects: nonland cards **in your graveyard** gain mayhem equal to their mana costs (to cast only if discarded **that same turn**, still observing instant/sorcery timing), and spells you actually cast **from graveyard** receive up to {2} generic-cost reduction. This permits short, high-velocity discard/recast bursts, differentiated from generic blue-black-red goodstuff. Artist's Talent, Shadow of the Goblin, Attunement, Faithless Looting, Breakthrough, Tolarian Winds, Windfall/Wheel, Ghostly Pilferer, Psychic Frog, Big Score and LED support specifically this identity. With zero/one-mana rocks, protection, Ad Nauseam, and Breach+LED+Brain Freeze or Oracle+Consultation/Pact finishes, the **natural ceiling is Bracket 5 cEDH-style turbo**, provided Goblin mechanics remain real. ## 4. Newly surfaced rules-source errors require targeted correction

A prior generated `grixis/interactions/norman-osborn.md` (blob **8b8e7589e24483c6b8b6d252a8378e1d46c7d875**) advertised "9 verified, 0 uncertain". Two of its claims are materially wrong in ways the Stage A neutral rules analysis avoided. Stage B directly compared the claims to **WotC 2026-09-25 Comprehensive Rules** `https://media.wizards.com/2026/downloads/MagicCompRules%2020260925.pdf` (especially 712.11b–c, 903.8) and **WotC Modern Horizons 2 release notes** `https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/modern-horizons-2-release-notes-2021-06-04`.

**A — Cast the back face from command zone:** The artifact wrongly inferred "must always cast Norman and transform; recast always front" from the fact the front's ordinary characteristics apply outside stack/battlefield. Actual modal-DFC *casting* lets the player select Green Goblin as the spell face and cast it **directly from command zone for {1}{U}{B}{R} plus any commander tax**, avoiding extra front cast/activation while foregoing front connive. This affects strategic choice, black/red access, early-cast efficiency and protective resources. **Cavern of Souls naming Human** can make **either** the Norman cast or the Goblin back-face creature cast uncounterable if the restricted Cavern mana is spent to cast it (both have Human type), contra the generated/original blanket "only Norman" line. Cavern does not make the **transform activated ability** uncounterable.

**B — LED cannot activate while casting a spell:** The same artifact incorrectly says LED can be activated "mid-cast" to discard while paying for another spell. **Lion's Eye Diamond activation timing is constrained to times a player could cast an instant.** A mana ability is still a mana ability, but that explicit constraint makes it unavailable *during* the cost-payment step of casting another spell. Correct lines activate before spell casting, or after a fully paid spell is already on the stack when priority permits, with discard, mana and response windows modeled honestly. Official WotC MH2 release notes explicitly confirms the Lion's Eye Diamond analogy. Existing prior combo certificates depending on mid-cast activation are not accepted merely because a generated interaction page labels them verified.

The above errors are *not a new original card ban or owner approval trigger*.
