## Commander identity, natural power ceiling and meaningful win paths

The approved 100 is **high-velocity Evelyn Worldgorger / exile-permission cEDH**, not casual Vampire tribal. The conspicuous scarcity of secondary Vampire bodies is not necessarily a construction defect: at cEDH speed, putting weak Vampires in play solely to exile another top card each may cost turns and interaction slots. Evelyn is a **flashable exile-based outlet and access engine** with a unique commander-as-loop-payoff role, supported by some independently powerful Grixis compact victories. The natural ceiling is **Bracket 5 — cEDH** as a strategic hypothesis, not a fabricated tournament win rate.

1. **Worldgorger engine central to identity:** `Entomb`, `Unmarked Grave`, `Faithless Looting`, `Gamble`, `Ripples of Undeath` and reanimation spells can put **Worldgorger Dragon** in graveyard and attach `Animate Dead`, `Dance of the Dead` or `Necromancy`. Dragon enters and exiles other permanents including its reanimation Aura; Aura leaving causes Dragon to be sacrificed; Dragon leaving returns exiled permanents including Aura. An Aura reentering can choose a new legal graveyard creature; if it repeatedly enchants Dragon, the ETB/LTB cycle continues and can repeatedly untap mana-producing permanents and recast Evelyn's collection trigger via battlefield reentry. Evelyn's **flash** allows deployment within an established interaction window as mana becomes available; her returning with each blink creates further collection-counter exile events. A real *outlet* or break condition is needed; blindly looping with no legal exit can force a drawn game rather than a win. Opponents can interact with instant-speed graveyard hate, exile/bounce of Dragon or Aura at timing windows, and counterspells before reanimation resolves.
2. **Oracle/Consultation fallback:** `Thassa's Oracle` plus `Demonic Consultation` or `Tainted Pact` forms a compact potentially decisive line when Oracle's triggered ability resolves with an empty library. Tainted Pact's exile-until-choice requires actual card-name distinction; the approved 100 has **no duplicate names**. Oracle can lose to removal/interaction on trigger in some states depending on devotion and library count; `Stifle`, blue counters, and opposing hate matter. `Demonic Consultation` names an absent card to exile the whole library but does not guarantee success if Oracle is removed before the trigger or if it is countered. This line itself does not express Evelyn's personality, yet provides cEDH fail-safe when stolen cards are inaccessible.
3. **Breach line:** `Underworld Breach`, `Lion's Eye Diamond`, `Brain Freeze`, tutors, free spells and an accurately counted graveyard support reuse and mana/storm; escape requires exiling **three other graveyard cards** per escape (on top of mana costs) and consumes those resources. Breach does not automatically make all exiled collection cards castable or permit playing multiple through Evelyn's one-per-turn text. The presence of both Worldgorger and Breach increases interaction resilience but may compete for graveyard resources.
4. **Native Evelyn card access:** flash Evelyn on an opponent's turn for an ETB batch of top cards gives the option of a *legal instant* from the exiled collection that turn, and future turns one playable card per turn while Evelyn remains in play. `Party Thrasher` noncreature exile-cast convoke can help pay for a *noncreature* spell from Evelyn's collection, but it has no blanket cost bypass, does not let you play a second Evelyn collection card, and uses creatures that may be needed to block. `Leyline of Anticipation` and `Borne Upon a Wind` can change timing of casting when relevant but do not themselves waive land-play restrictions. `Necropotence` skips draw step/replaces discards but does not prevent Evelyn from making a different resource pool in exile.

## Mana, curve, recovery and opponent counterplay

**High-efficiency nonland package** includes Sol Ring, Mana Vault, Chrome/Mox Diamond/Mox Opal, Lion's Eye Diamond, Lotus Petal, Arcane Signet/Fellwar Stone/Talismans, Dark/Cabal Ritual, Rite of Flame, Simian Spirit Guide, Culling the Weak; fast access matters because Evelyn 5MV isn't a turn-two spell with ordinary lands. `Ad Nauseam` and `Necropotence` can turn life into cards, but life cost and high-MV Dragon/Ad Naus still penalize reckless self-damage; pain/fetches and an opponent's pressure change acceptable lines.

**High-value disruption** Force of Will/Negation, Pact of Negation, Flusterstorm, Swan Song, Mental Misstep, Mindbreak Trap, Pyroblast/Red Elemental Blast, Stifle, An Offer, Redirect Lightning, Opposition Agent, Orcish Bowmasters, Dauthi Voidwalker, Vexing Bauble, Hexing Squelcher, Defense Grid and Spellskite protect while pressuring enemy search/draw/stack patterns. None is universally live: Force of Negation free alternate cost is restricted to opponent's turn, Fierce Guardianship is not in this list, Defense Grid taxes opponents but can interfere with our own reactive line if cast timing changes, and Vexing Bauble can constrain one's own free spells until removed/sacrificed. `City of Traitors` land sacrifice and the 26-land count punish slow setup or tax effects.

**Most consequential hostile responses:** Grafdigger's Cage effects and instant graveyard hate against Worldgorger/Breach, Disenchant/Aura removal timed to the loop, Opposition Agent stopping tutors, Drannith Magistrate restricting card play from exile (though casting commander from command zone also blocked), Narset/Orcish Bowmasters punishing card draw, Rule of Law stopping successive spells even with infinite mana, and hostile faster Thoracle stacks. If a reanimation Aura is removed *with Dragon's ETB trigger still on the stack*, Dragon may remain while its future ETB exiles other permanents, potentially removing your whole board until Dragon leaves—this is an exposure and requires exact stack handling, not a generic guaranteed bounce reset. With Evelyn already removed, collection cards remain but current permission to play is gone.

## Adversarial challenge and plausible cuts (no swap authorization)

**Primary exact controlled candidate: ADD `Brainstorm` (`{U}`, instant draw three then put two hand cards on library); CUT `Ripples of Undeath` (`{1}{B}` enchantment, at beginning of first main phase mill three; may pay `{1}` and 3 life to pick one of those milled cards into hand).** In short, high-pressure cEDH games, Ripples demands a two-mana tap-out AND surviving until a *future first main phase* before the first selection; further use costs another mana and three life each turn. Brainstorm gives immediate one-blue instant-speed triage in a deck with many shuffle-capable fetchlands, possibly improving the ability to find Worldgorger outlet, reanimation Aura, Oracle/Consultation, interaction or an early fixing piece while holding stack mana open. It is no additional net card after resolution unless a shuffle recycles two bad cards; it is faster *selection*, not an actual two-card draw advantage. The candidate is legal UBR and distinct from all 100 approved names.

**Strongest counterargument to this hypothesis / strongest KEEP case:** Evelyn specifically wants Worldgorger Dragon **in the graveyard**, whereas Brainstorm can only put cards **on top of library**, not discard/mill them. Ripples mills THREE cards each turn and can *recover any one of the just-milled cards* for a conditional additional {1}+3 life, so it actively enables graveyard Dragon and Breach rather than only select into an already full hand. Once played, it keeps generating value if an opponent stops the first attempt. Multiple cheap tutors, Looting, Gamble, Culling, and an already high low-cost free-interaction density lower Brainstorm's marginal benefit. Brainstorm also creates a poor top-two lock when there is no accessible shuffle and can leave the Dragon stuck in hand. Losing a commander-specific graveyard setup piece for a generic cEDH cantrip may be homogenization. That tradeoff can produce no improvement or an actual loss.

**Secondary alternatives, not automatic proposals:** `Consider` `{U}` instant surveil 1 then draw one can put a Dragon/Breach card into graveyard immediately without exposing 3 life or waiting next turn, but digs only one deep and cannot recover a milled card; `Careful Study` `{U}` sorcery draws two/discards two, excellent for Dragon from hand but is down a card, sorcery-speed and less resilient than a persistent Ripples. Either would use the SAME Ripples slot only if targeted comparison establishes actual benefit, not as a batch of forced swaps. Cutting `Party Thrasher` instead preserves Ripples but removes the exile-spell convoke and two-card impulse option tied directly to Evelyn, so it is weaker strategically as an initial cut. **No new card inserted.**

**Counterfactual evaluation:** compare exact approved 100 versus `+Brainstorm/-Ripples` with matched seven-card hands, cEDH opposition/tax boards and repeated legal actions. Measure turn-to-reanimation, Worldgorger in graveyard vs stranded hand, Evelyn cast timing/UBR hybrid colored source, actual mana/spells remaining for protection, Oracle/Consultation access, hand-lock probability without fetch, 3-life Ripples costs, Breach graveyard density, artifacts/counters and Rule-of-Law/Drannith disruption. Keep separate fast (<3 turns) races and slower interrupted (turn 4+) games. This will decide *speed versus graveyard identity*, not a generic preference for Brainstorm.
