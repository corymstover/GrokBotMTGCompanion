## Identity-driven disposition

Evelyn `{2}{U/B}{B}{B/R}`, five-mana flash Vampire Rogue, is built at her realistic **Bracket 5 — cEDH/fringe competitive** ceiling. Her own identity payoff is a collected-exile card pool from every controlled Vampire entry, notably her repeated **Worldgorger Dragon / Animate Dead, Dance of the Dead, or Necromancy** flicker. Evelyn allows one collected card *play* each turn, subject to normal costs/timing, with any-color mana substitution for casting; she does not grant free spells or unrestricted number of plays. Non-Evelyn Oracle/Consultation and Breach/LED/Brain Freeze remain legitimate high-powered fallbacks but are not proof of commander-centric execution. Adding random Vampire tribal cards would damage turbo optimization.
