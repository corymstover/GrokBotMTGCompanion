## Material mechanics: agreement and new caution

1. **Evelyn's identity must retain its actual cEDH purpose.** One may win through Oracle/Consultation or Breach without Evelyn, but the commander adds a distinctive **flashable Worldgorger blink and collection-access outlet**; adding weak generic Vampire tribal bodies would typically lose speed. Original authored review's +Displacer Kitten/-Wan Shi Tong or -Party Thrasher might be a more directly commander-first candidate than neutral Stage A's generic Brainstorm, yet Kitten costs `{3}{U}` plus deployed Evelyn and needs noncreature spells after both resolve, exposing multi-piece/removal tempo. Preserve this open as a separate **previously identified conditional trial**, not a proposed swap or measured upgrade.
2. **Stage A's newly proposed controlled tradeoff remains unresolved:** `Brainstorm` `{U}` is immediate instant-speed draw-three/put-two-back, very useful with fetchland shuffles and protection-mana discipline; `Ripples of Undeath` `{1}{B}` has no effect until the next first main phase after being cast in the ordinary window and can recurring-mill three, optionally recover one of those three for `{1}`+3 life. Original ChatGPT mentioned a different card, Dragon's Rage Channeler, versus the Ripples slot, but not the exact Brainstorm alternative. The strongest argument AGAINST cutting Ripples is that it puts Worldgorger/Breach resources into the graveyard without needing a second looting spell; this is commander-specific leverage Brainstorm cannot replace. A card-efficiency win for Brainstorm is not automatically a natural-strategy win.
3. **`Saw in Half` actual dies/legend handling:** original authored review corrected preapproval speculation that an owner moving Evelyn to the command zone would necessarily prevent Saw in Half copies. Under current Commander rules, dying sends the commander to graveyard and it can be moved to command zone subsequently by state-based action; the death condition of Saw in Half may be satisfied and two token copies can enter, triggering Evelyn's collection events even when legend rule soon removes one. If Evelyn is indestructible or a true death replacement effect prevents graveyard entry, copies are not generated. This rule correction is already existing work, credited to original author and reused rather than presented as new independent discovery.
4. **WGD must have escape/break and real priority:** Aura on Dragon recurring exiles/returns the other permanents and can produce repeated mana and Evelyn ETBs. If the Aura reattaches to Dragon forever without alternate legal graveyard target/instant break, the game can be forced to a draw; after opponents stop it, the controller may have lost key permanents. Drannith, Rule of Law, instant graveyard hate, counterspells and draw-hate are consequential. A library with zero cards is not *by itself* proof a collected-card route has won.
