## Agreements, newly discovered issues and corrected evaluation

**Strong agreements:** Stage A and author correctly identify the same counter-at-death delayed end-step return, commander distinct from Dealer of Death, 34-land base, meaningful non-Human undying/persist reset, Glen Elendra+Sage {U} per countered noncreature spell, and counter placement/cancellation as the indispensable identity. Grok's generic counter-theme saturation concern has some merit, but calling Danny Pink redundant ignores its *per creature each turn* draw with counter-seeding; calling Twilight Diviner a generic counter payoff misses once-per-turn token copying from Marchesa graveyard returns. No systematic requirement to cut these for additional generic draw/hate arises.

**Juri hypothesis sharpened and lowered in priority:** `Juri, Master of the Revue` at {B}{R} is a serious on-theme early curve alternative: existing sacrifice actions put +1/+1 counters on it; dying turns its power into targeted damage; counter on Juri at death qualifies it for Marchesa's delayed return. It starts 1/1 and loses accumulated counters when returning, so requires repeated sacrificial setup. In contrast, original author's detailed **Flayer of the Hatebound + Ashnod's Altar + Sage of Fables** source-paid line explains why cutting the six-drop is dangerous: sacrifice Flayer to Altar for {C}{C}, undying returns Flayer with +1, Flayer deals its power to target, spend two on Sage remove +1 counter and draw, repeat until opponents are dead **or deck runs out**, paying real loop costs and allowing graveyard interaction. Flayer also gives damage to *other* creatures entering from our graveyard under normal Marchesa revival. These are concrete wins Juri cannot replace by merely being two mana. This source-backed new fact reduces the first-pass challenger from 'potential major improvement' to **conditional curve/metagame tradeoff**, insufficient for an immediate new-owner card swap.

**Old author alternatives stronger than new Juri in relevant scenarios:**

- `+Village Rites / -Braids, Arisen Nightmare`: pay {B}, sacrifice a creature as additional cost and draw two now, accelerating emergency protection; but Braids repeats an end-step sacrifice and may cause three opponents to suffer loss or grant repeated draws. Old goldfish reportedly showed a pass for Rites despite not modeling Braids's effect; therefore it is *not* evidence of a true improvement. Against Marchesa's end-step queue, Braids's own-end-step sacrifice usually returns the next player's end step, whereas instant Rites can proactively schedule own end-step return.
- `+First Day of Class / -Danny Pink`: immediate +1/+1 counter/haste support for a specific persist combo, but one-turn support loses Danny's card draws over multiple turns/creatures, an identity-core source.
- `+Walking Ballista / -Mayhem Devil`: alternate colorless mana outlet, yet Devil pings on any player's sacrificed permanent and synergizes numerous outlets and countered creatures; not interchangeable by card popularity.
- `+Kokusho, the Evening Star / -Syr Konrad, the Grim`: fixed death five-life drain to all opponents vs Konrad's versatile death and card movement damage; needs actual commander-seeded death cadence, not simply mana-cost ranking.
- Grok `+Blowfly Infestation / -Twilight Diviner`: counters spread on deaths can actively put **-1/-1 counters** that cancel Marchesa's +1 protection, and lose Diviner's once-turn graveyard-entry token duplication. Source-independent adversarial analysis finds this a poor improvement unless a defined counter-combo line is established.

## Discrete rules stress and responsive counterplay

1. **Marchesa delayed vs alternate immediate return:** Black Rose never supplies same-stack infinitely repeated returns merely by dying with +1. With Mikaeus/Redcap (non-Human persist+undying) or Glen/Sage, an **immediate** persist/undying return may move the card so an already scheduled Marchesa next-end-step return cannot find it. Multiple sources of counters can actually disable one of those triggers. Persist looks for **no -1/-1 counter**; undying looks for **no +1/+1 counter** at death. A targeted exile before the immediate/delayed trigger resolves breaks repeatability.
2. **True high-impact source-paid examples:** Author demonstrates `Spark Double` entering as `Murderous Redcap` with additional +1, copying persist and cancelling its -1 on return, looping with Ashnod's Altar if a Redcap to copy persists; `Gev` allowing Redcap to enter with +1 when a player already lost life, then Redcap self-damage repeated pings are **not automatically opponent lethal** without a drain outlet. Flayer+Sage+Altar must stop before self-decking; no manufactured unbounded draw.
3. **End-step and wipe timing:** `Sneak Attack` return after sacrificial timing before/at beginning of end step changes which next end step Marchesa uses; `Toxic Deluge` continuous -X/-X until end of turn can kill returned creatures again immediately at that same end step, unlike a resolved `Blasphemous Act` one-time damage. Graveyard replacement such as Rest in Peace or exiling tracked cards denies Marchesa returns. Counter on Marchesa makes her own dies event eligible if trigger exists.
4. **Actual security/combo threat:** Compared with faster cEDH Oracle/Pact pods, this 34-land value shell has less universal creature-spell stack protection, and it is susceptible to `Rest in Peace`/`Grafdigger's Cage`, targeted commander removal, and efficient graveyard exile. The natural Bracket4 ceiling must be preserved; adding generic cEDH staples while removing core sac/counter cards needs explicit justification. No measured opponent win rate exists in source or Stage A.
