## Commander identity, ceiling and whole-list account

Marchesa, **the Black Rose** ({1}{U}{B}{R} 3/3 Human Wizard) is not Marchesa, Dealer of Death. She has dethrone, gives other creatures dethrone, and when a creature **you control** with a +1/+1 counter **dies**, sets a **delayed** trigger to return **that card** under your control at the beginning of the **next end step**. Dethrone triggers on attacking the player tied for or at highest life (not a planeswalker), places its counter before blockers; it does not depend on life total still being highest at resolution. No automatic counter appears on creature solely because it was cast; alternative counter seeding and sac-outlet timing matter. Her own death can be covered if she had a counter when she died and was on the battlefield just before death; the delayed return must still find the same card in the expected zone. Tokens cannot return after ceasing to exist. If a creature dies **during an end step after its beginning** its delayed return normally waits until the following turn's end step, and a card that left the graveyard in between can break the return.

Her genuine identity is **counter-seeded death-and-return value**, with controlled sacrifice, ETB/death payoffs, and a few precise persist/undying combo routes. A mostly synergistic **Bracket 4 optimized** natural ceiling is plausible; do not declare cEDH merely because Demonic/Vampiric tutors and fast lands occur. Test power-ceiling reassessment against exact combo speed, interaction and commander access rather than force a generic Grixis Breach/Pact package.

All 99 library slots are accounted for in the baseline: **34 lands**; **65 nonlands** consisting of early sacrifice outlets (`Viscera Seer`, `Carrion Feeder`, `Yahenni`, `Goblin Bombardment`, `Altar of Dementia`, `Ashnod's Altar`, `Phyrexian Altar`, `Warren Soultrader`, `Yawgmoth`, plus land-based `High Market`/`Phyrexian Tower`); counter creation or retention (`Unspeakable Symbol`, `Gev, Scaled Scorch`, `Sage of Fables`, `Metallic Mimic`, `Iron Apprentice`, `Forge of Heroes`, `The Ozolith`, `Thran Vigil`, `Uncivil Unrest`, `Agatha's Soul Cauldron`, Mikaeus undying and dethrone); death/entry engines (`Blood Artist`, `Zulaport Cutthroat`, `Mayhem Devil`, `Marionette Apprentice`, `Grave Pact`, `Gray Merchant`, `Flayer of the Hatebound`, `Syr Konrad`, `River Kelpie`, `Twilight Diviner`, `Satoru, the Infiltrator`, `Danny Pink`, `Grim Haruspex`, `Braids`, `Puppeteer Clique`, `Murderous Redcap`); tutor/protection/recursion (`Demonic Tutor`, `Vampiric Tutor`, `Diabolic Intent`, `Imperial Recruiter`, `Reanimate`, `Victimize`, `Fierce Guardianship`, `Flare of Denial`, `Lightning Greaves`, sweepers/removal); plus ramp/fixing (Sol Ring, Signet, Fellwar, three Talismans, sacrifice-mana altars). ## Source-paid mechanics that alter card value

1. **Delayed end step vs immediate persist.** `Murderous Redcap` persists if it died without a -1/-1 counter, while `Mikaeus, the Unhallowed` gives undying to non-Humans if they died with no +1/+1 counter. Persist returns with a -1/-1 counter; later undying returns with a +1/+1 counter; with a *free* sacrifice outlet these can alternate as opposing counters are absent on each death. Redcap's ETB target damage scales into an unbounded series only if the entire state is legal and opponent responses are accounted for. Marchesa's recursion is **not** an immediate substitute for a persist/undying trigger: she queues a delayed next-end-step return, so cannot independently support same-stack infinite loops. With `Metallic Mimic`, `Gev` (opponent life lost), or `Thran Vigil` (creature card leaving GY), +1/+1 and -1/-1 counters may cancel on state-based actions, permitting a separate persist loop; verify exact replacement/trigger timing for each source instead of claiming interchangeable copies.
2. **Self-protection.** `Unspeakable Symbol` can put a counter on a creature before sacrificing it. It must **already have a counter when it dies** for Marchesa to schedule return. A creature dying in response to an attempted counter activation may not be protected if the counter never resolves. Marchesa must be present immediately before the death event for her ability to trigger; later removal of Marchesa after she produced the trigger cannot automatically erase an already created delayed trigger. Returning creatures are new objects without old counters unless an entering replacement applies, requiring reseeding next loop.
3. **Control/zone nuance.** A creature under your control but owned by someone else (e.g. a stolen `Puppeteer Clique` target) can trigger Marchesa on death if it had the counter and Marchesa was on battlefield; Marchesa may return that card under your control from its owner's graveyard at next end step if still the tracked card in that graveyard. Flayer triggers only for creature **entering from your graveyard**, not every opponent-owned creature it returns. `Puppeteer Clique` has its own exile-at-end-step rider on stolen creature; a timely sacrifice before exile, plus counter, changes outcome. These paths must be traced with ownership and trigger ordering, not inferred as permanent steals automatically.
4. **Token and copy limits.** `Twilight Diviner` costs {2}{B}, ETB surveil 2, and once per turn makes a token copy of another creature entering/cast from a graveyard. A token copy of `Murderous Redcap` may have a real ETB ping, but a token that dies ceases to exist and cannot be recurred by Marchesa. `Satoru, the Infiltrator` draws on qualifying **nontoken** uncast creature entries; mass simultaneous entries cause **one** group trigger, not one per creature. `Danny Pink` grants each creature a first-counter-added-*each-turn* draw trigger, so repeated counters on the same creature during one turn do not make unlimited draw without it leaving/re-entering as a new object.
5. **Real interaction burden.** `Glen Elendra Archmage` plus `Sage of Fables` can repeatedly counter **noncreature spells** for {U} and a sacrifice, subject to Sage's Wizard-enter counter applying after persist's -1 and cancelling counters. It cannot counter creatures, doesn't pay its own {U}, and loses to removal on Sage/Archmage at a priority window. `Uncivil Unrest` is {4}{R}, grants creatures riot and doubles damage dealt by creatures you control with +1/+1 counters; it is a strong Marchesa reseeding/combat engine, not a cheap one-mana anthem.

## Actual conditional slots and independent challenger pool

Protected identity contributors: `Unspeakable Symbol`, `Goblin Bombardment`, `Viscera Seer`, `Mikaeus`/`Murderous Redcap`, `Metallic Mimic`, `Gev`, `Thran Vigil`, `Yawgmoth`, `Puppeteer Clique`, `Sage of Fables` + Glen, the counter-seeded sac package, and the high-value death drain. `River Kelpie` (5 mana) is slow but draws for recurring permanents from graveyards, supports persist; `Gray Merchant` (5 mana) drains the whole pod by black devotion and can be reused; `Syr Konrad` (5 mana) punishes death and GY movement; do not label these automatic cuts by cost alone.

`Flayer of the Hatebound` at **{5}{R} (6 mana)** is the Stage A most plausible conditional slot: powerful undying and a source-graveyard creature-entry damage multiplier, but high early-hand clunk and only triggers when a creature enters **from your graveyard**. Its full ceiling is credible during repeated end-step recursions, but not whenever ordinary tokens enter. `Twilight Diviner` (3 mana) has a token-copy once-per-turn payoff and ETB surveil; tokens cannot themselves be Marchesa-recursed, reducing certain presumed infinite claims. `Spark Double` at 4 mana adds a counter if copying a creature and can copy Marchesa without the legend restriction; strong, conditional on target. `Iron Apprentice` is cheap, modular transfer preserves counters upon death but only puts counters on **target artifact creature** under the exact modular keyword, not any arbitrary creature (verify with current Oracle). `Braids, Arisen Nightmare` pressures resources at each end step and has sacrificial cost, but opponents choose whether to sac matching type, with life/card consequences.

**Preferred off-list hypothesis for testing: `+Juri, Master of the Revue / -Flayer of the Hatebound`** (not owner approved). Juri is {B}{R}, a 1/1 that gets a +1/+1 counter each time **you sacrifice a permanent** and deals damage equal to its power to **any target** on death. Existing sac outlets, fodder and Marchesa's counter-at-death condition naturally support early Juri growth, a death damage payoff, then Marchesa end-step return under your control. Compared with six-mana Flayer it deploys before Marchesa and self-seeds on normal sacrifice; it fits this commander's sacrifice/counter identity more directly than a generic tutor. However, Juri's power/counters reset when it returns as a new object, it needs other sacrifice events to self-seed each cycle, and it supplies only one damage trigger for its own death whereas Flayer can turn **every** eligible creature returning from your graveyard into damage. The payoff tradeoff is strategically material and unmeasured.

Alternative potential cuts for Juri: `Gray Merchant of Asphodel` (higher-cost whole-table life drain but high black devotion and ETB value) and `Syr Konrad, the Grim` (five-mana broader death/movement damage and sacrifice-independent clock). Both have stronger group impact than Juri in sufficiently developed pod, and should not be displaced on cost alone. Other speculative additions like `Kokusho, the Evening Star` (six mana repeatable life-drain) and `Nim Deathmantle` (four-mana repeatable paid return) are slower/require multiple extra conditions and are less compelling as a first challenger, though each is plausibly on identity. Do not force a high-power goodstuff shell.

**Finite scenario A (developing turn):** T2 Juri via B/R, T3 one sacrificial outlet + a fodder permanent, trigger Juri +1/+1; with Marchesa available later, sacrifice Juri itself (not counted toward its own growth unless a separate trigger already resolved), deal current power to target, queue return at next end step only if she saw it die with counter. Contrast Flayer stranded in hand until six mana but with outs via `Reanimate`/`Victimize` if discarded/put into GY. Without a fodder permanent Juri is merely a 1/1 that dies without Marchesa protection.

**Finite scenario B (end-step mass return):** Marchesa plus Unspeakable Symbol and a free sacrifice outlet with multiple counter-seeded creatures. Sacrifice two on your main phase (costing 3 life for each newly seeded counter), then at next end step both return (actual trigger handling may involve multiple delayed triggers), cause ETB/death-value effects and, if Flayer is present and cards return from **your** graveyard, Flayer deals damage equal to each returning creature's power to legal target. Juri only deals damage on its own death and doesn't multiply each return. An opponent can exile tracked graveyard cards before the delayed return or remove the outlet/Marchesa before future setup; an early `Rest in Peace` changes dies/zone outcomes. No empirical percentage claimed.

**Finite scenario C (undying/persist combo):** `Mikaeus` + `Murderous Redcap` + free sacrifice outlet, absent graveyard exile, creates successive persist/undying ETB pings if the creature has the required opposite/no counters each death. A well-timed exile or noncreature counterspell stops setup; `Solemnity` would change counters. Marchesa's endstep recursion is **not** part of the infinite on-stack engine. Additional counter seeders can either enable persist recursions by cancelling -1/-1 or **disable undying** by leaving +1/+1 on the dying creature. Formal rules snapshot/test required.

**Strongest counterargument to preferred cut:** Flayer is not just another expensive death-trigger body; in this particular Marchesa deck with many repeated graveyard entries it converts every restored `Gray Merchant`, `Puppeteer Clique`/`Murderous Redcap` and other owned creatures into target damage, while its own undying is an independent counter source. Replacing it with Juri reduces a multi-body ETB win route to one fragile creature's own death and may sacrifice the commander's natural power ceiling despite greater turn-two playability. `Reanimate` and `Victimize` reduce effective Flayer access cost in nontrivial hands. No calculated advantage for Juri is established.
