## Commander-first strategic conclusion

Marchesa {1}{U}{B}{R} gives all other creatures dethrone and schedules a **next-end-step** return for any creature you controlled that **died with +1/+1 counter present**, subject to Marchesa being there just before death and tracking the actual graveyard card. This is the distinctive **repeatable sacrifice/ETB counter-protection engine**. It is not immediate self-reanimating loop machinery. Dethrone only triggers attacking highest/tied life player, not their planeswalker. The 34-land multicolor fixing and sac/counter density favor repeated attrition and compact persist/undying finishes, not max-speed cEDH spellslinger. Alternative noncombat seeding (`Unspeakable Symbol` 3 life per counter, `Gev` needing opponents to have lost life, `Sage of Fables`, `Metallic Mimic`, `Forge`, `Uncivil Unrest` riot, `Thran Vigil`) protects her from counterless creature exposure but imposes real setup/life/mana costs.

**Retained win/interactivity packages with explicit paid limitations:**

- `Mikaeus, the Unhallowed` + non-Human `Murderous Redcap` + a free sacrifice outlet can cycle persist/undying if no blocking counter of the required type is present at death. Redcap ETB pings any target with source-paid recursion. Marchesa's own delayed return is neither necessary nor itself instantaneous. A redcap with a +1/+1 counter on death does **not** undy; with a -1/-1 counter it does **not** persist. Counter cancellation effects alter eligibility. Opponent exile/stack interaction can break the sequence.
- `Gev, Scaled Scorch` may give Redcap a +1 on return provided an opponent has lost life this turn; cancelling persist's -1 can enable repeated ETBs, but **targeting Redcap itself for damage to reset without an outlet does not automatically damage opponents**. Need `Goblin Bombardment`, `Blood Artist`, `Zulaport`, `Mayhem Devil` or another valid payoff for an opponent kill.
- `Glen Elendra Archmage` + `Sage of Fables` can pay {U} and sacrifice Glen to counter an opposing **noncreature** spell; persist returns with -1 and Wizard-entry +1 cancels. A separate {U} is needed for each use and opponent removal can break the engine. Other targeted abilities and the stack matter; do not call this free omnipotent counterspell recursion.
- `Spark Double` can enter copying Redcap with an additional +1, survive the legend rule if copying Marchesa, and help the copying/persist reset lines with Altar. It must have a valid creature to copy each time; opponent removal of its exemplar at the wrong window may stop it.
- `Flayer of the Hatebound`, despite six mana, is not filler. It enters from **your** graveyard through Marchesa/undying and causes relevant entering creature to deal power as target damage. With `Ashnod's Altar` (+{C}{C} per Flayer sacrifice), `Sage of Fables` ({2} remove its +1 counter and draw), and Flayer's undying, repeat a **finite** number of damage/draw cycles until opponents die, **before** running out of library, with responses and graveyard exile possible. This is a payoff/kill that lower-cost `Juri` does **not** substitute across the whole board. `Flayer` also multiplies every other Marchesa-returned owned creature, even without combo.
- `Sneak Attack`, `Gray Merchant`, `Puppeteer Clique`, `Twilight Diviner`, `River Kelpie`, `Satoru` and `Danny Pink` all have timing or replacement restrictions but are coherent engines. Diviner's extra token cannot itself recur after dying; Satoru triggers on nontoken qualifying grouped entries, not once per creature in a mass return; Danny's first counter-on-each-creature-per-turn draw is not an unrestricted infinite; `Yawgmoth` -1/-1 counters can cancel the very +1 protection Marchesa needs.
