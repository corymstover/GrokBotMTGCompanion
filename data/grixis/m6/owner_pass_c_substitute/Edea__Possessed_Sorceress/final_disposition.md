## Commander identity, natural ceiling and verified interactions

Edea is a `{2}{U}{B}{R}` 2/5 Human Warlock with **ward {2}**. At beginning of EACH own-turn combat, she attempts targeted temporary theft of an opponent-controlled creature, untaps/gives it haste until EOT. If an opponent-owned creature dies under our actual control, she draws one and returns the card to its **owner's** battlefield if still in graveyard. She creates commander-specific value from sacrificial borrowed bodies but does not simply keep those creatures after they die. Strong outlets `Viscera Seer`, Ashnod's/Phyrexian Altars, Goblin Bombardment, High Market, Phyrexian Tower, Grim Haruspex, Grave Pact and `Industrial Advancement` reward this cycle; `Full Throttle`, `Karlach`, Edea clones and stack multipliers create more borrow opportunities. **Bracket 4 — Optimized** natural ceiling, tags **Theft / Sacrifice / Control**, no generic Grixis homogenization.

**Critical timing:** `Irma, Part-Time Mutant` and Edea's theft triggers occur at the same beginning-of-combat window. Irma's target must be a creature we **already control when choosing target for the trigger**. Edea's future stolen target is not legal for Irma that same combat even if Edea's trigger resolves first. However, Irma copying Edea this combat gains Edea's theft ability **for later combats/turns**, and copying precombat borrowed/earlier-retained creatures or our own engines remains legal. The neutral criticism is a necessary rules safeguard, not proof Irma is a bad slot. `Thassa, Deep-Dwelling` or `Conjurer's Closet` can legally blink an *until-end-of-turn* stolen creature at beginning of own end step (before cleanup) and return the new object under our control indefinitely, without triggering Edea dies/draw. `Nightmare Shepherd` and `Chaos Shrine's Black Crystal` can exile an original dying borrowed card ahead of the Edea return if stack/zone requirements are satisfied, while the Edea draw still resolves. Opponents can remove Edea or steal targets, keep empty creature boards and protect relevant targets; ward2 has limited protection.
