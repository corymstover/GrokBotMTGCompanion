## Commander identity, real power ceiling and source-paid rules

This approved list is intentionally **Bracket 5 / cEDH** and centers on Marchesa's `{U}{B}{R}` crime-triggered, **optional {1} look at top two, one to hand, one to GY** effect. It does **not draw** a card, does not limit itself to once per turn, and needs another one mana **per qualifying crime**. Wizards [OTJ release notes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/outlaws-of-thunder-junction-release-notes) establish crime upon casting/activating/putting a targeted trigger on the stack, one crime per spell/ability even with many opposing targets, and initial targeting rather than later retargeting. This legal-target network explains the unusual `Spatial Binding`, `Hex Parasite`, `Lapis Lazuli Talisman`, `Spellskite` and historically printed targeted utility lands. The plan is **source-paid interactive selection** plus Underworld Breach graveyard supply, not a forced separate crime-themed beatdown.

The 99-card structural audit is source-bound in sealed A: 29 land / 15 artifact / seven enchantment / 35 instant-or-sorcery / 12 creature / one planeswalker. `Ad Nauseam`, `Necropotence`, cheap interaction and many fast sources support real cEDH timing; all three of `Thassa's Oracle`+`Tainted Pact`, `Underworld Breach`+`Lion's Eye Diamond`+`Brain Freeze`, and `Aphetto Alchemist` equipped with `Hawkeye's Bow` are meaningful parallel finishes subject to exact stack/life/summoning-sickness requirements. Marchesa need not be on battlefield for these win lines; her commander-specific value is selection/interaction efficiency and Breach fuel. Do not homogenize her into a generic Grixis storm pile simply because these familiar cEDH wins exist.

**Key cases independently challenged:**

1. `Lapis Lazuli Talisman`: the targeted triggered ability goes on stack each time **a player casts a blue spell**; targeting an opponent's permanent is a crime **before** the optional `{3}` to untap is considered on resolution. It can yield a Marchesa look-two/hand/GY conversion for **{1}** without paying the `{3}` fee. Stage A explicitly caught and corrected an initial shallow 'slow three-mana untap' undervaluation *before sealing*. Treat Lapis as **identity core**, even though its value is contingent on blue spells and legal target access. The old author also recognized this. Do not cut it for an ordinary rock on the false premise each crime costs `{4}`.
2. `Hawkeye's Bow` is a {R} equipment: equipped creature becoming tapped deals 1 to each opponent **without targeting** (so that damage is no crime). On `Aphetto Alchemist` (not summoning sick), tap the creature to activate its `{T}: Untap target artifact or creature`, target itself, untap on resolution, repeat under priority and interaction. Bounded as an arbitrarily large finite set of tap activations, not a no-response shortcut. This makes GrokBot's rejected `Intuition` for Bow a strongly protected CUT even though Bow itself is noncrime.
3. `Spatial Binding`: one life per opponent-permanent targeted activation, then Marchesa {1}; `Hex Parasite` X=0 can target opponents for black/2-life Phyrexian mana plus Marchesa {1}; `Spellskite` targets a real opposing stack object to commit a crime and may not achieve a legal target change. These restrictions materially bound actual win and card-access timing.
4. `Copy Enchantment` costs **{2}{U}**, and may copy an enchantment already on battlefield, including `Rhystic Study`, `Underworld Breach`, `Mystic Remora`, or an opponent's enchantment; cutting it for another rock sacrifices genuine ceiling.
