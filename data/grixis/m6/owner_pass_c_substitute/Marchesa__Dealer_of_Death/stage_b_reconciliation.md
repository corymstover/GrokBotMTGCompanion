### Adversarial play-state comparisons (not measured outcomes)

- **Blue stack pod:** Marchesa + Lapis in play and two opponent blue spells during turn cycle: Lapis can put two targeted triggered abilities on stack, each selecting an opponent's legal permanent, causing two separate Marchesa crimes; with {2} available and both triggers allowed to resolve, acquire two cards to hand and two to GY, even if declining both untap payments. Talisman over Copy helps pay {2} but replacing Lapis would erase this entire source of triggers. If opponents remove Marchesa before those triggers, later crimes are not monetized.
- **Early colored-pip shortage:** opening containing two lands, zero mana rocks and Copy Enchantment has difficulty reaching UBR plus Marchesa fee; swapping Copy for Talisman may fix the third colored mana on time when available land colors complement B/R. Countercase: a six-card London mulligan or high density of existing premium duals/fast rocks already fixes mana, and in a longer game Copy replicates a live Rhystic/Breach line while a spare Talisman is redundant. Need matched actual 29-land shuffles, no percentages invented.
- **Graveyard combo table:** opponent has an imminent Breach or reanimation target in GY, available priority and Marchesa live: Ghost Vacuum can exile precisely that target for {T}, simultaneously create a crime; `Scrabbling Claws` tap mode targets a player but *cannot* force a particular card to be exiled. Against no graveyard target, Ghost cannot activate; Talisman remains a mana source and Glasses/Claws can create crimes from valid player targets. Under `Rule of Law` or instant-speed artifact removal these effects do not equal automatic wins.
- **Aphetto/Bow board:** Bow equipped to Alchemist, creature no longer summoning sick: each tap/untap iteration deals one damage to every opponent. Opponents can interact before the untap activation resolves and deny repetition; a proposed Bow cut removes this path regardless of Marchesa's direct availability. Protect this until a challenger passes elevated burden.
