## Genuine agreements, disagreements, missed opportunities

1. **All reviewers agree on commander identity**: each opponent individually losing 3+ life in our own turn before Davros's end-step resolution, one Dalek token per ability resolution if any qualifies, opponent-selected draw-or-discard per qualifying opponent, artifact/Dalek menace swarm and actual hand-attrition rewards. Bracket 4 optimized is a defensible natural ceiling. Raw list SHA, 34 lands and 100/95 audit align. The Stage A `NEEDS TESTING` aligns with the *reconciliation* rather than the shallow advisory KEEP; the prior ChatGPT document's KEEP is expressly provisional pending better candidate evidence.
2. **Newly sealed challenger `+Fiery Confluence/-Weeping Angel`** was absent from the earlier final shortlist, making it a distinct, not pre-scripted proposition. `2RR`, sorcery; choosing opponent-burn mode twice yields 4 loss to EACH opponent; all three burn modes yield 6. It can allocate modes to artifact destruction or 1-damage sweep. Under source-paid 4 mana, it directly triggers Davros for all opponents even through empty enemy hands. **Counter:** the approved list already has `Boltwave` for `{R}`/3-to-each and `Maestros Charm` for `{U}{B}{R}`/3-life-loss-to-each plus 3 self-gain at **instant** speed. The Stage A underenumerated the **existing Maestros Charm** as a direct full-table threshold source; this corrects the *comparison* without rewriting sealed A. Extra four-mana sorcery redundancy may not beat a 3-mana instant-speed Alien artifact defender. If Fiery Confluence chooses an artifact-target mode and that target becomes illegal, all its targets may be illegal and **none of the untargeted damage modes happen**; burn-only modes avoid that specific failure. Therefore Fiery is a **secondary** trial candidate after the prior lower-cost, more role-distinct proposals.
3. **`+Unlicensed Disintegration/-Weeping Angel`** is still the strongest one-shot *role-converting* proposal for creature-heavy tables: `{1}{B}{R}` instant creature destruction, plus exactly 3 damage to **that one creature's controller** if the caster controls an artifact on resolution; fails to damage if target illegal or if no artifact. It can trigger one Davros opponent during our turn for a mana cost close to Angel's while dealing with a threat, but it is not all-table damage, cannot target empty creature boards and does not replace an Alien artifact body. Other cut: `This Is How It Ends` (verify its own exact utility before choosing) rather than automatically cutting free `Snuff Out`. Existing October author scenarios and Wizards *Kaladesh Release Notes* support conditional legal-target interaction. Not a proven upgrade.
4. **`+Ingenious Artillerist/-Sycorax Commander`, or `/-Weeping Angel`** is still the strongest plausible repeatable all-table artifact-engine challenger. Its `{2}{R}` 3/1 **nonartifact** body deals each opponent damage equal to the number of artifacts in each simultaneous entry event. Three separate artifact entries before Davros resolves cause 1+1+1 = 3 to EACH opponent; a single batch of three causes 3 each. One Davros end-step token **after** threshold was already checked cannot bootstrap itself. Against removal or sparse early artifacts, Artillerist does nothing, and a nonartifact 3/1 sacrifices Ashad/myriad/Padeem/artifact protection synergies. This is a substantive trade, not a ranking instruction. Earlier author test traces are **hypothetical payable sequences**, not independent gameplay measurements; reuse those bounded traces rather than rerun whole deck analysis.
5. **Stage A nuance / corrected omissions:** The previous deep review correctly highlights `Sygg, River Cutthroat`'s **intervening-if** beginning-end-step gate: if nobody has lost 3 by the moment the end step begins, Sygg's ability does not trigger even if `Wound Reflection` later raises the loss before Davros resolves. The Stage A discussed Davros/Reflection stacking but did not distinguish Sygg; this is an important newly acknowledged gate. It also only summarized `Entropic Battlecruiser` discard payoff: after being stationed to 1+, when a *real card is discarded*, that opponent loses 3, turning `Court of Ambition`/`Geier Reach Sanitarium` into reliable threshold enablers if the triggering object survives long enough; Davros's *own* resolving villainous choice cannot retroactively qualify that player for that same resolution. This argues against naive additions that merely duplicate existing damage.
6. **Rules corroborations, no invented novelty:** Stage A correctly identified `Roaming Throne` needing Alien or Scientist (Davros is not a Dalek), Scientist Supreme can copy artifact-source Davros's whole trigger for 2 life once on your turn, Wound/Artist ordering matters, and an empty-handed opponent may choose to discard nothing. Both the prior authored audit and/or reconciliation already considered most of these; they are agreement, not new discoveries. `Doomsday Confluence` at `XXB` is a deliberately expensive Dalek/discard engine; `Cyberman Patrol`'s afflict 3 and `Weeping Angel`'s artifact body are not disposable generic slots.

## Actual rule anchors and bounded resolution traces

- Wizards *Doctor Who Mechanics* confirms the empty-hand Davros choice and sequential opponent decisions; *Doctor Who Release Notes* covers Myriad/Auton Soldier, Ashad and other card-specific mechanics. `Davros` life-loss qualification checks the end-step ability when it resolves; removal before the beginning-of-step blocks that trigger while later removal ordinarily does not.
- Wizards *Kaladesh Release Notes* explicitly confirms `Unlicensed Disintegration`: legal target but indestructible still deals 3 with an artifact; **illegal target means no damage**. Link: `https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/kaladesh-release-notes-2016-09-16`.
- Wizards *Commander Masters Release Notes* confirms Confluences choose modes on casting, can repeat each and cannot switch modes on a copy. Link: `https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/commander-masters-release-notes`.
- Wizards *Marvel Super Heroes Release Notes* confirms Scientist Supreme of A.I.M. copies artifact-source triggered/activated abilities, including a trigger with no targets; it does not grant separate original triggers. Link: `https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/marvel-super-heroes-release-notes`.
- `Ingenious Artillerist` current text is in 2022 Baldur's Gate card gallery/card database. No simulation program models every opponent choice, stacked event, target loss or removal response in this deck. Actual fresh measured-playtest count remains **zero**.

**Trace F1 (Fiery timing):** Our four accessible mana includes `{R}{R}`. With Davros alive, before our end step cast Fiery choosing 2 opponent-damage mode twice + 2 opponent-damage again (total 6 each), no targets. Each living opponent that takes all damage qualifies; Davros produces one token and each independently chooses. With a third artifact-destroy mode instead, burn deals 4 each only if the entire spell still resolves. Counterspell/prevention can stop qualification. Relative to `{R}` Boltwave and `UBR` Maestros Charm, this is expensive redundancy, not proof of increased win rate.

**Trace U1 (Unlicensed):** During our main phase with an artifact in play, target enemy A's creature with legal `1BR` instant; if it remains legal and an artifact survives until resolution, A's creature is destroyed (if destructible) and A takes 3; Davros then qualifies A at end step. If target is bounced before resolution, the spell has no remaining legal target and **A takes 0**. B/C remain unqualified unless other actions made them lose 3.

**Trace I1 (Artillerist):** Artillerist already in play before end step; cast/create three individual artifacts earlier this turn, triggers for one each to A/B/C. All lose cumulative 3 and qualify. If it is removed before the second entry, only first 1 occurs, insufficient. If the sole artifact generated is Davros's own end-step Dalek, that is **after** successful qualification, not a way to retroactively activate the trigger.

**Trace E1 (end-step stack):** If A earlier lost 2 and B only 1, stack Davros under `Wound Reflection`; Reflection resolves first causing A another 2 (A total4), B another1 (B total2). Davros only qualifies A. But if no one had lost 3 **as the step began**, Sygg's separate intervening-if trigger was never put on the stack. Order matters; no blanket extra draw claim.
