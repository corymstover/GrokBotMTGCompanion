## Verdict — NEEDS TESTING; approved 100 stays locked

The deck is a distinctive **Bracket 4 — Optimized** Davros/Doctor Who villainous-choice build: qualifying individual opponents for 3+ life loss on *our* turn, fabricating one menace Dalek per Davros end-step trigger resolution, forcing owner-choice draws/discards, and exploiting artifact creature attacks and actual discards. `Boltwave` (`R`, 3 to each), `Maestros Charm` (`UBR`, 3 life loss to each), `Loyal Subordinate` and combat-afflict routes give baseline eligibility, while `Wound Reflection`, `Perforating Artist`, `Entropic Battlecruiser` and opponent choices create complex timing edges. The 34-land/7-rock colored-base is promising but non-guaranteed given `UBR` requirement, conditional Mox Opal/Fellwar and several colorless utility lands.

**Stage A's genuinely separate challenger** was `+Fiery Confluence/-Weeping Angel`: repeatable choice of 2-damage-to-each mode can deal 4 or 6 to the whole table for `2RR`, and third mode options interact with creatures/artifacts. Stage B correctly demoted it from primary testing priority because **existing Boltwave and Maestros Charm already provide cheaper direct across-table qualification**, while losing Weeping Angel sacrifices 3-mana flash combat protection, an Alien/artifact body, and the Doctor Who/afflict/myriad ecosystem. Still worth one bounded comparison if resources allow; no demonstrated upgrade.

**Most consequential prior unresolved challengers**, not accepted additions:

1. `+Unlicensed Disintegration/-Weeping Angel` or alternative cut `This Is How It Ends`: `{1}{B}{R}` instant creature kill plus 3 damage to that creature's **controller** *if you control an artifact at resolution*. It qualifies just one opponent on **our** turn and needs a legal creature target; bounce of the target makes the entire spell ineffective. In creature-rich meta its interaction+life threshold may outdo Weeping Angel; otherwise no.
2. `+Ingenious Artillerist/-Sycorax Commander` or alternate cut `Weeping Angel`: `{2}{R}` fragile 3/1 nonartifact, each artifact ETB event damages all opponents for the quantity in that event. Three artifact ETBs before the end-step produce 3 life lost by each. Davros's *own post-threshold* Dalek token is too late to create the original qualifying loss. Repeatable synergy is real but loses some artifact/Doctor Who body value and is removal-sensitive.
3. `+Fiery Confluence/-Weeping Angel`: Stage A challenger after the above exact comparisons, potentially `/-Perforating Artist` on a separate targeted table. Sorcery-speed `RR`, mana and already-present group-loss redundancy are meaningful costs.

**Interaction checks with source-bound constraints:** Davros is an **Alien Scientist artifact creature, not a Dalek**; Roaming Throne names Alien/Scientist if doubling it. Roaming Throne generates an additional Davros trigger, `Strionic Resonator`/`Scientist Supreme of A.I.M.` copy the whole artifact-source trigger, and `The Valeyard` repeats the affected opponent's **villainous choice**, not the token creation. `Wound Reflection` can resolve before Davros at the beginning of our end step, bringing earlier opponent 2-loss to 4; `Sygg, River Cutthroat` has a separate start-of-step intervening-if gate and cannot begin triggering retroactively. With an empty hand, an affected opponent may elect Davros's discard option for no effect; neither a drawn card nor a discarded card is automatic. On-target damage from Unlicensed Disintegration fails if the target is illegal on resolution; opposing targeted removal can stop a would-be engine before triggering, and sweeper/blocker choices constrain 3/3 Dalek combat. Stage B holds exact rule sources and bounded response traces.

**Most credible argument against changing anything:** The Oct14 approval followed thorough whole-list verification, the original author’s full 95-name/100-copy assessment already examined substantial alternatives, and the new putative additions all cost either artifact/thematic identity, mana, defense, or opponent-independent reliability. The baseline already has effective one-card group-loss enablers; superficial increases in theoretical damage without paired timing and response tests do not justify breaking the lock. Conversely, a 3-mana instant answer that also grants an opponent's 3-life qualification or a repeatable artifact-entry burn piece may be **genuine** conditional improvements, so claiming unqualified KEEP would overstate existing evidence.
