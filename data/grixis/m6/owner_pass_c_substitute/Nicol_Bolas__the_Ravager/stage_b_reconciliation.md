## 4. Adjudicate principal historical disagreement: +Aetheric Amplifier / -The Eldest Reborn

**Oracle/cost requirements, bounded not measured:** Aetheric Amplifier {3} Artifact, {T}: add one mana of any color; separate {4},{T}: choose to double the number of counters on target permanent or on yourself, activate only as a sorcery. It is NOT a passive doubling replacement and does not automatically double every ETB planeswalker. Aetheric is a repeatable colored mana source on turns when not tapping to double, and an on-theme source of loyalty doubling when a planeswalker survives and four other mana is actually available. It is itself a noncreature artifact which Tezzeret the Seeker can tutor to battlefield at appropriate loyalty/payment, subject to targeting/setup. It becomes a true commander-first source when targeting transformed Arisen rather than a generic artifact combo.

**Exact transformational sequence (assume available resources and no response):**
1. Pay {1}{U}{B}{R} to cast front Ravager, ETB each opponent discards one. Later/if on table, during sorcery main phase pay {4}{U}{B}{R} for exile-and-return Arisen as NEW permanent with 7 loyalty, or {2}{U}{B}{R} if Training Grounds was present on front's activated ability and remains applicable during activation.
2. Have Amplifier already on battlefield or pay {3} to cast it, and have **four generic mana from elsewhere**; it cannot tap to produce 1 mana and tap again for counter doubling without untap assistance. Activate {4},{T} targeting *Arisen the planeswalker*, on sorcery timing; 7→14 loyalty if legal target remains. This is not the DFC casting step and does not grant extra loyalty activation.
3. If Arisen has **not used a loyalty ability this turn** and still survives with at least 12 loyalty, Arisen -12 can exile all but the bottom card of one target player's library. That player is NOT eliminated merely because library is now one card; a subsequent required draw from EMPTY library may eliminate them. Opponents can respond to activation or remove/protect/replace target effects. Against three players, only one library is affected per ultimate absent copies/loops; this is NOT automatic table lethal.
4. Without Amplifier, the deck has Deepglow Skate as another seven-to-fourteen route, The Elderspell, and proliferate to accelerate; Amplifier improves repeatability and mana fixing without forcing a change, but many turns it is simply a 3-mana rock while walkers aren't online.
5. With opponent discard/creature sacrifice and reanimation value, current The Eldest Reborn five-mana Saga contributes attrition independently of the transformed commander; removing it may reduce defenses in creature-heavy pods and break the Bolas story package. It's slower and cannot double loyalty. Aetheric is a legitimate thematic challenge but cannot claim a consistent win increase without source-paid matchup distributions.

**Conflict verdict:** The original author favors +Aetheric/-Eldest for owner consideration, GrokBot recommends KEEP (no exact direct negative on the candidate). Reconciliation correctly labeled NEEDS TESTING; neither side performed effect-aware paired games. This remains a priority-1 genuine disputed improvement. Aetheric is not illegal/irrelevant by mechanics and deserves exact paid scenarios, but there is no proven owner-ready strict upgrade as of this Stage B. No owner deck changes.

**Candidate-specific opponent counterplay and false simplifications:** Artifact exile/removal before doubling, planeswalker removal in response to activation or copied ultimate, Tezzeret ETB/tutor failure or tapped status, lack of separate four generic for doubling, no transformed Arisen on table, uncastable seven-mana transformation due colors, lack of active loyalty ability this turn, library exiling not lethal absent follow-up draw. Quest to ultimate may occur without Amplifier via Deepglow Skate/The Elderspell, diluting incremental benefit. The 34-land mana model cannot offer reliable statistics until DFC bug corrected.

## 5. New sealed neutral Savor/Whale challenger after comparison

Savor the Moment {1}{U}{U} gives extra turn after this one but **skips the untap step** of that turn. Multiple surviving walkers can generally each activate once in that extra turn; it is not a second loyalty activation in the original turn, not a free untap of Chain Veil, not a free seven-mana transform, and not automatic extra turn recursion. With Teferi, Temporal Archmage untapping permanents and existing mana sources, some extra-turn hands improve markedly; absent that, tapped mana and exposed planeswalkers can make it inferior. Dreamtide Whale {2}{U}, 7/5 with vanishing 2, proliferates when *any player casts second spell in a turn*, potentially on opponent turns and retaining itself via its time counters. In fast-spell tables with surviving Whale this is a powerful repeatable loyalty engine and blocker; in one-spell slow tables it dies soon. Grok named it weak but did not model this exact add/cut. No head-to-head testing.

**Substantive new case / lower priority:** +Savor/-Dreamtide Whale is identity-plausible, but existing Deepglow/Chain Veil/Talent already permit extra loyalty activations and skip-untap may undermine defenses. The **strongest counterargument** is a controlled board with many walkers, untouched mana sources and Chain Veil where Savor's extra turn yields several fresh loyalty abilities and attack-step resets. Need actual source-paid scenarios to decide; currently baseline KEEP is strategically sound and this is second tier behind the Aetheric owner dispute.
