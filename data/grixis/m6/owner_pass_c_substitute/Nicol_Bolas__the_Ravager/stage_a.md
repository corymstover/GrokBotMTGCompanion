## 2. Identity, interaction role and natural ceiling

The actual commander is four mana ({1}{U}{B}{R}), flying Elder Dragon. On entering, **each opponent discards one card**; this is not all-hand discard like original Nicol Bolas and occurs on entering, including legitimate re-entries if the creature returns from exile transformed? On transformation, the returned object is a **planeswalker** and the front-face creature ETB text is not active, so its creature-facing opponent-discard ETB should NOT trigger when it enters transformed. Its activated {4}{U}{B}{R} sorcery-speed ability exiles the creature and returns it transformed under owner's control as planeswalker Nicol Bolas, the Arisen, with seven starting loyalty. Its Arisen face has +2 draw two, -3 deal ten to creature/planeswalker, -4 reanimate any creature or planeswalker from a graveyard under your control, -12 exile all but bottom card of target player's library. It does **not** immediately activate for zero or inherently take extra turns; activating once per normal turn is the baseline and requires a legal planeswalker already on battlefield.

Deck identity is overt Bolas planeswalker-superfriends: all named Bolas variants (Dragon-God, God-Pharaoh, original Nicol Bolas Planeswalker, Deceiver, Ravager/Arisen), Jace, Tezzeret, Teferi, Vraska, Liliana, and others; loyalty and proliferate synergies from Flux Channeler, Inexorable Tide, Tekuthal, Tezzeret's Gambit, Dreamtide Whale, Karn's Bastion, Ripples of Potential, Ichormoon Gauntlet; repeated activations through The Chain Veil, Rings of Brighthearth, Teferi Temporal Archmage and Doubling-style growth from Deepglow Skate. The commander-first mission is to turn a castable four-mana Bolas discard body into a protected transformed, accumulating long-game Nicol planeswalker supported by distinct other Bolas planeswalkers, creating board and resource asymmetry without turning into a generic Grixis combo shell. Optimized natural **Bracket 4 candidate**, not certified and not cEDH by default: multiple legendary expensive walkers require board defense and often seven mana to transform.

The shell includes planeswalker-specific infrastructure:
- **The Chain Veil** grants additional loyalty ability activation windows to planeswalkers this turn after paying 4/tapping; it does not by itself untap the Veil or generate mana. Teferi, Temporal Archmage -1 can untap up to four target permanents and may interact with Chain Veil, mana rocks/lands, potentially repeating if costs and activations resolve. Requiring actual paid mana and exact eligible targets is essential before saying infinite activations.
- **Ichormoon Gauntlet** gives planeswalkers an optional zero loyalty proliferate ability and a -12 extra-turn loyalty ability, and adds loyalty to a planeswalker when casting noncreature spells. It does not grant loyalty counters to the transforming front creature before it becomes planeswalker, and it is not a guarantee of a turn loop. **Deepglow Skate** can double existing loyalty counters of controlled walkers on entry; **Tekuthal** adds extra proliferate but not unrestricted +loyalty without having counters.
- **The Elderspell** can destroy planeswalkers and add two loyalty counters per destroyed planeswalker to one you control; if opponents have none it may require sacrificing own planeswalkers to enable an ultimate. Enemy counterplay and casting/targeting windows matter.
- **Sanctum Lurker** from Reality Fracture ({2}{B}) gives all your planeswalkers a +2 ping/drain loyalty ability and suppresses being put in graveyard solely for zero loyalty while on battlefield. Its built-in empower Jace 1 is source-card specific; do not treat it as a generic proliferate card. It can protect a freshly transformed Arisen from immediate zero-loyalty state-based loss but does NOT allow paying a negative loyalty cost for free when insufficient counters exist.
- **Jaya's Phoenix**, a five-mana flying haste 3/3, copies the next loyalty ability activated after connecting in combat and re-enters from graveyard when a planeswalker spell is cast. It is a real separate combat-dependency engine, not generic filler.

Natural weaknesses: early walkers are vulnerable to wide combat boards; 34 lands may demand fast rocks and tutor-heavy starts; early front Ravager ETB one-card discard is modest relative to true game-ending transforms; 7-mana sorcery-speed activation to Arisen can be exposed to instant removal before activation, or planeswalker attacked immediately after successful transform; extra proliferate without surviving walkers/timed stack windows adds no value. Defensive sweepers Damnation/Toxic Deluge/Rollick and counters protect the plan but must not be removed for pure loyalty greed.

## 4. Independent weak-slot and proposed challenger (not owner ready)

**Primary new neutral testing hypothesis:** **+1 Savor the Moment / -1 Dreamtide Whale.** Savor ({1}{U}{U}, sorcery) grants a genuine extra turn **skipping only that turn's untap step**, not skipping upkeep/draw/main phases or automatically untapping mana. The entire superfriends board gets another turn-based opportunity to use each planeswalker's loyalty ability once, +2 and -2 loyalty increments across multiple walkers, another upkeep/land play/draw step, and another set of noncreature-spell triggers if mana still available. This is more aligned with the commander being a **planeswalker** than a generic damage spell, and costs less than five-plus-mana support.

`Dreamtide Whale` {2}{U}, 7/5, vanishing 2, proliferates whenever **ANY player casts their SECOND spell of the turn**, potentially at each opponent turn; no haste/flying is printed. It can be a huge three-mana defense, and proliferating its own time counters may prolong its board stay under active spells. However in low-spell-count/one-spell-per-turn attrition pods, it disappears after two upkeeps without repeatedly triggering and its actual loyalty contribution can be zero. Savor removes passive 7/5 bulk but guarantees an extra loyalty activation cycle if walkers exist and survive, with explicit skip-untap drawback. The trade is meaningful in a planeswalker-heavy plan; no "strict better" claim follows.

**Best counterargument against my favorite:** Savor consumes UU and three mana, yet with no surviving planeswalkers it's just an extra draw and land-drop turn with **no untap**; the existing deck already has high-cost seven-mana transformation and many loyalty expenses, so losing an untap can be catastrophic if tapping out for Savor leaves shields down and opponents act on its resolution/end. Dreamtide Whale is a 7/5 for three that can block large attackers, can produce proliferation repeatedly on opponents' spell chains, and can sustain its own vanishing counters in fast-spell tables. Cutting it may reduce both battlefield survival and loyalty acceleration exactly where superfriends needs support. The new proposal requires *matched* effect-aware evaluation and actual Oracle legality; baseline KEEP has priority.

**Alternative cuts to scrutinize without forcing them:**
- `Jaya's Phoenix` can be a fragile 5MV creature with attack-connection requirement for copying loyalty, but it has haste/flying and recurs whenever a planeswalker spell is cast, so replacing its combat-based copier with extra turn may remove resiliency.
- `Nicol Bolas, the Deceiver` is high-MV and its rate may be lower in fast pods, but it is an iconic Nicol planeswalker in a character-first encyclopedia. Cutting it for a generic Time Walk would erode distinction; not a preferred cut.
- `Teferi, Master of Time` can use loyalty on ANY player's turn, making extra ordinary player turns less marginally valuable for it than for other walkers. Removing it would still lose many per-rotation activations; no.
- `Dreamtide Whale` remains the most directly comparable creature-based, opponent-spell-conditional loyalty accelerator rather than cutting a premium land, counterspell, or identity centerpiece.
- `Experimental Augury` (1U instant look top3, select one and proliferate) is another low-cost efficient challenger in color, but it produces one proliferate and selection rather than an entire extra turn; compare as option if Savor skip-untap dominates.

## 5. Timing, play and opponent counterplay

**A. Front ETB / transform:** Pay 1UBR for Ravager, ETB each opponent discards a card as one triggered ability. Hold seven additional mana of exactly 4UBR at a sorcery-speed main phase, with priority and the creature on battlefield, to transform. Removal before activation (opponent has response if front is on stack? after resolution priority to active player can activate before opponent priority on an empty stack under sorcery conditions) depends on sequencing: the active player gets priority after ETB resolved; if activation legal, paying is not interruptible, but players can respond to the activation on stack and remove Ravager before it exiles/returns. Exact CR last-known timing affects transform fizzles. Recast from zone not transformed face; transforming does not start fresh commander tax on its own.

**B. Loyalty choice and response:** Newly entered transformed Arisen can use one loyalty ability during your current main phase with stack empty and priority if still controlled, unlike a creature's tap ability requiring haste; but if you already used another loyalty ability of that same new planeswalker object this turn, normally restricted. Opponents can respond to the actual activated loyalty ability with removal but its effect may resolve independently when source removed (legal targets permit). Planeswalker ward/hexproof and targeted effect legality matter.

**C. Savor extra-turn:** Without untapping lands/rocks during its extra turn, you can draw, play a land, cast spells with resources still untapped or mana from alternative sources, and activate planeswalkers once again. You can activate more than one planeswalker across the extra turn, but not two loyalties of the *same* walker in a single turn absent Chain Veil/other exception. Skip untap must be accounted for in shield availability and follow-on real opponent turns. If an opponent counters Savor, no extra turn or card advance occurs.

**D. Whale proliferation:** Trigger occurs after a player casts their second spell each turn, goes on stack above that spell. Can proliferate your walkers and own Whale vanishing time counters; controller chooses which qualifying permanents/players get counters. If opponent removes whale before second cast, no trigger; if ability already triggered, later removing Whale doesn't necessarily remove it from stack. Without a second spell, Whale's upkeep vanishing clock runs out; with timely proliferate you can extend life.

**E. Immediate board pressure:** 7/5 Whale blocks many large creatures to guard walkers, but has no flying/reach by default despite its art/name; actual fliers can strike walkers. The deck's Damnation/Toxic Deluge and several spot/free counters remain strategically essential.
