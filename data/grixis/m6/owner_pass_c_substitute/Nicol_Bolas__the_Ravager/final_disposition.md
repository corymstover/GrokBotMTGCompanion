## Commander-first power and plan

Four-mana ({1}{U}{B}{R}) flying Elder Dragon enters as creature and makes each opponent discard ONE card. An expensive **{4}{U}{B}{R}** sorcery-speed activated ability exiles the creature and returns him transformed as the new seven-loyalty planeswalker Nicol Bolas, the Arisen, with +2 draw2, -3 ten damage to creature/walker, -4 reanimation of creature/walker card from a graveyard, and -12 leave just the bottom card of target library. Activation can cost {2}{U}{B}{R} with existing Training Grounds, not 7 in every opening. The transformation is not a spell cast or commander tax event. A true four-mana ETB discard engine/repeated blink/planeswalker loyalty superfriends is the distinct identity; the related original Elder Dragon 8MV hand-discard-on-damage list is another deck entirely.

Preserve unique Bolas-franchise planeswalkers and their supporting mechanics rather than a homogenized Grixis turbo shell. This list contains five Bolas variants plus Teferi/Jace/Liliana/Vraska/Tezzeret, Deepglow Skate and The Elderspell loyalty leaps, proliferate from Whale/Flux/Tekuthal/Tide/Karn's Bastion, copied and repeated abilities via The Chain Veil/Rings/Rowan's Talent/Jaya's Phoenix, transformed/discard blink via Displacer Kitten, Sanctum Lurker preserving zero-loyalty walkers and providing a [+2] drain mode, and sufficient shields to resist creature combat. Existing optimized Bracket 4 is a plausible natural power ceiling, not a new measured calibration.

### Priority 1 — Aetheric Amplifier versus The Eldest Reborn

**Exact 1:1 candidate (research, NOT owner-ready):** +1 Aetheric Amplifier ({3} artifact), -1 The Eldest Reborn ({4}{B} Saga). Amplifier taps for a mana of any color on turns not used for its {4},{T} double-counters sorcery activation, addresses UBR source access, and when Arisen enters with seven loyalty can double to 14 if the controller has *four other generic mana* and a ready Amplifier. Arisen then may -12 one player's library to one card if loyalty ability hasn't been used and sorcery timing. Tezzeret the Seeker can tutor an artifact with sufficient loyalty, but tutor/tap timing, artifact removal and partial shield mana matter. Choosing Amplifier's mana tap AND counter doubling on same turn is normally impossible without untapping it. Sorcery-speed doubling gives opponents another response window. The Eldest Reborn provides creature sacrifice/discard and reanimation independently of Arisen across Saga chapters, stronger when planeswalker repeatedly removed or attacker boards overwhelming. Need compare exact opportunity cost, first successful Arisen transform and loyalty use, actual win turn, not "one extra ultimate equals instant victory." The original author's preference is credible; GrokBot KEEP is also defensible absent actual outcome evidence.

**Strongest counterargument to baseline KEEP:** Amplifier is lower MV, on-theme repeatable loyalty engine and mana fixing; the high-MV Saga's effects unfold over turns and may lose to faster opponents. Missing an already available commander-ultimate enabler can be a substantive opportunity cost.

**Strongest counterargument to swap:** Ravager must first cast safely for 1UBR and transform by paid 4UBR (or 2UBR with Grounds). If no Arisen survives, Amplifier is mainly a 3MV mana rock in a list with 13 fast sources; with existing Skate/The Elderspell it may be redundant. A 7→14 to -12 still requires follow-up draw/extra ultimate to finish enemies. Eldest's sacrifice/reanimate line may win attrition/defend walkers without Arisen and contributes specific Bolas flavor. Existing artifacts may invite sweepers. No measured outcome favors the change yet.
