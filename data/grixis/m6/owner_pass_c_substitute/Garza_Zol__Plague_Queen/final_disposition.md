## Commander-first strategic judgment

Garza is a seven-mana 5/5 flying-haste creature that grows from creatures **she personally damaged this turn** dying, and draws on **combat damage to a player**. Her strongest on-identity route is deathtouch-enchanced Garza-sourced damage through Basilisk Collar, Quietus Spike, Thornbite Staff, Fall of the Hammer, Chandra's Ignition and Nova Flame; counters/physical equipment and egress enable lethal combat. Grafted Exoskeleton gives a separate infect route; Kediss and Curiosity dramatically extend Garza-sourced damage/card triggers; Sword of Feast and Famine plus Aggravated Assault can produce additional combat phases when land mana and hits actually support them. Costly setup, U/B/R access, exile/edict/tax response and blocker interaction constrain the **Bracket 4 optimized/combat hypothesis**. Generic unrelated cEDH packages would erase what makes Garza distinct.

**Official/rules findings (targeted, not a fresh 100-card Oracle sweep):**

1. **Critical Stage A error corrected:** per official [Wizards Commander Legends release notes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/commander-legends-release-notes-2020-11-06), Kediss causes **Garza to deal additional noncombat damage**, not Kediss herself. It does **not** count as commander *combat* damage to the other opponents, but it carries Garza's infect/lifelink and is seen by Curiosity, unlike Garza's own combat-only draw. This was independently first misread in Stage A and expressly corrected after unblinding; immutable first-pass record preserves the mistake.
2. **Thornbite Staff is not free infinite ping:** tapping Garza costs {2} each time; death can untap but does not produce {2}. Death-triggered Garza counters require her own prior creature damage and a real death. A target can be protected; any creature death can grant Staff untap separately.
3. [Wizards Nova Flame release notes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/marvel-super-heroes-release-notes): {X}{2}{R}{R} adds X counters, then Garza damages **each other creature**. Target removal can blank the entire sorcery; Chandra's Ignition additionally damages opponents, noncombat. They trigger Garza counter gains from actually damaged dying creatures (including own creatures for Nova) but are not combat-damage draw or commander damage.
4. **Scythe of the Wretched** moves from Garza to a creature it returns, reducing repeat power/equip while rewarding her creature-damage deaths. **Robe of the Archmagi** requires {4} equip for a Vampire Noble; one-mana tribal equip option doesn't apply. **Quietus Spike** halves life via life loss, not commander damage, but adds important reusable deathtouch.
5. **Aggravated Assault** must be activated in a main phase with real `{3}{R}{R}` available; Sword of Feast and Famine depends on a valid player combat hit and land untap. Grafted Exoskeleton detaching sacrifices Garza. Opponents can interact before equip/damage and between double-strike damage steps.

## Exact outstanding improvement, alternatives, and adversarial balance

**Primary challenger for controlled testing: `+Fireshrieker / -Inquisitor's Flail`.** Fireshrieker grants repeatable double strike for `3 cast + 2 equip`, compared with Flail's `2 cast + 2 equip` and combat-damage doubling. At unmodified 5/5 and fully unblocked, either produces ten total combat commander damage. Fireshrieker can produce two Garza draw triggers, two Kediss/Garza-sourced splash events, and first-strike protection against normal five-toughness blockers. Flail costs one fewer mana and doubles damage **in one packet**, enabling lethal poison/commander damage before a responder can remove Garza between strike steps. With Exoskeleton, Flail may secure an infect table kill more safely at the immediate damage event; it also dangerously doubles damage dealt to Garza by blockers. **This is a credible tension, not a proven global upgrade.**

**Other considered exact cuts:** `+Fireshrieker / -Robe of the Archmagi` loses exceptional post-hit draw despite high equip cost; `+Fireshrieker / -Quietus Spike` loses one of two repeatable deathtouch sources and a creature-kill payoff. Other possible double strike challengers Lizard Blades and Temur Battle Rage differ in creature fragility, deployment, trample and one-shot cost; no full Oracle/role comparison was completed for these alternative cards. The original full authored review already documented and reasonably declined pinger/deathtouch candidates Viridian Longbow, Gorgon's Head, Rune of Mortality and Burning Anger, primarily due to opportunity costs of Scythe capture, Quietus deathtouch, and Robe card flow. Do not reopen them all reflexively.

**Strongest counterargument to the challenger:** Garza already has Psychotic Fury as a cheap cantripping double-strike trick, and Flail's low mana + single lethal infect packet is uniquely valuable. Fireshrieker creates an extra vulnerability window and must compete with commander casting/protection. Even though its blocked-combat survival and extra hits are attractive, repeated equipment deployment might be *less* effective than protecting the existing Flail explosive line. A faithful commander-first KEEP remains quite plausible.
