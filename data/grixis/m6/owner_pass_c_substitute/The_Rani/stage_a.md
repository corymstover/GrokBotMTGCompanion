## Candidate hypothesis, countercase, and overlap
Fresh direct question: **+Curse of Verbosity / -Maestros Confluence**. Curse of Verbosity is a 3-mana blue Curse encouraging opponents to attack enchanted player and may draw cards for each relevant combat; closer to the politics Aura density, cheaper than five-mana modal Confluence. Countercase: Curse of Verbosity is NOT itself a goad Aura nor a guaranteed Rani Clue trigger; enchanting players is distinct from enchanting opposing creatures, and it may incentivize opponent attacks without changing legal goad constraints. Confluence may generate more flexible damage, removal/discard or repeat card effects while maintaining colored UBR options and reactive flexibility. An alternative potential cut Five Hundred Year Diary would sacrifice high-value Clue mana/value synergy; avoid generic trimming of expensive identity pieces. Existing red Curse of Opulence has overlapping player-attack incentive but makes Gold, not Clues; Curse of Verbosity would be a second player Curse, not automatically additional Rani engine redundancy.
