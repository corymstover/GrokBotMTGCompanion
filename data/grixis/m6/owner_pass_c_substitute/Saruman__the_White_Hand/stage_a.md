## Commander identity, natural ceiling and whole-list profile
**Saruman, the White Hand** is a {1}{U}{B}{R} **2/5 Avatar Wizard**, not the five-mana "Saruman the White." Each **noncreature spell CAST** produces `amass Orcs X`, where X equals that spell's **mana value**, not necessarily mana paid. Goblins and Orcs you control have ward {2}. This genuinely distinct identity is **single growing Orc/Goblin Army + spell-casting + counter-ping and Army-to-damage finish**, not merely a generic Grixis Storm deck or low-MV spells-only pile. Natural expected ceiling **optimized Bracket 4** with high-damage conditional finish; there is no measured evidence yet that it plays as consistent cEDH Bracket 5.

**Mana and development:** The curve is supported by dedicated lands (six fetches, typed UBR dual/shock, multiplayer Luxury Suite/Training Center/Morphic Pool, Command Tower/Exotic Orchard/City/Confluence), three Talismans, Arcane Signet, Fellwar Stone, Sol Ring, Ancient Tomb, mana-from-spells Jeska's Will/Frantic Search/Mana Geyser/Storm-Kiln Artist. Commander costs *four* with exact **one blue, one black, one red**. Black-heavy finishers and double-blue counters can compete; some land-faced MDFCs require life or enter tapped. The deck has neither broad zero-cost turbo rock density nor Rograkh's free-commander rituals; it wants to cast Saruman and **still** have noncreature spells and red/pip access to profit. The no-commandER opening is mostly a fair midrange spellslinger.

**Engines/payoffs:** (a) all cheap noncreature spells, counters, draw/tutors, Talismans, The One Ring/All Will Be One, Mizzix's Mastery, Dig Through Time, Treasure Cruise and **Volcanic Salvo** all cast and yield an Army amass equal to mana VALUE; delve or cost reduction lowers mana paid without lowering mana value. An X in a spell's mana cost contributes to mana value **on the stack according to chosen X**; Shatterskull Smashing is not always printed-static X=0. (b) Harmonic Prodigy (Wizard trigger doubling), Wizard's Staff (equipped Wizard's triggered ability additional time) and Veyran, Voice of Duality (doubles applicable instant/sorcery cast/copy-triggered permanent abilities) multiply Saruman's cast events when their actual restrictions hold; copying a spell on the stack without CASTING does **not** create a new Saruman trigger. (c) Mauhúr, Uruk-hai Captain, Bothersome Noisemaker (amass Goblins 1 upon noncreature casts), The Great Goblin (fixed **2 damage to target opponent each separate event of putting counter(s) on Army/Goblin/Orc), All Will Be One (counter count damage), The Ozolith (preserves counters when a creature leaves), Sauron, the Dark Lord and The Black Gate make tribal attack and counter-value payoffs. Repeated amass usually grows the SAME Army; do not count each amass as a new-token ETB.
**Conversion**: Orcish Siegemaster provides trample to Orc/Goblin creatures; The Black Gate can help force an attack connection; Chandra's Ignition hits all opponents and other creatures using one creature's power; Soul's Fire and Kazuul's Fury convert accumulated Army power to direct damage (with different trigger timing); Widespread Brutality is Army-based selective wipe. This is a real primary Army plan, not a pure wheels/Thoracle shell.

**Role and counterplay sweep:** reactive Counterspell/Mana Drain/Force/Fierce/Swan Song, Deadly Rollick/Deflecting Swat, Cyclonic Rift/Feed the Swarm/Toxic Deluge/Blasphemous Act, supported by cantrips Brainstorm/Ponder, Mystic Remora/Rhystic/The One Ring/Archmage Emeritus, tutors Demonic/Vampiric/Mystical/Ringsight. Orcish Medicine protects a chosen creature with indestructible or lifelink and amasses 1 **if its target remains legal**; spell cast Saruman trigger remains independent if the spell fails. Removal of Saruman before the follow-up spell costs tempo and drastically lowers value; responding to a live amass with single-Army exile/bounce can erase many counters unless The Ozolith already present. Opponent repeat creature blockers or sacrifice effects defeat giant Army combat; mass exile ignores indestructible; gravehate weakens Treason/Dig/Treasure Cruise and Mizzix's Mastery; board wipes may kill the Army after it was cast-pumped; countering a noncreature spell does **not** counter Saruman's independent trigger once it is on the stack, but removing Saruman **before** casting another spell can prevent future triggers. Ward {2} protects only Goblins/Orcs, not Saruman himself unless he becomes one of those types. Four-color-cost commander access demands real sequencing.

## First-stage challenger: Reiterate exploiting an already-included Mana Geyser
**Provisional exact trial: ADD Reiterate / CUT Treason of Isengard**, alternate cut **Rage into the Valley**, both unapproved. Reiterate is **{1}{R}{R} instant with buyback {3}**. Paid with buyback it costs **{4}{R}{R} (six total, including RR)**, returns to owner's hand as it resolves, and copies target instant or sorcery **on the stack**. The copy isn't cast and doesn't independently trigger Saruman. Source: current WotC *Time Spiral Remastered* official release notes.

**Payable late-pod line and finite termination, NO fabricated win claim:**
1. Assume Saruman survives in play; have Mana Geyser ({3}{R}{R}) and Reiterate in hand, **at least eleven starting mana incl four red** (five to CAST Geyser, six to CAST first buyback Reiterate before any Geyser resolves; named reducers such as Goblin Electromancer can lower generic only), and multiple tapped opponent lands. During a legal main phase cast **original Mana Geyser**, which goes on the stack; Saruman triggers amass Orcs **5** first if it resolves.
2. Retain priority / respond to original Geyser with Reiterate **paying buyback {3}** targeting the original. This is a CAST noncreature spell, so Saruman amasses **3** (Reiterate mana value 3, buyback does not increase it). Then Reiterate resolves and goes back to hand, creating a COPY of Mana Geyser. Copy resolves while original stays on stack.
3. If **N≥6 tapped lands across all opponents**, the copy of Mana Geyser adds at least six red mana (its amount is checked as it resolves). That is enough to CAST Reiterate with buyback again on the original Geyser still pending. If N=6, repeated Reiterates can continue without generating NET red; N>6 generates surplus red. Each NEW Reiterate is cast, so each produces another Saruman `amass Orcs 3` trigger. Copies of Geyser are NOT cast and do NOT trigger Saruman. All Will Be One, The Great Goblin and Black Waltz No. 3, if present, convert those repeated counter/cast events into bounded damage to opponents; the Army grows large but cannot always immediately attack without an evasion/haste/Army-damage outlet.
4. Choose a finite number of iterations, then **stop casting Reiterate**, allow the original Geyser to resolve and move on. This is an optional finite repeat, not a mandatory infinite loop, and not a guaranteed win against hate. An opponent may counter Reiterate (then buyback replacement never returns it), counter/bounce original Geyser to remove the target, remove Saruman or a damage payoff during the window, or vary tapped lands/other response resources. The copy requires original legal target on the stack. N<6 requires draining your reserve or a cost reduction; no sustained loop follows by magic.
5. Conditional **Goblin Electromancer** cost reduction lowers generic components by {1} on both Geyser and Reiterate, changing seed and N threshold (Reiterate with buyback costs {3}{R}{R} five total). Must not apply reductions if reducer dies or isn't controlled. Even 5–6 red every iteration isn't an unconditional victory unless Saruman/another per-cast payoff survives; a naked large mana loop alone may need castable outlet.

This alternative is **commander-first**: repeated paid Reiterate is CAST, so repeatedly triggers Saruman and possibly Bothersome Noisemaker/Black Waltz. It adds a possible conditional spell-loop finisher without importing a generic unrelated two-card Oracle shell. **Counterargument**: it has a truly large 11-mana untapped beginning and needs opponents' **tapped** lands, the commander and payoff continuity; if the engine is not assembled at a live turn it is a conditional three-mana copy spell, and the approved `Treason of Isengard` already amasses Orcs 2, is noncreature MV3 (Saruman adds another 3), returns an instant/sorcery to library top (unless no target chosen), fuels Orc/Goblin politics, and costs only {2}{U}. Cutting it loses both theme and recovery. Cutting `Rage into the Valley` trades a real immediate draw + amass Goblins 2 sorcery for a situational copy-loop spell, worsening early hand smoothing and new The Great Goblin triggers. The stronger structural question is whether a higher combo ceiling justifies that loss in an optimized Bracket4 identity deck.

**Lower-confidence rival:** Roaming Throne ({4}, choose Wizard) could double Saruman (Wizard)'s triggered amass plus Archmage Emeritus's draw while present; Ward {2} protection, but it is a *creature spell* that does not trigger Saruman when cast, adds another four-mana setup body to existing Harmonic Prodigy/Wizard's Staff/Veyran redundancy, and may be less compelling than Reiterate despite broad ability reach. Trial cut `Treason of Isengard` is an alternative, not a second simultaneous use of same cut.

## Adversarial fixtures and decisive tests
- **T4 commander, one untapped land remaining:** Saruman alone is 2/5, no immediate Army until a FOLLOW-UP noncreature cast. Roaming Throne asks for another 4, Reiterate buyback another 6, so neither automatic upgrade in a slow-hand failure state.
- **Board: Saruman, The Great Goblin, Bothersome Noisemaker, empty army; cast a noncreature spell MV3.** Saruman triggers amass Orcs 3, Noisemaker independently amasses Goblins 1, both are separate counter-placement events on same Army chosen, Great Goblin can trigger twice for **2 damage each** if target opponents legal; the Army gains both subtypes. All Will Be One if previously present triggers separately for 3 and 1 damage, not 4 triggers of 1 each. Removing Goblin/Noisemaker changes this structure. No new tokens appear on the second amass if Army already exists.
- **Blasphemous Act with Saruman:** its MV is 9 even when board reduces cost to {R}, so Saruman amasses 9 **before** Act resolves. The Army may subsequently be killed by 13 to all, an Ozolith may retain counters, and preexisting All Will Be One gets its trigger from counters beforehand. Do not count Army survivor without sufficient toughness or protection.
- **Kazuul's Fury:** if cast using its instant front side, sacrificing the old Army is an **additional casting cost** paid BEFORE Saruman's cast trigger, so those newly amassed X=3 counters cannot enhance the *sacrificed* Army's damage. Soul's Fire/Chandra's Ignition instead reference a creature that remains through spell resolution and can see Saruman amass on cast, but their legal target can be removed.
- **Mana Geyser/Reiterate:** evaluate opponent tapped-land N=0/3/5/6/8 and exact starting sources, response window before the first copy resolves, pre-existing vs absent commander/payoff, counterspell/graveyard hate. Compare exact approved baseline to one-card trial with both candidate cuts; no 4-player win-rate claim or actual seeded simulation was performed.
