## Commander identity and defensible natural ceiling

**Bracket 4 — Optimized Army spellslinger** with setup-sensitive high damage, not presumed reliable cEDH. Saruman costs {1}{U}{B}{R}, 2/5 Wizard Avatar, amasses Orcs by **printed-on-stack mana value** each time a **noncreature spell is CAST**, and grants your Goblin/Orc creatures ward {2} (not Saruman himself as printed). The deck exploits discounted high-MV casts — Blasphemous Act MV9, Blood for the Blood God! MV11, Volcanic Salvo MV12 if existing total power reduces generic, delve Treasure Cruise/Dig Through Time MV8 — plus spellcast multipliers Harmonic Prodigy, Wizard's Staff and appropriate Veyran triggers. Bothersome Noisemaker places separate amass Goblins counters on the same Army, The Great Goblin rewards each counter-placement *event* for 2 damage, All Will Be One rewards the actual number of counters, and The Ozolith preserves some counters through destruction/exile/bounce until a subsequent legal combat transfer. Soul's Fire and Chandra's Ignition convert a retained Army's grown power; The Black Gate and Orcish Siegemaster support combat.

**Mana constraint:** 31 ordinary lands plus 4 flexible MDFCs, 3 Talismans, Signet/Fellwar/Sol Ring/Tomb and spell bursts; the 4-mana commander needs three different colored pips, protection competes for UU, conversion/large spells demand RR or BBR. Temporary spend reductions never change mana value; no zero-cost sorcery miracle implied. Without Saruman, the strategy relies more on fair multi-turn spell/Army payoffs, so every extra four-mana creature setup exacts tempo.

**Actual opponent counterplay:** remove Saruman *before* another noncreature cast; counter the original spell (its existing Saruman trigger still persists); exile/bounce Army after counter placement (Ozolith only if actually present, and counter transport only when subsequent start-of-combat trigger resolves); use untargeted removal that bypasses ward; hold lands untapped to reduce Geyser value; counter Reiterate so buyback does not return it, or eliminate original Geyser on stack to remove copy target; Rule of Law shuts down repeated Reiterate casts; kill pingers or block Army. A fully enlarged Army is not a guaranteed three-player win. These responses remain actionable, not simulated win percentages.

## Key verified interaction distinctions

1. **CAST versus COPY:** Cast Mana Geyser MV5 generates one Saruman amass5; copy of that spell made by Reiterate does **not** itself trigger Saruman. Yet each new buyback Reiterate is a **new CAST** of a noncreature MV3 instant and independently causes amass3. Dismissing the card solely because its copy isn't cast misses that possible commander-native loop.
2. **The minimum Reiterate/Geyser seed is severe:** originally cast Geyser costs {3}{R}{R}=5, first buyback Reiterate costs {4}{R}{R}=6, requiring **11 starting mana including RR+RR** before the first Geyser copy ever resolves; natural dynamic discounts/reducers may alter that if actually in play. Given at least **six tapped opponent lands** at every copy's resolution, each copy supplies six red to recast Reiterate (N=6 red-neutral; >6 red-positive). N=5 remains short by one red without other resources; a surviving Goblin Electromancer changes Reiterate's paid buyback generic from 4RR to 3RR and makes N=5 red-neutral. Finite voluntary repetition stops by not casting Reiterate again, allowing original Geyser to resolve if still on stack. A loop requires an actual damage/Army-conversion payoff to beat opponent interaction; no measured success rate assumed.
3. **Independent triggers:** Wizard's Staff/Harmonic Prodigy/Roaming Throne can apply when their respective creature/Wizard/equipment requirements hold; Veyran's constraint is instant/sorcery CAST/COPY-triggering abilities, not every artifact/enchantment spell. Noisemaker Goblin amass and Saruman Orc amass can both target the same Army at separate resolution events; The Great Goblin is 2 per such event, not 2 per counter.
