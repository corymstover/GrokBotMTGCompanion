## Reiterate / Geyser: deterministic resource fixture, not a match result

**Fixed assumptions:** The original Mana Geyser ({3}{R}{R}; MV5) remains a legal target on stack; Reiterate ({1}{R}{R}; MV3) is cast and paid with {3} buyback for **six mana incl two red**, and resolves returning to hand. Original Geyser cast five mana incl RR. Total *starting* cash before first copy **11**, with 4 red pips. The copy of Geyser is not cast. Count N = tapped lands controlled by all opponents at the moment each copy resolves; opponents can change this and counter/remove key pieces. Saruman on battlefield from before original cast: +5 from original Geyser cast, +3 from each *new* Reiterate cast, not +5 per copy.

| Opponent N | Copied Geyser makes | Next six-mana buyback Reiterate without other floating mana | Relative red resource after each further cast/copy, without reducer | Interpretation |
|---:|---:|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0R | NO | -6R | no loop, original Reiterate copy gives zero |
| 3 | 3R | NO | -3R | fails without supplementary mana |
| 5 | 5R | NO | -1R | fails without added mana/reducer |
| 6 | 6R | YES | 0R | voluntary repeat possible, commander yields +3 Army counters/cast |
| 8 | 8R | YES | +2R | repeat adds red surplus and +3 Saruman Army per cast |

**Cost reducers change boundaries:** A surviving Goblin Electromancer reduces generic on Reiterate with buyback from 4RR to 3RR (5 total, RR still required), so N=5 becomes red-neutral; does not turn mana value of Reiterate from 3 to 2 or trigger Saruman more for each copied Geyser. Exiled original, removal of target, countered Reiterate (no buyback return), no legal target on stack, or insufficient colored mana interrupt. Without Saruman and without another spell-cast payoff, repeated casts may make mana but do not inherently win; if an opposing player responds to a recurring loop with resource denial, finite completion can fail. Rule of Law prevents the proposed multi-cast cycle outright. No literal arbitrary looping simulator or measured pod-size/tempo/matchup performance was run in this stage.

**Advantage case:** with active Saruman and All Will Be One, *each* successful amass3 counter-placement event may damage a target opponent for three (or more through doubling); with The Great Goblin, each such event means another fixed 2 damage; with Black Waltz No. 3, each Reiterate cast triggers an additional 2 damage to *each* opponent. They are actual payoffs, but require separate permanents/survival. Beware target opponents, counter placement events, Army exile/bounce and over-claiming inevitability. Archmage Emeritus's magecraft can see CAST or COPIED instant/sorceries and draw cards, a possible upside or finite-loop self-decking risk.

**Strongest case for original KEEP:** Geyser wants opponents to tap lots of lands, Reiterate wants an additional six mana BEFORE Geyser first resolves, and Army finish may need still another protected source. Scarce early RR + 11 total seed makes this much slower than the printed two-card description implies. Treason of Isengard and Rage into the Valley feed Saruman inexpensively, grow/replenish Army, and smooth cards in otherwise fair hands. Historical author attention to Reiterate means this is a **newly articulated conditional line**, not a wholly unresearched card. Heavy removal, taxes, gravehate for the rest of the deck, and opponents' ability to hold lands untapped complicate its practical return.
