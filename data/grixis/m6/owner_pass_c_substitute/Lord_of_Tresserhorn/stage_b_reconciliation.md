### Correct an advisory mechanics error, without editing the advisory

GrokBot wrote that Torpor Orb 'also turns off Undead Augur and Zulaport-style ETB value'. This is **incorrect** for the cards as actually printed: Undead Augur reacts to **Zombies dying**, and Zulaport Cutthroat to **creatures you control dying**. Neither is an entering-creature trigger, so Torpor Orb does **not** shut them off. Karn also does not stop their ordinary die triggers. This false anti-synergy undercuts treating the brief advisory as a mechanically certified KEEP, but it does **not** establish a weakness in the approved deck. The original full author accurately classified both as death payoffs. No rules/legality blocker remains from this error.

**Additional timing conflict for targeted tests:** Dress Down temporarily removes abilities from **all creatures**, not merely Lord. If cast and resolved before Lord enters, Kediss already on the battlefield loses its trigger during that turn; an immediate haste/infect combat while Dress Down is still active will **not** create Kediss's normal postcombat propagation trigger. This does not negate a later turn's Kediss line, because Dress Down sacrifices itself at the end step. The exact order for infect granted by Phyresis or Tainted Strike versus Dress Down also invokes layer-6 grant/removal timestamps and should be stated in the test board. Do not casually count simultaneous Dress Down suppression and the full Kediss table kill on the same turn.

## Bounded adversarial manual comparisons (not measured games)

1. **Free protection, tapped out:** Cast prepositioned Torpor Orb ({2}) and later Lord ({1}{U}{B}{R}) using all available mana. Opponent responds with instant targeted **noncreature** exile after Lord resolves: Fierce Guardianship would be free **if it is in hand and Lord is still controlled**, while the paid Stubborn Denial/Muddle lines may be unavailable. It fails to protect when opponent counters Lord as a spell before it is controlled or uses creature-ability-based exile. **Test:** `+Fierce Guardianship / -Sheoldred's Edict` against `+Fierce Guardianship / -Nightscape Familiar` and exact baseline, retaining availability of edict outs and familiar fixing. No direct upgrade established.
2. **Through a chump blocker:** Lord already infected by Phyresis, but a 1/1 blocks it without trample. Soul's Fire ({2}{R}) can deal Lord-sourced 10 poison to a legal target **if Lord survives until resolution**, unlike a plain blocked attack. `+Soul's Fire / -Sheoldred's Edict` sacrifices a 2-mana answer to opponent hexproof/indestructible and is an existing author-tested *manual candidate*, not a measured lift. Replacement `-Muddle the Mixture` sacrifices a crucial transmute tutor for Torpor Orb/Phyresis. Both require paired source-paid hands and response traces.
3. **Passage vs strike:** An activated Rogue's Passage can make Lord unblockable each turn but costs four mana to activate and provides only colorless mana. Distortion Strike costs {U}, gives +1 power and unblockability with rebound next upkeep, but replacing this land directly makes only 34 lands, and rebound requires an available target on the next upkeep. **Test actual mana/fetch access and commander tax**, not only connected-hit rates.
4. **Dress Down + Kediss:** Cast Dress Down before Lord and grant haste to Lord. At the combat-damage event that turn, Kediss has no triggered ability while Dress Down remains: no other-opponent Kediss poison propagation. If a later turn is used or another trigger-suppressor is present instead, Kediss can function. Record whether Dress Down also precedes/subsequently follows the relevant infect-granting effect before declaring a poison win.
5. **Backup loop disruption:** With Phyrexian Altar, one other Zombie, Gravecrawler in graveyard and a death payoff, sacrificing Gravecrawler generates {B}, pays the same {B} for recast and repeats on priority windows. Removing the other Zombie or the payoff (including Lord) stops or devalues the loop. Ashnod's Altar alone only generates {C}{C}; Rooftop Storm's cost replacement can enable its version at substantially higher initial setup cost.
