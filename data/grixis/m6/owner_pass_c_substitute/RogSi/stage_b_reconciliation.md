### WotC-based corrections / actual payable interactions

- **Bauble vs Rograkh and LED:** Vexing Bauble triggers on a cast with *zero mana spent*, including Rograkh for {0} at first cast and LED escape for {0} under Breach. It also counters pitch or alternative-cost free spells if no mana was paid. It does **not** look only at mana value or printed cost. Rograkh with {2} commander tax has actual mana spent and avoids that condition. Casting 0 artifacts *before* Bauble resolves and holding {1} to tap/sac/draw when returning to free-spell Breach mode can reconcile its offensive and defensive roles, but both impose priority/time costs. Hexing Squelcher's standing no-counter effect can save our cast while present, not solve the need to draw both. Samut's split second does not suppress Bauble's triggered counter. Source: WotC *Modern Horizons 3*, *Lorwyn Eclipsed* and *Reality Fracture* release notes.
- **Early dead-card issue:** Demonic Counsel costs {1}{B}, searches for a *Demon card* without delirium. Exact 100 has **zero Demon card** names and card types (Rograkh Kobold, Silas Human, other named creatures non-Demon); a legal search may fail to find but it cannot become a generic tutor until 4 GY card types. A plausible turn-two graveyard is a fetched land + sacrificed Petal artifact + instant ritual, **3 types**; adding a sorcery (Infernal Plunge/Rite of Flame) makes 4 if those cards *actually went to graveyard*. Necrodominance exile replacement or gravehate prevents that development. Brainstorm costs {U}, sees three, works pre-delirium, and can leverage nine fetches to clear unwanted put-backs; once delirium is active Counsel finds arbitrary exact answer whereas Brainstorm merely selects from top three.
- **Silas and engines:** Silas targeted graveyard artifact must survive till damage trigger and later normal-timing cast; it cannot salvage exiled cards under Necrodominance. Cost 1UB, 2/2 without inherent evasion. Opponents' blocker/removal/gravehate responses can shut it down, but the turbo shell doesn't rely on connecting every game. Underworld Breach gives escape requiring exile 3 *other* cards in graveyard in addition to casting mana; no free infinite resource assumption. All of this was properly recognized in Stage A and author.
- **Protection and target-specific counterplay:** a Bauble shield of opposing zero spells is not the same as protecting an Oracle ETB trigger from Stifle, a graveyard from Endurance-equivalent effects, or an established Collector Ouphe/Rule of Law from stopping the combo. Hexing Squelcher and Samut are creatures vulnerable before the protected spell is cast; Bowmasters pressure similarly needs opponent draws and time.
