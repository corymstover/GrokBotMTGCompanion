## What the sealed first pass got right and what it missed

**Supported in both:** Missy's nonartifact death-to-artifact-Cyberman value is not an automatic self-recursing loop; *previously created face-down artifact Cybermen* dying do not themselves re-trigger her. Tokens cannot be restored. Commander death simultaneous with other nontoken nonartifact creature deaths still gives Missy last-known-state triggers. Squadron myriad tokens exile at end of combat, not available for later end-step villainous choice; yet myriad remains strong in combat and for Reckless Fireweaver artifact ETB. Both present a 35-land / six-mana UBR commander with slow high-end versus commander-speed tension. No actual new swap can be applied without Cory.

**Specific Stage A omission (recorded, not rewritten):** it described the free-reveal morph/flip package but did **not explicitly identify the actual three-card free repeat loops**. After unblinding, the dated Oracle-reviewed interaction evidence shows that face-up **Ruthless Ripper** (reveal black), **Dragon's Eye Savants** (reveal blue), and **Horde Ambusher** (reveal red) may return via Missy face down after sacrifice and turn face up for **zero mana** as a special action with a suitable color card in hand. Thus, with Missy + any such morph + a working free sacrifice outlet, the same original creature can die and return repeatedly. Ripper also causes 2 life loss on each face-up trigger. Goblin Bombardment already converts this to repeated targeted damage; Phyrexian Altar/Ashnod's Altar to mana; Altar of Dementia mills only if sacrificed body has positive power (Savants printed power 0). **Blasting Station** ({3}, {T}+sac a creature to ping 1, may untap on any creature ETB) creates an additional **tutor-accessible lethal sacrifice engine** because each returning Cyberman untaps Station. Station itself is an artifact legal target for Inventors' Fair tutoring; it is NOT a new commander-independent unlimited combo and does not replace graveyard safety. This is a material omission corrected only in Stage B; the original neutral Stage A SHA remains intact.

**Stage A Living Death hypothesis survives only as a lower-priority conditional question**, not an established better card. The comprehensive author did **not name Living Death in its 67K textual review**, and the advisory dismissed Dread Return-like recursion without adjudicating this exact spell. The Stage A first-pass candidate is therefore a genuine additional line, but the comparison reveals greater self-sabotage than the first-pass phrasing initially emphasized: Living Death's order exiles old grave creatures FIRST, sacrifices ALL creatures (including Missy and the incumbent artifact-Cyberman army), then returns old exile creatures. Missy triggers for the *other* nonartifact nontoken bodies that died even if she dies simultaneously, but existing artifact Cybermen are lost without recursion, the opponent's preexisting graveyard threats resurrect, and Missy will not be present at the following end step unless other effects return/cast her. The current deck deliberately uses **Their Name Is Death** and **Delete**, which spare artifact Cybermen and convert opposing nonartifact creatures asymmetrically. Thus the proposed +Living Death / -Cybership has a steep burden; do not prefer it over the original optimized Cyberman theft/Vehicle slot without interactive table evidence.

## Dispute 1: Blasting Station vs Cybermen Squadron (material; primary test)

**Argument FOR author proposal:** Station costs three to Squadron's seven, turns Missy + blue/red/black zero-reveal morph into a deterministic repeatable damage outlet when Station can untap after Cyberman ETB, and is fetchable by Inventors' Fair. Squadron's myriad tokens may trigger Fireweaver and strike additional opponents but do not persist for Missy's end-step choice. In slow games Station offers redundancy, improves early hand options, and remains commander-first.

**Strongest case for GrokBot KEEP / Squadron:** the deck already has **five** persistent mana-free sacrifice outlets, including Goblin Bombardment as its already-live repeated targeted damage outlet, plus Phyrexian Tower once per untap. Station adds an artifact activation that Null Rod/Stony Silence disable, whereas Bombardment enchantment persists. Squadron uniquely grants **myriad to every nonlegendary artifact creature**, scales damage across three opponents in one combat, supplies threats under graveyard hate (which disables Station loops), and reinforces Doctor Who Cybermen rather than adding a sixth mostly similar outlet. With Missy + three nontoken artifact Cybermen attacking into three opponents and Squadron remaining, myriad may create six temporary attacking tokens and trigger Fireweaver six times even though they exile before end step. This is a **rule-traced scenario, not measured win-rate evidence**, but shows Squadron's power is not solely tied to end-step count.

**Counterplay and exact timing:** Station must be untapped or receive an ETB to untap; sacrifice is an activated cost, Missy trigger goes on stack, can be answered by graveyard exile before return, and Station untap trigger then resolves. Removing Missy before next sacrifice, Rest in Peace-style replacements, graveyard exile, countering the initial Station/morph or preventing artifact activations stop the engine. In a graveyard-hate scenario, Squadron's combat line is comparatively live; against a non-blocking fast combo opponent Station is cheaper but not itself sufficient without Missy and morph. A six-mana commander plus support and appropriate colored card in hand is not a guaranteed early-win line.

**Precise decision:** DO NOT automatically endorse author's owner-ready proposal based only on three-card combo correctness. GrokBot's low-confidence KEEP is not a strategic certificate either. Preserve the row as **NEEDS TESTING** and compare **exact full 100 +Station/-Squadron**, baseline and **+Station/-Cybership** alternative. Protect critical defender/counter spells and all five original free-outlet functions; do not cut other low-cost recursion/mana.

## Dispute 2: Living Death vs Cybership (new Stage A; secondary low priority)

**Core interaction** is legal in ordinary circumstances at 3BB sorcery speed and is capable of turning multiple opposing *nontoken nonartifact* battlefield bodies into our tapped artifact Cybermen at trigger resolution. However replacing Cybership sacrifices an exact Doctor Who engine: 6-mana flying 8/8 crew4 on combat-damage-to-player takes **two cards from an opponent's library** face down as our Cybermen regardless of those cards being printed creatures. Creature-only Living Death cannot generate this non-graveyard identity payoff. Official Commander status of an incoming Living Death should be refreshed before any owner-ready proposal; no all-card 100-new-version gate has run.

**Board A (favorable):** with Missy and one face-up nonartifact morph, three opponents' nontoken nonartifact bodies, empty graveyards and zero existing Cybermen, Living Death sacrifices all including Missy; Missy's four other-creature death triggers can return four face-down tapped Cybermen, opponents have no creature reanimation from initially empty graveyards. Missy is gone, so no end-step choice. **Logical illustration, not measured result**.
**Board B (unfavorable):** Missy plus six artifact Cybermen versus an opponent's two nonartifact creatures and three strong creature cards already in their graveyard. Living Death sacrifices Missy, six Cybermen and opposing two; it returns the three old opposing grave creatures face-up and only the two newly sacrificed opposing nonartifact creatures to us as tapped Cybermen; old six Cybermen are lost. **Logical illustration, not measured result**.
**Board C (hate):** an in-play Rest in Peace-style replacement stops dies-to-graveyard and Missy recursion, reducing the spell to a symmetric destructive reset with a six-mana commander lost. Compared to existing asymmetric nonartifact wipes, it is often a downgrade.

**Decision:** **not owner-ready**. Include +Living Death/-Cybership only as a specific conditional side-test if staged evidence indicates frequent opposing creature-heavy/empty-graveyard boards with Missy present and existing Cybermen count low. This is NOT a new compulsory universal test; initial strategic judgment leans **KEEP Cybership** absent such meta evidence. Reject -Ugin's Mastery/-Panoptic Projektor shortcuts because their morph/colorless face-down and turn-up packages already support real repeated damage and draw.
