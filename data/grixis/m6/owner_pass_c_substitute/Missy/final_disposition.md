## Commander-first strategy and rules verified to available date

Missy {3}{U}{B}{R}, 4/5, transforms other **nonartifact creatures that actually die into graveyards** into tapped face-down 2/2 artifact Cybermen under our control; end step each opponent independently chooses damage from each artifact creature we control **or** our draw plus chaos ensues, in order. Opponents may choose the impossible/zero-damage option. The printed commander-specific strategy is **theft through death, Cybermen board pressure, zero-reveal face-up morph sacrifice loops, villainous choices, and Doctor Who artifact payoffs**. This is naturally **Bracket 4 — Optimized**, not a generic Grixis turbo conversion. A six-mana UBR commander plus 35 lands, 2 colorless-only lands and slow conditional duals imposes real early access/protection pressure.

**Important mechanics:** face-down artifact Cybermen **cannot trigger Missy again** on their own death; printed morph/disguise creature turned face up can regain nonartifact characteristics and then die to trigger Missy. **Ruthless Ripper** reveals black, **Dragon's Eye Savants** blue, **Horde Ambusher** red to flip for **no mana** as a special action, provided the right card remains in hand. Together with Missy and a functional mana-free sacrifice outlet they support repeatable returns/deaths; Ripper supplies 2 life loss per flip. **Blasting Station** can tap+sacrifice such a creature to deal one and untap each time that same Cyberman enters, but Station is vulnerable to artifact-activation hate and removal, and alone is NOT an infinite loop. Sacrifice during morph loop still admits opponent graveyard interaction. **Goblin Bombardment** already converts the same loop into unlimited damage without needing a tap/untap refresh. Panoptic Projektor and Ugin's Mastery enable important turn-up/cast-face-down sequences that should not be casually cut.

**Cybermen Squadron** {7} gives every nonlegendary artifact creature myriad, including itself; tokens enter attacking and exile at **end of combat**. These temporary tokens **do not** directly inflate Missy's later end-step artifact-creature count, a prior source overstatement, but they contribute real multiplayer combat reach and artifact entry payoffs (Reckless Fireweaver). **Cybership** {6}, flying 8/8 Vehicle with crew 4, places opponent's top two library cards face down as stolen Cybermen when it damages that player; it is a slow but unique and flavorful identity enabler.

**Living Death** {3}{B}{B} exiles old grave creatures, sacrifices all battlefield creatures, and returns the initially exiled creatures. With Missy present as all creatures die she can trigger for other nonartifact nontoken sacrificed bodies even if dying simultaneously; triggers resolve after the spell. However it **sacrifices Missy and our existing Cybermen**, which were artifacts and thus do not recur via Missy, and reanimates opponents' earlier grave threats. Its best-case nonartifact-theft sweep is not free, and it is often worse than existing **Delete** and **Their Name Is Death** asymmetric nonartifact wipes. Token deaths are not reusable; opponents can respond before the sorcery or to later Missy triggers, not between instructions during resolution. No exact card-swap improvement follows from rules validity alone.
