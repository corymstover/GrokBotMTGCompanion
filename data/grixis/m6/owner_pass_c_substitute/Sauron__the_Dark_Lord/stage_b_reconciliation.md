## Actual counterfactual timing and rules tests (deterministic traces, not empirical matches)

**Wraith bridge with 3 Nazgûl:** Maintain 3 live Nazgûl, an ordinary Orc Army1/1, Sauron Dark Lord and The Great Goblin. Without Adaptation, one **non-Sauron sourced** Ring event (e.g., Call of Ring) yields exactly 3 separate Nazgûl Wraith counter-placement trigger events on Wraiths, **zero Nazgûl counters on Army**; thus none of those Wraith-only placements triggers Great Goblin from the Orc Army unless other qualifying Orc/Goblin got counters. With Arcane Adaptation selecting Wraith, same event gives +3 Army counters in three separate events; each placement affecting Orc Army triggers The Great Goblin once for 2 → **6 damage total** to one or multiple target opponents, provided source persists, legal targets exist and triggers resolve. Do not mistakenly multiply 2 by number of creatures receiving counters in each event.

**Existing Throne and changed choice:** With only 3 Nazgûl/Army and **Roaming Throne naming Wraith** but no Adaptation, Throne doubles the Nazgûl temptation-triggered counters and Nazgûl ETB Ring abilities, but not Sauron's opponent-spell amass since Sauron is an Avatar Horror. With Adaptation naming Wraith, Sauron becomes Wraith, so the same Throne Wraith choice now doubles his own amass and Ring-draw triggers, and potentially other converted creature triggers. For one non-Sauron Ring event under 3 Nazgûl, the 3 counter triggers each occur twice (=6 Wraith counter placements), Army gains six if Adaptation stays on battlefield, and The Great Goblin (also converted to Wraith) can have its 2-damage counter-event trigger doubled by Throne (=12 ping triggers, 24 damage **only if all triggers resolve with legal targets**, and assuming no extra doubling/replacement). If the Ring event itself came from Army COMBAT damage to a player while Sauron is Wraith and Throne is naming Wraith, Sauron's Army-damage-to-Ring trigger is also doubled, producing **two separate Ring-tempt events**, which could increase later counters/pings again: do NOT apply the one-temptation 24 figure to that case as a final total. All branches terminate normally after finite events; there is no inherently infinite loop.

**Removal window:** Arcane Adaptation has static type effect. If an opponent removes Adaptation *before* Nazgûl Wraith counter triggers resolve, the Orc Army is no longer Wraith and does **not** get those counters; the triggers still affect remaining true Wraiths. If one Nazgûl dies after its counter trigger is on stack, the triggered ability may still resolve and put a counter on surviving Wraiths, provided relevant state; if Sauron removed before any opponent casts, no new Sauron amass triggers. This creates a targeted opposing enchantment-removal vulnerability that baseline Saruman (a 2/5 creature) handles differently, but Saruman itself can also be killed.

**Cut tradeoff:** Saruman White Hand is {1}UBR, adds MV-based amass on OUR noncreature CAST spells, and grants Orc/Goblin ward2; cutting it removes two direct ways to preserve/develop an Army when opponents slowroll spells. Arcane Adaptation {2}U is cheaper and may be tutored with Sauron-colored Ringsight after it has successfully Ring tempted and provided the actual shared-color legal target, but it requires Nazgûl and Ring events to yield counters and does not protect Army from targeted destruction. Cutting Sauron Lord of the Rings instead removes an eight-mana yet flavorful second Sauron CAST amass5+mill5+reanimate; possible mana-curve relief, but it can be a recovery bomb in slower pods. No side-by-side cast/mulligan/matchup success rates measured.

**Official checks:** WotC Ixalan Arcane Adaptation {2}U adds type to creature cards/spells/permanents rather than replacing other types; WotC LCI Roaming Throne adds ONE EXTRA trigger to abilities of other creatures of selected type, not a recursive cascade; WotC LTR Nazgûl up to nine Commander singleton exemption, Sauron opponent CAST and Ring rules; WotC Hobbit The Great Goblin fixed TWO damage per counter-placement event. The extra Hobbit card Great Goblin, Foul-Hearted has Army trample/ETB/attack amass Goblins3 on published card material; retained. Current full 100-card Oracle re-import NOT performed; selective effects source-checked.

**Other grounded concerns:** Ward—sacrifice a legendary artifact or creature is payable only from a player's own proper legend, may be bypassed by nontarget wipes, so cannot treat Sauron protected from all removal. Archfiend of Ifnir sees multiple individual discard cards from a Sauron full-hand reload; Sheoldred life recovery if Sauron actually draws four; both conditional but central engine, not generic fluff. Ringsight only tutors colored-sharing card given a legendary Ring bearer, cannot find colorless Roaming Throne just because Sauron is UBR. Commander + Arcane + 3 Nazgûl + Throne + Goblin takes enough total cards/mana that a maximized upper-bound event board is not evidence of real early lethal.
