## Strongest provisional challenger — Arcane Adaptation's Wraith type bridge

**Provisional exact trial, unapproved: ADD Arcane Adaptation / CUT Saruman, the White Hand**; alternative cut under later scrutiny **Sauron, Lord of the Rings** (8-mana secondary boss) if keeping Saruman's noncreature amass/ward is more important. Both cuts lose real theme/utility; do not treat as free cuts.

Arcane Adaptation costs **{2}{U}** enchantment. Choose creature type **Wraith** as it enters; creatures you control on battlefield, creature spells you control, and your creature cards off battlefield also gain Wraith in ADDITION to their other types. Consequently your **Orc Army** (still an Orc Army), 7 Nazgûl, Sauron commander, The Great Goblin, Sheoldred, Archfiend and Saruman (if retained) can all gain Wraith type. Each Ring temptation then puts **+1/+1 on the Army and every other controlled creature** for EACH resolving Nazgûl triggered ability, instead of only naturally Wraith creature subset. This is genuinely commander-first: the commander reliably makes an Army whenever opponents cast, then Ring temptation from that Army's player damage or Bilbo/Nazgûl/Call redirects Wraith counter value onto it and every other creature. Its own static type effect doesn't itself place a counter or generate Ring temptation. It is not a spell-copy or nontargeted board wipe.

**Roaming Throne in already-approved list** chooses creature type upon entering. With Adaptation active, choosing **Wraith** causes it to double the triggered abilities of other **Wraith** creatures you control: **Nazgûl's on-Ring counter triggers**, Sauron's opponent-cast amass and Ring reload if Sauron is Wraith, and The Great Goblin's counter-event damage if it is Wraith. This is one trigger extra for an eligible trigger, **not an infinite recursive creation of doubled triggers**. But a four-mana creature + three-mana enchantment + 3-mana Nazgûl plus six-mana Sauron setup is substantial and vulnerable to removing Adaptation/Throne.

**Concrete bounded fixture:** Sauron on battlefield, 3 Nazgûl surviving, an Orc Army with one +1 counter, no Throne, no Adaptation: one Ring temptation produces 3 Nazgûl counter events, giving +3/+3 to EACH existing Wraith Nazgûl but **ZERO direct Nazgûl counters to Orc Army**. Same battlefield with Arcane Adaptation choosing Wraith: those same 3 trigger events each add one +1/+1 counter to Army *and* every other creature, so Army gains three. If The Great Goblin present, it sees each **separate placement event** affecting an Orc/Army and deals fixed 2 target-opponent damage three times (6 damage total if legal and resolves); no multiplication by the total number of counters or creatures hit. With Roaming Throne choosing Wraith already, each of three Nazgûl triggers happens twice (6 separate events); Great Goblin also triggers an additional time if counted as Wraith by Adaptation, potentially 12 Great Goblin damage triggers totaling 24 damage to one or more legal target opponents, before counter/removal/protection. This is an upper-bound battlefield trace, NOT a tournament rate or deterministic assembly. If there is only one Nazgûl and no payoffs, the gained value can be just one counter on Army at the cost of three mana.

**Counterargument:** Arcane Adaptation may do NOTHING on an empty battlefield without Nazgûl/Army and is itself a three-mana setup enchantment with no immediate Ring event, no mana or cards. Saruman, the White Hand is an existing on-theme 2/5 at 1UBR giving our Goblins/Orcs ward2 and amass by mana value of EVERY noncreature spell we CAST, a protective axis Adaptation cannot replicate. Even when Ring yields many counters, opponents can still exile/bounce Army and remove Adaptation, and there may be insufficient Nazgûl on battlefield to beat losing Saruman's unconditional spell cast value. The alternative cut of eight-mana Sauron, Lord of the Rings removes a lore-significant guaranteed amass5+mill5+reanimation CAST trigger and late recovery, though it can be clunky in this commander, and might be an even worse thematic decision. The new three-mana bridge is plausible but not proven an optimal decklist change.

**Lower-confidence rival:** ADD **Maskwood Nexus** / CUT Saruman, the White Hand similarly makes all controlled creature types including Wraith (and also Goblin/Orc/Army), 4-mana artifact with token ability. More expensive and vulnerable to artifact hate; the broad all-types effect could unexpectedly mean any controlled creature is already Army, changing *which Army* is chosen for an amass and adding subtype interactions. Do not assume it functions exactly like only-Wraith Arcane Adaptation. This is not prioritized above 3-mana Adaptation without narrow real rules tests.

## Rules-specific adversarial fixtures

1. **Opponents' two noncreature casts in succession:** Sauron active triggers amass1 on each cast even if opponent spell countered, unless Sauron was removed BEFORE the cast. The two separate 1-counter events can each trigger The Great Goblin fixed2 damage; if Sauron removed while the two existing amass triggers are on stack, the existing triggers still resolve.
2. **Ring + discard/Archfiend:** after valid Ring temptation, Sauron triggers optional discard of **all current cards in hand**; choosing yes with four cards sends all four simultaneously to GY and draws four on resolution. Archfiend of Ifnir sees EACH discarded card and produces one -1/-1-on-opponent-creatures trigger per card, placed on stack after original Sauron draw resolution as applicable. Countered/refused discard selection yields no four draws and no Archfiend discard triggers. Opponents can respond to these separate triggers; toughness and replacement modifiers matter.
3. **Ward reality:** ward—sacrifice a legendary artifact or legendary creature triggers upon opponent spell/ability TARGETING Sauron and asks that opponent to sacrifice their own eligible legendary artifact/creature or have original targeted effect countered; cannot use our legend as payment. Wipe/edict/discard doesn't target Sauron, ward offers no protection. If Sauron itself ceases to be controlled during earlier interaction, future opponent casts won't amass.
4. **Wraith conversion:** one Nazgûl Ring trigger puts one counter on EACH Wraith, including Arcane-Adaptation-modified Army, not a counter per Nazgûl already on battlefield just because subtype Wraith; with n separate live Nazgûl triggers you get n distinct placement events. Arcane Adaptation adds types, preserves Army/Orc and Goblin where printed, Roaming Throne doubles eligible **triggering creature** not arbitrary spell/land triggers. Trigger ordering between Nazgûl and Sauron's discard/draw from same Ring event is selected by controller; drawing/discarding will not retroactively change who was Wraith when a previously resolved counter event occurred.
5. **Combat**: Sauron's Army Ring trigger requires actual PLAYER combat damage. Rogue's Passage ({4} activation) or targeted Black Gate evasion can help get past blockers at significant mana, Great Goblin Foul-Hearted gives trample but blocking may still prevent damage to player if Army fully absorbed; a 7/6 Sauron that connects isn't an Army and doesn't by itself trigger that specific ability, although it can be Ring-bearer for Ring emblem.
6. **Official Nazgûl construction:** 7× legal up to 9 printed rule overrides singleton. The Lord of Rings/Witch-king thematic cards are individual names and distinct; no invented future-release exception needed for already released Hobbit cards as of Oct21. Historic Nov13 release transition remains a separate future project trigger.
