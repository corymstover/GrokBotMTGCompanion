## Strategic conclusion: where Sedris really wins

Sedris grants *every creature card in your graveyard* a card-owned unearth {2}{B}; creature enters with haste for one turn, yielding immediate ETB and attack value, normally exiled at next end step and exiled instead if departing for another zone. Army of high-impact ETBs (Archon of Cruelty, Rune-Scarred Demon, Sepulchral Primordial, Burning-Rune Demon, Grave Titan, Metamorphosis Fanatic, Massacre Wurm), haste attack bombs (Etali, Primal Storm), self-graveyard assembly (Entomb/Buried Alive/Ripples of Undeath), normal reanimation backup, and optional end-step escape (Obeka, Sundial, Veil) define its power. This is optimized Bracket4 creature reanimator and timed exile manipulation, not a generic Grixis turbo combo shell. Six-mana UBR access across 31 lands plus 10 repeatable rocks, graveyard-exile hate, the commander's survival, colored black activation payments and available cast windows constrain actual ceiling.

**New candidate** Altar of Dementia {2} is a free sacrifice outlet converting the POWER of a creature you control into self-mill. The nominal Altar sacrifice of an unearthed Grave Titan (6 power) would exile Titan under unearth's replacement, but generate one target-six-cards mill ability; several new creatures potentially become Sedris unearth options later. There is no self-return of the sacrificed Titan, no free casting, and no automatic infinite loop. It's a novel challenger not named in the full original author review. Existing Ashnod's Altar and Goblin Bombardment can also sacrifice bodies but produce mana/damage, NOT deck-to-grave feed; extra outlet may be meaningful in a build needing renewable yard.

**Strongest reason to reject it:** Panharmonicon doubles powerful ETB triggers that are Sedris's actual payoff. Its four-mana upfront artifact costs tempo but, with unearthed Rune-Scarred Demon, can generate a second tutor; with Grave Titan, a second pair of Zombies; with Sepulchral Primordial or Metamorphosis Fanatic, extra reanimation access when legal. Altar asks for a body and target, mills stochastically, and may overfill a graveyard opponents can exile. Three free sacrifice outlets may be redundant, and the alternative cut Etali forfeits a haste attack free-spell effect. On current documentary evidence the incumbent is fully defensible. **No conclusion of proven swap superiority.**

## Rules defects identified after unblinding, plus specific safe interpretation

GrokBot advisory asserted Sundial does not preserve an unearthed body because that body would be exiled at the next opponent end step. **This is false when correctly sequenced.** At the beginning of YOUR end step, unearth's delayed exile trigger goes on the stack. End your turn with Sundial while this trigger is on the stack to exile the trigger from stack permanently; there is no new scheduled end-step exile trigger for the same object. Its separate leave-battlefield replacement remains. Obeka may produce equivalent stack-clearing end turn only when the ACTIVE player chooses it and her tap is available. If ending own turn *before* end-step trigger is created, the trigger can await the next end step, and that premature line is not safe. This precise correction was already documented in historical reconciliation **PR #363**; no reason to revisit the owner-locked Sundial slot.

Actual Ghostly Flicker performs exile-return and can create a NEW object because unearth's replacement permits explicit exile, but needs TWO legal targets, unlike Sundial's repeatable effect. No new Ghostly Flicker swap recommended or approved. Teferi's Veil phases ATTACKING unearthed creature at end of combat: no battlefield leave, and phased-out permanent unavailable for end-step exile; nonattacking creatures aren't rescued by it. Convergence of Dominion discounts unearth {2}{B} to {B} **only while Sedris is controlled**, not whenever four creature cards are in graveyard. Gifts Ungiven was officially unbanned April 2025; not a legality blocker. Selected WotC authoritative release notes, current banned list and rules were checked; no current full 99-name Oracle sweep or measured game win probabilities asserted.
