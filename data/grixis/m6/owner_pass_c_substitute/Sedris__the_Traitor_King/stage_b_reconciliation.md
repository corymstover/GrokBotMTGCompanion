## Specific finite rules/interaction traces, not match simulations

1. Sedris grants every creature CARD in own graveyard unearth {2}{B} as the CARD's own activated ability, usable at sorcery speed; if the graveyard card leaves before the ability resolves, no return. **Convergence of Dominion** reduces activation by {2} (to {B}, min one mana) ONLY when owner controls Sedris; it does not grant an ability or fix missing B.
2. An unearthed creature returns with haste, must be exiled when next end-step delayed trigger resolves, and if it WOULD leave battlefield to a zone other than exile it is exiled INSTEAD. **Sundial** works if, during our own end step, the actual delayed exile trigger has already entered the stack: end-turn removes that trigger forever; the creature persists, although later leave-battlefield exile replacement stays in force. If turn ended prematurely BEFORE the trigger appears, it can trigger at the subsequent end step. **Obeka** likewise requires the active player to choose ending the turn and a usable tap. GrokBot conflated the two timing windows.
3. **Ghostly Flicker** may target two qualifying permanents you control, actually exile each then return it as a NEW permanent, so an unearthed creature can be made independent of the old unearth effect. This is functional but card- and target-dependent, and cannot by itself establish superiority to repeatable one-mana Sundial. **Teferi's Veil** phases attacking unearthed creatures out at end of combat; phasing is not a battlefield departure; it cannot rescue nonattackers this way.
4. **New sealed Stage A swap: +Altar of Dementia/-Panharmonicon.** Two-mana Altar sacrifices a creature as activation COST then target player mills cards equal to creature's last known POWER (not number of cards equal to mana value, nor equal to the number of creature ETB triggers). Sacrificing an **unearthed** Grave Titan with power 6 exiles Titan instead of placing it in graveyard, but still mills six to the targeted self or opponent if target and ability legal. This could seed subsequent unearthed creatures but does not make the sacrificed Titan recurrable by Sedris. If target illegal at resolution, mill fails though sacrifice paid. New targets require later sorcery-speed unearth and additional {2}{B}/{B} payments.
5. The strongest argument AGAINST Altar cut: Panharmonicon doubles relevant **creature/artifact ETB** triggers on our entries (e.g. Grave Titan two-Zombie ETB, Rune-Scarred Demon, Sepulchral Primordial, Burning-Rune Demon, Massacre Wurm and Metamorphosis Fanatic). It does NOT double unearth activating, Grave Titan's attack trigger, or Etali's attack trigger. Altar is third sac outlet after Ashnod's Altar and Goblin Bombardment; self-mill is high-variance, vulnerable to graveyard exile, can deplete library; loss of double ETBs can be severe. No evidence yet of a net positive swap.
6. Alternative cut **Etali, Primal Storm** for Altar trades away valuable unearthed haste attack free spell access. Earlier author's separate Delver/Sheoldred candidate remains second-priority conditional; do not convert documentary KEEP into an automatically-approved swap.
