## Natural commander-specific strategy and power ceiling

This is high-powered reanimator TEMPO and graveyard ENGINE, optimized Bracket 4, with potential high-end combos if truly enabled by card-level evidence. Seed yard with Entomb, Buried Alive, Corpse Connoisseur, Vile Entomber, Unmarked Grave, Ripples of Undeath, Faithless Looting, Frantic Search, Windfall, Convergence of Dominion, etc.; establish Sedris and convert {2}{B} per death-tutor/draw/bomb into immediate haste, ETB or attack value. Existing creature targets include Archon of Cruelty, Grave Titan, Sepulchral Primordial, Rune-Scarred Demon, Burning-Rune Demon, Massacre Wurm, Etali, Primal Storm, Terror of the Peaks and Metamorphosis Fanatic. Reanimate/Animate Dead/Necromancy/Victimize/Dread Return/Living Death offer early or repeated routes when Sedris costs six and cannot be assumed present.

**Signature persistence subengine:** on YOUR end step stack the delayed unearth exile triggers; **Sundial of the Infinite** or **Obeka, Brute Chronologist** can end your turn with those triggers on stack, exiling the triggers from stack permanently, so the creature stays on battlefield afterward. Its persistent unearth *leave-battlefield replacement* still applies in later turns: any attempted bounce/sacrifice to another zone is exiled. Obeka must be untapped and the active player chooses to end the turn; she cannot force an opponent to choose. Sundial activates during controller's turn only. If you end your turn too early BEFORE delayed exile trigger goes on stack, the trigger may instead arise at the next end step, so hold priority after triggered abilities are stacked. **Teferi's Veil** phases attacking unearthed creatures out at end of combat: phasing is not a zone change; phased-out permanent is treated as not existing for end-step delayed trigger's resolution, then phases in on controller's untap. This line requires the creature actually attacking; non-attacking ETB utility creatures need another plan. Sedris does not need to remain on battlefield for an already-granted unearth ability to resolve after activation if creature card stays in graveyard.

**Convergence of Dominion official correction:** colorless {3} artifact; as long as you CONTROL YOUR COMMANDER, activated abilities of cards in graveyard cost {2} less, min one mana. It can reduce Sedris-granted unearth from {2}{B} to {B} per creature but does not itself grant unearth and stops discounting when Sedris is absent. Graveyard creature count is not its cost-reduction condition. Big-mana bursts (Ancient Tomb, Mana Vault/Grim Monolith, Signets/Talismans) aid six-mana UBR commander; a UBR paid commander followed by 2–3 further {2}{B} activations and a timing-protection piece is not routine early-game setup. Under graveyard hate, commander dies before untap, or opposing countermagic on Sedris, deck relies on conventional reanimation and interaction.

## Actual challenger: second-purpose free sacrifice plus graveyard velocity

**Candidate to test (NOT approved): ADD Altar of Dementia / CUT Panharmonicon.** Altar costs {2}, lets you sacrifice a creature as ability cost to make TARGET PLAYER mill cards equal to sacrificed creature's power, including yourself. Unlike existing Ashnod's Altar (colorless mana) or Goblin Bombardment (one damage), it immediately converts large disposable bodies into another graveyard batch, potentially finding more worthwhile unearthed targets for a following turn. Sacrifice is a cost; sacrificing an already-unearthed creature sends it to **exile instead of graveyard**, but Altar still sees the sacrificed creature's last known power and the mill ability resolves unless stopped separately. You cannot recycle that unearthed creature card through the sacrifice, nor claim it dies for ordinary graveyard death triggers from that replacement event. A creature *not* unearthed may go to the graveyard normally. Self-milling gets opponents' graveyard-hate risk and library-depletion risk; under instant-speed exile or opponent Ghostly Prison-type pacing, Altar may not replace deck's engine coherently.

The proposed cut Panharmonicon {4} doubles ETB triggered abilities from artifacts/creatures you control: Sedris has abundant important ETBs (e.g., Burning-Rune Demon tutor, Rune-Scarred Demon tutor, Sepulchral Primordial reanimation, Massacre Wurm sweeps, Grave Titan 2/2 tokens, Metamorphosis Fanatic) so it is **not filler**. It is slower and does nothing to Sedris's unearth **activation itself**, though it may be superior once those creatures ETB. Altar may exchange single-turn ETB explosion for repeatable graveyard development/instant-speed sac flexibility; this is a genuine tension, not a proven unconditional improvement. Altar's activation targets a player; a target made illegal can counter the entire ability, but sacrificing the creature as the activation cost cannot be undone.

**Alternative cut:** the six-mana attack-only *Etali, Primal Storm* is potent with unearth haste but needs to attack and remain alive through declaration; replacing it removes high-upside free spell access, so its cost is significant. Another plausible challenger **Entomb #2 via Oriq Loremage** would add creature-card search but a four-mana summoning-sick body is too slow relative to existing Entomb/Buried Alive tutors; deprioritize. A global Mesmeric Orb self-mill also exposes opponents and may be a poor match for controlled tutor sequencing; not an automatic inclusion.

## Colored access, actual opponent counterplay, and strongest objection

Lands include major original dual/fetch suite and singleton utility Ancient Tomb, Phyrexian Tower, Urborg, Otawara, Takenuma, Sokenzan; only two Swamps. Colored BLACK matters not only for commander but for repeated unearth; a hand with Ancient Tomb + colorless Grim Monolith does not itself supply UBR. Opponent's graveyard exilers (e.g. instant-speed exile between activating unearth and resolution), Rest in Peace replacement, targeted creature removal in response to end-step stack, countermagic on the six-mana commander, and counterplay to the two commander-persistence pieces are consequential. Early non-Sedris reanimation lines matter because unearth cannot be activated until the commander grants the ability.

**Strongest argument to KEEP unchanged:** 100 currently approved cards already include Ashnod's Altar, Goblin Bombardment, Buried Alive, Entomb, Ripples of Undeath, and a purpose-built creature suite. A third sac outlet can be redundant. Milling by sacrificed power is stochastic; the deck values deterministic tutors and immediate doubled ETBs, so losing Panharmonicon may harm actual kill/recovery turns more than Altar helps. Sedris's specific identity is *one-turn reanimation and exile timing*, not generic self-mill; an Altar swap must beat that opportunity cost in tested relevant states. Equally, a free mill sac outlet could be a material source of sequential unearthed bodies and sac interaction, so an automatic documentary KEEP would be premature.
