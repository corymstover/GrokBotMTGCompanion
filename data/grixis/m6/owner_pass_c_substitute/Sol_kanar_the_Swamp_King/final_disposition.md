## Commander-first strategic ruling

Sol'kanar is {2}{U}{B}{R} 5/5 Demon with swampwalk and **whenever ANY player casts a black spell, you gain 1 life**. Trigger requires Sol'kanar to be on battlefield for that spell's cast; his initial cast does not self-trigger. `Painter's Servant` choosing black extends that condition to future otherwise nonblack spells from every player without changing actual Commander color identity. `Demon's Horn` is a cheap independent, optional gain-1 source for the same eligible casts; the two separate lifegain events increase `Starscape Cleric` / `Marauding Blight-Priest` output even if total life gained is modest. `Vito` and `Enduring Tenacity` are amount-sensitive complementary outlet payoffs. `Exquisite Blood` and `Bloodthirsty Conqueror` provide interacting loss-to-gain halves, while `Bolas's Citadel` + `Sensei's Divining Top` + `Aetherflux Reservoir` is a high-value card-and-life loop; `Painter` + `Grindstone` offers another separately vulnerable mill route.

`Blanket of Night` and `Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth` give opponents Swamp lands to make swampwalk relevant, boost own Coffers/Swamp-scalers, but also make enemy black mana possible. The correct printed Swamp count, corrected in Stage B without changing immutable Stage A, is **six basic plus NINE typed nonbasic lands = 15**; 35 total land cards + one conditional land MDFC. The `Sheoldred, the Apocalypse` four-mana curve phrasing was likewise corrected post-seal. Multicolor requirements `U/B/R`, five-mana initial commander, six-plus cost recovery after taxes, heavy black pips, 35 lands with generic-only ramp, enchantment/artifact engine reliance and vulnerability to stack/sweeper interaction limit realistic tempo and resilient game frequency. Owner-approved natural **Bracket 4 — Optimized** remains appropriate, no generic cEDH homogenization justified.
