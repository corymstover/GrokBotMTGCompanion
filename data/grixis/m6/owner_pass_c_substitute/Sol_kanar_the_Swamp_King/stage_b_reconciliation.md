## 2. Convergence and disagreement: compare exact claims

**Convergence — commander identity:** Stage A, original author and reconciliation all locate value in black-spell-triggered `Sol'kanar` / `Demon's Horn`, independent life-gain EVENTS plus four existing gain-to-loss payoffs; `Painter's Servant` naming black broadens eligible casts, `Urborg` / `Blanket of Night` enable Swampwalk and Coffers. Agreed Bracket 4 optimized, not a generic Bracket 5 Oracle/Breach strategy. Three source documents also defend `Alhammarret's Archive` as a meaningful but five-mana, potentially slow amplifier.

**Historical GrokBot dispute — now explicitly resolved:** GrokBot offered `+Sanguine Bond/-Demon's Horn` on assertion the current list has only two `Exquisite Blood` feedback partners. The approved TXT includes `Vito`, `Enduring Tenacity`, `Marauding Blight-Priest` and `Starscape Cleric`—four independently functioning life-gain-to-opponent-loss abilities (amount-based first two; each-opponent event-based last two) that can form feedback with `Exquisite Blood` after a proper starting event, where permitted. `Bloodthirsty Conqueror` supplies another opponent-loss-to-own-gain half. Sanguine Bond would be a redundant **fifth** life-gain-to-loss outlet at {3}{B}{B}; Horn costs {2} and creates a distinct LIFE-GAIN EVENT for each black spell by any player, even when commander is absent. With commander + Horn on board, one black cast produces two gain triggers (not one gain of 2), which separately trigger Cleric/Blight-Priest; `Alhammarret's Archive` makes each one gain2 but does not increase event count. Cutting Horn trades enabling-event density for an already-saturated payoff category and weakens commander-out attrition. Stage A listed Horn as an alternate speculative cut; **post-seal counterevidence sharply downgrades it**. Preserve historical reconciliation KEEP and reject Sanguine Bond/Horn; no need to redo the 62/62 reconciliation.

**New unresolved divergence:** Stage A's `+Necropotence/-Alhammarret's Archive` is not the authored review's +Grimoire/Archive scenario and is not the GrokBot Sanguine Bond/Horn proposal. Necropotence has different mechanics: {B}{B}{B}, life-paid *activated* delayed access at the next end step, skip each draw step, and exile discards; it **does not draw** a card or trigger life gain, and Archive would not double that hand transfer. It may exploit the healthy starting life total and gain density, but the five-mana Archive both doubles Sol'kanar/Horn gain AMOUNTS and extra actual draws, especially where cards need to be seen *in the current turn*. This pair has not been tested under the existing source-paid effect coverage and remains unapproved. The newer suggestion does not invalidate prior correct Horn analysis.

## 5. Counterplay and alternative cuts

- `Necropotence` requires an enchantment to resolve and sufficient spare life; `Disenchant`-like removal after life was paid cannot stop its **already-created delayed card-to-hand abilities**, but removal/countermagic before activation can. It inhibits subsequent natural draw steps and turns discards into exiled resources. In a deck whose fast lines already pay life via `Citadel`, `K'rrik` or `Mana Confluence`, and whose `Ancient Tomb` / `City of Brass` can cause DAMAGE to their controller, additional paid life for cards may compete with resources needed to actually WIN.
- `Alhammarret's Archive` is an artifact subject to broad artifact hate, but is colorless to cast and scales both the life and draw sides of the commander strategy. Removing it for another draw engine may make `Aetherflux Reservoir` / Vito thresholds and Sol/Horn turn-cycle events weaker. Neither engine should be evaluated only on standalone average card quality.
- `Underworld Dreams` as alternate cut for Necro would replace a three-black proactive opponent-draw punishment and `Wheel/Windfall` multiplier. `Grindstone` as alternate cut loses the already established `Painter` two-card mill line; `Demon's Horn` as alternate cut removes the commander's distinctive backup gain-event engine. `Witch of the Moors` is slower but converts a gained-life turn into opponent creature sacrifice and recursive creature hand value; it is a different role. None is an identified free slot.
- Opponent answering commander twice (recast tax), exile of `Exquisite Blood`, removing `Painter` before `Grindstone` activation resolves, anti-draw pressure (`Narset` effects) versus anti-lifegain effects, and fast nonblack tables change which amplifier is desirable. Most of these are qualitative opponent windows already examined in the original author's ten-pod matrix; they need only **targeted** replay for the new Necro pair, not a universal comprehensive rerun.
