## Agreement and novel effect-aware challenge

**Agreements:** Sol'kanar is {2}UBR (five mana) 5/5 Demon; whenever ANY player CASTS a black spell (including countered black spells) you gain 1 life, and swampwalk applies if defending opponent controls a Swamp. Painter's Servant choosing BLACK makes every player's spells black and turns on Grindstone. Urborg/Blanket give enemy lands Swamp subtype but do NOT make nonblack spells black. Horn replicates life events even if Sol'kanar is absent, and black spell gain engines are commander-first. The natural ceiling is optimized Bracket4 with independently paid mana, timing and responses, not assumed turnkey high-percent cEDH.

**The newly surfaced neutral Stage A option:** **ADD Shadowspear / CUT Demon's Horn**, alt cut **Witch of the Moors**. Shadowspear costs 1+equip2, equipped Sol'kanar becomes 6/6 and gets lifelink/trample. With Urborg or Blanket on battlefield and opponent defending with a land, the King typically cannot be creature-blocked via swampwalk. On a clean opponent hit he deals 6 commander damage and gains **6 life from ONE lifelink event**, which can be paid into drain engines; provides flavorful direct King pressure without erasing black-cast identity. Shadowspear removal of opponent hexproof/indestructible for {1} is useful only combined with an appropriate follow-up interaction. Neither author nor GrokBot considered this named swap. Thus previous KEEP/KEEP or reconciled KEEP does not mechanically exclude it.

**Strongest objection (partially strengthened by unblinding):** Losing Horn removes repeated black-spell life gains **even while King is absent**, including ALL opponents' spellcasts when Painter BLACK, and thereby multiple independent triggers of **Starscape Cleric and Marauding Blight-Priest**, which lose precisely ONE opponent life per gain EVENT, not amount. With King+Horn and one black cast, two distinct 1-life gains create two separate Cleric/Priest pings; after -Horn one black cast produces just one King gain. One clean lifelink hit from equipped King is a single 6-life event, hence only ONE Cleric/Priest trigger; for Vito or Enduring Tenacity, the gain AMOUNT determines damage/life-loss, so a one-time six can be significant. Horn thus has BOTH cast-flood life-event-density and commanderless backup; removing it for combat lifelink could be a net loss even with six-point hit. Witch of the Moors instead is a five-mana conditionally triggered but identity-linked each-opponent edict/creature-recursion piece, not a consequence-free cut. Avoid declaring either swap proved.
