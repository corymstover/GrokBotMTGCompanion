## Identity-first strategic engine

**Black-spell-to-life engine:** Sol'kanar gains **1 life per black CAST** by any player, independently of black spell resolving. Painter's Servant may choose BLACK, causing spells cast by any player of any original color to be black as well; each then triggers Sol'kanar (and the separate included Demon's Horn, which independently has OPTIONAL gain 1 on each black cast). Painter also allows the existing **Grindstone** to repeat milling as matching-color cards are found; this is a distinct secondary pressure tool with possible library-reshuffle/interaction caveats, not a guaranteed one-trigger instant table win. Baleful Strix, Demon's Horn, Bontu's Monument, Crypt Ghast, K'rrik, Son of Yawgmoth, Jet Medallion, Black Market Connections and Dread Presence relate to black casting, mana/life conversion and black-colored permanents. The Soul Stone, Alhammarret's Archive and Bolas's Citadel are expensive high-ceiling life/mana/draw slots rather than replacements for Sol'kanar's unique trigger.

**Drain payoffs:** Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose, Enduring Tenacity, Marauding Blight-Priest and Starscape Cleric turn separate life-gain EVENTS into opponents losing life, with different targeting/each-opponent/activation constraints; Exquisite Blood then gains life after opponents lose life, creating potentially self-reinforcing finite-stack interactions until a player loses or an ability is interrupted. Bloodchief Ascension plus mill and damage, Bloodletter of Aclazotz doubling opponents' life loss, Bloodthirsty Conqueror, Gray Merchant, Underworld Dreams, and black X spells offer different win pathways. This is a black-cast lifegain/drain deck, NOT generic Grixis artifacts-storm despite containing fast mana, interaction and efficient combos.

**King's named evasion:** Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth makes ALL battlefield lands Swamps in addition to their other land types; **Blanket of Night** likewise gives lands Swamp subtype, so even an opponent playing no black has a Swamp while these effects remain. Sol'kanar's swampwalk then lets him attack that player unblocked by creatures, but not through nonblocking damage prevention or removal, and **does not itself grant lifelink**. This is important: the current equipment-free 5/5 can deal 5 commander damage but yields **no combat life gain trigger** intrinsically.

## Fresh unranked challenger

**Primary unapproved hypothesis: ADD Shadowspear / CUT Demon's Horn.** Shadowspear costs {1}, equips for {2}, grants equipped Sol'kanar +1/+1, lifelink and trample, giving a 6/6 commander. With Urborg or Blanket and at least one defending land, a clean player hit deals six combat damage and independently gains six life through lifelink; this can trigger Vito / Enduring Tenacity / Marauding Blight-Priest / Starscape Cleric, Exquisite Blood feedback, Aetherflux threshold, Witch of the Moors or Archives as actually applicable. Paying {1} to remove opposing permanents' hexproof and indestructible until end of turn provides noncombat utility (not itself removal or evasion against sacrifice/wipes). The addition makes the King personally a life-gaining threat instead of exclusively a stationary spell-response piece; identity-first case is stronger than a generic efficiency upgrade.

**Main tradeoff:** Demon's Horn {2} independently grants OPTIONAL **1 life per black spell cast by any player**, duplicated from the commander. Redundancy matters: if Sol'kanar is removed/countered, Horn maintains a low-frequency ongoing source for drain; when Painter is black, ALL table spells fire both and with Vito/Blight Priest each Horn gain is a **separate event**. Cutting Horn loses triggers EVERY turn, including opponents' turns. Shadowspear grants no life until a creature connects (or deals damage with lifelink) and its equip costs a total three mana even when Sol'kanar is absent; waiting on five-mana commander + equip and on combat is a huge opportunity. Even with swampwalk, opponents may destroy/bounce Sol'kanar, remove Urborg/Blanket, kill equipment, prevent damage or end game earlier. Therefore this is **NEEDS TESTING, not an automatic upgrade**.

**Alternative cut for same ADD:** Witch of the Moors, a relatively expensive and conditionally timed creature, looks more detachable from one-mana casts, but it gives legitimate end-step opposing sacrifice and graveyard reuse if life was gained, so cuts removal/recovery while adding combat setup. Do NOT call this low-cost by name alone. Secondary candidate Whip of Erebos grants team lifelink but four mana and reanimation feel generic; the one-mana Shadowspear is the more focused commander-specific thought experiment.

## Exact conditional rules and counterplay probes

1. With Sol'kanar on battlefield and one BLACK spell **cast by any player**: one King trigger grants 1 life even if spell gets countered. With Demon's Horn present, its separate optional trigger offers another life-gain event; life-loss engines may trigger for each distinct gain event. Creating a spell COPY, resolving an ability, or returning a card to battlefield is not another CAST. If Painter chooses BLACK, an originally nonblack spell cast still qualifies; if opponent removes Painter before a later cast, that later spell's original actual colors control.
2. With Urborg or Blanket maintaining Swamp subtype on defender's land, commander swampwalk prevents creature blocking. Equipped Shadowspear +1/+1 makes Sol'kanar 6/6 lifelink. A single unprevented hit on an opponent gains **6 life** via lifelink and does 6 commander damage; this is **not** six distinct life-gain events. For Vito, gain-six triggers ONCE with X=6; opponent targeted by Vito's trigger loses six if trigger resolves, not six separate one-point pings. Without Shadowspear baseline 5 commander combat damage causes no life gain by itself.
3. Urborg adds Swamp subtype, not color BLACK to spells or to nonland permanents. Blanket of Night makes Swamps but doesn't by itself make a spell black. Only Painter's Servant selected BLACK achieves global black coloring. Grindstone/Painter is a separate option, not a life trigger loop from a copy.
4. With Exquisite Blood and Vito/Enduring Tenacity/Marauding Blight-Priest/Starscape Cleric, a positive life-gain event and life-loss feedback can continue in turns; target availability, triggers, prevention, cards leaving battlefield and players losing must be accounted for. No win probability or unstoppable deterministic turn assumed.
5. Antagonists: early Disenchant-equivalent hit to Painter/Blanket/Shadowspear, instant removal of King before attack, fixed defenses even versus swampwalk, anti-lifegain effects, anti-trigger effects, broad enchantment removal, mana flood/screw, or pod casting few black spells are meaningful tests. No claim that a 6/6 Shadowspear King is immune to interactions or naturally a two-hit commander kill.
