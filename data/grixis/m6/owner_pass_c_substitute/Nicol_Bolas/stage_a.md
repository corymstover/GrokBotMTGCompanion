## 2. Unique identity and natural optimized ceiling

This is **original Nicol Bolas**, not Nicol Bolas, the Ravager. An eight-mana `{2}{U}{U}{B}{B}{R}{R}` legendary **Elder Dragon**, 7/7 flying; at the beginning of your upkeep you must pay `{U}{B}{R}` or sacrifice it, and **whenever Nicol Bolas deals damage to an opponent, that player discards their ENTIRE hand**. The trigger is **damage**, not specifically combat damage, so a *single* damage dealt by Bolas using a granted tap-to-ping ability or a direct damage spell is strategically comparable to an unblocked 7-damage combat hit for hand disruption. Prevented damage, dealing damage to creatures, damage by another permanent, or life loss do NOT satisfy its trigger. Natural mission: amplify an expensive terrifying Elder Dragon's direct-damage/hand-emptiness effect, reliably land/safeguard him, exploit emptied hands with `Waste Not`, `Geth's Grimoire`, `Tinybones`, `Raiders' Wake`, `Liliana's Caress`, `Court of Ambition`, and convert that resource asymmetry into protected damage/attacks/closure. Strong optimized **Bracket 4 plausible** within actual commander costs; do not claim a natural Bracket 5 cEDH speed on an 8-mana 2/2/2 colored requirement plus ongoing three-pip upkeep.

There is a remarkably specific existing interaction suite:

- **Direct activation**: `Viridian Longbow` equips original Bolas to grant `{T}: THIS creature deals 1 damage to any target`. `Quicksilver Dagger` Aura grants Bolas `{T}: this creature deals 1 damage to target player or planeswalker; you draw a card`. If Bolas targets an opponent, that **one** damage discards that opponent's whole hand, without needing an attack or combat evasion. With `Curiosity` attached to Bolas, Bolas dealing damage to that opponent triggers extra card draw; neither Curiosity nor a non-Bolas source itself forces their discard.
- **Attack-trigger damage bypasses combat connection**: `Ingris Stingerquill` says whenever a creature you control attacks, **that attacking creature itself deals 1 damage to each opponent**. If original Bolas attacks with Ingris present, the Bolas-caused separate point at declare attackers can provoke a full-hand discard trigger for EACH opponent before blockers/combat damage (unless prevented, redirected or otherwise interrupted). Ingris' generic token/haste activated ability at {4} is secondary; commander is still summoning sick unless given haste.
- **Combat spread**: `Kediss, Emberclaw Familiar` triggers *after* Bolas deals combat damage to one opponent, then the commander **itself** deals that much noncombat damage to each other opponent. The other opponents discard their hands from Bolas-caused damage, but only the first hit is combat damage for commander damage accounting. `Brotherhood Regalia` on Bolas grants ward {2} and unblockability with a favorable legendary equip cost; `Shizo, Death's Storehouse`, `Lightning Greaves`, `Swiftfoot Boots`, `Arena of Glory` and `Hall of the Bandit Lord` each carry timing, protection, color-source and targeting costs.
- **Entering battlefield**: `Dragon Tempest` gives a flying creature haste on entry and says an entering Dragon **itself deals** X to any target (X = count of Dragons). If original Bolas enters as your Dragon while the Tempest is in play, X≥1 and targeting an opponent can provoke a Bolas hand-discard trigger *the same turn* even if haste does not grant an immediate attack connection. If a Dragon is removed before the Tempest trigger resolves, detailed source/last-known-information can matter; do not count speculative extra Dragons that are absent.
- **Bolas as mass-damage source**: `Chandra's Ignition` targets Bolas and has **that creature** deal damage equal to power (normally seven) to every other creature and each opponent, wiping all opponents' hands if Bolas still a legal target at resolution. `Pain for All` Aura says when it enters, **enchanted creature** deals damage equal to its power to any other target; target an opponent with original Bolas to wipe that hand, plus later Aura triggers when enchanted creature is damaged. This costs exposure to instant-speed creature/Aura removal and rules about target legality at ETB resolution. Avoid treating other artifacts' or Auras' own damage as Bolas damage.
- **Extra combat**: `Aggravated Assault` plus `Sword of Feast and Famine` could support recurring combat cycles if sufficient mana, a successful hit, untapped lands, and opponents' ability to interact. The initial original Bolas hand wipe can enable lockout, but repeat combats aren't an automatic deterministic infinity because Sword damage triggers, blockers and colored/mana taxes must line up.
- **Story and fallbacks**: `Nicol Bolas, the Ravager // the Arisen` is a second 4-mana on-flavor legendary entry (not the commander) that makes opponents discard one, can transform into powerful planeswalker later. `Tergrid` captures permanents discarded by opponents; `Oppression` and discard enablers can maintain pressure when commander not accessible.

## 3. Mana, curve and recurring upkeep economics

**Color demands are the defining limiter.** The commander requires **eight mana with UU, BB, RR** on a single main-phase action, in a deck with U20/B21/R19 potential land sources, 37 lands, nine fetches, conditional/tapped/costly utilities. Recast cost ten then twelve mana **plus the recurring UBR each beginning of your upkeep**; a "turn-three Bolas via generic rocks" cannot be credited without specifically paying **all six colored pips**. Fast sources include Sol Ring, Chrome Mox, Mox Diamond, Mana Vault, Grim Monolith, Ancient Tomb, Fellwar Stone, Arcane Signet, three Signets, three Talismans and rituals `Dark Ritual`, `Cabal Ritual`, `Seething Song`, `Jeska's Will`. Most explosive colorless sources cannot pay second U/B/R pips without lands/filters; Signets require another input, Chrome Mox needs imprint, Diamond discards land, Cabal ritual threshold conditional, Will requires opponent hand and controller of commander for secondary mode.

Neutral mana report's 0.93313 "all required colors present by base turn 8" is **only** source-presence in a fixed no-mulligan packet, not turn-eight *cast probability*; precise double-pip, generic resource, land drop, tapped lands, ritual conversion and opponent disruption are not jointly computed. Land model inventories 14 "fast mana" including Ritual and rocks but cannot independently prove a fast protected commander.

Upkeep maintenance `{U}{B}{R}` must be set aside even once Bolas sticks. A draw-seven/wheel is absent here; the high card-advantage payoff comes from opponents' discarded hands via Geth, Waste Not, Tinybones, not drawing the table into answers. Tap-to-ping abilities cannot activate under summoning sickness unless Bolas has haste (either printed or granted), even if granted by equipment/Aura *earlier*; haste enablers must be explicitly available and attachment timing legal. `Lightning Greaves` shroud conflicts with targeting Bolas using `Fire Whip`, `Quicksilver Dagger`, `Curiosity`, `Pain for All` or a new equipment *while Greaves is attached*; moving Greaves may expose him to answers. Hexproof from Boots differs and allows own spells to target.

## 4. Neutral weakest slots, candid challengers and competing cuts

**Primary adversarial candidate, strictly NOT a swap recommendation:** **`+ Fire Whip / - Hypnotic Specter`**. `Fire Whip`, {1}{R} Aura enchant creature you control, grants **the enchanted creature** an activated tap ability to deal 1 damage to any target. This is one more low-mana way to convert an already-resolved, hasted or prior-turn original Nicol Bolas into *full-hand discard at instant speed*, bypassing blockers, protection-from-combat damage and repeat seven-mana attack sequencing. It complements existing Viridian Longbow and Quicksilver Dagger, increasing density of the commander's most unique damage trigger. It also has "sacrifice this Aura: it deals 1" utility, **but that mode is Fire Whip dealing damage, NOT Nicol Bolas**, so it does not trigger original Bolas's discard (an important attribution trap). `Hypnotic Specter`, 1BB small flying creature, can cause **one random discard** when it deals combat damage, is fragile and not Bolas-specific. Trading it for a Bolas pinger might substantially improve commander's individual ceiling and redundancy.

**Best counterargument to my favorite:** This is an **eight-mana commander** that must survive turn-cycle/haste and cost {UBR} next upkeep. Fire Whip alone does nothing without a vulnerable creature, and a pointed Aura can be a 2-for-1 loss under creature removal; it is dead when commander is countered/taxed. Bolas already has direct sources from Ingris, Tempest, Pain for All, Chandra's Ignition, Quicksilver and Longbow; a third tap source may be diminishing returns. Hypnotic Specter comes down around turn three and reduces opponents' hand before Bolas; it survives commander removal and contributes independent early disruption. Crucially the natural power ceiling may reward more early stax/discard, not a fragile eighth "make Bolas do damage" card. If that independent pressure is needed to reach turn eight, the swap backfires. **NEEDS TESTING** is more honest than asserting a strict upgrade.

**Other plausible contested cuts:**

- `The Eldest Reborn` is a 5MV slow saga removing creature, causing discard and reanimating; commander-flavored, but a weak slot in fast pods. `-The Eldest Reborn` for Fire Whip would keep Hypnotic early discard and trade generic late value for Bolas-specific damage; perhaps a better head-to-head than cutting Specter.
- `No Mercy` is a defensive 4MV pillow-fort that has no direct Bolas trigger but helps a slow eight-mana commander avoid dying before activation. Removing it to increase proactive combo density may lose to wide attackers.
- `Raiders' Wake` four-mana discard payoff/life drain can be inefficient after hands are emptied, but it does have hand-control synergy and may punish opponents who reassemble. Competing against Fire Whip requires calculating a realistic hand-discard event count, not judging a named enchantment weak on mana value.
- `Geth's Grimoire` draws on *each* opposing discard, potentially explosive when Bolas removes all hands; a tempting four-mana cut on abstract curve, but the commander would be less rewarding if it sticks. Do not auto-cut.
- `Viridian Longbow`, `Quicksilver Dagger`, `Pain for All`, `Ingris`, `Kediss`, `Dragon Tempest`, `Chandra's Ignition` are strongly commander-defining; proposed substitutions must explain why losing their different damage delivery window is worthwhile.

**Other unranked plausible challengers:** `Hermetic Study` and `Psionic Gift` can grant equivalent T:1 damage without Fire Whip's sacrifice option, in blue; `Thornbite Staff` grants a costed tap-to-damage channel with extra equip expense and a death-trigger untap (but not an immediate cheap Bolas pinger); `Whispersilk Cloak` supplies unblockability/shroud but prevents friendly Aura targeting until moved and may duplicate Regalia; `Dragon Breath` offers haste/firebreathing but overlaps already-dedicated haste suite. None is mandatory. Stage A purpose is to challenge, not produce a candidate-ranking pipeline.

## 5. Explicit opponent counterplay and matchup scenarios

**Scenario A — fast spell-combo table:** Against opponents who can threaten on turn 3–5, assembling original Nicol Bolas's 8-mana, six-color-pip spell may be unrealistic without premium fast colored mana and protection. `Hypnotic Specter`, `Oppression`, `Counterspell`, `Notion Thief` and cheap hand removal may help survive; `Fire Whip` in hand with no Bolas does not. Do not infer protected commander by turn four from mana proxies.

**Scenario B — medium-range creature table:** Bolas eventually lands with `Dragon Tempest` or haste and `Ingris`; an attack can discard all opponents' hands **on declaration**, but one timely removal on Ingris/commander before trigger resolution changes outcome. Instant-speed ping from Fire Whip versus longbow/dagger has value against obstructing flying/reach defenders, but `Toxic Deluge`, `No Mercy`, `Cyclonic Rift` may be what kept player alive.

**Scenario C — blue draw-go/counterplay:** Opponent counters 8-mana Bolas, holding UBR maintenance resources; protect with `Force of Will` (pitch a blue spell), `Fierce Guardianship` free only while controlling commander, or paid `Mana Drain`. A tutor for Fire Whip instead of armor/land can lose. If Bolas is on battlefield, opponent can destroy in response to equipping or casting Aura; Aura with illegal only target does not resolve. If original Bolas damage trigger is already on stack from a hit, removing Bolas after damage doesn't prevent the triggered discard.

**Scenario D — high-resource attrition with graveyard recursion:** `Tergrid` can convert hands into permanents, `Waste Not`/Geth into cards and creatures; discard can enable enemy graveyard strategies. `Bojuka Bog` targeted graveyard exile enters tapped, `Oppression` hits us too, `No Mercy` and protecting Bolas matter. Opponent can hold instant-speed removal in hand and use it in response to Bolas ping/trigger; if first ping hits, the entire hand becomes graveyard only when discard trigger resolves, not on damage.
