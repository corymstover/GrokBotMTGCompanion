## Distinct strategic identity and verified mechanical core

This is **original Nicol Bolas**, eight-mana flying 7/7 Elder Dragon with mana cost {2}{U}{U}{B}{B}{R}{R}, recurring beginning-of-upkeep {U}{B}{R} payment or self-sacrifice. Whenever **Bolas himself deals damage to an opponent**, that opponent discards their ENTIRE hand. Combat connection is not required: enabled damage to an opponent from Viridian Longbow, Quicksilver Dagger, Ingris Stingerquill attack trigger, Dragon Tempest entry, Pain for All entry, Chandra's Ignition and Kediss spread can separately trigger the discard. Damage by a different source or mere life loss cannot. The right commander-first natural ceiling is **optimized Bracket 4 plausible**, not a general Grixis turbo-cEDH template; this is a strategic assessment consistent with prior approved deck, not a formally measured power bracket.

Timing/identity details that materially matter:

1. Ingris makes **the attacking Nicol itself** deal 1 to EACH opponent on an attack-trigger resolution, before blockers if unopposed. Those points are noncombat; each real damage event can strip a full hand even when Bolas never connects in combat.
2. Kediss spreads Nicol's damage to other opponents only after an initial **combat damage** hit; additional spread damage is noncombat and doesn't count toward 21 commander-combat-damage rule, though it still causes separate hand-discard triggers.
3. Dragon Tempest makes an entering Dragon itself deal X damage, providing a possible first-turn Nicol-sourced hand strip; already existing haste does not bypass tap summoning sickness unless actually granted and active. Pain for All and Chandra's Ignition have enchanted/target creature itself deal damage to opponent, and require legal targets/timing. Ignition also damages **your other creatures**, potentially wiping Kediss/Ingris. Last-known-source text on removal before Dragon Tempest/Pain resolves is a specific still-to-verify edge case; don't promise discard after Nicol is gone and cannot trigger.
4. Viridian Longbow and Quicksilver Dagger give Nicol tap-to-damage actions. Fire Whip would also grant enchanted Nicol a tap-to-ping, but its independent "sacrifice this Aura" damage comes from **the Aura**, not Nicol, and will NOT trigger hand discard. Quicksilver also draws a card for the activated ability, an opportunity cost when contemplating replacement.
5. Recurring upkeep UBR can consume all flexible colored mana even after cast; 37 lands plus 14 fast-mana entries cannot transform a few generic rocks into six required colored casting pips. Neutral mana model U20/B21/R19 land potential, 0.93313 three-color-presence by nominal turn-eight is **NOT** a measured 8-mana protected cast probability. Signet filtering and rituals must be paid correctly; repeated tax rises to 10 then 12 total.
6. Greaves shroud impedes targeting own Aura/Equip spells, unlike Boots hexproof; opponents can remove Nicol/Aura before tap activation or ETB effect, and a damage-triggered whole-hand discard occurs only once the triggered ability resolves, leaving response windows. Tergrid and Tinybones, Bauble Burglar may compete for the SAME discarded permanent: both cannot take the same card.
7. Oppression's discard trigger hurts your own counters, and discard alone does not remove opponent board permanents or graveyards. No Mercy, Toxic Deluge, Rift, shields and tempo disruption survive for reasons independent of original Bolas's peak hand strip.

**Direct new hypothesis, not owner ready:** +1 Fire Whip / -1 Hypnotic Specter. Fire Whip (1R) grants original Bolas a reusable tap-1 damage ability: the commander-trigger payoff is an entire opponent hand and Curiosity trigger if attached. However it is vulnerable Aura-dependent redundancy; requires turn8 commander (or cheat), haste/turn-cycle, and gives up a 3-mana independent flying Specter. Additional head-to-head against **+1 Fire Whip/-1 The Eldest Reborn** tests whether preserving precommander disruption is more important than delayed five-mana value. Neither has evidence for measured net improvement.

**Strongest overlooked competitor:** Geode Golem could solve the MUCH larger hurdle of casting the eight-mana six-colored-pip commander: T5 Golem -> T6 combat connection can cast Nicol from command zone without printed cost, first cast with no tax; future tax still mandatory. But it's blockable/removable 5/3, must wait to attack unless hasted, and Golem's damage itself doesn't trigger Nicol. If Bolas fails to arrive in most relevant pods, an extra pinger is low impact. A genuine commander-first test must compare access vs activation, not only ideal hand wipes.

**Strongest counterargument to keeping everyone unchanged:** Commander costs eight colored-intensive mana and future UBR, yet opponents can still hold instant-speed answers. A cheaper damage-providing Aura, a connecting cast-cheat creature or timely protection could noticeably increase full-hand lock frequency. The original author did not evaluate Fire Whip specifically. Conditional alternatives merit tests.

**Strongest counterargument to ANY current swap:** The exact existing deck has overlapping delivery methods already, an unusually high premium fast mana and counter base, and early independent discard/defense that lets the expensive commander ever reach play. A fragile Aura or blockable cast-cheat threat can be inferior to Specter/No Mercy/late saga under real opponent interaction. The three previous keep sources show no measured lift, and this review cannot manufacture one. Existing 100 therefore stays intact.
