## Independently surfaced rules corrections from sealed Stage A

**1. Exact Lutri cost / distinct extra companion action:** Current WotC Oracle is `{1}{U/R}{U/R}` hybrid—pay one generic plus two **either U or R each**. The earlier reconciliation's `{1}{U}{R}` notation is one legal payment, NOT an exhaustive exact Oracle description. The companion {3} generic sorcery-speed *move-to-hand* action occurs first; casting from hand requires this hybrid mana, appropriate priority, and target own instant/sorcery spell on stack. Blinked/not-cast Lutri does not copy. Spell copy created by its ETB is **not cast**; normal cast triggers don't recur, Magecraft cast-or-copy triggers do. The existing deck doesn't need edits to correct the record.

**2. Cecily / no-maximum hand-size timestamp:** With Cecily (`max hand eleven`) and Triskaidekaphile or Thought Vessel (`no maximum`), per official WotC Reliquary Tower ruling, apply continuous maximum-hand-size effects in **timestamp order**. If Vessel/Triska entered *before* Cecily, later Cecily can impose eleven again; hand **thirteen** cannot ordinarily be held past own cleanup solely under the older no-max source. If Vessel/Triska enters *after* Cecily, no maximum holds, allowing thirteen into upkeep for Triska's exactly-thirteen win. One can also reach thirteen after cleanup before upkeep via opponent-turn draws, with protection; hand maximum is only enforced at own cleanup. Previous reviews mentioned Triska but did not spell out this exact state-order risk. This is a **rules/sequence correction**, not a banned-card or decklist problem.

**3. Apex of Power provenance with Cecily/Lutri:** Cecily grants cast-from-HAND without paying mana, so original Apex of Power really **was cast from hand** and on successful resolution exiles top seven AND adds ten mana of any one color. If Lutri then copies Apex on stack, the copy **was not cast**, so it exiles its separate top seven but **does not produce another ten mana**. WotC Core Set 2019 notes expressly state this. Conversely Mnemonic Deluge can create copies and CAST them as its own text says; that different result must not be generalized to Lutri. These distinctions affect real combo resources. No measured new game test claimed.

**4. Harmonic Prodigy vs extra cast potential:** State A: Cecily attacks with ten cards, Prodigy controlled, no other draw replacement; two separate triggers. First resolves draw→11, cast one instant/sorcery free → hand often10, then second draws→11 and MAY cast another distinct hand card free, provided no opposing disruption/first resolution changes hand differently. At nine before attack, first trigger reaches10/no cast, second reaches11/one cast. This is actual expected behavior; a purported guaranteed two casts whenever Prodigy exists is false. Lutri only copies a targeted spell existing on stack when its cast ETB triggers.

## Exact novel rival question vs approved payoff

**Stage A UNAPPROVED proposed comparator: `+Strionic Resonator / -In Garruk's Wake`.** Strionic at two mana plus {2}, tap can copy a Cecily **triggered** attack ability (not Bjorna's activation). With starting ten-card hand and two high-impact legal payloads available, its copied attack trigger can cast a first free spell; the original attack trigger can then cast another after its own draw if hand remains at least eleven. At nine it may upgrade zero to one free spell. It is a sacrificable artifact to Bjorna if it outlives its usefulness. This **is a genuinely commander-specific idea** that neither previous review evaluated as an exact pair.

The preservation cost is major: In Garruk's Wake is a nine-mana free-cast **one-sided enemy creature and planeswalker sweep**, precisely the kind of huge payoff Cecily exists to exploit and a way to clear board/keep Cecily attacking. Resonator duplicates Harmonic Prodigy's *function* but needs two mana *held up before attackers*, itself dies to artifact hate and doesn't replenish hand. If the first free spell spends cards and the second lacks legal targets or cannot be cast, upside collapses; removal of Cecily before attack prevents both. A single Resonator can't act as a spell payoff when opponent kills Cecily repeatedly. Current Bracket 4 plan already has high-output spell copying via Lutri, Harmonic and protection.

**Strongest counterargument to retaining Wake:** expensive sorceries stranded when Cecily cannot reach eleven are the exact 33-land, two-commander reliability risk; Resonator at a lower cost increases frequency of the free ability rather than just its payoff, and backs up vulnerable Prodigy. This merits bounded comparison of actual paid mana, 10-/9-hand start, artifact available for Bjorna, one-sided wipe need and opponent interaction. It's a *material unresolved rival question* but not proven positive or owner approval.
