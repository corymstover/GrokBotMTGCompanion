## Commander identity, distinct team roles and natural ceiling

**Bjorna, Nightfall Alchemist:** low-curve UR Human, `{T}, Sacrifice an artifact:` deal 1 damage to *target creature* and goad it if it survives; cannot aim this activation at players or get further damage merely from Magecraft. He weaponizes Academy Manufactor/Big Score/Brass's Bounty/Storm-Kiln Artist/Clues/Treasures/Sai artifacts, turns expendable tokens into blocker disruption and attacks elsewhere, and gives Cecily a safer swing. Artifact sacrificed is permanent cost; Bjorna activation is not an instant/sorcery spell for Lutri to copy. His artifact synergy is *secondary protective tempo*, not default cEDH combo.

**Cecily, Haunted Mage:** UBR Human Wizard with `Your maximum hand size is eleven`. Whenever she **attacks**, draw one and lose one life, **then** if eleven or more cards remain in hand, you may **cast** one instant/sorcery card from hand without paying its mana cost *during the attack-trigger resolution*. Cast is real (triggers magecraft / Storm-Kiln Artist / cast triggers); target and extra costs remain legal, and the free-cast spell itself later resolves normally. Need eleven **after** draw and loss and before free casting; Robe of the Archmagi combat-damage cards arrive LATER in combat, so ordinarily do not help the **same** attack trigger reach eleven. Attacking doesn't require combat damage to trigger, but she must survive/retain ability until declared attacker. Fierce Guardianship/Deflecting Swat/Greaves/countermagic protect the dangerous swing.

**Lutri, the Spellchaser** is a 3/2 Flash Otter with companion singleton restriction; to access it from outside the game, pay **{3} at sorcery speed to put it into hand**, then separately pay its normal casting cost `{1}{U/R}{U/R}` (unless another *lawful* spell effect alters payment) and **cast it**. Its ETB triggers only if Lutri was CAST, copying one *target instant/sorcery spell you control* currently on stack, new targets allowed. That *spell copy is not cast*, so ordinary cast triggers do not trigger from the copy, but magecraft "cast or copy" does. No repeatable infinite spell copying from the companion slot by itself. Lutri's formal owner exception is not an instruction to pretend companion costs life rather than mana.

**Game plan:** make Treasures/Clues/Food via Academy Manufactor, Big Score, Brass's Bounty, Storm-Kiln, Anvil, Sai and Third Path Iconoclast; leverage Bjorna's small-creature goad while retaining a huge hand via Rhystic Study, Mystic Remora, The One Ring, Yawgmoth's Bargain project exception, Wheel of Fortune, Windfall, Whispering Madness, Dark Deal, Teferi's Ageless Insight, and protection. Swing Cecily at a weak player to **cast** a high-mana spell from hand: Apex of Power, Mnemonic Deluge, Cruel Ultimatum, Peer into the Abyss, In Garruk's Wake, Brass's Bounty; build storm/copy outcomes, apply Niv-Mizzet/Psychosis Crawler pressure, or threaten Triskaidekaphile exact-thirteen alternate win. On-demand Lutri copy adds strategic immediacy at extra mana/priority cost. Natural high ceiling **optimized Bracket 4 with explosive spell-chain turns**; two specialized commander roles and knowingly waived external legality distinguish it from generic tutor/turbo-combo Grixis. No competitive win-rate claim.

## Mechanically consequential checks for Stage B and later testing

1. **Harmonic Prodigy and Cecily:** Cecily is a **Wizard**. Prodigy makes her attack ability trigger one extra time. Each attack trigger draws/loses and independently checks current hand size on resolution, so the second may or may not cast a further instant/sorcery free after the first changed the hand. **Bjorna is a Human, not a Wizard** and its activated artifact-sac ability isn't a triggered Wizard ability. Copies of a spell by Lutri do not create an extra Cecily attack trigger.
2. **Apex of Power split provenance:** If Cecily casts the **actual Apex card from hand** free during attack-trigger resolution, it WAS CAST FROM HAND and on resolving adds ten mana of one color in addition to exiling top seven; zero mana paid does not erase hand zone. If Lutri later copies that spell on stack, the COPY was **not cast from hand** (indeed not cast), and **does not add ten extra mana**—it still exiles its own top seven. Wizards Core Set 2019 official note states this directly. If the original is countered, no 10 mana from its resolution; a surviving copy can still exile cards but not create its own ten mana.
3. **Actual Lutri timing:** You cannot use normal casting cost to cast directly from outside game; {3} companion action is sorcery-speed put-to-hand. If a spell is already on stack when Lutri is merely outside the game, you cannot add it to hand during that opponent-turn stack interval; prepare earlier on own main phase. To copy an instant/sorcery with Lutri, cast with flash while its target still exists, keep target legal at Lutri ETB trigger resolution. Lutri itself ETB if **cast**; blinking it later without casting does not enable its cast-only copy trigger.
4. **Hand-size timestamps and Triskaidekaphile:** Cecily sets max hand size 11. Triskaidekaphile and Thought Vessel state **no maximum**. WotC confirms multiple hand-size continuous effects apply **in timestamp order**: if no-max permanent is older and Cecily later enters, Cecily's eleven may become effective again until an even newer no-max effect; if Cecily is older and Vessel/Triska newer, no maximum survives. This affects carrying **exactly 13** cards into next upkeep for Triska's win (but max-hand enforcement occurs only in own cleanup; an opponent end-step draw can still establish thirteen without this issue). A blanket statement 'Triska always makes Cecily max unlimited' is unsafe. Triska's exact-thirteen threshold is not Cecily's eleven threshold.
5. **Magecraft vs spell copy:** Archmage Emeritus draws from *cast OR copy* instant/sorcery. Storm-Kiln Artist Magecraft makes a Treasure from cast/copy; Harmonic Prodigy may duplicate triggers of Wizards/Shamans but only proper subjects. Cecily's free spell is CAST; Lutri's duplication is COPY only. `Mnemonic Deluge` may say cast its generated copies, a different mechanism: distinguish text and zones rather than collapse into generic "copy triggers casts".
6. **Trade Secrets / Bargain official status:** these cards have real strategic hand advantage, and their formal project exception is intentional. They must remain labeled project-only; any outside sanctioned Commander legality report must separately FAIL the banned pieces. Do not use a routine legality filter to delete them silently.
