## Commander uniqueness and realistic power ceiling

Mairsil's entrance **may exile an artifact or creature card from hand or graveyard with a cage counter**. For as long as owned exiled cards keep these counters, Mairsil has **their activated abilities**, and **each acquired activated ability** may be activated only once each turn. Cards remain caged even after Mairsil leaves the battlefield; returning Mairsil sees the same reservoir and adds another cage. The exiled card's static or triggered abilities are **not** borrowed. Wizards specifically confirms that colon-written activated abilities transfer, self-references refer to Mairsil, identical abilities acquired from different exiled cards are separately usable once each, a countered ability still consumes that turn's activation, linked abilities can have special failure states, and merely creating a copy of Mairsil can trigger a new cage even if legend rule immediately removes a copy. **Do not misapply an exiled card's ETB, characteristic-defining, upkeep or static ability to Mairsil.**

The engine is therefore **cage acquisition -> haste/survive a turn -> activate one or more carefully chosen tools -> blink/recast/new object or copy an ability -> repeat**. Main lines are caged **Arcanis** (tap draw 3), **Asmodeus** (`BBB` activated draw 7 without automatically acquiring the Archfiend's separate replacement static ability), **Gilded Lotus** (tap 3 mana of one color), **Tree of Perdition** (tap exchange opponent life total with Mairsil's toughness), **Hateflayer** (red/generic + untap-symbol cost to damage equal to creature power), **Minion of Leshrac/Shauku** removal, **Nevinyrral's Disk** sweep, **Cephalid Inkshrouder** discard-for-shroud/unblockable, **Omen Hawker** ability-only mana, and **Aetherling/Endling/Morphling/Torchling** escape/survival/untap. **Anger** in a graveyard with a Mountain you control, **Lightning Greaves**, and **Thousand-Year Elixir** address tap-symbol summoning sickness; **Displacer Kitten**, **Essence Flux**, **Aetherling**, **Spark Double** and **Pack Rat** influence cage count or activation diversity. The natural strategic identity is a **single adaptable cage toolbox**, not Grixis generic goodstuff; the ceiling appears optimized but commander-dependent and weak to removal, exile/graveyard hate before caging and restricted activated abilities. Provisional **Bracket 4 — Optimized** subject to source confirmation after seal, not an unsupported cEDH ranking.

## Mana, availability and timing weaknesses

- **32 lands**, three basics, several fast/colorless rocks and conditional `Mox Diamond`. `Ancient Tomb`, `Minamo`, and some channels provide little/no black/red, while modal Pathways commit to one face, and Xander's Lounge/conditional slow lands may create early ETB tempo. Mairsil needs UBR by turn 3–4, then colored `{U}`, `{B}{B}{B}` or `{2}{R}` to use the best caged abilities. **Gilded Lotus** in the grave/hand can be caged and immediately provide triple colored mana only if Mairsil can use its acquired tap ability, so haste and activation timing still apply; using its tap consumes Mairsil for other tap abilities that turn unless untapped.
- The **cage bottleneck** is the real constraint: a card must be in **hand or graveyard** when Mairsil enters, not just in deck, and exiling is optional but exiled resources are no longer graveyard resources. `Buried Alive`, `Entomb`, `Unmarked Grave`, `Intuition`, `Gamble`, `Faithless Looting`, `Oriq Loremage` and cheap loot improve access but do not ensure the correct protected commander survives. `Unmarked Grave` cannot find **legendary** creature cards; it can find many important nonlegendary tools but **not Asmodeus, Arcanis** or similar legendary targets.
- **Haste and removal windows:** Mairsil normally enters as a 4/4 with summoning sickness; `{T}` and `{Q}` (untap symbol) activated abilities are constrained by having controlled the creature continuously since the start of the turn unless it has haste. Greaves gives haste but also shroud, which can hinder your `Essence Flux` and `Minamo` target lines. If Mairsil blinks with Aetherling, it returns at the beginning of the **next end step**, not immediately, and the original object no longer exists; the current-turn activation budget of the returned new object is distinct, but the delayed return is an opportunity for other players.
- **One-activation restriction:** repeated untapping with Horseshoe Crab, Morphling or Staff **does not, by itself, permit a second use of a caged Tree/Arcanis activation in the same turn**. `Rings of Brighthearth` copies a nonmana activation on resolution of its trigger for `{2}`, not by independently activating an ability; that is different from simply ignoring Mairsil's per-turn limit. A copied ability may need separate targets and legal source state to matter.
- **Activated-only asymmetry:** caging Asmodeus gives a raw `BBB: draw seven` activated ability but not its static exile replacement; caging Disk gives its `{1},{T}` sweep without inheriting Disk's enters-tapped property. Caging an artifact or creature with mainly *triggered* value is wasted. Caged `Omen Hawker` generates `{C}{U}` restricted to **activating abilities**, not paying for commander or countermagic. Self-referencing abilities are applied to Mairsil (including sacrifice costs!).

## Paid, conditional kill and opponent responses

**Tree of Perdition + Hateflayer on one Mairsil:** With Mairsil able to use `{T}` this turn, two caged abilities available and enough `{2}{R}` for Hateflayer, tap via Tree's ability to exchange one target opponent's life total with **Mairsil's current toughness (normally 4)**. That opponent becomes 4 life and Mairsil's toughness becomes the old opposing life total, while its **power normally remains 4**. Hateflayer's untap-symbol cost can then untap this tapped Mairsil to deal power-based damage to the same target for lethal if no lifegain/prevention, and the target remains legal. The untap is a *cost*, not a response window after tapping; the damage ability itself uses the stack and may be responded to. If the opponent removes/blinks Mairsil before Tree's exchange resolves, official Tree rulings state the exchange does not happen. Effects changing base power/toughness, shroud, opponent protection and any commander death change this math. This is a **two-ability single-opponent** line, not an automatically demonstrated multiplayer instant win; repeat use in one turn needs independently valid fresh ability instances or blink/new objects.

**Quicksilver Elemental / Marvin complications:** Caged Quicksilver can grant new activated-ability instances to Mairsil by targeting a creature, possibly Mairsil itself; copy effects and once-per-turn restrictions must be traced under actual current CR and source object identity. `Marvin, Murderous Mimic` on field may mirror abilities without becoming Mairsil and has its own timing/tap limits. **Do not declare unlimited uses or deterministic wins from these labels** without mapping which instance has which limitation, source, target, activation payment and summoning-sickness state. `Spark Double` as a nonlegendary copy may independently activate caged abilities and acquire its own cage from entering. A cloned legendary Mairsil still faces the legend rule unless modified, though the ETB trigger exists.

**Adversarial opponents:** stack interaction can counter commander, blink or removal of Mairsil can strand a delayed kill; grave hate before cage (e.g. instant grave exile) can remove targets if a caged candidate is expected from graveyard; Torpor Orb/strict ETB suppression may prevent new cages; `Cursed Totem`, `Collector Ouphe` for relevant creature and artifact activations, `Pithing Needle` naming Mairsil, and generic stax hit the toolbox. Exiled cages themselves persist through creature death, but counterspell/tax on a 4-mana commander plus delayed reentry is significant. Greaves shroud defends targeted enemy spells but also prevents friendly targets.

## Unranked challengers, alternatives and best objections

**Primary neutral challenger: `+Agatha's Soul Cauldron`.** A `{2}` artifact with a tap activation that exiles a card from a graveyard; when the exiled card is a creature, a target friendly creature receives a +1/+1 counter. Creatures you control with +1/+1 counters gain the **activated abilities of creature cards exiled by Cauldron**, and activation costs of such creatures are discounted. This is a **commander-first structural challenger**: a second activated-ability archive accessible even when Mairsil is removed, plus potential ability versions on a countered Mairsil **not created by Mairsil's own one-use-per-turn cage-grant**. But these exiled cards do **not automatically receive cage counters** and are disjoint from Mairsil's caged exile archive; Cauldron and Mairsil compete for scarce best graveyard candidates. The Cauldron tap activation may be summoning-independent as a noncreature artifact, but its granted **creature tap/untap abilities still obey summoning sickness**. Any proposed immediate multi-activation win therefore remains a rules/test hypothesis, not established.

**Meaningful potential cuts for Cauldron:** (a) **`Gilded Lotus`**—five-mana generic-to-triple-colored artifact can be slow as hardcast but is a premium *caged* tap color engine, especially with mana-intensive Asmodeus and activation copy; (b) **`Pack Rat`**—its discarded-card clone ability may appear slow yet creates an entering Mairsil-copy cage trigger even if legend rule removes the token; (c) **`Nevinyrral's Disk`**—slow hardcast artifact but exceptionally efficient as a caged optional sweep, particularly with Aetherling response. These slots are **not obviously worse**: each removes a unique Mairsil tool and could make Cauldron a downgrade. The only defensible current claim is that the addition merits targeted evidence.

**Other scoped candidates:** `Illusionist's Bracers` copies activated nonmana abilities without Rings' recurring `{2}` but costs `{2}` plus **equip 3**, falls off when Mairsil blinks and doesn't become a usable ability when caged merely for equip; `Sakashima of a Thousand Faces` as another Mairsil copier, but current Spark Double/Marvin/Pack Rat already provide copy diversity and its four mana cost is real; a second instant blink such as `Ghostly Flicker` can add cages and rescue the commander but is harder to cast than Essence Flux. Reject blanket substitution of commander identity with generic Grixis storm or cEDH Oracle/Consultation without demonstrable gain.

**Strongest counterargument to Cauldron:** It splits the deck's two exile archives and can strand the wrong tool in the wrong exile pile. Mairsil's ETB itself already stores a persistent reusable cage across deaths; a second two-mana engine may reduce tutor density/interaction, while losing `Gilded Lotus` or `Pack Rat` removes actual commander-enabled combo resources. A Cauldron on the battlefield can be hit by artifact hate, and its activation to collect a creature is bottlenecked by untap step (unless effects supply more). It is often a *sidegrade* rather than unequivocal optimization.
