## Commander-first strategy and natural ceiling

Mairsil (`{1}{U}{B}{R}`) builds a persistent **cage-counter exile toolbox**: each ETB exiles an owned artifact or creature card from hand or grave, each acquired activated ability can be used **once per turn**, and the caged cards remain available across blinks, death and recasts. `Entomb`, `Buried Alive`, `Unmarked Grave`, `Intuition`, `Faithless Looting` place targets; `Anger`, `Lightning Greaves`, `Thousand-Year Elixir` enable timely tap/untap abilities; `Aetherling`, `Essence Flux`, `Displacer Kitten`, `Pack Rat`, `Spark Double` allow new objects/cage acquisition; `Rings`, `Training Grounds`, `Quicksilver Elemental` reward precise activated-instance management. **Tree of Perdition + Hateflayer** can turn one opponent's life total to Mairsil's toughness and then use an untap-symbol cost to deal its power as lethal damage, if source/timing/priority are correct. **Asmodeus** provides `BBB: draw seven` when caged without its separate nonactivated exile-on-draw replacement. This is a real identity-driven and opponent-interactable power plan, best assessed at source **Bracket 4 — Optimized**, not inflated to generic Oracle/Consultation Bracket 5.

Mana availability matters disproportionately: **UBR on time to cast Mairsil**, then additional U/B/BBB and haste before executing activated answers, plus cards to cage. Colorless acceleration is not a proxy for meeting pips. Protect the current 67 nonland library cards absent a stronger tested reason to cut one.

## Rules correction and interaction risk

1. **Marvin, Murderous Mimic:** October 14 approval-era source claimed Marvin inherits unrestricted Mairsil cage abilities and creates uncapped loops. Engineering `data/grixis/m1_corrections.json#mairsil-10` **reversed that earlier hypothesis** under CR 113.10a: restrictions travel with the abilities added by Mairsil, so Marvin gets separate **once-per-turn** versions. That is a high-confidence **source-derived** interpretation, not a named official Wizards ruling. GrokBot left it unresolved; do **not** treat earlier uncapped combo claims as valid Phase B proof.
2. **Quicksilver Elemental:** copying an acquired activated ability can produce new instances with their own usage records, but each may still inherit a printed/instructional use limit. Counting copies from Quicksilver, `Rings`, Marvin and separate Mairsil objects requires precise latest comprehensive rules and paid mana/stack trace, not casual multiplication to "infinite". This is an explicit priority-2 mechanically uncertain combo test.
3. **Tree/Hateflayer:** pay `Tree` `{T}` under haste, resolve life/toughness exchange with one target, then pay Hateflayer's untap-symbol cost plus `{2}{R}` to deal Mairsil's **power**, usually 4, to that target. Opponent life changes and Mairsil's toughness changes; power normally does not. A blink/removal in response can undo exchange or leave no target, and each per-turn cage instance is consumed. Don't claim table-wide lethal by targeting four players with one activation.
4. **Asmodeus and special cage rules:** caging activated `BBB: draw seven` doesn't acquire unrelated replacement static; caging linked activated abilities may leave them nonfunctional without their linked imprint/exile state. Tapped-ability activation needs haste; Greaves shroud blocks friendly targeted blink/Minamo while attached.
5. **Current legality status:** 2026-10-14 exact owner approval plus Scryfall-backed engineering source verification supports the baseline. Targeted current Oracle sources were checked here; **not every card received a fresh 2026-10-21 all-100 Oracle/legal refresh**.
