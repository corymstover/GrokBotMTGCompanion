## Final exact challenger and protected cut

**Proposed add: `+1 Grim Monolith`. The Pass C record itself accepts no swap.

**Why it may improve this specific commander:** Grim costs **`{2}`**, immediately taps for **`{C}{C}{C}`** and so can effectively frontload one generic mana in a same-step artifact-development line. Mai's firebending can pay its generic casting cost during combat while flash permission applies; the Monolith then produces colorless for additional artifacts/activations. It is more special here than in generic Grixis because **Unwinding Clock**, already in the list, untaps the tapped Grim each *opponent's* untap step while Mai permits flashed artifacts throughout the turn cycle. Three opponent turns can create three separate opportunities for CCC, provided a legal spell/activation exists in the relevant step and Mai/Clock survive. Grim is currently Commander-legal and within Grixis color identity; it is a Game Changer but Bracket 4 already includes such cards. Verified sources: [Scryfall Grim Monolith](https://scryfall.com/card/ulg/126/grim-monolith), [Unwinding Clock](https://scryfall.com/card/nph/164/unwinding-clock), [official banned list](https://magic.wizards.com/en/banned-restricted-list).

**Why Tank is the precise costly removal:** Approved **Tundra Tank** (`{2}{B}`, 4/4 Vehicle, Crew 1, firebending 1 when it can actually attack) grants temporary indestructible to a targeted friendly creature when it enters. Under Mai's flash ability this is a real answer to an already-on-stack destroy spell targeting Mai, if the Tank enters and its trigger resolves in time. It also supplies Avatar machinery and extra combat mana only after a crewed legal attack; it is not an always-on 4/4 creature. Losing Tank removes an uncommon commander-preserving answer and theme element; other cheap counters and protection cannot always perform that exact ETB role. The extra generic mana may lose to the future RRR attacks Tank would have saved. Against exile, sacrifice, bounce and -X/-X, Tank's indestructible never sufficed. Compare against other cuts: **Bender's Waterskin** supplies colored opponent-turn mana and untaps naturally; **Baleful Strix / Thought Monitor** replace cards and create flashable artifact creatures; **Avatar Roku** uniquely creates SIX RED whenever **any player attacks**, fueling other players' combats. These should not be silently exchanged for the Grim slot.

**Important false combo prevention:** **Grim + Rings of Brighthearth does NOT produce the same positive unbounded mana as Basalt + Rings.** Grim's `{4}` untap plus `{2}` Rings copy tax consumes the two `{C}{C}{C}` outputs in repeatable use (net zero growth); it cannot replace Basalt without another enabling effect. It offers burst/Clock throughput, not a newly deterministic infinite line. Its colorless can't pay Mai's `{U}{B}{R}`, colored countermagic or blue/black pips. `Null Rod`, `Stony Silence` and `Collector Ouphe` shut down key artifact abilities and can remove this gain; opponent responses to a flashed Grim or to Mai can collapse the intended line.
