## Commander-first identity, natural power ceiling and game plan
Ashnod is a 2UBR 1/4 deathtouch legendary Human Artificer: whenever the controller **activates** a nonmana ability of an artifact or creature and sacrificed at least one permanent **to pay its activation costs**, Ashnod may copy that ability, with new targets if desired. The extra copy is an ability on stack; no second activation or second sacrifice occurs. Her realistic ceiling is a very fast, combo-capable optimized artifact sacrifice engine where each sacrifice pays for **two** draws, tutor effects, conversion effects, removal shots or extra turns, backed by Grixis interaction and recursive artifacts. Its real advantage is *qualitative* conversion of sacrifice costs—not generic Grixis storm.

The list deliberately layers:
1. **Repeatable commander-copy activators:** Time Sieve (five artifacts as one cost yields two extra turns if Ashnod survives until activation), Repurposing Bay (one sacrifice obtains two artifacts each with mana value exactly +1), Kuldotha Forgemaster (three artifacts for two artifact library cheats), Retrofitter Foundry (a sacrificed Servo or Thopter yields two tokens rather than one), Blasting Station, Grinding Station, Arcbound Ravager, Oni-Cult Anvil, Trading Post, Dockside Chef, Executioner's Capsule, Wayfarer's Bauble, Dross Skullbomb, Moonsilver Key, Mnemonic Sphere, Mishra's Bauble, Pyrite Spellbomb, Clan Crafter-granted commander ability. Benefits vary by exact targets, modes, availability and timing; two legal targets/cards may not always exist.
2. **Artifact and death loops/value infrastructure:** Scrap Trawler, Myr Retriever, Emry, Goblin Engineer, Goblin Welder, Scrap Welder, Academy Ruins, Dross Scorpion, Esoteric Duplicator, Sai and Urza, plus cheap artifacts/cost reducers. Some of these engines function without commander, but the commander multiplies sacrifice-cost utility.
3. **Converting loops/value into wins:** Disciple of the Vault, Reckless Fireweaver, Marionette Apprentice, Mayhem Devil, Crime Novelist, Walking Ballista and Ashnod-doubled Bolas's Citadel final activation. Bolas's Citadel's `{T}`, sacrifice ten nonland permanents is a nonmana **artifact** activated ability and deals 10 to each opponent per resolution: with Ashnod it can cause 20 life loss to each (not automatically lethal from 40). WotC War of the Spark notes confirm paying that cost can sacrifice the Citadel itself.
4. **Actual interaction and protection:** Fierce Guardianship, Deflecting Swat, Mana Drain, An Offer You Can't Refuse, Cyclonic Rift, Chaos Warp, Toxic Deluge, Vandalblast. This shell is not an unchecked goldfish; creature/combo timing and the need to resolve a five-mana commander matter.

## Mechanics and false-positive exclusions to guard the ceiling
- **Ashnod's Altar, Krark-Clan Ironworks and Lotus Petal generate mana**: the sacrifice is part of a mana ability, so Ashnod **cannot copy** them. Sacrificing Treasure solely to make mana to fund a nonmana ability also **does not** satisfy her triggering condition.
- Sacrifice to cast a **spell** (e.g., a potential Kuldotha Rebirth challenger) is not activating an artifact/creature ability; no Ashnod copy. Also, sacrificing on *resolution* rather than in the *activation cost* (e.g., Goblin Welder's exchange) is insufficient.
- Copying an activated ability isn't activating it anew and doesn't recursively retrigger Ashnod. Pay the sacrifice/tap/mana activation cost only once. Rings of Brighthearth can separately copy a qualifying nonmana activation for its own {2} payment but does not copy Ashnod's triggered ability just because she triggered.
- **Time Sieve:** 5 artifact sacrifice is substantial and does not inherently replenish five artifacts. Ashnod enables two future turns per activation, not a proven infinite turn lock without reliable feedstock; removal, graveyard disruption and artifact taxes challenge the plan.
- **Repurposing Bay:** it searches twice for cards meeting the SAME mana value (+1 compared with the *one* sacrificed artifact), not for a freely increasing chain of values. It is limited to sorcery speed, tapping, two total artifacts actually available in the library at that value, and opponent interaction.
- **Retrofitter Foundry:** its tapped {T} token-conversion modes cannot be immediately reactivated without an untap route; copies create another token but do not untap it. Tokens copied are not cast spells and no new activation costs are paid.
- **Krark-Clan Shaman:** a sacrifice-cost creature activation is doubled and each resolving ping damages **each nonflying creature**, including Ashnod herself. Two damage from one activation does not kill her 1/4, but additional activations can. This is a double-edged sweeper, not a free one-sided board wipe.
- **Bolas's Citadel:** both resolutions of the copied non-targeting life-loss ability can occur even after the Citadel itself was sacrificed for the cost; ten sacrificed nonland permanents and triple-black casting demand are material. This is not a standalone 40-life kill.
- Removal of Ashnod *before* an activation prevents triggering; removal after the trigger has already triggered does not retroactively remove the trigger from the stack. A named copy target must remain legal on its resolution. Recursion is vulnerable to exile and graveyard hate.

## Mana/curve/access and vulnerabilities
Thirty-three lands include strong untapped Grixis fetch/dual coverage, some naturally colorless utility slots, artifact lands that are unusually valuable sacrifice targets, and eight basics for Wayfarer's Bauble. Fast mana includes Sol Ring, Ancient Tomb, Lotus Petal, Mox Opal conditional on metalcraft, Signet/Talismans, Fellwar/Mind Stone, Etherium Sculptor and Foundry Inspector. Ashnod's five mana with all three colors and Bolas's Citadel's 3BBB must be audited in realistic opening hands; too many colorless lands plus reactive cards can delay the engine. Artifact wipes, Stony Silence-style activations shutoff, graveyard exile, creature removal before she arrives, Rule of Law effects on storm-casting, and stolen tempo by faster combos are actual counterplay. Deflecting Swat/Fierce Guardianship depend on keeping a commander, not proactive blanket protection. This is an optimized combo engine with real setup and interruption windows; provisional optimized Bracket 4 (potential high-end with accessible loops), **not** a proved metagame win-rate or automatic cEDH Bracket 5.

## Adversarial cuts and unranked challengers
- **Krark-Clan Shaman** is a *conditional* weak slot in token-heavy boards, because it clears the controller's nonflying engines and may force repeated sacrifice for larger creatures; countercase is that doubling cheap scalable damage is itself a very Ashnod-specific asymmetrical-sometimes rescue, with many Thopter flyers spared. Never label it dead.
- **Bolas's Citadel** is an expensive BBB, life-dependent, ten-permanent-sacrifice card; countercase is it is both top-of-library engine and uniquely Ashnod-copyable 20-to-each finisher, one of the most commander-justified six-drops.
- **Dross Scorpion** requires artifact-creature death and useful tap targets, potentially low floor; countercase its untaps are exactly how high-ceiling Retrofitter Foundry/Station/Bay sequences sustain.
- **Crime Novelist** is secondary sac payoff and vulnerable creature; countercase its resource conversion on artifact sacrifice and early curve function are natural, not generic filler.
- **Thopter Assembly**, not in the approved deck: six-mana, conditional assembly of five artifact Thopters (only when no other Thopters) plus Time Sieve creates a recurring turn-feed package; Ashnod's two extra turns could increase resilience. But existing Sai and Foundry routinely make Thopters, creating serious internal nonbo, and 6MV demands recasting; not an automatic upgrade. Compare against conditional Shaman or other flex, and require an actual sequence/anti-hate accounting.
- **Ichor Wellspring** / **Servo Schematic**, unranked challengers, are cheap disposable artifact/card/token fodder; their triggered ETB/dies text is **not copied by Ashnod**, unlike sacrifice-activated Dross Skullbomb/Wayfarer's Bauble or Foundry. More fodder may improve Time Sieve consistency, but a superficial 'cheap cantrip artifact' addition could worsen commander identity.
- **Mirkwood Bats**, potential finishing converter for token creation and sacrifice, triggers rather than activation and costs four; overlap with stronger low-curve Disciple/Fireweaver/Marionette/Devil must be justified. Does not solve the five-mana Ashnod access problem.
- Rejected as false synergies: generic spells with additional-sacrifice costs and mana-sacrifice outlets are not automatically multiplied by Ashnod. No recommendation based on a top-card ranking.

## Strongest counterargument and provisional verdict
The strongest counterargument to KEEP is that **a single extra repeatable five-artifact token supply (such as Thopter Assembly, or a cheaper fodder generator) could turn the existing Time Sieve into a much more reliable turn lock**, and high-power Commander can demand that ceiling; allowing a situational self-damaging Krark-Clan Shaman may be less valuable than that route. Against this: Ashnod is already dense with 1-for-2 activations and flexible tutors, current 33 lands/BBB/5CMC means a clunky six-mana additional combo piece can lower average reliability, and Thopter token-producing existing cards interfere with Assembly's upkeep trigger. A claimed infinite combo without executable timing/resource count is not evidence.
