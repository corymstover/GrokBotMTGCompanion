## Agreements and independent stress findings

**Full documentary agreement KEEP**: Stage A, original authored, GrokBot and reconciliation all reach KEEP; no actual approved change is disputed and no prior test demonstrates an upgrade. The independently authored Stage A was not blinded to its own GPT-6 lineage, but prior detailed candidate verdicts were committed-and-sealed before opened. **Confirmed rules distinctions** (prior sources and neutral Stage A converge):
- Only activated nonmana abilities from an artifact or creature with sacrifice as an activation cost are copied. Dockside Chef sacrifice/draw: 1 fodder +1B -> **2 cards**, rather than two sacrifices; Trading Post **two of four** modes; Time Sieve five artifacts -> two extra turns; Bolas's Citadel ten nonland permanents -> opponents lose 10 twice (20 total), if both resolve; Repurposing Bay one artifact -> two independent searches both exactly MV+1, with library availability restrictions.
- Artifact land **Treasure Vault** has a nonmana sacrificial activated ability and IS an artifact source; for paid X=2, its {X}{X}+tap+land sacrifice creates 2 Treasure per resolving effect, **4 total** with Ashnod. Tradeoff: lose an actual colored-neutral land and spend four mana, so not free mana production. In contrast, nonartifact Buried Ruin has sacrifice in land ability but is **not** an artifact/creature source. Artifact land mana taps, Lotus Petal, KCI and Ashnod's Altar sacrifice mana abilities are never doubled. Goblin Welder sacrifice occurs on resolution, not cost.
- Altar of Dementia copy mills the same sacrificed last-known creature POWER again and may choose a new target; Grinding Station gives two three-card mills; Retrofitter Foundry Servo/Thopter sacrifice conversion doubles tokens; Krark-Clan Shaman doubles **symmetrical** nonflying damage and may harm Ashnod. Sai's sacrifice-two-artifacts draw activation is commander-copied; its artifact-cast Thopter creation trigger is not.
- A copy is not a new activation; Rings of Brighthearth's optional {2} payment may generate an additional copy, totaling three ability resolutions for one qualifying sacrifice. Existing Ashnod trigger remains even if Ashnod is removed after it triggered. Copies still require legal targets as they resolve.

**Newly articulated Stage A challenger that earlier reviews did not resolve in detail: `+Thopter Assembly / -Krark-Clan Shaman`** (NOT an approval proposal). Six-mana Assembly has a start-of-upkeep condition that you control no *other* Thopters. With untapped Time Sieve and Ashnod, five newly produced Thopters can be sacrificed for two extra turns, and paying six to replay Assembly enables repetition while all relevant upkeep/tap/land and hand requirements remain met. But it is an expensive *three-piece* commander-dependent line; `Sai, Master Thopterist` automatically creates a Thopter on **casting** Assembly, leaving an *other* Thopter that prevents the later conditional upkeep trigger unless removed. Foundry and other onboard Thopter makers can similarly block the condition. The independent-stage challenger also competes with a one-mana copied artifact-sacrifice sweeper that clears hate creatures. No finite precondition-neutral reliability improvement or damage advantage justifies deleting Shaman. Keep both the approved choice and this possible targeted future comparison clearly distinguished.

**Earlier M5 ranked challenger context:** `+Salvaging Station / -Dockside Chef` is precisely worse for immediate Ashnod ability doubling: Station is 6MV and has NO sacrifice as an activation cost (despite strong death/recursion utility); Chef is inexpensive repeatable doubled draw. `Detective's Satchel`'s Thopter activation's cost also lacks sacrifice; artifact sacrifices earlier in turn are a *condition*, not payment. `Filigree Sages`, `Mycosynth Lattice`, `Basalt Monolith`, `Padeem` can provide distinct utility but fail as direct substitutions at the listed cuts. The two model reports already rejected such false-equality candidate rankings; no reopening warranted.

**Existing deterministic combo receipts, reused with their limits:** (a) Myr Retriever on field + Scrap Trawler in graveyard + Ashnod's Altar + Etherium Sculptor can cycle both artifact creatures, producing net +1 colorless per full round once starting zones/resource conditions are met. This is an altar **mana** combo, not an Ashnod-ability copy. (b) Marionette Apprentice, Nim Deathmantle, Ashnod's Altar and a fabricated Servo generate neutral-mana artifact deaths and drain if reanimation resolves. (c) Dross Scorpion, Retrofitter Foundry and Ashnod's Altar can cycle Servos with net-zero mana and infinite chosen finite artifact-ETB/death triggers given initial {2}, but not via copied Foundry's base 2+tap ability; payoff required. Opponents may interact, and these are algebraic state constructions—not witnessed game results. They explain why a simplistic 'missing an infinite' conclusion would misstate the deck's ceiling.
