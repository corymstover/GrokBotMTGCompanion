## Bounded rules re-trace and correction

**Payable four-piece line, not measured play:** With Abaddon, Loyal Subordinate and Neheb on battlefield at normal combat, lieutenant trigger checks commander and each of three opponents loses 3 (aggregate 9). Neheb's trigger at the beginning of the first postcombat main uses the stack and makes 9R if it resolves. Cast Aggravated Assault from hand for {2}{R}, leaving 6R; with X≥3 the spell has one Abaddon-granted cascade trigger, though no useful hit is assumed. Resolve Assault; pay {3}{R}{R} (5R) to activate in that main, 1R left. Extra combat's Loyal trigger loses another 3 each (aggregate now 18), the following postcombat main's Neheb trigger adds 18R, and the sequence can continue if all relevant permanents survive, lieutenant condition holds, opponents remain, and life loss is not prevented. It is not an automatic T4 kill: gathering four pieces and accessing the first 9R postcombat is substantial setup. Opponents may remove Abaddon before lieutenant resolves, remove Neheb before postcombat, counter its triggered ability, destroy Assault before reuse, or stop life loss. In the shorter Neheb+Assault case, five cumulative life lost funds each five-red activation; with exactly five, the chain is **zero net red** and lacks guaranteed lethal unless a combat/trigger outlet exists. A positive proxy score without these abilities modeled supports no improvement claim.

**Supplemental corrections acknowledged:** The authored record expressly notes Call Forth the Tempest is MV11 with two printed cascades and Abaddon can add a third at X≥11; baseline MV0 pool has seven names, several situational (LED, Pact) or one-shot mana. The old advisory's broad 'Grafdigger's Cage stops cascade' is inaccurate—cascade casts from exile rather than library or graveyard—though Cage does affect Breach. Drannith and Rule of Law impede cascade. These corrections refine matchup analysis, not proof of Assault's win-rate effect.

**Legality provenance:** No Abaddon owner exception. Existing 2026-10-13/16 94-name audit and 2026-10-19 Commander ban check support baseline; GrokBot reported direct Scryfall spot checks October 20. This Stage B consulted Wizards' current Commander banned list and Neheb/cascade release notes; it did **not** run a fresh 100-card Scryfall bulk verification or per-card effect simulator. Both additions Aggravated Assault and Guttersnipe need exact current official Oracle/legality receipts in eventual Phase B or M7 owner packet. Do not misread Standard's Vivi ban or Competitive Brawl restrictions as a Commander ban.
