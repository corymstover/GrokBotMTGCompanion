## Corrections and strongest objections after unblinding

**1. Dark Ritual has more native heist value than the Stage A shorthand conveyed.** It is true that `{B}` → `{B}{B}{B}` does **not** itself pay Nathan's full UBR cast. But once Nathan attacks, controller has priority with the **attack trigger on the stack**. They may cast `Dark Ritual` from hand as an instant *in response*, retaining BBB in mana pool through the trigger resolution. If Nathan survives and his static any-color-spend effect remains, that BBB may pay three arbitrary colored/generic units of the **opponent-owned** spell actually selected from the exiled tops. This is a valid concrete combat-window funding line, not just generic precombat acceleration. Ritual also finances expensive black spells in other windows. Limitations: spending B to cast Ritual requires a source; only two net mana are added; the money expires as a step/phase ends; it is speculative until the random exiled card is known; and if Nathan is removed before resolution his any-color permission vanishes, so BBB cannot automatically satisfy an off-color spell. `Thieving Varmint` and `Fire Nation Palace` provide alternative restricted/combat money but each requires its own setup. **This substantially strengthens KEEP Ritual** relative to Stage A's provisional cut assessment. Do not rewrite the sealed first pass.

**2. Dolmen Gate's benefit comes principally on *later* attacks.** Nathan's triggered heist occurs on being **declared an attacker** and resolves in the declare attackers step before blockers/combat damage. A 5/5 blocker killing the 3/2 commander after first-strike combat does **not** prevent the current attack's heist spell. Gate would preserve Nathan for subsequent attacks (and other attackers), but it does not make the *first* triggered spell castable, solve graveyard/cast restrictions, pay for selected exile cards, grant unblockable, prevent targeted removal or preserve his on-board static ability in response to the trigger. Nathan already has Brotherhood Regalia cheap legendary equip for **ward2 + unblockable**, Greaves haste/shroud, Boots haste/hexproof, Shizo fear, plus first strike against smaller blockers. Gate is indeed a commander-first survivability alternative, but its marginal gain is *conditional* on large/fatal blockers, attackers lacking existing evasion, and survival of enemy instant interaction.

**3. Both sides have meaningful cost.** Removing `Dark Ritual` lowers ability to preload mana into the exact heist resolution in hands with black sources and temporarily accelerates expensive thief/protection plays less. Keeping Ritual increases one-shot variance, consumes a card and may do nothing when a revealed exile selection is a land/unpayable high-MV card; it also cannot pay UBR Nathan cast by itself. Adding `Dolmen Gate` is a low-cost permanent defensive body that protects every attacker from damage, not just Nathan, but commits turn-two mana (competing with rocks/Regalia) and does not answer ghostly/prison attack taxes or spot removal. It does **not** lose its static prevention to Null Rod, though artifact sweepers can remove it. A binary card quality rating cannot settle the actual table split.

**4. Source-supported rules specificity:**
- `Nathan Drake` attacks: exile **four** tops in a four-player game (including own), optionally **cast exactly one spell** from that batch **while trigger resolves**, paying total costs. Not a sustained permission and not a no-pay cast. Unchosen exiles remain inaccessible through Nathan alone. An opponent cannot interrupt the literal middle of trigger resolution; responses occur while the trigger is on stack, or to the spell after it is cast.
- `Roaming Throne` choosing Rogue duplicates the triggering event; each separate Nathan trigger may cast one paid spell. `Firebender Ascension` starts quest progression from attack-caused abilities and only conditionally copies at counter threshold, requiring correct ordering. `Karlach` extra combat produces another attack opportunity if Nathan remains untapped/alive. None supplies free mana by itself.
- Palace/Firebending mana must be made available **after** the attack and **before** resolving Nathan's cast choice; a Palace activation before combat costs mana, target must remain valid, and red firebending money lasts through combat only. That money cannot pay to attack through Ghostly Prison in that same declare-attack procedure. Azula spell copies made while Azula is attacking are **not cast**, whereas real Nathan-allowed casts do trigger Nalfeshnee/Prosper/Passionate and potentially Wild-Magic's first-exile-spell cascade.
- **Dauthi vs Tinybones anti-synergy the neutral Stage A underweighted:** Dauthi's replacement exiles opposing graveyard arrivals with void counters, so Tinybones' combat-damage permission to cast an opponent-graveyard permanent often has no eligible cards; however Dauthi's hate and free exile casting can be strategically greater. This is an existing whole-list issue, not a novel rule blocker. The original author tested Lannery as a conditional change but preserved Tinybones' cheap theft theme.
- **Current legality scope:** Oct15 canonical all-card approval and GrokBot's Oct20 exact-list Scryfall collection report Commander-legal UBR. The proposed incoming Dolmen Gate is indexed as colorless and Commander-legal; this pass used indexed Scryfall text and Wizards release/rules notes but could not retrieve all current 100 Oracle entries via full live collection. No new official ban detected; a swap requires a new full audit, not inherited legitimacy. No project exception is invoked.

## Controlled Stage B conclusion

**The sealed Stage A `NEEDS TESTING` question remains bounded but with a stronger current preference to **KEEP the approved Dark Ritual** unless combat attrition evidence is specifically consequential.** Gate solves a repeated-attack survival problem *after* an attack trigger already yielded its spell; Ritual solves a current-turn paid spell-cost problem that is central to Nathan's printed textbox, especially through his any-color spending on opponent-owned spells. With Regalia plus two protective haste pieces, the marginal Gate benefit may be low. However that judgment is not a measured disproof in heavy-blocker pods, so preserve the hypothesis as a **conditional priority-3 strategic weakness test**, not an automatic swap or compulsory blanket benchmark.

For a decision-grade test, compare baseline vs exactly `+Dolmen Gate / -Dark Ritual` with matched hands and real blocker/removal profiles, including the response-timed Ritual line, Nathan's static ability disappearing on removal, Regalia equipment access, four-player heist spell casting, UBR turn-three sources, extra combats and multiple attacks. Only if protection/attacker survival materially improves **usable stolen spells, pressure or win condition access** net of lost immediate mana should the replacement be recommended for Cory approval. The author Lannery/Tinybones proposal remains a historical test option if those exact conditions arise, not a JLK endorsed swap. The clean historical KEEP/KEEP classification is preserved as factual, though a fresh conditional Stage A challenge is documented.
