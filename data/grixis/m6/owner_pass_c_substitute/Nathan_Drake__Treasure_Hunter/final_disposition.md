## Material rules, legal limits and actual opponent counterplay

1. **Attack-time casting:** Each Nathan trigger exiles four top cards in a four-player table and permits one paid spell from that trigger **during resolution**; no land and no second free spell from that same trigger. Unchosen cards remain exiled. Roaming Throne's additional trigger or Karlach's extra attack are separate events, potentially one additional paid spell each when correctly stacked. Azula copying a spell is **not casting it** and does not trigger exile-cast damage again; Nalfeshnee and Wild-Magic process actual casts under their own respective conditions. Firebender Ascension needs quest counters and a valid attack-caused trigger to copy.
2. **Ritual / stack / static:** Before resolving the triggered exile/cast, cast `Dark Ritual` while one black source and priority exist, float BBB, then (absent response and with Nathan still present) spend that mana under his any-color replacement for a not-owned selected spell. If Nathan is removed in response, the attack trigger still resolves but the color substitution normally disappears. `Fire Nation Palace` needs its own grant and firebending attack mana to be ordered first; `Thieving Varmint` makes restricted mana only for not-owned spells. No free mana/win is assumed from any of these.
3. **Damage vs survival:** Dolmen Gate prevents **combat damage to our attacking creatures**, including lethal damage from a 5/5 blocker, but not damage to blockers/players, removal, attack taxes, sacrifice or noncombat damage. Current attack's ability resolves before blockers under normal rules, so a combat-dead Nathan can still have cast that turn. Regalia equipped to Nathan makes him unblockable and ward2, rendering Gate's block-survival marginal in that configuration. If opponents can remove Regalia before blockers, the assessment changes. A Gate static effect is **not** stopped by Null Rod, though the artifact itself can be destroyed.
4. **Anti-synergy not ignored:** Dauthi Voidwalker replacement can deprive Tinybones the Pickpocket of opposing graveyard permanents; the original authored rule/source analysis already flags it, so it is not a newly uncovered blocker. The deck values Dauthi grave hate and the different theft axes; do not conflate their opponent exile/graveyard permissions with Nathan's top-of-library attack trigger.
5. **Opposition:** Drannith Magistrate preventing opponents from casting outside their hand, Rule of Law, prison costs before attacker declaration (Palace attack mana arrives too late to pay Ghostly Prison), instant-speed Nathan exile/bounce, destroying equipment/attack enablers and countering selected stolen spell are real interaction points.
