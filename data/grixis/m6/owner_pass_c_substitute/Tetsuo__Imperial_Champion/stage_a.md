Heavy Equipment, equip alternatives (Brass Squire, Hammer of Nazahn), artifact reduction/protection, attack-enablers, extra combat turns, and zero-cost suspended spells (Ancestral Vision, Inevitable Betrayal, Profane Tutor, Wheel of Fate) provide the distinctive equipment-to-free-spell bridge. Removing high-MV Equipment can materially reduce free-cast ceilings; Bitterthorn may be a downgrade even if efficient generally.
