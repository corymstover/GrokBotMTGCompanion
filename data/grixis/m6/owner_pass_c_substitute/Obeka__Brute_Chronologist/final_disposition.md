## Obeka-specific strategy and natural power

The commander {1}{U}{B}{R} 3/4 taps to *offer the active player* to end the turn. On our own turn this creates unique tactical control over **one-shot delayed sacrifice/exile/lose-game triggers** from Sneak Attack, Hate Mirage, Flameshadow Conjuring, Jaxis, Feldon, Rionya, Hellkite Courser, Puppeteer Clique, Come Back Wrong, Final Fortune, Last Chance, Warrior's Oath and related mechanisms. At beginning of the proper end step, after the delayed trigger is **already on stack**, end turn to exile that triggered instance and make the creature/token/turn safe. Ending the main phase BEFORE a *next-end-step* trigger merely defers it. The game still processes cleanup and ends duration-based "until end of turn" effects. Opponents do not have to accept the offered end on their turns.

This needs ready Obeka with haste / Thousand-Year Elixir / Minamo plus correctly colored U,B,R from 34 lands and rocks, and real protection against removal. `Sundial of the Infinite` and `Discontinuity` are valuable redundancy; extra turns and temporary stolen/cloned creature effects are **commander-specific**, not automatic wins. Natural ceiling **Bracket 4 — Optimized**. `Isochron Scepter` imprint `Final Fortune` can theoretically set up repeatable paid/untapped extra turns with safety each extra turn; `Dualcaster Mage` + `Twinflame` can generate multiple hasty token copies with protection/timing, but real opponent interruption and legal targets matter. Do not homogenize into generic Grixis cEDH for a higher paper rating.

**Known constraints:** Structural no-mulligan seven-card land-only P(0)=4.87%, P(1)=19.30%, P(2+)=75.84% at 34 lands; *not* simulated time-to-Obeka or observed win rates. Static mana report supplies approximate U/B/R source access and MV4, with uncertainty around conditional lands and mana; stage A independently counted the approved list. Obeka being four mana, summoning sick or removed makes missing a queued lose trigger game-ending. Extra turns must be timed and protected; no universal immunity to counterplay.

## Rules adjudication, agreement and substantive conflicts

**Historical author:** 2026-10-20 exhaustive original ChatGPT authored review HOLD approved 100, but proposed **+Footsteps of the Goryo / -Delina, Wild Mage** for owner consideration. WotC Sep2 2022 Oracle change `https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/oracle-changes-2022-09-02` states Delina tokens are granted a **normal recurring** `At end of combat, exile this creature` ability, *not a delayed one-shot*. Obeka exiling one such ability does not permanently save the token; it triggers in a future combat again. Footsteps of the Goryo is a sorcery {2}{B} returning one's own graveyard creature without haste; its **next-end-step sacrifice is a one-shot** that can be permanently removed from stack. This makes an intuitively plausible improvement, **but** Footsteps' target/tempo/hasteless restrictions and graveyard hate vs Delina's immediate attacking ETB/copy/d20 upside have NOT been decision-grade tested. No owner approval.

**GrokBot advisory:** 2026-10-20 low-confidence KEEP, no swap; historical reconciliation NEEDS TESTING from this disagreement. The advisory also claims a `Glorious End` loss follows "every later end step" so player must repeatedly skip all future end steps. This is **wrong once its one-shot delayed loss ability has actually triggered and been removed from stack**. Official first-party WotC `https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/amonkhet-release-notes-2017-04-14` expressly says Glorious End's delayed trigger, once countered or otherwise removed from stack, **will not trigger again**. Obeka rules and source `https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/commander-legends-release-notes-2020-11-06` confirm: allowing next-end-step trigger onto stack and then ending turn evicts it permanently; ending turn *before* the relevant end step simply postpones. The advisory's related blanket phrasing that ending turn only delays Sneak/Kiki/etc obligations likewise omits the triggering-on-stack winning case. **Flag: ADVISORY_RULES_CORRECTION_REQUIRED for downstream evidence reuse, not a new official legality blocker.** Preserve historical advisory file; make any future correction in new source-bound evidence.

**Independent sealed Stage A challenger:** **+Mimic Vat / -Avaricious Dragon**, neither previously tested in the Oct20 original deep review nor approved. Mimic Vat {3} artifact on nontoken creature death may imprint, then {3},{T} creates hasty token copy with **one-shot next-end-step exile**; after that trigger is stacked and Obeka exiles it, the token can remain. Dragon draws one additional card in each draw step but triggers a fresh discard-of-hand at every own end step, forcing Obeka activation every turn to avoid loss. Vat may better use a one-shot commander activation while creating durable ETB bodies; counterargument: it needs an actual suitable death, six mana across deployment/activation, Obeka, artifact survival and a real token before end-step timing, while Dragon's extra card may win by consistency earlier. **Unproven, second-priority candidate.** The fact the original considered Dragon a conditional Footsteps cut reinforces that the slot deserves scrutiny, but is not a result favoring Mimic Vat.

**Other exact rules:** `Identity Thief` can exile another nontoken creature until one-shot next end step; Obeka evicting return trigger might permanently strand the exiled card, but Identity Thief's copy still wears off in cleanup. `Avaricious Dragon` and `Psychic Vortex` each create **new** obligations on future appropriate turns; no permanent erase of printed recurring triggers. `Delina` current end-of-combat trigger can be evicted only for one combat; ending our turn there loses postcombat/main opportunity. `Glorious End` loss does not continue after already stacked-and-exiled once. Sundial cannot be activated outside its controller's turn; Obeka can't force opponents to agree on theirs.
