## 2. What all three reviewers really agree upon

- Obeka is a {1}{U}{B}{R} 3/4 who taps to *offer the active player* an opportunity to end the turn. It is reliably chosen on our own turn, not automatically on an opponent's. It skips to cleanup, exiles the current stack, and causes "until end of turn" effects to expire.
- Permanent creature preservation requires a qualifying *one-shot delayed trigger to have ALREADY triggered* and been put on stack before Obeka exiles it. Ending prematurely merely defers a generic "next end step" trigger to another end step. The actual opponent can respond to the activation and source still matters.
- The 100 includes many meaningful Obeka-specific cards: Sneak Attack/Feldon/Jaxis/Rionya/Flameshadow/Hate Mirage temporary copies, Apprentice Necromancer/Puppeteer Clique/Hellkite Courser temporary reanimation, Identity Thief exile-and-return manipulation, and Final Fortune/Last Chance/Warrior's Oath/Alchemist's Gambit risky extra turns supported by Obeka/Sundial/Discontinuity. `Isochron Scepter` imprinted with `Final Fortune` can supply repeatable extra turns *with* an Obeka or Sundial reset, 2 mana and untapping of artifacts/commander each iteration, and correct protection, but is not guaranteed through opponents' removal.
- 100 names exactly distinct, 34 land and UBR official Oct15/16/20 legality snapshots, not a current fresh all-list legality certificate. Natural **Bracket 4 Optimized**, protect unique commander engine, preserve approved list while tests remain.

## 3. Formal Pass C detects a material advisory rules error

**GrokBot advisory asserted:** "Glorious End says 'your next end step', so the loss trigger follows you. You must skip your end step on every later turn (Obeka/Sundial again) until you win." This is **not correct once the delayed ability has TRIGGERED and been removed from the stack**. It conflates the case of repeatedly skipping the end step *before the trigger ever occurs* with the case of *letting it trigger and then exiling the single one-shot instance*.

First-party WotC `https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/amonkhet-release-notes-2017-04-14` explicitly states for **Glorious End**: its delayed loss trigger is **one-shot**; if it is countered or otherwise removed from the stack, **it will not trigger again**. WotC `https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/commander-legends-release-notes-2020-11-06` makes the exact same timing distinction for Obeka:
- **Wrong line A:** Glorious End on own turn, Obeka ends before any subsequent own end step begins, so the "next end step" delayed trigger is deferred; it could appear at a later eligible end step.
- **Correct permanent-protection line B:** when the Glorious End **lose-the-game trigger is already on the stack during the next appropriate end step**, Obeka (or appropriate Sundial/Discontinuity) ends the active turn and exiles that trigger. The one-shot delayed ability does **not** continue hunting for future end steps. No forced ongoing activation each subsequent turn.
- This changes whether Obeka's finite tap/untap resources need to be allocated forever to Glorious End, and how to model a single Glorious End plus many subsequent temporary-copy engines. The existing generated interaction page and original exhaustive ChatGPT review mostly observe the correct one-shot rule; do not let GrokBot's single contradictory advisory statement override primary WotC. The ability is still dangerous until actually exiled or otherwise removed from stack.

**GrokBot additionally said** Sneak Attack, Kiki-Jiki, Rionya, Hate Mirage "ending your turn only delays [their next-end-step obligation] to next player's end step and doesn't keep creatures." This is accurate **only if end-turn is performed BEFORE the one-shot trigger enters the stack**; as a blanket statement it omits the key successful Obeka line in which the triggered object is *already on stack*. WotC Commander Legends makes that condition explicit. Qualified correction required, not an assertion that all token copying is permanently safe.

These are **rules-source/advisory correction issues**, not new official card illegality. Document and feed targeted deterministic regression into Phase B/I/J. Do not claim the prior advisory is formally independent or that its low-confidence KEEP is thereby a reliable certification.

## 4. Original disputed Footsteps/Delina finding and neutral new Mimic Vat branch

### Historical high-priority add/cut: +Footsteps of the Goryo / -Delina, Wild Mage

The original author is correct about **Delina's special Oracle trap**. WotC Sep 2 2022 `https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/oracle-changes-2022-09-02` unambiguously classifies the token's intrinsic exile as a **normal recurring triggered ability**, not the one-shot next-end-step action typical of an Obeka-safe Sneak Attack token. Under ordinary combat, a Delina token retained by exiling one end-of-combat occurrence triggers again at each future end-of-combat. Moreover, Obeka must end our own turn at end of combat to suppress the instance, forfeiting remaining combat/postcombat actions and potentially needing another untap before own end step to preserve Sneak/Feldon tokens. This weakens *commander-specific persistent copy utility*; Delina's attack roll still may create immediate attacking value, including extra copies on higher rolls, and is not categorically useless.

`Footsteps of the Goryo` has the more Obeka-compatible one-time next-end-step sacrifice trigger and cheaply reanimates **a creature card in own graveyard**, but grants **NO haste**. A high-quality creature needs to exist in graveyard, be legally targeted, resolve, survive until end step, and require a live Obeka/alternate end-turn then. With 34 lands, no dedicated mass creature-mill engine, a high instant-speed reanimation suite already present, and a time-sensitive Bracket4 plan, it may be worse than Delina on early-mid turns without targets. **This is the first disputed swap to test because it was an actual prior author vs advisory disagreement, not proof that it is an upgrade.**

Required controlled alternatives: baseline 100 vs Footsteps/Delina; optionally Footsteps/Avaricious Dragon as original author alternate cut (also unapproved). Score actual reanimatable creature access, expected ETB/combat per target, available Obeka+haste and end-step timing, extra-turn risk and response sequences, not a static "delayed trigger good" heuristic.

### New Stage A pair: +Mimic Vat / -Avaricious Dragon

Stage A selected this **independently** before author verdicts were read; original Oct20 deep report does not evaluate Mimic Vat by name. `Mimic Vat` is a {3} artifact with imprint on real nontoken creature death, {3}/{T} token copy and a **one-shot next-end-step token exile**. Obeka can make each such token sticky after its particular delayed trigger goes on stack and is exiled. `Avaricious Dragon` supplies extra draw on your draw step but recurs its own discard-entire-hand every subsequent own end step; to suppress the discard, Obeka must spend a tap on it each cycle. Vat may concentrate end-step triggers with other tokens and turn multiple deaths into creature material, but setup cost (3+3 mana plus a real creature death), artifact hate, Imprint selection and late-game dependence may be too slow compared with Dragon's earlier draw.

This pair is **second-tier**, after the already-disputed Footsteps/Delina, under owner post-JLK priority. The Stage A preference is only **NEEDS TESTING**, not automatic upgrade.
