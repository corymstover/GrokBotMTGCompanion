## Commander identity, power ceiling and actual stack interactions

**Bracket 4 — Optimized** natural ceiling, requiring no generic Grixis cEDH package. Parnesse {2}{U}{B}{R}, 4/4 Vampire Wizard, taxes targeted opponent spells/abilities 4 life each relevant trigger or counters them; a spell-copy event **you control** permits **up to one** target opponent to also copy that spell and change the new copy's targets. Choose zero target opponents when political generosity risks your board. The commander **does not** copy your own spells herself. Magecraft, Thousand-Year Storm, Prismari-granted storm, Twinning Staff, Wizards/prodigy and stack-copy instants are the purposeful engine. Multiple on-list 5–7 mana payoffs mean colors/mana, threat timing, held protection and opponent choices are genuine ceiling constraints. Many copy generators operate with Parnesse absent, but removing her political/defensive characteristic from the list would homogenize it.

Core rules:
- Each spell copy on the stack ordinarily isn't cast; it triggers `when you copy` and magecraft but doesn't advance storm's count of *prior spells cast*. Prismari can grant Grapeshot another **distinct** storm instance; copies from those storm triggers are not recursively cast.
- Parnesse can choose no opponent for each own copy and avoids automatic aid. If an opponent does obtain a copy and retargets it at you/a permanent, Parnesse's **targeting tax** can apply; counterspells aimed at your spells on stack are **not** aimed at you or permanents and aren't automatically taxed.
- Harmonic Prodigy and Wizard's Staff on this Wizard can increase her protection-tax *and* her opponent-copy-sharing triggers. That isn't identical to more copies under **your** control. Veyran's additional-trigger condition concerns instant/sorcery casting/copying by you, not every opponent-controlled targeting event.
- Sunken Palace enters tapped and exiles seven graveyard cards for special mana; if that mana copies an activated ability, Parnesse does **not** trigger simply because an ability was copied. Pyromancer's Goggles' red mana guarantees a copy only if spent to cast a **red** instant/sorcery; a spell can have other colors, but it must be red. Ordinary U/B counterspells are not automatically copied.
- Reiterate buyback {3} plus mana cost {1}{R}{R} requires six mana for each re-cast. Mana Geyser must produce **at least seven** from currently tapped opponent lands for positive red per copy, and a first copy/buyback must be **funded before its payoff**; finite sequence and interruption rights are not optional. This conditional line doesn't guarantee a no-setup win.

## Exact competing candidate decisions — not canonical edits

**Priority 1: disputed newly functional combo** `+Twinflame / -Wandering Archaic // Explore the Vastlands` (GrokBot had instead cut Big Score). Twinflame {1}{R} must target another own creature. With **Dualcaster Mage** {1}{R}{R} in hand, cast it with flash in response to original Twinflame, using total **{2}{R}{R}{R} (5 mana, triple red)**. Mage ETB makes a *spell copy* of still-on-stack Twinflame redirected to Mage; the resulting hasty Mage token ETBs and copies the original Twinflame again. Each iteration can trigger Parnesse and magecraft. Repeated copies can yield finite arbitrary hasty Mage tokens; to terminate, change/retain target to a non-Dualcaster own creature. With Professor Onyx or Ral on field copies can directly kill via drain/damage; precombat tokens can attack unless opponents tax/prevent combat. Merely drawing with Archmage or making Treasures with Storm-Kiln isn't itself lethal, and drawing without termination may lose on empty library. Opponent countermagic on original Twinflame, spot removal in relevant windows, sweepers, attack taxes/fog and original timing can disrupt. **Parnesse is not needed to execute the token loop**. Twinflame alone creates a **creature permanent token**, not a spell copy and therefore doesn't trigger her second ability unless coupled with a separate spell copier. Removing Wandering Archaic sacrifices repeated Parnesse-native, opponent-payment-dependent spell copies in longer tables. Confirmed direct spell-copy loop **is not enough to certify whole-deck upgrade** without testing its floor, cut costs and player-specific tempo.

**Priority 2: independently sealed Stage A hypothesis** `+Krark, the Thumbless / -Pyromancer's Goggles`, competing alternate cut `-Double Vision`. Krark's {1}{R} wizard spell-cast trigger gives a coin-dependent **copy** or returns the original to hand. It can operate before five-mana Parnesse, interact with Harmonic Prodigy, and generate additional Parnesse offers/magecraft. However returning Counterspell/board interaction to hand mid-stack can lose games, including under Hexing Squelcher which prevents countering but not spell return; and Goggles reliably funds and copies red spells and is hardier versus creature removal. Double Vision copies first instant/sorcery each turn including opponents' turns where eligible, although also five mana. This is a credible **new** commander-specific test, not an automatically superior card on a list already subject to substantial original auditing.

**Priority 3: competing earlier author selection** `+Twincast / -Wandering Archaic`. Twincast produces an instant-speed certain spell copy, uses {U}{U} and naturally triggers Parnesse. Against a fast pod paying Archaic's {2} tax it may be better than waiting for opponent casts. It lacks Twinflame's extra two-card finishing route. One cut cannot fund both; select via tests and intended deck identity, not decklist homogenization.

**Rejected as an unproven preferred cut:** GrokBot's `+Twinflame / -Big Score` takes out a four-mana spell which discards then draws two and creates two Treasure tokens, copying cleanly on the instant stack and helping provide the multi-red mana for its very own combo alternatives. The reconciliation's choice of alternate cut has legitimate grounding. Stage A's `Fury Storm / Big Score` lower-confidence idea is likewise not currently preferred.
