## Controlled source-paid challenger comparison (not gameplay simulation)

**Scenario 1 — relevant spell on stack, RR accessible.** Opponent casts a counterspell or removal instant; cast `Dualcaster Mage` {1}{R}{R} while legal priority is held, causing nontoken Wizard ETB with its own copy-instant/sorcery trigger and Inalla trigger. Put Eminence appropriately and pay `{1}`; a token Dualcaster ETB may copy another spell that remains on stack, perhaps the same original spell, and optional new targets can alter combat. **Costs:** `{1}{R}{R}+{1}` plus a living instant/sorcery spell to target; spell and copy resolution order/retarget legality matter. Against a removal spell that kills original Mage, an ETB may still resolve, but it cannot copy a target instant that already left the stack. This is tactical Wizard interaction, not a fixed two-copy protection win.

**Scenario 2 — no useful spell on stack or cannot make RR.** The unmodified Kefka `{2}{U}{B}{R}` on ETB may force all opponents to discard and draw by card-type variety; if commander Eminence is available and `{1}` paid, a legendary token Kefka can trigger ETB then legend-rule one of the two away, producing a second discard/draw event without retaining the token. In contrast Dualcaster is a 2/2 with no meaningful ETB target when the stack is empty, and RR can be especially awkward in a 27-land/black-heavy ritual plan. A single well-timed Kefka reanimation/ETB may produce more future winning cards than any reactive copy trick.

**Scenario 3 — direct Wizard-tutor alternative.** `Micromancer` `{3}{U}` enters, fetches **MV=1** instant/sorcery, Inalla `{1}` doubles the tutor; while this is not Spellseeker's MV≤2 and costs four-plus-one vs 3-plus-one, it finds Culling/Unearth/Entomb/Reanimate even with an otherwise empty stack. Against low-blue turns it's potentially more accessible than RR Dualcaster; however 4MV + a commander trigger can consume the turn and it doesn't alone reproduce Spellseeker's documented one-card engine. `Snapcaster Mage` `{1}{U}` grants flashback to a single grave spell on ETB and its Eminence copy can grant another flashback window, but the spells still need paid costs and a legal timing window. Compare *specific* cEDH openings, not popularity.

**Scenario 4 — source-paid native Spellseeker continuation.** Owner approved list has Spellseeker {2}{U}, Culling B+creature sacrifice→BBBB, Inalla `{1}` token fetch Unearth B, nontoken Spellseeker return and tutor Burnt Offering B, further Inalla copy fetch Finale of Promise (MV2 library, X1 costs1RR), and Finale recasts instant/sorcery from grave. The prior authored A–R traces additionally support Scholar/Bloodline recursion under correct target and grave sequencing; none measured a multiplayer win rate or established no further upgrade. Critically, Inalla remaining a command-zone **same object** throughout pending triggers and opponent responses to mana/tutor windows must be preserved. Original remains KEEP if no compelling weakness measured.

**Scenario 5 — remove protection instead of Kefka.** `-Hexing Squelcher` loses spells-can't-be-countered for us and life-paid ward for other creatures, a source of protection in race windows; despite its non-Wizard type, it directly shields the commander-first tutor line. `-Hoarding Broodlord` lowers 8MV Ad Naus exposure but loses a powerful reanimation/convoke tutor backup. The alternate cut is not automatically cleaner than Kefka; all demand scarce role-correct opponent-response tests.
