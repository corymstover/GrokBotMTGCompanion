## Effect and rules adjudication

1. **Author-identified Samut/Beseech conflict VERIFIED:** Samut, Tyrant of Naktamun `{1}{U}` makes instant and sorcery SPELLS you control have SPLIT SECOND while they are on the stack. `Beseech the Mirror` `{1}{B}{B}{B}` bargain allows a ≤4MV found card to be CAST *during Beseech's resolution*. A Beseech that currently has split second is still on stack during its own resolution, so casting even that otherwise-permitted spell is **prohibited**. The library SEARCH/reveal/exile itself still works, but the bargain free-cast step fails. If Azula copies Beseech during combat with real flash permission, the copy of Beseech likewise has split second while on stack, and its own cast opportunity fails. Later casts from hand at normal timing are still possible only if separately given permission. This is not an official deck-legality blocker, but is a material self-interference in one core tutor line. The earlier author, not JLK, first documented it.
2. **Azula/Artist trace validated, not performance tested:** After legal Azula attack, firebending2 creates two red mana through combat. With surviving `Storm-Kiln Artist` already in play, one actual instant/sorcery CAST triggers Artist once; its Azula-created SPELL COPY triggers Artist again, producing TWO Treasures. A copied spell is not cast and does not trigger Azula again; Artist specifically counts copying. In contrast, casting the incumbent `Big Score` for `{3}{R}` (additional single discard cost) while Azula attacking creates its own copied instance, meaning original+copy each draw2 and create2 Treasure: **four drawn, four Treasures** for one discarded card if both resolve, with no Artist needed. `Storm-Kiln` requires at least two other doubled instant/sorcery spells to produce four Treasure and no intrinsic draw. Mana, speed and creature vulnerability are missing from any raw token-count comparison.
3. **Actual flash and priority:** Azula copies ANY spell cast while attacking, including sorcery if valid flash; `Expedite` cannot grant that timing. The firebending mana is **red** and expires at end of combat, it cannot pay `U U` for Narset's Reversal or `B B B` for Beseech without different sources. Cast-versus-copy and paid additional costs must be modeled; `Demand Answers` creates ZERO Treasure even when copied, whereas `Big Score` does.
4. **Other combo routes still contingent:** Breach/LED/Brain Freeze requires escape fodder, proper cast/storm sequencing, and graveyard accessibility; Oracle/Consultation/Pact needs actual legal library state. The prior author carefully examined Narset's Reversal + Frantic Search loop, but interaction and correct targeting remain open. These are not a reason to replace every four-drop with generic Grixis staples.
