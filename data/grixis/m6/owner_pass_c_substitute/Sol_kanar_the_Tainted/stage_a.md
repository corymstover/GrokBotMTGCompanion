Commander-first game plan: Sol'Kanar is a five-mana UBR 5/5 with a beginning-of-your-end-step choice among four modes not previously chosen by that object: draw one; opponents each lose two and you gain two; three damage to another creature/planeswalker; or exile and return Sol'Kanar under an opponent's control. A true blink makes a new object and resets its mode history; merely changing control does not. The approved list integrates Thassa, Deep-Dwelling, Conjurer's Closet, Planar Incision, Homeward Path and exchange/donation cards, plus risky gifts Demonic Pact, Illusions of Grandeur, Immortal Coil, Captive Audience, Archfiend of the Dross, Greed's Gambit. Avoid conflating these gifts' loss conditions.

New Stage A hypothesis: +Displacer Kitten / -Conjurer's Closet. Kitten costs 3U and triggers on casting our noncreature spells to blink another nonland permanent we control, including Sol'Kanar, resetting mode history before the end step. It is cheaper than Closet and can reset other owned engines. Strongest countercase: Closet provides a free recurring end-step blink precisely aligned with Sol's modal end-step trigger, without needing an additional spell, and survives creature sweepers; Kitten is a fragile 2/2 and must have spell fuel. Alternate cut Planar Incision loses instant-speed defensive reset and is not automatically superior.

Important timing: At the beginning of our end step, put Sol'Kanar's trigger above Closet/Thassa trigger to resolve a useful mode then blink and reset. If blink happens first, old triggered ability does not magically inherit the new object's history. Homeward Path can regain a donated creature we own but does not itself reset chosen modes. Assault Suit's sacrifice prohibition does not stop Sol's exile. Casting, not copying, a noncreature spell triggers Kitten. A newly blinked Sol entering during end step does not generate a fresh beginning-of-step trigger.
