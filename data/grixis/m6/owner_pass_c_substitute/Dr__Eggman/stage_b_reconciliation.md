## Agreements, surprises and adjudication

**A. Fundamental agreement.** Stage A independently agrees with the substantive prior author: commander `{2}{U}{B}{R}` five mana, 3/6 flying Human Scientist, draws **one per resolved own-end-step trigger**, then each opponent separately chooses actual discard OR allow one eligible card from hand directly onto field; the empty-hand “discard nothing” choice is legal. Actual discards trigger Caress/Waste Not/Grimoire, no-op discards do not. Only subtype-qualified CARDS from hand are legal. Roaming Throne naming Scientist/Human can double commander triggers, while Valeyard doubles choices rather than underlying draws; Resonator doubles a particular trigger; none ensures opponents take the bad branch. Under end-step entry, Cyberdrive's 'until end of turn' animation dissipates before ordinary next-turn combat and Cityscape Leveler's CAST trigger does not fire. These are real strategic restrictions, not rules blockers.

**B. Stage A's exact challenger is meaningfully novel relative to these files.** The comprehensive review searched/recounted 244 prior candidates and a narrow Pass-B set, but contains **zero mentions of `Goblin Welder`**. The October14 published approval-ready closeout likewise did not name it. Thus `+Goblin Welder/-Liliana's Caress` is a genuine *new targeted hypothesis*, not an upgrade proven by a new test. **Crucial comparison**: Welder's activated tap exchange requires an artifact in play and an artifact card in graveyard of the same player, and does not depend on an opponent accepting the Eggman-cheat branch. This directly addresses discarded/destroyed payload retrieval. It does *not* help deploy machine cards **still in hand**; it cannot tap on the turn summoned without haste and requires the graveyard to be stocked. Current source has Academy Ruins, Scrap Trawler, Master Transmuter and Kuldotha Forgemaster, but **not** a dedicated graveyard-stockpiling mill package; the redundancy may render Welder unnecessary. Its 1/1 fragility and sacrifice/target risks must be included in tests.

**C. `Liliana's Caress` has stronger strategic defense than merely 'conditional damage.'** Oct14 approval deliberately preserved it as one of four discard-conversion engines alongside Waste Not, Tinybones and Geth's Grimoire. The commander is a villainous **CHOICE** card; removing a discard punishment can cheapen opponents' voluntary discard costs and make them less likely to put a Construct or Robot. The Valeyard/Strionic/Throne may repeatedly expose actual cards, raising Caress's aggregate multiplayer life-loss, while Welder lacks passive value if both valid targets never coexist. Conversely, if opponents spend hands and legally choose to discard nothing, **Caress's payoff is precisely zero** and recursion may bypass their no-op. No concrete expected value or superior 100-card list is established either way. Therefore **retain approved Caress** for now, and submit only a bounded test candidate.

**D. Prior `Scrawling Crawler` has genuine upside but is already studied.** It is a low-cost eligible Phyrexian Construct and on **your upkeep** draws each player a card while causing opponents loss from draw; this may make opponents hold expendable cards at Eggman's end step, raising the cost of their villainous choice. It also hands each opponent a free card and possible instant removal, and it cannot force that card still be held on end step. Its potential exact cuts `Blitzwing` or `Cyberdrive Awakener` remove different theme/combat functions. It was explicitly considered and *rejected* before October14 approval, then reflagged as conditional test by postapproval author/reconciliation. Do not claim a new overlooked discovery or automatically re-open a clean KEEP; test only if actual opponent-empty-hand failure is consequential.

**E. Other preexisting candidate evidence reused, no new bulk reviews.** `Torrential Gearhulk` can be put as a Construct and ETB cast a qualifying instant from GY for free, but opponent chooses entry, it needs an actual instant and legal targets; an `Eggman` own-end-step put generally cannot cast targetless counterspells when no spell on stack, and Cyclonic Rift can't use overload under a free alternative cast cost. `Imposter Mech` is a low-cost Vehicle copying an opponent-controlled creature as a **noncreature** artifact vehicle, with crew burden and opponent-board dependence. Neither decisively displaces a named existing machine. A 32nd land may address real 31-land/5MV commander variance but requires identifying a loss of machine/payoff functionality and comparing identical mulligan/colored-pip requirements, not merely counting land.

## Bounded, mechanically payable adversarial cases (not measured testing)

1. **Empty-hand no-op:** Eggman alive, own end step, all three opponents zero cards. Eggman draws one. Each legally selects “discard,” discards zero and gives no machine. Liliana's Caress, Geth's Grimoire and Waste Not all see zero actual discards. Even with Valeyard multiplying choices, the zero-hand opponent can pick zero discard each time. Welder, if alive since prior turn and equipped with **both** own artifact/Treasure in play and a large artifact card in graveyard, can independently exchange; without those conditions it does nothing.
2. **One live card each:** Three opponents each hold an expendable card, Eggman controller has Cityscape Leveler. All three could discard an expendable card, denying Leveler entry and causing `Liliana's Caress` **6 total life loss (2 per player)**; actual Geth/Waste Not triggers are separate conditional card types. Cutting Caress removes that guaranteed three-player payoff in this stated line. This is a comparison of *resolved rules consequences*, not a probability claim about opponent rationality.
3. **Welder recovery line:** Our seven-mana Gearhulk is in our GY, one Treasure on field, `Goblin Welder` under our control since a prior turn. Tap Welder targeting our Treasure and our artifact Gearhulk card. If both targets legal, sacrifice Treasure and put Gearhulk onto battlefield; its ETB happens normally. An opponent can exile targeted GY card or destroy targeted Treasure in response to make exchange fail. Welder can't take an artifact card from **our hand** by this action and cannot target an opponent's graveyard card while sacrificing **our** Treasure because targets must associate with same player. This is a legal single hypothetical resolution, **not** win-rate or recurrence evidence.
4. **31-land colored-source failure:** Academy Ruins, Ancient Tomb, Inventors' Fair and Sol Ring can generate substantial **colorless** mana but no `{U}{B}{R}` package; conditional Mox Opal still needs three artifacts. A starting hand heavy on these is not an automatically payable Eggman opener. Three colored lands plus Sol Ring/arcane fixing may cast Eggman early and hardcast some robots; measure both hands rather than extrapolating.
5. **Copy/ETB caution:** Roaming Throne choosing Scientist produces two Eggman *trigger instances* at start of own end step; `Strionic Resonator` can add a third resolving trigger with `{2}` and tapping, for three draws plus up to nine original villainous choices across three opponents, doubled as individual choices by Valeyard if live. There is **no guaranteed six/nine artifact placements**, since opponents can always select discard. A Leveler PUT into play skips its on-Cast removal, whereas Demonic Junker's enters trigger can affect board immediately after a qualifying PUT.

These are five deterministic scenario analyses using Oracle/rules and already-approved source, **not randomized games, true adversarial selection distributions or effect-aware Phase B certification**. No new full Oracle/legal name scan or win-rate claim is manufactured. They support **targeted testing rather than an immediate approved-card change**.
