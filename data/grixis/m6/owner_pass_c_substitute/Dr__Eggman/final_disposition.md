## Commander-first verdict and rules integrity

Eggman's unique, on-theme win path is **five-mana 3/6 flying Human Scientist into a repeated own-end-step card draw and multiple sequential coercive decisions**, putting eligible high-impact **Construct, Robot or Vehicle cards from hand** onto battlefield only when opponents permit; the complementary side turns **actual** opposing discards into Caress/Grimoire/Waste Not/Tinybones payoffs. End-step extra triggers via Roaming Throne naming Human/Scientist, Strionic Resonator, nonlegendary Spark Double and extra steps from Y'shtola multiply the relevant triggers. `The Valeyard` multiplies villainous choices, **not Eggman draw count**. A card **PUT** into play is not cast: Cityscape Leveler's cast-only destruction will not trigger, while Demonic Junker's ETB may. Cyberdrive Awakener's until-EOT animation has poor timing after being put during Eggman's end step. **Natural ceiling: Bracket 4 — Optimized**, tags **Villainous Choice / Robots / Artifact Cheat**. Do not convert to generic Grixis combo/control at expense of this plan.

**Opponent counterplay is profound and legal:** any opposing player with an empty hand can choose “discard a card” but discard none, deny a machine placement, and avoid any 'when discards' ability. Players choose in turn order and can react to the previous player's deployed machine. With large hands they may discard a small card instead of allowing a major Robot; with our own hand missing a legal payload they may choose the deployment branch for nothing. Five mana with all U/B/R pips and only 31 lands/seven early generic/colored rocks (some conditional) can delay the commander or strand hardcast threats, and opposing artifact wipes hamper recovery. Current counter suite and artifact engines mitigate but do not erase those limits. No active unknown rules/legality blocker has been found.
