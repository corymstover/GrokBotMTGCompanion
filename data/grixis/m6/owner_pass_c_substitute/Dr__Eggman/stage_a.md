## Commander-first identity, dependencies and realistic ceiling

Eggman is a **repeated end-step coercion-and-machines** engine, not merely an artifact-staples commander: he supplies incremental card draw even when opponents deny a free machine, forces an adverse choice every turn, and occasionally bypasses the mana cost of an oversized Construct, Robot or Vehicle held in hand. The opportunity is highest when all three opponents have enough cards to make discard painful, while the Eggman controller holds at least one **eligible** payload; a player with an empty hand can still freely choose to discard nothing, and a rival with an expendable land or low-value card will often discard to prevent `Cityscape Leveler` or `Ultron, Machine Overlord` entering.

There are multiple overlapping but not interchangeable modules:

- **Legal Eggman payloads** include cards with the exact relevant creature/artifact subtype in hand: large Construct Gearhulks (`Combustible`, `Noxious`, `Oildeep`, `Coalstoke`), `Cityscape Leveler`, `Metalwork Colossus`, Robot Ultron threats, and Vehicle cards such as `Nautiloid Ship`/`The Regalia`. A generic costly artifact without these card types is **not** puttable solely by Eggman; a token already on battlefield cannot be put from hand. Payload quantity and held-hand concentration matter more than generic artifact count. A `Cityscape Leveler` entering without being cast cannot produce its cast-only trigger, but its attack trigger remains relevant.
- **Artifact deployment and recycling**: `Master Transmuter` exchanges a battlefield artifact for another artifact card in hand, subject to its costs and activation timing; `Kuldotha Forgemaster` can tutor any artifact to battlefield by tapping and sacrificing three artifacts; `Scrapyard Recombiner` finds Constructs, `Scrap Trawler` recovers smaller artifacts, `Academy Ruins` puts artifacts back atop library, and `Unwinding Clock` can provide repeat untaps for **artifacts**, not arbitrary nonartifact activated creatures.
- **Villainous/discard payoff**: `The Valeyard` makes each relevant villainous choice occur an extra time, substantially multiplying decision pressure, while `Waste Not`, `Geth's Grimoire`, `Liliana's Caress` and `Tinybones, Bauble Burglar` require **actual discards** as worded; choosing the discard option with an empty hand does not trigger discarded-card payoffs. `Davros, Dalek Creator` can layer other choices contingent on opponents' life loss. A hybrid engine must survive rational opponent branch selection.
- **Trigger multiplication**: `Roaming Throne` naming **Human** or **Scientist** can double Eggman's **beginning-of-end-step** trigger while Eggman remains that type, yielding two resolving draws plus two sequences of opponent choices (not one draw per opponent). Merely choosing `Robot` or `Construct` for Roaming Throne does not double the Human Scientist commander's ability. `Strionic Resonator` pays/taps to copy a triggered ability, `Spark Double` can enter as a nonlegendary copy. The copies still face rational choices and eligible-in-hand constraints.

The hypothesis is an **optimized Bracket 4** natural ceiling: capable of snowballing cards, discard costs and immense machines in multiplayer but lacking a guaranteed fast end-step payoff, being five mana with three distinct colored pips, and facing counterplay through discard/no-op branch selection, artifact removal, graveyard hate and instant-speed commander removal. No empirical power score or cEDH win-rate claim is made.

## Mana, density, fragility and opponent play

The exact deck has **31 lands**, including Ancient Tomb (colorless), three artifact lands, fetches, original duals, shocks, rainbow sources, a triome, Inventors' Fair/Academy Ruins and Urza's Saga. Its early nonland rocks include **Sol Ring, Mox Opal, Arcane Signet, Fellwar Stone, and three Grixis Talismans** (seven), with Foundry Inspector/Tezzeret discounts/alternative artifact lines. Tomb/Sol Ring speed generic mana but cannot alone meet `{U}{B}{R}`; early `Mox Opal` requires metalcraft, and `Spire of Industry` unrestricted colored mana requires an artifact. Colored access and making five total mana by the relevant turn are **testable**, not inferred probabilities. A board wipe that destroys rocks can strand large hardcast threats in a 31-land deck.

The deck deliberately contains a large upper curve: Cityscape Leveler, Metalwork Colossus, several Gearhulks, high-mana Robots and heavy deployment enchantments/artifacts. Since opponents choose Eggman's branch, those threats may be **stranded** until hardcast, `Master Transmuter`, `Kuldotha Forgemaster` or another specific path is established. Conversely, extra rocks and artifacts can make heavier spells realistically playable, and cutting a synergistic expensive machine to fix raw curve may destroy the commander-first payoff.

Protection and interaction include Lightning Greaves, Padeem, Cryptothrall, Fierce Guardianship, Deflecting Swat, Deadly Rollick, Mana Drain, Chaos Warp, Cyclonic Rift, Withering Torment, Toxic Deluge and Blasphemous Act. They cannot guarantee Eggman survives end-step trigger windows: killing him **in response to the end-step trigger does not remove that ability from the stack**, but removal before end step denies new triggers, and the machine payload may be targeted by interaction after entering. Opponents may discard a low-impact card to deny cheat, or empty their hands to make that choice free. They may also prevent discard payoffs by choosing a machine instead when available. `Unwinding Clock` untaps artifacts on each other player's untap step, not Eggman or a plain nonartifact creature by itself.

## Independent adversarial challenger — not approved

**Primary alternative:** ADD **`Goblin Welder`**, CUT **`Liliana's Caress`**. The proposed one-mana red Goblin Artificer can, after a turn or haste, tap and target both a **controlled artifact** and an **artifact card in the same player's graveyard**. If both remain legal, their controller sacrifices the on-board artifact and returns the graveyard artifact to battlefield. That would trade spent artifact fodder, including expendable artifact tokens, for previously destroyed/discarded *huge machines* without needing another end step or an opponent choosing the “allow machine” branch. It also complements Scrap Trawler, artifact creature deaths and the many high-value Construct/Robot/Vehicle artifacts. The commander identity stays focused on machine deployment, and its one-red-mana rate is attractive at 31 lands.

`Liliana's Caress` is a plausible cut, not a proven weak card: as a two-mana black enchantment it triggers **2 life loss to each opponent on each actual discard** but does not pressure an opponent who chooses a discard from an empty hand. Its standalone output is conditional and it neither creates a payload nor solves hardcasting one. Replacing it potentially weakens the punishing-branch side of Eggman's **actual stated villainous choice**, however, and may make opponents *more comfortable* simply discarding one real card every end step. It also reduces interaction with `The Valeyard`, `Waste Not` and `Geth's Grimoire`; the latter two continue to reward discard on their own. `Goblin Welder` is fragile, summoning-sick, susceptible to graveyard exile, dependent on BOTH legal targets, and is itself neither Robot, Construct nor Vehicle for direct Eggman cheating. Graveyard access must be real; no self-mill or free discard-to-graveyard engine has been presumed.

**Strongest KEEP rebuttal:** the deck already has multiple authentic artifact recovery/redeployment tools (`Academy Ruins`, `Master Transmuter`, `Kuldotha Forgemaster`, `Scrap Trawler`); adding a fragile, noncheatable 1/1 might overinvest in a graveyard that the deck doesn't deliberately fill. Eggman's villainous-choice design may specifically require making *each actual discard* painful so that opponents sometimes choose to put a machine onto battlefield. `Caress` is scalable with all opponents' forced/discretionary discard and The Valeyard, while Welder may wait uselessly for a large artifact to hit graveyard. Keeping the approved list could therefore remain superior.

**Other bounded leads:** compare current **31 lands / seven straightforward rocks** against a source/turn-five stress test before assuming an eighth rock or 32nd land is needed; avoid recommending a specific land cut without measured bottlenecks. `Scrap Mastery` provides possible artifact mass recovery, but its symmetric battlefield reset and existing artifacts at risk require a careful zones/ordering test. `Portal to Phyrexia` is high-powered but **is not itself an Eggman-eligible Construct/Robot/Vehicle** and must use an alternate cheat/tutor. These leads do not justify another unbounded candidate sweep.
