## Agreement, novel concerns, and corrected assumptions

**Agreement on the commander:** All reviews identify the same five-mana, beginning-of-combat Pirate graveyard engine: target Pirate card already in OUR graveyard, return as base 4/4 haste with finality; explosive Angrath's Marauders and Port Razer turns but disruptable through grave hate and board interaction. Both the earlier authored review and sealed Stage A independently see Power Conduit as on-identity finality-counter removal and agree that cheap Looting/Entomb/Buried Alive, draw/treasure, and recursion need to remain. This agreement is not three technically independent model votes.

**Land-count correction and provenance:** Stage A manually recounts **34 front-face land CARD names** (six basic copies; the remainder nonbasics). Earlier author prose labels **35 lands** because its type matrix includes Brass's Tunnel-Grinder as 'Legendary Artifact // Legendary Land — Cave' and counts the TRANSFORMED Tecutlan backside. Front-face Tunnel-Grinder is a three-mana ARTIFACT, cannot be played as a land from the opening hand, and only transforms after three qualifying descended end steps. The 100/97 count is consistent; for turn-five color/access trials, use **34 ordinary lands + one artifact whose eventual backside is a land**, never treat it as a 35th unconditional early land. Wizards Lost Caverns release notes confirm both faces and transform conditions. The original author audit's printed-card role totals need this nuance; it is NOT grounds to alter sealed Stage A.

**Additional source-supported sequencing corrections:**
- Roaming Throne naming Pirate makes Brass's ETB mill AND combat trigger an additional time. For two returned Pirates the triggers choose targets separately when put on stack; one Pirate card cannot be reanimated twice if first resolution moved it to battlefield. Throne is not a Pirate creature card in graveyard itself.
- Merchant Raiders (Human Pirate 3U) creates an ETB tap lock of up to one targeted creature on its own entrance; each later Pirate enters supplies another trigger. The chosen creature fails to untap during its controller's untap step WHILE Raiders remains under our control. With Roaming Throne choosing Pirate, Raiders triggers are doubled; removal of Raiders releases the continuous lock. This is much more than a 4/4 hasty attacker and overturns the GrokBot reason 'low-impact reanimation target'.
- The Indomitable is a Vehicle, not a Pirate creature card; its graveyard cast requires three tapped Pirates/vehicles and paying 2UU; it does not return for free from Brass. Wonder in graveyard enables flying only while controlling Island land subtype; Wonder cannot be targeted by Brass and reanimating it conventionally can turn off its grave-dependent payoff.
- Power Conduit removes one finality counter as an activation cost, once per tap (without additional untapping); Nesting Grounds instead has two actual target permanents and sorcery activation. The opponent can respond to an activated ability's stack/targets; paying Conduit's counter-removal cost is not reversed by such response. A blink resets finality but also removes Brass's base-4/4 setting by creating a new object. Port Razer extra combats have the one-opponent-once-per-turn attack restriction; no unconditional loop claimed.

## Most important unblinded disagreement: Ravager versus Raiders

GrokBot suggests **IN Dire Fleet Ravager / OUT Merchant Raiders**. This is a REAL consequential unresolved proposal, not a source dispute or generic rank.

**Benefits of Ravager.** Five-mana 4/4 Orc Pirate with menace/deathtouch whose entry makes EACH PLAYER lose one-third of their CURRENT life, rounded up. Brass returns it as a haste 4/4 without paying 3BB, so the entry triggers immediately without connecting through blockers. Roaming Throne naming Pirate makes the ETB ability trigger an additional time. At 40 life each, a single ETB changes every player 40 to 26; two sequential ETB triggers change each player 40 -> 26 -> 17. This is genuine high-pressure Pirate identity, and proactive noncombat threat against fogged ground boards. It is LIFE LOSS not damage; Angrath's Marauders does not double it.

**Costs of Ravager.** This is symmetric, including Cory's controller, while baseline also has Reanimate, Ancient Tomb, Black Market Connections, Mana Confluence and Toxic Deluge life expenditures. At controller 13 vs opponents 40, a single Ravager results in owner 8 vs each opponent 26; doubled by Throne it yields owner 5 vs opponents 17. This can be a losing line without immediate closing pressure. Hand-cast 3BB is less flexible than the 3U Raiders. Ravager does not suppress the very blockers that Port Razer must successfully connect through.

**Benefits of Raiders.** Self-entry lock plus future Pirate-entry locks directly enable attack triggers, Port Razer player connection and Admiral Beckett Brass's secondary steal condition. A live Raiders and Roaming Throne add two potential taps on each entering Pirate; this is a cumulative board-denial engine, not merely a tempo trick. If blockers matter, the asymmetric denial can outperform symmetrical life loss. Some noncombat/pillowfort pods would favor Ravager. Stage A independently identified Raiders as a conditional alternative cut for an unrelated engine, but did NOT identify Ravager before seal; neither earlier verdict is retroactively its own Stage A discovery.

**Alternative cuts.** Fathom Fleet Captain (cheap Pirate body and pay-2 token production) is a plausible weaker immediate Brass-return than Raiders, but removing it decreases 2-mana tribal density and early combat setup. Dire Fleet Daredevil depends on opponent graveyard instants/sorceries yet has a real Pirate ETB spell-use role. Admiral Beckett Brass has no immediate entry ability but identity-heavy Pirate anthem/conditional permanent theft. A targeted Ravager addition must compare at least Raiders and Fathom Fleet Captain with identical state; cannot choose the allegedly weakest one by generic ranking. No clear zero-sacrifice cut established.

## Fate of sealed Stage A challengers

1. **Nesting Grounds / Crumbling Necropolis:** official Wizards MH3 notes confirm legal counter transfer and sorcery activation. Conduit already solves the same class of problem at instant-speed activation without displacing a U/B/R source. The swap reduces on-time five-mana 2UBR reliability in a deck with only 34 opening-deck land cards. In turn after combat Nesting can rescue a creature if both targets survive, but cannot answer a mid-combat removal spell by activating while stack not empty. **No unambiguous improvement; reject as immediate proposal**, retain optional future mana-constrained test only if counter resilience emerges as actual major failure.
2. **Conjurer's Closet / Admiral Beckett Brass** (alternative Merchant Raiders): not a prior accepted owner candidate. The Closet must be cast for 5 mana in addition to Brass, survive until our end step and have a creature to flicker. It can refresh a finality-marked Pirate and ETB, but loses Brass's 4/4 overwrite and occupies a non-Pirate slot. Beckett provides board anthem and thematic theft, while Raiders is a live combat enabler. **Reject both speculative exact cuts as unproven**; no broad testing priority unless follow-up evidence shows finality-counter loops are decisive.
3. **Hex Parasite** in the original author review offers additional counter removal for recurring life/mana payments, but removing The Indomitable or Shared Animosity for this low-toughness NON-Pirate loses meaningful combat engines. Existing review held rather than approved it; no new trial compels reopening.
