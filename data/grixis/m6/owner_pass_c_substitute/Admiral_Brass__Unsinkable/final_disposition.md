## Strategic identity and natural ceiling

Commander Admiral Brass, Unsinkable costs {2}{U}{B}{R}, ETB mills four, then at beginning of each of our combat steps may target a **Pirate creature card in our graveyard** and return it to battlefield with finality, base 4/4 and haste to end of turn. An arriving high-MV Pirate such as Angrath's Marauders, Port Razer, Amphin Mutineer, Hostage Taker or Coercive Recruiter can produce immediate ETB/combat advantage without paying its printed cost. Entomb, Buried Alive, Faithless Looting, Mary Read/Anne Bonny, Tunnel-Grinder and self-mill supply cards; Power Conduit removes a finality counter as an activation COST to preserve future recursion; Reanimate, Victimize, Living Death and Patriarch's Bidding offer backup. Roaming Throne choosing Pirate adds a triggered instance of Brass mill and each begin-combat reanimation plus eligible other Pirate triggers. It does NOT retroactively change a non-Pirate creature CARD in the graveyard into a Pirate merely because it is Pirate while on battlefield.

**Natural power ceiling working Bracket 4 — Optimized**, with powerful recursive Pirate combat, theft, damage multipliers and free interaction; not an independently proven cEDH Bracket 5 engine. Commander costs five, target Pirate must be available by start combat, the graveyard and finality counter are vulnerable, and damage payoffs may need actual connection. A generic non-Pirate reanimation shell or blanket combo package would erase what defines this commander.

## Current source-backed rules, threats, and limitations

- Brass only triggers at the beginning of our combat steps; legal Pirate target must already be in our graveyard when put onto the stack. Targeted grave removal in response can counter/fizzle that return. Removing Brass before the beginning-combat trigger prevents it; after trigger is on stack, removal does not automatically stop that instance.
- Port Razer's combat damage to a *player* makes additional combat and untaps creatures; it cannot attack a PLAYER it already attacked in this turn. Its extra combat can give Brass another beginning-combat trigger; do not claim infinite repeats without legal opponents/attacks.
- Finality applies a battlefield-to-graveyard exile replacement, not bounce/exile from other zones. Power Conduit can remove as paid activated COST (tap restricted); Nesting Grounds chooses two targets at sorcery speed; blink returns a new object without its old counter and without Brass's base-4/4 characteristic-setter. Roaming Throne induces extra triggers, not trigger-copying.
- If Merchant Raiders leaves before its continuous untap-denial period ends, the lock ceases; opponent responses to Brass or Raiders matter. Dire Fleet Ravager ETB can resolve even if Ravager is removed after its trigger goes on stack; removal alone doesn't undo queued life loss. Roaming Throne doubles Ravager's symmetrical trigger, not only opponents' loss.
- Caveat on source legality: the original October 16 Scryfall CSV reported all names Commander-legal and within Grixis with no project exception; this Stage C spot-checked Wizards official Brass/finality/Throne/Tunnel-Grinder/Port Razer/Nesting-Grounds interactions. It did NOT fetch and recertify every card's current Oracle/legality as of this Git commit, nor did it run four-player effect-aware games.

Opponent counterplay includes exiling targeted graveyard Pirates, removing five-mana Brass before combat, stopping Port Razer connection, removal of Raiders before future triggers, and finality-exiling marked Pirates on death. Grave hate and commander tax make Ravager's flashy life loss less reliable than an uninterrupted goldfish suggests.
