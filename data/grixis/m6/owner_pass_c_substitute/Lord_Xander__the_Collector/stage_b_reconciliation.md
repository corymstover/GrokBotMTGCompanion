## Five bounded paid/state comparisons

1. **Lord in command zone, only Entomb/Reanimate:** Entomb finds a *library* card, not Lord Xander; neither Persist nor Necromancy bypasses the need first to access Lord in an actual graveyard or hand. Hand requiring Xander to be fetched from command zone by Entomb is invalid. Existing acceleration may hardcast seven mana, then later recur.
2. **Lord in graveyard, three mana available:** after Lord dies and owner leaves it in graveyard, Persist ({1}{B}) **cannot choose it as a legal target**. Necromancy ({2}{B}) can return Lord, producing the targeted enters discard-half effect; if an opponent removes Necromancy, Lord is sacrificed and its targeted dies trigger can resolve, which may offset aura vulnerability. Opponent graveyard hate before Necromancy's ETB trigger resolves can prevent return. A player cannot rely on Necromancy with only two mana.
3. **Auton Soldier in graveyard, Xander on battlefield:** Persist ({1}{B}) can return the **nonlegendary card**. Auton Soldier's as-enters copy effect can choose Lord, so it becomes a nonlegendary Xander copy with myriad and triggers discard-half; two-mana Persist has a real advantage over three-mana Necromancy. If Lord is not on battlefield, Auton Soldier cannot copy Lord **from his graveyard**. Do not mis-state Persist as fully inert.
4. **Haste burst:** Goryo's Vengeance ({1}{B}) on a legendary Xander **in graveyard** brings back haste for one declared attack; an end-step exile does not trigger Lord dying. Sacrificing Xander to pre-positioned Altar of Dementia before exile gives mill-6 plus Xander dies-trigger, but only with proper priority/availability. Necromancy cast as though flash at off-sorcery timing causes enchantment sacrifice in next cleanup and Xander's death, but does **not** inherently grant haste, so cannot be scored as equivalent burst access.
5. **Copy/attack/payoff:** with original Xander declared attacking, Blade's token copies enter tapped and attacking, generating Xander ETBs and possible legend-rule deaths but not their own attack triggers. With an Auton-Xander copy **declared attacking next turn**, that actual declared attack triggers one Xander mill; its myriad new tokens generate enter discards not attack. If 41 cards remain on target player's library, Xander+Bruvac mills 40 leaving one; any instant-death inference from empty/one library alone is invalid.

These are manual deterministic rules/cost traces; no stochastic hand simulation, player-response Monte Carlo, or effect-aware Phase B certificate was generated. Reuse the full authored 21-case manual audit rather than repeat it. New test capacity should be spent on **exact +Necromancy/-Persist against baseline and Goryo alternative**, controlling actual Xander zone, fast mana, graveyard hate, off-turn vs sorcery-time returns, opposing enchantment removal, and whether a declared attack occurs. Only then resolve which tradeoff improves commander-first outcomes.
