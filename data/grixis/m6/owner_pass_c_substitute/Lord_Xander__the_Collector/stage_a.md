## Exact baseline, power ceiling, commander identity

The 97 decklist rows sum to **100 physical cards** (one commander + 99 library). **97 distinct card names**; only basic **Island ×2, Mountain ×2, Swamp ×2** repeat. **33 lands**, some conditional/tapped/colorless utility lands. All visible identities are compatible with Xander's U/B/R; no special project exception appears relevant to this exact deck, with full bulk identity/ban screening outside this Stage A.

Lord Xander is a **seven-mana {4}{U}{B}{R} 6/6 Vampire Demon Noble** with three asymmetric intimidation acts: (1) enter: target opponent discards floor(half their hand), (2) **attack declaration**: the defending player mills floor(half their library), (3) die: target opponent sacrifices floor(half their **nonland** permanents) of their choice. The attack ability does **not target** the player; the ETB and death abilities do. It does not simply destroy half opposing lands, nor force the opponent to sacrifice specific best nonlands. Its power is in repetitively layering these proportional effects against the right targets.

The list's natural upper ceiling is **Bracket 4 — Optimized** as a strategic provisional placement: explosive fast mana, reanimation, nonlegendary copies, trigger multipliers, combat-multipliers, and a milling finish with Bruvac/Fraying Sanity/Riverchurn/Guildmage. It contains general-purpose powerful pieces (Underworld Breach/Lion's Eye Diamond/Brain Freeze) but a plausible Xander-first spine; do not imply cEDH bracket merely because isolated staples are powerful. Ordinary seven-mana Xander is slow and highly public; speed and graveyard recursion are central to this commander's own power ceiling.

## Acceleration, color access, role dependencies

- A seven-mana U/B/R commander and a **33-land** base are credible only because of dense low-cost acceleration (Ancient Tomb, Sol Ring, Mana Vault, Grim Monolith, Arcane Signet, three Talismans, Dark Ritual, Jeska's Will, Lotus Petal, Lion's Eye Diamond) and top-grade dual/fetch fixing. This is **not** an empirical assertion of reliable turn-three Xander: ritual/LED hands can run out of cards; a tapped Hall of the Bandit Lord/Bojuka Bog/Xander's Lounge or utility Hall of Echoes/Urza's Saga alters actual colored pips.
- Lion's Eye Diamond can generate mana toward Xander from the **command zone** because Xander is not discarded from hand when LED discards the hand; it must be activated at a permitted priority window **before** casting, not during the casting cost-payment substep. Force of Negation's free alternative is generally limited to opponents' turns, so it cannot be counted as zero-mana protection on the pilot's own attack/combo turn.
- Deploying Xander demands a target for hand discard, but the attack demands keeping a seven-mana creature alive to **declare** an attack, unless haste from Greaves/Bandit Hall or copied nonlegendary haste token from Helm is available. The death trigger is valuable when Xander **dies**, not when exiled, bounced or shuffled; when using a reanimation spell from graveyard, choosing commander command-zone movement prematurely can break an expected return.
- Cheap Entomb/Reanimate/Animate Dead engines actually access Xander; copied Xanders (Auton Soldier, Sakashima, Spark Double, Molten Duplication, Phantasmal Image, Helm) multiply enters/dies/attacks with very different haste, legend-rule and copy-text outcomes. No assertion that an enters-attacking copy triggered an attack was made.

## Material mechanic trace: high-risk pathways

1. **Rounded halves:** with 5 cards in hand, Xander ETB makes selected opponent discard **2**; on attack against a defender with 61 cards in library, they mill **30**; on dies versus 7 nonland permanents, targeted opponent chooses **3** to sacrifice. Two *separate* halves occurring sequentially recalculate from each new quantity, not automatically remove everything; Roaming Throne and Strionic Resonator copy separate triggered abilities when properly applied.
2. **Bruvac:** a single Xander attack on an opponent with N cards asks that player to mill `floor(N/2)`; Bruvac doubles the **actual number milled** to `2×floor(N/2)`. With odd N, exactly one card can remain. 'Xander + Bruvac empties any library' is **not universally true** without a further mill/draw; Fraying Sanity/Monument can close the remainder.
3. **Myriad versus declaration:** Blade of Selves generates attacking token copies but those tokens **did not attack**, so they do not produce Xander attack/mill triggers. Legendary Xander copies can instead prompt a legend-rule selection and **dies triggers** even after their enters triggers are placed. Exiling myriad tokens at end of combat is not dying. Auton Soldier copies Xander as a **nonlegendary artifact** with myriad; its entering-attacking tokens yield Xander ETB discards but not attack-mill triggers, and expiry exiles rather than dies.
4. **Hall of Echoes:** activating for {5} makes an already present land a temporary Xander copy; the object did **not enter**, so there is **no Xander ETB**. It may attack for the attack trigger if controlled continuously since turn start or otherwise hasted. Legend rule is suspended for the turn and returns as the copy ceases at cleanup. A fresh land with no haste cannot become a creature and attack without summoning sickness.
5. **Saw in Half / Feign Death:** destroying Lord in Saw in Half triggers the dies effect if it genuinely goes from battlefield to graveyard; token copies then enter and trigger discard, and legendary token conflicts can create additional death triggers. Without a no-legend-rule modifier, do not count both legendary token copies indefinitely surviving. Feign Death's death/return window requires leaving commander accessible in graveyard so its delayed trigger can bring back this same card, not moving it to command zone first. Removal/exile in response can stop both.
6. **Persist scope:** printed `Persist` ({1}{B} sorcery) explicitly returns **target nonlegendary creature** from **your** graveyard with a -1/-1 counter. **Legendary Lord Xander is not a legal target**. Persist *can* return relevant nonlegendary utility engines such as Opposition Agent, Orcish Bowmasters, Duskmantle Guildmage, Roaming Throne, and nonlegendary Auton Soldier, but cannot itself recur the namesake 7-mana commander. This is a severe commander-first role limitation despite Persist being officially playable in Commander.
7. **Named but exposure-acknowledged challenger:** `Necromancy` (3-mana reanimation enchantment) can choose a creature card in **any** graveyard, including legendary Xander. At sorcery timing it can preserve an attached recurring Xander until removed; when it leaves, the returned creature is sacrificed, enabling rather than negating Xander's dies trigger. Its unusual flash-like cast clause sacrifices Necromancy at next cleanup if cast when a sorcery could not be cast, causing a delayed Xander dies line. Compare exact stack, aura-removal and entry response sequencing; do not count an immediate flash return and permanent retention at no cost.
8. **Mill/draw payoff:** Cruel Calculations draws for actual cards put from *target player's library* into graveyard **this turn**, not cards discarded from hand or sacrificed from field. Riverchurn Monument's exhaust ability scales with the selected player's graveyard and once-per-object exhaust use; Brain Freeze counts actual spells cast before it resolves. A Mindcrank + Duskmantle Guildmage loop requires activation, a starting loss/mill event and sufficient finite library/life to kill; arbitrary mathematical 'infinite mill' isn't automatically lethal on a single trigger if library becomes empty first.
9. **Enemy counterplay:** exile Xander instead of killing; counter original Xander in command zone (free Fierce Guardianship cannot apply until Xander controlled); hold removal for haste-combat declaration, remove Bruvac before mill replacement, graveyard-hate the creature targeted by reanimation, remove Necromancy after reanimation, disrupt copies before triggers/priority where allowed, or prevent combat damage for the Sword of Feast and Famine/Aggravated Assault engine. A reanimation aura leaves a commander vulnerable to both creature and enchantment interaction.

## Neutral challenger and actual weak-slot hypothesis

The strongest non-generic structural challenge is **`Persist`**: unlike Animate Dead/Reanimate, it cannot bring back the commander whose huge enter/die triggers define the deck. This does not make it a dead card (the list has utility creatures worth reviving), but it makes it questionable in a very fast-mana, graveyard-enabled 7-mana-commander shell where the most valuable body is legendary.

- **Challenger:** Necromancy (prior handoff exposed name; zero claim of independent suggestion) against Persist. Upside: legendary/any-yard reanimation and Xander death-trigger leverage after enchantment exit, conditional instant surprise. Cost: {2}{B} versus Persist's {1}{B}, enchantment removal vulnerability and self-sacrifice if flashed, slightly worse low-curve setup. Alternate neutral mechanic challenger **Dance of the Dead** ({1}{B} aura; returns any creature including Xander), at two mana, but entered creature tapped/untap-tax and attached-enchantment vulnerability affect attack-mill velocity.
- **Competing cuts:** rather than only 'Persist is not Xander', also test whether a slow alternate copy multiplier like Helm of the Host, Hall of Echoes, or four-mana Roaming Throne can be removed with less loss of unique **ETB / attack / death** access. These are very costly but each has distinct Xander-first upside; cutting one may be strategically worse than losing utility-Persist.
- **Counterargument to replacing Persist:** nonlegendary Auton Soldier is a powerful entrant whose return creates a nonlegendary Xander ETB, and Persist's cheaper rate may be decisive on constrained turns; it avoids the enchantment attachment failure mode. Necromancy is not universally superior without comparing exact hands, graveyard targets, mana and opponent interaction. Source-level commander targeting still creates a credible challenger.
- **Other structural danger:** 33-land, high seven-mana commander plus 4–6-mana clone/extra-combat effects could draw high-impact spells without a payable plan; Wheel/Remora/Rhystic/One Ring/top tutors mitigate but not conclusively. The "fast cEDH staples" package can conceal slow failure states. Do not claim win-rate figures from density inspection.
