## Commander-specific strategy and natural ceiling

Lord Xander ({4}{U}{B}{R}, 6/6 legendary Vampire Demon Noble) forces hand discard on **entry**, half the **defending** player's library to grave on each **declared attack**, and half a targeted opponent's **nonland** permanents sacrificed on **death**, always rounded **down**. Actual 7-mana colored access, graveyard zone choices, copy text/legend rule, haste and permission windows determine whether those crippling events materialize. The approved list deliberately centers reanimators, clone/trigger engines, extra combats and synergistic mill payoffs, with fast mana, tutors, protection and a Breach/mill backup. It is credibly **Bracket 4 — Optimized** by natural ceiling, not merely a pile of off-plan cEDH cards.

## Checked material interactions and opponent responses

1. **Persist's nonlegendary target restriction:** the approved `Persist` ({1}{B}) cannot target the legendary Lord Xander even if Lord is in graveyard. It can legally recur **Auton Soldier, Phantasmal Image, Spark Double, Roaming Throne, Bowmasters, Opposition Agent** and other *nonlegendary* bodies; when Auton enters and copies Lord on battlefield, this can indirectly create Lord ETB value. `Entomb` and `Gamble` cannot search the command zone for Lord. This limitation is important **strategically**, not a format legality blocker.
2. **Necromancy hypothesis:** for {2}{B}, enchantment can recur Lord after he actually reaches a graveyard, including legendary and enemy graveyards. Lord returning yields ETB discard. When Necromancy leaves, the returned Xander is sacrificed, giving the Xander dies trigger if it goes to graveyard; cast outside sorcery timing, its cleanup sacrifice provides a possible death event but **does not grant haste**. One extra mana versus Persist, enchantment-removal exposure and graveyard hate are consequential costs. The named swap was already proposed by the prior author; it is **not owner approved**.
3. **Goryo alternative:** for {1}{B}, Goryo's Vengeance on Xander in graveyard grants haste and temporary access to declare a mill-half attack. The end-step **exile** normally does not give Xander a death trigger; Altar of Dementia can sacrifice it before exile for a death trigger if protected and available. Different win-vector emphasis versus persistent Necromancy.
4. **Myriad no-attack mistake:** tapped-attacking Xander copies from Blade of Selves or Auton Soldier **didn't attack**, so their Xander attack triggers do **not** occur. They may trigger **enter** effects; legendary Blade tokens may trigger **dies** effects through the legend rule, while ordinary end-combat **exile** does not count as dying. Hall of Echoes becoming an existing creature copy produces no ETB. Helm of the Host copies early combat with haste and can be **declared** attacking, a meaningful distinction.
5. **Mill/poison-free math:** with Bruvac, Xander's `floor(N/2)` mill becomes `2·floor(N/2)`, which leaves exactly one card for odd N. An opponent does not lose solely from having an empty library; a required draw from empty must occur (or a separate loss effect). Grok advisory's unqualified 'mill entire library' contradicted its own odd-card note; its "Auton/Blade multiply attack trigger" claim similarly confuses entering attacking with declaring attack. Its claim of an unconditional 'infinite' Mindcrank/Guildmage outcome omits library/life termination. None makes the canonical deck illegal.
6. **Response windows:** opposing grave hate can stop Necromancy/Animate Dead/Reanimate, target removal can break Xander before attack, Pac/Force pitch modes have costs/time constraints, and enchantment removal can force sacrificed Xander at an inconvenient time. `Force of Negation` is not free on your own turn. Hall of the Bandit Lord and several specialty lands complicate colored access in a 33-land base. Sword of Feast and Famine needs actual combat damage to untap land before postcombat Aggravated Assault can repeat; blocking can break the loop.
7. **Oracle/legality boundary:** The historical 2026-10-14 complete canonical gate, author-reported October 16 engineering Oracle source, GrokBot-reported October 20 Scryfall /cards/collection, WotC current Commander and comprehensive rules, WotC Doctor Who/Reality Fracture release notes, and focal 2026 indexed Oracle checks support the current material claims. **No newly executed full 97-name Scryfall legality recheck or quantitative games are claimed by this Pass C.** Owner's documented November 13 future-release verification trigger remains separate.
