## Commander identity and evidence-backed KEEP

Don Andres excels at **(1)** taking real opponent-owned creatures to make them `+2/+2`, menace, deathtouch **Pirates** for lethal attacks/Admiral Beckett/Malcolm/Breeches; and **(2)** actually **casting opponent-owned noncreature spells** for two **tapped** Treasures per cast. It rewards control/ownership distinction and real foreign-card casting, not generic impulse draw, own exile casts or owned spell copies. `Siphon Insight`, `Praetor's Grasp`, `Arcane Heist`, `Stolen Strategy`, `Laughing Jasper Flint`, `Thief of Sanity`, `Etali`, `Wand of Wonder` and related theft keep the second half; `Gilded Drake`, `Hostage Taker`, `Coercive Recruiter`, `Reanimate`, `Animate Dead`, `Insurrection`, `Thieving Amalgam` and Pirate attackers keep the first half. **Bracket 4 — Optimized** remains the working natural ceiling.

**Validated rules distinctions / opponent answers:**

1. A spell copy created on your stack is **owned by you** despite using opponent text and does not meet Don's nonowner spell-casting condition. A stolen real opposing instant/sorcery cast with Arcane Heist *does* qualify. A stolen creature spell does not produce Treasure, but its battlefield creature receives Don buffs. Stolen face-down manifested opposing cards also qualify as opponent-owned creatures under your control; our own generated token copies do not.
2. Don's Treasures enter **tapped**, and absent an independent untap effect cannot pay the next stolen spell in the same turn. Roaming Throne choosing Pirate may produce an additional Don trigger, but cannot double his static +2/+2/Pirate effect. Cast triggers may resolve even if the actual foreign spell is countered later, unless Don was removed before the spell was cast.
3. A `Coercive Recruiter` control switch is **not** an enters event; Beckett counts Pirate *damage dealt to one player* and can lose its theft condition to combat answers. Removing Don removes the acquired static power/keywords immediately. Enemy graveyard hate, creature-light opponents, uncastable exiled cards and tapped Treasure timing are serious real limitations.
4. `Thieving Amalgam`'s seven-mana delay is a meaningful curve cost but generates up to three opponent-owned enhanced manifest Pirates per opposing turn cycle, and drains when a nonowned creature actually dies. It is commander-first, not generic filler. 35 lands and nine cheap rocks provide plausible colored fixing but do not prove turn-four UBR in every hand; source restrictions of Cavern and Path remain.
