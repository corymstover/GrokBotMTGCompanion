## Substantive agreements and disagreement adjudication

1. **Stage A and prior ChatGPT reach the same commander-first diagnosis:** an opponent-owned *creature permanent* controlled by you gains +2/+2, menace, deathtouch and Pirate; a **cast** opponent-owned **noncreature** spell (not creature/land, not your own copied spell) makes **two tapped** Treasures. Cast-vs-put-battlefield, control-vs-ownership and tap restrictions are the main false combo risks. `Roaming Throne` choosing Pirate can double a qualifying Treasure trigger but never doubles the commander static buff. `Thieving Amalgam` manifests one opposing owned face-down card per opposing upkeep and has a coherent seven-mana ceiling; **do not** cut it merely because of converted mana cost.
2. **The Stage A Mob Rule versus Insurrection challenger was already explicitly evaluated in the comprehensive review.** It found the same reason to reject it: `Mob Rule` needs `{4}{R}{R}` but takes just one power subset, while `{5}{R}{R}{R}` `Insurrection` takes *all* creatures, strips all opponent blockers and gives every stolen creature Don's bonus. The neutral hypothesis was a real economic tradeoff, but **not a newly overlooked improvement**. A prior KEEP is not quantitative testing; nevertheless the current record contains no positive outcome from exact paired hands to refute the superior all-board ceiling. Stage A provisional `NEEDS TESTING` therefore yields a conservative Stage C **KEEP** for the existing list; retain Mob Rule as nonurgent contingency if future targeted real games reveal repeated eight-mana stranded hands.
3. **GrokBot's proposed exact Mnemonic replacement remains rejected on rules/curve evidence.** Wizards official Guilds of Ravnica notes confirm `Mnemonic Betrayal {1}{U}{B}` exiles all opponents' graveyards and permits **paid casts of spells, no land plays**, only that turn, with normal timing; remaining cards return at next end step. Don creates **tapped** Treasures for qualifying casts, which generally **cannot pay another Mnemonic stolen spell that same turn**, absent a separate untap effect. This directly contradicts GrokBot's immediate chain self-funding claim, even though it later acknowledged the Treasures are tapped. Its upside is real with spell-rich graveyards and *preexisting* mana, but against early/empty/hated graveyards the spell provides nothing.
4. **`Siphon Insight {U}{B}` is an engine enabler, not a generic low-yield slot.** Wizards Midnight Hunt notes confirm at instant speed it banks one of an opponent's top two cards, permission lasts as long as exiled, includes *land* plays within normal land-drop restrictions, and has flashback `{1}{U}{B}` for a second acquired card. A cast stolen instant/sorcery triggers Don; stolen creature doesn't create Treasure but is subsequently buffed. This is low-curve, resilient, flexible and does not rely on graveyard density. The GrokBot out-card rationale ignored these qualitative axes. The already-merged reconciliation's **KEEP Siphon** is supported.
5. **Further challenger `Bribery` remains a conditional open case, not a deck defect.** Original deep review correctly identifies that `{3}{U}{U}` Bribery directly takes the best opponent creature from their library under our control for immediate Don buff, while replacing `Thieving Amalgam` loses ongoing three-opponent-upkeep manifests and death drain, and replacing `Jhoira, Weatherlight Corsair` loses repeatable historic/noncreature access. As with Stage A's secondary `Zara, Renegade Recruiter`, the exact best opponent threat density, cost window and named cut are not resolved by score. No justification for auto-swap exists.

## Source-paid bounded cases; no new gameplay measurements

- **M1 — 6 mana + mixed table:** Don alive; opponents have creatures with printed/current powers 2, 3, 4 and 6. With `{4}{R}{R}` available but no eight mana, Mob Rule can choose the low group (2 and 3) or high group (4 and 6), not both. The stolen subset then gains +2/+2/menace/deathtouch under Don and can attack with haste; it may be enough to kill a player if blockers are sparse. This is an authentic turn-6 advantage for Mob Rule over an uncastable Insurrection, not evidence of broad dominance.
- **M2 — 8 mana + mixed table:** With `{5}{R}{R}{R}` available, Insurrection takes **all four** opponent creatures, untaps them all, grants haste and yields all four Don-buffed Pirates plus removing every original opposing creature blocker (if no outside intervention). Mob Rule at six takes at most a power band and leaves the other band blocking. An active Don can be killed before combat, removing buffs; fog/prevention or opponent instant interaction can prevent lethal even from Insurrection. Insurrection's ceiling is strictly stronger on this mixed board once the extra cost is paid.
- **M3 — graveyard-empty:** Three opponents have no graveyard spells. Mnemonic at three mana exiles nothing, creates zero stolen casts/Treasure. Siphon at two instant chooses top cards from a library, banks one indefinitely with flashback later. Neither card itself is nonowned when cast; Don doesn't trigger until a **real** stolen noncreature is subsequently cast. `Siphon Insight` can legally pick a land for later land play, but playing it does not cast a spell, hence zero Don Treasure.
- **M4 — three spell-rich graveyards:** Mnemonic resolves, grants a one-turn menu of opponent-owned spells. If available untapped mana can cast two instants and one sorcery, Don creates six **tapped** Treasures total when three foreign noncreature spells are cast (or twelve with an appropriately chosen Roaming Throne), but they do not fund this same-turn window without untap help. If graveyard hate exiles cards in response, options decrease; with Rule of Law, the Mnemonic cast itself commonly exhausts the one-spell permission that turn. Two opponent-owned creatures cast instead generate zero Treasures though they may become enhanced Pirates.
- **M5 — stolen and copied ownership:** You exchange with Gilded Drake and take the target opponent's actual creature: it gains Pirate/+2/menace/deathtouch while Don remains. Creating your own token copy of that creature produces a token **owned by you**, which gets **none** of Don's owner-condition buffs. `Arcane Heist` may cast the original opponent-owned graveyard instant/sorcery during resolution, triggering Don if he survives; a cipher-generated copied Heist is owned by the copier and does not itself trigger. `Coercive Recruiter` taking control isn't an ETB and doesn't create another Pirate-entry trigger.
- **M6 — blockers and windows:** `Admiral Beckett Brass` requires three Pirates to actually deal combat damage to the **same player**; three Pirates merely attacking while one is blocked don't qualify. If a boardwipe resolves after Don is removed, formerly stolen creatures no longer have the bonus, and exiling a manifest with `Dauthi Voidwalker`'s grave replacement can prevent the ordinary *dies* trigger on `Thieving Amalgam`. All scenarios are payable deterministic rule traces based on sourced Oracle; none is a measured distribution of games.
