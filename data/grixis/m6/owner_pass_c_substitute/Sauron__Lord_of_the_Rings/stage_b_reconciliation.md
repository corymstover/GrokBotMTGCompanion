## Directed rules fixtures and correction checks

**Same-mana 2U comparison:** With a reanimation spell available and an otherwise unprepared graveyard, original Forbidden Alchemy looks at four *top library* cards, moves one to hand and three to graveyard, potentially finding an immediately needed land or protection; may flash back 6B if future mana exists. Intuition searches **any three** distinct library cards and chooses a target opponent who chooses one for your hand, other two to your GY. Intuition pile of **Archon of Cruelty / Ancient Brass Dragon / Metamorphosis Fanatic** guarantees TWO useful creature cards in graveyard IF all still in library and Intuition resolves; opponent can keep Fanatic in hand, but it does NOT delete the other two valid threats for Sauron or existing reanimation. A pile can deliberately use alternative roles (mana sources/reanimation spells) at cost of giving opponent selection. Counter, Opposition Agent analogs or gravehate alter it; neither spell itself returns creature or draws guaranteed combo. At cost 2U, this is a cleaner high-value source of comparison than committing five mana and life to Doom Whisperer.

**Mouth vs Doom:** The Mouth's ETB mills its TARGET player three, then reads INSTANT/SORCERY card count in THAT PLAYER graveyard, not number of creature cards or just the three milled. Self mill plus existing two instants/sorceries can yield a distinct Army 2+; opponent graveyard might fuel X≥7 but won't stock our reanimation pool. Doom Whisperer flying 6/6 costs 3BB, pay2 life for each surveil2 and may put creatures into graveyard at instant speed; it produces no immediate Orcs and doesn't gain from Ring/Nazgûl tribal triggers. A delayed repeated surveil requires surviving battlefield access and expendable life; Sauron already has Entomb/Buried Alive and 3+ cheap reanimators. **No credible no-cost upgrade.**

**Commander / graveyard windows:** Opponent counterspells on Sauron spell do not stop its cast triggered Army/mill/reanimate. A Stifle-like answer to *the whole trigger* stops all; opponent can grave-exile before the trigger starts, but cannot respond after the five milled cards appear and before one is returned. Real reanimation Auras/sorceries have legal targeted cards and gravehate can fizzle them separately. Reanimating Sauron itself as creature without CAST doesn't yield its cast trigger. Sneak Attack from hand bypasses costs of fatties but also is NOT casting Sauron. Command Beacon moves your commander from command zone to hand: casting Sauron afterward **from hand** pays ordinary printed 5UBR, NOT commander tax; the +2-per-prior-command-zone-cast increase applies to casts FROM COMMAND ZONE. Beacon still requires its tap/sacrifice activation and leaves only 32 land resources, so it is not costless acceleration.

**Prepared / miracle distinction:** Grave Researcher 2B 3/3 has upkeep surveil1 then becomes prepared if >=3 creature cards in GY, may cast B copy of Reanimate while prepared; it cannot cast B copy directly when drawn. Metamorphosis Fanatic 4BB creature can Miracle1B only from timely first DRAW in turn and grants lifelink on reanimated target. Actual separate Reanimate card {B} from hand is readily available and may lose life equal high CMC on resolution. A mass life payment to Doom hurts those pathways.

**Nazgûl and Ring:** With four live Nazgûl, one legal Ring tempts event puts four separate Nazgûl counter triggers on stack; each affects Wraiths if still controlled as resolves. Sauron's opposing commander death ability applies only while Sauron is on battlefield; if owner returns opposing commander to command zone AFTER it died, Sauron still sees death. Four Nazgûl are independently useful thematic outlets and not forced singleton cuts.

**Incarnation Technique demonstrates:** Its copy trigger occurs when spell is CAST. Accepting demonstration creates own copy and selected opponent copy, opponent's copy resolves BEFORE ours, then original; each mills five and returns a creature (if any). This can give the opponent a catastrophic ETB before we get ours. Declining is safe but 4B for a single self-mill five + return, inferior to lower-mana setup lines in many turns. Countering spell and copying on stack are distinct.
