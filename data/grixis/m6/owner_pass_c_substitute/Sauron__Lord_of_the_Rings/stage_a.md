## Commander-first strategy and credible ceiling

**Natural optimized Bracket 4, with high ceiling but an eight-mana commander bottleneck.** Engine: early **dump premium creature to graveyard**, recur Archon of Cruelty, Ancient Brass Dragon, Jin-Gitaxias Core Augur, Valgavoth Terror Eater, Sheoldred Whispering One, large Cave-Hoard/Voracious threats; use early reanimation to buy time or win without Sauron; accelerate/ramp to actually CAST Sauron (rather than merely put him onto battlefield) for his one-spell guaranteed Army + selective graveyard resurrection; loop through his cast again via late commander tax where feasible, or rely on the already-established threat board. Ring/Nazgûl are meaningful secondary armies and progression, not substitutes for reliable reanimation.

**Included synergistic packages**

Grave Researcher // Reanimate is **Strixhaven 2026 prepared**, NOT an Adventure nor an always-castable second B sorcery: first it must be a {2}{B} permanent that reaches upkeep with three or more creature cards in GY to become prepared, then copies its {B} Reanimate spell by cast from its prepared status. ## Neutral strongest challenger: Doom Whisperer over The Mouth of Sauron

**Provisional unapproved exact alternative ADD Doom Whisperer / CUT The Mouth of Sauron.** Doom Whisperer costs **{3}{B}{B}**, 6/6 flying trample Nightmare Demon, may **pay 2 life to surveil 2** as an activated ability, repeatedly with priority. Surveil puts any of those top cards into graveyard and reorders retained top cards, enabling selective creature burial BEFORE a reanimation spell or BEFORE Sauron's cast-trigger resolves (if Doom Whisperer already on battlefield and there is a response window). It is a nonlegendary **Persist** target, a potential 6/6 independent combat threat and a reanimation target itself; it can dig for a specific creature without spending an additional card, at real life and timing cost. Repeated activation after the Sauron cast trigger is on stack can place a creature in graveyard; after that trigger starts to resolve, nobody can interrupt the amass→mill→reanimate sequence. Doom Whisperer is neutral current Gatherer-listed UBR-compatible and not on current WotC Commander ban list.

Why **The Mouth of Sauron** is the precise challenger cut: it costs **{3}{U}{B}**, legendary creature, ETB **target player mills three** and **amasses Orcs X equal to the number of instant/sorcery cards in THAT player's graveyard**. This creates a commander-identity tension: to fuel our reanimation plan one targets oneself and wants **creatures** milled, but an impressive X needs **instant/sorcery concentration** in the same graveyard. Targeting an opponent who has many instants/sorceries can create a big Orc Army but mills THEIR deck three and doesn't stock our yard. It is absolutely flavorful and has a board-widening Army function Doom Whisperer doesn't, and it works on reanimation ETB; the question is whether its five-mana one-shot mill3/conditional Army can outcompete repeatable life-funded self-surveil2 + stronger independent threat in *this* expensive-cast/reanimation deck.

**Counterargument to own preferred improvement:** five mana double black Doom Whisperer can be slower in opener than Faithless Looting/Entomb/Buried Alive and competes with ramp to 8-mana Sauron; paying 6–10 life to surveil 6–10 may be disastrous when Reanimate and life-costed lands already lower life. Its 6/6 body adds a generic strong Demon rather than reinforcing Nazgûl/Ring/Orc density. The Mouth is original Middle-earth identity, fetchable/reanimatable creature, makes an immediate separate Orc Army, and can produce enormous X if graveyard already contains a long sequence of noncreature setup spells. Removing one of a finite set of native amass effects may turn deck toward a standardized black reanimator pile; no victory percentage shows Doom actually superior. **So provisional is NEEDS TESTING, not PROPOSE UPGRADE.**

**Lower-confidence second challenger:** ADD **Incarnation Technique** ({4}{B}, demonstrate; mill five then reanimate a creature from own graveyard on resolution), CUT The Mouth of Sauron; unlike Doom it combines exactly Sauron's own mill-then-reanimate sequencing as a five-mana sorcery. If demonstrate, our copy and an opponent's copy both resolve before original and **opponent's copy resolves FIRST** (WotC Strixhaven official notes). Opponent can reanimate its best threat, a huge political/strategic cost. Can choose not to demonstrate: one five-mana sorcery mills five then returns one body, but this is less efficient than existing {B} Reanimate or 2–3-mana animation lines and cuts a creature reanimation target/Orc theme. Test only if primary line warrants a focused challenger comparison, not as automated strong recommendation.

**Other credible friction slots considered, NOT selected as free cuts:** 6-mana Voracious Fell Beast flying forces **each opponent** sacrifice a creature on ETB, creating Food; as reanimation target this can be a game-winning asymmetric wipe and cannot be labeled generic filler. Grave Researcher is slow prepared but can ultimately provide repeatable {B} Reanimate copy and upkeep surveil, so its setup tax is real but so is dedicated engine. Persist's nonlegendary restriction is binding, yet Archon/Cavern-Hoard Dragon/Ancient Brass Dragon are major existing targets for a cheap 1B spell. Relic of Sauron at four mana is slow, but accelerates Sauron's eight-mana CAST and preserves Mordor flavor. Cutting Nazgûl just because there are duplicates misses their printed nine-card exemption and build identity.

## First-stage deterministic tactical fixtures (NOT measured simulation)

1. **Sauron cast vs counter:** pay {5}UBR (eight total), put Sauron spell on stack, its CAST triggered ability on top. If opposing Counterspell counters only **the Sauron spell**, the already-created trigger still amasses Orcs 5, mills five and reanimates one creature as it resolves. A Stifle/Disallow effect aimed at the **trigger** stops all three components of that single trigger; do not call each substep a separate trigger. If an opposing instant exiles graveyard before this trigger resolves, new top-five milled cards may still yield a creature; if opponents hold gravehate until AFTER seeing five mill, no priority opens inside the trigger to exile the new choices before the return. Sauron can be countered while on stack without ever creating a 9/9, leaving the Army and returned creature.
2. **Nazgûl 4 on battlefield, Ring tempts once:** each Nazgûl sees the event and separately places one counter on **each Wraith** you control, so four separate triggers each put counters across the existing four Nazgûl (four counters per Wraith overall if all survive, none from hypothetical copies of Sauron's trigger). Ring-bearer selection follows actual Ring emblem progression and choice constraints. Opponent commander must DIE and Sauron already exist for Sauron's specific trigger; moving commander to command zone after dying does not cancel it.
3. **Legendary target eligibility:** Persist's legal nonlegendary target Archon of Cruelty vs illegal legendary Sheoldred/Jin-Gitaxias/Sauron Dark Lord. Sauron's own trigger can return legendary creatures, and does not target, but if another copy of same legendary creature you control exists, legend rule state-based actions apply when reanimated threat enters. Do not count two same-named Saurons as independent continuing creatures.
4. **Selective surveil with Doom Whisperer:** with five mana already invested and 30 life, pay two life three times with priority = 6 life, look at up to six cards in three separate surveillance operations, put desirable creatures into graveyard and keep necessary mana/counters on library; do not equate to unconditional draw6 or Entomb any-library-card. Opponents can kill Whisperer in response to its activations (existing abilities still resolve if their source leaves) or exile graveyard before the eventual reanimation resolves. If life below 3, cannot pay 2 life without losing to state-based death risk; actual legality of paying requires adequate life.
5. **The Mouth five-mana fork:** with graveyard {Archon, Anger, Island, Faithless Looting}, entering Mouth targeting self mills three and X counts **instant/sorcery cards in self graveyard after mill**, NOT creature cards; count maybe 1–4 depending what milled. If target opponent with 7 instant/sorceries already in graveyard, can produce at least X7 Army counters after three mill, but our graveyard gains nothing. Doom instead surveils toward a chosen fatty at discretionary 2-life increments with no immediate Army. Compare actual life + card advantage + Army impact, not only gross library milling.
6. **Incarnation Technique demonstrate:** can decline demonstrative branch to deny opponent copy. If accepted, opposite player's copy resolves before our copy before original, and each resolving copy mills five and returns one eligible creature, so with legal graveyard material ours can receive two creatures, opponent one. Extra creature ETB triggers resolve in normal sequence; no reanimation is guaranteed if graveyard empty after milling or gravehate replaced cards.
