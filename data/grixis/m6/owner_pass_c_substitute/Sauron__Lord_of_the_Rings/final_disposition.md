## Commander-first identity, potential and weak states

Sauron's **CAST**, not battlefield ETB, produces one independent ability: (i) amass Orcs 5, (ii) mill five, (iii) return any one creature card from own graveyard to battlefield, no target. This lets an expensive eight-mana UBR commander produce a large Orc Army and premium reanimated body even if the Sauron creature spell is subsequently countered. The 9/9 trampling body and Ring temptation from an opponent's commander **dying while Sauron is present** add a distinctly Middle-earth finishing plan. Reanimating Sauron as a creature via Reanimate, Sneak Attack, Victimize or Necromancy **does not** trigger its CAST ability, so ramping and actually casting him is a unique requirement.

**Realistic Bracket 4 — Optimized**, not presumed Bracket 5. The existing 32-land UBR shell accelerates with rocks/ritual, pre-buries giants through Entomb/Buried Alive/Moria Scavenger/Ripples/Faithless Looting and can animate Archon, Ancient Brass Dragon, Jin-Gitaxias, Sheoldred, Valgavoth or Metamorphosis Fanatic before commander mana comes online. Anger/Wonder in graveyard can grant haste/flying when one controls a land **with Mountain/Island subtype**, including relevant original duals and shocks. Four Nazgûl reward each Ring temptation and amass is retained; do not cut one just for being the fourth copy. This is an intentionally hybrid Orc/Ring + boss casting + high-target reanimator, not a generically substituted cEDH Oracle/Breach pile.

**Consequential weaknesses:** eight-mana three-color cost, one/two commander taxes when recasting directly from command zone, limited total lands and early colored source choices; selected 2B/4-mana rocks must be paid before commander; targeted reanimators vulnerable to graveyard exile; big dragons can sit in hand without proper loot; removal after cast can deny Sauron's 9/9, while mass exile/faster enemy combo punish late setup. A repeated commander spell is expensive. **Command Beacon is useful tax bypass**: when Sauron moves from command zone into hand, a later cast **from hand** pays the printed 5UBR without command-zone tax, but consumes Beacon as a mana source and still costs eight; Sauron must actually be CAST to trigger. No immediate win rate invented.

## Actual rules and opponent counterplay receipts

1. **Cast-trigger stack:** Sauron's trigger is ONE ability that performs amass5 → mill5 → return a creature, all during resolution; no priority to exile newly milled cards between mill and return. Countering only the original Sauron creature spell does not counter that trigger. An effect countering a **triggered ability** itself stops all parts, and opponent may grave-exile preexisting cards before it resolves; milled cards can still create new legal resurrection choices.
2. **Graveyard targeting:** Sauron's CAST trigger does **not** target a creature card. Ordinary Reanimate and animate Auras do target from graveyards and can fail if the target leaves; Persist can return only NONLEGENDARY creatures, so Archon/Ancient Brass Dragon valid, but Jin-Gitaxias/Sheoldred/Valgavoth/Sauron Dark Lord legendary targets invalid. Victimize has cost sacrifice plus two distinct creature-card targets and opponent may deny targets in response. Sneak Attack from hand cheats battlefield entry but does not count as casting Sauron.
3. **New prepared card:** Grave Researcher // Reanimate ({2}{B} creature) needs its upkeep surveil and at least three actual creature cards in own GY to become prepared, then can cast a {B} copy of its spell; never a freely selectable {B} spell from hand at any time. Metamorphosis Fanatic {4}{B}{B} ETB returns a creature and gives lifelink, with Miracle {1}{B} available only via first drawn card on a turn with proper reveal/cast timing. Source: official Secrets of Strixhaven and Duskmourn notes.
4. **Four Nazgûl:** one Ring-tempt event triggers all four live Nazgûl independently, which each put +1/+1 on every Wraith controlled as respective triggers resolve. Their deck exception permits up to nine; both official card legality and special singleton treatment correctly applied. Opposing commander death then command-zone choice does not stop Sauron's already triggered temptation, assuming he was present at the death.
5. **Doom vs Mouth reality:** Doom Whisperer at {3}{B}{B} has {pay 2 life}: surveil 2, activation repeatable at priority and source may die after ability goes on stack without voiding it; not a free guaranteed tutor, draw or Ring trigger. The Mouth of Sauron at {3}{U}{B} on ETB targets a player to mill three, then amasses Orcs based on **that player's instant/sorcery count in graveyard**, not creature cards. Self-mill helps Sauron, but creature-heavy target density doesn't automatically increase Army counter X; target opponent can pump Army at cost of not filling own yard. Retaining Mouth preserves flavorful Orc identity and avoids life cost, while Doom can enhance selective graveyard access and independent combat. This is a meaningful tradeoff, not an automatic upgrade.
6. **Intuition vs Forbidden Alchemy:** both {2}{U} instants. Intuition picks **any three cards** in own library; target opponent chooses one to hand, other two go to graveyard. If exact full-library pile Archon/Ancient Brass/Fanatic is available, two become Sauron/reanimate-eligible regardless of opponent's hand choice; choice impacts optimal ETB chain. Forbidden Alchemy looks at TOP FOUR, picks one to hand, other three to GY, can flashback {6}{B}; can find a needed land/protection rather than precommit three fatties, which may be essential in 32-land eight-mana shell. Opponent gravehate/stack and remaining library names matter to both. This is a credible *same-cost* targeted candidate, not proven faster from counts alone.
7. **Incarnation Technique caveat:** {4}{B} sorcery mills five then returns one GY creature; optional demonstrate can cause opponent's copy (and its strong reanimation) to resolve **before our copy**, then original. It can be declined, in which case it remains five mana for one action. Its effects are synergistic but opportunity cost and enemy interaction are substantial; no forced inclusion.
8. **Counterplay beyond gravehate:** countermagic on targeted reanimation, exile in response, Rule of Law/spell timing, removal of Sauron before opponent commanders die, blocker/removal response to Dragon attack triggers, lost ETB through stifling effect, board wipe during investment and incidental life pressure from Tomb/Reanimate/Doom are not converted to hypothetical no-opponent combo rates. Commander damage with 9/9 trample requires multiple hits and legal attacks, not one guaranteed lethal.
