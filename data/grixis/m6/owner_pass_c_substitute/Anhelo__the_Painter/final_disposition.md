## Commander identity / ceiling

Anhelo (UBR, 1/3 deathtouch) grants casualty 2 to the FIRST instant or sorcery the controller casts on EACH turn. Sacrifice a separate creature of power at least 2 as an additional cost while casting to copy that spell when the casualty trigger resolves. Copying is not casting. The deck's unique engine is powerful instants/sorceries amplified by expendable 2+ power bodies available BEFORE the important spell, often on opponents' turns. Core support: Jadar, Bloodghast, Lagomos, Poppet Stitcher, Ghoulish Procession, Cryptic Pursuit, Black Market Connections, Deekah/Talrand, Startle and Army of the Damned; payoffs include copied Dig/Big Score/Time Warp/X damage, Archmage Emeritus, Veyran, Ral, Twinning Staff and Thunderclap Drake. Existing fast/flexible mana, free interaction and tutors support a credible **Bracket 4 optimized** spell-copy/sacrifice power ceiling, with conditional repeatable Reiterate/Mana Geyser or Dualcaster/Twinflame closes; no sustained Bracket 5/cEDH results verified.
