## Commander-specific strategy and ceiling

Anhelo, the Painter costs UBR, 1/3 deathtouch, and gives the FIRST instant or sorcery spell we cast on EACH turn casualty 2. We may pay the additional cost of sacrificing a creature with power at least 2 as we cast that spell, generating a copy on the stack. This is a commander-led one-spell-per-turn value multiplier with repeatable 2-power creature fuel, not generic Grixis storm. Reusing Anhelo on opponent turns means having an instant, spare 2-power body and enough mana; it is not an unlimited spell-copying engine on our turn. Color-fixing, low-cost interaction and board presence must coexist.

Fodder engines include Jadar's end-step 2/2 decayed Zombie, Lagomos's own-combat 2/1 temporary elemental, Black Market Connections' 3/2 changeling, Talrand's 2/2 Drakes, Deekah's spell-mana-value-scaled Fractals, Poppet Stitcher's 2/2 decayed Zombies, Cryptic Pursuit's manifest 2/2s, Ghoulish Procession's delayed death-trigger decayed 2/2s, Bloodghast's 2/1 landfall recursion, and the huge tapped Zombies from Army of the Damned. Each has timing restrictions: Talrand/Deekah/Poppet/Cryptic generate bodies AFTER a spell is cast, too late to pay casualty for that same spell. Lagomos makes his token at the beginning of our combat, not before the precombat main phase or on each opponent's turn. Jadar fires at our end step, after our turn's common sorcery window. Cryptic Pursuit only triggers from hand casts, not copied spells. Procession requires one or more nontoken creatures to die, not just sacrificing decayed tokens.

Crackle with Power, Torment of Hailfire, Time Warp, Maestros Charm, Dig Through Time and explosive Big Score/Mana Geyser produce exceptional value if copied on the right FIRST spell. Twinning Staff, Veyran, Archmage Emeritus, Ral, Galvanic Iteration, Thunderclap Drake and Kess reward copies or reuse; some are independent combo engines. Mana Geyser with Reiterate buyback and a damage/copy payoff can win with enough tapped enemy lands, subject to exact mana thresholds and priority; Dualcaster Mage/Twinflame produces repetitive token copies if correctly set up but is not reliant on Anhelo's commander identity. Those are legitimate ceiling tools, not a reason to replace sacrifice-and-cast timing with a generic combo shell. Working natural ceiling: Bracket 4 optimized spells/treasures/creature-fodder with conditional Bracket 5-flavored combos; bracket certification remains open.

## Mana and opportunity cost

34 ordinary lands including 3 of each Grixis basic and fast fetch/dual sources. Signets (Dimir/Izzet/Rakdos), three Talismans, Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Fellwar Stone, rituals and Treasure generators provide enough nominal acceleration for UBR commander and large X spells. Colors matter more than total: Anhelo asks UBR, Counterspell and Mana Drain UU, Reiterate requires RR plus potential buyback, and Torment needs BBB. Lands such as Xander's Lounge/Undercity Sewers enter tapped, other duals conditionally; Cavern-style tribal restriction absent. No opening-hand or on-turn colored-mana Monte Carlo performed, and transforming Primal Amulet is not an early land.

Big Score, Startle, Frantic Search and A Little Chat have non-obvious commander contributions beyond generic card quality. Startle creates a 2/2 decayed token while drawing, enabling FUTURE casualty; if it is the first spell and we already have a 2-power body, its casualty-copy produces additional tokens/cards. Big Score discards as COST, draws and creates Treasures on resolution, and casualty-copy can repay mana expenditure; do not assume treasure is available before paying the original spell. Frantic Search's untap up to three lands can fund interaction when its copied resolutions are sequenced. Army of the Damned is eight mana and makes 13 TAPPED 2/2 bodies; it is a massive next-turn casualty reserve, not an immediate untapped attack force. The presence of late bombs risks clunky hands if cheap 2-power fuel is absent.

## Stage A challenger tests, alternative cuts, and best argument against change

1. **Add Fable of the Mirror-Breaker / cut Ghoulish Procession** is a plausible independently conceived test, not a recommended swap. Fable gives an immediate 2/2 Goblin Shaman whose attack Treasures and later rummage/Reflection are useful; it is self-sufficient before casting big copied spells. But Procession directly replenishes a 2/2 Zombie from nontoken creature deaths across turns, including sacrificial Bloodghast or other eligible fodder; Fable is not repeatable immediate casualty supply and demands a multi-turn transform. Fable's generic value could obscure a commander-first loss. Alternative cuts Startle or Cryptic Pursuit are weaker because both directly supply 2/2s by casting useful spells; none is clearly defensible without real matching.
2. **Add Plumb the Forbidden / cut Army of the Damned** tests sacrifice-to-draw velocity and copy/magecraft triggers versus eight-mana high-ceiling 2/2-army payoff. Plumb consumes creature bodies as additional cost and copies once per creature sacrificed; Anhelo casualty 2, if added to it as first instant/sorcery, also wants a distinct 2-power creature (not double-count the same one for two sacrifices). It could starve the board rather than sustain it, and cuts a uniquely thematic large-token payoff. If offered, compare cutting a lower-efficiency spell instead; not an immediate recommendation.
3. **No change** preserves sources and exact spell-copy payoffs. The central risk is first-cast sequencing versus delayed token production, not an isolated weak card. Test a realistic turn where Anhelo is on board but no 2-power creature exists; casualty does nothing. Extra 1/1 tokens from generic popular spellslinger cards are poor fuel for casualty 2. Generic dualcaster combo density cannot automatically substitute for reliable casualty bodies.

Candidate Fable is a two-to-three turn tempo alternative, Plumb a card draw burst; neither automatically solves opponent-turn casualty or first-spell timing. Replacing interaction/rocks is particularly dangerous because Anhelo already requires three colors and protects a vulnerable 1/3 commander.

## Exact rules traces to defend or falsify

- Sacrifice for casualty is paid WHILE CASTING as an additional cost, not after the token-making triggers. If Anhelo supplies casualty 2 to our first instant/sorcery on an opponent's turn, a fresh 2/2 is required then. Copies of spells are generally NOT CAST and won't trigger an effect demanding "cast" unless text explicitly also says "copy".
- A Little Chat ALREADY has casualty 1. If it is the first instant/sorcery while Anhelo is controlled, it has both casualty 1 and casualty 2; separately paying each with its OWN legal sacrifice can generate two casualty-copy triggered abilities (before Twinning Staff effects), instead of simply upgrading casualty 1 to 2. Paying one cost does not pay the other. With Twinning Staff in play, each copy-creation effect adds one additional spell copy, subject to proper trigger/cost sequencing.
- Twinning Staff is a replacement effect increasing number of copies, not granting extra casualty costs and not casting the copies. Archmage Emeritus/Veyran/Ral care about casts OR copies under their actual text; Cryptic Pursuit's manifest trigger is for original HAND cast. Galvanic Iteration being cast before Time Warp may consume Anhelo's FIRST-spell casualty privilege on the small setup spell.
- Reiterate costs 1RR with buyback 3 = total six mana before reductions. With Mana Geyser on stack and enough TAPPED opponent lands to generate at least six red mana per copy, buyback Reiterate can be cast again repeatedly while original remains: six is mana-neutral but yields repeat cast/copy triggers, seven or more can grow red mana. This depends on exact battlefield, land taps and legal stack target, is interruptible, and is not a guarantee of win merely from two card names. Storm-Kiln Artist/discounts may change thresholds; calculation must include them, not assume.
- Thunderclap Drake Oracle erratum allows new targets for its commander-cast-count copies; its cost reduction only reduces generic costs. It is a 2/1 body itself qualifying once for casualty 2, but sacrificing it for its own activated copy effect removes it as a possible casualty creature.
- Time Warp copied twice on owner target gives two extra turns from resolved copies/original, but opponent counters/removal and the requirement to already have 2-power fodder are real. Any non-target or modal spell copy retains relevant spell characteristics and X chosen on cast, not a newly paid X.

## Opponents' practical counterplay

Kill or exile the only spare 2-power creature before the owner can cast a key spell, counter Anhelo before main-phase value turn, exile token makers, bounce/counter the copied X spell after casualty paid, stifle casualty triggered copy, or attack graveyard lines through Underworld Breach/Kess. One-for-one spot removal after a token has already been sacrificed cannot undo its casualty cost but might disrupt remaining engines. Anhelo's 1/3 deathtouch blocks some creatures yet does not create fodder itself. Table-dependent Mana Geyser has little mana if opponents' lands are untapped, and opponent interruption can neutralize the buyback chain.
