## Commander-first strategic verdict

Natural ceiling **Bracket 4 (Optimized), provisional**. This distinctive deck turns newly-entered evasive attackers, Ninjas arriving tapped and attacking, repeat disposable hasty attack tokens and double-strike hits into **5/5 flying Dragon Spirits**. Dragon Tempest, Terror of the Peaks and repeated combats convert the earned Dragon board into lethal threat without changing commanders into an unrelated Grixis combo shell. Preserve real UBR turn-three access with 33 lands (some conditional/tapped) and reserve colored mana for counterspells, repeated `{1}{R}` haste, ninjutsu and extra combats. Weaknesses are commander removal before damage, opposing flyers/instant interaction, artifact/enchantment suppression, creature wipe, and post-sweeper draw starvation. A token on the battlefield from a previous turn cannot trigger Goro merely by attacking again.

**Current relevant rules checked:** Goro fires once per player per damage event when at least one *this-turn-entered* creature deals that player combat damage; fresh ninjutsu and sneak creatures can qualify despite not being declared attackers, but Karlach-style attack triggers do not retroactively fire. A fresh double-striker can produce one Dragon on each separate first/regular damage event. Goro `{1}{R}` only grants haste to current bodies; new Dragons need later paid activation or Samut/Frostcliff Siege's continual haste or Dragon Tempest's own entering-flying haste trigger. For Breath of Fury, order Goro trigger to make receiver Dragon before Breath sacrifice/reattach; the Aura must end attached to a new legal creature, and a true extra attack still needs haste, a viable connector, and opponent failure to interrupt. Breath without legal recipient fails to grant untap/extra phase. Frostcliff Siege Jeskai yields only one draw per player per damage event, but Temur gives team +1/+0, trample and haste; **mode is chosen once at entering**. Bident draws independently for each successful connecting creature. Sources: [Wizards March of the Machine release notes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/march-of-the-machine-release-notes), [Wizards Tarkir: Dragonstorm release notes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/tarkir-dragonstorm-release-notes), [Scryfall Bident](https://scryfall.com/card/ths/42/bident-of-thassa), [Scryfall Bitterblossom](https://scryfall.com/card/mor/58/bitterblossom), [Breath ruling](https://mtg.wtf/card?q=%21Breath+of+Fury).
