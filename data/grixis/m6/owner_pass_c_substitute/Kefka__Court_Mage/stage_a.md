### Protected identity and realistic ceiling

Kefka is a five-mana Human Wizard in Grixis with an **enter-or-attack** symmetrical one-card discard followed by a draw for each *distinct card type discarded on that specific resolution*. His sorcery-speed `{8}` activated ability makes each opponent choose and sacrifice a permanent and transforms Kefka; the transformed face draws cards equal to life lost by opponents during your turn. The commander is not an unconditional wheel, not a deterministic discard lock, and not a free transformational combo: he needs five correctly colored mana and sustained access/haste/blinks or a long-game eight-mana activation.

This approved baseline rightly supports a **high-power/cEDH-speed hybrid**: mana acceleration and free interaction, Kefka's discard-value/ETB repetitions (Harmonic Prodigy, Gogo, Phantasmal Image, Phyrexian Metamorph, Molten Duplication, Saw in Half, Essence Flux, Displacer Kitten), discard payoffs (Waste Not; Tinybones, Bauble Burglar), haste/reanimation (Anger, Arena of Glory, Reanimate), and compact Thassa's Oracle + Demonic Consultation/Tainted Pact or Underworld Breach + Lion's Eye Diamond + Brain Freeze lines. The compact win packages are powerful but should **not crowd out real Kefka-specific value solely for an interchangeable generic shell**. Natural ceiling is competitive high power/possible Bracket 5 with commander-specific midrange resilience, but no gameplay measurement or bracket reclassification is asserted here.

### Whole-list structure and consequential dependencies

**Mana/curve.** The source has 28 land slots: nine fetchlands, rainbow access (Command Tower, City of Brass, Mana Confluence; Starting Town subject to its printed condition), typed duals, fast colorless accelerants (Ancient Tomb, City of Traitors), and Cavern of Souls for a Wizard commander. Fast starts draw on Mox Diamond/Chrome Mox/Mox Opal, Lotus Petal, Sol Ring, Arcane Signet/Fellwar/Talismans, Mana Vault/Grim Monolith, Spirit Guide and rituals (Dark/Cabal/Rite/Culling). Red/black/blue simultaneously plus two generic matters: Ancient Tomb/City of Traitors do not substitute for all three colored symbols; Lion's Eye Diamond requires discarding the hand and does not function as an ordinary no-cost reserve rock. With only 28 lands, colored-source and mulligan risk are consequential, although no numerical keep rate is claimed. Ad Nauseam has high-end hits and life-pressure exposure.

**Card access / win packages.** Demonic Tutor, Vampiric Tutor, Imperial Seal, Gamble, Diabolic Intent, Beseech the Mirror, Wishclaw Talisman, Mystical Tutor and conditional Demonic Counsel support combo access; Rhystic Study, Mystic Remora, Necropotence, Ad Nauseam and Kefka refill. Consult/Pact + Oracle needs the exact resolution and correct mana; singleton supports Tainted Pact's distinct-name condition. Breach/LED/Brain Freeze needs actual escape fuel and graveyard availability; it loses to graveyard hate, countermagic and certain timing windows, not automatically a win upon assembling three names.

**Protection/counterplay.** Force of Will/Negation, Fierce Guardianship, Pact of Negation, Flusterstorm, Swan Song, Pyroblast/REB, Deflecting Swat, Mental Misstep, Mindbreak Trap, An Offer, Chain of Vapor, Into the Flood Maw, Toxic Deluge offer meaningful coverage. The deck can still face pre-Kefka creature removal, stax against noncreature spell chains, graveyard hate, and removal/ability counters at the transform or ETB windows. Free commander-dependent protection requires the commander on battlefield; protection density should not be mistaken for immunity.

**Discard payoff tension.** Kefka forces its controller to discard too; Anger is intentionally useful when discarded **to the graveyard** and a Mountain is controlled (typed Badlands/Blood Crypt/Steam Vents/Volcanic Island qualify). Necropotence exiles cards discarded instead, obstructing Anger setup and some graveyard enablement. Waste Not triggers on opponents' discarded cards, not the controller's own card, while Kefka's draw counts distinct types across all players, not total discarded cards. A handless opponent does not provide a discard card or Waste Not reward. Transformation is mana-intensive; transformed Kefka's opponent-life-loss draw can be explosive but can also overdraw a short library and is easier to interact with than its static-looking wording suggests.

### Adversarial weak-slot examination and unranked challengers

1. **Demonic Counsel** is the clearest conditional slot. Its normal mode searches only for a Demon; this source has **no evident Demon card** in its 99 library slots. Until four card types reach your graveyard (delirium), it can spend `1B` and find nothing. Fetchland (land), spell (instant), tutor (sorcery), and sacrificial artifact or creature can establish delirium in real lines, but availability *by the turn an early tutor matters* is unmeasured. It is excellent **after** delirium (unrestricted two-mana search), so calling it dead in all states would be wrong.
2. **Culling the Weak** needs an actual creature to sacrifice. Sacrificing Ragavan or a disposable clone can accelerate Kefka/Oracle, but paying with Harmonic Prodigy, Orcish Bowmasters, Kefka or a scarce early creature can destroy the very engine it was accelerating; no target on battlefield makes it unusable. Compare against cuts only if an effect-aware cost/board test indicates real dead draws.
3. **Anger** is discard-driven haste and fits commander identity, but requires its own card in a graveyard plus a controlled Mountain. Graveyard hate and Necropotence replacement shut down common lines. Arena of Glory supplies an independent haste avenue; redundancy may still be purposeful. Do not cut it from superficial low-floor reading.
4. **Gogo, Mysterious Mime** and the clone/blink package are more expensive than compact wins. Nevertheless the named/mimic copying supports extra Kefka trigger access and identity. The beginning-of-combat copy does not ignore summoning sickness, and a copied legendary name exception must be checked before claiming an extra permanent Kefka. A KEEP is plausible despite slower curve.
5. **Grim Monolith/City of Traitors** are high-output but colorless/temporary resources. Their contribution to five-mana UBR Kefka must be evaluated as complete colored sequences rather than generic total mana.

**Strongest named challenger:** **Spellseeker** for the conditional Demonic Counsel slot. Its unconditional three-mana Wizard ETB finds a mana-value-2-or-less instant/sorcery: Demonic Consultation, Tainted Pact, Brain Freeze, Essence Flux, many interaction pieces or select rituals. Harmonic Prodigy can double its Wizard triggered ability, while Displacer Kitten and clone/bounce resources can reuse it; this produces additional commander-adjacent agency instead of generic tutor homogenization. Cost is one more mana and less card-type breadth, so this is a **hypothesis**, not a confirmed improvement.

**Other plausible tests:** Intuition for Counsel or a less reliable mana slot (instant-speed setup and graveyard-filling, but an opponent controls which of three cards reaches hand; a Breach/LED/Brain Freeze pile is **not** automatically deterministic if Breach is chosen to go to the graveyard); Roaming Throne for a slow support slot (doubles Wizard/Kefka triggers but costs four and has comparatively little immediate protection); Windfall for a discard payoff slot (helps Waste Not but refreshes every opponent's resources and can undercut Kefka attrition). Generic goodstuff and fast unrelated combo changes are not presumed improvements.

### Realistic timing, response windows and test definitions

- **Turn-2/3 tutor contrast:** compare Demonic Counsel with/without delirium versus Spellseeker while holding one blue/black source, fast acceleration, and a valid desired tutor target. Record the proportion of truly available 4-type graveyards from real source sequences rather than asserting a number.
- **Turn-2/4 Kefka sequence:** accelerants must pay `2UBR` with exact colors; trigger can be countered (e.g., Stifle) or the creature removed in response, but removal after the ETB trigger is on the stack does not itself remove the trigger. Each player chooses/discards during resolution; drawing occurs later within that resolution, for card types actually discarded.
- **Wizard amplification:** Harmonic Prodigy gives an additional Kefka triggered event (each resolving separately), rather than doubling card counts from one event; opponents depleted by the first event may not discard in the second. Spellseeker as a Wizard is eligible; copied/temporary objects require layer, timing and legend checks.
- **Flicker/clone:** blinked Kefka is a new object and re-enters triggering once (or twice with Prodigy). Phantasmal Image or Metamorph copying the legendary Kefka normally collides with the legend rule, yet relevant enters triggers may still be created; do not count two persistent legendary originals. Confirm precise Gogo, Molten Duplication and Saw in Half copy exceptions before relying on more.
- **Escape/Oracle route:** Trace each cast, exile cost, LED timing, graveyard count, enemy graveyard hate, Oracle trigger interaction and Pact/Consult risks. No loop or win-rate was simulated in this Stage A.
- **Control/attrition test:** against an early Rule of Law/Drannith-type stax piece or graveyard shutdown, compare whether Spellseeker's creature body and flexible spell access make Kefka more functional than Counsel's unrestricted delirium potential. Against instant creature removal, the ETB trigger may still give value, but a counterspell on Spellseeker prevents it.

### Strongest counterargument to the favored change

A fast deck deliberately producing land, instant, sorcery and artifact/creature graveyard types could activate delirium surprisingly early (fetchland + Lotus Petal + tutor + instant, for example). Once active, Demonic Counsel costs **two rather than three**, retrieves **any** card—including the non-instant/sorcery win permanent Thassa's Oracle—and is not a creature spell vulnerable to creature-only counterplay. Spellseeker is slower, vulnerable to creature-specific hate, and cannot itself find Oracle, Ad Nauseam, a needed land or other permanent. Displacer Kitten/Prodigy synergies may be low-frequency win-more interactions. The unconditional tutor advantage must win enough realistic pre-delirium states to offset the higher cost and narrower target set.
