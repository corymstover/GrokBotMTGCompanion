## Corrected and checked interaction traces (not gameplay simulation)

1. **Counsel vs Spellseeker, resource state R1:** The own graveyard holds a fetch land, an instant and a sorcery, no fourth type; opponent may have stopped creature deployment. With `1B`, Demonic Counsel only searches Demon and the approved library has no Demon, hence no usable find. Spellseeker needs `2U` not `1B` and only finds an instant/sorcery MV<=2; if three correct mana is available, it finds Consultation, Tainted Pact or Brain Freeze without grave condition. This is an effect-and-cost fact, *not a frequency estimate*.
2. **Resource state R2:** Add sacrificed Lotus Petal/artifact as fourth grave type. Counsel now searches **any** card for `1B` including Thassa's Oracle; Spellseeker still needs `2U` and cannot find a creature. Counsel wins this specific tutoring comparison. Necropotence does not invalidate a type already in grave, but self-discard under its replacement can fail to add expected graveyard fuel.
3. **Synergy state R3:** Spellseeker enters while Harmonic Prodigy (another Wizard you control) is present; the Wizard ETB ability triggers an additional time, allowing two separate legal searches, possibly Consultation and a protection instant. Both triggers can resolve if Spellseeker is removed after entering; countering the creature spell before entering prevents them. But the combined setup already involves a 2MV support creature, 3MV tutor and blue mana plus next-spell costs; not an early automatic win.
4. **Gogo correction:** `{3}{R}` Gogo's beginning-of-combat trigger targets controlled Kefka; on successful resolution Gogo copies Kefka except its name and gives both creatures **+2/+0, haste, and an attack-if-able requirement**. If both attack, two separate attack triggers; no Gogo ETB trigger arises by becoming a copy. This is why cutting Gogo for generic Intuition carries a real commander-specific cost.
5. **Opponent disruption:** Graveyard hate can suppress Counsel delirium and Breach simultaneously; an uncountered Spellseeker remains useful in those states but sorcery-speed creature development may fail against a counterspell or Rule of Law constraint. Against tutor-limiting opponents, Counsel once delirium is live may search directly for a noninstant/non-sorcery permanent unlike Spellseeker.
6. **Consult/Oracle and Breach:** Correct casting, zones, colored mana and stack priority matter. With Oracle ETB on stack a paid Consultation/Pact may empty the singleton library; a countered Oracle trigger can lose the game. Breach escapes need payment of printed spell costs plus three other grave cards each, and a fueled Brain Freeze storm; no zero-fuel infinite assertion. Displacer Kitten blink and Harmonic repeatability are separate interaction hypotheses, not measured benefit.
