## Genuine agreements and disagreements

**Major agreement:** Kefka cEDH high ceiling with a real 5-mana commander-specific discard/draw engine, Gogo haste/copy, Harmonic Prodigy and Anger, plus Oracle/Breach compact finishes. 28-land turbo must be evaluated by actual U+B+R payment, not land counts alone. An 8-generic activation is a late fallback, and front trigger draws depend on the **union of distinct card types**, not card count. Prior reviews correctly observed that `Demonic Counsel` without delirium can't access a useful Demon in this exact list; Stage A agrees.

**Actual disagreement:** Stage A asks whether sacrificing the late unconditional `Demonic Counsel` for one-mana `Entomb` makes Anger consistently usable *before* the 5-mana Kefka entry/attack; earlier author chose KEEP Counsel and instead framed early delirium through `Dragon's Rage Channeler`. An Entomb in hand can place Anger in GY and support haste when a Mountain subtype land is already controlled, but the more generic tutor into hand is entirely lost. Neither side has measured paired opening-hand and opponent interaction trials. Grok's low-confidence `Intuition/Gogo` rejected swap also preserves commander identity; Entomb doesn't supplant Gogo.

**Novel potential nonbo to consider:** `Tinybones, Bauble Burglar` removes opponent discarded cards from their graveyards into stash-counter exile, reducing what `Mnemonic Betrayal` can later reuse from *graveyards*. This is not a rules blocker: Tinybones grants its own later mana-paid playable cards during your turn, and the original Kefka discard-type draw still counts cards discarded when it resolves. But its payoffs and Betrayal must not be double-counted on the same discarded cards. `Waste Not` triggers also remain valid from opponents' discards even when Tinybones later exiles them.

**Authored evidence clarifies copy rules:** Saw in Half on *a destroyed and actually dying* Kefka makes two DFC legendary copies that both trigger ETB and then require a legend choice before triggers resolve; Gogo copying Kefka retains name Gogo, can coexist, grants haste but no new ETB; a single-faced Phantasmal Image / Metamorph copying front-face Kefka cannot transform just because Kefka has a back face. Transforming DFC tokens themselves may be transformable under post-March of the Machine rules. Stage A had not asserted a single-faced clone could transform. No current Oracle error requiring an emergency canonical change found.

## Four source-paid scenario tests, analytical only

**A — Entomb haste works:** With a Badlands (Mountain), Kefka in command zone, B for Entomb plus otherwise complete 2UBR+five-mana cast access, put Anger from library to grave with Entomb, cast Kefka 2UBR, ETB triggers, then declare attacker in combat; grave Anger plus Mountain grant haste. No special ability from Kefka's discards is needed to find Anger. If all red lands are only `Arena of Glory`, `Luxury Suite`, `City of Brass` or `Mana Confluence` with no Mountain subtype, Entomb->Anger grants no haste; you must not count it as a guaranteed attack.

**B — Counsel premature floor fails:** With own grave only a fetchland (land) and Dark Ritual (instant), plus Chrome Mox imprint in **exile**, Demonic Counsel costs 1B and delirium is false. It can only search a Demon card; this exact 99 has no Demon. It can legally fail to find one / shuffle but doesn't tutor desired Breach or Oracle. `Entomb` instead finds Anger or another grave resource immediately, though neither tutor puts a winning Oracle card in hand in this state.

**C — Counsel late ceiling:** With own grave containing land, creature, instant and sorcery, Counsel is a 1B unrestricted hand tutor for final Consultation / protection / Breach piece. Entomb can find grave cards, not place any named desired win spell in hand, and grave hate can strand chosen targets. Cutting Counsel here is a decisive loss even if Entomb improved earlier Kefka attacks. A matched trial must count both states rather than calling a dead floor permanent.

**D — opposed to Gogo cut:** Control Gogo and Kefka before combat, Gogo's beginning-of-combat trigger resolves copying Kefka except for Gogo's name, both gain haste +2 and are compelled to attack when able; declaring both attackers puts two Kefka-pattern triggers (four with an already-existing Harmonic Prodigy) on stack, each causing one *separate* discard round and distinct-type draw. Copying doesn't make a new enter trigger. Replacing Gogo with generic Intuition loses those combat opportunities, but Gogo's 4 mana cast plus 5 mana Kefka and removal exposure are serious. Actual outcome and timing matter more than 2-to-1 card text comparisons.

These are reproducible card-text/stack cases, NOT measured games, an effect-aware Phase B certification, exact 100-card alternate Oracle gate, or statistical win-rate improvements.
