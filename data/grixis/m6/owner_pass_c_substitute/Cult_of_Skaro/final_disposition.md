## Commander identity and rules-critical distinctions

Cult of Skaro is a 4MV 4/4 Grixis legendary artifact Dalek. Each time **Cult is declared as attacker**, its single trigger randomly takes one of four modes: Thay gives each controlled artifact creature a +1/+1 counter; Caan draws two; Sec creates a 3/3 black artifact Dalek with menace; Jast makes **each opponent lose four life**. Connection is unnecessary for the attack trigger. It has no intrinsic haste, and a newly created myriad token *already attacking* did not attack for purposes of this trigger. Its viable **Bracket 4 — Optimized** identity is Dalek/Cyberman artifact board development, multiple legal Cult copies, repeatedly declared attacks, and additional combats—not generic Grixis cEDH homogenization. Present Archidekt themes **Artifacts / Extra Combats / Copies** stay intact.

**Critical verified rule:** 603.3c requires the random modal Cult outcome to be chosen when the attack trigger is placed on the stack. **Strionic Resonator** and **Scientist Supreme of A.I.M.** copying that object retain the already chosen mode; **Roaming Throne** choosing Dalek creates a second actual attack trigger with its own independent mode roll. The GrokBot advisory wrongly alleged Resonator re-rolled; the previous reconciliation had **already fixed** this error. The current Stage A independently agrees. This is a rules finding, not a newly discovered permanent blocker. WotC Doctor Who notes confirm Auton Soldier becomes a **nonlegendary** copy with myriad, but its newly entered, already-attacking myriad token copies do not produce fresh `whenever attacks` triggers; The Master, Multiplied can preserve creature tokens after end of combat. Helm of the Host makes a nonlegendary haste token before attack declaration, creating a real additional Cult trigger if actually declared. Nanogene Conversion before declaration can turn other friendly and hostile creatures into nonlegendary Cult copies; prior declared attackers cannot gain retroactive attack triggers.

34 lands include colorless/utility sources and artifact lands vulnerable to sweepers. UBR pips and double blue/extra red costs create tangible deployment pressure, despite rocks and artifact reducers. Repeated combats need a surviving and ready Cult, and Sword of Feast and Famine untap requires actual combat damage to a player. **Full Throttle** and **Great Train Heist** do not create intervening main phases; **Aggravated Assault** does, but its 3RR activation is sorcery speed and must be repaid absent sufficient Sword-funded land untaps. None of this guarantees favorable random Cult modes.
