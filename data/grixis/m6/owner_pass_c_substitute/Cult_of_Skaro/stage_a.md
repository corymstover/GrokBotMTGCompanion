## Distinct commander and natural ceiling

**Cult of Skaro `{1}{U}{B}{R}`**, 4/4 legendary artifact creature — Dalek. **When it is declared as an attacker**, it chooses **one of four modes at random**: Thay puts a +1/+1 counter on each artifact creature you control, Caan draws two cards, Sec creates a **3/3 black Dalek artifact creature token with menace**, or Jast causes **each opponent to lose four life**. It does **not** guarantee each payoff, does not trigger merely because a creature is put onto the battlefield tapped and attacking, and does not need to connect for the genuine declaration-of-attack trigger. There is no built-in haste. Expected single-trigger exposure is one 1-in-4 selection for each named mode, not a deterministic draw-or-token every attack. Its true design is **multiplying and exploiting varied attack triggers across a Dalek/artifact-tribal board**, and creating repeated combat steps—not generic Grixis spell combo.

Core native packages are Dalek Drone, Dalek Squadron, Davros, Dalek Creator, The Dalek Emperor, Genesis of the Daleks, Exterminate!, the Cult itself, and copy/attack engines. The deck also deliberately uses Doctor Who Cybermen and artifact-copy synergies: Cybermen Squadron, Cyberman Patrol, The Cyber-Controller, Missy, Ashad, the Lone Cyberman, Auton Soldier, Sakashima of a Thousand Faces, Spark Double, Irenicus's Vile Duplication, Phyrexian Metamorph, Helm of the Host, Brudiclad, Nanogene Conversion and Roaming Throne. Steel Overseer/Master of Etherium/Adaptive Omnitool supply artifact scaling. **Provisional natural power ceiling is Bracket 4 Optimized**: potentially explosive with wide tokens/copies and extra combats, but conditional on attack survivability, several 4–7MV pieces, colored access and some random ability distribution, not inherently a consistent turn-two cEDH combo shell.

## Whole-list structural and weak-slot audit

**Mana and colors.** Thirty-four lands include UBR original duals/shocks, fetches, Command Tower, Exotic Orchard, Luxury Suite, Morphic Pool, Training Center, Spire of Industry and Xander's Lounge. Three of each basic provide nine stable basic slots; Academy Ruins, Inventors' Fair, Urza's Saga, Arena of Glory and artifact lands (Great Furnace, Seat of the Synod, Vault of Whispers) bring colorless utility or colored artifact-count benefits. Urza's Saga cannot serve as a stable long-term mana source beyond its chapters. Sol Ring, Mana Vault, Mox Opal (metalcraft conditional), Arcane/Dimir Signet, Fellwar Stone and three Talismans accelerate. Etherium Sculptor and Enthusiastic Mechanaut can discount artifact spells, including Cult while it is a spell, but not supply colored UBR pips directly. A four-mana UBR commander is more reachable than Crosis but heavy six-plus mana copy/board payoffs and three-color access need real opening-hand analysis; no numerical mulligan simulation has been run here.

**Cards and rebuild.** Baleful Strix, Thought Monitor, Research Thief, Thopter Spy Network, Mana Drain and commander Caan roll supply cards; Emry, Lurker of the Loch and Academy Ruins recur certain artifacts. Research Thief is a five-mana flash 3/3 flyer drawing only when an **artifact creature deals combat damage to a player**; high ceiling into Dalek menace but late, interaction-prone and draw conditional on damage. Big opposing board stalls make the commander roll helpful (draw two can bypass blockers) but unreliable at only one mode in four. Their Name Is Death, Delete, Chaos Warp, Cyber Conversion, Cyclonic Rift, Desynchronization, Doomsday Confluence and the premium free counter/removal suite defend an already developed board; a sweeper can still remove your artifact creatures.

**Combat setup/counterplay.** The list supports haste/evasion and more combats with Lightning Greaves, Swiftfoot Boots, Arena of Glory under its conditions, The Dalek Emperor's haste grant to *other Daleks*, Sword of Feast and Famine, Aggravated Assault, Great Train Heist and Full Throttle. Haste from Emperor is not inherent in Cult and requires the Emperor to exist, which itself has substantial cost even with affinity. Opponents can counter/remove the commander before attack declaration, remove Sword/Aggravated Assault on the stack or before a subsequent combat, sweep a wide artifact board, profit from blockers/fog, or deny red sources for extra-combat effects. Dalek-sized 3/3 tokens with menace still need two blockers, but are exposed to creature/artifact removal and may be awkward into larger blockers.

## Exact rules-sensitive engine checks

1. **A Cult attack trigger is modal.** Under CR 603.3c the random mode is selected/announced while placing that trigger on the stack. **Strionic Resonator copies the already-chosen mode**; it does not reroll the copied trigger. Thus copying a lucky Caan may draw two more, a Jast may drain each opponent four more, or an unfavorable/conditional mode repeats. **Roaming Throne** choosing Dalek causes Cult to trigger an **additional time**, rather than copying; the two triggered instances choose modes separately at random. These are mechanically different, and treating Resonator as an independent random roll is a potentially material mistake.
2. **Helm of the Host** produces a nonlegendary haste copy at the beginning of combat, so it may be declared attacking in that combat and trigger its own Cult attack mode. Spark Double/Irenicus's Duplication/Sakashima offer different nonlegendary or legend-rule permissions and generally cannot attack immediately without haste. The actual token-copy characteristics and current Oracle should be reconfirmed for each at final rules gate.
3. **Auton Soldier** can copy Cult **except it is not legendary**, becomes an artifact additionally and gains myriad (confirmed in Wizards Doctor Who notes). On *declared attack*, the Auton Cult copy triggers its Cult attack choice and myriad. The myriad tokens enter already attacking and **do not retroactively trigger** Cult's `whenever attacks` ability on their entry; if The Master, Multiplied prevents the controller's mandatory exile of myriad creature tokens at end of combat, surviving tokens may attack in later turns, where they can actually trigger. This is not immediate three extra Cult rolls in the turn of the first myriad attack.
4. **Nanogene Conversion** can target a controlled Cult, and each **other creature** becomes a nonlegendary Cult copy for the turn, including opponent creatures. If cast **before attackers are declared**, your copies declared as attackers can each trigger; casting after declaration creates no retroactive attacks. Also overwrites allied utility creature abilities for that turn, which may turn off Roaming Throne's extra-trigger effect or other setup; does not change controller. Sequencing and opponent responses matter.
5. **Aggravated Assault** adds a combat **followed by a main** after a sorcery-speed 3RR activation during a main phase. With Sword of Feast and Famine on a consistently connecting creature, Sword's land untap each combat can supply 3RR again if the lands produce at least that much incl two red, creating repeated attacks and Cult rolls until disruption. **Great Train Heist's** spree extra-combat mode is an instant used during the combat phase and **does not grant an extra main** (WotC OTJ notes); it is not interchangeable as a general Assault loop engine.

## Neutral challenger pool and competing actual cuts

**Primary challenger: +Kindred Discovery / −Research Thief.** Kindred Discovery `{3}{U}{U}` chooses *Dalek* when it enters and draws a card **whenever your Dalek enters or attacks**—no combat damage and no random Caan roll required. Cult itself triggers it upon attacking, as do new Dalek token entries and actual Dalek creatures/copies; this is strongly tied to building an army around Cult's identity and could help refill after interaction. Research Thief `{4}{U}` instead draws per **artifact creature connecting**; it covers a wider coalition of Cybermen, Necron/Thopter/artifact copies and provides a 3/3 flying artifact body with flash, and it only requires a single blue pip. Discovery costs double blue, contributes no artifact count, and creates no board body; Dalek density alone might not match Research Thief in mixed-artifact draws. The exact copy identity of Biotransference-modified cards needs careful distinction: making a creature an artifact does not make it a Dalek. **Alternative cut: Thopter Spy Network**, another slower engine with recurring Thopters and artifact-combat draws, but removing it sacrifices repeated non-Dalek token generation.

**Secondary native challenger: +Reckless Fireweaver / −Canoptek Spyder** (or −Cyberman Patrol). Fireweaver `{1}{R}` deals one to *each opponent* whenever an artifact enters under your control: Dalek tokens, Cybermen/Thopters, copy tokens and artifact plays each become all-table drain, a direct native go-wide payoff. But Canoptek Spyder's combat and artifact presence could help attack-trigger access; Fireweaver is vulnerable 1/3 and doesn't itself increase attacking board width, Cult dice outcomes or mana. It could incentivize doing generic artifact ETBs instead of commander attacks; exact opponent/interruption cases matter.

**Third conditional challenger: +Mechanized Production / −Doomsday Confluence** (alternatives must preserve needed interaction). Production `{2}{U}{U}` can enchant a Dalek artifact token, add one each upkeep, and sometimes meet its eight-identical-name artifact alternate-win check. It is a vulnerable four-mana aura with an upkeep clock and requires appropriate artifact-name counts; many of the approved artifact creatures are *not* named Dalek, and duplicate commander copies retain names/legend constraints. Doomsday Confluence is flexible and cannot be labeled inferior without current Oracle and role review. This is a research hypothesis, not a proposal.

**Counterargument to primary:** Kindred Discovery on Dalek provides reliable ETB/attack draws and can dwarf a five-mana body, but turns all card access into a five-mana double-blue enchantment that cannot help stabilize the battlefield. Research Thief may flash into a protected attack on a wide **mixed artifact-creature** board and draw 3–5 from actual connecting bodies while being itself artifact creature for anthem/count synergies. If the deck's actual observed board is mostly Cybermen/Thopters rather than Daleks or its 34-land curve rarely supplies UU after its early spells, Kindred may be a regression. Conversely, assuming all Dalek combat will connect just to save Research Thief misses Discovery's key advantage: it triggers on attack declaration and token entry regardless of blocked combat.
