## Exact rules correction and independent agreements

- **Cult modal attack trigger:** under official CR 603.3c, the random mode is chosen/announced as the modal triggered ability is put onto the stack. A copy of that triggered object retains its selected mode. Thus **Strionic Resonator** and **Scientist Supreme of A.I.M.** repeat the same selected Thay/Caan/Sec/Jast mode, **not another random choice**; the GrokBot advisory's contrary fact is wrong. **Roaming Throne**, choosing Dalek, causes another actual Cult trigger, whose new mode is independently selected. The earlier reconciliation already corrected this, so do not count a previously resolved error as a brand-new one.
- **Actual attacks versus enters tapped/attacking:** Auton Soldier may nonlegendary-copy Cult with myriad, but the immediate myriad tokens that enter attacking do not newly trigger Cult. The Master, Multiplied may keep myriad creature tokens after combat so they can actually attack later. Helm of the Host creates a nonlegendary hasty token before attack declaration, which can attack and trigger that combat. Nanogene Conversion can create many Cult copies of *all other creatures*, including opponents', and must be resolved before attackers are declared for your copies to receive fresh attack triggers. These agree with prior deep/reconciliation mechanics and were independently neutral-reviewed.
- **Extra combats:** Full Throttle gives two extra combats, no extra main phases; Great Train Heist's paid extra combat mode gives no extra main phase. Aggravated Assault uses 3RR at sorcery timing, with additional main and combat; Sword of Feast and Famine must CONNECT to untap lands on each iteration. Karlach's extra combat from the first attacked combat does not require dealing damage, but needs a declared attacker, untapping and live combatants. Neither Karlach nor Kindred Discovery turns the four random modes into deterministic Jast, though each adds more *opportunities* for them.

### Explicit adversarial sample traces (rules deductions; no simulation receipts)

- **Early stabilized board:** cult attacks with Roaming Throne (Dalek chosen). Two separate abilities randomly choose modes on stack; e.g. Thay+Sec grows the army and creates another Dalek. With Strionic Resonator targeting first Thay, the copy is also Thay, **not** a reroll for Caan/Jast. Extra triggers can be high impact but are not controllable mode selection.
- **ETB swarm before damage:** with Kindred Discovery naming Dalek, each Dalek entering and each Dalek declared attacker causes a card draw before any damage. Three non-Dalek Cybermen attacks under the same enchantment cause *no* draws, while Research Thief would draw on each of those three only if they dealt combat damage with an artifact-creature identity and Thief remained. This is a meaningful role substitution; do not compare single best-case Dalek attack to worst-case Thief by cherry-picking board state.
- **Five mana Karlach turn:** Karlach on battlefield, one existing ready Cult, and Cult declared attack: Cult gets random trigger and Karlach grants another combat with untapped attackers; if Cult is removed in response to first stack or before next combat, only the first Cult trigger survives. Biotransference would have converted spells to artifacts and rewarded subsequent casts with 2/2 Necron tokens; an exact opportunity-cost comparison must account for those lost tokens/artifact-count advantages.
- **Board wipe:** Research Thief being itself an artifact-creature can be swept away with the board, whereas Kindred Discovery survives normal creature wipes but has no creatures to draw from until rebuilding. Canoptek Spyder and Biotransference differ: one draws on nontoken ETBs and one creates tokens on cast, so the choice materially affects post-wipe recovery.
