## Distinct commander engine and protected natural ceiling

`Mishra, Eminent One` costs `{2}{U}{B}{R}`, 5/4, and **at the beginning of combat on your turn** creates a token copy of **target noncreature artifact you control**, except it is named **Mishra's Warform**, is a **4/4 Construct artifact creature** in addition to its other types, and gains haste until end of turn; you sacrifice it at the beginning of the next end step. The original noncreature artifact remains. This is fundamentally **not** the cast/self-counter ability of `Mishra, Artificer Prodigy`: the deck should repeatedly exploit Warform ETB and leave/death, copy artifacts with activated abilities before the end step, double/multiply tokens, and use sacrifice outlets before the delayed cleanup, without relying on changing artifact spells on the stack.

Protected packages:

1. **ETB and LTB copy value:** `Ichor Wellspring` draws on entering/graveyard death; `Cryogen Relic` draws on entering/leaving; `Prized Statue` generates Treasure from entering/dying; `Coveted Jewel` draws three on entering and can tap for three mana; `Lich's Relic` offers an ETB `{2}` payment for removal of up to one opposing creature/planeswalker per player; `The Mightstone and Weakstone` offers an ETB choice; `Portal to Phyrexia` forces each opponent to sacrifice three creatures on entering. These are not equivalent to copying a vanilla artifact.
2. **Consumable activated abilities:** `Mindslaver` can be copied as a hasty 4/4 Warform; paying `{4}`, tapping and sacrificing the copy lets the *real Mindslaver* remain for the next combat, potentially repeatedly controlling one opponent's next turn if protected and funded. `Gonti's Aether Heart` can produce energy on artifact entry and consume eight energy plus exile itself for an extra turn, but requires actual energy accrual and legend/token details; it is not a free extra-turn loop. `The One Ring` copy can draw via tap ability but its cast-only protection does **not** trigger when token enters; Warform token is sacrificial, while original Ring continues with its burden counters.
3. **Artifact token copy multipliers/recursion:** `Brudiclad, Telchor Engineer`, `Determined Iteration`, `Worldwalker Helm`, `Strionic Resonator`, `Lithoform Engine`, `Machine God's Effigy`, `Simulacrum Synthesizer`, `Repurposing Bay`, `Smelting Vat`, `Master Transmuter`, `Goblin Welder/Engineer`, `Emry`, `Krark-Clan Ironworks`, `Scrap Trawler`, `Myr Retriever`, `Esoteric Duplicator`, `Spine of Ish Sah`, `Arcum Dagsson`, and `Urza` convert copies/ETBs/deaths into advantage. `Worldwalker Helm`'s artifact-token replacement adds **a Map token**, not automatically a second Warform; distinguish Map value from same-copy multiplication. Multiple timing/sacrifice rules are key to whether a proposed card is better.

The natural power ceiling is plausibly an optimized, commander-dependent artifact-copy value/control and combo shell (roughly Bracket 4, potentially stronger under reusable Mindslaver/Portal turns), **not** a certificate for generic Bracket-5 artifact turbo. The commander costs five, needs untap-to-combat access, targets an existing noncreature artifact at the beginning of combat, and a removed target before resolution can stop that Warform completely. Preserve unusually rich copy targets over undifferentiated Grixis staples.

## Neutral adversarial candidates and exact cut alternatives

**Preferred first-pass challenger: `+Roaming Throne / -Strionic Resonator` — `NEEDS TESTING`.** `Roaming Throne` ({4}, artifact creature, ward {2}) chooses a creature type on entry; choose **Artificer** (or Human) to make another matching creature's triggered ability trigger an additional time. Mishra is an Artificer. Instead of activating Resonator for `{2},{T}` on each combat, Throne can make Mishra's Warform creation trigger **twice every combat** without additional activation mana once both permanents survive. Throne is an artifact, supports `Urza`/KCI and triggers certain other Artificer ETBs, but it is a *creature artifact* and cannot itself be targeted as Mishra's **noncreature** artifact source. Crucially this enhancement is conditional on choosing the appropriate type and controlling the commander; it does **not** copy any arbitrary triggered ability or double activated abilities.

**Strongest counterargument to Throne:** `Strionic Resonator` costs only `{2}` initially, copies **any target triggered ability you control** for `{2},{T}`, can copy individual high-impact *Warform ETB triggers* (not only Mishra), works in a broader set of boards without naming a creature type, and can be recurred as a noncreature artifact/copy target. Throne must be cast for four, protected as a creature, and consumes a slot otherwise able to support flexible trigger copying; late commander removal strands its main purpose. More Warforms copied from legendary source artifacts may invoke legend-rule cleanup because both token copies retain legendary and have the same **Mishra's Warform** name. This is **not** clearly a free doubling of two permanent legendary targets. Target validity and independent trigger ordering can also limit the value. No measured win rate or direct comparison has been produced.

**Alternative cut for the same challenger:** `Lithoform Engine` ({4} and activation costs) is another versatile but setup-heavy copying engine. Cutting it for Throne may preserve Resonator's cheaper flexibility but loses Engine's artifact, activated and spell-copy modes; source-paid trigger/activation tests required. `Worldwalker Helm` is more plainly a Map-generating token replacement and a second token-copy route, but cutting it may lose post-combat artifact count/value; do not conflate it with Throne. `Determined Iteration` remains a cheaper ({1}{R}) commander-forward populate at beginning of combat, especially when ordering Mishra's Warform creation before its populate trigger; it sacrifices its created token at the next end step. It deserves a different test than the four-mana engines.

**Off-ranking neutral alternatives:** `Panharmonicon` ({4} artifact) doubles ETBs of artifacts/creatures you control and could reward Cryogen/Coveted Jewel/Portal/Ichor, but does **not** double Mishra's beginning-of-combat triggered ability itself or activated Mindslaver, so it is not a straightforward Strionic replacement. `Mirrorworks` ({5}) can pay {2} to copy a **nontoken** entering artifact but typically does **not** trigger on Mishra's Warform token entry; less commander-forward than Throne. `Conjurer's Closet` ({5}) blinks a creature at end step, but copying Warform artifacts whose token departs can cease to exist and requires a proper exilable creature; no speculative “save all tokens” line accepted.

Other conditional cut-pressure: `Uthros Research Craft` wants station counters before draw; `Gonti's Aether Heart` needs repeated energy and an exile cost; `Simulacrum Synthesizer` checks cast/CMC artifact ETB conditions, and token copies do not count as cast; `Esoteric Duplicator` may be specific to **nontoken** artifacts leaving; `The One Ring` copied via Mishra is not “cast” so its ETB protection line is not repeatable. Each still has real synergy or utility, and cutting them solely on apparent mana value would be shallow.

## Exact rules/priority windows and opponent counterplay

1. **Single Warform turn:** before beginning combat, control Mishra and an untapped noncreature `Ichor Wellspring` (it need not be untapped for targeting); Mishra's trigger targets the Wellspring. On resolution it creates 4/4 Warform copy; token ETBs and draws one card. At beginning of the next end step delayed sacrifice moves the token to graveyard briefly and it ceases to exist; Wellspring's graveyard-from-battlefield trigger draws another card. If target Wellspring was destroyed/exiled before Mishra's trigger resolved and no legal target remains, no Warform is created. If token is sacrificed to KCI or Arcum before end step, that earlier outlet sets the time of its death/exit but does not create a second token.
2. **Cryogen/Lich distinction:** `Cryogen Relic` copy draws on both enter and leave (leaving need not specifically be dying); `Lich's Relic` Warform ETB may pay {2} and, if legal choices exist, destroy up to one target creature/planeswalker per opponent. Triggered removal is not free, does not bypass target restrictions and can be responded to before resolution. These are commander-specific repeated effects, not mere artifact count.
3. **Mindslaver copy:** Mishra Warform copy gains haste and retains Mindslaver's `{4},{T},Sacrifice` ability. It can use the copy on the same combat/turn with four unrestricted mana, exiling itself if required by any cost? Mindslaver's own printed cost is sacrifice (not exile), so the token is sacrificed and ceases to exist. The source Mindslaver remains. Opponent can respond before the controlled-turn ability resolves or remove the original to stop later copies. No free infinite-turn claim.
4. **Brudiclad sequencing and legend:** at beginning combat, controller has Mishra and Brudiclad triggers and may order them. Resolve Mishra first to put a Warform on field; Brudiclad may then transform other tokens into copies of a chosen token. Copy effects on previously existing tokens are not the same object as the specific token referenced by Mishra's delayed sacrifice instruction, so do not automatically sacrifice *all* tokens turned into Warforms. However if the Warform retains `legendary` from a copied legendary artifact, multiple legendary Warforms with the same name cannot all survive under one controller; test actual copies and state-based actions. Tokens cloned as nonlegendary creatures need not collapse.
5. **Throne versus Resonator:** with Throne choosing Artificer, Mishra's combat trigger can happen twice and each trigger selects a legal noncreature artifact target; if that artifact disappears, that trigger may fail independently. With Resonator, Mishra triggers once, then you can pay {2} and tap Resonator to copy the *triggered ability* and choose new targets; the copy resolves separately. If you have insufficient open mana or Resonator is tapped, no second trigger. With Throne, if opponent removes the commander before the beginning of combat, nothing triggers, and if they remove Throne before combat, no doubling; controlling both is a real two-permanent prerequisite.
6. **Interaction windows:** targeted artifact hate before the Mishra trigger resolves, countering Mishra before combat, instant-speed creature removal, graveyard exile against Welder/Emry, KCI removal before sacrifice, and artifact-wide sweepers punish the engine. A stolen `Coveted Jewel` or copied legendary Mindslaver can have controller/legend-rule wrinkles.
