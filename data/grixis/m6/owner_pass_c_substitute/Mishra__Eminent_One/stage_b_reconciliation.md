## Commander-first rules red team after comparison

1. **Mishra's cast/timing:** five-mana UBR commander does nothing until beginning of combat with a legal noncreature artifact target. Target removal before trigger resolution stops that Warform; both the original and substitute cannot be credited with instant value on casting Mishra alone. Only the token-copy creation and downstream ETBs count; no artifact spell is cast to make Warform.
2. **Roaming Throne trigger rather than copy:** Throne choosing Artificer causes Mishra's triggered ability to trigger an additional time each combat if the commander and Throne survive to that event. Each is a separately triggered object, with its own legal target and independent resolution; it does **not** require `{2}` mana every combat. It does not automatically double Ichor Wellspring ETB/dies triggers (a token Warform is a Construct, not necessarily an Artificer), unless the resulting token has an appropriate creature type/interaction independently. The chosen type matters.
3. **Strionic Resonator broader effect:** `{2},{T}` copies any owned triggered ability on stack, including the Mishra combat trigger and a *single* important Warform ETB, `Gonti's Aether Heart` energy gain, `Portal to Phyrexia` sacrifice trigger, or `Coveted Jewel` draw. It can copy a trigger **even without Mishra in play**. A Warform created as a copy of a noncreature Strionic Resonator is an artifact creature with haste and the artifact's activation, but it arrives only after the original Mishra combat trigger resolves; copying *that same resolved trigger* is no longer possible. It could copy downstream ETB triggers if costs/priority are available. Throne is already an artifact creature, so it is **not** a legal Mishra noncreature artifact source target.
4. **Mana-turn tradeoff:** if Resonator is cast on turn two, Mishra on five with five mana available, the ability-copy still needs two additional open mana to double the first combat. Throne cast for four on a previous turn can allow two Warforms at that first protected combat for the five-mana commander turn without paying two more. But Throne's initial four-mana slot, ward/creature vulnerability and correct chosen type are true development costs; these are *illustrative payable-state differences*, not simulations. Disruption before combat makes both fail as commander-doublers, while Resonator retains independent ETB-copy utility.
5. **Warform legend and cleanup:** token copy of a legendary noncreature artifact has a copied legendary supertype but special **same name `Mishra's Warform`**. Two such legendary tokens under one controller collide under the legend rule even if their original noncreature artifact source names differed. Their ETB triggers can still occur in the relevant resolution/state-based sequence; do not claim both persist as separate legendary permanents. Nonlegendary Warform tokens can coexist. Brudiclad's change of other tokens into Warform copies does not create Mishra's delayed-sacrifice effect on all those unrelated objects; source-of-copy legend interactions still matter. The printed original legendary artifact has a different name from Warform and generally can remain.
6. **Cryogen/Lich/Prized payoff:** Cryogen leaves even if not dying to grave; Ichor draws death when from battlefield to grave; Lich's Relic requires {2} on ETB and correct opponent targets; Prized Statue value depends on real Treasure creation. Compare duplicated **artifact value per combat**, not only number of tokens. The original Oct19 author manually showed Panharmonicon versus Determined Iteration Wellspring lines but no Roaming Throne cost/resilience line.
7. **Core native alternative win/rules**: repeat Mindslaver token uses `{4},{T},sacrifice`, not Workshop's artifact-spell-only CCC, and needs original source retained/target legal. Heart extra turns require eight energy and token legend cleanup; Helm/Urza/Thousand-Year Elixir engine requires actually paid `{1}{U}` costs, tap permission and board presence. Throne may double token creation but does not by itself pay those costs or produce deterministic extra turns.
