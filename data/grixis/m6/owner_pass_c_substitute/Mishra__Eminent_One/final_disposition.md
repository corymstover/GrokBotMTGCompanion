## Material mechanics and counterplay

1. **Trigger windows:** If the targeted original noncreature artifact is removed before Mishra's triggered ability resolves, no token appears for that trigger. Removing Mishra before beginning of combat prevents it triggering; removing Mishra after its already-triggered ability is on stack does not retroactively erase that trigger. Removing Throne before combat stops extra trigger; removing it after two triggers have occurred ordinarily doesn't remove those existing triggers. Stack/priority matter.
2. **Paid token uses:** A copied `Mindslaver` token gets haste; pay **{4}, tap and sacrifice that token** to control target player's next turn while preserving original. `Mishra's Workshop` restricted mana cannot pay Mindslaver's activated cost or cast Mishra. A copied `Coveted Jewel` ETBs to draw three and can tap for three mana if legally untapped/has haste; its theft ability remains a risk. `Lich's Relic` copies require **{2}** to choose and destroy legal opposing creatures/planeswalkers. `The One Ring` copied without casting does not yield its cast-only protection.
3. **Death/legend:** A Warform copied from a legendary source retains legendary but is specially named **Mishra's Warform**; two legends of that name under one player clash even if source artifacts were differently named. ETB triggers may still go on stack after legend-rule state-based actions; they do not make two legendary Warforms permanent. Tokens sacrificed/leaving stop existing as tokens after the zone change, and the delayed sacrifice belongs to the specific created token, not arbitrary copies of it created later by Brudiclad.
4. **Opponent interaction:** Destroy/exile copy target in response to Mishra, remove commander/Throne, stop Arcum/Welder activation, graveyard exile, Null Rod/Collector Ouphe on artifact activations, overloaded Vandalblast against many artifact permanents, and holding up counterspells all alter actual output. No “two free Portals” guarantee exists if the source Portal is not available or protected by the combat trigger.
