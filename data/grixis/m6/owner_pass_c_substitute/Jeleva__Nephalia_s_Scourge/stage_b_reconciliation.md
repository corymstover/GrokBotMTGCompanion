## Disagreements, corrections and commander-first weight

- **GrokBot's critical timing premise is false.** It described the Long-Term Plans card at position three as waiting for draws/Top/Rack before use. Jeleva's **cast-paid four-card ETB exile captures positions one through four immediately**; no two draws or added rearrangement are required. The reconciliation correctly caught this; Stage A also independently described the top-three hit, although that does not prove prior knowledge had no influence. Treat the claimed delay as a *substantive mistaken rationale*, not evidence that `Long-Term Plans` is weak.
- **Exact Intuition swap not approved as superior.** Intuition for 2U places only one opponent-chosen card from three in hand, two in graveyard, and shuffles. Its Breach/LED/Brain Freeze pile can be useful, but does not itself grant cast permission, sufficient mana or grave protection; it can erase a staged Jeleva top library. Long-Term Plans at 2U targets *any* instant/sorcery from deck to Jeleva's impending exile window, providing a qualitatively different native payoff. The most defensible final position is **preserve Long-Term Plans unless narrowly targeted comparison proves better elsewhere**.
- **ChatGPT's Galvanic Iteration line merits bounded attention but is not a free doubling engine.** For UR, Galvanic can copy the NEXT instant/sorcery cast this turn, including the spell **cast** by Jeleva's attack trigger; spell copies themselves are **not cast** and do not increase storm count. Its usefulness varies by exiled spell: copies of `Peer into the Abyss`/some removal can be impactful; second `Mnemonic Betrayal` may be almost redundant because the first effect emptied graveyards, a copy of `Yawgmoth's Will` duplicates a static permission effect, a second `Doomsday` may cost additional life and damage the selected pile, and copying `Mind's Desire` adds an ordinary copy, not an extra storm trigger. It also requires UR available before a successful attack, exposes a response window to removal, and replaces `Borne Upon a Wind`'s independent ability to cast normal spells on otherwise illegal timing. Original A–T traces are reasoned cases, **not proof of improved wins**.
- **Other alternatives need honest costs.** `-Scheming Symmetry` risks a rival's tutored card, yet for B it places a Jeleva free-cast target at the top and can expose an opponent's tutored instant/sorcery to Jeleva's ETB; cutting it would weaken a native plan. `-Mind's Desire` reduces in-hand Ad Naus risk but removes a marquee free-hit payoff. `-Fellwar Stone` creates visible color and recast risk in 27 lands. `-Torch Courier` denies cheap same-turn attack; `-Harmonic Prodigy` discards double ETB and double attack triggers. No cleanly dominated proposed cut was demonstrated.

## Mechanically bounded test sketches (not measured Phase B)

**S1 — top-three line (favors baseline in its intended window).** Cast Long-Term Plans for 2U before Jeleva; it shuffles the deck, puts `Peer into the Abyss` third. If no intervening draw, shuffle, milling or replacement changes top order, cast Jeleva with actual 1UBR spent, X=4: third card **is exiled**. With Lightning Greaves already present/equipped or Courier sacrificed, declare Jeleva attacking: may legally cast Peer for no mana at attack trigger resolution, with opponent interaction windows. `Intuition` cannot directly reproduce this staged-exile outcome.

**S2 — Intuition combo package (conditionally favors challenger).** Cast Intuition 2U to reveal Underworld Breach, Lion's Eye Diamond and Brain Freeze. An opponent selects ONE to hand and puts the other two into grave; you must assemble sufficient mana, cast permission/recursion for missing pieces, enough other graveyard cards for escape, and protection from hate. None of these outcomes grants all three cards for free; `Long-Term Plans` isn't an equivalent graveyard pile assembler. Test with multiple hostile opponent choices, not a friendly choice.

**S3 — Galvanic with Jeleva spell (conditional).** Cast Galvanic Iteration UR precombat; Jeleva attacks and casts free `Mind's Desire`: Desire is CAST, triggering its own storm and Galvanic delayed trigger; Galvanic copy is not CAST and therefore does not add an additional storm trigger. The turn's earlier spells, stack order, and legal targets determine actual exiled-card gain. On a free `Mnemonic Betrayal`, copying it may produce little extra if graveyards were already emptied. No generic claim of twice the final payoff is valid.

**S4 — stax/punishment (baseline vulnerability not solved by a swap).** If Drannith Magistrate or an applicable Rule of Law restriction stops casting from exile, neither a perfect Long-Term Plans topdeck nor extra Galvanic value makes Jeleva's attack cast work. If an opponent removes Jeleva before she declares attack, no attack trigger. If after her attack trigger is on stack, resolution needs an exact-linked exiled spell and legal casting permission; do not describe removal as retroactively canceling a trigger. Grave hate can defeat Intuition/Breach more thoroughly than it defeats the direct Jeleva top-stage route.

Each trace is logical case analysis, not an effect-aware multi-game sample, Scryfall 100-card recheck or local-zero-improvement certificate. A genuine matched pilot-vs-disruption test is postponed to risk-prioritized Phase B following completion of all 62 Pass C reviews.
