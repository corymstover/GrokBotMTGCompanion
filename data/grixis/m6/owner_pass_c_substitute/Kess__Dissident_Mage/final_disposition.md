## Kess-first mechanical judgment

Kess `{1}{U}{B}{R}` allows one **paid, legally timed instant or sorcery cast from own grave on each own turn**, exiling that spell if it would return to grave. She does not give opponent-turn free counters or the permission to cast Breach directly from grave; other zone permissions such as escape do not consume Kess's allowance. A **new** Kess after Displacer Kitten blink may use another card from grave that turn. Kess's most distinctive combo: cast Demonic Consultation (or properly sequenced Tainted Pact) from hand to *find Thassa's Oracle*, then cast Oracle and replay the now-grave Consultation via Kess **in response to Oracle's ETB trigger**, emptying library before Oracle resolves. This requires real U/U, B then B, an unspent Kess permission and a legally resolving Oracle trigger. `Gifts Ungiven` is **officially legal** since April22 2025 and can search up to four names, two selected names guaranteeing grave placement when the opponent must put two there. `Intuition`, `Entomb`, Looting, DRC and Spellseeker reinforce native spells-in-grave play, not merely generic turbo.

**Priority 1 disputed exact add/cut (unapproved):** `+Jeska's Will / -Ledger Shredder` from the earlier reconciliation. Will {2}{R} with Kess controlled can choose two modes (red for target opponent's hand count, plus exile top three for this turn) and Kess can cast it again from grave later that same turn if mana/allowance permit. When opponents have 5–7 cards this can enable a large red burst and extra looks; with zero to two cards, no Kess, a countered spell, blocked target or blue/black bottleneck the real ceiling falls. Ledger Shredder 1U repeatedly connives when a player casts their second spell per turn, stocking grave and preserving a blue Force-pitch card, and may matter more in slow control tables. **Needs source-paid comparison; not an accepted replacement.**

**Priority 2 new first-look hypothesis (unapproved):** `+Frantic Search / -Personal Tutor`. Search 2U draws/discards two and untaps up to three actual *lands*, not the fast rocks used in 27-land turbo. Kess replays Search from grave once and exiles it; it improves filtering/grave fuel when three relevant lands are actually available, but Personal Tutor at U can select game-winning sorcery topdecks and be recast by Kess for U. Only open a swap if tested functional gains exceed loss of cheap tutor.
