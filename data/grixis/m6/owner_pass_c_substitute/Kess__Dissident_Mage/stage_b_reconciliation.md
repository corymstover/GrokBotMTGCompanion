## Corrections and significant agreements

**Agreed:** Kess 1UBR flies, recasts ONE paid instant/sorcery from own grave during EACH OWN TURN and exiles that particular spell if it would return to grave. Sorcery timing still applies; opponent-turn counters in grave do not magically recast. Spell cast via different zone permission (flashback/escape) doesn't consume Kess's once use, and Displacer Kitten blinking Kess creates a new object that can grant a fresh once allowance. Cheap graveyard loaders, Gifts/Intuition, and extra-turn spells create distinct cEDH value, and Gifts Ungiven is officially Commander-legal since Wizards April 22 2025 unban (not a project exception). Twenty-seven all-nonbasic lands and multiple fast artifact sources make colored Kess timing and Search land untap critical.

**Important native win missed in Stage A's explicit examples, not invalidating it:** The prior comprehensive review lays out a *double-consult* line: with Kess controlled and allowance unused, cast **Demonic Consultation** from hand naming `Thassa's Oracle` (if it finds Oracle, exile top six and reveal until found), cast Oracle from hand, then cast the **same Consultation from grave via Kess** in response to Oracle's ETB trigger, naming a card not in library to exile the remaining library. The Kess replay exiles Consultation itself when finished. Needs legal U/U for Oracle, two separate B consultation costs, the card actually found on first cast, correct opponent priority, and a resolving Oracle trigger. Analogous `Tainted Pact` needs singleton names (present), sufficient mana and correct search-stop sequencing; none is guaranteed by merely controlling Kess. This makes spell-specific tutor utility more valuable than generic grave draw counts.

**Authentic separate challenger:** `Jeska's Will / Ledger Shredder` is materially more serious than an arbitrary add. Jeska's Will for 2R is red mana equal to target opponent hand and/or exiles three library cards for turn, with both modes if Kess is controlled as cast. First cast from HAND then Kess re-cast from GRAVE in the SAME own turn is legal if the first spell resolved and landed in grave, allowance unused and enough mana remains. This can finance further instant/sorcery grave casts and find win pieces. It is nonetheless *not* unconditional mana or six free cards: opponent hand may be low; impulse exiles require paying costs, obeying timing and available land drops, and they expire turn-end; chosen opponent target going illegal may stop spell resolution. Sacrificing Ledger Shredder loses a 1U blue pitch card that connives on each second spell cast per turn (including opposing turns) and repeatedly seeds Kess's grave, valuable against grind/stax. The two-model PROPOSE UPGRADE is owner-pending, **not** accepted.

**Stage A Frantic Search challenge:** At 2U, it draws two/discards two and untaps up to THREE lands, with Kess recast once at payment/timing cost. The line is strong when lands actually pay the cast (especially a land producing more than1 mana), but far worse if 27-land turbo hands derive two of the three mana from rocks. `Personal Tutor` at U is a direct sorcery-to-library-top setup for Beseech, Demonic Tutor, Windfall/Wheel; Kess can replay that tutor. Thus Stage A's proposed swap is only a test-worthy hypothesis, not a priority over a source-paid Will vs Shredder dispute.

## Bounded decision cases (not measured testing)

**1. Source-paid Jeska high-hand case.** Kess controlled, your turn, opposing player holds 6 cards, with three mana {2}{R} to cast Will from hand (choose both). If it resolves and target still legal, produce 6R, exile 3 cards playable this turn at normal costs, then Will into grave. With Kess allowance unused, spend 3R to cast Will again from grave (choose both if Kess still controlled), gain a fresh number of R based on target's hand at second resolution and exile 3 more. Red mana may fund red parts, generic mana, or artifact activations but cannot substitute U/B; do not count this as an infinite loop or guaranteed combo.

**2. Opponent-controlled fragility.** Kess absent and opponent holds 1–2 cards, Will 2R is only 1–2R OR top-three impulse, usually not a net burst. Ledger Shredder played earlier for 1U can have connived on opponents' second spells to filter into Breach/Consult targets; loss of a blue pitch card weakens Force density. A countered Will cannot supply mana. Compare cards in actual grave and costs, not headline upside.

**3. Frantic Search rock-vs-land split.** Casting Frantic via three tapped lands can effectively refund three lands after draw2/discard2, making two Kess-paid cast opportunities materially better; paying two with Sol Ring/other artifacts and one U land untaps only the U land, meaning significant net mana cost. Kess from hand itself may consume lands/rocks before Frantic. Losing a U Personal Tutor can delay Beseech/Wheel unless other tutor draw. Need matched opening turns with Kess not already assumed deployed.

**4. Gifts/Consultation nuance.** Gifts may choose just two instant/sorcery pieces and guarantee those two into grave, but Kess grants only one cast per own turn and cannot cast Breach (enchantment) through that allowance; Breach escape has separate 3-other-card grave exile and mana. Consultation from hand to get Oracle, then Kess grave replay in response to Oracle ETB is a real commander-native compact finish, provided life/hand/mana/stack are legal. Exiling Consultation from Kess use prevents third reuse via a new Kess.
