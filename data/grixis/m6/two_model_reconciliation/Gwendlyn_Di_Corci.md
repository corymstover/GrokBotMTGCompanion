## Exact proposal: +Megrim / -Dream Salvage

**VERDICT: REJECT THIS EXACT CUT; KEEP DREAM SALVAGE.**

- **Megrim:** {2}{B}, black enchantment, Commander-legal, '**Whenever an opponent discards a card, this enchantment deals 2 damage to that player.**' It reacts **once per discarded card**, separately from Gwendlyn's one-card-at-random tap, each copy of her ability, Bottomless Pit, Oppression and discards due to actual wheel effects. On resolution after an opponent discards seven cards, seven triggers -> fourteen noncombat damage to that player (if they resolve and damage isn't prevented). Damage is NOT commander damage and is NOT identical to the life loss from on-list Liliana's Caress.
- **Dream Salvage:** {U/B} instant, color identity both U and B, legal. '**Draw cards equal to the number of cards target opponent discarded this turn.**' Targets **one opponent**, not each opponent, and the opponent's cumulative discard total **during that turn**, not just the most recent discard effect. The amount is measured **when Dream Salvage resolves**, so intervening discards before resolution count. After one Gwendlyn tap targeting an opponent who actually had a card, draws **one for a single hybrid mana**, functioning as a one-mana cantrip. Bracers/Stone/Pemmin can force multiple random discards from the same hand for 2–5 cheap draws. After a Wheel discarding seven from chosen opponent, it draws **seven cards for one hybrid mana**, an explosive, efficient way to refill after using the Gwendlyn discard/wheel engine.
- **Timing qualification:** This card cannot be cast for value **in response before Wheel of Fortune resolves** with an expectation of counting Wheel's discard unless that discard has actually occurred before Dream Salvage itself resolves. Correct sequence: cast Wheel; let Wheel completely resolve (discard + draws); with priority later in same turn cast Dream Salvage targeting a real opponent that discarded. A player can also cast Dream Salvage earlier and then arrange real discards to occur before it resolves on the stack if legal, but 'in response to Wheel on the stack' resolves BEFORE Wheel and does not count Wheel's future discards. Dream Salvage in a turn without any eligible opponent discard draws zero, a real floor.
- **Direct synergy:** This deck already has a major **draw engine** Geth's Grimoire and Rhystic Study/Remora, but Dream Salvage gives self-contained, cheap, immediate **post-discard resource refill**, different from persistent damage engines. Since Gwendlyn often uses mana on repeated untaps, a one-mana instant has disproportionately good timing, is castable even under tight UBBR mana and can dig for discard enablers, defense or next wheel.
- **Pro-Megrim countercase:** Megrim is a protected-ish enchantment with no need for cards in your hand after landing, scales with all three opponents' individual discarded cards on symmetrical wheels and can create large damage with Liliana's Caress; Dream Salvage mostly targets one opponent and yields none under continuous empty-handed lock if no wheels are available. If Gwendlyn cannot stay on battlefield, Megrim still monetizes other discard spells. But adding redundant damage while removing the efficient refill that supplies those same discards can lower engine sustainability.
- **Color/curve:** Megrim MV3 vs Salvage MV1; replacement adds +2 mana curve, black permanent setup, and zero immediate discard impact on its own. Dream Salvage helps draw into untap/protection with 1U/B held open. This is meaningful in a four-MV UBBR commander's expensive protected activation plans.
- **Commander-first criterion:** 'More Megrim damage on discard' is NOT enough when removing one of the best cheap spell-velocity reward engines for repeated random discard. This pair loses recursion into fresh hands. No measured, effect-aware win delta supports GrokBot's dismissal of Dream Salvage as weak.

### More plausible alternative cuts for Megrim, but not proven

1. **Underworld Dreams** {B}{B}{B}: all opponents drawing causes one damage per card; extra Black-heavy, does not trigger from commander discards. In **full-hand discard / refill** wheels, Megrim generally deals two per *discarded card*, while Dreams deals one per **drawn card**, and Notion Thief replacement steals opponents' nonfirst draws, making Dreams do none for those prevented draws while Megrim retains original discard value. This makes `+Megrim / -Underworld Dreams` a MORE commander-first candidate than `-Dream Salvage`. **Counterargument:** Gwendlyn empties hands: with no opponent hand before Wheel and 7 new cards drawn, Megrim yields zero while Dreams deals seven per opponent; this is essential complementary pressure. With Chains of Mephistopheles / Notion Thief, different replacement effects shift relative value. **Disposition: NEEDS TESTING, not automatically recommended.**
2. **The Raven Man:** mainly one end-step Bird payoff despite multiple opponent discards, can be insufficient damage acceleration; however its 1/1 fliers and the flavour of controlled hand attrition can matter. It is a cheaper value source than 3-MV damage-only enchantment and can fuel blocking/evasive pressure. **HOLD** without replacement value demonstrated.
3. **High-density untap/copy support:** cutting a repeat untapper to add Megrim risks making Gwendlyn herself irrelevant. Existing Stone, Seal, Minamo, Bracers, Pemmin and Elixir are synergistic, not interchangeable: under copy-stack rules and haste windows the marginal source matters. Existing authored **Aphetto Alchemist + Bracers** on-the-fly untap loop is a higher-impact mechanical lead, but requires haste or readiness, equip to Alchemist instead of commander, and actual opponent cards left to discard.
4. **Liliana's Caress / Bloodchief Ascension / Waste Not / Geth's Grimoire:** losing a cheaper per-card non-damage life-loss payoff, conditional card-to-graveyard drain, type-based mana/card/creature resource or discard-to-cards engine for Megrim is at best lateral. These are not preferred cuts.

### Rules and finite table checks

- Opponent A holds 5, Gwendlyn has Bracers, can legally activate on your turn, both original and copied tap target same player (may choose new target but needn't), that player random discards twice if both resolve; Megrim (hypothetical) triggers two times for four damage, **Dream Salvage afterward draws 2 for one mana** from A's cumulative discard. This is not an arbitrary number of activations: two from one copy-enhanced tap, with legal target and hand.
- After Windfall resolves with opponents A/B/C each discarding 5, Dream Salvage targets A only and draws five (not fifteen); Megrim produces five triggers on A, B, C each (30 damage aggregate). If a Notion Thief replacement stops opponents drawing refill, their original discards still produce Megrim triggers, while Underworld Dreams has no opponent draw triggers.
- Opponents all zero cards, wheel causes no initial opponent discards and then draws seven: Salvage (target opponent) draws **0** unless other actual discards earlier turn; Megrim produces **zero**; Dreams deals 7 to each opponent as long as draws actually occur. This is a reason not to blindly cut Dreams.
- Anvil of Bogardan / Chains may replace or force discard on draw; count actual discard events and whether graveyard replacements (Dauthi Voidwalker) prevent cards hitting grave, separate from the discarding *action*. Cards discarded under a graveyard-to-exile replacement still count as discarded for Megrim, but Bloodchief Ascension's 'put into graveyard' requires actual zone receipt. More precise rules check if making a new combo claim.
