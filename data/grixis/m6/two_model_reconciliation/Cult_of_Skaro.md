## Exact commander roll and copy rules

`Cult of Skaro` {1}{U}{B}{R}, 4/4 legendary **artifact Dalek**, on ATTACK chooses one of FOUR modes AT RANDOM: Thay +1/+1 each controlled artifact creature until end turn, Caan draw2, Sec create a 3/3 black menace Dalek artifact creature token, Jast each opponent loses4 life. **Connecting combat damage not necessary to receive attack trigger.** Random mode is chosen when the modal trigger is put onto stack; a copy of that on-stack trigger **COPIES ITS ALREADY CHOSEN MODE**. `Strionic Resonator` {2},{T} and `Scientist Supreme of A.I.M.` copy a specific Cult ability object, so they **DO NOT INDEPENDENTLY ROLL ANOTHER RANDOM MODE**. This is a material GrokBot advisory RULES ERROR, not merely a preference. Conversely `Roaming Throne` choosing Dalek makes actual extra triggered ability occur from attack, so each separately triggered instance independently chooses a random mode. This distinction changes expected damage/card/token output and the valuation of ability-copy pieces. No card swaps follow automatically: Resonator can still double a chosen favorable mode.

**Combat multiplication:** `Full Throttle` costs `{4}{R}{R}` and creates TWO extra combats after its main, with relevant attackers untapped at start of each combat but **no extra main phases**. `Aggravated Assault` costs `{3}{R}{R}` ACTIVATION at sorcery speed to provide another main + combat, and `Sword of Feast and Famine` opponent damage untaps lands, but Cult attack trigger alone does not imply that the Sword bearer connected. `Great Train Heist` Spree choosing extra combat only works when cast DURING combat and with actual {2}{R}{R} paid for the pertinent mode, not free. `Karlach, Fury of Avernus` first-combat attacker trigger creates next combat even if Karlach itself does not attack; triggers when attackers declared, not when combat damage dealt. Extra trigger from Throne on each qualifying combat is a separate random mode again.

**The actual thematic resource web:** `Biotransference` turns own creatures and spells to artifacts, gives 2/2 Necron artifacts on ARTIFACT CAST with 1 life spent, boosting Thay and army density. `Canoptek Spyder` draws from NONTOKEN artifact creature ETB, so Necron TOKENS from Biotransference do NOT trigger it. `Adaptive Omnitool` provides repeatable artifact-dig when EQUIPPED attacker attacks (equip3), not merely a blank two-cost body. `Master of Etherium`, `Steel Overseer`, Cybermen/Daleks, `Thay` mode amplify all-artifact attack plan. `Strionic Resonator`, `Roaming Throne`, `Scientist`, `Rite` and extra combats reward real attack declarations, not draw-damage alone.

## Material alternatives and adversarial sequencing

**Best challenger `Karlach, Fury of Avernus` IN / `Biotransference` or `Canoptek Spyder` OUT:** Costs 4R, nonartifact; adds one extra combat on first attack each your turn, so available ready Cult attacks may occur twice and independently roll twice. Removing Biotransference loses creature-artifact conversion and repeat 2/2 production which both feed Thay. Removing 5MV Spyder loses draw on nontoken artifact creature entries and helps late-draw stability, though it is slow. **Hexplate Wallbreaker** 5MV equipment with `For Mirrodin!`, equipped-attacker first-combat extra phase, is more artifact-native but equip `3R` after cast is sizable setup; `Combat Celebrant` cheaper exerted attacker needs itself eligible and surviving; `Akki Battle Squad` needs modified attacker and is 6MV. Strongest advantage of Karlach is guaranteed second attack roll with no combat damage; downside is expensive extra nonartifact enabler and weaker token engine. No measured first-attacks survived rate/actual board state proves exact swap.

**Ranked cut reversals to reject:** `Cybermat` for `Full Throttle` removes two guaranteed extra Cult attack chances for a fragile 2/1; for `Adaptive Omnitool` removes recurrent card access for an attacker needing connect; `Time Sieve` for `Genesis of the Daleks` consumes five artifacts PER extra turn, not self-sustaining off one Cult-generated 3/3 random token. `Mirror Room` additional trigger/copy setup can clash legend rule and high split-door costs; source effect not automatically more reliable than existing Throne/Resonator.

**Board traces:** (A) Cult attacks, random Caan chosen, Resonator copies -> **draw4** total if both resolve, not one new independent Jast possibility; with Throne separately triggering, its trigger rolls independently and may pick different output. (B) Cult survives and Karlach already under your control first combat -> extra combat, Cult can attack again if untapped by Karlach and still exists; an opposing removal in response to first trigger stops SECOND Cult attack but first already on stack. (C) Biotransference casting artifact generates 2/2 artifact token with life cost, reinforces Thay; its token is not nontoken Spyder card draw.
