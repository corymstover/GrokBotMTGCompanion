## Commander identity / mechanics under adversarial play

Ashnod's triggered ability copies a NONMANA ACTIVATED ABILITY of an ARTIFACT or CREATURE whenever **one or more permanents were sacrificed to ACTIVATE it**, not when sacrificed later during resolution or merely as separate trigger. No once-turn limit. One payment yields two legal effect resolutions; `Rings of Brighthearth` may generate a third if it triggers and {2} paid. Specific on-list direct payoffs `Dockside Chef` (one sacrifice plus {1}{B}, DRAW TWO with copy); `Trading Post` (only two of FOUR modes sacrifice permanent in activation COST — artifact-draw and creature-recursion); `Oni-Cult Anvil` (one artifact sac to drain/gain twice); `Altar of Dementia` (sac creature mills its power twice); `Time Sieve` (sac FIVE artifacts for TWO extra turns if both ability objects resolve); `Bolas's Citadel` (sac TEN NONLAND permanents to have opponents lose 10 life TWICE); `Repurposing Bay` (sac artifact card as cost to search exact **MV+1** twice), `Executioner's Capsule` and `Retrofitter Foundry` appropriate modes. Real costs must be paid; copying doesn't refund them. `Goblin Welder` sacrifices DURING RESOLUTION not as cost, so unlike `Goblin Engineer`/Bay is not duplicated. Mana outputs from altars/Petal/KCI never duplicated; Mayhem Devil/Marionette triggered effects aren't themselves Ashnod-copied abilities. This is intentional artifact-ability doubling, not generic artifact goodstuff.

**GrokBot underexplored risk:** Actual printed wording of popular suggestions. `Salvaging Station` {6} has untap-on-death and tap recursion, but NO sacrifice activation cost; it does NOT trigger Ashnod doubling. Existing `Dockside Chef` at {B} and {1}{B} activation converts spare artifact/creature to TWO immediate draws with Ashnod — deleting this changes native card access profoundly. `Detective's Satchel` has a conditional Thopter-making activation IF artifact sacrificed earlier that turn, not as activation cost, so not copied; its separately created Clue tokens DO have sacrifice-for-draw activations that qualify. `Filigree Sages` untaps artifacts but its cost lacks sacrifice. `Mycosynth Lattice` makes more ARTIFACT SOURCES but does not make their activated costs include sacrifice; another expensive artifact increases sweeper exposure. `Padeem` grants artifact hexproof against targeted removal but cannot stop overloaded non-targeted artifact destruction, while removing Chef loses draw. `Basalt Monolith` for `An Offer You Can't Refuse` changes a cheap stack shield into colorless ramp that can't pay UBR itself or create direct Ashnod copy. `Walking Ballista` is an actual mana-to-damage finisher from recursive artifact loops, despite not itself having commander-copy ability.

**Finite outcomes/risks:** (A) Ashnod + Chef, one Treasure and {1}{B}: sacrifice ONE Treasure and resolve two Chef draw abilities -> TWO draws, not two Treasures expended. (B) Ashnod + Time Sieve, five artifacts: pay its tap plus five artifacts once; copied ability and original grant TWO extra turns, but replacing five artifacts by each next turn is NOT automatic and Sieve itself must untap. (C) Bolas Citadel activation with ten nonland permanents gives two life-loss ten per opponent if copies resolve, not twenty permanents paid. (D) Opponent `Stony Silence`/Collector Ouphe locks artifact activation; overloaded Vandalblast removes many mana and outlets even under Padeem; no measured win rate of source candidate algorithms can substitute these actual constraints. (E) Graveyard hate breaks Myr Retriever + Scrap Trawler recast loops; copied abilities still need cards in correct zones/targets.
