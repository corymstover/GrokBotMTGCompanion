## Commander identity and natural ceiling

**Mai and Zuko** costs `{1}{U}{B}{R}`; is a 3/5 Human Noble **Ally** with **firebending 3** and allows casting **Ally and artifact SPELLS as though they have flash**. Its trigger adds 3R **on attack**, not when entering or when its creature is first cast. That red mana remains only until combat ends, while other floating mana normally empties as steps/phases end. It doesn't allow flash for unrelated enablers such as nonartifact/non-Ally `Urza, Lord High Artificer` or `Underworld Breach`. `Katara` wants spells on others' turns; `Zuko` wants spells during combat. `The Blue Spirit` values **nontoken creatures entering during combat**, not arbitrary token artifacts. Unique gameplay is red combat fuel -> **surprise artifacts and Allies**, experience-counter/combat payoffs and opponent-turn artifact reloading with Unwinding Clock and Waterskin, with existing Basalt/Rings/Ballista as high ceiling. Natural current bracket **4 — Optimized**, not a generic cEDH goodstuff reshaping.

## Exact proposal: +Grim Monolith / -Tundra Tank

**Disposition: PROPOSE UPGRADE FOR CORY'S EXPLICIT OWNER APPROVAL (moderate confidence); DO NOT APPLY.** The mechanistic improvement is compelling, although the lost protection/flavor is genuine and no matched game win-rate was obtained.

### Verified Oracle and legal mechanics

- **Grim Monolith**: {2} colorless artifact, legal Commander. Enters UNTAPPED; `{T}: add {C}{C}{C}`, `{4}: untap`; **doesn't untap on YOUR untap step**. Its colorless mana cannot alone pay Mai's `{U}{B}{R}` nor colored artifact / protection pips. Immediate tap after casting requires no summoning-sickness wait because it's a noncreature artifact, and mana ability doesn't use stack. Normal sequence cast for 2, tap for 3 = **+1 net generic this turn**, consuming a card and leaving tapped artifact; do not pretend it is recurring Sol Ring without support. It is MV2 versus Tank's MV3. Mai's artifact flash permits casting Monolith at unusual priority windows even on opponent turns, but without other uses mana still empties at step transitions.
- **Tundra Tank**: {2}{B} artifact **Vehicle**, MV3, crew 1, 4/4 if crewed, **firebending 1** when it attacks as a creature. Enters with a targeted ETB granting **indestructible until end of turn** to a creature you control, including Mai if still a legal target when ability resolves. With Mai on battlefield it can be flashed **in response to a destroy spell** and protect Mai after ETB resolves; doesn't prevent bounce/exile/sacrifice/-X/-X or an opponent responding to the ETB with removal before grant. Tank's printed 4/4 stats do NOT make it a creature while uncrewed, and it cannot simply attack after casting/without crew. As a crewed creature it may be subject to summoning sickness unless haste; its own firebending 1 only on its ACTUAL attack and the R expires at end of combat. Distinct Avatar-flavored defensive and combat utility, not a blank.
- **Unwinding Clock** is already approved: untaps ALL controller artifacts **during each other player's untap step**. Unlike Grim's own "doesn't untap during your untap step", this **works** across opponent turns: Clock refuels a tapped Grim for **CCC on each other player's turn**, particularly potent because Mai grants flash to artifact spells; mana has to be used inside legal steps. No extra free colored mana. In four-player pods, three opponents' untaps can offer three separate opportunities to use CCC across the full round, not a bank of nine permanently stored mana.
- **Rings of Brighthearth** + **Basalt Monolith** remains the existing verified unlimited-colorless route with {2} to pay for Rings' copied Basalt untap trigger, starting untapped Basalt and sufficient seed mana. **Grim Monolith + Rings is NOT a substitute for Basalt/Rings loop:** Grim adds CCC, paid untap costs 4, Rings copying an untap adds another 2 => isolated repetition is not self-sustaining even with clever double untap. Don't count Grim as another deterministic Rings combo partner without an additional independent untap/payoff engine. It does supply one-time 3C to help assemble and pay genuine Basalt/Rings seed costs, and can be searched by artifact tutors.
- **Legality:** Both optional Grim and incumbent Tank are legal in Commander and within Mai's UBR identity. Original **Urza's Legacy** Grim printing is legal; gold-bordered World Championship commemorative printing is not separately sanctioned for tournament play, so distinguish legal card from nonlegal special printing. No project exception required, no change in approved Bracket 4 justified without empirical reassessment.

### Full-function comparison and representative turns

**A. Early acceleration without commander.** T2 with two lands paying 2 for Monolith -> tap for CCC. Available **3 colorless** for artifact/activation development this same phase, a net +1 generic; it can accelerate `Basalt Monolith`, `Rings`, `Uthros` (still requires U), `Unwinding Clock` with another source, or setup artifact board. Tank costs 3 including BLACK, enters only to grant temporary indestructible and cannot tap for mana; if Mai has not been cast yet, Tank's protection often has no critical commander target. However colorless acceleration may leave Mai missing UBR; a "T3 Mai" claim needs three appropriately colored sources, not Monolith alone. **Mai-first** trajectories benefit indirectly by enabling further castable artifacts and mana left over for responses, not automatic UBR.
  
**B. Flash/Clock high-value case.** Control Mai and `Unwinding Clock`; flash Grim at opponent A's end step for {2}, with a spell or ability available in that step/next opponent turn. Tap for 3C to pay for a flashed artifact; at next opponent B's untap Clock untaps it; use another 3C in B's main/combat for flashed artifacts or source-payable activations. Opponent C untaps -> repeats if needed. This is a legitimate **commander-specific multiturm resource throughput**, exploiting BOTH current flash permission and current clock. If Mai is removed, Clock still untaps Grim but artifacts lose commander-granted flash, so the value is less interactive.
  
**C. Defending Mai from destroy spell.** Control Mai, have 2B and Tundra Tank in hand. Opponent targets Mai with destroy spell; flash Tank, target Mai with ETB indestructible. If ETB resolves before destroy spell, Mai survives. Grim instead gives no protection. This can preserve both engine and next combat RRR. After a sweeping `Toxic Deluge`, Tank's indestructible does **not** prevent -X/-X lethal reduction, so it cannot shield from every board reset.

**D. Attack-phase combat value.** Tank can crew 1 before attackers, attack if no summoning restriction, and its firebending trigger adds R usable until end of combat. That R can fund Mai-flashed artifact/Ally spells, an on-theme extra spell and `Zuko` experience. **But a one-time additional 1R only when Tank is crewed and attacks** is modest compared with Monolith's flexible 3C and Clock reloads. Keeping combat flavor matters, yet current source already has Mai firebending 3, Zuko, Firebender Ascension, Avatar Roku, Waterskin and Drill supplying distinct combat interactions. This decreases the marginal cost of losing Tank's *secondary* firebending role without erasing Fire Nation identity.

**E. Adversarial table.** `Stony Silence`, `Collector Ouphe`, `Null Rod` suppress Grim's activated mana ability, and artifact removal can destroy it before activation only if responding to its cast or a suitable removal timing (once resolved, active player may retain priority to activate mana ability). These same anti-artifact effects interfere with Tank's crew ability (artifact activated ability) and most of this already artifact-heavy deck, though its static ETB protection still works when it resolves. Removing Mai removes flash permission but not Grim's generic early acceleration; conversely Tank without Mai is not conveniently cast at instant speed (but still naturally protects another creature in own main phase).

### Alternative cuts investigated, and why Tank remains least damaging among realistic candidates

- `Baleful Strix`: cheaper nontoken artifact creature with ETB card draw and deathtouch. Mai can flash it in combat to trigger `The Blue Spirit` and in enemy combat as blocker; improves both hand access and artifact cast count. Cutting it for Monolith removes a stronger low-cost commander-specific card and a colored source of defense.
- `Thought Monitor`: affinity may make cheap cantrip + flying nontoken artifact creature, synergizes artifact swarms and mid-combat flash; retaining actual card flow is preferable to another mana rock in an already high-fast-mana shell.
- `Bender's Waterskin`: {3} rock untapping on every opponent's untap, with **colored** mana supporting repeated Ally/artifact flash and Mai's three pips. Removing it would replace repeatable colored fixing with fragile colorless burst, less efficient for the commander's unusual opponent-turn resource plan.
- `The Fire Nation Drill` / `Firebender Ascension` / `Avatar Roku, Firebender`: stronger distinct Avatar combat mechanics and opponent-combat resource windows than Tank's one-R crewed attack; cutting these removes more uniqueness for generic artifact mana. Avatar Roku is expensive ({3}{R}{R}{R}), but gives six R on EACH player's attack and genuinely synergizes with Mai artifact flash in other turns' combats.
- `Uthros Research Craft`, `Aetherflux Reservoir`, `Walking Ballista`: existing artifact-cast draw and combo conversion, including Basalt/Rings infinite colorless into Ballista. Removing any one reduces endgame capabilities more than removing Tank. `An Offer You Can't Refuse` and protection suite preserve the commander from game-losing stack interaction.
- `Path of Ancestry` is a credible separate untapped-land challenger (Spire of Industry), not a one-for-one spell slot. Reducing **31 lands to 30** for Grim is dangerous to colored UBR casting. Do not take this apparently "easy" cut. `Roaming Throne` choosing Ally boosts Mai's attack firebending and other Ally effects; that's more central than a lone crewed Vehicle.
- The only **strong** counterargument is that Tundra Tank may be indispensable in local destroy-heavy pods where flash-indestructible protects Mai from removal; it also carries theme. This upgrade should be evaluated as a trade in specific metagames, not a universal theorem.

### Decision / evidence scope

**PROPOSE UPGRADE:** `+1 Grim Monolith / -1 Tundra Tank`, for owner consideration, **not applied**. This is a moderate-confidence structural improvement in **early generic acceleration and opponent-turn artifact throughput** through pre-existing Mai+Clock, at loss of authentic instant-speed protection. The prior ChatGPT KEEP was reasonable but did not deeply compare the peculiar **Clock/Grim** off-turn refueling; GrokBot's critique identified a materially on-strategy option. Multiple real incumbents were compared; Tank is the narrowest distinctive combat payoff, not a generic no-identity filler.

**Power/bracket:** retain current Bracket 4 provisional. On adding Grim, neither pre-existing Basalt/Rings combo nor new Grims alone imply a newly deterministic cEDH speed; an owner-approved future candidate would need fresh 100/legality/genuine rules gate and bracket review.

**Still needed for high-confidence acceptance:** matched paid multi-opponent artifact-flash/Clock scenarios, actual commander-turn UBR access, ability to maintain Maia on battlefield, counterspells and artifact-hate prevalence, Tank saves vs Grim acceleration, existing earlier M5 generic-mana screens can't measure this. These uncertainties do **not** prevent a transparently conditional owner proposal, but they prevent measured win-rate claims.
