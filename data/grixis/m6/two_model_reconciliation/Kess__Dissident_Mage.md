## Commander, Oracle and playable sequencing

**Kess, Dissident Mage** {1}{U}{B}{R}, 3/4 flying: during each of YOUR turns cast **one instant or sorcery card from your graveyard**, paying its mana cost and legal sorcery timing; when a spell CAST using Kess's permission would go to graveyard, exile it instead. Cannot cast under Kess on an opponent's turn. It can be recast from grave after having been originally cast from hand earlier on the same turn, if Kess currently permits and the spell is in grave and the Kess allowance still unspent. Breach's alternative escape is distinct from Kess permission, not freely stackable with it on one cast. Kess is already commander on battlefield for the Will modality condition.

**Jeska's Will** {2}{R}, legal mono-red sorcery, choose one and **may choose both if control a commander as spell is cast**: generate R per card in target opponent's hand, and exile top 3 cards of own library with permission to PLAY them **THIS TURN** (normal costs, timing and land-drop restrictions apply). When choosing both, requires legal opponent target for red-mana mode; if the opponent becomes an illegal target before resolution, the entire single-target spell may fail to resolve including impulse mode (the actual spell has one target). Red mana persists through steps/phases in the *same* turn but not across turns; chosen hand card count measured at resolution, not casting. Card access does not refund blue/black pips or lift normal casting restrictions.

**Ledger Shredder** {1}{U}, legal 1/3 flying: whenever **any** player casts their **second spell during a turn**, it connives (draw one, discard one, +1/+1 only when nonland discarded), including opponent turns where double-spelling occurs. Shredder can filter a desired **instant/sorcery** to own grave for Kess, supply discard for Breach fuel/delirium, pitch protected card into grave and block flyers; connive itself isn't a cast or free spell. Unlike Will it is a 2-MV early set-and-grow body, not conditional opponent hand size and not a one-turn mana burst. Shredder does not specifically have to remain alive when Kess is cast for prior looting, but creature sweeps remove its value. Kess already includes on-theme DRC surveil, Faithless Looting, wheels, tutors and grave tutors that can partially replicate its filtering.

**Worked finite Kess replay (not unconditional win):** With Kess on battlefield, a target opponent holding 7 cards, and sufficient {2}{R} for first Will, cast Jeska's Will from HAND (choose BOTH modes, target that opponent). On normal resolution gain seven red mana and exile top three own cards; then Will goes to your graveyard. With Kess's own-turn permission unused, spend three red from pool to CAST Jeska's Will from grave, choose BOTH again (if commander still controlled and target available). It resolves for a new seven R if opponent still holds seven, exiles next three library cards, then goes to EXILE instead of returning to grave. Starting immediately before replay with 7R: pay 3R => 4R left, receive 7R => 11R left (plus whatever one had before first cast's mana cost), and see up to six exiled cards usable *this turn* at their ACTUAL costs. If opponent had 2 cards, first produces 2R while costing {2}{R}; there may not be enough second-cast mana and the 'net mana' claim disappears. This is Kess-specific two-resolution ramp/selection, not automatic free mana, unlimited loop or immediate cast of exiled cards. Countering original spell may deny red mana and card access even if it reaches grave; a later Kess cast may still be possible if resources and conditions. All chosen modes/timing checked against current Oracle.

## Head-to-head and other realistic cuts

**Case for adding:** Kess's graveyard replay naturally turns one Jeska's Will into up to two source-paid accelerations and two three-card impulse looks; more effective when opponents hold 5–7 cards. Can accelerate own-turn double-spell turbo windows towards Consultation/Oracle and Breach with mana + velocity. Jeska's Will already recognized by the deep authored review as interesting, but its comparison there was against premium **Mox Opal** and **Ragavan** instead of this specific less-exclusive grave-filter slot. GrokBot's alternate cut deserves a new, fair decision. It does not introduce a novel unrelated combo win, but facilitates more *own-turn Kess-recast spells* and cEDH turn windows.

**Case to preserve Shredder:** Kess requires stocked own graveyard, and Shredder filters prior to and after commander arrival at two mana; opponents' second spell also improves it. Ledger offers repeated draws + selective discard on both own and opposing turns, independent of a full opposing hand or successful four-MV Kess. Cutting it weakens resilience in removal- and counterspell-heavy games, may make early low-land/tutor hands worse, and can delay or prevent key spells reaching the GY; Will with Kess absent/removed can be mediocre three-mana red-only ritual/impulse. Among 27 lands, early set-up and blue pitch count matter. Ledger is a **BLUE** card potentially pitched to Force of Will/Force of Negation; Jeska's Will is **RED**, reducing pitchable blue cards by one. This is nontrivial in high-power tables.
**Alternative cuts:** Don't remove `Mox Opal`, `Ragavan`, `Chrome Mox`, `Lion's Eye Diamond`, `Grinding Station`, `Intuition`, `Borne Upon a Wind`, `Demonic Consultation`, `Tainted Pact`, premium interaction or 27th land speculatively: acceleration/pile/timing/colored-mana benefits are vital. `Dragon's Rage Channeler` costs just R and surveils on each noncreature cast, an even better low-mana GY initializer than Shredder for this archetype. Shredder is the most negotiable because it waits for *second spell* and provides neither mana nor direct compact win. It still has a real attrition role, so evidence does not prove Will strictly dominates for every pod.
**Game Changer and power bracket:** Jeska's Will is a Game Changer in Commander currently; if approved later, review count/curated format bracket under relevant official thresholds; this deck already owner-approved Bracket **5 — fringe cEDH** and one replacement does not by itself justify a bracket change. Neither card has Grixis color identity or official legality blocker. Do not count it as approved or currently on-list.
