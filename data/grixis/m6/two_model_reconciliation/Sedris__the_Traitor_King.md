## Commander-specific engine and actual rules

Sedris is a six-mana {3}{U}{B}{R} 5/5 legendary Zombie Warrior; each creature card in your graveyard has **unearth {2}{B}** (sorcery-speed activation). Resolving unearth returns that creature from grave to battlefield, grants haste, schedules a **ONE-SHOT delayed triggered ability** to exile it **at beginning of the next end step**, and establishes a separate continuous **replacement effect**: **if that permanent would leave the battlefield, exile it instead**. These are DIFFERENT effects. Unearth is an activated ability, not casting the creature; it triggers ETB/enter-from-grave effects, not cast triggers. Preserving ETB toolbox for Archon/Grave Titan/Primordial and attacks with Etali, plus turn-ending/rescue/phasing of actual unearthed bodies, is the deck's distinctive core. Deadeye Navigator, Teferi's Veil, Obeka Brute Chronologist and Sundial are present rescue pieces; Sneak Attack adds another delayed end-step sacrifice axis. Don't remake a six-MV unearth toolbox as generic blink goodstuff.

### GROKBOT MATERIAL ERROR: Sundial **CAN retain** unearthed creatures

GrokBot asserted `Sundial doesn't keep unearthed creatures; the trigger moves to opponent's end step`, without distinguishing **when** the activation occurs. That conclusion is **FALSE** in the critical line:

**Valid step sequence — own end step, delayed trigger already on stack:**
1. On own turn before end step: activate one or more legal unearth abilities, return the creature cards; the one-shot delayed exile triggers are armed for **next end step**.
2. Move to beginning of **own end step**, let each of those “exile this” abilities TRIGGER and be placed onto stack (all already triggered even if multiple unearthed bodies). Priority now available before they resolve. Control untapped `Sundial of the Infinite` and {1} mana.
3. Pay `{1},{T}` to activate Sundial **during YOUR turn**; ability resolves, **ENDS TURN**, exiling ALL spells/abilities on stack, including all pending unearth delayed exile triggers. Those *already-triggered one-shot* abilities **do not trigger again next end step**. **ALL such unearthed creatures remain on battlefield** (subject to other effects).
4. The **separate unearth zone-change replacement** persists for each such unearthed permanent. A later would-die, bounce or nonexile leave attempt is replaced by exile, not a re-unearth into grave. Another existing SNEAK ATTACK "at beginning next end step" sacrifice trigger similarly can be stripped from stack with correct timing, but Sneak Attack does NOT itself have unearth's zone-change replacement.

**Wrong step sequence:** Activating Sundial in second main BEFORE the end step triggers skips own end step entirely, so a delayed exile **has not triggered** and will instead trigger at beginning of next actual end step (often opponent's); you cannot use Sundial then because its ability is **activate only during your turn**. This is the limited truth GrokBot mistakenly generalized to all Sundial timing. Likewise, activating during cleanup is not allowed at will without specific trigger/passing priority; best reliable play is own end step with triggers on stack.

**Source-specific distinction:** Wizards' official Sundial ruling explicitly says triggering “at beginning of end step” is **postponed** if turn ended **before** that end step. Wizards' unearthed creature rulings explicitly say that **countering/otherwise exiling the delayed trigger after it triggers preserves the creature, while the replacement effect continues**. Rule references: [Sundial Oracle and official Sept 22 2011 rulings](https://mtg.wtf/card/m12/218/Sundial-of-the-Infinite); [Modern Masters 2017 official unearth and blink rulings](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/modern-masters-2017-edition-release-notes-2017-03-03); [Wizards Comprehensive Rules](https://magic.wizards.com/en/rules). In these sources `countering` the delayed trigger with Stifle and `exiling` that triggered ability off stack with Sundial both prevent the sole queued trigger from resolving, not from recurring later.

### Ghostly Flicker legally rescues unearthed creatures, but no functional superiority over Sundial established

**Ghostly Flicker {2}{U}**, legal on-color instant: “Exile **two target** artifacts, creatures, and/or lands you control, then return those cards to battlefield under your control.” Both targeted objects must still be legal on resolution to be affected; if one invalid, other still exiles/returns if legal. Can target one unearthed creature PLUS one land, or two unearthed creatures; requires two **distinct legal targets** on cast. It can rescue up to two creatures at once and rerun valuable ETB abilities.

Unearth's replacement is **not a veto** against Flicker: it exiles unearthed creatures that would leave for another zone, but if effect **itself instructs exile**, exile proceeds, and that very spell subsequently returns the EXILED CARD. Re-entered card is an entirely **NEW OBJECT** with no relationship to previous unearth, no lingering end-step exile and no prior unearth replacement. It doesn't grant haste on return unless printed/other effect supplies it. **Official Wizards Modern Masters 2017 release notes explicitly rule this use works**, with Ghostly Flicker itself named in same flicker section. This is why untargeted generic bounce does not rescue unearth: attempted return to hand replaced by EXILE with no spell instruction to return exiled card.

- **Sundial strength:** on-board repeatable {1},tap activation can preserve **ANY number** of unearthed permanents whose delayed triggers are queued during the same end step, plus multiple Sneak Attack end-step sacrifices and other own end-step detrimental delayed triggers; requires own end-step and untapped Sundial. Still leaves exile-on-departure replacement.
- **Ghostly strength:** 3-mana instant may blink **up to 2** targets (two are specified) at ANY legal instant window, including BEFORE a lethal spot-removal or opposing combat, creates new object with fully NORMAL future departures, and triggers powerful ETB twice with Panharmonicon etc. It doesn't end the turn, allowing same-turn followthrough, and can blink non-unearthed ETB permanents and a land. A copied Flicker could increase count if available, not assumed.
- **Ghostly limits:** one-shot spell, single U + two generic cost (vs Sundial one mana after setup), requires two eligible targeted permanents, counter/removal sensitivity, no ability to preserve three or more already unearthed targets at one instant, later exiled blinking under grave hate may still be blocked by targeted removal. While Sundial alone cannot prevent replacement if unearthed creature leaves, Ghostly can permanently remove that restriction from specifically blinked objects; distinguish.
- **Other approved alternatives:** `Deadeye Navigator` + `Peregrine Drake` reusable {1}{U} blink loop and `Terror of the Peaks` actual damage payoff already included; `Obeka, Brute Chronologist` can end turn when timing permitted; `Teferi's Veil` phases out **attacking creatures** after combat, preserving them through end-step checks. Each has different investment/hazards and doesn't obsolete Sundial.

## Competing exact cuts and commander-first judgment

**GrokBot exact pair: +Ghostly Flicker / -Sundial of the Infinite — REJECT EXACT, KEEP Sundial.** The proposed replacement removes an important actual functionality based on invalid timing interpretation. Sundial's ability to rescue **several** unearthed creatures as a group, preserve Sneak Attack/Obeka delayed end-of-turn setup, and stay for repeated turns is deeply commander first. A single Flicker rescuing at most 2 while requiring two legal targets and repeated draw/recursion cannot fully replace this. No proof net better, and source already has relevant Deadeye/Veil.

**Alternate cut: `Sheoldred, Whispering One` (exploratory)**: seven-MV end-game graveyard/upkeep engine offers opponent sacrifice and reanimate each own upkeep, but an unearthed Sheoldred normally exiles before opponent upkeep if not rescued — this makes it a *particularly strong Sundial-dependent* reason to keep Sundial, though the 7MV hardcast and tapped/hasty value can be slow. One could evaluate replacing a slow high-end threat for Ghostly if ETB recursion and speed matter more, but removing Sheoldred may decrease long-game inevitability; **no established strict upgrade**.
**Alternate cut: `Jin-Gitaxias, Core Augur`**: 10MV conventional monster whose own end-step draw-seven does work during same end step as unearth exile, subject to controller ordering, but opponents' max-hand-size reduction needs remaining alive across turns. Replacing with Flicker improves tempo/early protection but deletes a terrifying Sedris-rescuable lock threat; not unambiguous.
**Alternate cut: `Panharmonicon` or `Convergence of Dominion`**: inappropriate to cut core repeat ETB multiplication or the cost reduction on every 2B unearth; these are commander-identity layers. `Ripples of Undeath`, `Entomb`, `Buried Alive`, `Unmarked Grave` are the reliable graveyard setup; don't delete to fit generic blink. `Teferi's Veil` is weaker for nonattacking ETB bodies but powerful cheap reusable support for attacking unearthed Etali/Titan; different enough to remain.
**Alternate `Victimize`**: two-for-one real graveyard recursion at sacrifice cost (can sacrifice an unearthed creature but it exiles, won't fuel its own retrigger), arguably overlaps with blink but helps before Sedris six-mana entry; keep without real test.

## Paid and adversarial scenarios

1. **End step, two unearthed bodies:** Sedris controlled; unearth `Archon of Cruelty` and `Grave Titan` for 2B EACH, have untapped Sundial, {1} reserved. Both delayed unearth exile triggers go on stack at own end step. Activate Sundial {1}, tap, exiles BOTH triggers; both permanents remain, retain replacement effects. With only one Ghostly Flicker, exile and return same pair as new objects {2}{U}, retrigger both ETBs, future death goes to grave normally. Both valid; Sundial repeatability and unlimited count vs Ghostly new object and ETB immediate value distinct.
2. **Bad main-phase Sundial:** End turn before end step; both bodies still there during cleanup but their next-end-step delayed triggers are postponed and will fire during *opponent's* end step. Sundial cannot activate in opponent turn; likely bodies exiled. Explains exact GrokBot misconception.
3. **Rescue in response to exile/spot kill:** Opponent aims removal at unearthed `Terror of the Peaks` during own main. Activate Sundial early could skip end step and not cancel delayed trigger; Ghostly Flicker can instead target Terror plus legal land, exile+return, new normal permanent, ETB; subject to legal targets/responses. Thus Ghostly has a real niche not dismissed.
4. **Tapped Sundial at end step:** No ability to pay/activate => exile resolves unless Obeka or other source stops. Multiple turns or separate end-step triggers require untapping Sundial first; no automatic infinite rescue.
5. **Teferi's Veil with attacker:** Unearthed `Etali, Primal Storm` attacked; triggers ETB? Etali attack trigger resolves before end combat. End of combat Veil phases Etali out, so end-step delayed exile sees phased-out creature as non-existent and doesn't exile; when phases in next own untap, unearth replacement still relevant. A stationary ETB-only Archon doesn't phase out via Veil and is exiled without Sundial/Flicker.
6. **Deadeye/Drake blink:** Existing Navigator soulbond pair can blink unearth-permanent while legal and paid, then returned as new object; `Terror of the Peaks` makes repeated Drake return ETB into real damage. Ghostly does not create a new stronger loop automatically.

## Conclusion

**Final disposition KEEP approved 100; REJECT exact Ghostly Flicker-for-Sundial proposal.** GrokBot's blanket “Sundial doesn't work with unearth” is **a confirmed MATERIAL RULES ERROR**. GrokBot correctly identified Ghostly Flicker as a legally viable additional ETB-rescue line, but that is not evidence that a working, repeatedly useful end-step-control artifact should be removed. No better proven alternative cut; prefer existing Sundial, Deadeye, Veil, Obeka, reanimator suite. **Power:** retain approved Bracket 4 — Optimized, **Unearth / Reanimator / End-Step Control**; no owner upgrade proposal or canonical mutation. **No new current Commander legality blocker** for both cards, no project exception.
