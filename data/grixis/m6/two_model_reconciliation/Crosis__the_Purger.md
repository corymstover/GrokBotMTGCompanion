One Megrim not in list; Sedraxis Specter is present; Liliana's Caress, Fell Specter, Waste Not, Geth's Grimoire, Painter's Servant, Hymn to Tourach, Mind Twist, Wheel of Fortune, Windfall, Dark Deal, Syphon Mind, Oppression, Bottomless Pit, Kroxa, Tinybones payoffs, Brotherhood Regalia, Sword of Feast and Famine are included. Spot-check here verified Crosis/Painter/Megrim/Sedraxis/Caress/Fell Oracle and Commander legality, not full 92 card current Scryfall rescan.

Crosis the Purger {3}{U}{B}{R}, flying 6/6: when it deals **combat damage to a player** its trigger lets you pay **{2}{B} upon resolution**, choose a color **upon resolution**, victim reveals their hand and discards every card of that color. With Painter's Servant naming a color, **cards in all zones, including all victim hand cards and even colorless lands/artifacts, have that chosen color in addition to their original colors**. Thus ONE successful Crosis combat trigger + 2B can discard the victim's entire current hand if Servant survives until resolution. No automatic trigger for noncombat commander damage, no choice revealed early to opponents, no free activation. Protecting commander connectivity and color purge is core; avoid generic off-identity combo substitution.

## GrokBot's exact swap: +Megrim / -Sedraxis Specter

**Oracle and legality.**
- **Megrim** {2}{B}, black enchantment: "**Whenever an opponent discards a card, this enchantment deals 2 damage to that player.**" Commander legal and mono-B within Grixis. It triggers **for each discarded opponent card**: a six-card hand discarded to one Crosis/Painter trigger yields **six Megrim triggers ×2 = 12 noncombat damage** to the affected opponent. This is **actual damage** from a black enchantment, not life loss that bypasses damage prevention; it is not commander damage and does not enhance Crosis's 6 commander combat damage. It also triggers when opponents discard during wheel/discard effects, and when they discard to cleanup if applicable. Does not trigger when *you* discard. Must already be on battlefield when the cards are discarded; cannot be cast after a purge to retroactively trigger it.
- **Sedraxis Specter** {U}{B}{R}, 3/2 flying Specter; whenever it deals **combat damage to a player**, that player discards **one** card. **Unearth {1}{B}** returns it from grave with haste and exile conditions; this is NOT casting a creature spell and is one-shot unless some other legal effect changes the zone interaction. Legal and Grixis. Unlike Megrim, it can attack independently of Crosis, advance a one-card denial plan without other enablers, provide a blocking flying body when hard-cast and work as an evasive Sword carrier, but needs to connect for each discard and is removable as a creature. Its printed casting requires all **U B R** colors by turn 3, while Megrim needs generic mana plus just B.
- **Liliana's Caress**, already on-list for {1}{B}, triggers per opponent card discard but causes **2 life loss**, not damage; Megrim supplements it with redundancy rather than being an identical effect under all prevent-damage rules. **Fell Specter** ({3}{B}) is a 1/3 flying body with ETB target discard and an each-opponent-card-discard 2-life-loss punishment, acting as a costly creature-based redundancy. Unlike Megrim, it can die to sweepers/removal, and unlike Sedraxis it pays off any opponent discard without needing to connect. The new addition remains a meaningful native discard punishment, not an unrelated staple.

**Synergy:**
- With Crosis 6/6 connecting against opponent with exactly 6 hand cards, Painter survives and you spend 2B: Crosis combat deals 6 commander damage; its trigger discards all six; Megrim supplies **12 further life damage** (separate, noncommander). Liliana's Caress supplies another **12 life loss** if present; all three can coexist. Six Geth's Grimoire draws are independent optional triggers. This is a large upgrade to the payoff of the commander's defining action, even without adding another generic win line.
- With a resolved Wheel of Fortune/Windfall where each of three opponents discards 7 cards, Megrim produces **7 ×2=14 noncombat damage to each** and **42 total** over three opponents (assuming all triggers resolve). Refill from Wheel still occurs, and opponents can respond to Megrim triggers after wheel has finished; no fabricated immediate automatic kill or freely looped wheels.
- Existing **Oppression, Bottomless Pit, Dark Deal, Hymn to Tourach, Mind Twist, Syphon Mind, Kroxa, Recoil, Specters** force discard even when Crosis is blocked/removal-targeted. This density makes an enchantment payoff more independent than a pure Painter/Crosis two-piece setup. **Teferi's Puzzle Box is an IMPORTANT exception:** putting hand cards on library bottom is NOT discard; Megrim, Caress, Waste Not and Fell Specter are not triggered by the bottoming action. Nor does Painter simply changing a card's color cause a discard event.
- Painter's Servant's color modification makes Crosis discard **all cards**, but itself does not directly increase Megrim damage until opponent actually discards. Tinybones, Pocket Nuisance 'whenever player discards one or more cards' differs; it triggers once per player/event, not once for each of six discarded cards. Megrim/Liliana/Fell trigger separately per card.
- Dark Deal/wheels also discard your own hand; Megrim does not punish its controller's discard because it says **opponent**. Opponents' draw after wheels can be mitigated by Notion Thief, but Notion changes card-draw allocation, not Megrim trigger count for the original discarded cards.

**Against the upgrade / cost:**
- The deck explicitly needs early independent discard threats for the 6-MV Crosis to be effective; Specter's unearth 1B can deliver a final hasty attack after it dies, and its 3/2 flying body provides actual pressure/creature presence and possible Sword of Feast and Famine connection. Megrim is noncreature dead material when opponents have emptied hands and there is no remaining discard engine/wheel; it never helps Crosis *connect* or cast earlier. Against players quickly casting all cards, payoff redundancy is poor; sedraxis body could deal repeat 3 combat damage even to a depleted hand.
- Megrim costs 3 including just one black vs Liliana's Caress 2. In hand without discard sources Megrim produces zero immediate damage. A high count of redundant damage-only enchantments can diminish resilience to enchantment stax and early pressure, but 2 existing payoff permanents (Caress/Fell) are not saturation proof.
- The original author placed high value on retaining all Blizzard, Sedraxis and Fell Specters for flavorful combat-hand-disruption. Cutting one reduces 'Specter hand-attack' subidentity even while increasing core **Crosis color-purge** identity; it is an explicit commander-first trade, not forced optimization.

**Best cut relative to alternatives:**
- **Sedraxis Specter (preferred):** one opponent one card **after landing combat damage**, requires specific UBR printed mana, unearth is once; redundant connection requirement with six-MV Crosis. A reactive table can kill the lower-toughness specter before it pays off; replacing it improves payoff **from the commander's own attack**, wheels and other discard independent of combat.
- **Blizzard Specter (alternative, weaker cut):** more expensive 4MV U/B flying **haste** with discard-or-bounce flexibility on connection; bounce a problematic permanent without targeting and replay/discard pressure is a rare effect in this Grixis discard-control list. Removing that unique choice would narrow control, so prefer preserving Blizzard. Its presence also supports possible sword/Assault interaction.
- **Fell Specter (reject as cut):** ETB discards and then threatens repeat 2-life-loss per card discarded. Removing for Megrim changes body + guaranteed one discard into 1 cheaper mana + same-card-damage payoff; lower impact than trading out Sedraxis, and loses a multi-function incumbent. Protect Fell unless removal-heavy metagame strongly reverses equation.
- **Liliana's Caress / Waste Not / Geth's Grimoire (do not cut):** already core native discard payoffs, each different role: Caress unblockable life loss, Waste Not cards/BB/creatures based on types, Grimoire repeat drawing. Swapping any one for Megrim weakens diversity.
- **Dream Salvage, Brotherhood Regalia, Painter, Sword, Cheap mana, protection (do not cut):** source setup and conversion, no justification to weaken 6-MV commander or ability cost for another payoff.

## Rules sequencing / adversarial stress

1. **Protected attack:** Crosis already equipped with Regalia, Painter on field and Megrim on field, defending opponent six cards; Crosis connection, triggered ability on stack, possible response window. If Painter removed before Crosis resolution, Crosis still chooses a color during resolution, but can only discard cards actually bearing it and Megrim only sees truly discarded count. If Megrim removed before cards discarded, no new Megrim triggers; if removed after triggers put on stack, independent abilities may still resolve.
2. **Six cards and normal pay:** With all setup surviving, combat 6 commander damage, optional 2B paid during trigger resolution, six matching cards discarded, Megrim six queued damage triggers. If damage is prevented/protection applies, Megrim may do less or zero; Caress loses life independently. Damage-based elimination is life-total dependent and not proof of competitive game rate.
3. **Exhausted hands:** Crosis attacks an enemy at zero cards and Megrim triggers zero; only its body remains relevant until a discard effect gives opponents new cards (wheels). Sedraxis would still connect for 3 combat damage but its forced discard would do nothing. Pilot must retain both access to opponents' hands and a protected combat clock; do not overstate +Megrim as always-active damage.
4. **Initial curve:** Specter is a three-color turn-three play versus Megrim at 2B, but both consume 3; removing Specter also reduces flying blockers at early turns. A metagame with early evasive aggro may favor baseline. No exact deckwide mana-color cast analysis or effect-aware matched outcomes has been measured in this reconciliation.

## Disposition

**PROPOSE UPGRADE (explicit owner approval required):**
```
+1 Megrim
-1 Sedraxis Specter
```
**Why:** Megrim creates one *additional lethal conversion source* per discarded card specifically on the commander-defining Painter-assisted full-hand Crosis purge and a large suite of native discard spells, whereas Specter's third-color-dependent combat/one-card pressure overlaps with Crosis's already invested attack plan. The strategic case supports an upgrade proposal without pretending measured win-rate superiority. **Power bracket:** retain optimized Bracket 4 pending actual list validation; no claim of new bracket or cEDH consistency. 

**Secondary hypothesis (not an owner proposal):** Original authored `+Aggravated Assault / -Blizzard Specter` remains untested and could work with Sword of Feast and Famine on a protected/unblockable Crosis; it needs five-land mana refund and adds a very different repeated-combat combo. It should not supplant this targeted discard-payoff upgrade without real comparative tests. The Megrim change is the more direct and lower-complexity commander identity improvement from the two-model evidence.

**Evidence blockers:** No target-pod distribution of average Crosis-trigger cards discarded; no color-aware turn3 cast frequency for Sedraxis vs Megrim, no matched-hand multiplayer effect simulation of pressure/damage. These uncertainties limit performance claims but do not invalidate the *legal* native synergy or owner's ability to approve a proposed change.
