## Actual commander ability boundaries

Lynde `{1}{U}{B}{R}` 2/4 deathtouch:
1. Whenever a **Curse is put into YOUR graveyard FROM THE BATTLEFIELD**, the ability sets a delayed return at the beginning of the **next** end step **attached to you**. The Curse must actually move from battlefield to grave; Aura control and enchantment target alone do not create triggers. If an opponent enchanted by your Curse leaves the game, the Aura goes to its owner's grave and can trigger Lynde (official ruling). If Curse was milled/discarded from another zone, no Lynde trigger; under Rest in Peace replacement exile, no graveyard entry, so cannot use Lynde's return. Multiple Curses going from battlefield to grave create separate delayed returns, and can all return to YOUR side at the same next end step.
2. At beginning of **EACH of YOUR upkeeps**, Lynde gets ONE optional trigger: attach **one** Curse already attached to you to one of your opponents, and if the transfer succeeds draw TWO cards. The move does **not target** opponent nor Aura, so a hexproof opponent remains eligible (official card ruling), but any Curse's enchant-player restrictions must remain valid; no opponent available means no transfer and no draw. More upkeeps or copying the triggered ability can potentially transfer more than one, but each trigger moves at most one. **Paradox Haze** and/or The Ninth Doctor and Obeka extra upkeeps can increase opportunities only under their own exact preconditions and timings. No instant catch-up by one trigger.

## Independent disputed timing: moving Curse of Thirst

- **Curse of Thirst {4}{B}** is an Aura Curse: `At the beginning of enchanted player's upkeep, this Aura deals damage to that player equal to the number of Curses attached to them.` Its official ruling states **count Curse attachments when ability resolves**, not at trigger time. Because the trigger started at beginning of YOUR upkeep when Thirst enchanted you, **'that player' refers to YOU for this queued trigger even if the Aura later moves**; moving Thirst does not cancel the existing trigger.
- On your upkeep with **only Thirst attached to you**, both the Thirst triggered ability and Lynde's transfer trigger are controlled by YOU; choose relative stack order. Put Thirst's trigger on stack first, Lynde's trigger on top. Resolve Lynde: move Thirst to opponent, draw two; on resolving the preexisting Thirst ability **count Curses still attached to you = ZERO**, resulting **ZERO damage to you**. The distinction between 'trigger remains' and 'nonzero effect remains' is essential. GrokBot's warning that transfer cannot 'stop damage' without checking resolution count is materially incomplete.
- With Thirst and **two other self-attached Curses**, transfer Thirst away, then queued Thirst damage resolves at **2** to you (two Curses remain); if transfer instead moves a different Curse, Thirst remains and another other Curse remains, thus damage **2**. Damage can be amplified by `Curse of Bloodletting` if it is still attached to YOU at damage resolution; if you transfer Bloodletting first, the static doubling on yourself ends. All amounts are actual damage, not guaranteed life loss if damage can be prevented.
- If you resolve Thirst FIRST before Lynde transfer, count all 3 Curses attached to you: Thirst deals THREE to you, then you move one and draw. Opponents may respond to triggered abilities and Lynde, or alter enchantment state prior to resolution. A Curse returned earlier this upkeep rather than present at upkeep start does **not retroactively trigger** at that same upkeep.

**Other harmful triggers / statics cannot be generalized:**
- `Cruel Reality` begins on the ENCHANTED PLAYER's upkeep and that queued effect remains for the player whose upkeep caused the trigger; moving the Aura before resolution does not erase its forced sacrifice/life-loss event. You must prepare to pay the actual sacrifice/life cost even if you move it.
- `Curse of Death's Hold` affects ENCHANTED PLAYER's creatures with continuous -1/-1 while attached; move it and the ongoing debuff ceases instantly; not a queued damage trigger.
- `Curse of Bloodletting` is a CONTINUOUS damage-doubling replacement effect against enchanted player while attached; moving it can prevent future *doubling* of subsequent damage to you even if a different Curse's queued damage trigger still resolves. It is itself not a one-shot upkeep trigger.
- `Curse of Misfortunes` triggers at beginning of **YOUR upkeep** (Aura/controller), NOT 'enchanted player's upkeep'. When enchanting an opponent it still tutors a Curse onto **that opponent** each own upkeep; when self-attached can fetch another Curse and Lynde may move a Curse from you after the tutor. Tutor and Lynde order chosen by controller when both trigger. Copying Curse of Misfortunes' enchantment permanent is not copying the spell on stack; don't count that as Parnesse effect. `Bitterheart Witch` searches a Curse from library for direct battlefield attachment upon death, not CAST, so the Curse's ETB effects work while any spell-cast payoff does not trigger.
- **Delayed return during end step:** if a Curse is sacrificed/destroyed AFTER the BEGINNING of current end step, Lynde cannot put it back at the already-passed beginning; delayed trigger fires at the *next* end step. If many Curse permanents simultaneously die under a wipe, their delayed returns can overwhelm the one-Curse-per-upkeep transfer rate; extra upkeeps solve only throughput when payable/survivable.

## Strategy and cutting analysis

Lynde is a low-end Bracket 4 Curse-transfer/recursion attrition build, not generic Grixis control or turbo-combo. Actual owned 100 has 35 lands, **Paradox Haze, The Ninth Doctor, Obeka Splitter, Claws of Gix, Despotic Scepter, Perilous Research, Braids**, and a broad Curse collection. Self-harm exposure depends on *when* sacrifices happen, simultaneous-return counts, which Curses are self-attached at exact trigger placement and at resolution, available opponents and extra upkeeps. Merely counting 'seven Curses MV5+' is curve exposure, not automatically proof any specific Curse should be cut.

- **Retain Curse of Thirst provisionally:** it scales with number of Curses actually attached to victim, a commander's punishment plan. It can be moved away before its own queued damage resolution, and self-damage is then zero if none remain. An objective 'must cut as self-harm' is NOT supported.
- **Retain Curse of Bloodletting provisionally:** doubles damage directed at its current enchanted player; dangerous when returned to self but instrumental in opponent-damage amplification and with Obeka combat plan. Compare to other bulky Curse and per-turn misplacement scenarios before cutting.
- **Competing meaningful challengers:** `Strionic Resonator` costs {2}, tap to copy Lynde's upkeep trigger, moving another self-Curse and drawing another two if one remains; counters stack and payment timing are nonfree. The exact plausible cut `Wicked Visitor` is a lower-impact constellation-ping but provides efficient cheap 2-MV on-theme damage each enchantment ETB and may help close games without dangerous self-attached Curses. `Beseech the Mirror` (bargain a controlled Curse) puts it to grave for next end-step return and tutors, but BBB cost and bargained cost are real; `Perilous Research` already sacrifices Curse at instant speed while drawing two. No tested superiority over cut established.
- In adversarial no-untap/upkeep lock scenarios extra Paradox Haze can double self-Curse harm, so not automatically protective; one must order beneficial redistribution before harmful triggers when possible. Wide sweepers can return many Curses self-attached while commander killed; once delayed abilities are set, killing commander later won't cancel their returns.
