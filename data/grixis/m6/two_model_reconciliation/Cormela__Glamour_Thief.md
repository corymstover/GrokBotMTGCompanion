**Commander-identity first:** 4MV hasty Cormela taps with paying **{1} GENERIC** to add UBR, which can be spent ONLY **CASTING instant or sorcery spells**; cannot pay creature/Thassa's Oracle, artifact/LED activation, enchantment or the activation's own {1}. On DEATH returns target instant/sorcery CARD from OWN graveyard to hand; a sacrificed Cormela must stay in grave instead of moving to command zone if another spell wants to reanimate it. Recurring creature haste allows multiple tap-for-spells and death-trigger returns when legal. With `Ashnod's Altar` sacrifice gives CC mana separately (Altar's mana isn't Cormela restricted), and `Shallow Grave` returns TOP graveyard creature CARD (not targeted) with haste, while `Goryo's Vengeance` targets legendary card: if another creature enters your GY above Cormela, Shallow can bring WRONG creature, breaking shortcut. Those distinctions support a natural Bracket5 cEDH spell recursion commander, not just a consultation shell.

**Missing “Doomsday” isn't proof of underoptimization**: adding a new pile-dependent cEDH card would need a cut, protected pile formation, actual draw effects and interaction windows. Already on-list `Thassa's Oracle` + Consultation/Pact and `Underworld Breach` + LED + Brain Freeze are independent backups; own Cormela `Saw in Half`, `Goryo`, `Shallow`, `Birgi` and `Molten Duplication` are signature. `Molten Duplication` and `Dualcaster Mage` can repeatedly copy a resolving spell targeting the newest Dualcaster token after flashing original Dualcaster, generating finite arbitrarily large hasty token attackers if the stack isn't disrupted; must cast/pay initial spells and legally redirect targets, not automatic because both in deck. `Cormela` restricted UBR can pay for Duplication/spells but NOT the original Dualcaster creature. If a chosen return loop requires Cormela in GY rather than command zone and opponent removes it, interrupt.

**Actual cuts challenged:** M5 favored `Archaeomancer/Ardent Elementalist` replacing `Faithless Looting`, but four-mana ETB spell recursion is redundant to Cormela DEATH trigger and loses one-red early loot/discard/grave setup plus flashback. `Altar of Dementia` replacing `Lion's Eye Diamond` sacrifices key zero-color burst/Breach conversion for creature-sac milling, not automatically better than actual Altars. `Blasting Station` for `Pyroblast` removes live one-red answer to blue combo while Ashnod/Phyrexian Altar provide mana-sourcing body sacrifice; `Arcane Denial` for `Wheel of Fortune` can cover another counter but removes seven-card hand reset and Breach fuel. `Big Score` for `Culling the Weak` loses actual instant black ritual that can pay for spell chain and cause Cormela death; `Heat Shimmer` for `Birgi` deletes spell-cast red generation key to Molten/Dualcaster. 30 lands/limited creature count makes cheap spell/card access especially important. Extra generic creature recursion isn't naturally commander-first upgrade.
