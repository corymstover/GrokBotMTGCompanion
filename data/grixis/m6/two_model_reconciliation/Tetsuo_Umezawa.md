## Specific commander plan — why the *tap* matters

**Tetsuo Umezawa** costs {U}{B}{R} and is 3/3 Human Archer, **cannot be target of AURA SPELLS** (equips/equipment abilities still legal). Tetsuo has activated **{U}{B}{B}{R}, {T}: Destroy target TAPPED or BLOCKING creature.** No generic mana in activated cost. Tap/untap and color are critical: repeated destroy requires Tetsuo available to tap, all four specific colored pips, a current legal target and untapping Tetsuo for additional use. "Goad" is not “tap now.” Goaded creatures must attack each combat IF ABLE and attack someone other than goader if able; vigilance preserves untapped, and restrictions like Propaganda can prevent attack. A creature may be BLOCKING and thus Tetsuo-eligible even while untapped. Target legality must be met when activating AND when resolving; removing an Icy-created tap condition with instant untap can make the Tetsuo target illegal unless target still blocking.

The approved 100 deliberately creates target conditions through `Flood`, `Puppeteer`, `Rathi Trapper`, `Fatestitcher`, `Blustersquall`, `Hylda's Crown of Winter`, `Retreat to Coralhelm`, `Frozen Aether`, `Urabrask the Hidden` and political `Geode Rager`, `Fumiko`, `Disrupt Decorum`, `Karazikar`, `Firkraag`, `Kardur`, `Bloodthirsty Blade`. `Aphetto Alchemist`, `Magewright's Stone`, `Thousand-Year Elixir`, `Patriar's Seal`, `Sting`, `Minamo`, `Illusionist's Bracers`, `Rings of Brighthearth` and `Retreat` supply untaps/ability copies for on-identity repeated removals. Commander density here is not generic Grixis removal.

## Exact GrokBot pair: +Icy Manipulator / -Geode Rager

**Disposition NEEDS TESTING, canonical KEEP for now.** Icy is a legitimate commander-first alternate enabler, but currently unproven to be a better **total** strategic contribution than Rager's board-wide goad/political pressure. This is a material disagreement worth testing, not a rules violation or an immediate owner proposal.

### Actual card text, costs and legal windows

- **Icy Manipulator** {4} COLORLESS ARTIFACT; `{1},{T}: Tap target artifact, creature, or land.` Enters as a noncreature artifact, so can activate its tap cost on turn it enters if untapped and mana available. Its effect can be used at instant speed when priority exists, including **during opponent's upkeep** after their untap step, tapping a specific problematic creature and thereby making it legal Tetsuo prey. Tapping a land before an opponent's main phase also has tactical utility, but they can respond with instant abilities/tap for mana; floated mana from upkeep empties as steps end. **Not a dedicated mana producer or hard removal**, and disabling artifact abilities (`Stony Silence`, `Null Rod`) shuts it down. During Tetsuo followthrough, original 4 to cast plus 1 + tap Manipulator and full **U B B R + tap Tetsuo**; on the actual interaction turn that is **five mana across two abilities**, FOUR specific colors, plus presence/untapped readiness of both permanents. The three extra mana cost of Icy versus the cheaper Rathi/Puppeteer active enablers matters.
- **Geode Rager** {4}{R}{R}, 4/3 **first strike** RED elemental. Landfall — “Whenever a land you control enters, **goad each creature target player controls**.” Does NOT instantly tap or destroy them; from an ordinary land on YOUR turn it goads every existing creature a targeted ONE opponent controls until your next turn. Fetchlands may generate distinct land entries (fetch itself then fetched land) and can target two separate players if relevant, at paying land/priority costs. Goaded nonvigilant attackers tap when declared and become Tetsuo-eligible for UBBR destruction on their controller's combat if Tetsuo ready, but are ALREADY attacking another player, which generates indirect political value without consuming your Tetsuo activation. Creatures with vigilance do not become tapped merely because goaded; tapped/nonattacking goaded creatures cannot attack if not able. First strike 4-power creature can survive combat or trade, though it is removal-sensitive and demanding {R}{R} on top of 4 generic.
- **Icy+Rings**: Activate Manipulator for {1},tap, Rings triggered copy may be paid for {2} to tap up to TWO distinct eligible permanents via original+copy. Each legal target must be checked when each ability resolves; this does not give more Tetsuo paid destructive activations. **Icy+Aphetto Alchemist** can untap the ARTIFACT Icy via ready Alchemist, facilitating another {1} tap activation if mana available; applying Alchemist to Icy instead of Tetsuo reduces available commander-untap density. Illusionist's Bracers cannot be equipped to a normal noncreature Icy; do not claim it doubles Icy by itself.
- The present list already has `Flood` as repeat tap for nonfliers (but costs U and cannot touch fliers), `Rathi Trapper` {B},{T} taps any creature (needs a ready creature), `Puppeteer` {U},{T} taps or untaps any target creature, `Retreat to Coralhelm` landfall taps/untaps, and `Hylda's Crown` with direct tap functionality. Manipulator is valuable because as artifact it bypasses summoning sickness and targets artifacts/lands too, NOT because there are no sources of targeted taps in the actual list.

### Comparative strategic cases

**Scenario A: opponent upkeep, a fresh combo creature.** Icy already on field and untapped, Tetsuo ready with UBBR and one more mana. After opponent's untap step on their upkeep, pay 1 and tap Icy, target enemy utility creature; Icy taps it if allowed. Pay UBBR and tap Tetsuo to destroy now-tapped creature, provided it remains tapped at resolution. Opponent can counter ability or instant-untap target before destruction (if no longer blocking); can also activate the target's non-tap ability in response to Icy. **This is an exact real advantage over Geode Rager** which cannot make specific enemy nonattacking creature tapped at will, and should not be dismissed.
  
**Scenario B: Rager with landfall and creature army.** Control Geode Rager and play land. Goad all creatures of target opponent A. Their next combat, four nonvigilance creatures if able must attack another player. They become tapped attackers; Tetsuo with UBBR destroys the MOST IMPORTANT of them, while the other three pressure other opponents for free. With Icy instead of Rager, you could tap one creature at 1 mana, destroy it for UBBR, but those three others remain free to block/attack you, and no free political pressure is generated. This is the commander-first argument for keeping six-mana Geode even though its curve is weak.
  
**Scenario C: tapping land in own upkeep.** Icy taps opposing land during their upkeep; they may float mana for upkeep instants, but cannot retain it until their main phase, so conditional temporary mana denial exists. Geode Rager cannot touch lands, but this utility is more *generic Grixis prison* than Tetsuo identity, a secondary benefit. If opponents rely on mana from untapped land for instants they can respond, so no false strip-all-mana claim.
  
**Scenario D: Tetsuo costs and setup.** Casting Icy for four on T4 with four sources consumes turn; next opponent turn if lands produce UBBR plus one more available generic, can start Tetsuo two-step removal. If commander cost paid T3 but color intensive, waiting another land/mana is real. Geode Rager costs six with RR and then needs a landfall after resolving; on turn-six without seventh land/topdeck/retained land it doesn't goad a creature immediately. Icy is thus materially faster as a target-maker, but the list already has several earlier targeted tap enablers.
  
**Scenario E: vulnerability.** Enemy `Collector Ouphe` / Stony Silence stops Icy's activated ability while Rager landfall still works; creature removal kills Rager while Icy may survive creature sweepers. An opponent with no creature but powerful land artifacts might justify Icy utility but not Tetsuo destruction. A meta full of creature go-wide decks increases Rager's relative value; fast single nonattacking commander/combo creatures increases Icy's. No actual matchup frequencies established.

### Explicit alternative cuts / final evaluation

- `Frozen Aether` {3}{U} causes OPPOSING permanents enter tapped, powerful for Tetsuo if creatures enter while it's around, and denies haste/mana activation; cutting it for Icy would lose **multi-player, continuous** tap generation. It doesn't tap already-present threats, making Icy a conditional complementary tool. No clear superior cut.
- `Fumiko the Lowblood` {2}{R}{R} forces all opposing creatures to attack if able, more reliable earlier (4MV) than Rager's landfall but less targeted goad to attack OTHER players. It may invite attacks against you. Cutting it reduces broad target access and defensive politics; not obviously better than Rager cut.
- `Urabrask the Hidden` {3}{R}{R} grants YOUR creatures haste (Tetsuo early tap access) and opposing creatures ENTER tapped (tap condition). Replacing with Icy loses two rare abilities, though 5MV; not preferred.
- `Disrupt Decorum` 2RR sorcery goads enemy entire board for one turn, potent panic button, single-use; removing it for Icy could improve repeatability but loses zero-upkeep mass political defense; uncertain.
- `Puppeteer`, `Rathi Trapper`, `Flood`, `Aphetto Alchemist`, `Hylda's Crown`, `Sting` and `Magewright's Stone` are lower-cost target-makers/untaps core to Tetsuo. These aren't plausible cuts just because Icy is an artifact.
- `Geode Rager`, the proposed removal, has the MOST expensive native goad source but creates massive board-wide landfall target events. It remains the **leading conditional cut** *if* an effect-aware test finds 6-mana landfall goad too slow to matter; Icy could then increase protected precise commander activations. Cutting `Baleful Strix`, counters, colored sources or `Verity Circle` card advantage instead would cause more damage to consistency. No alternate incumbent established strictly weaker.

**Strongest against KEEP:** A cheap-to-activate ready noncreature Icy may make Tetsuo's ability useful against specific combo utility creatures that never attack, in pods where Geode is a six-mana dead card. A one-turn cast cost gap of {4} versus {4}{R}{R} plus needing landfall could be significant. M5 candidate heuristics often miss exact target creation, so one can genuinely propose after targeted real cases.

**Strongest against replacement:** The approved list already has **multiple single-target taps**, and Rager is a rare **repeatable full-player goad**, structurally important to *Forced Combat / Activated Abilities* identity. Tetsuo's high UBBR activation cost means merely making one more legal target does not ensure you can actually destroy it. A single target tap can duplicate redundancy rather than raise true win/lock rate.

## Disposition and certification

**NEEDS TESTING — keep exact approved Geode Rager while checking whether Icy wins across paid combat/upkeep states.** It's a credible proposed improvement to PRECISE tap enabling but not demonstrated net commander-first upgrade at this cut.
