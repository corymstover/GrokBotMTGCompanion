## Commander-first mechanics: key overlooked interaction
Jeleva {1}{U}{B}{R}, flying 1/3, when enters exile top **X** from EACH player's library, where X is the amount of mana actually spent *to cast Jeleva*. A normal first cast paid 1UBR means **4 cards from each library**, including positions 1–4. On each attack her separate trigger may **cast one instant or sorcery** exiled by THIS Jeleva ETB without paying mana cost (normal timing restriction for type is bypassed, restrictions and additional costs remain). Recasting/blinking creates a new object and exile cohort, cannot retrieve previous Jeleva's exiled cohort. Zero-mana free casts or non-cast ETB can reduce X. In a 4-player game this is a uniquely powerful topdeck tutor/exile engine, not merely an efficient generic Grixis spell combo commander.

**Long-Term Plans {2}{U}** is a legal instant that searches for ANY card, shuffles, then puts selected card **third from library top**, NOT into hand. Its exact position is deliberate for Jeleva: 
1. During previous opponent end step or your own prior priority window, cast Plans for an instant or sorcery to be free-cast (e.g. Peer into the Abyss, Mind's Desire, Mnemonic Betrayal, Yawgmoth's Will, Doomsday, or Wheel of Fortune).
2. If you cast Jeleva spending **at least 3 mana** and neither you nor opponent draws/mills/shuffles that new top three before her ETB resolves, position 3 is among X cards exiled. On regular first normal Jeleva cast with 4 mana spent, your staged card is captured.
3. Give Jeleva haste (on-list `Lightning Greaves`, `Torch Courier`, `Arena of Glory`) or survive to next turn; her attack trigger may cast this staged instant/sorcery without cost. The paid tutor 3 plus Jeleva 4 plus haste/attack costs and opposition are NOT trivial, but it can convert a selected 7–10 MV sorcery into a legal free cast; **no arbitrary two-draw wait** is needed.
4. If Plans stages a land/creature, Jeleva exiles that card but CANNOT free-cast it via her attack; choose spell types deliberately. If you cast Plans after Jeleva has already resolved ETB, it cannot retroactively add exiled cards. Opponent shuffle/top disruption and countering Jeleva interrupt line.

**Intuition {2}{U}** is also legal instant: search **three cards**, reveal, TARGET opponent chooses one to your hand; the other TWO go to your graveyard; shuffle. Thus delivers graveyard fuel for **Underworld Breach**, **Yawgmoth's Will**, LED/Brain Freeze plans and opponent-choice-resistant 3-piece graveyard setups, with no requirement to connect Jeleva. Higher generic turbo performance is plausible; usable if target opponent exists and can't remove the targeted player. But Intuition does **NOT** deterministically stage a chosen card in the top X for Jeleva (indeed it shuffles library, disrupting prior topdeck setup). It cannot guarantee that one specific important spell enters hand (opponent chooses), and two others going to grave risk graveyard hate. This is a different **graveyard combo** engine, not a strict improvement over Jeleva's top-staging tutor.

## Cost and alternatives

- Both cards MV3 and blue, so no direct curve/color changes, but replacing top staging with arbitrary pile tutor changes the **identity engine** and the deck's vulnerability to grave hate.
- Existing Grim Monolith, LED, Ad Nauseam, Breach, Doomsday, Thassa's Oracle and Consultation/Pact already support generic fringe-cEDH compact combo. Intuition can heighten that package; Long-Term Plans uniquely binds strong free spell to Jeleva's normal cast.
- Existing `Mystical Tutor` and `Personal Tutor` place instants/sorceries on TOP; `Brainstorm`, `Scroll Rack`, `Sensei's Divining Top` also manipulate the top. These can stage Jeleva when timed properly, but do not make this off-beat **third-from-top tutor** redundant in every state; e.g., a library-top revealed card may be drawn off-turn and absent by cast. Jeleva exiles first X at once; tutor can stage position3 with no further draw.
- Other candidate cuts: `Torch Courier` might be vulnerable one-use haste but allows immediate Jeleva attack; `Arena of Glory` occupies a land slot and accelerates haste; `Borne Upon a Wind` supports flexible spell timing independent of the Jeleva trigger, not obvious dead. `Fellwar Stone` improves 27-land cEDH color access; `Wishclaw Talisman` unrestricted one-card tutor and can be bounced/reused. None shown strictly worse than Intuition in this approved 100.
- Existing `Mnemonic Betrayal` is already in Jeleva and makes high-MV free exile payoff; it is not automatically the right cut either. `Long-Term Plans` is a commander-specific protection slot against having big spells trapped in hand by using the next cast to exile them from deck.
