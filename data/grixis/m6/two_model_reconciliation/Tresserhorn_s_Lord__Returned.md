## Actual commander, role mix and meaningful mechanical checks

`Tresserhorn's Lord, Returned` costs **{2}{U}{B}{R}**, is a 10/4 legendary Zombie with indestructible and trample. It enters with a triggered requirement to provide **three creature resources, 3 life, and three cards for a target opponent** or else give up the commander. This is a triggered effect on battlefield entry, not casting cost. Each creature sacrificed triggers the commander's mandatory `draw a card`. Paying full entry yields **three separate card-draw triggers**. If the commander itself is sacrificed, including for its own entry condition, its own sacrifice **also** produces a card-draw trigger using last-known-information lookback, expressly verified in [official Wizards MBC rulings](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/mystery-booster-commander-edition-release-notes). Indestructible doesn't protect against a sacrifice requirement.

The 100 contains genuine cheap fodder (`Bitterblossom`, `Mogg War Marshal`, `Jadar`, `Kher Keep`, `Ophiomancer`, `Chittering Witch`, `Weaponcraft Enthusiast`); outlets (`Goblin Bombardment`, `Phyrexian Altar`, `Ashnod's Altar`, `Viscera Seer`, `Carrion Feeder`, `Yawgmoth`); reusability (`Gravecrawler`, `Bloodghast`, `Pitiless Plunderer`, `Warren Soultrader`); draw payoffs (`The Locust God`, `Chasm Skulker`, `Skullclamp`); drain payoffs (`Blood Artist`, `Zulaport Cutthroat`, `Bastion of Remembrance`, `Marionette Apprentice`). The plan is generate multiple bodies BEFORE the commander, turn those resources into card access, and use 10-power combat and token synergies; not homogenized Grixis turbo combo.

Representative exact interactions:
- With **The Locust God**, drawing produces one Insect each card; a free creature-cost outlet can restart the process, and Bombardment converts each iteration to damage. The real physical library is finite; it is NOT automatically infinitely repeatable without a draw-replacement/shuffle mechanism, and opponents retain responses.
- **Gravecrawler** may be cast from graveyard while Tresserhorn or another Zombie exists; Phyrexian Altar can produce B by using it, paying for next cast, with one commander draw trigger each time. `Warren Soultrader` pays LIFE per use, so requires a matching life-gain support before sustained repetition.
- **Not of This World** discounts to zero for targeting the ten-power Lord but doesn't counter untargeted mass effects; `Nightscape Familiar` reduces only one generic from the commander, not twice because it is both U and R.
- **Blasphemous Edict**'s requirement to sacrifice thirteen creatures can include your 10/4 indestructible Lord. To keep him needs at least THIRTEEN OTHER creatures. This actual anti-synergy is documented and still retained under owner's locked baseline; if used at fewer bodies, might convert lost board to cards but increases commander recast tax. No false claim it is a one-sided wrath.

## GrokBot weakest-slot confrontation and alternatives

**Infantry Shield** is particularly tied to ten power: with correct equipment/equip timing and attack, its mobilize can create a substantial number of temporary Warrior creatures, potentially TEN from ten power. If those bodies are subsequently sacrificed while Lord remains, the commander can draw once per creature; a sacrifice outlet can also use them proactively. Its drawback is equip/setup and risk of losing commander before combat/end step. A generic cost-reducer might cast the commander earlier, but deleting Shield without an exact on-identity, source-paid stronger replacement erases a distinctive ten-power-to-fodder engine. **KEEP in absence of proven improvement**.

**Chasm Skulker** grows with actual draws and later makes creature tokens if it DIES, not if exiled or bounced. Draw from Tresserhorn makes that capacity significant but requires time and protection, and the resulting bodies arrive only after Skulker death. The existing low-MV immediate fodder suite is faster. Reasonable potential cut in fast metagames if a specific cheaper creature can supply requisite three bodies by turn four; GrokBot provided no named upgrade. Alternatives `Endrek Sahr` (sustained token casting but five MV), `Blasphemous Edict` (self-damaging sweep vs cheap answer), `Bitterbloom Bearer` (on-list token role) were recognized rather than assuming Skulker is uniquely mandatory. **KEEP with risk flagged**, not a new swap proposal.

**Source-paid opposing scenarios:** (A) By turn five commander has three legal expendable bodies: satisfy ETB, opponent draws3, generate three mandatory draw triggers; if opponent removes one body while ETB is on stack, two remaining cannot satisfy requirement and commander sacrifices itself, drawing1. (B) With 10-power Lord, targeted counterspell protection may cost 0 through Not of This World; it doesn't stop an edict. (C) Existing Blasphemous Edict with five total controlled creatures removes even the indestructible commander; commander triggers based on sacrificial creatures can still draw. (D) Gear-based Warrior-making on combat gives meaningful pressure, but no immediate cards if Lord is removed before later sacrifice events and no other outlet exists. (E) Chasm Skulker counters accrued from real draw matter only if it later dies; exile is a real opponent answer.
