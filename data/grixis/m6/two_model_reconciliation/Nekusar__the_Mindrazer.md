## Commander-first rules, precise effects

**Nekusar** {2}{U}{B}{R}, 2/4 Zombie Wizard: each player's draw step triggers one extra card draw; each **opponent drawing a card** causes **one damage from Nekusar**, a separate triggered ability. His normal opponent draw-step damage begins on regular draw; his extra-draw trigger produces another draw later if he survives. These draw-based damage events can trigger **Ghyrson Starn** (two more damage for each separate exactly-one-damage event), and Nekusar's own Wizard type allows **Wizard's Staff** and Harmonic Prodigy to produce extra triggered instances. **Krang, the All-Powerful** increases instances of on-draw damage triggered abilities of controlled permanents; it does not turn forced life loss from Phyrexian Tyranny into exactly-one damage or retroactively alter drawn cards.

**Forced Fruition** (Lorwyn) {4}{U}{U}, BLUE ENCHANTMENT, official Commander legal: "**Whenever an opponent casts a spell, that player draws seven cards.**" The draw is **MANDATORY** and happens when the triggered ability resolves, while the opponent's triggering spell is normally still on stack; it need not resolve or deal damage. Seven actual drawn cards make seven Nekusar damage triggers, one for each draw, assuming Nekusar was controlled when those draw events happened. An opponent's spell cast from ANY zone qualifies (unless other spell-specific limitations intervene), including an instant cast in response to some later damage abilities. This is dramatically different from a one-card draw on draw step; but **opponents can draw answers** before Nekusar damage triggers resolve and may respond to those triggers, subject to priority and further Forced Fruition triggers if they cast spells.

**Kami of the Crescent Moon** {U}{U}, 1/3 legendary Spirit creature, Commander legal: at start **each** player's draw step that player draws an **additional** card. One opponent turn with Kami + Nekusar still alive yields the regular turn-based draw, plus one from Kami and one from Nekusar: **3 total draws**, thus **3 Nekusar 1-damage triggers** with no other multipliers. Without Kami, there are two, so Kami adds only **one** draw/damage per opponent's draw step; it does NOT copy Nekusar's damage trigger or make a second Nekusar. On **YOUR** draw step, Kami supplies an extra card without Nekusar damaging you directly; it may however create severe self-damage/life-loss from on-list **Spiteful Visions**, **Phyrexian Tyranny** and their Krang/Ghyrson multipliers, if those are controlled. Kami is fragile/cheap and can act as a blocker; Fruition neither draws cards for you nor affects draw steps.

**Timing and interactions:**
1. Both Kami's and Nekusar's "at beginning of draw step" triggers go on stack *after* ordinary first-turn draw; if you control both, you can order their relevant triggers. Normal draws made while Nekusar is present make damage triggers regardless of whose extra-draw instruction caused them.
2. Forced Fruition triggers when opponent **casts** (not when spell resolves); drawing seven occurs on resolution of Fruition trigger above triggering spell, and creates seven separate Nekusar triggers put on stack after its full resolution. Killing Nekusar **before the seven cards are drawn** prevents the NEW draw-damage triggers. Killing it after the seven are drawn does not erase already-triggered damage. If enchantment itself is removed in response after its trigger is created, that existing draw trigger still ordinarily resolves.
3. With Nekusar + Ghyrson, seven draws can produce **7 Nekusar damage + 14 Ghyrson damage = 21** against caster from ONE Forced Fruition trigger if effects resolve. At 40-life target without prevention, two separate opponent spell casts can be enough to kill via these triggers, but precise survival/priority, preventing damage, opposing spells, board state and life totals matter. With Wizard Staff, Harmonic Prodigy or Krang amplifying Nekusar, the multiplier can be much larger. Those do NOT themselves increase Forced Fruition's own spell-trigger frequency, absent an effect specifically doubling enchantment abilities.
4. Another danger: opponent has 6 cards remaining library, casts a spell with Forced Fruition active; instruction draws seven (if replacements don't alter), attempted draw from empty causes the opponent to lose the game at the next state-based-actions check, independent of Nekusar damage. Opponents with ample library instead receive seven extra hand cards and may exploit them if the damage engine is removed, so calling Fruition automatic draw-death is unsupported.
5. No **Notion Thief** presently in the 100; adding one would divert opponent's extra draws and suppress Nekusar's damage. Effects that replace or prevent draws need their actual wording considered; don't mix card-put-to-hand with draw. `Teferi's Puzzle Box` bottoms cards but then instructs true draws; it doesn't discard itself.

## Exact add/cut comparison

**GroKBot proposed:** `+1 Forced Fruition / -1 Kami of the Crescent Moon`. **Reconciliation disposition: NEEDS TESTING, default KEEP.** The add is both legal and genuinely commander-first; the **actual cut has unresolved power/consistency tradeoffs** that cannot be decided from seven-draw arithmetic alone.

**For replacing Kami:** 
- Current shell already has Nekusar's extra draw, `Anvil of Bogardan`, `Fevered Visions`, `Spiteful Visions`, `Stormfist Crusader`, `Jace's Archivist`, repeat wheel effects, `Wheel and Deal`, `Teferi's Puzzle Box`, `Whispering Madness`, `Ghyrson`, `Wizard's Staff`, `Krang` and `Harmonic Prodigy`. Kami's one extra draw **on draw step** is narrower/less lethal in later turns with multiple damage multipliers than Fruition's seven draw shocks per opponent spell. More than one of these enablers already assures extra draw density.
- Fruition is not a symmetrical group-hug: only **opponent spell casts** trigger it, so its own controller needn't draw extra cards and die to their own Spiteful/Tyranny/Ghyrson effects. With protected Nekusar, it converts opponents trying to advance the game or respond to a wheel into high-risk actions before their original spells resolve.
- It is an enchantment rather than a 1/3 creature; in creature-sweeper-heavy pods can survive board wipes that kill Nekusar/Kami, but **without a remaining Nekusar/other opponent-draw damage source, Fruition actually refills opponents instead of punishing them**. Other independent punishers like Sheoldred, Underworld Dreams, Fate Unraveler, Scrawling Crawler, Kederekt Parasite or Razorkin Needlehead have their own precise trigger restrictions.
- Explicit opportunity: two opponent cast spells under protected Nekusar+Ghyrson can output 42 damage to the caster from the mandatory 14 draws if all triggers resolve, often lethal. A two-mana Kami cannot do this without many turn cycles and/or wheel.

**Against exact replacement:**
- SIX MANA DOUBLE BLUE for Forced Fruition compared to TWO MANA DOUBLE BLUE for Kami is **+4 mana** and late setup. In an already five-MV commander deck with `Peer into the Abyss`, Consecrated Sphinx, Niv-Mizzet and multiple expensive engines, drawing Fruition early with insufficient mana may be materially worse than drawing the immediately playable Kami. Current 35 lands/fast rocks do not prove protected T5–T6 UU availability and successful six-mana resolve.
- Kami also draws its controller an extra card each own draw step, finding wheels/protection/lands before Nekusar comes down; Fruition gives **zero self-card advantage** and offers opponent card access even when Nekusar is absent. Kami's body can absorb damage/block and be cast T2; removal vulnerability is real but it advances actual commander-first setup during games that end before T6.
- Against spell-light opponents already out of cards or constrained by Rule of Law or post-wheel locks, Fruition may trigger only zero or one time before the game ends, while Kami triggers every available draw step. Forced Fruition can be disastrous if opponent exiles/bounces commander in response BEFORE it resolves or uses newly drawn protection to thwart subsequent damage (existing Nekusar triggers persist if already created, but future drawings are unpunished). It also gives opponents large hands for `Force of Will` and other free counter/instant protection on later turns.
- Enchantment can be countered, Narset/Thief-style effects nullify much of opponent actual drawing, and Rule of Law limits multiple opponent spell casts but doesn't stop draw-step triggers. Hexproof/shroud player cannot prevent un-targeted seven draws, but effects preventing drawing/damage can.
- **Power arithmetic alone** doesn't establish that the 6MV card improves cumulative win rate more than early extra draw and existing wheel-density; M5 candidate screening does not model opponent draw triggers or self-damage fully.

## Alternative cuts: scrutinized instead of automatically deleting Kami

- **Fevered Visions**: {1}{U}{R} three-MV enchantment; own-player end step each player draws one, opponents with 4+ cards may take 2 damage (trigger specifics and hand condition checked in source authored review). Replacing it with Fruition improves opponent cast punishment but removes a lower-cost predictable draw trigger engine that Nekusar uses and can provide your own resources. **Plausible alternative cut ONLY if an actual match demonstrates slow delayed/group-draw underperformance**.
- **Spiteful Visions**: {2}{B}{R} group extra card and EXACT one damage per draw, but **also damages the controller**, and on-list Ghyrson amplifies each controlled 1-damage source hitting you. This is a significant true self-kill risk. **Potentially better cut than Kami in self-damage-heavy metagames**, but removing Spiteful also gives up an independent all-player draw-ping finisher, which powers many wheel kills even when Nekusar is dead. Compare `+Ophidian Eye / -Spiteful` or `-Phyrexian Tyranny` mitigation alongside Fruition; no head-to-head measured win superiority yet.
- **Phyrexian Tyranny**: {U}{B}{R} enchantment: **whenever ANY player draws a card, that player loses 2 life unless they pay {2}**. It triggers once per actual drawn card, including yours; seven self-draws after a wheel can be catastrophic if you cannot afford the seven separate payments. Removing it may increase own survivability, but replacing with a 6MV opponent-cast draw engine reduces cheap independent permanent punishment; requires actual paid simulation of your own life as well as opponents' hand sizes. **Don't assume Forced Fruition objectively improves role density**.
- **Consecrated Sphinx** and **Peer into the Abyss**: high-MV but major live finishes/access; Sphinx's draws are optional and peer can target opponent for lethal with Nekusar; removing these may cripple endgame more than cutting Kami despite curve benefits. **Rejected as easy auto-cuts**.
- **An Offer You Can't Refuse, Swan Song, mana fixers, Windfall, Wheel of Fortune, Breach, Ghyrson and Wizard's Staff:** protect paid wheel lines, commander and signature multipliers; cannot be traded for six-mana symmetrical-on-opponent-cast draw just because spellbook expects it. Retain.

## Representative counterfactuals (source-paid thought experiments, NOT measured games)

**A: turn-two early draw engine.** T2 have UU. Cast Kami. On each later player's draw step, if he survives each player gets +1 card, and you increase hand/protection volume before Nekusar is cast. Force Fruition remains unusable until 4UU, and even then does not increase your own draw. If table has meaningful removal and finishes T4–5, Kami can provide more relevant actual influence.
  
**B: T6 protected Nekusar + Fruition.** Control Nekusar + Ghyrson + Fruition, opponents at 40. Opponent casts instant. Fruition trigger added above spell; opponent can respond first, but assuming no relevant removal, draws seven, yields seven Nek 1-damage triggers each followed by Ghyrson 2, totaling **21**. If opponent casts another spell while effects live, potential 21 more. These are real draw triggers but not guaranteed player death if hand contains answers or some draws are replaced.
  
**C: remove Nek before draw.** Opponent casts removal at Nek, triggering Fruition. With Nek still on battlefield as spell cast, Fruition normally resolves first, drawing seven and generating damage. But opponent/other players may cast or resolve **another removal** in response to Fruition trigger, killing Nek before cards drawn; first trigger then draws seven without Nek damage, and the original removal spell remains on stack. They may thereby exploit seven free cards. The triggering spell is **not required to resolve** for Fruition to draw.
  
**D: prior self-damage risk.** Current Kami causes one extra YOUR draw each draw step. If `Spiteful Visions` on board under your control with Ghyrson, that extra draw can create 1 Spiteful damage +2 Ghyrson to **you** and potential additional Krang/Tyranny effects, unlike Nekusar's own opponent-only damage. A protected additional-draw asset is thus not automatically safe; choose setup based on own life, not just opponent 40-to-zero calculation.
  
**E: opponent cast control lock.** Opponent plays few spells each turn / Rule of Law; Fruition may cause one packet of 7 only, and extra card grants opponent options. A low-MV Kami + wheels draws and punishes in a longer attrition table even when no one casts extra spells.

## Final finding and measured uncertainty

**Disposition: NEEDS TESTING — retain exact locked Kami, no proposal for immediate owner upgrade yet.** GrokBot correctly identified a **legitimate high-ceiling, identity-positive** card absent from approved list; ChatGPT's generic KEEP did **not** fully adjudicate this 6MV opponent-cast packet against current 2MV draw setup and Ghyrson self-harm. The existence of a 21-damage packet (or larger Staff/Prodigy/Krang bursts) makes Fruition a high-priority challenger, not a proven rate/consistency improvement. There is no reason to eliminate a working T2 card without facing realistic table speeds and own life budget.

**Highest-value effect-aware targeted test:** compare source-paid actual casting by T4–T7, number of opponents' spells AFTER Fruition resolves, intervening counters/removal, self-card access before Nekusar, damage produced pre-first wheel, opponent answers gained from new draws, Ghyrson/Krang damage to caster under self-draw, and 3-vs-1 threat scenarios with `Kami` and `Fevered/Spiteful/Tyranny` alternative cuts. Observational EDHREC or generic goldfish pass not enough. No such actual live multiplayer simulation was performed in this artifact.
