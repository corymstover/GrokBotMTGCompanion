**Commander identity:** Ashad grants **casualty 2 to FIRST NONLEGENDARY ARTIFACT SPELL cast per turn**. A 2+ power creature pays that spell's additional sacrifice cost as CAST; a resulting spell-copy of artifact permanent resolves as TOKEN, not another CAST. `Twinning Staff` adds copy, `Shimmer Myr` grants off-turn flash for artifact spells, `Biotransference` turns own creature spells to artifacts without removing legendary type, `Poetic Ingenuity` 3/1 tokens and Biotransference 2/2 tokens can pay only on LATER casts after entering. 1/1 `Thopter Spy Network` tokens alone do not pay casualty2. `Ichor Wellspring`, `Solemn Simulacrum`, `Wurmcoil Engine`, `Portal to Phyrexia`, `Worldwalker Helm`, `Urza`, `Krark-Clan Ironworks` and damage/recursion payoffs create artifact-copy gameplay unlike generic Grixis goodstuff. Own 30 lands / color pips plus removal of commander or fodder are substantive weaknesses.

**Material new pair: +Three Steps Ahead / -Artificer Class.** Spree `Three Steps Ahead` costs `{U}` plus `{3}` for copy-your-controlled-artifact/creature mode: **3U**, instant; LEGAL Commander/on-color. With `Worldwalker Helm` and `Urza, Lord High Artificer` already controlled, copying Helm as token generates Map via existing Helm replacement, giving a token Helm that can be paid `{1}{U},{T}` to copy artifact tokens, generate further token Helms/Maps and tap artifacts through Urza for U. This can bootstrap conditional token/blue mana payoff IMMEDIATELY, rather than waiting for `Artificer Class` to level THREE and trigger at own end step. Opponent may counter the instant or remove Urza/Helm in response; the token copies are not casts and do not trigger Ashad. Other Spree modes supply temporary interaction/draw at their respective extra costs.

**Loss:** `Artificer Class` is a 1U lasting artifact-cast cost discount and, once upgraded with additional paid sorcery-speed mana (not free), copies an artifact EVERY eligible end step. It feeds Ashad's cheap first artifact CAST and provides repeatability when Helm/Urza are not both present. Three Steps is one-shot NONARTIFACT instant, cannot use Ashad casualty, and can be stuck in hand without target. This is a genuine combo-speed vs artifact-identity/long-game tension, not a strict upgrade by mana arithmetic alone.

**Alternate cuts:** `Thopter Spy Network` creates weak 1/1 fodder but helps artifact-body combat/draw; replacing it with Steps would keep Class and lose independent flying draws, less directly aligned with solving expensive Class bootstrap. `Padeem` shields artifacts and commander but doesn't stop mass non-targeted hate; cutting removes vital resilience. `KCI` enables actual artifact-token consumption/death triggers; `Twinning Staff` specifically doubles casualty spell-copy output, so don't replace either with generic artifact ramp. `Sai, Master Thopterist` versus Spy Network is separately plausible cast-token question but still creates 1/1 nonqualifying casualty bodies.
