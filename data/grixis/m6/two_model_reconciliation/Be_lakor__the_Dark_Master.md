## Commander-first mechanics, actual payoff

Be'lakor costs **{3}{U}{B}{R}**, 6/5 flying Demon Noble. Its own ETB `Prince of Chaos` draws X and loses X life based on Demon count **when ability RESOLVES**, including itself if still around; own ETB doesn't trigger own `Lord of Torment`. The latter triggers on **ANOTHER Demon entering** and has **entering Demon deal damage equal to THAT Demon's power** to chosen legal target, not Be'lakor's power. Existing `Rite of Replication`, `Spark Double`, `Panharmonicon`, `Roaming Throne`, `Molten Echoes`, `Reflections of Littjara`, `Ghostly Flicker`, `Whip of Erebos`, `Sneak Attack`, `Herald of Slaanesh`, `Urza's Incubator` and Demon payoffs support more enters, duplicate triggered effects and life management. `Liliana's Contract` needs FOUR differently NAMED Demons at beginning upkeep; five tokens all named Be'lakor do not count as five different Demon names.

**Critical Rite proof** (clearer than GrokBot's “each copy triggers Lord separately”): control JUST original Be'lakor and cast `Rite of Replication` kicked paying `{7}{U}{U}` total NINE mana, target original. Five token Be'lakor copies enter simultaneously. Original Lord sees FIVE other Demons -> FIVE Lord triggers. Each incoming copy sees the other FOUR tokens entering simultaneously -> 5×4 TWENTY triggers, **25 Lord triggers** total. They source damage from incoming 6-power Demons; up to **150 damage across legal targets** if all trigger objects resolve, without reduction/protection/pumping. Legend rule forces all but one of six legendary cards/tokens to leave before triggers go to stack, but the already-triggered Lord damage can use last-known power; five additional Prince ETB draws are based on **surviving Demon count when Prince resolves**. If only ONE original Be'lakor survives, five Prince triggers cause five single-card draws and five life loss, NOT 30. Opponent can remove targeted original in RESPONSE to Rite to make target illegal and entirely prevent copy event. If opponents use protection, hexproof/no valid target or prevent damage, 150 is not an unconditional table kill. Damage-trigger targets are chosen as triggers placed on stack, not after combat.

**Other exact pitfalls:** `Spark Double` enters as NONLEGENDARY Be'lakor with +1/+1 so original sees an entering 7-power Demon (trigger damage) while both Lords survive for next entrants. `Panharmonicon` doubles both Prince ETB and Lord enter triggers, including self-life cost; `Roaming Throne` choosing Demon adds triggered occurrences for **OTHER** Demon creatures, not its own unrelated ability. `Whip` lifelink on Demon damage can offset Prince life cost but Prince LIFE LOSS itself does not deal damage and can't trigger lifelink. `Bloodletter of Aclazotz` doubles OPPONENT life loss on YOUR turn, never halves Be'lakor's own ETB life costs. `Heartless Summoning` -1/-1 reduces ETB damage from affected Demons, and may eliminate fragile tokens. `Vilis` triggers extra draws when you lose life, risks decking or losing to one's own resource engine. `Molten Echoes` only triggers for NONtoken Demon creature entries, no spontaneous loop from generated token.

## Meaningful rival cards and weakest-slot challenge

- `Sakashima of a Thousand Faces` as additional stable nonlegendary Be'lakor clone improves sustained multi-Lord damage and retains legend exceptions, but expensive setup (4 MV plus six-mana commander), already `Spark Double`, `Rite`, `Reflections`, `Molten Echoes`, clone targets vulnerable. Alternate cut `Balor` or an expensive middle Demon would reduce Demon name/attack draw content and can't be called net positive without turn/uptime trials. Conditional **HOLD**, not an exact proposal.
- M5 `Abyssal Persecutor` for `Sower of Discord` adds cheap large Demon BUT “you can't win” until it leaves, with few dedicated ways to remove it; strong anti-win risk. `Bloodcrusher of Khorne` for `Archfiend of Despair` sacrifices existing endstep automatic life-loss multiplier that does not require combat; reject. `Ancestral Statue` for `Roaming Throne` removes unique Demon-type double-trigger multiplier; Statue is non-Demon. `Brainstorm` for `Unholy Annex // Ritual Chamber` loses recurring draw and black Demon token creation; not an easy upgrade. `Dimir Signet` for `Fellwar Stone` changes color conditionality and mana input in 36-land early colors; no obvious gain. `Curse-Marred Demon` observed in newer rankings had insufficient independent Oracle text/legality review to justify recommending it — not imagined.
- Weaknesses not denied: six-MV commander plus high-MV Demons often require cost cheating, 36 lands include ETB-tapped sources, graveyard/board wipe hate and Torpor Orb stop entry payoffs, and the commander drawing many/life-losing when recast can be unsafe. Do not remove interaction for extra unverified big Demon simply because source EDHREC popularity.

**Counterfactuals:** Own turn nine mana with existing Lord protected -> kicked Rite can create finite high-damage victory; opposing spot removal on Lord while Rite still targeting original fizzles cast. A four-player midgame with only three lands and colorless cost reducer cannot reliably cast 3UBR commander; another clone in hand is dead without Lord. Torpor Orb on opponent board prevents both Prince and Lord ETB triggers; `Cyclonic Rift` / `Bedevil` interaction necessary. If Be'lakor Prince would lose 6 life at 4 life, draw itself may kill you; Whip only gains life from actual Demon damage once resolved. These direct opponent windows outweigh abstract “more Demon” ranking.
