## Davros game plan, exact costs/timing

`Davros, Dalek Creator` {1}{U}{B}{R} artifact creature, seeks **each opponent having lost 3 OR MORE life THIS TURN** by the time its OWN END STEP trigger resolves. Its actual end-step effect creates a menace 3/3 Dalek under the threshold conditions and presents qualifying opponents with **villainous choice**; opponents elect discard versus giving Davros's controller a card, so no “we force discard and draw” claim. A player who lost exactly 3 from damage or life loss counts, while a player who lost only 2 does not. Attacking Daleks with afflict/menace, `Boltwave` all-opponent 3-damage, `Court of Ambition`, `Torment of Hailfire`, `Unlicensed Disintegration` if included, `Doomsday Confluence` etc create risk. `Entropic Battlecruiser` stationed 1+ lets discarding a real card cause THREE life loss, but doesn't retroactively make a player qualify for ALREADY resolving Davros if damage/discard happens too late. Commander isn't generic black burn; artifact Dalek menace + group loss + choice loop is natural Bracket4 strategy. Existing `The Dalek Emperor`, `Dalek Squadron`, `Auton Soldier`, `Scientist Supreme of A.I.M.`, `Padeem`, `Roaming Throne`, `Strionic Resonator`, `The Valeyard`, `Labyrinth Raptor`, `Genesis of the Daleks` preserve Doctor Who choices/copies and artifact layer.

**Material direct upgrade candidate #1** `+Unlicensed Disintegration / -Weeping Angel` (or another situational removal). The instant costs **{1}{B}{R}**, destroys target creature if legal, and IF you control an artifact, it deals **3 damage to that creature's controller** as part of resolution. A live artifact is likely with Davros and Daleks but not guaranteed under Null Rod/board wipe (Null Rod doesn't stop static control of artifact). Cast on YOUR TURN, with legal enemy creature, makes that ONE enemy qualify for endstep and answers a threat in same three mana. On OPPONENT TURN the damage cannot satisfy your future turn's “this turn” unless Davros's endstep somehow that turn; qualify own endstep requires own-turn cast. If target becomes illegal before spell resolves, none of spell resolves and **zero 3 damage**; not guaranteed all three opponents. Cutting `Weeping Angel` loses Alien-type artifact-creature/flash/combat identity; cutting free `Snuff Out` would sacrifice key zero-mana response. Opponent no creatures yields no legal target; an interaction-rich pod can justify, but not universal.

**Material direct candidate #2** `+Ingenious Artillerist / -Sycorax Commander` or `Weeping Angel`. Artillerist {2}{R} fragile 3/1 NONartifact Human: whenever ONE OR MORE artifacts enter under your control, it deals damage to EACH opponent equal to number of artifacts in that event. ONE entry per trigger does ONE; three distinct 1-artifact events accumulate 3 per opponent, while ONE batch of three artifacts causes 3 to each immediately. Real Dalek/Treasure/Construct ETBs can feed this BEFORE Davros's end step; an artifact Davros creates AS A RESULT of end-step threshold cannot retroactively satisfy that trigger if no earlier loss. It reaches all players more reliably than a one-target kill ONLY when actual three artifact entries happen while Artillerist lives. Cutting `Sycorax Commander` removes Doctor Who body/group-slug synergy, cutting `Weeping Angel` removes flash/combat/copy lines; not objectively stronger without table ETB timing tests.

**Other cut alternatives rejected:** `Dire Fleet Ravager` loses own life and has three-mana group-loss ETB at later game but not artifact/Dalek; replacing `Labyrinth Raptor` removes concrete menace destruction pressure. `Unstoppable Slasher` needs attacking and connecting ONE player, not guaranteed all three. `Havoc Festival`, `Fraying Omnipotence`, `Heartless Hidetsugu` are risky symmetry and/or sluggish, and replacing `Genesis`, `Doomsday Confluence`, `Torment` erodes Doctor Who/Torment multi-target finish. `Time Sieve` paying FIVE artifacts does not form repeated extra turns from one random Dalek every end step. Generic M5 goldfish doesn't simulate multi-opponent threshold/choices.

**Finite adversarial outcomes:** (A) Davros in battlefield, own-turn `Boltwave` resolves 3 to each opponent -> all three qualify endstep even if no attacks, unless prevented/replaced. (B) Opponent A dies? Their later choice no longer exists; don't count postdeath card reward. (C) Unlicensed targeting opponent A creature with artifact controller resolves own turn -> A takes3, not B/C; if target bounced before resolution no damage. (D) Three single artifacts enter while Artillerist controlled, separately trigger each opp1, totaling3; but one enters after Davros threshold check too late doesn't count for same trigger. (E) artifact removal/lack of UBR can prevent commander and payoffs while opponents cast combo; cannot claim user-safe all-3 qualification from existence of enchantment.
