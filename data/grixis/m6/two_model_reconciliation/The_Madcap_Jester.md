## Actual commander-specific Oracle/rules and strategy

**The Madcap Jester** {1}{U}{B}{R}, Clown Wizard 3/2: **on ATTACK**, each opponent exiles top card of their library, independent of combat damage. **When spell or ability YOU CONTROL causes one or more cards to be exiled from an opponent's LIBRARY**, triggers permission to PLAY those cards **until the end of YOUR NEXT TURN**, spending mana as though any type to cast them. This means actual card(s) are **in exile**, not in hand; only ordinary legal timing/cost conditions apply (including land drop quota). An opponent choosing to exile their own cards independent of your spells/abilities is not automatically attributable to you, nor is your own library exiling. One action exiling N cards triggers once for that action; multiple separate spell/ability events can each trigger.

**Dragon's Smile**, future legal MBC non-commander card already present: expensive **7MV** Vehicle, once during each of your turns may pay **{0} INSTEAD of casting mana cost** for ONE spell cast from exile; separate ability exiles opponent library cards on combat damage. Creature-vehicle crew requirements, timing and additional costs still matter. This is commander-specific access/mana relief, not a must-cut merely for high mana or temporary project exception.

Approved library-exile engine includes `Extract`, `Sadistic Sacrament`, `Tasha's Hideous Laughter`, `Bitter Ordeal`, `Jester's Cap/Scepter`, `Ashiok, Nightmare Weaver`, `Inside Information`, `Psychic Surgery`, `Circu` and commander attacks; and payoffs `Bilbo`, `Don Andres`, `Prosper`, `Nalfeshnee`, `Wild-Magic Sorcerer`, `Passionate Archaeologist`, `Dragon's Smile`. ### Exile-cost distinctions

- `Inside Information` itself allows an alternative-cost pay LIFE equal to exiled spell mana value for THIS TURN; Jester provides separate ordinary-mana permission with mana type relaxation through end of your next turn. `Dragon's Smile` supplies a third once-per-your-own-turn alternative-cost permission to cast a spell from exile for {0}. **Choose ONE alternative cost**; never combine “pay {0} + pay life” as if multiplicative. Additional costs and original timing still apply.
- `Demonic Consultation` and `Tainted Pact` exile from your OWN library to find secondary win and **do not** trigger Jester's opponent-library permission. They remain a secondary compact finish, not proof the commander itself provides that win.
- `Bitter Ordeal` gravestorm copies are not CAST. Each copy resolving can exile opponent-library card and grant Jester's permission if both effects qualify and Jester present; the copy itself doesn't increase storm count.
