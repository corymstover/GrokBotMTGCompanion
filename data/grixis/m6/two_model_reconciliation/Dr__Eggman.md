**Commander Oracle/timing:** Dr. Eggman costs **{2}{U}{B}{R}**, 3/6 flying Scientist. At start of **YOUR END STEP** you draw one; then EACH opponent in turn order can DISCARD a card OR let you **PUT from your hand** a creature/artifact card with qualifying Construct/Robot/Vehicle subtype DIRECTLY ON battlefield. Putting doesn't CAST, doesn't trigger artifact-Cast, doesn't activate cast-cost reductions, and the opponent picks which branch helps them least. An **EMPTY-handed opponent CAN choose discard** and do nothing instead of allowing free machine, not an enforced “free eight-drop.” Your own eligible hand can be empty and free-placement branch accomplishes nothing. Choosing branches over three opponents is sequential, so after first machine enters, others can alter decisions. An opponent's discarded card may trigger Caress/Waste Not only if one was actually discarded. “Golem/Automaton” is NOT qualifying Robot/Construct without its precise Oracle type.

**Best contested inclusion `Scrawling Crawler` {3}, Phyrexian Construct**: Eligible for Eggman's machine-put condition, smaller MV. On YOUR upkeep each player draws one and each OPPONENT loses1 from their draw according to card wording. Extra draws can give empty-handed opponents actual cards to DISCARD when Eggman's end-step ability triggers, making fork less empty; also grants opponents additional answers and ability to play/discard the card before your end step. Thus “guarantees a card at end step” is FALSE. Exact plausible cut `Blitzwing, Cruel Tormentor` six-MV Robot (or `Cyberdrive Awakener` 6MV Construct), but Blitzwing contributes a powerful Robot/combat multiplier, and Cyberdrive makes artifact board flying/attacking value when on field.
