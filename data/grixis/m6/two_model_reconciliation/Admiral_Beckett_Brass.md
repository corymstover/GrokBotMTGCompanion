## Identity and realistic game tests

Admiral Beckett Brass costs **{1}{U}{B}{R}**; at beginning of YOUR end step, if a target player was dealt **combat damage by at least three distinct Pirate creatures** this turn, permanently gain control of target nonland permanent that player controls, subject to qualifying actual damage and legal targeting on resolution. It isn't “three Pirate damage” from one attacker, and THREE different Pirates cannot split hits across three players. Changeling Outcast is a Pirate; Roaming Throne chooses Pirate and becomes a Pirate while doubling Beckett's creature-triggered end-step ability. `Don Andres` can make stolen opponent-owned creatures Pirates, feeding future attacks. Pirate density ~33 printed Pirates/changelings plus Throne; early flyers, menace, unblockable and `Open into Wonder` enable specific three-body connections, with `Cover of Darkness` fear blocked by black/artifact blockers. `Daring Piracy` creates a hasty Pirate at beginning of combat, enabling immediate third body. `Zephyr Singer` convoke can tap Pirates that then can't attack that combat, a real sequencing anti-synergy. `Coercive Recruiter` temporary opposing creature becomes Pirate with haste when appropriate, helping three hits. Removal/Counterspell suite holds commander alive until end-step theft.

**The meaningful missed off-ranking challenger:** `City of Brass` versus `Crumbling Necropolis`. Both are UBR-relevant lands; City costs no ETB tempo and adds any color, with triggered **1 damage each time tapped**. Necropolis always enters tapped but deals no personal damage, and also adds any one of U/B/R. City is Commander legal/colorless; source Oct19 checked card Oracle and no exception. In the approved 35-land list with low-cost one/two mana Pirates and four-cost commander, an always-tapped land is a material constraint. Replacing a LAND with a LAND changes neither Pirate density nor creature/theft slots. Current approved list already has `Mana Confluence` but **does not have City of Brass**. Taking a second fast multicolor land does not turn the deck into generic goodstuff; it specifically supports paid early Pirates and protective counters.

**Strongest source-paid counterfactual:** T4 play fourth land while possessing three untapped producing lands with correct U/B/R pips: as `Crumbling Necropolis`, fourth land cannot pay {1}{U}{B}{R} this turn; `City of Brass` entering untapped can provide the last pip, and its tap creates 1 damage on controller, letting Beckett resolve now. On T1 City also permits one-drop Changeling Outcast/Spectral Sailor/Siren Stormtamer, while a T1 Necropolis loses that action. No guarantee this happens in every game; chance of drawing one singleton in first ten library cards ~10/99, BEFORE accounting turn/color constraints. Repeated City taps can lose many life over a long match, matters versus aggressive life loss and Ad Nauseam-like generic plans; in four-player optimized combat, the early turn ahead is an actionable upside. It can be incidentally shut by Stony Silence? Lands unaffected; artifact hate doesn't prevent City's tapping.

**Evasion alternatives rechecked rather than treating City as universal fix:** `Slippery Scoundrel` depends on City's Blessing at **10 permanents** before becoming unblockable and costs mana to deploy; currently `Daring Saboteur`, `Storm Fleet Sprinter`, `Changeling Outcast`, `Spyglass Siren`, `Siren Stormtamer`, `Cover of Darkness`, `Open into Wonder` cover evasive Pirates. Cutting flying `Zara` for Scoundrel loses card-theft/tempo even if low curve; cutting Saboteur loses immediate paid self-unblockability. Another Pirate anthem `Fell Flagship` is a Vehicle not Pirate creature itself, doesn't supply qualifying third damage source without crew, and proposed source cuts `Edward Kenway` (Treasure per tapped Pirate) or `Open into Wonder` remove important distinct payoffs. `Koh, the Face Stealer` is a six-MV non-Pirate and doesn't advance immediate three hits. None independently beats the low-cost land tempo proposal.

## Decision and owner gate
