### Rules correctness and limits
- **Boltwave** is a one-red sorcery: 3 to each opponent; at three opponents, 9 aggregate life lost if it resolves. **Flame Rift** costs `{1}{R}`, 4 damage to each player; 12 opponents' aggregate damage, also 4 to the pilot. The pilot's survival/life is **not** tracked. Both are actually paid from known sources when used.
- **Nightscape Familiar** costs `{1}{B}`, and while controlled reduces **once**, by generic `{1}`, blue or red spell cost, even when a spell is both. `+Ignus/-Familiar` loses both this early development and its per-loop colorless-mana advantage.
- **Rakdos, Lord of Riots** cannot be cast until an opponent has lost life this turn. After it is present, its generic creature-spell reductions apply to the modeled creature costs, never colored pips or printed mana value. Do not treat its giant evasive body, or discounts to unmodeled creatures, as absent value.
- **Birgi's front** returns `{R}` after every spell *cast while it was controlled*. The trigger **uses the stack and can be answered**, confirmed in official Kaldheim release notes. It does not add mana on the spell that initially puts Birgi into play, and the back-face Harnfel is not its front trigger.
- **Ignus** can only be bounced in its controller's main phase under its sorcery restriction. Its `{R}` return activation creates `{C}{C}{R}`; if that can pay its next full or discounted `{2}{R}` cost, the pilot actually recasts Ignus from hand and Birgi triggers again. A loop witness with five further recasts records **cascade triggers** only when Abaddon was already present on turn and opponents lost at least 3 life **before each cast**; the cascade triggers are *not* assumed to cast any hit.
- **Response windows** are separate adversarial conditional cases, not events sampled in the seed trajectories. Removing Abaddon in response to a cast does not undo an already-granted cascade, but stops its grant on future Ignus casts. Countering Ignus does not retroactively cancel cast triggers, but prevents that permanent from reentering for further activation. Removing Birgi leaves an already-triggered mana ability on the stack (a **spell-cast triggered ability, not a mana ability**) but stops future Birgi triggers. Rule of Law restricts repeated Ignus casts; Drannith Magistrate can stop cascade exile casts despite cascade having triggered. These tests do **not** assign likelihood to counterspells or removal.

**Important strategic caveat:** This limited pilot casts only named engine pieces and two red sorceries from the physically drawn library. It omits Sol Ring, Chrome Mox, rituals, repeated wheel/draw, all tutors, other pingers, nonhand casts, actual cascade hits, blockers/combat, Loyal/Neheb/Assault activations, opponents' hands, and creature pressure. Thus its low setup frequency cannot be interpreted as actual deck combo consistency. It is also optimistic that permanents survive indefinitely and Boltwave/Flame Rift always resolve. Neither bias is calibrated against true multiplayer wins.
