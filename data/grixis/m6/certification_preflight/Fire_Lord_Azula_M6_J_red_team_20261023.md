# Fire Lord Azula — M7 red-team memo (2026-10-23)

1. **Commander removed:** Azula (MV 4) is the copy engine. The Turnabout and Frantic Search loops need her attacking. The Oracle + Consultation/Tainted Pact and Breach + LED/Petal/Wheel/Grinding Station + Brain Freeze lines do not. Deflecting Swat, Redirect Lightning and the counters protect her. Mistrise Village can make her cast uncounterable.
2. **Split second / Samut cut (FLAGGED):** the owner added Samut, Tyrant of Naktamun. Its split second stops our bargained Beseech free cast and Narset's Reversal into Frantic Search or Turnabout (702.61a/b; Samut ruling 2026-08-21). Cutting it loses "our instants and sorceries can't be countered while Samut is out". The keep-Samut four-swap is staged.
3. **Counter-heavy tables:** Swan Song (cut) was a 1-mana answer to opposing counters. Mistrise Village's uncounterable ability and Cavern of Souls remain, and Force of Will, Force of Negation, Fierce Guardianship, Pact, Mindbreak Trap, Flusterstorm, Pyroblast, REB and Strix stay. Protection count net 0.
4. **Graveyard hate (Rest in Peace, Dauthi, Bojuka Bog):** stops Breach and the new Flashback recursion; Turnabout and Frantic loops are unaffected.
5. **Turnabout loop mana boundary:** net 2L − 6 per iteration. It needs at least 3 untapped-able lands for unbounded storm and 4 for unbounded mana, and the colours are limited to what the lands make. Tapping our lands in response to removal on Azula breaks the iteration. Each iteration is an optional finite sequence.
6. **Windfall discard and Flashback targets:** Windfall's discard is real when cast outside combat. Flashback needs an instant/sorcery in our graveyard and is dead on turn 1.
