# Ashnod the Uncaring — M7 red-team memo (2026-10-23)

1. **Commander removed or taxed:** Aether Spellbomb, Priest and Reshapers still work at single strength; Thopter Squadron +
   Altar/KCI is not infinite without Ashnod and Novelist. Ashnod (MV 5) is the main loss risk; protection is unchanged.
2. **Mana screw:** two fewer rocks (42 → 40 static sources); the curve rises slightly (2.21 → 2.26). See the mana memo.
3. **Graveyard hate (Rest in Peace, Bojuka Bog):** blanks the Engineer + Monitor + Petal loop and the welders/Trawler/
   Retriever lines. Squadron's loop does not use the graveyard and survives.
4. **Counters/removal:** Ashnod's copy is a triggered ability; Stifle-type effects stop only the copy. Priest and Reshapers
   need to survive a turn (302.6).
5. **Stax/artifact hate (Collector Ouphe, Null Rod, Stony Silence):** Ouphe/Null Rod stop artifact activations, including
   Spellbomb, Squadron and the Engineer loop; Priest (creature) and Reshapers still work.
6. **Wipes:** more creatures are exposed (25 → 27). Corridor Monitor is recursion fodder; Priest needs bodies.
7. **Combo pods:** Aether Spellbomb gives copyable instant-speed double bounce. Stack interaction is unchanged.
8. **Bracket disclosure:** adds three Spellbook-listed Ashnod lines: Ashnod + Goblin Engineer + Corridor Monitor + Lotus Petal
   (1414-1649-2068-4404, infinite artifact ETB/sacrifice, with Novelist infinite red mana) and Ashnod + Thopter Squadron +
   Crime Novelist + Ashnod's Altar or KCI (2034-2068-2762-5346 / 2068-2762-4659-5346, sorcery speed). The baseline already
   had 15 included combos, so the bracket is unchanged. No two-card infinite. Game Changers are unchanged at 6 (Ancient Tomb,
   Bolas's Citadel, Cyclonic Rift, Demonic Tutor, Fierce Guardianship, Vampiric Tutor). Bracket 4 Optimized.
9. **Losses:** Esoteric Duplicator (EDHREC 43%), Walking Ballista, Foundry Inspector, Fellwar Stone, Talisman of Indulgence.
