# Evelyn, the Covetous — M7 red-team memo (2026-10-23)

1. **Commander removed:** Evelyn (MV 5, 2/5 flash) is a value engine and theft outlet, not the combo itself. The wins (Thassa's Oracle + Consultation/Tainted Pact, Breach + LED/Petal + Brain Freeze, Worldgorger + Animate Dead/Dance/Necromancy) do not need her. Burnt Offering can sacrifice her in response to removal for 5 B/R mana.
2. **Graveyard hate (Rest in Peace, Dauthi Voidwalker, Bojuka Bog, Soul-Guide Lantern):** stops Worldgorger and Breach lines and Insolent Neonate's discard-to-bin role. The Oracle/Consultation/Pact lines remain. Unchanged from the baseline; no add makes this worse.
3. **Targeted removal on a combo turn:** the redirect pair (Spellskite, Redirect Lightning) is cut, so a removal spell on Thassa's Oracle or a Breach turn is answered only by counters (Force of Will, Force of Negation, Pact, Mindbreak Trap, Flusterstorm, Swan Song, Pyroblast, REB) and Hexing Squelcher's ward. This is the main loss and is disclosed.
4. **Stifle / fetch punishers:** fetches 7 → 9; an opposing Stifle or Trickbind on a fetch costs a land. Blazemire Verge (cut) was immune. The four-swap keeping Verge is staged.
5. **Vexing Bauble self-counter:** the owner-kept Bauble counters any spell cast with no mana spent, including our own Force of Will (alternative cost), Pact of Negation, Mindbreak Trap, Gitaxian Probe (life) and Daze-style cards. No add is cast for free: Neonate {R}, Brainstorm {U}, Burnt Offering {B}. Daze was rejected partly for this reason.
6. **Insolent Neonate is fragile:** a 1/1; its value is the enter trigger with Evelyn out (four cards exiled) and the instant-speed discard. It is not a Vampire tribal package and nothing else keys off Vampires.
7. **Worldgorger loop exit:** the loop is mandatory (104.4b / 732.4). With no second creature card in a graveyard or an instant-speed outlet, the game is a draw. Neonate adds an exit. With Evelyn on the battlefield each iteration also exiles our own top card (we may decline the command zone, 903.9a), so the repeats are finite and can shrink our library.
8. **Combo / bracket disclosure:**
   - Commander Spellbook find-my-combos: the **same 7 complete combos** in baseline and candidate (742-1295, 1295-3093, 1368-1414-4856, 1368-3518-4856, 4812-4846, 2896-4846, 3605-4846). Almost-included 103 → 117.
   - Worldgorger lines give arbitrarily large mana from lands tapped in response and need a sink or exit; Saw in Half on Evelyn is finite (2 tokens, up to 4 triggers, 16 cards across four players). **No new infinite is claimed.**
   - Game Changers 19 → 19. **Bracket 5 (cEDH)**, unchanged.
9. **Exceptions:** none. Evelyn, the Covetous (Streets of New Capenna `snc`, released 2022-04-29) is Commander-legal per live Scryfall and is not a Game Changer. All 100 are legal and inside U/B/R; no companion.
10. **Losses:** Thundering Falls (9% EDHREC cEDH), Spellskite, Redirect Lightning, Wan Shi Tong, Librarian, Blazemire Verge (6%). None is an explicit owner keep.
11. **Prior owner history:** owner-removed Displacer Kitten, Otawara, Morphic Pool, Talisman of Creativity and the 2026-10-14 rejections (Wheel of Fortune, The One Ring, Dragon's Rage Channeler, Cyclonic Rift, Deflecting Swat, Into the Flood Maw, Hoarding Broodlord, Beseech the Mirror, the optimized-tribal Vampire package) are not re-added. Insolent Neonate is flagged against the Vampire-package rejection.
12. **Distinctness (nonbasic names shared, baseline → candidate, canonical exports):** Don Andres, the Renegade (#14) 27 → 29 (Marsh Flats, Verdant Catacombs); Cormela, Glamour Thief (#9) 67 → 69 (Marsh Flats, Brainstorm; also a cEDH shell). No Gonti deck is in the registry. The deck stays commander-first to Evelyn's Vampire-enter exile and once-per-turn play-from-exile Oracle.
