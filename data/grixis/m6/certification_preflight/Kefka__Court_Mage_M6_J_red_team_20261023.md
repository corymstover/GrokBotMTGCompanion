# Kefka, Court Mage // Kefka, Ruler of Ruin — M7 red-team memo (2026-10-23)

1. **Commander countered or removed.** Kefka can be countered before it enters and strand five mana. It can be removed before attackers are declared to deny the attack trigger. An enter or attack trigger already on the stack resolves without its source. Recasting costs commander tax (CR 903.8), which is why the deck relies on non-cast enter triggers (blink and copy).
   - Protection: Hexing Squelcher and Cavern of Souls (uncounterable), and the free counter suite (Force of Will, Force of Negation, Fierce Guardianship, Pact of Negation, Deflecting Swat).
   - Force of Negation's alternative cost works only when it is not your turn (Oracle).
2. **Stax and hate.** Graveyard hate stops Underworld Breach lines and turns off Anger, whose haste ends if it is exiled. Thassa's Oracle with Demonic Consultation or Tainted Pact remains. Rule of Law-style effects slow the Dualcaster loops, since each Dualcaster copy is not cast (CR 707.10) but the first spell and Dualcaster are.
3. **Dualcaster Mage (the swap).**
   - The Molten Duplication and Saw in Half lines need the original spell on the stack; a counter on Dualcaster's enter trigger (Stifle effects) or on the original spell breaks the loop.
   - Saw in Half tokens have no haste without Anger and a Mountain; the Molten Duplication tokens have haste, so that is the combat kill for five mana.
   - With Harmonic Prodigy, the spare copy may target Kefka (CR 707.10c, CR 603.2d). A hasty token Kefka triggers on entering; the legend rule (CR 704.5j) puts one Kefka into the graveyard; a token there ceases to exist.
   - **Owner-direction flag:** the Molten Duplication kill does not require Kefka, which is close to the owner's reason for rejecting the Floodcaller bounce-combo package. It is disclosed, not hidden.
   - **Data caveat:** adoption is neutral to slightly against (EDHREC cEDH 497 vs 861 of 2232; EDHTop16 conversion 0.1706 vs 0.1867, inside noise).
4. **Entomb (Pass C).** It roughly doubles the early Anger haste window (turn 5: 0.0863 → 0.1642), but the absolute gain is small, it finds nothing for your hand and it is exposed to graveyard hate. Rejected over both Demonic Counsel and Borne Upon a Wind.
5. **Transform.** The {8} transform is sorcery-speed; Valley Floodcaller only flashes noncreature spells, not activations. The back face draws only on actual life loss during your turn. Not an automatic turbo line.
6. **Combo / bracket disclosure.**
   - Commander Spellbook find-my-combos: 6 → 9 complete combos:
     - Thassa's Oracle with Demonic Consultation or Tainted Pact.
     - Underworld Breach with Lotus Petal + Brain Freeze, Lion's Eye Diamond + Brain Freeze, Wheel of Fortune + Jeska's Will, or Lion's Eye Diamond + Wheel of Fortune.
     - New: Dualcaster Mage with Molten Duplication (147-5487), Saw in Half (147-500) or Essence Flux (147-4598).
   - Game Changers 20 → 20. **Bracket 5 (cEDH)**, unchanged.
7. **Exceptions:** none.
   - Kefka, Court Mage // Kefka, Ruler of Ruin (Final Fantasy, `fin`, released 2025-06-13) is Commander-legal per live Scryfall and is not a Game Changer.
   - All 100 cards are legal and inside U/B/R. Dockside Extortionist, Jeweled Lotus, Library of Alexandria and Mana Crypt are banned and absent; there is no companion.
8. **Losses.** The candidate loses Culling the Weak: one free-ish black ritual (net +3 black mana) in creature-on-board hands, Breach fuel and a sacrifice outlet for Phantasmal Image. What the alternatives would lose:
   - Dualcaster over Diabolic Intent: a tutor to hand that finds either combo half or Thassa's Oracle.
   - Talisman of Indulgence fallback: no new combos, but a permanent B/R rock.
   - Entomb: Demonic Counsel's late any-card tutor or Borne Upon a Wind's flash.
9. **Prior owner history.** The owner-removed and owner-rejected cards and classes in the 2026-10-14 decisions are not re-added (see the I memo).
10. **Distinctness.** Nonbasic names shared, candidate/baseline, from the registry Archidekt exports:
    - Kess, Dissident Mage 80/80
    - Jeleva, Nephalia's Scourge 78/78
    - Inalla, Archmage Ritualist 72/73 (Inalla runs Kefka itself in its 99)
    - Evelyn, the Covetous 67/68

    The swap lowers overlap: Culling the Weak is shared with Inalla and Evelyn, and Dualcaster Mage is in none of the four. The cards unique to Kefka against all four are: Anger, Diabolic Intent, Dualcaster Mage, Gogo, Mysterious Mime, Molten Duplication, Phantasmal Image, Phyrexian Metamorph, Starting Town, Tinybones, Bauble Burglar and Waste Not.
11. **Verdict:** RESEARCH_ONLY. One genuine swap; the five-swap floor is not met without padding. Not owner-presentable and NOT owner approved.
