## 1. Multiple removal effects and commander attrition

**Verdict: conditionally resilient, but two-copy Abaddon engines are commander-sensitive.** Removing the real Abaddon before Sakashima enters may eliminate the intended second Abaddon to copy. A previously resolved copy does not help if both are later removed before subsequent hand casts; however, two stacked granted cascade abilities already triggered on an earlier cast are not undone by later removal. Sakashima at four mana is a powerful multiplicative value play only when the commander or another worthwhile creature survives its entry decision, and the legend-rule exemption must be active.

The early backups have distinct removal profiles. Impact Tremors and Sanctum are enchantments, so creature sweepers missing enchantments do not remove them, but a Disenchant or enchantment wipe can stop next-turn thresholds. A Coruscation offspring token is a separate creature after its 2-generic paid entry, and killing the original does not automatically destroy the token. Sheoldred has no protection from a one-for-one removal spell and an ordinary Wheel cast after the Sheoldred dies provides no Sheoldred opponent-draw loss. Existing free/cheap stack interaction includes Force of Will, Fierce Guardianship, Deflecting Swat, Pact of Negation, Flusterstorm, Swan Song and Pyroblast; Deadly Rollick instead supplies creature removal. These cards provide finite defense, not perpetual protection.

Underworld Breach, Lion's Eye Diamond and Brain Freeze remain a graveyard-dependent separate win path, while Neheb / Aggravated Assault / Loyal Subordinate forms another attack/combat resource loop. If opponents simultaneously hate graveyards and suppress attack phases, the redundancies are narrower than two lists of combo names imply. **No claim of a percentage survival advantage.**

## 2. Pillowfort, targeted-player restrictions and one-spell limits

**Verdict: life-loss access is genuinely less combat-dependent, but anti-cast stax remains an honest weakness.** Sanctum at first main produces each-opponent direct life loss without targeting; Mount Doom with {1}{B}{R},{T} pays for each-opponent damage without casting; Impact Tremors and Mage also ping each opponent, subject to their own noncombat/ETB/cast conditions. No combat connection is needed for those sources, unlike creature attacks; Loyal Subordinate supplies combat-start loss when its condition holds without dealing attack damage.

Rule of Law and similar one-spell-per-turn limits suppress Abaddon's follow-up cascade *casts*, even though cascade triggers can still occur. Impact Tremors + repeated Ignus **cannot** establish a cast loop under such a limit. Mount Doom's ability and Sanctum's trigger are nonspells and still can create X, but that does not defeat the cast limit. Drannith Magistrate prevents some exile casts; graveyard hate stops Breach. More X does not solve every permission restriction. Chaos Warp can target a troublesome nonland permanent when legally targetable but is not an unconditional permanent answer, and its owner may reveal a replacement permanent.

## 3. Token stalls, sweepers, hidden taxes and adverse trigger windows

**Verdict: the precombat/ETB package improves noncombat reach but requires setup time and payable resource.** With only one Shrine, a valid beginning-of-first-main trigger causes each opponent to lose one; if an opponent removes Sanctum before resolution and no Shrines remain, the resolved trigger's value is zero. Similarly, an Impact Tremors trigger already on stack persists after enchantment removal, but no future ETB trigger is made while Impact absent. Paid offspring creates one token; extra cost is never financed simply by the free cascade spell cost waiver unless real resources exist.

The historical Keeper of Secrets could deal an opponent a hit's mana value on **every** nonhand cast and apply a relevant high-MV payoff against creature stalls. Its removal sacrifices this 40K source. Retaining Noise Marine, Nalfeshnee, Passionate Archaeologist, The Lost and the Damned and Call Forth the Tempest keeps distinctive Warhammer cascade/exile payoffs, but these engines are not interchangeable: Nalfeshnee copies the *spell* cast from exile; the copy is not a cast and itself does not create new cast triggers; Call Forth has printed double cascade. Blasphemous Act and Delayed Blast Fireball can clear creatures while also killing Mage, Birgi, Sheoldred or other own creatures; count this opportunity cost. This exact tradeoff favors preserving Noise/Nalfeshnee over the alternative seven-swap package that sacrifices a second identity-defining engine.

## 4. Artifact/enchantment denial, theft and player draw interaction

**Verdict: this revision diversifies permanent types but also exposes the entire threshold engine to hate.** The Ruinous Powers' opponent-exile theft chance is lost: Sanctum offers deterministic early turn loss rather than high-impact random opponent spells. Sheoldred's Wheel/Windfall payoff is a large one-turn opportunity, not a deterministic standalone lock: the draw spell can be countered, an opponent may prevent or replace draws, and removing Sheoldred before the draw event prevents the relevant triggers. At three opponents, a fully resolving Wheel drawing seven each provides Sheoldred 21 triggers *after* the wheel was cast; X42 if all resolve (potentially X84 under active own-turn Bloodletter), which can qualify *later* hand spells—not retroactively the Wheel's own Abaddon granted cascade. Windfall depends on the largest hand size, not a fixed seven.

Mount Doom creates a B/R source (with a real life payment) and late nonspell X access, but sacrificing its ability to produce mana by tapping it for X may strand a needed colored cast. Its large board wipe also requires sacrificing a legendary artifact and is an expensive, sorcery-only fallback—do not advertise it as a reliable early sweeper.

**Decision and limits:** The protected-wheel six is the leading authored exact-100 candidate because it gains *three new modes* (early pre-main X, paid ETB/offspring life-loss, duplicated Abaddon cascade plus Sheoldred draws) while retaining Noise, Nalfeshnee, Abaddon/Birgi/Familiar/Ignus, free stack protection, Brain Freeze/Breach and Neheb/Assault. The competing five-, six- and seven-swap exact portfolios are retained as reviewed alternatives, not arbitrarily ignored. The cost of losing Keeper, Ruinous, Kessig/Thermo and Ponder is explicitly material and may still alter a meta-specific choice; no unmodeled game win-rate claim is made. Formal fresh M6 I tested-pool selection and M6 J hash-bound reproducibility remain to be finished before owner-ready M7 presentation.
