# Admiral Brass, Unsinkable: M6 J red team (2026-10-23)

Adversarial checks GrokBot ran against its own proposal. These are not an independent critic and not owner review.

1. **Graveyard hate** (Bog, Rest in Peace, Dauthi). The commander engine stalls. Mitigation: Ragavan, Warkite and Larcenist are worth casting from hand; this is still a real weakness of the shell.
2. **Finality exile.** Every returned Pirate is a one-shot unless Power Conduit removes the counter. Larcenist's Treasure conversion ends when it leaves, so a wiped Larcenist gives control back.
3. **Navigator overrated?** It needs the blessing (10 permanents) plus 4 spare mana after it returns. The finite test shows a turn-N+1 witness and a same-turn counterexample (Brass cast this turn leaves too little mana).
4. **Jhoira life loss.** With Throne, up to four reveals per turn cycle. Life equal to MV can be significant alongside Ancient Tomb and Talismans. The reveal can also hit a land-free low-MV artifact (low value).
5. **Ragavan as a one-drop.** Weak late off the top. It's included for turn-1 pressure and as a Brass-recursion Treasure source; MODERATE confidence.
6. **Cutting Changeling Outcast** loses unblockable Pirate damage for Beckett Brass's 3-Pirate theft trigger. Warkite/Larcenist flying offsets this partially.
7. **Bracket.** Still 6 Game Changers (unchanged). No new tutor or mass land denial, so it stays Bracket 4.
8. **Provenance.** The registry recorded digest ≠ raw source; this is disclosed, bound, and the canonical is untouched.
