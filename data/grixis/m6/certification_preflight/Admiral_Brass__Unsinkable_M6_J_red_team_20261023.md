# Admiral Brass, Unsinkable: M6 J red team (REV2, 2026-10-23)

Adversarial checks GrokBot ran against its own proposal. These are not an independent critic and not owner review. ChatGPT's external review of rev1 is answered in the G packet's "Response to ChatGPT review" section.

1. **Graveyard hate** (Bog, Rest in Peace, Dauthi). The commander engine stalls, and Ripples of Undeath adds graveyard reliance. Mitigation: Larcenist, Warkite and Siren's Ruse work from hand. This is a real weakness of the shell.
2. **Finality exile.** Every returned Pirate is a one-shot unless Power Conduit or Siren's Ruse resets it. Ruse returns it at printed size without haste, so post-combat use is best. Larcenist's Treasure conversion ends when it leaves.
3. **Navigator overrated?** It needs the city's blessing (10 permanents) plus 4 spare mana after it returns. The finite test shows a turn-N+1 witness and a same-turn counterexample.
4. **Jhoira life loss.** With Throne, up to four reveals per turn cycle, plus Ripples' optional 3 life. Life equal to MV can be significant alongside Ancient Tomb and Talismans.
5. **33 lands.** Land-drop odds drop by about 3 points (P(≥4 lands in 10) 0.470 → 0.441). This is accepted because of 8 rocks plus Treasure makers; MODERATE confidence.
6. **Pitiless Plunderer cut.** It still pays off on non-finality deaths (tokens, stolen creatures, hard-cast creatures, reset Pirates, our side of sweepers), so the loss is real but incidental.
7. **Ragavan reverted.** Outcast's guaranteed connection as a Brass return was undervalued in rev1.
8. **Bracket.** Still 6 Game Changers (unchanged). No new tutor or mass land denial, so it stays Bracket 4.
9. **Provenance.** The registry recorded digest ≠ raw source. This is disclosed and bound, and the canonical is untouched.
