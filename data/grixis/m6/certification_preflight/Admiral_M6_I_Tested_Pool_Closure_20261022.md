## Actual adjudicated comparison

The explicit decision criterion is maximum realistic **Admiral-specific repeated three-Pirate combat hits against one opponent** with sufficient early U/B/R access, ongoing draw and ability to survive typical blockers/removal. It is not the highest standalone creature power, fastest goldfish noncombat kill, or generic Grixis card selection.

| Complete configuration | Final comparative judgment |
|---|---|
| **Canonical baseline** | Strong Pirate-tutor and repeatable entry-drain via Forerunner, Poisoner flash/deathtouch, Captain combat damage and Saboteur draw/discard. | **C** | Retains Raiders/Indomitable but trades Open into Wonder's `{3}{U}{U}` X=3 unblockability **and three possible card-draw triggers** for Blustersquall `{3}{U}` mass tap without draw.
