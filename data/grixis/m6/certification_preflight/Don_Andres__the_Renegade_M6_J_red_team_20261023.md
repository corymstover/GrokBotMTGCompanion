# Don Andres, the Renegade — M7 red-team memo (2026-10-23)

1. **Commander removed or taxed:** Don (MV 4) supplies the +2/+2, menace, deathtouch and Pirate type and the Treasures.
   - Without Don, stolen creatures are still ours to use until the theft ends; they lose 2 power each (bounded grid: Insurrection on the five tables gives 84 power without Don).
   - Gríma and Fallen Shinobi still cast opponents' cards without Don; only the Treasures are lost.
   - Lightning Greaves (kept) protects Don; Swiftfoot Boots was rejected because Don has no targeted ability, so shroud costs nothing.
2. **Tapped Treasures:** Don's Treasures enter tapped (Pass C point kept). They never pay for the spell that made them or for the next stolen spell that turn without an untap effect; the mechanics test asserts this. Mnemonic Betrayal's "Treasure value" argument was rejected for this reason (and returned permanents are not cast).
3. **Copies and ownership:** a copy of a spell is not cast (707.10), and tokens we create are ours (111.2), so copy effects never trigger Don. Manifests from Thieving Amalgam are opponent-owned (701.40a) and get Don's static.
4. **Treachery fragility:** an Aura; removing it or the creature ends the theft (303.4e). Its land untap is already spent. Control Magic and Sower were judged weaker.
5. **Fewer sweepers:** cutting Blasphemous Act leaves Toxic Deluge and Cyclonic Rift. Against go-wide boards the deck relies on stealing blockers (Insurrection, menace/deathtouch). The four-swap keeping Blasphemous Act is staged as the retained alternative.
6. **Red mana dependence:** Insurrection needs RRR. Red land sources 22 → 21 (22 with Blazemire's conditional {R}); lands-only ≥3 red by 13 cards 0.5901 → 0.5529 (identical when the Verge condition holds). Disclosed, not repaired.
7. **Verge conditions:** each Verge's second colour needs an Island/Swamp or Swamp/Mountain; 9 names qualify for each, including fetches. With none in play they give one colour only.
8. **Combo / bracket disclosure:**
   - Commander Spellbook find-my-combos: **0 included combos** in both baseline and candidate; almost-included 23 → 19 (they need cards not in the list). No infinite is claimed.
   - Game Changers 5 → 5 (Ancient Tomb, Cyclonic Rift, Fierce Guardianship, Opposition Agent, Vampiric Tutor). **Bracket 4**, unchanged.
9. **Exceptions:** none. All 100 are Commander-legal and inside U/B/R per the live Scryfall batch; no companion.
10. **Losses:** Professional Face-Breaker, Seize the Spotlight, Blasphemous Act, Shipwreck Marsh, Smoldering Marsh.
11. **Prior owner history:** the owner's 2026-10-14 approval removed the reanimation package (Buried Alive, Living Death, Victimize), symmetrical/slow value (Cunning Rhetoric, Share the Spoils, The Ruinous Powers, Revel in Riches), Gonti, Lord of Luxury, Zara and four slow/tapped lands, and rejected Bribery, Agent of Treachery, Captivating Crew and Memory Plunder. None is re-added; Bribery, Zara, Memory Plunder, Agent of Treachery and Captivating Crew were evaluated and rejected as owner-removed. Treachery is single-target theft (the deck's existing class: Hostage Taker, Gilded Drake), not a removed class.
12. **Distinctness (nonbasic names shared, baseline → candidate):** Nathan Drake, Treasure Hunter 62 → 61; Admiral Brass, Unsinkable 55 → 53; Admiral Beckett Brass 54 → 52; Edward Kenway 49 → 49; Madcap Jester 43 → 44; Cormela, Glamour Thief 31 → 31; Evelyn, the Covetous 27 → 28. Gríma is also in the Nathan Drake list and Blazemire Verge in Evelyn. Overlap with the Pirate decks falls slightly; it is disclosed, not a design goal.
