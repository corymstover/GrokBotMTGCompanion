# Cormela, Glamour Thief — M7 red-team memo (2026-10-23)

1. **Commander removed or taxed:** Cormela (MV 4) is the engine.
   - Without her, Thassa's Oracle + Consultation or Pact and Breach + LED/Petal + Brain Freeze still win.
   - Twinflame and Frantic Search keep some value: Twinflame copies Dualcaster (2-card infinite, Spellbook 147-1235), and Frantic Search is free with three lands.
   - Hexing Squelcher gives Cormela "Ward—Pay 2 life", which taxes targeted removal by 2 life. It does not stop wipes or edicts.
2. **Counterspells:** Squelcher makes all our spells uncounterable while it lives, and Mistrise Village protects one spell. Neither stops:
   - ability counters (Stifle effects on storm, death or Breach triggers),
   - "can't cast" effects (Drannith Magistrate, Silence),
   - Squelcher's own removal in response.
   Stack interaction is unchanged except REB → Pyroblast only.
3. **Hate pieces:**
   - Cursed Totem shuts off Cormela's mana ability.
   - Null Rod or Collector Ouphe stop LED, Petal, altars and Talismans.
   - Grafdigger's Cage and Rest in Peace stop Breach and reanimation.
   Answers: Chain of Vapor, Otawara, Saw in Half (our own creature only) and Pyroblast. Abrade is a staged, unselected alternative.
4. **Mana screw:** lands stay 30, with no always-tapped land. See the mana memo.
5. **Wipes:** Squelcher, Birgi and Dualcaster die. Cormela returns via the command zone, and her death trigger still fires (903.9a).
6. **Graveyard hate:** blanks Breach and the reanimation lines. The Cormela death trigger returns cards to hand, so it still works unless the cards are exiled first.
7. **Life:** Squelcher's ward is a cost to opponents. Underground River pings us; Necropotence, Ad Nauseam, Mana Confluence and fetches already cost life. Probe's 2-life cost is gone.
8. **Combo / bracket disclosure:**
   - Commander Spellbook baseline: 23 included combos. Candidate: 25. The two new ones are **147-1235** (Dualcaster Mage + Twinflame, infinite hasty tokens) and **392-1235-4867** (Cormela + Birgi + Twinflame).
   - The Twinflame loop alone is finite: −1 unrestricted mana per iteration. Birgi's {R} per cast makes it unbounded.
   - Twinflame + an altar is **not** claimed as infinite.
   - Frantic Search with Saw in Half is **not** infinite (−1 per loop).
   - Game Changers are unchanged at 20. **Bracket 5 (cEDH)**, unchanged.
9. **Exceptions:** none. All 100 are Commander-legal and inside U/B/R per the live Scryfall batch.
10. **Losses:** Sewers' surveil, Orchard's any-color mana, Looting's flashback, Probe's free storm count and peek, and REB.
