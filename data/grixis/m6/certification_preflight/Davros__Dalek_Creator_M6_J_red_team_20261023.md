# Davros, Dalek Creator — M7 red-team memo (2026-10-23)

1. **Hellbent opponents (701.55b):** an opponent with no cards in hand may choose "that player discards a card" and lose nothing.
   - Davros's choice is weakest exactly when Tinybones' {4}{B}{B} (each hellbent opponent loses 10) is strongest.
   - Entropic Battlecruiser at 8+ and Tergrid's Lantern also punish an empty hand.
   - Disclosed, not repaired.
2. **Commander removed or taxed:** Davros (MV 4) is the engine. Greaves, Boots and Padeem (artifact hexproof) still protect it.
   - Auton Soldier can copy Davros as a non-legendary artifact, which gives a second end-step trigger.
   - The adds still punish life loss without Davros: Bloodletter, Purphoros, Fiery and Tectonic Giant drain or burn on their own.
3. **Artifact wipes:** Davros, Dalek tokens, rocks and Roaming Throne are artifacts.
   - Purphoros (enchantment, indestructible), Bloodletter, Tergrid, Tectonic Giant, Wound Reflection and Court of Ambition survive an artifact wipe.
   - Unlicensed Disintegration then needs an artifact to deal its 3.
4. **Purphoros exiled or bounced:** indestructible does not stop exile, bounce or -X/-X when it is a creature (devotion to red 5+ is rare here).
5. **Bloodletter removed:** a 2/4 flyer and the obvious lightning rod. Without it, the deck falls back to the baseline's 3-damage enablers (Boltwave, Maestros Charm, Loyal Subordinate, Fiery, Tectonic Giant).
6. **Dauthi Voidwalker vs Tergrid:** Voidwalker exiles cards that would go to an opponent's graveyard, so Tergrid cannot return them.
   - Disclosed anti-synergy; Voidwalker is kept as a 3-power shadow qualifier.
   - The Kroxa-over-Voidwalker alternative was rejected.
7. **Fiery Confluence target failure (608.2b):** if every target is illegal (for example, the chosen artifact is sacrificed), the spell does not resolve. Choose 2-2-2 when in doubt.
8. **Off-turn loss does not count:** Court of Ambition (our upkeep) counts. Wound Reflection's end-step loss counts only at our end step when ordered before Davros.
9. **Combo / bracket disclosure:**
   - Commander Spellbook find-my-combos: baseline and candidate both have **0 included combos**. Almost-included lines (e.g. Blasphemous Act + Repercussion) need cards that are not in the list.
   - Bloodletter + Wound Reflection is a finite doubling, not a loop. No infinite combo is claimed.
   - Game Changers 5 → 6 (Ancient Tomb, Cyclonic Rift, Demonic Tutor, Fierce Guardianship, Jeska's Will + Tergrid). **Bracket 4**, unchanged (registry ceiling 4).
10. **Exceptions:** none. All 100 are Commander-legal and inside U/B/R per the live Scryfall batch.
11. **Losses:** Death in Heaven, Weeping Angel, The Cyber-Controller, Blasphemous Act, Vandalblast, Swan Song.
12. **Prior owner history:** the 2026-10-13 Proposed_Final (canonical main 9b26aa28) contained Liliana's Caress, Bottomless Pit, The Rack and Oppression (discard and hellbent punishers). The approved 2026-10-14 list removed them. Megrim (same role) is therefore rejected here. Tergrid is a different punisher (it steals permanents) and needs owner review.
