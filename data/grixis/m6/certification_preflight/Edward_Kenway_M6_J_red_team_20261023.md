# Edward Kenway — M7 red-team memo (2026-10-23)

1. **Commander removed:** Edward (MV 5, 5/5, no protection of his own) is the Treasure engine and the theft trigger. Protection comes from the stack suite (Force of Will, Fierce Guardianship, Mana Drain, Swan Song, Deflecting Swat), Siren Stormtamer and Otawara. Without Edward, Malcolm, Keen-Eyed Navigator, Vaan, Ramirez, Captain Storm and the Vehicles still attack, and Bats still drains on Treasure from other sources.
2. **Mu Yanling removed:** the flying grant leaves with her. Five Vehicles still fly on their own (Copter, Skysovereign, Nautiloid Ship, RMS Titanic, Vertibird). The Chandra five-swap is staged; it animates a Vehicle each combat but gives no evasion.
3. **Mana:** turn-5 Edward castability from lands plus durable rocks falls 0.5283 → 0.5025 on the play (Petal excluded). The four-swap without Bats keeps Fellwar Stone.
4. **Artifact wipes:** Vehicles, Treasures, Clues and Vertibird's power all fall to artifact removal. Toxic Deluge (ours) leaves uncrewed Vehicles alive.
5. **Compulsory draws:** Fateful Discovery stays (explicit owner keep). With Academy Manufactor, two tapped qualifiers mean 6 compulsory draws and four mean 12, so a small library can deck us. This is recorded in the bounded measured test.
6. **Crew tension:** Vertibird's crew 2 and the Vehicles' crew costs tap creatures that might attack. Crewing taps Pirates/Assassins, so they still count for Edward at end step (over-crew is legal, 702.122a). The crewed Vehicle counts only if it is also tapped (attacking).
7. **Bats is off-type:** it is not a Pirate, Assassin or Vehicle, so Edward does not count it. It is a removable 2/3; its drain is finite per token event.
