Loss of Abaddon before a *later* hand cast ends those new cascade grants; a cascade already triggered on cast remains on stack after removal. ## 2. `pillowfort` — attacks or targeting constrained

**Verdict: Abaddon has a genuine noncombat/untargeted pivot, but is not immune to anti-cast hate.** The deck does **not** need commander combat damage or clean attack lanes to set X. Boltwave (3 damage **each opponent**) and Flame Rift (4 each player) create X=9/X=12 at a typical three-opponent table before prevention; Kessig Flamebreather pings each opponent from noncreature spells; Loyal Subordinate (with commander controlled) causes **each opponent** to lose 3 at combat start without connecting an attacker. However, Keeper of Secrets and Passionate Archaeologist each have a **target opponent** requirement for their respective cast triggers, so hexproof/shroud-like player protection can suppress those payoff targets. Rule of Law can leave cascade triggers that cannot cast their resulting spells; Drannith Magistrate prevents relevant casts from exile. **M6 conclusion:** commander-native progress remains possible behind combat pillowfort with real remaining stax weaknesses. **Limit:** no assumed universal answers to every protected/enchantment lock.

## 4. `artifact_enchantment_interaction` — losing own engine, answering opposing noncreature threats

**Verdict: selected Chaos Warp fills a real answer category; one answer does not ensure recovery.** Chaos Warp ({2}{R}) targets a nonland permanent, potentially including a problematic resolved Rule of Law enchantment or Rest in Peace; it shuffles the target into its owner's library, then the owner reveals the top card and may put that card onto battlefield **if it is a permanent**.
