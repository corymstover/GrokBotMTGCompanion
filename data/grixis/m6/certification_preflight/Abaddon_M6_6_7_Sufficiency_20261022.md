## Material finding: copied instant/sorcery Magecraft was untested

Storm-Kiln Artist ({3}{R}) grants Magecraft whenever its controller **casts OR copies an instant or sorcery**. This differs from Abaddon's hand-only cascade, Nalfeshnee's exile-cast copy source, Prosper's exile **play** Treasures, The Lost and the Damned's nonhand cast/land-entry Spawn, Keeper's nonhand-cast damage, and Passionate Archaeologist's commander-granted exile-cast damage. Copying Boltwave with Nalfeshnee or creating Brain Freeze's storm copies triggers Storm-Kiln per **real copy made**, though each virtual copy is **not cast**. Magecraft also fires for the actual original instant/sorcery **cast**. A Nalfeshnee permanent-spell copy is not an instant/sorcery and never triggers Storm-Kiln on its creation. Source: Wizards' *Commander Masters* release notes `https://media.wizards.com/2023/downloads/CMM_Release_Notes/EN_MTGCMM_ReleaseNotes_20230616.pdf`, and `https://mtg.wtf/card/stx/115/Storm-Kiln-Artist` (2021-04-16 rulings).

This omission is **material to the deck's mana velocity, token generation, and Breach backup**. Do not reverse the correct conclusion that Nalfeshnee's virtual copies are not spell **casts**; instead distinguish copy-triggered Magecraft from cast-triggered effects. P0-028 registers bounded, physically conserved source-paid Call, counter, Breach/LED/Brain Freeze, and Prosper land-play scenarios. No unbounded combo win is inferred.
