## Material finding: copied instant/sorcery Magecraft was untested

Storm-Kiln Artist ({3}{R}) grants Magecraft whenever its controller **casts OR copies an instant or sorcery**. This differs from Abaddon's hand-only cascade, Nalfeshnee's exile-cast copy source, Prosper's exile **play** Treasures, The Lost and the Damned's nonhand cast/land-entry Spawn, Keeper's nonhand-cast damage, and Passionate Archaeologist's commander-granted exile-cast damage. Copying Boltwave with Nalfeshnee or creating Brain Freeze's storm copies triggers Storm-Kiln per **real copy made**, though each virtual copy is **not cast**. Magecraft also fires for the actual original instant/sorcery **cast**. A Nalfeshnee permanent-spell copy is not an instant/sorcery and never triggers Storm-Kiln on its creation. Source: Wizards' *Commander Masters* release notes `https://media.wizards.com/2023/downloads/CMM_Release_Notes/EN_MTGCMM_ReleaseNotes_20230616.pdf`, and `https://mtg.wtf/card/stx/115/Storm-Kiln-Artist` (2021-04-16 rulings).

This omission is **material to the deck's mana velocity, token generation, and Breach backup**. Do not reverse the correct conclusion that Nalfeshnee's virtual copies are not spell **casts**; instead distinguish copy-triggered Magecraft from cast-triggered effects. P0-028 registers bounded, physically conserved source-paid Call, counter, Breach/LED/Brain Freeze, and Prosper land-play scenarios. No unbounded combo win is inferred.

## Subsequent source-completeness re-audit — P0-029 Mind's Desire

This appendix **supersedes only** the historical claim in the preceding section that 22 of 97 physical names had direct P0 cases. Under `data/grixis/m6/certification_preflight/abaddon_97_card_effect_coverage_20261022.json`, P0-029 registers **Mind's Desire**, creating **23/97** directly associated named limited P0 cards; **74/97** remain with fresh name/Oracle/legality coverage or prior partial model and no direct P0 case. Neither class is an adequate stand-alone universal effect correctness proof.

**Why the omission matters:** `Mind's Desire` ({4}{U}{U}; MV6) is an actual approved source-card and a qualifying Abaddon hand-cast if opponents have lost ≥6 life earlier this turn. Its printed storm creates **spell copies, never extra casts**. Wizards explicitly rules that each storm copy **does trigger Magecraft**, and each copy independently shuffles and exiles a real card; if nothing occurs between copies, multiple shuffles may be shortcut by mutual agreement, but this cannot justify assuming a known deck order. The permitted exile card may be **played**, subject to the usual timing (land drops and sorcery speed) without paying the spell's mana cost until the **end of the current turn**, not the end of the next turn as with Prosper's distinct Mystic Arcanum. Source: https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/strixhaven-school-mages-and-commander-2021-edition-release-notes-2021-04-16 and current Oracle https://mtg.wtf/card/scg/41/Minds-Desire .

**Critical discrimination:** A virtual Mind's Desire copy *does not* count toward Abaddon's hand-cast cascade, Nalfeshnee's exile-cast copied trigger, Prosper's exile **play**, The Lost and the Damned's nonhand **cast**, Keeper of Secrets, or Passionate Archaeologist. But a real Mind-exiled spell subsequently cast from exile *does* trigger the appropriate cast/payoff engines; an actual land **played** from exile triggers Prosper and (on battlefield entry from exile) Lost without being a cast. Mind's Desire's **storm count** includes prior real casts, including countered spells, but never spell copies. A real Mind **cast from exile** (e.g. hit off Call Forth MV8) *does* trigger Nalfeshnee and may produce another independent Mind virtual copy; that copy does not itself trigger printed storm.

Seven finite physical 100-deck scenarios preregistered in `data/grixis/m6/phase_b_plans/abaddon_p0_minds_desire_20261022.json` test explicit source-paid prior hand spell costs and Mind {4}{U}{U}, original+storm shuffled physical exile, deferred free-card plays, legal land/expired permission, original countering (copies survive), X9 Abaddon-granted cascade plus independent storm, an exile-cast Mind causing both storm and Nalf copies without recursive cast, and a zero-printed-mana-cost Ancestral Vision legally cast for free and drawing three **real** library cards. An arranged card after each shuffle represents one possible outcome, *not* an estimated incidence or constructed draw in a tested game. P0 source conservation asserts 99 actual library cards plus tracked Abaddon separately. Seven cases must actually pass exact-head GitHub Actions to be claimed; current record is only preregistration until those receipts exist.

**Strategic M6.7 impact:** Mind's Desire multiplies the value of actual exile-play payoffs and provides a novel path to no-mana-cost `Inevitable Betrayal` when that card remains in the whole 100, *without relying on printed cascade*. This increases the credible conditional opportunity cost of cutting Betrayal from the researched preferred package; it does **not** measure creature theft quality or prove the Keeper-cut alternative superior. Retain the approved locked baseline, authored qualified Ignus+Chaos Warp preferred two-swap, and Keeper-cut alternative until an exact-source owner decision.
