# Crosis, the Purger — M7 red-team memo (2026-10-23)

1. **Commander removed or taxed:** Crosis (MV 6) is the engine.
   - Without him, the punishers still convert Hymn, Mind Twist, Bottomless Pit, the Specters, Rankle, Tinybones, Tourach and the wheels into damage.
   - With Liliana's Caress, Megrim and Raiders' Wake out, a Wheel of Fortune or Windfall on 7-card hands costs each opponent 42 life. That is finite, not an infinite.
   - Deadly Rollick costs {3}{B} without a commander in play. Brotherhood Regalia (ward 2) and Greaves/Boots still protect Crosis.
2. **Blockers / fogs / removal in combat:**
   - Flying, Regalia (can't be blocked) and Sword (protection from black and green) help Crosis connect.
   - Rollick clears a flyer for free while Crosis is out.
   - The Sword + Assault loop ends the first time damage is prevented or Crosis is removed.
3. **Enchantment removal:** Megrim, Caress, Raiders' Wake and Aggravated Assault are all enchantments. One wipe (Farewell, Austere Command) resets the punishers. Opposing graveyard exile does not matter to them.
4. **Empty hands:** punishers do nothing against empty hands. Rankle's draw mode, Wheel of Fortune/Windfall/Dark Deal and Notion Thief refill or punish. Oppression was cut partly because it empties hands before Crosis purges them.
5. **Symmetry:**
   - Rankle's modes are symmetric (we choose which apply).
   - Bottomless Pit, Dark Deal and the wheels are symmetric, as before.
   - Our own discards trigger none of Megrim, Caress, Wake or Waste Not ("an opponent").
6. **Mana:** rocks 13 → 11, with 36 lands unchanged. See the mana memo.
7. **Graveyard hate:** it hurts Tergrid, Kroxa, Reanimate and Tinybones, Bauble Burglar, as before. Dauthi Voidwalker was rejected for exactly this anti-synergy.
8. **Combo / bracket disclosure:**
   - Commander Spellbook baseline: 1 included combo (2286-3221 Notion Thief + Teferi's Puzzle Box lock). Candidate: 2.
   - New: **900-3750** (Sword of Feast and Famine + Aggravated Assault, "infinite combat phases"). It needs five or more untapped lands to pay {3}{R}{R}, eight for a Crosis purge each loop, and the equipped creature connecting every combat. It is disclosed as conditional, not as an unconditional or deterministic win.
   - Game Changers are unchanged at 14. **Bracket 4**, unchanged (registry ceiling 4).
9. **Exceptions:** none. All 100 are Commander-legal and inside U/B/R per the live Scryfall batch.
10. **Losses:** Jet Medallion, Izzet Signet, Blizzard Specter's bounce mode, Dream Salvage's draw and Oppression's lock.
