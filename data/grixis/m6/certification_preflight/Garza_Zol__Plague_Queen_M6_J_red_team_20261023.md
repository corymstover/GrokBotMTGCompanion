# Garza Zol, Plague Queen — M7 red-team memo (2026-10-23)

1. **Commander removed:** Garza costs 7 plus commander tax. Every engine (draws, counters, commander damage, Kediss, Spike, Exoskeleton poison) needs her in combat.
   - Protection: Swiftfoot Boots, Mithril Coat, Commander's Plate, Shadowspear, Slip Out the Back, Plaza of Heroes, Deflecting Swat, Fierce Guardianship, Counterspell and Mana Drain.
   - If Garza leaves, Lizard Blades becomes unattached and stays as a backup 1/1 double-strike creature.
2. **Removal between strike steps:** with double strike, removal after first-strike damage stops the second hit (CR 702.4c): 5 damage instead of Inquisitor's Flail's 10. Flail is kept in the research four, so Flail + Lizard Blades still deals 10 in that case.
3. **First-strike, big or indestructible blockers:**
   - Double-strike Garza trades with a 5/5 first striker.
   - She dies to an 8/8 or a 5/5 indestructible blocker unless she also has Inquisitor's Flail (Flail + Lizard Blades survives the 8/8) or Mithril Coat.
   - Garza flies, so most ground blockers can't block her.
4. **Edicts and sweepers:**
   - Sheoldred's Edict-style effects hit Garza when she is our only creature. Lizard Blades, if unattached, and Kediss can be sacrificed instead.
   - Attached Lizard Blades is not a creature, so sweepers miss it.
5. **Equipment hate:** Giantfall's artifact mode and Sink into Stupor answer opposing artifact hate; Bojuka Bog handles graveyard decks.
6. **Life cost:** both MDFC lands cost 3 life to enter untapped; Talismans and fetches cost life too.
