# Inalla, Archmage Ritualist — M7 red-team memo (2026-10-23)

1. **Commander removed or taxed.** Eminence works from the command zone, so the Wizard-copy engine survives Inalla's removal.
   - Only the tap-five finisher needs Inalla on the battlefield.
   - If Inalla changes zones while an Eminence trigger waits, the intervening-if check fails and the trigger does nothing (CR 603.4; official ruling).
2. **Stax and hate.** Graveyard hate stops the Scholar of the Ages / Unearth / Reanimate lines; the Thassa's Oracle and Underworld Breach + Lion's Eye Diamond + Brain Freeze backups remain.
   - Counter protection: Force of Will, Pact of Negation, Mental Misstep, Flusterstorm, Red Elemental Blast and An Offer You Can't Refuse.
   - Hexing Squelcher (a Goblin Sorcerer, not a Wizard) protects with "can't be countered".
3. **Dualcaster alternative.** It would add two two-card instant-speed combos (147-500 with Saw in Half, 147-4598 with Essence Flux), both reachable by Spellseeker.
   - It is the best-evidenced single change in the pool.
   - It re-adds an owner-removed card, so it stays an OWNER DECISION. It is not hidden.
4. **Samut, Tyrant of Naktamun.** Split second (CR 702.61a) protects combo spells, but Samut is owner-rejected and 5 MV. Rejected.
5. **Defense Grid.** By CR 601.2f, an opponent's pitched Force of Will or Pact still pays {3} off-turn. It is real protection, but marginal over the existing counter suite, and it taxes the owner's own off-turn Red Elemental Blast and Flusterstorm. Rejected.
6. **Combo / bracket disclosure.**
   - Commander Spellbook find-my-combos: 16 → 16 complete combos (Thassa's Oracle with Demonic Consultation or Tainted Pact; Underworld Breach + Lion's Eye Diamond + Brain Freeze; Scholar / Saw in Half loops).
   - Game Changers 18 → 18. **Bracket 5 (cEDH)**, unchanged.
7. **Exceptions:** none.
   - Inalla, Archmage Ritualist (Commander 2017, `c17`, released 2017-08-25) is Commander-legal per live Scryfall and is not a Game Changer.
   - All 100 cards are legal and inside U/B/R. Dockside Extortionist, Jeweled Lotus and Mana Crypt are banned and absent; there is no companion.
8. **Losses.** None, because the candidate equals the canonical list. What the alternatives would lose:

   - Snapcaster: Thundertrap Trainer's 0.973 top-4 selection.
9. **Prior owner history.** The owner-removed and owner-rejected cards and classes in the 2026-10-14 decisions are not re-added (see the I memo).
