## Mechanically decisive fact: exactly three Pirates, SAME opponent

The win-condition is a beginning-endstep Admiral trigger only if **three or more Pirate sources dealt combat damage to the same player earlier in the turn**. Total damage, merely attacking, non-Pirate Monkey token damage, incidental damage from Wave or being a Pirate in hand do not count. Admiral must survive to the beginning of the end step; once her triggered ability is on the stack, its later independent resolution follows ordinary rules if it still has legal target. A resolved Wave prevents all opposing creatures blocking that turn, but does not prevent an opponent using combat-damage prevention, counterspell, instant removal of one exact Pirate or removal of Admiral before the trigger. None of our qualitative judgments treats evasion as a guaranteed theft.

Three existing 2026-10-22 source-bound datasets bind Oracle text, mana values, legalities and color identities: the 121-name original full snapshot; the later eleven-card Scryfall source; and six new EDHREC-lift challenger Oracle records. The one critical previously overlooked name was `Cosmotronic Wave` and its sorcery-speed `{3}{R}` team can't-block effect. Another critical retained card was `Fiery Cannonade` and its `{2}{R}` **instant** 2 damage to all non-Pirate creatures. Under those exact costs, it is rational to favor keeping a distinct defensive role while swapping the redundant `Raise the Palisade` five-mana non-Pirate bounce slot for Wave. But it is **not** rational to call Wave's four-mana assault unique to this choice: both six-swap variants contain Wave.
