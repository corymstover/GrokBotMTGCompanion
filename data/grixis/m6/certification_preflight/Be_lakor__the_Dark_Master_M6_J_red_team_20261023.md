# Be'lakor, the Dark Master — M7 red-team memo (2026-10-23)

1. **Commander removed or taxed:** without Be'lakor the five adds still work at their base value:
   - Desecration Demon and Reaper are 6/6 fliers;
   - Belzenlok digs for cards;
   - Dominance is still a one-sided wipe;
   - Horn still reduces Demon costs and reveals Demons.

   Be'lakor (MV 6) is the main loss risk. Protection is unchanged (Fierce Guardianship, Deflecting Swat, Mana Drain).
2. **Mana screw:** one fewer rock (43 → 42 static sources). Horn's {1} reduction on every Demon, including Be'lakor, offsets this. See the mana memo.
3. **Wipes:** we keep Toxic Deluge and add Kindred Dominance (Demon survives), replacing Blasphemous Act. Opponents' symmetrical wipes still
   hit us. Reanimate, Whip of Erebos and Gyruda recover.
4. **Graveyard hate:** blanks Reanimate and Whip. The adds do not depend on the graveyard.
5. **Desecration Demon** is tapped by any opponent's sacrifice. This feeds Reaper's morbid trigger, but it is a weak attacker.
6. **Self-damage:** Belzenlok deals 1 damage to us per nonland card it exiles and draws, plus there is Prince X life loss, Vilis and Ancient Tomb.
   The Rite line's five token Prince triggers can cost about 25 life; stack them under the Lord triggers.
7. **Combo pods / stack:** no new combos. Be'lakor's triggers are abilities, so Stifle effects stop single hits. Cyclonic Rift and the counterspells are unchanged.
8. **Bracket disclosure:**
   - Commander Spellbook lists one included combo in both lists: Be'lakor + Rite of Replication (1744-4071). Spellbook labels it
     "near-infinite", but it is **finite**: 25 Lord triggers at 6 damage, or 50 with Roaming Throne naming Demon.
   - No infinite combo exists, and no two-card infinite.
   - Game Changers are unchanged at 5 (Ancient Tomb, Cyclonic Rift, Demonic Tutor, Fierce Guardianship, Vampiric Tutor).
   - The deck is **Bracket 4 Optimized**, unchanged.
9. **Losses:** Blasphemous Act (EDHREC-common emergency wipe), Bloodthirster's extra combat, Rakdos's 6-power body and mana,
   Fellwar Stone, and Dream Devourer's foretell.
