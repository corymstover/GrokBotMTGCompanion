# Cult of Skaro — M7 red-team memo (2026-10-23)

1. **Commander removed or taxed:** Cult (MV 4) is the engine.
   - Hammer of Purphoros lets a recast Cult attack at once; Greaves, Boots and Padeem still protect it.
   - Clones (Quantum Misalignment, Spark Double, Sakashima, Metamorph, Auton Soldier, Irenicus, Helm, Shaun) need a Cult on the battlefield to copy.
2. **Shaun dies:** all Synth tokens are exiled ("When Shaun leaves the battlefield"). Quantum and Helm copies are unaffected.
3. **Wipes:** Their Name Is Death and Delete spare our artifacts; opposing artifact wipes reset the board. Kindred Discovery and Hammer are enchantments and survive creature wipes.
4. **Blockers / fogs:** Skorpekh Lord gives artifact attackers menace; Dalek tokens already have menace. Cult's attack trigger does not need damage, but Research Thief, Thopter Spy Network and Sword do.
5. **Random modes:** none of the swaps makes a mode choice; more triggers only raise the expected value (1/2 card, 1/4 Dalek and 1 life per opponent per trigger). Resonator and Scientist Supreme copies keep the rolled mode; Roaming Throne rolls again.
6. **Kindred overdraw:** draws are mandatory. A Genesis chapter III turn with Karlach can draw 8+ cards; deck-out (CR 704.5b) is possible in long games. Disclosed, not modeled.
7. **Mana:** unchanged 34 lands and 9 rocks; one fewer reducer. See the mana memo.
8. **Combo / bracket disclosure:**
   - Commander Spellbook: baseline and candidate both have exactly one included combo, **900-3750** (Sword of Feast and Famine + Aggravated Assault, "infinite combat phases"). It needs the equipped creature to connect every combat and five or more untapped lands (two red) after each untap. Disclosed as conditional, not as an unconditional or deterministic win.
   - Karlach's extra combat is once per turn (first combat only); it is not an infinite.
   - Game Changers unchanged at 3 (Cyclonic Rift, Fierce Guardianship, Mana Vault). **Bracket 4**, unchanged (registry ceiling 4).
9. **Exceptions:** none. All 100 are Commander-legal and inside U/B/R per the live Scryfall batch.
10. **Losses:** Cybermen Squadron, The Master, Multiplied, Canoptek Spyder, Adaptive Omnitool, Anrakyr the Traveller, Etherium Sculptor.
11. **Prior owner history:** an earlier owner revision cut Skorpekh Lord for Ashad. Here Skorpekh returns over Etherium Sculptor and Ashad is kept. This is disclosed for the owner.
