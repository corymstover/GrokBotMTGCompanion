## Commander-first budget and adversarial restrictions

With real mana and appropriate same-turn opponent loss, the selected MV3 additions create more Abaddon-qualified hand-cast opportunities at lower X thresholds than MV4 Rakdos (which has stringent colored casting costs). Ignus is engine-positive chiefly with Birgi/paid return-recast access; its body alone is poor as a threat. Against Rule of Law, a paid hand cast may consume the spell for turn and leave cascade unable to cast another spell. Against Drannith, exile-cast followups can be prohibited. Removing Birgi/Abaddon interrupts repeated threshold cascades; countering a previously cast trigger does not remove a cascade trigger already on stack. The mana plan has fast artifacts and rituals but is sensitive to graveyard hate for Breach and to land/source sequencing for repeated red.
