When Raiders is in play, a Daring Piracy token entering at the beginning of each combat may trigger Raiders to tap one opponent creature and keep it from untapping as long as Raiders remains. That persistent lock is **conditional on Raiders surviving**, not unconditional control over all blockers. When The Indomitable is on the battlefield it draws for **each** creature you control dealing player combat damage, even if the Vehicle is not crewed. Three distinct successful Pirate combat sources in that board trigger three draws. Open's X=3 grant produces up to three *additional* combat-hit draws that turn if the affected sources connect. These synergistic resources are real, but no test assumes every payoff will coexist or survive at a multiplayer table.

Blustersquall's overload is **4 mana, one blue**, not a permanent lockdown or guaranteed long-term protection. Open's X=3 is **5 mana, two blue**, and only castable on its ordinary sorcery timing; Blustersquall's cheaper instant timing is a legitimate adversarial advantage under mana pressure. Slippery Scoundrel's ascend is a **ten-permanent** precondition, not a free turn-three unblockable creature. Herald's Horn does not discount noncreature Pirate spell costs, and Neckbreaker's increased damage does not itself satisfy Admiral's **three separate Pirates dealing combat damage to one player** requirement.
