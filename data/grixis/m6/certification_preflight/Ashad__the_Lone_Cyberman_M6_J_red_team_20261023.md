# Ashad, the Lone Cyberman — M7 red-team memo (2026-10-23)

1. **Commander removed or taxed:** Skullclamp, Royal Warden, Necron Deathmark and Piggy Bank all work without Ashad. Lost
   Thoughtcast is replaced by Clamp draw. Lightning Greaves and Padeem remain as protection.
2. **Mana screw:** one fewer rock (39 → 38); the cheapest adds are 1–2 mana, so the curve is lower (3.41 → 3.35).
3. **Graveyard hate:** blanks Razorlash recursion and the conditional loop, Royal Warden unearth, and the welders/Scrap Trawler.
   Skullclamp, Deathmark and the Warden tokens are unaffected.
4. **Counters/removal:** Deathmark has flash and is copied on opponents' turns. Countering the first artifact spell still
   uses the slot; play bait first when shields are up.
5. **Stax/artifact hate (Collector Ouphe, Stony Silence, Vandalblast):** worse for Skullclamp. Creature bodies still attack
   and pay casualty.
6. **Wipes:** more creatures are exposed. Razorlash comes back at instant speed, Warden can unearth, and Piggy Bank leaves a
   Treasure. Thopter Spy Network (which rebuilt after wipes) is lost.
7. **Combo pods:** Deathmark gives copyable instant-speed creature removal. Stack interaction is unchanged (Arcane Denial,
   Mana Drain, Fierce Guardianship, Deflecting Swat).
8. **Bracket disclosure:** adds the Spellbook-listed three-card conditional loop Razorlash + Pitiless Plunderer + Phyrexian
   Altar (4050-4871-6429) with in-deck payoffs (Mayhem Devil, Disciple). No two-card infinite. Game Changers are unchanged
   at 3 (Cyclonic Rift, Demonic Tutor, Fierce Guardianship). Bracket 4 Optimized.
9. **Losses:** Marionette Master drain, Fireweaver pings, Spy Network card draw and rebuild, Fellwar Stone mana.
