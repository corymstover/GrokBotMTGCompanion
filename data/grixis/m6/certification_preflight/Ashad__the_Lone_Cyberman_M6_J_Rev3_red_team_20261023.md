**No approval or canonical lock.** Tested exact candidate raw `352911498345e78bd55773f4c9d1c49d05599610d5fc806f7b779c85eb5f6267`. Five explicit full-100 alternatives, source 118-name Oracle pool, no global optimality claim.

## Attack 1 — first-spell and copy misconception
Ashad's first **nonlegendary artifact spell cast** on each turn is the only eligible spell for casualty 2; a player can spend it without sufficient sacrifice fodder. Sacrificing a power-2+ creature is an additional cost to copy. A casualty **spell copy** becomes an artifact token upon resolution but was not cast/created (Worldwalker Helm does not double that event). An artifact-token spell cannot receive an extra Ashad casualty trigger as a copy.
**Result:** limited to source-verifiable correct interactions. Shimmer Myr and Liberator both grant flash-casting permission; Liberator is legendary and itself cannot be the qualifying artifact spell. Its 1/2 stats initially cannot pay casualty 2; it grows later on qualifying casts.

## Attack 2 — alternate candidates
- Kappa Cannoneer is a synergistic legitimate card, improvise accelerates it, ward {4}, growing unblockable pressure. Cutting it **reduces** the combat clock, but adds redundant flash-artifact permission, more directly leveraging Ashad across a turn cycle (qualitative net positive).
- Cyberman Patrol afflict3 and 2/2 body increase pressure but Ichor Wellspring's ETB and death draw are valuable and can be copied repeatedly; retention wins engine resilience.
- Technomancer at {5}{B}{B} has a compelling reanimation ETB to double under casualty, but needs eligible artifact creature cards in graveyard. Seven MV and BB make it slower than reusable flash permission; preserve as explicit alternative.
- Canoptek Spyder does not draw from token artifact creature copies, only other **nontoken** artifact creature entries. Cryptek requires {1}{B} and tap before death and is narrower. Borg Queen was Scryfall `not_legal`, excluded.

- Conditional Razorlash Transmogrant / Pitiless Plunderer / Phyrexian Altar loop requires opponent with four nonbasic lands. Transmogrant recursion costs {B}{B} in that state; Altar yields {B} and Plunderer's Treasure yields another {B} — **mana-neutral**, requires sacrifice/die payoff. Unearthed Royal Warden sacrificed for casualty is exiled, so its death triggers do not occur.
