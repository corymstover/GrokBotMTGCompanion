# Ashnod M7 Rev2 — M6 J adversarial review

1. **Five-MV commander absent/taxed:** Basalt/Forensic and Basalt/Rings still make mana without Ashnod, Animation Module + Ravager/Novelist or Altar/Novelist still work, and Pit remains a free sacrifice outlet; Priest, Ruthless and Aether only reach doubled efficiency with Ashnod. A token-sacrifice base remains playable without commander.
2. **Early colored mana:** Grok's two rock cuts dropped 42->40 source cards. Rev2 restores one via Basalt to 41 and swaps Lounge/Orchard to unconditional untapped rainbow lands at life/damage cost. 33 lands and 8 rock/Petal cards still carry an opening-mana risk.
3. **Basalt untap trap:** It taps for colorless triple but does NOT untap at upkeep; the two-card loops require Rings trigger mana or Gadgeteer's artifact activated-cost reduction, and both are answerable. Walking Ballista is preserved to close colorless-infinite states. Infinite colorless alone is NOT a win assertion.
4. **Pit manual combo conditions:** Ashnod must copy a nonmana activated artifact ability with a creature sacrificed to PAY its cost. Pit first ability qualifies. The Pit/Novelist loop starts with a creature; if nonartifact, requires 1 starting generic mana; if artifact, Novelist provides R on first sacrifice. Novelist must survive. The token is an artifact creature and can sacrifice to the Pit, refreshing two charge counters with Ashnod and R with Novelist. This line is manually derived from Oracle, not represented as a verified Spellbook entry.
5. **Animation Module timing:** Its replacement Servo is created from a triggered optional payment of 1 after a counter is put on Ravager/Novelist. Ashnod does not copy a Module triggered ability; the Novelist mana pays 1. The archived Spellbook lists three near-included patterns with Module missing. Sacrifice fuel and counter trigger timing are not interchangeable with mana abilities.
6. **Priest summoning sickness and two-creature fodder:** Requires a full turn, plus two other creatures. The target-player wording prevents its activated ability from being a mana ability; Ashnod doubles the entire effect. Token engines mitigate but do not erase the creature-fodder cost.
7. **Ruthless Knave repeats but not for free:** {2}{B} and one creature become four Treasures with Ashnod. It can pay this cost using Treasures, but any net Treasure growth still consumes creatures; drawing two via sacrifice-three-Treasures costs three Treasures, not zero. No infinite from Knave alone.
8. **Removed real value:** Esoteric Duplicator's end-step copy loop, Fireweaver's ETB group damage, Jhoira's historic spell draw, Farid's Scrap tokens/Deathmantle route, one Talisman, and Inspector's cost reduction all matter. Each was compared to a more direct Ashnod value/combo piece. No claim that these incumbents are inherently bad cards.
9. **Graveyard and stax:** Engineer/Monitor/Petal depends on graveyard access and Engineer surviving summoning sickness. Collector Ouphe/Null Rod stop many activated artifacts (Pit/Module activations except Module triggered ability); Priest remains a creature ability. Artifact sweepers disrupt infinite combos. Protect timing with available interaction.
10. **Legality/provenance:** 100 physical /95 unique, 33 land/8 ramp including Petal, six historical Game Changers, current 2026-10-23 Oracle full selected names legal/UBR. Canonical historical source and Grok first-pass list are byte preserved. Exact SHA pinned to a new Rev2 J bundle and explicit finite tested pool; no new owner approval inferred.
