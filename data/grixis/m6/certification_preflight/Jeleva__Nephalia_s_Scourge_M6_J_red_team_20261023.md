# Jeleva, Nephalia's Scourge — M7 red-team memo (2026-10-23)

1. **Commander removed before the attack.** The linked exile pool is lost if Jeleva leaves the battlefield (CR 607.2a; 2013-10-17 ruling). A recast exiles a new, larger pool, because commander tax raises X (CR 903.8).
   - Protection: Lightning Greaves (shroud and haste), Hexing Squelcher, and the free counter suite (Force of Will, Force of Negation, Fierce Guardianship, Pact of Negation, Deflecting Swat).
   - Force of Negation's alternative cost works only when it is not your turn (Oracle).
2. **Stax and hate.** Graveyard hate stops Underworld Breach lines; Thassa's Oracle + Demonic Consultation / Tainted Pact / Doomsday remain. Rule of Law-style effects limit the free cast. Opposition Agent punishes opposing tutors.
3. **Intuition (Pass C).** Under hostile opponent choice it fails the Breach and Oracle piles (bounded T2). It stages nothing for Jeleva. Rejected.
4. **Galvanic Iteration (Pass C).** Copies are not cast (CR 707.10). A copied Doomsday is harmful. A copied Final Fortune adds nothing: each copy's lose trigger is tied to its own extra turn, so you lose at the end of the first one (Final Fortune ruling 2022-12-08 on the order of extra turns). Losing Borne Upon a Wind removes the end-step Jeleva line. Rejected.
5. **Snap alternative.** It protects and resets Jeleva and is free with two untapped lands. It stays visible as an OWNER DECISION and is not hidden.
6. **Combo / bracket disclosure.**
   - Commander Spellbook find-my-combos: 7 → 7 complete combos:
     - Thassa's Oracle with Demonic Consultation, Tainted Pact or Doomsday.
     - Underworld Breach with Lion's Eye Diamond or Lotus Petal + Brain Freeze, Wheel of Fortune + Jeska's Will, or LED + Wheel.
   - Game Changers 20 → 20. **Bracket 5 (fringe cEDH)**, unchanged.
7. **Exceptions:** none.
   - Jeleva, Nephalia's Scourge (Commander 2013, `c13`, released 2013-11-01) is Commander-legal per live Scryfall and is not a Game Changer.
   - All 100 cards are legal and inside U/B/R. Dockside Extortionist, Jeweled Lotus and Mana Crypt are banned and absent; there is no companion.
8. **Losses.** None, because the candidate equals the canonical list. What the alternatives would lose:
   - Intuition: a direct stager (P(at least one) at 8 cards falls from 0.6246 to 0.5875).
   - Galvanic: a haste/flash enabler.
   - Snap: Gitaxian Probe's free storm/Breach fuel.
   - Reversal + Scepter: Scroll Rack and Personal Tutor.
