# Dr. Eggman — M7 red-team memo (2026-10-23)

1. **Commander removed or taxed:** Eggman (MV 5, all three colours) must survive to our end step. Without Eggman, payloads must be hardcast (average nonland MV 4.221). Padeem gives artifacts hexproof, but Eggman is not an artifact. Eggman's protection is Lightning Greaves plus the stack suite (Fierce Guardianship, Mana Drain, Deflecting Swat); cutting Cryptothrall removes no Eggman protection.
2. **Opponents' free answers:** in the bounded 64-cell grid, at least one opponent has a no-cost answer in 40 cells (an empty hand, or the put branch when our hand has no payload). Payloads 28 → 31 narrow the second case; nothing fixes the first. Opponents choose in turn order (701.55) and can react to an earlier put.
3. **Put is not cast:** cast triggers never fire on a put (Cityscape Leveler is the only cast-only payload). Enters and dies triggers fire (603.6a). Put Vehicles are uncrewed; Possession Engine and Skysovereign get their value on entering, and Eggman's 3 power crews either alone.
4. **Possession Engine fragility:** the steal lasts only while we control the Vehicle. Artifact removal returns the creature, and the stolen creature can't attack or block.
5. **Removal density vs owner history:** the owner rejected Tri-Sentinel on 2026-10-14 because the deck "already has substantial removal density". The candidate adds Possession Engine, Skysovereign and Behemoth (removal on payloads) but cuts Blasphemous Act, so the number of removal effects is not raised by a pure removal add. Disclosed for the owner, not presented as a fix.
6. **Fewer sweepers:** cutting Blasphemous Act leaves Toxic Deluge, Their Name Is Death (spares our artifact creatures, not Eggman) and Cyclonic Rift. The four-swap keeping Act is staged. **FLAG:** the owner named Act as retained interaction.
7. **Foundry Inspector cut:** **FLAG:** the owner named it as retained infrastructure. A keep-Inspector five-swap (cutting Weapons Manufacturing) is staged.
8. **Graveyard hate:** Interceptor Mechan, Academy Ruins and Scrap Trawler recur from the graveyard and lose value to graveyard exile; Goblin Welder (rejected) depends on the graveyard even more.
