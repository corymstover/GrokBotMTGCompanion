## P0-025 Nalfeshnee — registered source-paid spell-copy verification

After merged P0-024, the next specific material on-identity gap is Nalfeshnee ({5}{R}). Its current Oracle text (https://mtg.wtf/card/clb/678/Nalfeshnee) copies an actual spell **cast from exile**. Copies are *not themselves cast* under Magic Comprehensive Rules 707.10, and copies of permanent spells become tokens on resolving without an independent "create a token" event under rule 111.13. Permanent copies have haste and sacrifice themselves at the beginning of the end step; the original physical permanent remains. For a Boltwave exile-cast ({R}, 3 damage to each opponent), its virtual spell copy resolves separately from the physical original; preventing or countering the original does not generally extinguish the already-triggered independent copy ability. Spell copies **do not** trigger additional Abaddon cascade, Wild-Magic cascade, The Lost and the Damned, or Keeper of Secrets on-cast damage. Target changes are allowed only if the original copied spell has targets, and those new targets are legal.

Nine bounded scenarios were preregistered before implementation in `data/grixis/m6/phase_b_plans/abaddon_p0_nalfeshnee_20261022.json`, physically using the exact preferred 100-card research challenger. The deterministic tests cover permanent copies/scheduled end-step sacrifice, Boltwave copy versus original, 3 independent Call Forth cascades, no post-cast retroactive Abaddon cascade from Boltwave life loss, countering the original, removing Nalfeshnee after ability trigger, Drannith/Rule of Law, Nalfeshnee first entering during the original cascade turn, and first-exile Wild-Magic nested cascade without infinite Nalf copy recursion. Three-player no-prevention and topdeck arrangement are explicitly **conditional**, not estimated odds or multiplayer winner outcomes.

## P0-026 preregistered — Passionate Archaeologist grants owned commander a targeted exile-cast ability

Oracle: https://mtg.wtf/card/sld/2220/Passionate-Archaeologist (Commander legal, {1}{R}). Its text grants **commander creatures you own** the trigger `Whenever you cast a spell from exile, this creature deals damage equal to that spell's mana value to target opponent.` The trigger is on the owned commander creature on the battlefield; simply having Abaddon in the command zone does not create a source on the battlefield. Damage is targeted at **one opponent per actual cast**, not each opponent. It is noncombat damage and does not count as commander **combat** damage. An MV0 exile cast still triggers and chooses a legal target but deals zero. A nonhand-cast spell countered after casting still caused the trigger; a Nalfeshnee copied spell was never cast and adds none. If an existing trigger is on stack, removing the Background cannot erase that trigger. If no legal target exists, the ability cannot be placed on the stack, even though the underlying spell was cast.

Ten specific tests were preregistered in `data/grixis/m6/phase_b_plans/abaddon_p0_archaeologist_20261022.json`, then implemented using a complete 100-card preferred challenger and paid Call Forth 5RRR off 3 actual Mountains plus 5 other physically present basics. The tests cover X9 three cascades and different targeted recipients; X0 Boltwave added life loss without retroactive Abaddon third cascade; MV0 triggered zero; countering an exiled original; absent commander; Background removal; Drannith/Rule of Law; Nalfeshnee copies; Wild-Magic Sorcerer nested actual cascade; and legal opponent target selection. Topdeck sequence and combat life loss are conditional fixtures, not empirical probabilities.
