## Explicit additions / cuts / qualitative judgment (seven, not a target or cap)

1. +Lizard Blades / -Scythe of the Wretched. Reconfigure {2} and two persistent first/damage strike steps scale Garza combat draws, Kediss, Mask of Memory, the Swords, the Reaver Cleaver and poison. Scythe costs {2}+equip {4}, and once a damaged victim dies its mandatory-looking trigger returns that victim under our control and **attaches the Scythe to the resurrected creature**, detaching from Garza; no longer efficient continuous Garza Voltron equipment. Loss: Scythe's powerful theft/reanimation value and +2/+2.
2. +Two-Handed Axe // Sweeping Cleave / -Inquisitor's Flail. Axe {2}{R}, equip {1}{R} doubles Garza's power on each attack; the {1}{R} instant Adventure can independently give double strike then let us cast Axe from exile. Unlike Flail, Axe also increases later Garza-sourced Soul's Fire/Giantfall damage that turn and does not double combat damage *to* Garza. Cost: one more to cast than Flail and a colored equip requirement, and no damage doubling before declaring attackers (or if Garza entered already attacking). Flail plus Lizard yields the same 20 base unblocked commander damage in one combat; Axe plus Lizard also 20, with extra flexible power and Adventure upside. Do not count the Adventure and artifact as two physical cards.
3. +Conqueror's Flail / -Robe of the Archmagi. Garza itself guarantees three colors among permanents: +3/+3 from a {2} cast / {2} equip, while attached opponents cannot **cast spells** during our turn (not abilities, and they may respond before equip resolves). This protects commitment to an equipped seven-mana commander and combo-combat turns. Loss: Robe's potentially enormous combat-damage-proportional card refill, especially with double strike. Existing Garza draw plus Curiosity, Mask of Memory, Rhystic Study and Sword of Fire and Ice lowers the need for another {3}+equip {4} draw engine; this is a deliberate owner-added-card re-challenge, not a free gain.
4. +Giantfall / -Rapid Hybridization. {1}{R} instant makes Garza the damage source against an opponent's creature and pairs with deathtouch and death/counter triggers, or destroys an artifact without Garza. Loss: {U} Rapid answers a creature even before Garza arrives, including indestructible, at half the mana with no setup. Keep Pongify and Deadly Rollick as early independent exile/destroy-type effects; losing this redundancy is real.
5. +Seize the Day / -Sheoldred's Edict. {3}{R} sorcery untaps Garza and provides an extra combat, and flashback {2}{R} allows a second use; directly multiplies Garza draws, counters, Kediss, poison, the Reaver Cleaver, and especially repeated Axe attack-power-doubling within the turn. Loss: edict answers hexproof/protected threats and planeswalkers (and sacrifices bypass indestructibility). Seize targets a creature and can fail if Garza becomes an illegal target; it does not automatically make infinite combats.
6. +Temur Battle Rage / -Psychotic Fury. Both give one-time double strike for {1}{R}. Because Garza starts at 5 power, Ferocious gives trample if her power is at least four when Rage resolves; that lets commander damage/infect push through chump blockers and interacts with Quietus Spike. Loss: Psychotic Fury draws a card and works on any multicolored target, whereas Rage neither cantrips nor guarantees trample after power-reduction. Double strike is not cumulative if Lizard is already attached; trample is the marginal effect then.
7. +Malakir Rebirth // Malakir Mire / -Takenuma, Abandoned Mire. At {B} and 2 life the spell side can save Garza from destroy/sacrifice damage death and avoid commander tax **if its owner leaves Garza in the graveyard rather than moving her to the command zone**, but she returns tapped, as a new object, loses counters and drops Equipment. Retains a 38th land face as black; the Mire always enters tapped. Loss: Takenuma is untapped black and has a channel recursion option, even to recover Garza from graveyard if deliberately left there. This untapped-to-tapped substitution has a real early-mana tempo penalty and is not called free.

## Deliberately rejected / deferred alternatives

- Grok's +Lizard/-Fellwar reduced castability for a seven-mana commander. Preserve Fellwar; cut Scythe instead.
- Grok's Sink into Stupor / Exotic Orchard loses multi-color fixing and costs life/untapped tempo. Preserve Orchard.
- Grok's Fell the Profane / Takenuma provides generic {2}{B}{B} removal rather than an interaction specifically protecting/empowering Garza. Prefer the low-cost Malakir spell side, with tapped land cost disclosed.
- Fireshrieker over Inquisitor's Flail is defensible but lacks the Axe's double-power/noncombat growth and Adventure flexibility, and has {3}+equip {2} setup cost.
- Hammer of Nazahn over a Talisman again weakens deployment; no rock cut.
- Nibelheim Aflame over Nova Flame or Toxic Deluge was not shown superior; do not erase the historically chosen flexible sweepers.
- Arcbond/Fire Whip/old pingers require extra damage setup, risk historical owner direction, or lose flexible removal. No manufactured eighth swap.
