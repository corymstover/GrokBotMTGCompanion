## Verified bounded mechanical arithmetic

1. **Self-safety, 7 of our own draws:** historical Spiteful + Tyranny + Krang + Ghyrson board causes 14 Spiteful damage events; each event deals 1 to us and triggers Ghyrson for 2 to us = 42 own damage; 14 Tyranny triggers can cost 28 more life absent {2} payments. **70 total potential self-life reduction** with no healing, responses or opponent elimination. Both symmetric punishment cards are now out. This is conditional event arithmetic, not an observed frequency or win rate.
2. **Trigger multiplicity:** original Nekusar with Wizard's Staff + Harmonic Prodigy + Wizard-chosen Roaming Throne + Krang has **4** draw-step extra-draw triggers and **5** damage triggers per actual opponent draw. Krang does not affect beginning-of-step. A copied Nekusar from Spark Double (not Staff-equipped) adds **3** extra-draw triggers and **4** damage triggers. With both creatures, normal draw included, 8 actual draws at that draw step and 9 Nekusar damage events per draw, assuming all remain in play.
3. **Bloodletter + Ghyrson:** Bloodletter modifies life lost, not the original 1 damage event, so Ghyrson still triggers. During our turn one opponent-draw event with Nekusar+Ghyrson and Bloodletter corresponds to **6 opponent life loss** (damage events 1+2, doubled life loss). A seven-draw wheel yields 42 per opponent only under that stable specified board, no responses/alternate prevention.
4. **Glint-Horn:** five actual cards discarded by us create five separate 1-damage events to each of three opponents. With Ghyrson, each opponent is dealt 15 damage (45 total across three), ignoring prevention; Winds of Change/Teferi's Puzzle Box put or shuffle cards into libraries rather than discarding, so that line is zero on those effects.
5. **Faerie Mastermind:** second card per opponent per turn is a single threshold crossing, even if a wheel forces seven draws. For three opponents drawing seven each on one turn, three Mastermind triggers, not 18.
6. **Cavern/Sigil caveats:** Cavern has to actually supply its restricted colored mana for Wizard Nekusar to make him uncounterable; Sigil bouncing targets are chosen on trigger stacking and can fail for lack of legal targets or intervening changes.

12 local source-bound unittest methods passed on exact baseline/candidate bytes. This is **finite rules arithmetic and integrity**, not playtesting. P0 8 methods and P1 4 bounded methods; no unsupported win-rate estimate.

## Whole-99 re-challenge, retained identity, mana, and rejected alternatives

The Oct15 99-slot audit is the fixed baseline. All noncommander roles were challenged again, with special scrutiny of slow reciprocal draw (Kami, Fevered, Stormfist, Anvil, Spiteful), punisher redundancy (Fate, Underworld Dreams, Caress, Tyranny), wheels (Magus/Jace/Whispering/Misfortune and instantaneous alternatives), defensive spells and colorless utility lands. Core retained: Nekusar, Harmonic Prodigy, Wizard's Staff, Krang, Ghyrson, Sheoldred, Orcish Bowmasters, Underworld Dreams, Kederekt Parasite, Razorkin Needlehead, Scrawling Crawler, Curiosity, Niv-Mizzet Parun, Wheel of Fortune, Windfall, Winds of Change, Dark Deal, Teferi's Puzzle Box, Wheel and Deal, Waste Not, Bloodchief Ascension, and entire efficient stack-interaction suite.

The self-risk fix deliberately trades some **independent** draw punishment for commander-specific damage/discard and bounce. The second Nekusar may increase how many cards the table draws, and can be answered; it is not an unconditional lock. Retained 35 lands and all nine fetchland sources; Cavern is a functional upgrade to Reliquary Tower *for resolving Nekusar*, not an upgrade to fixing non-Wizard spells or maintaining a maximum hand size. Mana curve is not certified by a simulated mulligan study; BBB Bloodletter competes for black sources, though Peer required BBB at seven total mana.

Serious rejected or deferred contenders:
- **Forced Fruition over Kami (M6 dispute):** six mana and doubled blue pips; can force 7 draws on each opponent spell, but can flood opponents with answers and comes down later than two-mana Kami. Conditional ceiling insufficient to claim lift without matched source-paid tests. Kami retained. No implicit M6 dispute closure.
- **Mindcrank over Niv-Mizzet (M6 Stage A):** requires active Bloodchief Ascension and changes to commander-independent life-loss/mill loop, deletes Niv/Curiosity alternate finisher. Rejected on strategic dependency, not misreported as tested loss.
- **Ophidian Eye over Phyrexian Tyranny:** genuine alternate self-safety fix, but second Curiosity-like effect helps own drawing more than Glint-Horn's proactive wheel discard damage in the targeted commander-first finish.
- **Timetwister / Memory Jar / Time Spiral over weaker wheel slots:** powerful controlled burst but current wheel density ample; marginal extra wheel is weaker than correcting self-lethal interactions and supplying Wizard-trigger multipliers.
- **Mox Diamond / Gemstone Caverns / Forbidden Orchard:** additional speed/fixing options; land-loss, pregame-dependence or gift of opposing creatures argues against silently removing the current on-plan engine or already premium lands.
- **Howling Mine / Seizan / Master of the Feast:** symmetric opponent resources without an immediate hard conversion, and early-turn aid to opponents.
- **Notion Thief / Narset, Parter of Veils / Thassa's Oracle consultation / Mindcrank+Ascension:** suppress actual opponent draws or shift victory to generic Grixis combo. Not the natural identity.
- **Roaming Throne vs another weak slot; Spark Double vs independent Fate:** adopted with explicit redundancy loss; more compact and Nekusar-specific than slow card engines.

Commander-necessity result: the added Wizard-doubler, nonlegendary Nekusar copy, optional self-discard wheel ping, bounce Aura, life-loss-on-own-wheel multiplier, second-draw card advantage and anti-counter land all become substantially better in the actual Nekusar draw-punishment shell. **Power Bracket 4 — Optimized.
