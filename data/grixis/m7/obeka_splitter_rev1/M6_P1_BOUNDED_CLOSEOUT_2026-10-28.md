Run: `python3 data/grixis/m7/obeka_splitter_rev1/m6_p1_closeout.py`. The suite checks eight grouped, positive/adversarial **rules-level scenarios** from original K4–K6/E1–E2/W1–W3/F2–F3. It is not a full stack game engine, a multi-opponent win-rate measurement, or an I/J certificate.

- **Trigger multiplication:** Roaming Throne naming Ogre or Warlock and an actually equipped Wizard's Staff each add another Obeka damage trigger. They are **additive**, not `2 × 2`: two unprevented combat damage with both effects means three damage-trigger instances and **six** extra upkeeps, not eight. No connection means zero.
- **Artifact availability:** Thopter Spy Network makes a token only when the needed artifact survives through its intervening-if check; Smaug makes one Treasure per upkeep if present; Court of Vantress needs monarch and a legal other artifact/enchantment to make a copied token. Its non-monarch branch instead changes the Court itself and does not create token artifacts.
- **Time Sieve win route is CONDITIONAL:** Time Sieve requires tapping plus five artifacts as activation cost. If the deck sacrifices the Sieve itself, that activation is not a repeatable engine. To keep it for subsequent turns, sacrifice five *other* artifacts. One Obeka hit also does not guarantee another legal, unblocked hit on the extra turn; engines and sufficient new artifact makers must survive. This is a reproducible candidate for conditional successive turns, **not proven unconditional infinite turns**.
- **Mechanized Production:** one same-name artifact plus seven payable/upkeep triggers can reach eight and win, assuming the aura, enchanted artifact and token events remain legal. `Darksteel Reactor` is not in this exact 100. The original Darksteel branches are **INAPPLICABLE_AFTER_REVISION_PENDING_FORMAL_DISPOSITION**, not passed tests.
- **Counterplay:** opponents can stop the setup by removing Time Sieve before activation or prevent combat damage; once activation costs are paid, later removal cannot retroactively undo the ability on the stack. Mana-intensive upkeep engines are not free early-game development.

**Decision:** Preserve Obeka's combat-damage-to-upkeeps engine. No card addition or cut is authorized or clearly justified by these bounded cases.
