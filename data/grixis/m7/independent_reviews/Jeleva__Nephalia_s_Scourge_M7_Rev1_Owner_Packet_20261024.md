## Exactly two proposed swaps

1. **`+Cavern of Souls` / `-City of Traitors`.** Choose **Wizard** as Cavern enters. It fixes one colored U/B/R mana specifically for casting Jeleva and gives that creature spell can't-be-countered protection if Cavern's relevant mana is spent. It also helps resolve the alternative `Thassa's Oracle` creature spell (Merfolk Wizard), and the deck's `Harmonic Prodigy` creature spell (Human Wizard). Unlike City, Cavern persists when subsequent lands are played. This strengthens the *commander itself* and a secondary creature win line instead of a generic spell shell. Wizards' official [Lost Caverns of Ixalan notes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/the-lost-caverns-of-ixalan-release-notes) independently support the chosen-type rule. **Material loss:** City taps for **two generic mana** and can deploy early two-mana acceleration or help pay commander-tax recasts, while Cavern only taps for one. Neither fixes colored mana for noncreature spells; Cave cannot shield Jeleva's attack/ETB ability or Oracle's ETB win trigger, and `Drannith Magistrate` can still stop casting Jeleva from command zone. In a deliberately restricted three-land colored-pip coverage enumeration, replacing City with Cavern changes the ability of three distinct lands to supply U/B/R for Jeleva from `1,975/2,925` to `2,250/2,925` (**+9.40 percentage points**) but omits City's generic burst, rocks, rituals, and actual turns. This is **not measured castability or win rate**.
2. **`+Fire Covenant` / `-Toxic Deluge`.** A `{1}{B}{R}` instant rather than `{2}{B}` sorcery. Both can clear a hand-cast opposing Drannith (Fire Covenant X=3 life/damage; Deluge X=3 life/-3/-3). Unlike a Jeleva freecast spell that is blocked by Drannith, Fire can answer that hatebear from hand first. Once Jeleva attacks, a free-cast Fire Covenant from her linked exile pool can allocate damage among selected opposing blockers/hatebears and **leave Jeleva and Harmonic Prodigy alive**, preserving future attack-casts. Its instant timing is also valuable before an opponent's win turn. The official current Scryfall Oracle in the preserved Oct23 Jeleva 204-card candidate snapshot verifies its text and Commander legality. **Material loss:** Fire must **pay X life as an additional cost even when Jeleva casts it free**, and X damage must be distributed/targeted; killing two 3-toughness hatebears costs 6 life instead of Deluge's 3. It cannot beat indestructibility, shroud/hexproof or damage prevention as reliably as untargeted -X/-X, and its extra `{R}` colored pip is more demanding from hand. A restricted three-land color check for candidate lands supports B/R on `2,645/2,925` triples versus at least B on `2,869/2,925` triples; ignores other mana/tempo. **Strategic judgment:** selective instant removal that preserves the central attack engine outweighs the narrower late sweeper capability in the intended fringe-cEDH natural ceiling; this is not a universal claim for every matchup.

## Important rejected alternatives

- `Flame of Anor` over `Mindbreak Trap`: Jeleva is a Wizard, enabling two modes, including draw-two/artifact destruction or five damage to a creature. This is real synergy and remains a strong future card, but removing the potentially **free** stack-exile anti-storm interaction is not clearly positive in the highest-velocity cEDH turns.
- `Spellseeker` over `Mnemonic Betrayal`: with Harmonic Prodigy its Wizard ETB can tutor two <=2-value instants/sorceries, but a three-mana creature is uncastable via Jeleva's own instant/sorcery-only attack effect, and Betrayal is a distinctive freecast opponents'-graveyard payoff.
- `Savage Beating` over `Mind's Desire`: uniquely grants an extra combat **after the current combat phase** when Jeleva casts it from attack, unlike extra-combat spells requiring a main phase; however five mana stranded in hand and losing powerful `Mind's Desire` storm lines are too consequential.
- `Snap` over `Gitaxian Probe`: true Jeleva rescue and commander reset, but loses a zero-mana storm/hand-information cantrip; bouncing her to hand also **does not increase commander tax** on a subsequent hand cast.
- `Gemstone Mine` / `Starting Town` over `Morphic Pool`: small early color-coverage improvements with finite mine counters or tapped-after-turn-three Town costs, weakening multi-turn Jeleva recasts.
- `Cavern of Souls` over `Morphic Pool`: replacing a normal UB land destroys noncreature spell fixing; 243/2,925 fewer three-land UBR spell color options in the bounded model. City is the superior displaced land type, despite its real generic acceleration.
- `Plaza of Heroes` over City: legendary casting mana and expensive protective exile line, but does not make Jeleva's spell uncounterable and requires substantial mana to save her after resolution.
- Extra low-quality swaps were not invented to reach five. See full decision and negative-control files in this PR.
