## New genuinely independent challengers

1. **+Savage Beating / -Mind's Desire (research candidate, not accepted).** This is the *right* timing-specific extra-combat effect. Wizards' official [Spider-Man release notes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/spider-man-release-notes) explicitly say `Savage Beating` is an instant cast during your own combat and its untap + additional combat happens immediately after that phase. Jeleva casts from exile **during the declare-attackers step**, so she can choose that mode and attack again, receiving another linked cast trigger. Its entwine `{1}{R}` is an additional cost and can still be paid if the spell is cast without its mana cost. However, it needs Jeleva to survive and an additional instant/sorcery in her linked exile pool on the next attack. From hand it costs **{3}{R}{R}**, whereas removing Mind's Desire sacrifices a key six-mana storm payoff that profits from free casts and can win without a surviving commander. Timing-rules PASS, whole-100 structural PASS; *not yet a proven quality-positive trade at fringe cEDH speed*.
2. **+Delayed Blast Fireball / -Toxic Deluge (research candidate, REJECTED after red-team).** Official [Baldur's Gate release notes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/commander-legends-battle-baldurs-gate-release-notes-2022-06-01): damage is **5** to each opponent and their creatures whenever cast from exile, irrespective of foretell. Jeleva indeed casts an exiled Fireball at attack-trigger resolution **before blockers**, so a staged Fireball can clear normal small opposing blockers without harming Jeleva. **Fatal anti-stax drawback:** an opposing **Drannith Magistrate** forbids casting spells from anywhere except your hand; [Wizards' Avatar release notes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/avatar-the-last-airbender-release-notes) explicitly say this overrides cast-from-exile permission. Fireball from Jeleva's exile therefore **cannot be cast while Magistrate is present**, and its {1}{R}{R} hand mode deals only **2**, insufficient for Magistrate's 1/3 body. The incumbent Toxic Deluge is hand-castable at {2}{B} with -3/-3 (3 life), killing Magistrate **before** attempting Jeleva. Fireball also cannot scale damage or defeat indestructibility. It is not an overall quality-positive cut of Deluge for this cEDH list. Keep the research file as a negative control, not an approval swap.
3. **+Haste Magic / -Torch Courier (research candidate; rejected for speed).** Haste Magic (FINAL FANTASY, {1}{R}, instant) gives a creature +3/+1 and haste and exiles top card playable until next end step. It adds a redraw-like impulse option but requires **six mana on Jeleva's natural 4-mana cast turn** for a same-turn attack, whereas Torch Courier can be predeployed for {R} and sacrificed with no turn-of-cast mana. Does not satisfy a decisive improvement.

8. **The Madcap Jester (new October 19, 2026, not staged).** Its {1}{U}{B}{R} static-trigger effect can let you play any types of opposing cards exiled by Jeleva's ETB until your next turn, with mana of any type paying their costs. This is *real* commander synergy, but requires a fragile four-mana creature established before the four-mana commander, too slow for the owner's fringe cEDH ceiling. EDHREC new-card appearance is discovery, not a quality proof.
9. **+Snap / -Gitaxian Probe (additional 100-card challenger; still marginal).** `Snap` {1}{U} returns **any target creature** to its owner's hand and untaps up to two lands. When its two untapped lands can provide the mana spent, the bounce is mana-neutral. Unlike `Into the Flood Maw` it can rescue own Jeleva from creature removal before attacks, reset her linked exile ability on a later recast, or temporarily bounce an opposing Drannith Magistrate from hand. **Commander-tax nuance:** [Wizards' Commander rule](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/commander-legends-release-notes-2020-11-06) says a commander recast **from hand** pays **no commander tax** and does not increase future command-zone tax; therefore a Snap-bounced Jeleva cast from hand ordinarily exiles just four on {1}{U}{B}{R}, NOT an automatically larger tax-boosted pool. Owner may elect to send her to command zone instead to pay tax and exile more, at a prohibitive tempo premium. When Snap is cast off Jeleva's attack-trigger exile pool, it resolves after that ability finishes; bouncing her breaks the old linked exile association. Costs versus Probe: loses a 0-mana (2-life) opponent-hand peek, cantrip, storm/Breach fuel and must hold blue mana/two lands. Under a slow interactive game the resilience is meaningful; in fast combo openings Probe can be strictly superior. **Unresolved, not counted as a proven upgrade.**
10. **+Forbidden Orchard / -Exotic Orchard (additional 100-card challenger; still marginal).** Official [Scryfall Oracle](https://scryfall.com/card/chk/276/forbidden-orchard) confirms any-color mana and a **1/1 colorless Spirit creature token** for a chosen opponent each time the land is tapped for mana. The token has **no flying**, so by normal flying rules it cannot block the 1/3 flying Jeleva; Grok's generic "feeds opponent bodies" downside is valid for sacrifice engines but does not imply these ground Spirits block the commander's attacks. Unlike Exotic Orchard, Forbidden produces U/B/R even if opposing lands produce none of those colors. However, if opposing lands already cover U/B/R, Exotic is effectively equivalent fixing **without** giving tokens, and each Forbidden activation gives adversaries sacrifice/attack/defense resources. All 27 land slots and direct color sources persist in the challenger. **Pod-composition-dependent, not automatically superior to the owner's explicitly retained Exotic Orchard.**

12. **+Flare of Duplication / -Mnemonic Betrayal (further full-100 stress; rejected as a demonstrated upgrade).** The Oct23 204-card Scryfall Oracle snapshot verifies the {1}{R}{R} Flare is legal in UBR and can instead be cast from hand by sacrificing a nontoken red creature. Casting Flare without paying its mana cost also cannot choose the creature-sacrifice alternative cost (unnecessary anyway). `Mnemonic Betrayal` is a distinct three-mana, hand-castable opposing-graveyard storm engine and is exiled as it resolves; surrendering it for a conditional copy is **not a demonstrated improvement**. ## Exact bounded land-color coverage (independent additional stress)

One *limited* check isolates the proposed land upgrade: draw any three distinct lands from the 27, model each as able to produce one of its advertised colored mana options and test whether the three jointly produce {U}, {B}, {R}. For fetchlands, allow any accessible U/B/R shock/dual target still in the library, without modeling fetch/life/tempo costs. Assume two opponents for Morphic Pool untapped, optimistically assume Exotic Orchard can produce UBR, and conservatively assume Gemstone Caverns lacks a luck counter and produces colorless. Lands cannot each pay multiple pips. This is **not** probability of casting the four-mana commander on curve (ignores the fourth generic mana, rocks, draw sequence, sequencing, conditional land entry, combat and opponents).

- Historical 27-land multiset: **1,975 of 2,925** three-land combinations cover U/B/R, or **67.52%**.
- Replace `Morphic Pool` with `Gemstone Mine` and assume Mine has at least one mining counter: **1,984 of 2,925**, or **67.83%** — gain **9 / 2,925 = ~0.31 percentage points**. The lost land after three Mine activations is **not** in this model.
- Replace `Morphic Pool` with `Starting Town`, assuming played and untapped within turns 1–3 and willingly paying one life: likewise **1,984 of 2,925**. A Town played on turn 4 or later enters tapped, a material limitation omitted by this static check.
- Replace `Exotic Orchard` with `Forbidden Orchard`, with Exotic optimistically fixing all U/B/R: **1,975 of 2,925** unchanged. Forbidden gives opponent Spirits; Exotic may fail colors in some pods not represented here.

Conclusion: tri-color options can improve some particular opening configurations, but the gain is too small and the situational downsides too consequential to certify a five-swap proposal on lands alone. Explicitly avoid labeling these numbers actual commander castability, win rates, or multiplayer simulation.

## Exact timing trap caught

- `Fury of the Horde`, `Seize the Day`, `Relentless Assault`, and `Overpowering Attack` schedule an extra combat **after a main phase**. Wizards' [Marvel Super Heroes notes on Seize the Day](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/marvel-super-heroes-release-notes) and [Spider-Man notes on Relentless Assault](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/spider-man-release-notes) make explicit that an off-main-phase resolution merely untaps creatures. They **do not** grant another combat when Jeleva casts them off an attack trigger. `Savage Beating` explicitly says **after this phase** and is combat-castable; it does.
- Rebound and cast-from-hand triggers also cannot be asserted for Jeleva-freecast spells. Jeleva does not pay optional alternative costs and `{X}` remains zero on spells cast free; optional additional costs such as entwine can still be paid.
- Jeleva's attack ability **casts during the trigger's resolution, before blockers**, per [Wizards' original Commander 2013 notes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/commander-2013-edition-release-notes-2013-10-22). It bypasses timing restrictions based on card **type** only, not additional card-specific restrictions, and it loses to Drannith's cast prohibition. Do not mistake "an exiled card is available" for "the spell is castable."

## Bounded next-attack spell opportunity (new independent exact count)

The canonical 99-card library includes **45 instants/sorceries**. In the +Savage Beating / -Mind's Desire challenger it still includes exactly **45**. Conditional on deliberately staging Savage Beating on top and on the other 98 library cards being uniformly randomized, there are **44** other instant/sorcery cards and **54** other cards in that remaining library. A first Jeleva cast with mana spent X=4 exiles Savage plus three other own cards. Exact hypergeometric probability of *at least one further own instant/sorcery NAME* in those three is `1 - C(54,3)/C(98,3) = 0.836919`; for a tax recast X=6 and five other cards `0.953431`; X=8 and seven `0.987199`. These are **conditional card-type availability calculations only**, not success rates, castable-spell probabilities, opponent-pool predictions, or simulations. Some instant/sorceries require a target, additional cost or other condition. Jeleva also exiles from opponent libraries, which is not modeled. Even when a second eligible spell is present, Jeleva must survive for a second attack and it must be legal to cast.

This supports `Savage Beating` as a genuine commander-synergy candidate but does **not** prove removing the baseline's powerful `Mind's Desire` storm-finisher improves the complete cEDH 100. A 5-mana combat-only spell stranded in hand is a material cost.
