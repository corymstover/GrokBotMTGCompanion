## New genuinely independent challengers

1. **+Savage Beating / -Mind's Desire (research candidate, not accepted).** This is the *right* timing-specific extra-combat effect. Wizards' official [Spider-Man release notes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/spider-man-release-notes) explicitly say `Savage Beating` is an instant cast during your own combat and its untap + additional combat happens immediately after that phase. Jeleva casts from exile **during the declare-attackers step**, so she can choose that mode and attack again, receiving another linked cast trigger. Its entwine `{1}{R}` is an additional cost and can still be paid if the spell is cast without its mana cost. However, it needs Jeleva to survive and an additional instant/sorcery in her linked exile pool on the next attack. From hand it costs **{3}{R}{R}**, whereas removing Mind's Desire sacrifices a key six-mana storm payoff that profits from free casts and can win without a surviving commander. Timing-rules PASS, whole-100 structural PASS; *not yet a proven quality-positive trade at fringe cEDH speed*.
2. **+Delayed Blast Fireball / -Toxic Deluge (research candidate, not accepted).** Official [Baldur's Gate release notes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/commander-legends-battle-baldurs-gate-release-notes-2022-06-01): damage is **5** to each opponent and their creatures whenever cast from exile, irrespective of foretell. Jeleva indeed casts an exiled Fireball at attack-trigger resolution **before blockers**, so a staged Fireball can clear small opposing blockers/hatebears without harming Jeleva. From hand it is **{1}{R}{R}** for just 2 damage to opponents' boards, and unlike the owner's retained `Toxic Deluge` it cannot pay life to scale damage or give -X/-X through indestructible. Conditional Jeleva synergy PASS, but 27-land UBR tempo/reliability versus flexible sweeper NOT PROVEN.
3. **+Haste Magic / -Torch Courier (research candidate; rejected for speed).** Haste Magic (FINAL FANTASY, {1}{R}, instant) gives a creature +3/+1 and haste and exiles top card playable until next end step. It adds a redraw-like impulse option but requires **six mana on Jeleva's natural 4-mana cast turn** for a same-turn attack, whereas Torch Courier can be predeployed for {R} and sacrificed with no turn-of-cast mana. Does not satisfy a decisive improvement.
4. **+Samut, Hazoret's Champion / -Torch Courier (research candidate; rejected for speed).** New Reality Fracture 2026 {1}{R} 2/2 gives all creatures you control haste continuously. Recurring haste helps tax recasts, but demands a two-mana predeployment over the owner's one-mana Courier and is creature-removal vulnerable. Net tempo gain NOT PROVEN.
5. **+Expedite / -Torch Courier (not staged).** One-mana haste cantrip looks cleaner superficially, but needing red mana **on the commander turn** raises the immediate attack requirement from four to five available mana; predeployed Courier avoids that. A cantrip is not automatically better in this turbo shell.
6. **Owner-rejected multipliers and generic tutors still rejected.** Strionic Resonator requires {2} to cast + {2} and tap for one trigger; Roaming Throne costs four, unlike already-included two-mana Harmonic Prodigy. Beseech the Mirror needs {1}{B}{B}{B} when hand-cast, Bargain fuel, and is generic tutor redundancy. None earned an incumbent cut.

## Exact timing trap caught

- `Fury of the Horde`, `Seize the Day`, `Relentless Assault`, and `Overpowering Attack` schedule an extra combat **after a main phase**. Wizards' [Marvel Super Heroes notes on Seize the Day](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/marvel-super-heroes-release-notes) and [Spider-Man notes on Relentless Assault](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/spider-man-release-notes) make explicit that an off-main-phase resolution merely untaps creatures. They **do not** grant another combat when Jeleva casts them off an attack trigger. `Savage Beating` explicitly says **after this phase** and is combat-castable; it does.
- Rebound and cast-from-hand triggers also cannot be asserted for Jeleva-freecast spells. Jeleva does not pay optional alternative costs and `{X}` remains zero on spells cast free; optional additional costs such as entwine can still be paid.
