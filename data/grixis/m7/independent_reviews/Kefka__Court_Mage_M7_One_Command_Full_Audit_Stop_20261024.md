## Independent whole-100 slot challenges — substantive final dispositions

1. **Demonic Counsel → Step Through: SELECT_ADD, provisional.** Always-active two-generic Wizardcycling directly reaches Prodigy, Gogo, Floodcaller and Oracle without delirium. Loses owner's consciously added late-game tutor for *any* card when delirium becomes active; five-mana printed value worsens Ad Nauseam reveals by +3 vs Counsel and cannot be bargained Beseech free-cast. This is role-specialization rather than strict functional dominance.
2. **Phyrexian Metamorph → Sakashima of a Thousand Faces: SELECT_ADD, provisional / high cut cost.** Retaining original Kefka and same-name clone/tokens alongside Sakashima amplifies the actual Kefka attack/discard engine. Loses three-mana-plus-two-life artifact-or-creature clone, fast artifact copying, and opponent creature utility. Four-mana blue setup is removal-sensitive; losing Sakashima causes excess Kefkas to face legend rule immediately.
3. **Culling the Weak → Dualcaster Mage: RETAIN_ALTERNATIVE.** Real copy loops with the already retained Molten Duplication, Saw in Half and Essence Flux; but many are commander-independent, and a five-mana commander wants fast black. Grok's candidate counts neither natural castability nor guaranteed partner access.
4. **Culling the Weak → Generator Servant: RETAIN_ALTERNATIVE.** Five-mana Kefka receives two colorless generic plus haste after a surviving prior-turn creature, often enabling ETB and attack. Culling can achieve same-turn black burst and pays for black requirements; Servant needs red on prior turn, other U/B/R sources and a survival window. EDHREC 4.4× lift cannot annul tempo cost.
5. **Rite of Flame → Generator Servant: REJECT.** Alternative cut retains Culling but removes a cheap immediate red ritual/Breach line for a slower, visible two-drop. Haste upside is real only in specific turn-three sequences; not superior overall in turbo matchups.
6. **Demonic Counsel / Phyrexian Metamorph → Chameleon, Master of Disguise: RETAIN_ALTERNATIVE.** Name-retaining Kefka copy and mayhem from Kefka discard are real; casting Kefka plus mayhem together ordinarily needs eight mana, Chameleon cast timing is same-turn only, and either proposed cut sacrifices valuable tutoring or flexible immediate ETB cloning. Not a *third* quality-positive simultaneous swap automatically.
7. **Demonic Counsel / Phyrexian Metamorph → Flesh Duplicate: REJECT vs leading two-slot package.** Immediate Kefka ETB for {U}{U}, but not a persistent second legendary Kefka, adds vanishing and U/U pressure, and cannot beat Sakashima's whole-copy-token payoff plus retained original cheap Phantasmal Image.
8. **Demonic Counsel → Glasspool Mimic // Glasspool Shore: REJECT.** Copies own creatures and land-mode adds a conditional 29th mana source, but Shore always enters tapped; losing live or later broad tutoring for a weaker clone/tapped land harms fast windows.
9. **Diabolic Intent → Spellseeker: RETAIN_ALTERNATIVE.** Wizard/ETB multiplier can fetch compact <=2-value spells; costs one more mana and cannot find permanent engines or arbitrary cards. Intent is already an active any-card tutor with creature-sacrifice synergy.
10. **Phantasmal Image / Culling the Weak → Irma, Part-Time Mutant: RETAIN_ALTERNATIVE.** Irma controlled since previous turn copies Kefka at precombat with her own name and can attack when newly cast Kefka cannot; no Kefka ETB, no automatic haste, no commander damage, no payoff if Irma itself has summoning sickness. Both cuts lose cheaper ETB cloning or immediate black burst.
11. **Toxic Deluge → Fire Covenant: RETAIN_ALTERNATIVE.** Instant selective opposing removal protects own Kefka in some combat windows, but B/R and paid life per point are real; Deluge defeats indestructibility and does not target, valuable versus resilient threats.
12. **Borne Upon a Wind → Entomb: REJECT.** Entomb accelerates Anger graveyard access only with viable Mountain + attack timing and no guaranteed colored Kefka deployment; Borne Upon a Wind enables protected flash wins on opponent turns and replaces itself.
13. **Talisman of Creativity → Vivi Ornitier / island → Mistrise Village: REJECT.** More Grixis spell synergy and counter protection respectively, but both impair reliable two-mana color/fetch/basic access required for timely Kefka; the prior Grok study already weighed the actual untapped-source costs.
14. **Molten Duplication → Twinflame; Culling/Intent → a Dualcaster combo pair: REJECT.** Same-two-mana clone slot already provides haste and Kefka ETB; a forced independent generic token kill displaces fast mana/tutoring without improving Kefka's unique engine enough.
15. **Demonic Counsel → Naban, Dean of Iteration: REJECT.** Doubles relevant Wizard ETB trigger but not Kefka attack trigger, fragile and narrower than Prodigy/Step Through.
16. **A free spell / copy slot → Cursed Totem: REJECT.** Commander cEDH stax adoption is real (17% / 3.2×), but shuts down Kefka's creature-activated transformation and interferes with some of the deck's creature activations while occupying a commander-independent nonengine slot.

The strongest complete 100-card two-change configuration remains the pinned **Step Through / Counsel + Sakashima / Metamorph** research artifact. Rival full-100s pairing Step Through with Generator, Dualcaster, Chameleon, Irma or Fire Covenant and broader three-swap stress configurations already exist on PR #700. An additional three to five swaps cannot be justified without deleting core acceleration, fast protection, efficient tutors or real independent clone utility. This is a **comparative quality stop**, not a five-swap quota or a model claiming a global optimum. The serious counterargument is that Sakashima's extra {U}-bearing fourth mana and enemy removal may make retaining Metamorph better, and that Counsel's delirium tutor flexibility may beat Step Through in longer cEDH games. Present those costs honestly when asking the owner for any exception.
