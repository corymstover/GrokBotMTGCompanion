## Commander-first objective

Cult of Skaro {1}{U}{B}{R} is a 4/4 legendary artifact Dalek with a random mode **whenever it is declared as an attacker**, not when it deals damage: Thay (+1/+1 counters to artifact team); Caan (draw two); Sec (3/3 menace Dalek artifact); Jast (each opponent loses 4). Expected per trigger is one-half draw, one-quarter Dalek and one life per opponent, before copied/multiplied triggers. Clone and extra-combat packages should scale attack **declarations**, supported by mass haste, protection, and enough UBR mana. Myriad tokens are created already attacking, so **do not trigger Cult on their entry** (CR 508.4); persistent copies *can trigger Cult on later turns* when legally declared.

**Material Grok correction: keeping The Master, Multiplied.** Its Oracle says triggered abilities you control cannot cause you to sacrifice/exile creature tokens. When Shaun leaves the battlefield, the Shaun-controlled triggered ability normally exiles *all Synth tokens*; **while The Master is present at resolution**, that effect cannot exile Synth creatures. The Master also prevents myriad's delayed end-of-combat exile of Auton Soldier's nonlegendary Cult copies (or Dalek Squadron tokens), letting those copies remain, *then trigger Cult when declared on later turns*. This is not an immediate trigger from entering tapped and attacking. The Master is a six-mana setup piece vulnerable to removal; preserving it is a ceiling choice with real opportunity cost. Grok's Shaun-over-Master pairing dismantled that retained-token synergy and is rejected.

**Material mana correction:** Retain Etherium Sculptor {1}{U}, which discounts Cult's generic casting cost and many artifacts; removing the third reducer slowed a 34-land, 9-rock deck. Replace Baleful Strix with Skorpekh instead, accepting lost early blocker/cantrip. The first-pass six-swap configuration and original M6 J remain untouched for independent comparison.

**Additional optimization research:** EDHREC commander-specific general/optimized/upgraded pages and 210 Oct23 Scryfall challengers were examined, including Reverse the Polarity (indexed optimized high lift), Cryptothrall, Foundry Inspector, Combat Celebrant, Mirror Box, Mechanized Production and alternate utility lands. The existing clones (Auton, Spark Double, Sakashima, Irenicus, Helm, Shuri, Nanogene), trigger multipliers (Roaming Throne, Resonator, Scientist Supreme) and extra combats (Aggravated, Full Throttle, Great Train Heist) were retained. Cavern of Souls naming Dalek would be powerful but would remove unconditional colored availability for Kindred/Mana Drain/Hammer from a 34-land base; not added. Sokenzan's nonartifact 1/1 Spirits do not enter as Daleks and were not compelling enough to trade another basic. No seventh extra-combat card was added just to inflate changes.

## Oracle and rules checks / honest bounds

- All selected 96 names are source-bound to Cult Oct23 210-name Scryfall archive plus exact two channel-land Oct23 Oracle records already archived in canonical Crosis verification; full Oracle ID/text, legality, UBR color. Source addon is additive, not a mutation of Grok snapshot. Land count stays 34: basic Island 2, Mountain 3, Swamp 2; 27 nonbasics. B/U color sources unchanged from swapping same-color untapped lands, but exposure to nonbasic hate increases.
- Original Commander Spellbook Sword of Feast and Famine + Aggravated Assault repeat-combat line remains **conditional** on connecting, enough untapped lands with {R}{R}, and proper main-phase activation; no deterministic guaranteed win asserted. 
- Cult and any Cult-copy attack trigger chooses one random option; Resonator/Scientist copies inherit an already chosen mode, Roaming Throne's separate trigger can pick independently. Shaun's token entering attacking does not trigger Cult until it can be declared in later combat with haste and/or under controller since turn began.
- Shaun/Karlach stacking: if Shaun creates Synth attacking, Karlach untaps all attackers, but Synth can declare in Karlach's extra combat only with haste (Hammer or The Dalek Emperor) due summoning sickness. Thus no automatic three Cult triggers without haste.
- Master can prevent **our triggered** Synth/myriad token exile while present; not opponent-originated exile, state-based actions, or removals. If Master leaves first, delayed/myriad may exile as normal.
- No artificial quantitative win-rate, exhaustive interaction solver, simulation, new third-model Pass C, or formal I/J certification claimed. All previously locked decks unchanged; **owner consent needed for the exact hash** before any canonical Crosis-to-Cult progression.
