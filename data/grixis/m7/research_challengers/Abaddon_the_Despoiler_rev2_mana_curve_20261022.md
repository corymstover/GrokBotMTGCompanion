# Abaddon revised 100-card mana, access, cost and physical opportunity audit

**Scope:** exact source-approved 100 and the three source-conserved M7 revised physical portfolios. This is a transparent analytic review, not a Monte Carlo result, current table win-rate estimate, guaranteed curve probability, or automatic M6/M7 approval.

## Actual source and invariants

The live canonical approved Abaddon remains 100 cards and 34 lands: 3 Island, 3 Mountain, 3 Swamp; on-color fetches, ABUR/shock duals, multiplayer duals, rainbow lands, Urborg, two channel lands, Ancient Tomb and tapped Xander's Lounge. The new six-swap proposal and both five-swap alternatives retain **exactly this same 34-land base**. The new card package changes nonland roles and pip demands, so it does not silently weaken the source's original land/fixing inventory.

Abaddon itself is {2}{U}{B}{R}; Ancient Tomb, Sol Ring and Mana Vault cover generic but do not alone provide U/B/R. Arcane Signet, three Talismans, Chrome Mox, rainbow lands and seven fetches supply early colored options. No exact turn-five casting percentage has been asserted; previous simulations lacked a complete color/interaction model. Fetchability and colored mana access can be objectively checked without pretending a fixed multiplayer rate.

## Slot-level payment differences

| Source change | Exact relevant payment or MV | Role and casting limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rakdos → Grinning Ignus | BBRR conditional 4 vs Ignus {2}{R}, bounce activation {R} | Lower splash-specific pip requirement, but Birgi and Abaddon X>=3 needed for repeated meaningful hand-cast cascades. Ignus is not a mana rock by itself. |
| Inevitable Betrayal → Chaos Warp | Betrayal MV0, no payable printed cost; Warp {2}{R} | The normal hand now contains a payable three-MV interactive spell instead of a suspend-only card, at the cost of a free zero-MV cascade hit. |
| Bituminous Blast → Spark Double | {3}{B}{R} vs {3}{U} | One fewer targeted five-mana spell, one additional four-mana blue commander-copy payoff; both require actual mana and a board state. |
| Cabal Ritual → Orcish Bowmasters | {1}{B} ritual vs {1}{B} flash creature | Exactly the same initial generic/black source requirement. Cabal adds B net and can add BBB net at threshold; Bowmasters is not acceleration, so burst black availability decreases. |
| Force of Negation → Cyclonic Rift | Force {1}{U}{U} or free on opponents' turn; Rift {1}{U} or overload {6}{U} | Cheaper normal paid spell and one fewer colored blue pip; seven paid mana for overload, only one U pip. Loss of an opponents'-turn free counter remains important. |
| Exalted Flamer → Aggravated Assault | Flamer {2}{U}{R} vs Assault {2}{R}, ability {3}{R}{R} | Smaller initial multicolor requirement, but extra combat is impossible without five actual paid mana each time, supplied conditionally by Neheb. |

The combined changes add no land, do not require new out-of-identity colors, and shift some casting costs away from BBRR and UU toward accessible {R}, {B}, {U}. They also reduce generic direct acceleration and one free counter; this is a real opportunity cost, not a free improvement.

## Direct commander threshold and legal hand-cast requirements

- Opponents losing three each via Boltwave or a *resolved* Loyal Subordinate trigger produce X=9 in a three-opponent game; after that, all six new cards with printed MV2–4 qualify for Abaddon cascade when actually cast from hand on your turn. Without an actual resolution of that loss, no such grant is assumed.
- Orcish Bowmasters by itself triggers once on entering; three opponents each drawing seven **during your turn** outside their draw-step first-draw exemption can cause up to 21 separately resolving one-damage triggers. These may increase X as damage resolves, not on declaration or when queued.
- Rift overloaded payment of seven retains printed MV2; a free cascade cast from exile cannot also select overload. Spark entering as a nonlegendary Abaddon copy requires a controlled original at entry and applies to **future qualifying** hand spells.
- Neheb starts making red in each postcombat main based on **total opponents' life lost that turn**. With Loyal-triggered X=9, there is a source-paid route to Assault's activation with or without drawing extra colored mana once Neheb resolves. Opponent interaction may interrupt at every step.

## Resilience, draw and land roles

The deck retains Wheel of Fortune, Windfall, Rhystic Study, Mystic Remora, Brainstorm, Ponder and premium tutors; replacing Cabal rather than adding Dig/Treasure preserves six or more plausible grave cards for Underworld Breach escape. The compact LED/Breach/Brain Freeze backup remains unmodified and is not presumed to be infinite under every graveyard or priority context.

The loss of Force of Negation is the principal defense reduction against opposing-stack combo on their own turns. Preserved free or one-mana interaction includes Force of Will, Fierce Guardianship, Deflecting Swat, Pact of Negation, Swan Song, Flusterstorm and Pyroblast. Rift and Warp add resolved-permanent answers that were missing or conditional in the source. Neither is a permanent cure for every stax effect.

## Result and alternatives

These three 100-card variants all preserve the approved 34-land count and singleton. The preferred six-swap version improves access to multiple payoffs without inserting an untapped-land cosmetic change merely to meet an owner swap count. Both five-swap alternatives preserve the original Exalted Flamer and trade away one distinct win engine, which may be safer in some removal-heavy games. The six-swap version is authored as the strongest realistic Abaddon-first natural-ceiling candidate **with explicit variance**, not an experimentally proven superior win-rate.

Before exact final owner approval, the revised candidate must pass source-bound mechanical and I/J receipts and the strict M7 decision gate. No official source list, owner approval, original Pass C provenance or milestone acceptance is modified by this report.
