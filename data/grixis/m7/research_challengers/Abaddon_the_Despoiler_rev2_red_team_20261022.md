## Adversarial case 1: the copied commander dies or never survives

Spark Double for {3}{U} is the biggest engine upside precisely because it copies Abaddon's defining grant as a nonlegendary creature. If original Abaddon leaves before Spark enters, there is no Abaddon to copy. If both creatures are removed before subsequent paid hand casts, no additional cascade grant remains. The benefit is **two distinct cascade triggers for each qualified future hand-cast**, not an automatic win or multiplier on spells only copied or cast from exile. A conventional mono-interaction creature-heavy table might favor retaining Bituminous Blast's immediate 4-damage and printed cascade instead. Reasoned selection: Spark increases commander-first chain efficiency over several spells and can copy another creature/planeswalker if Abaddon is unavailable; the list keeps Deadly Rollick, Blasphemous Act, Noise Marine and Call Forth creature control. This is a synergy-ceiling decision, not observed win superiority.

## Adversarial case 2: the wheel and Bowmasters never overlap

Orcish Bowmasters provides a single ETB trigger even without a wheel, not 21 damage by itself. A Wheel of Fortune or Windfall on its controller's turn can produce 21 separate triggers for three opponents drawing seven apiece; pending triggers do **not** raise Abaddon's X until their actual opponent-directed damage resolves. Removal before draws, a countered wheel, target protection, or changing hand counts changes the result. Cutting Cabal Ritual also removes an unrestricted black ritual and conditional five-black ceiling; this can delay casting Abaddon or a Breach loop even if Bowmasters has bigger tactical upside. Selection: a 1B flash card doubles as creature interaction and potentially lowers the barrier to X>=8 without giving opponents free cards itself; original wheels were already wanted for Breach/refill. Sheoldred's 2BB draw punishment has a larger conditional X ceiling but worse early deployment and no meaningful benefit to exceeding maximum relevant payoff thresholds by dozens.

## Adversarial case 3: resolving a stax piece is not equivalent to preventing a counterspell

Replacing Force of Negation with Rift trades free counterspell protection **on opponents' turns** for a real answer to already resolved nonland permanents. Rift normally costs {1}{U}; overload costs {6}{U}; it retains MV2 for Abaddon's cascade if **actually cast from hand** on your turn with X>=2. It cannot take both the no-mana-cost cascade option and overload simultaneously. The bounced stax card can be replayed. The list still has Force of Will, Fierce Guardianship, Swan Song, Flusterstorm, Pact of Negation and Deflecting Swat to defend a stack-based line, plus Chaos Warp for permanent answers. A stack-heavy no-permanent combo table could prefer Force of Negation; a pod with Rule of Law, Drannith and resolved artifacts justifies Rift/Warp.

## Adversarial case 4: Assault adds another dead three-mana draw

Assault's {3}{R}{R} activation is not mana-free. A controlled commander plus Loyal Subordinate during regular combat (three opponents lose 3 each, X=9) and Neheb in the following postcombat main provide 9 red: enough to cast Assault from hand ({2}{R}) and activate once (five additional red) with one red remaining. Subsequent combat and postcombat main can grow cumulative same-turn loss and Neheb mana. Removal of Abaddon before Loyal resolution, removal of Neheb before the postcombat trigger, or removal of Assault before activation breaks that loop. Cutting Exalted Flamer of Tzeentch removes a 40K instant/sorcery trigger and delayed random recursion; that is the six-swap portfolio's highest identity cost. The clone-focused **five-swap alternative preserves Flamer**. Selection: on-list Neheb and Loyal already form the unusual required pieces, and Assault unlocks a secondary commander-first finish when expensive spells/exile chains stall. This is conditional, not unconditional infinite combat.

## Adversarial case 5: losing printed cascade and MV0 theft reduces baseline floor

Bituminous Blast supplies printed cascade plus 4 damage at instant speed, and when Abaddon qualifies its hand cast it can have two cascades; Spark only multiplies later actual hand-casts. Inevitable Betrayal is a free zero-MV cascade hit that can put a powerful creature from an opponent library onto your battlefield; replacing it with Warp lowers zero-MV hit density. Keeping the original can be better in creature-heavy high-power pods that lack important resolved noncreature stax. Selection: the combined package adds direct answers, repeatable hand-cast chain and two source-specific victory conversions. The original list retains Profane Tutor/Ancestral Vision as zero-MV cascade value and printed cascade in Noise Marine and Call Forth the Tempest. No claim every single cascade hit improves.
