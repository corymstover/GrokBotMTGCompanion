## What EDHREC newly contributed

Read the live [Admiral commander overview](https://edhrec.com/commanders/admiral-beckett-brass) (unfiltered, 2026-10-22), [Admiral theft-theme view](https://edhrec.com/commanders/admiral-beckett-brass/theft), [EDHREC's October 1 lift change](https://edhrec.com/articles/changelog-replacing-synergy-with-lift-on-edhrecs-card-pages), and [EDHREC's methodology FAQ](https://edhrec.com/faq). The current overview labels its distinctive recommendations **High Lift**, a ratio measuring co-inclusion against a relevant baseline, **not** the older synergy percentage. Its 10 highest-inclusion cards are already in the locked canonical 100; those statistics are no justification to retain them automatically. The high-lift section revealed six cards outside the canonical 100 and prior 24 research candidates: Storm Fleet Aerialist, Kitesail Corsair, Dreamcaller Siren, Kukemssa Pirates, Deadeye Brawler and Prying Blade; Slippery Scoundrel was already analyzed. The exact observed percentages and values, current Scryfall Oracle text/legality and differentiated strategic candidate dispositions are preserved in `Admiral_Beckett_Brass_EDHREC_inclusion_lift_discovery_20261022.json`.

**Strongest overlooked leads:** Storm Fleet Aerialist is a 2-mana **Pirate flyer** that can enter as a 2/3 after you attacked, increasing actual Pirate air density; Dreamcaller Siren is a 4-mana **flash Pirate flyer** that can tap two nonland permanents when another Pirate is controlled. Both can contribute to Admiral's *three distinct Pirates damaging the same player* in later combats, with real timing/mana constraints. These are genuinely role-specific cards, not ubiquitous Grixis value staples.

**Important adverse findings:** Aerialist entering after the turn's combat gets the Raid counter, but cannot retroactively be a damage source. Dreamcaller tapping only two defenders leaves blockers if more than two are present; unless haste is independently granted, its newly entered body cannot attack that combat. Kukemssa Pirates trading its own combat damage for artifact control directly obstructs Admiral's three-damage-source condition. Deadeye Brawler costs four and needs ascend and successful combat for one draw, versus the existing Bident engine. Prying Blade demands cast + equip + combat success and creates no Pirate. Kitesail Corsair's flying is attack-only, with a meaningful floor versus other evasive Pirates. Popularity and lift are discovery signals only.
