## Important new full-deck judgment

The previous A/B alternatives removed **Merchant Raiders** and package A also removed **The Indomitable**. Both are disproportionately valuable here. Present Scryfall Oracle confirms Merchant Raiders' tap lockdown persists during the opposing controller's untap step **for as long as you control Raiders**; Daring Piracy supplies a Pirate on every turn's combat to fuel that trigger. The Indomitable draws whenever **any creature you control** connects even if the Vehicle is not crewed. `Open into Wonder` at X=3 supplies three guaranteed unblockable sources and three potential combat-draw triggers for five mana. Cutting all this for one-shot blocker tap plus finishers is less appealing than preserving it.

The strong 100-card *research hypothesis D* preserves Merchant Raiders, The Indomitable, **and Open into Wonder** while moving five strategically challenged slots toward cheap evasive Pirate sources, attacking haste and exact-turn UBR access.

City's tapped-trigger damage is **not a mana activation life-payment cost**. Its boast cannot be used before attack, twice in the turn, or with a Treasure also spent for mana.
