### Why keeping Keeper wins the strategic judgment, despite its high MV

Keeper is **not merely a high-end Warhammer decoration**. For each spell actually cast outside your hand while Keeper is controlled, Keeper's *Symphony of Pain* triggers and deals damage equal to that spell's printed mana value to **one chosen target opponent**. This provides direct, repeatable opponent life loss **during Abaddon's turn**; the added loss raises X *after the trigger actually resolves*, permitting subsequent higher-MV hand casts to gain cascade. The newly recommended Ignus engine creates more opportunities for such nonhand hits. A cast from exile also produces a 3/3 Spawn under The Lost and the Damned, so retaining Keeper gives **damage plus tokens**, not a choice between the two. Keeper is a 6/4 haste/first-strike body as a secondary attacker. Six mana and removal exposure are real downside; an adversarial removal spell before the exile casts disables Keeper's additional life-loss triggers.

Inevitable Betrayal is a **genuinely strong alternative casualty**, not a dismissed bad card. Its lack of a mana cost makes its MV zero, allowing it as a free hit to any cascade whose originating spell MV is at least 1. It can steal a high-impact creature straight from one targeted opponent's library. However: it cannot be normally paid and hardcast from hand; its natural suspend mode costs 1UU and delays three upkeeps, and a suspended spell cast from exile **does not gain Abaddon's hand-cast cascade**. The effect **puts** an opponent's creature onto your battlefield; it does **not cast that creature spell** or create an additional Keeper/Lost cast trigger. The opponent-library quality is outside our control and may lack a relevant creature. Keeper's trigger on *casting Inevitable Betrayal itself* would deal **zero damage** since its MV is zero. Cutting Betrayal lowers premium MV0 cascade-hit density and may decrease access to opponent combo creatures, but the total 40K cascade/damage engine remains intact; The Ruinous Powers, Keeper and Lost are all deliberately protected.

**Most powerful rebuttal to this choice:** Betrayal being a zero-MV cascade hit makes it disproportionately attractive when Abaddon fires low-value cascades early. In creature-dense optimized pods, stealing a creature can end a game and avoids needing six mana to deploy Keeper. Keeper's conditional trigger can fail to occur at all in a fast, removal-heavy pod. That is why the Keeper-cut variant remains a documented **RETAIN_ALTERNATIVE** portfolio, not a forbidden backup. An actual decision-changing Oracle defect or live opposing-context evidence could reverse this qualitative judgment; rerunning a goldfish with no opponent creatures cannot.
