## Strategic objective and complete opportunity cost

Admiral requires **combat damage dealt to one player by at least three separate Pirate sources** in the same turn, then remains on the battlefield for her beginning-of-end-step triggered ability to target a nonland permanent controlled by that player. This is neither three total combat damage, nor three attackers declared, nor three generic creatures. The strongest whole deck must first deploy actual cheap Pirates, create **same-player multi-source combat connections** against live blockers, survive responses, and retain card access / re-deployment after interaction. The theft payoff is repeated rather than an isolated one-shot combo.

The D baseline contains **100 physical cards, 35 lands, 34 creature cards** and preserves `Open into Wonder`, `Bident of Thassa`, `The Indomitable`, `Kindred Discovery`, `March of the Drowned`, `Path of Ancestry`, `Fiery Cannonade`, `Merchant Raiders`, and `Daring Piracy`. These are not all untouchable, but every extra challenger must pay for the exact job it displaces:

- **Colored mana / development.** D's City of Brass for Crumbling Necropolis removes one forced tapped land and provides U/B/R for Admiral's actual `{1}{U}{B}{R}` cost. Shizo for Path is not automatically improved mana: Shizo is black-only and its fear activation consumes a second black mana plus a tap; Path taps but subsequently produces any commander color and scries on Pirate casts. The physical 35-land count alone cannot establish color reliability. Preserve Arcane Signet, signets/talismans, Cavern, Secluded Courtyard, Command Tower and untapped colored sources when evaluating curve.
- **Low-drop Pirate supply and evasion.** `Cloud Pirates` and `Departed Deckhand` add early evasive Pirate *sources*, `Skyship Plunderer` a flying Pirate/counter interaction, and `Broadside Bombardiers` a Pirate attack-pressure tool. Storm Fleet Aerialist and Kari Zev both have Pirate printed subtype; Aerialist changes the *kind* of evasion, not the number of printed Pirate cards. Kari Zev's temporary attacking Ragavan is a red **Monkey token, not Pirate**; the separate `Ragavan, Nimble Pilferer` card **is a Monkey Pirate**. Do not confuse the two.
- **Cross-turn draw.** `Bident of Thassa` costs `{2}{U}{U}` and can draw for *every* one of our creatures dealing player combat damage every combat, not just Pirates; `The Indomitable` has a second such combat-draw trigger and potential graveyard recast conditional on tapped Pirates/Vehicles. `Kindred Discovery` fuels Pirate entries/attacks. `Open into Wonder` at X=3 costs five mana, renders three chosen attackers unblockable for the turn and draws up to three on hits. The package survives individual trades and feeds recovery; removing Bident or Open for one-source evasion loses real repeat-card access.
- **Recursion / wrath recovery.** `March of the Drowned` at `{B}` returns two Pirate cards or one creature, making two-player-removal trades recoverable very cheaply. `Admiral Brass, Unsinkable` and `Double Down` are slower different recursion/copy engines. `Captain Storm, Cosmium Raider` can buff Pirates from artifact/Treasure entries but does **not** compensate for March's recovery, and +1/+1 counters alone do not solve three blockers.
- **Removal / protection / adverse opponents.** `Fiery Cannonade` is a **three-mana instant** dealing two to each non-Pirate creature, both a reactive survival tool and favorable Pirate board reset against small blockers. `Toxic Deluge` can kill bigger boards at a life cost, but can also kill our Pirates. `Raise the Palisade` at five mana mass-bounces nonselected creature types (select Pirate), a second one-shot way to clear blockers. `Cyclonic Rift`, counterspells, Siren Stormtamer, Lightning Greaves and free interaction supply different safety/timing. `Cosmotronic Wave` costs four at sorcery speed, deals one to each opposing creature, and prevents *all* opponents' creatures blocking that turn. It creates an actual three-source offensive window against bulky blockers but trades away Cannonade's speed/two-damage coverage if swapped into that slot.
- **Protected commander-first conversion.** `Merchant Raiders` creates an ongoing targeted blocker tap-lock from Pirate entries. `Daring Piracy` supplies a hasty menacing **Pirate** token each combat as a possible third hit source. `Roaming Throne` doubles relevant Pirate triggered abilities, `Don Andres` helps exploit stolen resources, `Forerunner of the Coalition` (cut by D) would topdeck a Pirate and repeatedly drain on Pirate entries; losing it is a real consistency and reach cost, not a free cut. `Port Razer`'s extra combat cannot re-attack the same player with itself that turn but can allow other Pirates more combat chances; `Zara, Renegade Recruiter` is a five-mana flying Pirate with a separate temporary-theft axis. None should be cut merely to enlarge the change count.

## Finite opposing boards; what the mechanics really discriminate

1. **Three ready Pirates vs four conventional blockers, sufficient mana.** `Open into Wonder` X=3 costs `{3}{U}{U}` and can make all three qualifying Pirates unblockable, drawing cards after damage. `Dreamcaller Siren`'s tap-two on entry cannot guarantee the same outcome against four blockers; entering at flash speed does not make Siren able to attack immediately. `Cosmotronic Wave` at `{3}{R}`, if resolved in the active player's main phase before combat, gives the whole team a one-turn unblocked window regardless of blocker toughness. Each plan still loses the Admiral threshold if opponent removes/fogs one of exactly three qualifying sources or Admiral leaves before the end-step trigger.
2. **Aerialist vs Kari on different block configurations.** With two ground-only blockers and no flying defense, Aerialist can connect as Pirate while Kari can be double-blocked under menace. With a lone flyer or reach blocker and no ground blocker, Kari's menace gets through but Aerialist can be blocked. Kari's Ragavan Monkey attack may create additional **Bident/The Indomitable** card draws if it connects, even though it cannot count toward Admiral's three Pirate sources. Neither choice globally dominates; do not count Aerialist as +1 Pirate subtype.
3. **Small-creature pressure before our theft combat.** An opponent develops multiple 2-toughness non-Pirates and threatens lethal damage with open combat. Cannonade can be cast as an instant during that attack and deal two damage broadly without killing our Pirate creatures on subtype grounds; Wave cannot be used at opponent's combat and its one damage is not comparable. **This is a non-negotiable weakness of both current Wave-for-Cannonade lists**.
4. **Large-toughness defender, our one decisive theft window.** The opponent's blocker has five toughness; Cannonade's two damage by itself does not clear it. Wave prevents it blocking this turn and needs one fewer colored blue mana than Open's X3 three-Pirate variant. But Wave draws no cards and is sorcery-speed. If the three Pirates already exist and mana is available, Wave creates an extra tactical approach rather than strictly replacing Open.

No deckwide match win rate is asserted. The finite fixtures can validate exact costs/Oracle limits and specific legal battle states, **not** the frequency of these states against live opponents or absolute package superiority.

## Commander-specific candidate decisions (qualitative, bounded)

- **Storm Fleet Aerialist: RETAIN_ALTERNATIVE.** Real low-drop flying Pirate with conditional Raid size, low commitment. Can win airborne races if opponents favor ground blockers; loses Kari Zev's menace, first strike and Monkey-token card-draw upside. No source-bound evidence yet that removing Kari is better in *this* whole deck.
- **Cosmotronic Wave: RETAIN_ALTERNATIVE, strongest new full-team unblock axis.** Its extra offense can make the actual 3-hit target achievable. Yet replacing Fiery Cannonade weakens reactive defense; a different incumbent cut must be examined before a final choice.
- **Brotherhood Regalia: RETAIN_ALTERNATIVE.** Repeatable single legendary Pirate unblockability and ward can preserve Admiral herself, but Bident draw loss is real; not selected in the two resilient lists.
- **Glaring Spotlight: RETAIN_ALTERNATIVE.** A whole-team one-shot with a meaningful hexproof interval, but after sacrifice the engine is gone; direct Bident cut is costly.
- **Dreamcaller Siren: RETAIN_ALTERNATIVE in exactly-two-blocker/flash windows, REJECT as current replacement for Open into Wonder.** It offers later flying Pirate attacks but is not an immediate attacker and cannot reliably neutralize three or four blockers with two taps.
- **Captain Storm, Cosmium Raider: RETAIN_ALTERNATIVE.** Counters synergize with artifact entries / Skyship Plunderer but do not by themselves make ground Pirates hit; preserving March is preferred in the current contested resource model.
- **Shizo, Death's Storehouse: RETAIN_ALTERNATIVE.** Untapped B and one legendary evasion line matter in narrow starts; color range / scry and activation costs argue to retain Path absent discriminating mana-hand evidence.
- **Kitesail Corsair: RETAIN_ALTERNATIVE, lower priority than Aerialist.** Attack-only flying is more conditional than always-on flying when defending/engaging combat.
- **Kukemssa Pirates, Deadeye Brawler, Prying Blade: REJECT.** Exact sourced EDHREC discovery record captures their insufficient hit-conversion-to-cost, own attack-hit substitution, ascend dependency or equip setup.
- **Subira, Tulzidi Caravanner: REJECT** in standard anthem-board model: her power-2-or-less evasion restriction excludes base-2 Pirate attackers once Admiral's +1/+1 is present, and Subira herself is not Pirate.
- **Teleportal: RETAIN_ALTERNATIVE** for wider than three attack crews; overload costs five and lacks Open X3's draw, despite +1/+0.
- **War Cadence, Strionic Resonator, Molten Echoes, Amphin Mutineer: RETAIN_ALTERNATIVE / oracle-gated**, not validated selections. Tax payment, trigger-copy timing, transient token/legend rules and encore multi-opponent attack restrictions require source and fixture proof before use.
