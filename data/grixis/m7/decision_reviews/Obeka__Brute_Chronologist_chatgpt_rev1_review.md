## The actual strategic decision

Obeka's `{T}` permits the *active player* to end the turn. The commander cannot force an opposing active player to agree. For our own `at the beginning of the next end step` liabilities, let the trigger enter the stack and THEN end the turn over it. Skipping our end step too soon generally postpones the liability to the next end step. Removing a one-shot delayed trigger after it has fired is permanent; removing a normal repeating triggered ability once is not. `until end of turn` durations still expire.

The October 15 list contains excellent ready-Obeka infrastructure (Greaves, Boots, Thousand-Year Elixir, Arena of Glory, Minamo, Courser), all four risky-turn effects, Scepter/Final Fortune, Sundial/Discontinuity backups and counterspells. Preserve those. Its weakest opportunities are attack-gated five-mana creatures, opponent-dependent temporary clones, a symmetrical wheel and one copied-creature mechanic whose Oracle is frequently misread. The chosen six additions turn the deck into a more consistently accessible one-shot engine with cloneable tutor ETBs and Kiki/Twin finishing routes, without adding unrelated Oracle/Consultation shells.

### Exact six additions and cuts, with honest costs

1. **+Footsteps of the Goryo / −Delina, Wild Mage.** Footsteps (2B) has a true one-shot next-end-step sacrifice that Obeka permanently clears for the reanimated creature. Wizards explicitly clarified Delina's token has a **normal, recurring end-of-combat** exile ability. Exiling it once does not make a Delina token safe forever. Footsteps needs a creature already in our graveyard and grants no haste; Delina was still capable of attacking clones and a conditional Kiki high roll.
2. **+Entomb / −Windfall.** One-black instant loads Courser, Spellseeker, Zealous, Kiki or a suitable creature into our graveyard for Footsteps/Reanimate/Necromancy/Apprentice/Feldon. Windfall's symmetric draw could be useful but also refills opponent interaction and is not a specific Obeka engine. Entomb is ineffective alone against graveyard hate.
3. **+Mimic Vat / −Purphoros, Bronze-Blooded.** Vat converts a nontoken death into a repeatable hasty token with one-shot next-end-step exile; Obeka saves token copies, particularly tutor/copy ETBs. Its initial cast is 3 and activation costs another 3/tap; it competes with reanimation for imprinted cards. Purphoros provided indestructible global haste and another hand-cheat route; numerous haste enablers and Sneak Attack remain.
4. **+Zealous Conscripts / −Ilharg, the Raze-Boar.** Both cost five, but Conscripts is hasty and acts on ETB to steal/untap immediately. Its untap matters for Obeka, and with Kiki-Jiki it creates finite-repeatable hasty Conscripts copies without requiring an attack turn from Ilharg. The original Ilharg provided repeated any-color creature cheat, and ordinary stolen control from Conscripts expires at cleanup.
5. **+Splinter Twin / −Hate Mirage.** Enchant an own creature and make hasty one-shot-exile copies that Obeka can keep; with Conscripts enchanted, each token untaps the source, enabling repeated tokens. It also multiplies Spellseeker, Courser and Puppeteer ETBs. Hate Mirage could clone two opposing bombs immediately and needed no Aura exposure; Twin needs a ready creature and can be removed.
6. **+Spellseeker / −Avaricious Dragon.** Spellseeker's ETB tutors any instant/sorcery MV2 or less, including Final Fortune, Twinflame, Entomb and cheap protection, and is itself excellent with Vat/Reflection/Kiki/other clones. Avaricious Dragon's extra card each draw step was potent with Obeka managing its *recurring* discard, but slower and less pointed. Psychic Vortex remains as the strong high-lift recurring draw engine alongside Rhystic Study and Mystic Remora.

All six fit the natural commander plan; mana value decreases from 25 total removed to 19 added across these six slots, while the 34-land colored base stays intact.

## Post-floor quality challenge and rejected complete packages

This review did not stop at five: twelve incumbent roles and seventeen material new-card possibilities were adjudicated in the JSON decision record, including recent EDHREC high-lift/inclusion and optimized-tag discovery. Live EDHREC general Obeka listed Footsteps at 11% inclusion / 37 lift, and optimized view showed Zealous Conscripts 19% / 14 lift and Splinter Twin 16% / 32 lift. These figures are discovery, not performance tests. [General](https://edhrec.com/commanders/obeka-brute-chronologist), [optimized](https://edhrec.com/commanders/obeka-brute-chronologist/optimized).

- **Exact seven-swap alternative:** Add Muddle the Mixture / cut Counterspell on top of selected six. Gains transmute for both Scepter/Final Fortune (MV2) but sacrifices a needed hard counter for enemy permanents. Complete physical 100 staged, SHA-256 `867c8b564567c1a1f74fa56aca6a7479ee77c8ee2d54c3d381a6689e7c50cd1c`.
- **Exact eight-swap alternative:** Also add Corpse Dance / cut Rionya, Fire Dancer. Gains hasty top-graveyard recursion with buyback (total five mana), but loses durable repeating own-combat token copies and becomes more graveyard/order sensitive. Complete physical 100 staged, SHA-256 `cff999cafa5861ed6e66d4f6b1ed95ce37cd639d5dfc984e4a3db62dc0eb5337`.
- **Dawn of the Dead** lost to preserved Psychic Vortex: Dawn is five mana with **BBB**, needs creatures in the yard and starts on upkeep. Vortex's cumulative upkeep asks the controller to **draw** a growing number of cards, while Obeka handles the new discard/land-sacrifice end-step trigger; far higher unique value.
- **Cauldron Dance** is combat-only, six mana and needs both hand and grave creatures to beat retained flash-modal Venser. **Treacherous Urge/Zara** are opposing-hand-dependent. **Orthion** is slower tap-based cloning; **Tribute Mage/Imperial Recruiter** require trading away two-mana ramp or unconditional Counterspell. **Stifle** only answers one trigger rather than the whole emergency turn. **The Master, Multiplied** protects tokens by reducing the need to use Obeka at all, violating the blueprint's commander-first rule.

There is no intellectually honest seventh quality-positive cut after comparing these alternatives. The smaller selected package is better because it keeps both general answers and recurring native cloning. No performance win rate or high-volume model trial is claimed; the old M5 goldfish scores were effect-limited.
