## Identity and ceiling

Missy is a six-mana Grixis nonartifact-creature death converter and villainous-choice Cyberman payoff. Her defining engine is: a nonartifact creature dies while Missy is present; Missy returns its card face down and tapped as a 2/2 artifact Cyberman; a printed morph creature may turn face up (ceasing to be an artifact), then die again to repeat the process. Missy also steals eligible opponents' dead creatures. Repeatable free reveal-morph bodies and free sacrifice outlets generate commander-dependent loop kills.

Natural ceiling: **Power Bracket 4 — Optimized**, not a generic consultation/breach cEDH shell. Protect cheap nonartifact death fodder, morph access, artifact ETB payoffs, opposing-creature removal, villainous choices, and explicit Cyberman conversion.

## Adversarial candidate challenges and rejected additional changes

- **Mox Diamond**: additional zero-mana perfect fixing, but 35 lands plus opening turn needs make land discard a meaningful failure mode; Chrome Mox retains lands.
- **Gemstone Caverns**: excellent pregame opener conditional on not starting, but otherwise a colorless land; no clear improvement over remaining 35-land colored mana.
- **High Market**: uncounterable land-based sacrifice outlet, but taps only for colorless, requires tapping to sacrifice, and 6 independent no-mana outlets are already present after Carrion Feeder.
- **Mana Drain**: hard counter plus future colorless burst, but UU is demanding and free Missy-contingent Guardianship better addresses immediate removal during the six-mana turn.
- **Snuff Out**: free targeted destruction and stolen nonblack creature upside, but protection and cheaper repeatable death/conversion won current slots; it cannot hit black or indestructible creatures.
- **Imperial Seal**: powerful tutor, but deck already retains Demonic Tutor, Diabolic Intent and Vampiric Tutor, and six-mana commander reliability/protection was a stronger bottleneck.
- **Warren Soultrader**: Treasure-producing sac outlet at a life per activation; finite without life gain, compared against unconditional free Carrion Feeder and existing Phyrexian Altar.
- **Mirkwood Bats/Marionette Apprentice**: efficient artifact-token/death punishers, but the face-up repeat loops die as nonartifact creatures, so Blood Artist/Zulaport/Mayhem/Reckless Fireweaver and new Brood already convert them into kills.
- **Vesuvan Shapeshifter**: flexible morph body but two mana to turn up, and inconsistent combo assistance compared with free-reveal-morph suite.
- **Roaming Throne**: choose Time Lord for Missy's triggers, but four mana setup is board dependent and does not double the separate artifact/face-up payoffs; the selected protection and acceleration are more urgent.
- **Bastion of Remembrance**: good additional nontoken-death finisher, but redundancy behind Blood Artist, Zulaport, Bombardment, Fireweaver and Brood made it lower priority.
- **Dark Ritual, Mox Opal, Lotus Petal**: extra burst acceleration considered; Grim Monolith can be reused and Chrome Mox fixes permanently. Opal requires metalcraft.
- **Biotransference**: explicitly rejected because it makes your own dying creatures artifacts and prevents Missy's nonartifact death trigger.
- **Ixidron**: explicitly rejected because it turns Missy face down, suppressing her abilities.
- **Deadly Rollick**: exiles rather than kills an opponent's nonartifact creature; prevents Missy's conversion.
- **Generic Thoracle/Consult/Breach package**: rejected as forced homogenization, as Missy already has deterministic commander-dependent free-turn-up loops.

## Source-bound finite mechanics

- Missy triggers on another *nonartifact creature* dying. The returned permanent is a tapped face-down **artifact** Cyberman 2/2, so its next death will **not** trigger Missy until it turns face up to regain printed nonartifact status. The permanent does not leave and re-enter when turned face up.
- Ruthless Ripper (reveal black), Dragon's Eye Savants (reveal blue), Horde Ambusher (reveal red) have mana-free morph actions. Their infinite return/reveal/death cycles require Missy, a suitable free sacrifice outlet and a card of the reveal color in hand.
- With a free outlet, **Altar of the Brood** mills each opponent on every Missy-return ETB, including the 0-power Savants route. Altar of Dementia on Savants still mills zero without a power increase.
- **Goblin Sharpshooter** untaps when any creature dies, so it can provide repeatable damage and help kill opposing small nonartifact creatures. It must be eligible to tap; no summoning sickness exemption is asserted.
- **Yawgmoth** sacrifices as a cost, pays 1 life and can draw/-1/-1; an unlimited Yawgmoth death loop would require an additional life-gain source, unlike the truly no-mana/no-life outlets.
- **Gift of Doom** can turn up for sacrificing another creature and attach to Missy, protecting from destroy effects but **not** exile, bounce, sacrifice or -X/-X.
- **The Valeyard** still applies an extra villainous choice to each opponent at Missy's end step.
- **Panoptic Projektor** reduces the next face-down creature *spell* cost, not the cost to turn a face-down card up; its second ability multiplies true face-up triggered abilities.
- **The Master, Gallifrey's End** complements Missy when a face-down artifact Cyberman dies and Missy cannot recur it directly.
- **Cavern of Souls** can choose Time Lord for Missy; its colored mana does not automatically fix all non-Time-Lord creatures and other spell costs.
