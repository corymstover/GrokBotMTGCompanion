## Main red-team findings and rules evidence

1. **Forsaken Miner trigger on its own casualty sacrifice** is unusually important. Official source `https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/outlaws-of-thunder-junction-release-notes` specifically says if you sacrifice Miner as a spell's additional cost and target an opponent or their object, it triggers from your graveyard. No target to opponent = no crime. The casualty spell copy is not cast and does not by itself create a second crime.
2. **Case** initial suspected Skeleton can pay Anhelo's casualty after entering play. At own end step with zero suspected Skeletons case solves; only *then* its paid activated tutor can work at sorcery time, not as part of the casualty stack. Source `https://scryfall.com/card/pmkm/80s/case-of-the-stashed-skeleton`.
3. **Fable** front face is a Saga, not a creature; chapter I creates the 2/2 token. Chapter III turns into Reflection, a 2/2 creature that can copy another **nonlegendary** creature, `{1},{T}` required, token sacrifices next end step. Source `https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/kamigawa-neon-dynasty-release-notes-2022-02-09`.
4. **Misty fetch** checks basic land *types* Forest/Island, not only basic cards; typed duals are valid. It has colorless identity, no illegal green-pip issue.
5. **Sinister Concierge** returns (cast free) after three time-counter ticks unless suspended trigger thwarted; any exiled opposing creature also normally returns after its owner's upkeeps. Temporary tempo, not permanent removal.
6. **Projected constraints:** graveyard hate stops Miner/Shade recurrence; Miner is conditional on crime targets; Case tutorable but slow; Fable token/copy engine delayed; City deals repeated damage; three-color Anhelo still competes for scarce colored pips. Scenarios and source strings are checked by focused regression tests, not a real multiplayer engine.
7. **Official Commander legality** as per 2026-10-23 115-card Scryfall snapshot + 6 new live direct Scryfall GET records; 94 distinct / 100 physical, 91 nonbasic unique line items plus 3x each basic, all color legal.
