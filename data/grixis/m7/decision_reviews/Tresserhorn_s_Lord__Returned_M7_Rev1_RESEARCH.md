## Commander-first protected identity

The five-mana, UBR, 10/4 indestructible trampler needs **three other creatures at ETB resolution**, three paid life and three cards gifted to an opponent to stay. Those three creature sacrifices draw three cards from the Lord; a failed payment causes a self-sacrifice and draws one. The primary strategy is **cheap precommander bodies → free sac outlet → commander-as-draw-engine → aristocrat or Gravecrawler/Locust conversion**, with Infantry Shield, Wand of Orcus and 10-power combat as the secondary route. Never substitute the original `Lord of Tresserhorn` deck's Torpor Orb/ETB-suppression Voltron thesis.

Protected core: `Mogg War Marshal`, `Weaponcraft Enthusiast`, `Jadar, Ghoulcaller of Nephalia`, `Ophiomancer`, `Bitterblossom`, `Bitterbloom Bearer`, `Gravecrawler`, `Phyrexian Altar`, `Ashnod's Altar`, `Warren Soultrader`, `Goblin Bombardment`, `Zulaport Cutthroat`, `Blood Artist`, `The Locust God`, `Infantry Shield`, `Wand of Orcus`, `Not of This World`.

Natural ceiling: **Bracket 4 — Optimized**, rather than cEDH. The commander demands five mana, three disposable creatures, three life and an opponent receiving three cards before its real advantage can begin. The candidate contains three Game Changers (Demonic Tutor, Vampiric Tutor, Fierce Guardianship; current frozen card-index flags). Bracket 4 is a qualitative optimized power target, not a claim that three Game Changers mandate Bracket 4. Suggested Archidekt tags: **Aristocrats / Sacrifice / Commander Damage**.

At least two additional post-floor, different named incumbent challenges: `Chasm Skulker` versus Sifter of Skulls (Pass C noted nontoken-only Spawn and a strong Skulker draw-based token bank); `Chittering Witch` versus Sengir Autocrat (both can make four bodies at a four-player table, Witch also offers paid shrink/sac outlet, so a **second** Autocrat-style slot is not justified). `Kher Keep` versus Kazuul's Fury similarly trades a repeatable land-body source for a one-shot spell land. A higher swap count is not a value metric.

**Additional candidate dispositions (all provisional):**

- **SELECT PROVISIONALLY** Sengir Autocrat specifically over five-mana delayed `Endrek Sahr`, not over `Chittering Witch`: four bodies at one mana less, including an independent nontoken creature to sacrifice; key exile-on-leave vulnerability remains.
- **REJECT** Beetleback Chief over Chittering Witch/Weaponcraft Enthusiast: four-mana three bodies doesn't clearly dominate Chittering Witch's four bodies in a four-player game plus paid removal or the three-mana Enthusiast triple-body package.
- **RETAIN_ALTERNATIVE** Tombstone Stairwell over Endrek: four-mana repeat Zombies can be explosive with graveyard setup, but make **zero** Zombies with zero creature cards in a graveyard, feed opponents symmetrically, and require escalating upkeep. Its contingent turn-five contribution loses to Autocrat's guaranteed three Serfs in the immediate-precommander context.
- **REJECT** Hordeling Outburst: three tokens for three mana ties the Enthusiast's three-body count but lacks Enthusiast's nontoken body, fabricate choice and artifact Servos; Song also offers an optional haste turn.
- **RETAIN_ALTERNATIVE** Pawn of Ulamog: helpful for nontoken Gravecrawler recasts, but does not trigger from token churn; Nightscape Familiar is a Zombie for Crawler and reduces multicolored Lord by **one**, not two, generic mana.
- **RETAIN_ALTERNATIVE** Kazuul's Fury: ten-power burn at instant speed and a self-sacrifice draw; the land face always enters tapped and the spell sacrifices the expensive commander.
- **RETAIN_ALTERNATIVE** Dread Return: excellent three-creature flashback to reanimate at zero mana and take three Lord draws, but it competes with already efficient Reanimate/Victimize/Living Death and costs one pre-commander fodder source if placed over Endrek.
- **REJECT** Sifter of Skulls as an auto-swap for Chasm Skulker: nontoken death only, whereas the Lord's draw engine grows Skulker and later produces many Squid.
- **REJECT** Mirkwood Bats: strong token payoff, but four-mana payoff-only and redundant drain density against a five-mana commander needing bodies/protection; Meathook combines payoff, life and removal.
- **REJECT** Reassembling Skeleton: flexible recursion but costs {1}{B} per return and supplies only one body; Bloodghast and Gravecrawler are cheaper actual loop fuel.
- **REJECT** Torpor Orb/Karn, Argent Defender: ETB suppression prevents the Lord's intended three-sacrifice/three-draw conversion.
- **RETAIN_ALTERNATIVE** Flare of Malice and Withering Torment for interaction-heavy metagames; the former demands a nontoken black sacrifice for a free cast, the latter costs one more than Feed the Swarm but is instant-speed and caps life loss at 2.
- **RETAIN_ALTERNATIVE** Chthonian Nightmare for resource-recursion environments, though energy, mana-value ceilings and sorcery-speed setup compete with the fast Crawler/Altar plan.

The **strongest counterargument** to the research seven is not "only seven swaps": replacing cheap Village Rites with a free-only-when-commander-is-out answer, a flexible but painful Swamp, and established repeatable Endrek Thrulls with a fragile one-shot Autocrat package can weaken the engine after an opponent removes Autocrat or after the first cast. Alt5 remains live because the bounded same-start resource comparison below exposes competing strengths, not a dominant configuration. No random win-rate evidence exists. The post-floor stopping criterion will be all plausible additional setup/protection/castability opportunities losing a full-100 comparison for commander necessity, not reaching seven.

**Named-state Alt5 comparison, four bounded tests (not shuffled starts):** with the same four land/mana sources on turn four and the different challenger card available, Rev1 can cast four-mana Autocrat and have three Serfs for a five-mana turn-five Lord; Alt5's five-mana Endrek cannot. With five sources on turn five and the Lord on turn six, Alt5 Endrek instead yields five Thrulls **on cast** before Lord ETB, leaving two Thrulls after the three-sacrifice payment, while Autocrat alone retains itself after three Serfs. If opponents remove Autocrat before the Lord's ETB trigger resolves, its Serfs disappear and the Lord sacrifices itself; removing Endrek after his cast trigger has resolved leaves his Thrulls intact. With one black mana, one spare body and no commander, Alt5's one-mana Village Rites draws two and Rev1's three-mana Swat is uncastable. This pins a real turn-five speed advantage **and** a credible resilience/early-card-flow deficit; without whole-deck opening-hand evidence we retain both full 100s as live alternatives.
