## Adversarial open-ended post-floor comparisons
- **Into the Flood Maw vs Abrade over Preordain:** Abrade permanently removes most artifact stax and small creatures; only deals 3 to creatures and cannot touch Rule of Law/Rhystic or other enchantments. Into reaches all opposing nonland permanent classes for one blue (gift tapped Fish), albeit temporarily. For a fast combo turn, cheaper breadth wins. EDHREC cEDH Cormela snapshot: Into 53/130 (+0.359 synergy), Abrade 11/130 (−0.011), Preordain 10/130 (+0.019); not a substitute for actual rules/value judgment.
- **Into vs Snap over Preordain:** Snap returns **any creature** and untaps up to two lands, potentially converting restricted UBR cast into land mana, an excellent Cormela-specific alternative. But it cannot answer Null Rod, Cursed Totem or Rule of Law, and its untap depends on lands already being tapped. Into wins anti-hate coverage/turn efficiency at {U}. Snapshot Snap 30/130 (+0.132).
- **Burnt Offering over Windfall** (would be seven swaps): Cormela sac cost creates four unrestricted B/R mana and her death trigger, but Burnt Offering is still on the stack when that trigger resolves and cannot return *itself*. It overlaps Culling the Weak, the two altars, Tower and Diabolic Intent. Cutting Windfall loses unique mass redraw, Breach fuel and Bowmasters punish; rejection is strategic, not floor cap.
- **Dramatic Reversal** (62/130) can untap Cormela and repeatable mana artifacts; it does **not** recast itself, is limited without many untapped rocks and is especially poor into Null Rod/Ouphe. The valuable low-curve cantrip or existing real combo piece it would displace is not clearly weaker; keep as researched alternative rather than force a seventh.
- **Yawgmoth's Will, Final Fortune, Beseech the Mirror, Toxic Deluge, Dragon's Rage Channeler, Spellseeker, Valley Floodcaller, Sacrifice, Into the Flood Maw, and Verdant Catacombs** were screened. Generic combo redundancy, colored-cost/time pressure, stax exposure, and no demonstrably weaker cuts prevent further automatic adds. Do not assume EDHREC inclusion is optimality proof.
- **Land challenge:** Seven fetchlands access typed UBR duals; Verdant Catacombs eighth fetch is plausible, but replaces stable repeatable colored production and increases Opposition Agent pressure. Underground River is the chosen guaranteed U/B source; its R weakness and life cost remain disclosed.
- **If a seventh material cut emerges**, reopen research rather than manufacture a swap. At present six is the quality-based stopping point.
