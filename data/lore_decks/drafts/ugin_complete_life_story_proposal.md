# Lore Deck #9 — Ugin: The Spirit Dragon's Complete Biography

**Title:** Ugin: The Spirit Dragon — From the Ur-Dragon's Brood to the End of the Meditation Realm

**Status:** Finished proposal. Awaiting Cory's approval. Lore Decks #1–#8 and the 62-deck Grixis Commander Encyclopedia remain untouched.

**Commander:** [The Ur-Dragon](https://scryfall.com/card/c17/48/the-ur-dragon?utm_source=api) — C17 #48. A **five-color ancestral narrator** for the biography. Wizards' original stories establish it as the primordial progenitor of Elder Dragons, including Ugin and Nicol Bolas, but do **not** identify its gender. It is not verified as Ugin's literal mother in a human sense. Official primary reference: [The Returning Legends of Modern Horizons 2](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/returning-legends-modern-horizons-2-2021-06-10); [Chronicle of Bolas: The Twins](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/chronicle-bolas-twins-2018-06-13).

## The actual chronology (not release dates)

1. **The origin:** the Ur-Dragon's egg-stones fall onto ancient Dominaria; Ugin and Nicol hatch as unusually small twins. They meet older dragon siblings, see the death of their sister Merrevia, and grow apart as Nicol prioritizes power.
2. **The first break:** Bolas's manipulation of the Dragon-killers' War and betrayal shatter Ugin's trust, sparking his planeswalker journey. Ugin wanders the Multiverse, learns the distinction between power and wisdom, and develops colorless ghostfire. In a later confrontation in the **Meditation Realm**, he apparently dies but his spirit survives: this is a **different incident** from his later near-death on Tarkir.
3. **Tarkir:** Ugin's magic entwines with the plane and forms the dragon tempests. Dragon broods and generations of cultures grow under that influence.
4. **The Eldrazi:** thousands of years before the Gatewatch, Ugin and Sorin Markov recruit Nahiri; together they lure and contain Emrakul, Kozilek and Ulamog using Zendikar's hedrons.
5. **The original Tarkir catastrophe and its revision:** Bolas strikes Ugin down around 1,280 years before Khans-era Tarkir. In the original history, dragonstorms die out. Sarkhan goes back and saves him; he recovers in a hedron cocoon, the storms continue and the eventual dragonlords dominate Tarkir. **The Khans-of-Tarkir timeline is erased; its cards still serve as pages of the story that was prevented.**
6. **The long reckoning:** after waking, Ugin disputes with Jace over killing or containing the Eldrazi. His much older contingency with Azor's Immortal Sun eventually matters as Bolas harvests sparks on Ravnica. Ugin discovers the key to Bolas's defeat; Bolas loses his spark and is imprisoned with Ugin as jailer.
7. **2025–2026, an unresolved ending:** In *Tarkir: Dragonstorm* (2025), Jace's and the others' activities culminate in the Meditation Realm's destruction; Bolas's bonds break, Ugin lunges to restrain him, and they vanish together. The narrated scene **does not confirm either one's death or survival**. In *Reality Fracture* (2026), Jace uses the old realm's magic and recalls what Ugin understood about it. That is a legacy reference, **not proof Ugin appears or remains alive**.

**Chronology caution:** Older *Fate Reforged* material sometimes calls Tarkir Ugin's native plane, but the fuller 2018 *Chronicle of Bolas* explicitly places the twins' birth on ancient Dominaria. This dossier follows the latter narrative. The decks' story acts are chronological; the geographic land appendix is intentionally separate. Azor's device is an **ancient flashback** presented in the chapter where its anti-Bolas plan pays off.

## Canonical caveats, narrative exclusions, and Rule 0

- The Ur-Dragon is a primordial progenitor, not canonically identified as Ugin's female biological mother.
- Ugin's birth occurred on Dominaria; some older Fate Reforged prose calls Tarkir his native plane, which conflicts with later Chronicle of Bolas. This deck follows the more explicit 2018 birth narrative.
- Crux of Fate/Bitter Revelation and the five Khans fetch-land flavor texts depict the original Tarkir timeline that Sarkhan rewrote; they must not be mistaken for the surviving continuity.
- Ugin and Sorin/Nahiri imprisoned rather than killed the titans; later Gatewatch members killed Ulamog and Kozilek against Ugin's preferred stasis approach.
- Azor and the Immortal Sun appear as an ancient flashback whose plan pays off only during the War of the Spark, not as a new 2019 invention.
- In the 2019 finale, Bolas was desparked and Ugin served as his jailer in the Meditation Realm.
- In the 2025 Tarkir: Dragonstorm story, the Meditation Realm collapsed, Bolas's bonds broke, Ugin tried to hold him, and BOTH disappeared; their survival is explicitly uncertain in the cited story. Do not assert definitive death, rescue, or continuing imprisonment.
- The 2026 Reality Fracture story invokes the power of the Meditation Realm in Jace's Echoverse project; this is not evidence Ugin is alive or physically participates.
- Emrakul, the Aeons Torn is banned in sanctioned Commander and is retained as a deliberate story-first Rule 0 exception.
- The original Ugin, the Spirit Dragon is colorless; the biography's colored allies/antagonists are included because the Ur-Dragon frames WUBRG, not because Ugin was personally color-identified with those cards.
- Generic landscape and evocative cards are labeled as locations, consequences, or symbolic portraits rather than fabricated literal appearances.

**Explicitly not fabricated:** Te Ju Ki, Merrevia Sal, the first death/reconstitution in the Meditation Realm, and the full 2025 realm collapse do not each have exact literal card portrayals that could be substantiated here. They are still documented in the chronology above. Metaphoric, later-printed Ugin cards are labeled honestly; none is claimed to depict an event it does not.

**No generic deck optimization:** No Sol Ring, generic five-color fixing package, or invented card-selection claims merely to improve power. The Ur-Dragon and historical Elder Dragons make the deck mechanically playable in principle, but mana requirements and high-cost lore cards are not tuned for wins. `Emrakul, the Aeons Torn` is banned under sanctioned Commander rules and requires Rule 0 permission. No claim of certified Bracket 1/2 or comprehensive combo-audit status.
