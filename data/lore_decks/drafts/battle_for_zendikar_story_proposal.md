# Lore Deck #6 — The Battle for Zendikar: Rise of the Gatewatch, Fall of the Titans

**Status:** Finished story-first proposal; awaiting Cory’s approval. **Exactly 100 cards.**
**Commander:** Tazri, Beacon of Unity (Zendikar Rising, ZNR #44). **Color identity:** WUBRG.
**Legality:** One deliberate exception: Emrakul, the Aeons Torn is banned in ordinary Commander. This is a house-rule/story-exhibit list, not fully Commander legal. No other hard legality failures on the 2026-10-28 printing snapshot.
**Purpose:** A chronological visual history of ancient imprisonment, the opening of the Eye, Ulamog's devastation, Sea Gate's coalition, Ob Nixilis's sabotage, the Gatewatch's founding, and Zendikar's survival. The cards are selected for narrative, not efficiency. Printed editions below provide precise art/flavor references where possible.

> **Canon:** Only Ulamog and Kozilek were killed on Zendikar. Emrakul had departed and later appeared on Innistrad. The Aligned Hedron Network trapped Ulamog briefly but did not end the conflict. Kiora helped with Thassa's bident but did not take one of the four founding Gatewatch oaths.

## What is literal, what is illustrative?

- **Directly tied to the battle:** *Aligned Hedron Network* depicts Ulamog temporarily bound; *Witness the End* describes Kozilek rising; *Fall of the Titans* explicitly connects Gideon's stand and Chandra's burning of the trapped titans; *Sea Gate Restoration* portrays Tazri's reconstruction era.
- **Period / character portraits:** Some Planeswalker versions show a relevant person but were printed in earlier sets (*Sarkhan Vol*, *Jace, the Mind Sculptor*, *Chandra Ablaze*) or on another plane (*Ugin, the Spirit Dragon*). Their specific art does not necessarily depict the exact Zendikar scene.
- **Interpretive Zendikar geography:** *Valakut, the Molten Pinnacle*, *Oran-Rief, the Vastwood*, *Bojuka Bog*, and related lands are geographic/atmospheric anchors, not evidence that every pivotal confrontation occurred there.
- **Cross-plane artifact with genuine story role:** *Bident of Thassa* is a Theros printing, but Kiora physically brought the stolen bident to Zendikar in the published storyline.
- **Later perspectives:** *Tazri, Beacon of Unity*, *Drana, the Last Bloodchief* and *Sea Gate Restoration* represent the surviving plane after the climax.
