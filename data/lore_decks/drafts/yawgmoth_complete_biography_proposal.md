## Xantcha-to-Karn heartstone bridge

**Previous unapproved SHA:** `3ca63d2cdb6811ca8d4adc21c0b1d5d39d0d00358a8d0ef68cc0c0659d1a5b81`. **Additional swap:** Crovax the Cursed → Heartstone.

Xantcha’s Phyrexian heartstone was used as Karn’s consciousness core, connecting a Yawgmoth-origin creation to the Legacy’s eventual defeat of him and the inheritance of Phyrexian oil on Mirrodin. The printed **Heartstone** chosen is Stronghold #134 and bears Hanna’s direct quote. Story source: https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/the-brothers-war-chapter-2-antiquities

## How this differs from the approved *Legacy* deck #1

Deck #1 recounts the same catastrophe chiefly through Urza, Sisay, the Weatherlight and Coalition. This one centers Yawgmoth: his crimes as a Thran physician, acquisition of a preexisting Phyrexia, cult and created soldiers, manipulating the brothers through Gix, Rath and invasion generals, death, and the oil/ideology surviving into New Phyrexia. Ramos supplies only the five-color historical framing commander; all five Bones and Henge were deliberately removed from this 99 at Cory’s request.

## Canon and rules cautions

- The Legacy (#1) is a different, approved Weatherlight/coalition narrative. This is the antagonist-centered Yawgmoth biography.
- Yawgmoth did not create the nine-sphere plane of Old Phyrexia. An unnamed earlier planeswalker created it; Dyfed introduced him to it. No named Dyfed paper card is assumed.
- Yawgmoth is not a planeswalker simply because Dyfed physically took him to another plane.
- K’rrik’s Son title does not prove literal parentage.
- Ramos served Mishra, was captured and repurposed by Urza, and eventually yielded five Bones of Ramos to the Legacy. His biography is a framing story connection; he did not eyewitness the ancient Thran events.
- At Cory’s request, the five Bones of Ramos and Henge of Ramos are no longer included in the 99. Ramos remains only the five-color historical framing commander.
- The original Thran portal and the Antiquities-era Jarsyl Gate to Phyrexia diary describe distinct temporal visits.
- Original and later reprint flavor are separately designated. New Phyrexia exists after Yawgmoth’s death, not under his living command.
- Research on the 1999 novel The Thran uses Wizards official summaries and detailed secondary plot accounts; the full 311-page book was not available, so this is not a first-hand chapter-by-chapter novel certification.
- Gix originally wounded Glacian as an Untouchable thousands of years before his later Phyrexian praetorship and manipulation of Mishra. The printed praetor card depicts the later identity, not the earlier attack.
- Thran-war stonecharger chemical death clouds and the later Apocalypse depiction of Yawgmoth as a gigantic black death cloud are different events.
- The Mana Rig card depicts the Shivan installation, a separate surviving Thran powerstone factory; it does not depict the Halcyon rig where phthisis affected the Untouchables.
- The original Mirrodin artifact-land art of Ancient Den and Vault of Whispers is not assumed to depict Halcyon/Koilos, even where the chosen Brothers War Commander printings quote those locations.
- Three Commander-banned cards are knowingly included under Cory’s story-first Rule 0 exception: Yawgmoth’s Bargain, Sundering Titan, and Tolarian Academy.
- No power bracket or sanctioned Commander legality is asserted.
