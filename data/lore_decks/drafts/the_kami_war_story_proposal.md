# The Kami War: The Theft of a God and the Sisters of Flesh and Spirit

**Lore Deck #5 — 100 physical cards — Kyodai, Soul of Kamigawa — unapproved proposal.**

## Narrative and evidence

The original Kamigawa is divided between the mortal world (utsushiyo) and the spirit realm (kakuriyo). Lord Konda, with moonfolk assistance, steals a child/part of O-Kagachi for immortality, secretly imprisoning the future Kyodai in That Which Was Taken. Reito is massacred as the enraged kami attack mortals over a twenty-year war. Toshiro Umezawa helps Michiko learn the truth and free Kyodai. Kyodai and Michiko end the threat of O-Kagachi and Konda and work to restore harmony.

The five core chapters are Konda's theft; the Reito disaster and the kami's vengeance; mortal resistance by samurai, scholars, ogres and moonfolk; Toshiro and Michiko's quest; and the new Sisters of Flesh and Spirit. The final geographic chapter contrasts original Kamigawa with the same plane about 1,200 years later.

Primary official sources: [Kamigawa plane](https://magic.wizards.com/en/story/kamigawa-plane), [The Kami War](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/kami-war-2022-01-11), [Neon Dynasty Guide](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/planeswalkers-guide-kamigawa-neon-dynasty-2022-02-03), [Returning Legends](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/legends-kamigawa-neon-dynasty-2022-02-11), [Life of Toshiro Umezawa](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/life-toshiro-umezawa-2022-01-12), [Michiko's Reign of Truth](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/michikos-reign-truth-2022-01-13), and [Kamigawa Saga histories](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/card-preview/sagas-kamigawa-neon-dynasty-2022-01-27).

The selected edition is linked next to each card. **Printed flavor** means those words actually appear on that specific card or one of its faces; **Saga** denotes historical chapter text; the other cards have explicit character, place, cultural or openly thematic connections. The explanations are paraphrased, not invented quotes.

## Canon and rules safeguards

- Kyodai is **not** O-Kagachi himself; she is the stolen part that becomes an independent new kami.
- The mortal population originally did not know Konda's crime caused the kami to attack.
- Reito's destruction is evidenced by multiple original *Champions of Kamigawa* flavor texts, including Ghostly Prison, Befoul, Rend Flesh and Horobi, Death's Wail.
- Meloku and Soratami Mirror-Mage illustrate moonfolk society. Their card printings do not individually establish participation in Konda's conspiracy.
- Toshiro's role is morally complicated. Later Neon Dynasty Sagas are explicitly **retrospective** history cards, not time travel or separate new copies of those people.
- Reverse Saga faces (including O-Kagachi Made Manifest, Fragment of Konda, Portrait of Michiko and Memory of Toshiro) are **part of their front cards**, not additional physical cards.
- Spirit-Sister's Call, some postwar restoration material and Neon Dynasty lands have qualified thematic/epilogue roles, not invented direct eyewitness status.
