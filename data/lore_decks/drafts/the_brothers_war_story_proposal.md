# The Brothers' War: Urza, Mishra, and the Ruin of Argoth

**Lore deck #4 · Commander: Ramos, Dragon Engine · 100 cards · Unapproved proposal**

## Historical thesis

The deck is a five-act documentary in actual card form. It follows the boys at Tocasia's dig site, the divided Thran powerstone, their rival Yotian and Fallaji empires, Gix's Phyrexian manipulation, the independent Third Path and Argothian resistance, and Urza's catastrophic use of the Sylex. The final land-art sequence foreshadows the Ice Age.

**Why Ramos commands:** Ramos is a historical Brothers' War dragon engine later reprogrammed by Urza and linked to refugees who escaped the Sylex disaster. It has five-color Commander identity despite being a colorless artifact on its face; all five colors are genuinely needed to represent Urza, Mishra, the Third Path, Gix, and Argoth. Its printed card has no flavor text, so this is a character/story choice, not a literal flavor quote.

**Primary story and design evidence:** [Official BRO design, *Act of War, Part 1*](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/act-of-war-part-1) (the childhood, adult-war and final-conflict structure and exact landmark events), [*The Brothers' War Card Stories, Part 1*](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/the-brothers-war-card-stories-part-1), [*Part 2*](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/the-brothers-war-card-stories-part-2) (Urza's final transformation), [official Dominaria plane history](https://magic.wizards.com/en/story/dominaria-plane) (the war caused the Ice Age), [*The End*](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/the-brothers-war-episode-1-the-end), [*The Ink of Empires*](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/the-brothers-war-episode-4-the-ink-of-empires), and [*As Cruel, As Necessary*](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/the-brothers-war-episode-5-as-cruel-as-necessary).

**Evidence boundaries:** `Flavor: yes` identifies flavor actually printed on the linked edition, not an invented quote; `Saga: yes` means the card bears actual chapter text. Other inclusions have named-character, depicted-event, original-setting, contextual, or thematic relevance, as disclosed per card. All notes below are paraphrases. The individual scenes within long phases sometimes overlap rather than occur at exact minute-by-minute intervals.

## Edition-by-edition story atlas

**Commander:** [Ramos, Dragon Engine](https://scryfall.com/card/brr/47/ramos-dragon-engine?utm_source=api) — BRR #47 · genuine participant and narrator; its BRR retro printing belongs to the 2022 Brothers' War release, and original C17 printing is another valid choice.

### I. Two boys, one discovery: Tocasia, the Powerstones, and the broken bond

- [Tocasia's Welcome](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/30/tocasias-welcome?utm_source=api) — **BRO #30** · flavor text. Tocasia receives the orphaned brothers, creating the human beginning of the tragedy.
- [Tocasia, Dig Site Mentor](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/224/tocasia-dig-site-mentor?utm_source=api) — **BRO #224** · name/art/story. The archeologist and adoptive teacher who guides Urza and Mishra to the Thran ruins.
- [Loran, Disciple of History](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/13/loran-disciple-of-history?utm_source=api) — **BRO #13** · flavor text. Tocasia's devoted student and a future steward of the Sylex story; importantly, not a third brother.
- [Urza, Powerstone Prodigy](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/69/urza-powerstone-prodigy?utm_source=api) — **BRO #69** · name/art/story. Urza as the curious young scholar before power and bitterness consume his life.
- [Mishra, Excavation Prodigy](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/140/mishra-excavation-prodigy?utm_source=api) — **BRO #140** · flavor text. Mishra as the skilled young desert explorer, contrasted by the original card's flavor.
- [Ornithopter](https://scryfall.com/card/atq/60/ornithopter?utm_source=api) — **ATQ #60** · flavor text. An early mechanical experiment; Antiquities printing explicitly treats it as Urza's youthful artifice.
- [Mightstone](https://scryfall.com/card/atq/55/mightstone?utm_source=api) — **ATQ #55** · flavor text. The stone Urza discovers in the Hall of Tagsin, according to the Antiquities flavor text.
- [Weakstone](https://scryfall.com/card/atq/78/weakstone?utm_source=api) — **ATQ #78** · flavor text. The stone Mishra discovers in the same underground chamber, according to the Antiquities flavor text.
- [Splitting the Powerstone](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/63/splitting-the-powerstone?utm_source=api) — **BRO #63** · flavor text. The original Brothers' War card records the breakup of the stone and the brothers' relationship.
- [Sibling Rivalry](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/152/sibling-rivalry?utm_source=api) — **BRO #152** · name/art/story. An explicit title for their competition before it becomes a war among nations.
- [Brotherhood's End](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/128/brotherhoods-end?utm_source=api) — **BRO #128** · flavor text. Tocasia's death as the brothers fight, the decisive end of their childhood together.

## Three meld stories, correctly represented

- `Urza, Lord Protector` + `The Mightstone and Weakstone` → **Urza, Planeswalker**. The meld result is the reverse side, not a 101st physical card.
- `Mishra, Claimed by Gix` + `Phyrexian Dragon Engine` → **Mishra, Lost to Phyrexia**. The tragic meld result is not separately legal as an item in the 100.
- `Titania, Voice of Gaea` + `Argoth, Sanctum of Nature` → **Titania, Gaea Incarnate**. The land itself is part of the actual deck and the back face tells another view of the Argothian resistance.

## Guardrails and intentional distinctions

- **The earliest Powerstone conflict is *Splitting the Powerstone***. *Powerstone Fracture* instead depicts an **Ashnod wartime demolition of a behemoth**; it belongs with the industrial war, not the childhood cave scene.
- *Ramos, Dragon Engine* is chosen through a character-lore connection. Unlike the Antiquities powerstone cards, its BRR print has **no** literal flavor sentence asserting his entire history.
- *The Brothers' War* and *The Antiquities War* are retrospective Saga pages. *Urza's Saga* is a retrospective land; it is not claimed as a geographical battlefield.
- *Glacial Chasm* and the original *ICE* snow-covered basic lands stand for the broader Ice Age, **not** the claim that a specific fictional glacial chasm was created by the Sylex.
- Although *Portal to Phyrexia* was a BRO card, the literal original flavor references the nightmare's return five thousand years later; that specific modern-return framing makes it less appropriate for this purely ancient-war story.
- *One with the Multiverse* specifically depicts **Urza's planeswalker ascension**, confirmed in Wizards' design commentary; the mere back face of a melded card is not a standalone entry.
