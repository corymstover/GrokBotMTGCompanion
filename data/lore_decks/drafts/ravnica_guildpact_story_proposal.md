**STATUS:** Finished, independently validated, awaiting Cory’s approval. **Not locked.**
**Commander:** `Niv-Mizzet, Guildpact` (MKM #220); the 2024 incarnation is the modern narrator. `Niv-Mizzet Reborn` is deliberately in the 99 as a genuine 2019 rebirth event.
**Exact raw decklist SHA-256:** `79f2b5b1bca4927b07b9feeb1a78d68b7fcb5ad3c67791f66d01037a7b3bdb58` (`ravnica_guildpact_story_decklist.txt`, UTF-8, one entry per line, final newline).
**100 cards:** 1 commander, 61 historical/story nonland cards, 33 named Ravnican nonbasic lands, and five period basic lands. **38 lands.**
**Commander rules:** Five-color identity, 100 cards, singleton, no Commander bans or hard legality problems in the 2026-10-28 Scryfall archive. The local evaluator estimates a **minimum Bracket 3** because it counts *Grand Arbiter Augustin IV* and *Bolas's Citadel* as Game Changers; this is not a tested table power level or a full combo audit.
**Archidekt tags:** `Ravnica` • `The Guildpact` • `Lore/Story-First`.

> **Historical honesty:** The 10,000-year original magical pact, its rupture, Teysa's nonmagical compromise, Jace solving Azor's Implicit Maze, and Niv-Mizzet's later rebirth are separate milestones. The guild leaders represented here are **not** all ancient founders. The illustrated lands document guild geography, not the exact site of every battle.

## The story in five acts

1. **An impossible city:** Ten bickering guilds share one plane because Azor's magically binding Guildpact gives each a civic domain. Guild founders and later institutional portraits anchor the era.
2. **The contract shatters:** Agrus Kos investigates Szadek, who exploits the letter of the Pact with Augustin IV's machinations. Savra, Razia, Rakdos, the nephilim, the Project Kraj fiasco, and the crashing original Parhelion mark the aftermath. Teysa drafts a lesser, nonmagical accord.
3. **The Maze gives the law a body:** The ten guilds regain their familiar identities and the Implicit Maze resolves. Jace wins the contest by solving its underlying challenge and becomes the new Living Guildpact.
4. **The guilds tear themselves apart:** Isperia attempts interguild cooperation, Vraska assassinates her, and Bolas's allies manipulate guild leadership. Ravnica again faces an existential test.
5. **A city that refuses to die:** Niv-Mizzet dies and is resurrected with the guilds' help to become the Living Guildpact. Ravnica withstands the War of the Spark and later the Phyrexian invasion, but Karlov Manor exposes new rivalries and murders. The current Guildpact remains Niv.

## Ravnica itself is the mana base

The thirty foundational locations were chosen as **ten original guildhalls + ten Guildgates + ten original guild landscapes**; the three remaining nonbasics portray the implicit maze and shared public institutions. This is a deliberately geographic story rather than a generic five-color goodstuff mana base.

### Three additional historic city landmarks

- **Maze's End** — the terminal node of Azor's original Implicit Maze, and the map's centerpiece.
- **Plaza of Harmony** — post-Guildpact civic peace, however precarious.
- **Guildmages' Forum** — a public space for the practitioners of guild traditions.

- **Festival of the Guildpact (RAV #17)** — a rare ceremonial pause in interguild violence.
- **Guardian of the Guildpact (DIS #10)** — spirits are literally charged to protect the magical contract.
- **Enemy of the Guildpact (DIS #44)** — a dead dissenter objects to guild power and its bargain.
- **Transguild Courier (DIS #168)** — a neutral messenger is needed because guild leaders distrust direct contact.
- **Guild Summit (GRN #41)** — Isperia explicitly proposes cooperation amid suspicion.
- **Assassin's Trophy (GRN #152)** — the flavor directly connects Vraska to the vacuum left in Azorius leadership.
- **Firemind Vessel (WAR #237)** — the Izzet dragon left behind an instrument for his own resurrection.
- **Awakening of Vitu-Ghazi (WAR #152)** — Nissa animates the Conclave's sacred tree during the invasion.

## Caveats and boundaries

- Ancient **paruns** and more recent **guildmasters** are identified separately. *Borborygmos* and *Momir Vig* are not presented as original founders.
- **Azor, the Lawbringer** is a later Ixalan printing of the ancient founder. The card identifies the correct person, but its art does not depict the founding ceremony.
- **Parhelion II** in *War of the Spark* is a reconstructed later vessel; it is not the original Parhelion that crashed into Prahv in *Dissension*.
- *Razia's Purification* and *Supreme Verdict* are expressive examples of guild ideology; neither is claimed to be a photograph of the first Guildpact breaking.
- 2026 **Reality Fracture/Echoverse** alternate-reality characters are excluded; they cannot simply be inserted into this main-continuity chronology.
- **No power optimization claim.** The local validator reports zero hard issues and a minimum Bracket 3 due to two identified Game Changers. A complete two-card-combo audit was not performed.
- This story proposal does not alter or unlock any of the 62 approved Grixis decks, nor the earlier six lore proposals.
