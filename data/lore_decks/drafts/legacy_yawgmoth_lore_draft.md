# The Legacy: The Rise and Fall of Yawgmoth — first story-first EDH draft

**DRAFT — not approved or locked by Cory.** Separate from all 62 frozen Grixis decks. Proposed commander: **Sisay, Weatherlight Captain**, WUBRG color identity. This is the **casual-table cut**, whose historic-card choices favor Story over gameplay efficiency.

## Validated scope (2026-10-28)

- **100 cards including commander**, 36 lands, 10 basic land copies, WUBRG identity, singleton, legal according to the offline 2026-10-28 Scryfall snapshot and the repo's `evaluate_decklist.check` (zero hard errors).
- Automated barometers: **minimum Bracket 2**, zero Game Changers, zero detected mass-land-denial cards; **not a guaranteed bracket**. A full Commander Spellbook combo audit and table-level Rule 0 discussion still remain.
- **59 of the 90 distinct nonbasic names** have nonempty flavor text on the explicitly suggested printing; other inclusions are named people, Saga chapters, events, historical places, or transparent color-fixing/story-setting exceptions. The 10 basics are intentionally paired as one Brothers' War and one Invasion printing per color. Some later reprints/retrospectives refer to other moments and are clearly labeled below.
- Sources: [Official Dominarian history](https://magic.wizards.com/en/story/dominaria-plane), [official premodern Multiverse/Phyrexia history](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/known-mutliverse-2008-03-19), [Urza profile](https://magic.wizards.com/en/story/urza-planeswalker), [Rebbec profile](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/card-preview/legendary-characters-commander-legends-part-1-2020-10-30), [Glacian profile](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/card-preview/legendary-characters-commander-legends-part-2-2020-11-06), [Brothers' War Sylex story](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/the-brothers-war-episode-5-as-cruel-as-necessary), [Weatherlight crew report](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/weatherlight-report-2007-12-03). Scryfall links below prove *printing text/art identity*, not every historical interpretation.

## Historical narrative

1. **Ancient Thran:** Glacian and Rebbec's powerstone era, Yawgmoth's control of preexisting Phyrexia and the Thran catastrophe. Yawgmoth did **not** create Phyrexia's plane; it already existed.
2. **Brothers' War:** Urza/Mishra's fateful stones, Gix's involvement, artifact war and Sylex detonation at Argoth. Both brother meld pairs are intentionally in the 99.
3. **Urza's long war:** Xantcha, Karn, Barrin, Teferi, Serra's Realm and the crafting of the Legacy.
4. **Weatherlight quest:** Sisay's captaincy and capture, Gerrard's band, Volrath/Crovax/Rath and Karn's recovery of the Sliver Queen's heartstone.
5. **Invasion / Apocalypse:** the Phyrexian assault, Coalition resistance, Legacy Weapon and Yawgmoth's fall; later land printings offer explicit retrospective epilogues.

**Historical sequence does not imply each later card printing is contemporaneous:** Dominaria, The Brothers' War, Dominaria United and Planar Chaos retell or revisit history. Low-confidence scenery cards are labeled contextual below.
