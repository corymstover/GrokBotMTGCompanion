# Lore Deck #2 — The Mending: The Death of the Old Gods

**100-card finished story-first proposal · narrator/commander: Jodah, Archmage Eternal · pending Cory's approval.**

## What this is

This is a chronological *Time Spiral / Planar Chaos / Future Sight* historical exhibit, not a high-powered deckbuilding exercise. Jodah is a five-color Dominarian historian and framing device, **not** the central savior. The Mending is chiefly Jeska's sacrifice after a sequence of perilous closures; the title 'Death of the Old Gods' means the end of godlike Oldwalker power, **not** that all pre-Mending Planeswalkers died.

## The six story beats

1. **Earlier catastrophes.** The Sylex blast, Tolaria's experiments and destruction, Teferi's earlier phasing of Zhalfir, and Jeska's strange transformations through Phage and Karona create wounds that later become impossible to ignore.
2. **The Rift Era.** Broken lands, people from alternate times, a depleted Skyshroud, Foriys, Krosa, and a world unmoored from its own chronology; the 2006–07 cards convey this era through unusually specific flavor quotations.
3. **The survivors gather.** Teferi and Jhoira meet Venser and Radha, while Karn, Freyalise and Windgrace face specific time rifts. Leshrac, Nicol Bolas, the Myojin's mask, Multani and Jeska complicate events.
4. **The cost of healing.** Teferi loses his spark at Shiv, Freyalise dies at Skyshroud, Windgrace dies at Urborg, Karn gives up his spark at Tolaria, and the closing of other wounds converges on Jeska's final sacrifice at Otaria.
5. **The world begins again.** The Mending restores Dominaria and transforms the nature of the Planeswalker spark. Later cards speak about the martyrs and the forest's recovery.
6. **Lands as historical map.** Tolaria, Shiv, Skyshroud, Urborg, Yavimaya, Krosa, Keld and Zhalfir appear as named, time-specific sites or later memorials. Basic-land art uses one Time Spiral-era and one post-Mending Dominaria printing of each type.

**Chronology caution:** The major-cause links among old events are retrospective and not proof that every depicted spell individually created a rift. Planechase-only sites like Talon Gates of Madara are kept as research references rather than smuggled into the 100 normal deck slots. Old set printing ≠ later scene; the table distinguishes direct period wording from later memorial, symbolism and inference.

## The finale's exact printed evidence

- **`Obliterate` (INV 156):** Barrin's act of destroying Tolaria as a pyre to his family. That is a historical *cause*, not a spell cast by Karn at the later rift.
- **`Mirari's Wake` (MH2 291):** The later printing directly recalls the false god Karona and the Mirari sword that slew her.
- **`Teferi, Mage of Zhalfir` (TSP 83):** The original line foreshadows his impossible choice.
- **`Gemhide Sliver` (TSP 196):** Freyalise herself describes a dying Skyshroud and the search for mana.
- **`Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth` (PLC 165):** Windgrace describes Yawgmoth's body as a wound poisoning the universe.
- **`Jeska's Will` (CMR 187):** The dialogue is addressed directly to Radha, Jeska's partner in the final crisis.
- **`Mox Amber` (BRR 98):** The special printing explicitly names the Mending Era; the regular Dominaria printing does not, so edition choice matters.
- **`The Mending of Dominaria` (DOM 173):** The Saga names the consequence and captures recovery as a sequence of chapters.
- **`Llanowar Envoy` (DOM 169):** Its text names the *Mending of Dominaria* and the martyrs who saved the world.
