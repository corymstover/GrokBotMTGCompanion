# The Legacy: The Rise and Fall of Yawgmoth

**Final story-first proposed edition — 100 physical cards, five-color Sisay narrator, intentionally not Commander-legal without Rule 0 consent.**

This is Cory's first lore-first historical 'book in a deck.' Card selection follows named characters, artifacts, conflicts and original or retrospective printing flavor. It is **not** built to win games or satisfy bracket limits. Nothing here modifies or reopens the 62 locked Grixis Commander Encyclopedia decks.

## Important Rule 0 notice

The list deliberately includes three Commander-banned cards: **Yawgmoth's Bargain**, **Rofellos, Llanowar Emissary**, and **Tolarian Academy**. Cory specifically prioritized historical storytelling over restrictions. It can be displayed as an archive or played only with everyone's express agreement; it is not an officially legal Commander list. All card names, WUBRG color identity, 100-card count, and singleton status (other than allowed basic-land copies) were checked against the Scryfall 2026-10-28 bulk snapshot.

## Reading the story

The opening Thran chapter is Yawgmoth's transformation from physician to ruler of Phyrexia; he did **not** originally create the plane. The Brothers' War carries the Phyrexian threat back to Dominaria through Gix, the brothers, and the Golgothian Sylex. Urza's centuries-long preparation joins Karn, Xantcha, the Tolarian Academy and the realm of Serra to the Legacy project. Sisay and Gerrard gather the Weatherlight crew across Rath and recover the Legacy pieces. The Phyrexian Invasion and the Nine Titans' expedition end with Gerrard, Karn and the Legacy Weapon destroying Yawgmoth. Historic lands are physical 'settings' for these scenes, and some later printings function as epilogues.

The original Antiquities **Mightstone** and **Weakstone** printings independently narrate the brothers' discoveries in the Hall of Tagsin. **Gate to Phyrexia** quotes a diary by Jarsyl; this represents *later* exploration, not a claim that Jarsyl visited the Thran's original portal. The chosen **Legacy Weapon** reprint explicitly narrates Yawgmoth's obliteration. For **Phyrexian Tower**, the chosen Karn quote is deliberately a retrospective epilogue, not a contemporaneous battle quote.

## Completeness and accuracy notes

- The Nine Titans' mission happened during the Invasion, so the Saga **Urza Assembles the Titans** sits in the final-act chapter; four other printed Titan character cards accompany it. The full original roster includes members without literal name-matching cards. Urza's character cards are earlier in the deck, but his arc continues through the end.
- **Yawgmoth's Bargain** is symbolic of his ideology rather than evidence of one named bargain; **Yawgmoth's Will** is a direct thematic card. **Urza's Guilt** is more literally anchored to Urza's confrontation with what he believes to be Mishra.
- The chosen **Sisay, Weatherlight Captain** is her original *Modern Horizons* printing, not unrelated crossover flavor. **Tsabo Tavoc** is selected in the original *Invasion* printing, not promotional flavor.
- Cards with `—` in the flavor column do not have printed flavor on the chosen physical edition. Their names, chapters, and historic depictions still earn their slots.
- The museum deck has only 30 lands; this reflects Cory's stated story-first constraint, not a claim of reliable Commander gameplay.
- This is a **finished proposal, pending Cory's approval**. No locked encyclopedia deck is changed and this proposal is not silently locked.
