# War of the Spark: The Fall of Nicol Bolas

**Lore deck #3 — Commander:** Niv-Mizzet Reborn · **Status:** proposed, not approved or locked · **Deck size:** 100

## The story this deck tells

1. **The long conspiracy.** Bolas cultivates the God-Pharaoh's Eternals on Amonkhet, captures the Planar Bridge through Tezzeret and weaponizes the Immortal Sun, then infiltrates Ravnica's guild politics.
2. **The invasion.** Tezzeret's portal carries the lazotep army and the corrupted God-Eternals onto Ravnica. Planeswalkers summoned by the Interplanar Beacon find themselves trapped.
3. **The city fights back.** The Gatewatch and guilds rally; Niv-Mizzet's earlier contingency leads to a resurrection using his bones, the Firemind Vessel, Ravnican leylines and the ten guilds. A direct account is in Wizards' *Operation Desperation*.
4. **The sacrifice.** Gideon's desperate Blackblade attack fails. The Elderspell begins with Domri's spark; Liliana breaks free of Bolas's control even though doing so should kill her, and Gideon takes her death upon himself.
5. **The fall.** Resistance continues, Bolas loses his attempted godhood, and Ugin imprisons his twin in the Meditation Realm. **He is not killed.** The last Triumph cards memorialize what the survivors learned.

Sources: [War of the Spark: Ravnica—Operation Desperation](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/war-spark-ravnica-operation-desperation-2019-06-05), [War of the Spark: Ravnica—Ashes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/war-spark-ravnica-ashes-2019-06-12), [War Games](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/war-games-2019-04-22), [More War Games](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/more-war-games-2019-04-29), and Wizards' [War of the Spark card gallery](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/card-image-gallery/war-of-the-spark).

**Evidence standard:** Each selected physical printing is linked below. `Flavor: yes` means that exact edition carries flavor text; `Flavor: no` means inclusion rests on identifiable characters, depicted events, artifacts, or location rather than a made-up quotation. A named character/setting alone is weaker evidence than a direct battle scene; the justification column makes that clear. These are paraphrased annotations, not copied card flavor text.

## Chronological exhibits and edition-specific lore annotations

**Commander:** [Niv-Mizzet Reborn](https://scryfall.com/card/war/208/niv-mizzet-reborn?utm_source=api) — **WAR #208**. The reborn five-color Firemind is central to the actual climax and is the new Living Guildpact; this supplies WUBRG for an otherwise impossible blend of Bolas, Gatewatch, Amonkhet and ten guilds. This commander is selected for its narrative role, not power.

## Audit and integrity

- **100 physical cards:** one five-color commander, 69 nonland spells, 20 distinct nonbasic lands, and ten basic lands.
- **Commander rules validation:** 100-card count, singleton aside from basic lands, WUBRG identity, Commander legality and named-card existence all passed against the available card database.
- **Banned-card policy:** Cory welcomes banned cards if story requires them; this proposal happens to contain no Commander-banned card. One game-changing card, Bolas's Citadel, is included because it is literally Bolas's stronghold.
- **Printings:** 90/90 nonbasic named cards (including commander) have resolved selected editions. 68/90 use original WAR editions; 54/90 selected printings have authentic flavor text. For older prequel cards and guild geography, linked editions deliberately reflect the historical story period.
- **Chronology:** The spell sections are narrative exhibit order; later cards can depict events that occurred in parallel. The guildgates, Plaza of Harmony, Guildmages' Forum, Aether Hub, and Survivors' Encampment are contextual locations, not all documented battle sites.
- **Canon safeguards:** The Immortal Sun is an anti-planeswalking trap. The Elderspell uses stolen sparks. Gideon dies for Liliana. Bolas survives as Ugin's prisoner. Niv-Mizzet's resurrection is not a claim that he single-handedly defeated Bolas.
- **Provenance:** Cards/flavor from linked Scryfall edition pages; event interpretations checked against the linked Wizards historical stories. Suggestions are a proposal for Cory's approval, not a replacement of any of the 62 locked Grixis Encyclopedia decks or the separate Yawgmoth/Mending lore decks.
