## What stands out

1. **Excellent starting breadth; thinner reusable staple depth.** The 751
   names span all five colors, Commander strategies, multiple Universes Beyond
   sets and old-school creature/spell themes. But the *only two* physical copies
   of **Sol Ring**, **Arcane Signet** and **Command Tower** are already allocated
   to the two keep-built decks. Future IRL-only Commander lists have to solve
   ramp and fixing honestly without those cards, or make a separately approved
   purchase/transfer.
2. **Naya is the clearest ready-made rebuilding base.** Rith's exact list
   returns green ramp (**Cultivate**, **Farseek**, **Kodama's Reach**, **Wood
   Elves**), flexible answers (**Beast Within**, **Path to Exile**, **Austere
   Command**), fixing (**Exotic Orchard**, **Jungle Shrine**, **Canopy Vista**)
   and creature/Dragon pieces. **Atla Palani, Nest Tender** is a particularly
   interesting creature-first alternative, or **Rith** can be reconstructed
   with a more focused Dragons/tokens direction. Naya options are promising,
   not automatically optimized 100-card builds from these cards alone.
3. **There is a genuine Grixis spells package.** Saruman frees **Kess,
   Dissident Mage**, **Marchesa, Dealer of Death**, **Guttersnipe**, **Goblin
   Warchief**, **Goblinslide**, **Stormcatch Mentor**, cantrips, interaction
   and a large number of red/blue/black basics. **Kess** offers a plausible
   spellslinger direction but would need a careful count of cheap spells,
   reliable fixing and recursion; it should not cannibalize Xander casually.
4. **Other archetypes have identifiable seeds, not guaranteed full shells.**
   **Krenko, Mob Boss** plus **Impact Tremors** and red Goblins suggests
   tokens; however only about 13 distinct printed Goblin creature names in
   the post-disassembly pool have a mono-red color identity in the dated
   index. **Jenson Carthalion** / **O-Kagachi** offer five-color ambition, but
   demanding five-color mana is a real constraint. **Zaxara** is physically
   present but the index shows no Hydra creature depth beyond Zaxara itself.
   The recent **Star Trek** material offers fun Borg/artifact and Klingon
   seeds, not yet a deep Commander-sized tribal library on its own.
5. **The two keep-built decks are coherent and worth reserving.** The exact
   Xander 101 has a mill/discard/punishment theme, **37 lands**, and synergistic
   pieces such as **Traumatize**, **Mind Funeral**, **Fractured Sanity**,
   **Jace's Phantasm**, **Syr Konrad**, and **Altar of Dementia**. Seven-mana
   Xander, taplands and medium-cost ramp still imply a slower casual plan.
   Dihada has **36 lands**, many legendary creatures, **Primevals' Glorious
   Rebirth**, **Day of Destiny**, **Heroes' Podium**, **The Reaver Cleaver**,
   and the relevant protection/removal tools. It looks particularly
   commander-identity-aligned for a casual legends strategy.

  Overgrown Tomb, Cinder Glade, Canopy Vista, Prairie Stream, Farseek,

- **Payoffs and engines:** Krenko, Mob Boss; Impact Tremors; Kess, Dissident
  Mage; Guttersnipe; Marchesa, Dealer of Death; Atla Palani, Nest Tender;
  Rith, Liberated Primeval; Xyris, the Writhing Storm; and several creature,
  legendary, Dragon, token and spellslinger packages.

**Caution:** EDHREC rank in the dated card index measures popularity, not
card strength or a live ban/legality guarantee. Multiple physical copies of a
nonbasic card usually contribute only one copy per ordinary Commander deck.
The collection files do not identify exact printings, condition or prices, so
no defensible market valuation is possible from this snapshot alone.
