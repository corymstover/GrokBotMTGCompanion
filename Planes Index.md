### Kaldheim
- **Overview (✅):** Norse-inspired plane "made up of many individual realms" — mini-planes connected to, and rotating unpredictably around, the cosmic **World Tree**, which its peoples see as the origin of all life. The Hall of the Valkyries is visible from every realm as the "Light of Starnheim".
- **The Cosmos (✅):** the aurora-like space between realms, home of the ancient **Cosmos monsters** (said to be the World Tree's first "fruits" and now its guardians). The first and oldest is [Koma, the Cosmos Serpent](cards/K/ko.md#koma-cosmos-serpent); the tiny squirrel [Toski](cards/T/to.md#toski-bearer-of-secrets) is one too. When the Skoti overthrew the Einir gods they barred Koma from entering any realm.
- **Travel between realms (✅):** **Omenpaths** sometimes open between realms as they shift; a **Doomskar** is a violent overlap of two realms ([Doomskar](cards/D/do.md#doomskar)). Gods, shapeshifters and the Omenseekers' rune-enhanced longships can also cross. *(Note: the word "Omenpath" here means Kaldheim's inter-realm passages; for the post-invasion Multiverse-wide Omenpaths see [Special realms & cosmology](#special-realms--cosmology).)*
- **Gods (✅):** the ruling **Skoti** supplanted the ancient **Einir** a few centuries ago; the Einir's descendants are the shadow elves and wood elves. The Skoti draw their power from the **Cosmos Elixir** that [Esika](cards/E/es.md#esika-god-of-the-tree--the-prismatic-bridge) brews from **Tyrite**, the World Tree's "sap". Gods and domains: [Alrund](cards/A/al.md#alrund-god-of-the-cosmos--hakka-whispering-raven) (wisdom) · [Reidane](cards/R/rei.md#reidane-god-of-the-worthy--valkmira-protectors-shield) (justice) · [Halvar](cards/H/ha.md#halvar-god-of-battle--sword-of-the-realms) (battle) · [Egon](cards/E/eg.md#egon-god-of-death--throne-of-death) (death; ages backwards) · [Toralf](cards/T/to.md#toralf-god-of-fury--toralfs-hammer) (thunder) · [Birgi](cards/B/bi.md#birgi-god-of-storytelling--harnfel-horn-of-bounty) (boasting) · [Cosima](cards/C/cos.md#cosima-god-of-the-voyage--the-omenkeel) (sea; born a Cosmos monster) · [Tergrid](cards/T/te.md#tergrid-god-of-fright--tergrids-lantern) (fear) · [Valki](cards/V/va.md#valki-god-of-lies--tibalt-cosmic-impostor) (lies) · Esika (World Tree) · [Kolvori](cards/K/ko.md#kolvori-god-of-kinship--the-ringhart-crest) (kinship) · [Jorn](cards/J/jo.md#jorn-god-of-winter--kaldring-the-rimestaff) (weather).
- **The ten realms (✅):** **Bretagard** (humans) · **Axgard** (dwarves; three mountain ranges) · **Gnottvold** (wilds, giant ruins, trolls) · **Immersturm** (fire, demons, constant war) · **Istfell** (mist-shrouded plain at the base of the World Tree) · **Karfell** (glacier-bound; once ruled by dynasties of High Kings and Queens) · **Littjara** (mutable realm of shapeshifters) · **Skemfar** (shadowy elven forest; seven great trees bind the losers of ancient god-battles) · **Starnheim** (top of the World Tree; Valkyries and the honored dead) · **Surtland** (volcanoes bursting through snow and ice).
- **Bretagard's five human clans (✅):** **Beskir** (law; Lawspeakers enforce the inter-clan *Code of the Clans*) · **Omenseekers** (navigators based in the Kirda Pillars, led by [Inga Rune-Eyes](cards/I/ing.md#inga-rune-eyes); their Vedrune wizards can open Omenpaths) · **Skelle** (raiders of the Skelle Mire, shaped by the demon [Varragoth](cards/V/va.md#varragoth-bloodsky-sire); they forswore the Code with the Bloodsky Massacre) · **Tuskeri** (glory-seeking warriors of the Tusk Mountains) · **Kannah** (named as a relatively lawless clan). The Feltmark plain holds most of Bretagard's people.
- **Story (✅):** *Kaldheim* (2021-02-05). Kaya arrives on Kaldheim, travels with Inga's Omenseekers and meets a god travelling in mortal guise ([Episode 1](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/episode-1-travelers-2021-01-07)); Tyvar "of Skemfar" and Kaya fight in the Battle for Kaldheim ([Episode 5](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/episode-5-battle-kaldheim-2021-02-03)); ([Episode 3: The Saga of Tibalt](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/episode-3-saga-tibalt-2021-01-20); Tibalt's card face is [Valki // Tibalt, Cosmic Impostor](cards/V/va.md#valki-god-of-lies--tibalt-cosmic-impostor)). In the run-up to the Phyrexian Invasion, **Vorinclex stole essence from the World Tree** ([PW Guide: ONE](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/planeswalkers-guide-to-phyrexia-all-will-be-one)) — see [Phyrexian Invasion of the Multiverse](Conflicts%20%26%20Arcs.md#phyrexian-invasion-of-the-multiverse).
- **Native Planeswalker (✅):** [Tyvar Kell](cards/T/ty.md#tyvar-kell), younger brother of the king of the elves ([profile](https://magic.wizards.com/en/story/tyvar-kell-planeswalker)). Characters: [Characters Index](Characters%20Index.md).
- **Sources:** [Planeswalker's Guide to Kaldheim, Part 1](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/planeswalkers-guide-kaldheim-part-1-2021-01-08) · [Part 2](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/planeswalkers-guide-kaldheim-part-2-2021-01-14) · story episodes above.

### Eldraine
- **Overview (✅):** fairy-tale plane split between **the Realm** — small hamlets and farmland ruled by five royal courts — and **the Wilds**, the unruly, ungoverned lands of the fae and many other creatures. Before humanity, elves ruled the land.
- **The five courts and virtues (✅):** life in the Realm revolves around five virtues, each established by a sentient **relic**: **Ardenvale** — loyalty (Circle of Loyalty; ruled by [Queen Linden](cards/L/li.md#linden-the-steadfast-queen)) · **Vantress** — knowledge (the Magic Mirror, true name **Indrelon**, leads the court; see [Gadwick, the Wizened](cards/G/ga.md#gadwick-the-wizened)) · **Locthwain** — persistence (a flying fortress; its knights seek the Cauldron — cf. [Cauldron of Souls](cards/C/cau.md#cauldron-of-souls) ⚠️ name match only) · **Embereth** — courage (the Irencrag) · **Garenbrig** — strength.
- **The High Throne (✅):** about once a generation the [Questing Beast](cards/Q/qu.md#questing-beast) chooses a knight to undertake the Great Quest for the High Throne of the Realm.
- **Wilds of Eldraine era (✅):** [Talion](cards/T/ta.md#talion-the-kindly-lord), ruler of Eldraine's fae, joined with three witches during the Phyrexian invasion to create the **Wicked Slumber**, a curse that stopped the Phyrexians but now afflicts the Realm ([A First Look at Wilds of Eldraine](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/a-first-look-at-wilds-of-eldraine); [Episode 1](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/episode-1-pure-of-heart): "The Wicked Slumber might have stopped the Phyrexians in their tracks, but the Realm struck a foul bargain to pay for it."). The witch sisters are [Agatha](cards/A/ag.md#agatha-of-the-vile-cauldron), [Hylda](cards/H/hy.md#hylda-of-the-icy-crown) and [Eriette](cards/E/er.md#eriette-of-the-charmed-apple) ([Ten Stories Tall](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/ten-stories-tall), design column). Protagonist: [Kellan](cards/K/ke.md#kellan-the-fae-blooded--birthright-boon), with [Ruby](cards/R/ru.md#ruby-daring-tracker); Kellan later learns Oko is his father on Thunder Junction (see [Thunder Junction](#other-planes-in-recent-stories-omenpath-era)).
- **Story & sets:** *Throne of Eldraine* (2019-10-04); *Wilds of Eldraine* (2023-09-08); invasion side story [Eldraine: The Adventures of Rankle, Master of Love](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/the-adventures-of-rankle-master-of-love) ([Rankle](cards/R/ran.md#rankle-master-of-pranks)).
- **Sources:** [Planeswalker's Guide to Eldraine](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/planeswalkers-guide-eldraine-2019-10-31) · links above.

### Ikoria
- **Overview (✅ 🗣️ narrated by Vivien Reid):** "a world of monsters", which can evolve or mutate. Humans are the only "civilized" species to survive, mostly inside protective **sanctuaries**; sanctuary society strictly forbids associating with monsters.
- **Bonders (✅):** humans who form a magical bond (**eludha**) with monsters, e.g. [Winota](cards/W/wi.md#winota-joiner-of-forces) (lost an arm trying to kill the monster she bonded with) and [Kinnan](cards/K/ki.md#kinnan-bonder-prodigy) (bonds with many).
- **The five triomes (✅):** regions where three types of mana mix (per Narset, as reported by Vivien), each with a dominant monster clade and an apex monster:

| Ketria | Rivers and crystalline forests | Elementals | [Illuna](cards/I/il.md#illuna-apex-of-wishes) | [Ketria Triome](cards/K/ke.md#ketria-triome) |

| Zagoth | Rainforests and wetlands | Beasts | [Brokkos](cards/B/br.md#brokkos-apex-of-forever) (believed oldest) | [Zagoth Triome](cards/Z/za.md#zagoth-triome) |
- **Drannith (✅):** the largest human sanctuary (tens of thousands), in Savai, built around the giant crystal **Argalith** and defended by the **Coppercoats** (cf. [Drannith Magistrate](cards/D/dra.md#drannith-magistrate)).
- **Story & sets:** *Ikoria: Lair of Behemoths* (2020-04-24); invasion side story [Ikoria: Survival of the Fittest](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/survival-of-the-fittest).
- **Sources:** [Planeswalker's Guide to Ikoria](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/planeswalkers-guide-ikoria-2020-04-02).

### New Capenna
- **Overview (✅):** a "plane-in-a-city": one colossal tower built atop the rubble of a forgotten kingdom's capital, a lone sentinel in an empty wasteland holding almost the whole population. Its people act as if there were no history before the city.
- **History (✅):** **Old Capenna** was lost in a cataclysmic war against a forgotten enemy. The archangels and archdemons raised the fortress tower; the survivors fled inside. The archdemons then betrayed the archangels: through their own mutual sacrifice they made five mortal champions into demonic hybrids, who founded the **five families**. The public was told the angels sacrificed themselves to create **Halo** — a divine, oil-like fluid with magic-enhancing and curative power — and the families rose to power.
- **Boroughs (✅, bottom to top):** **Caldaia** (industrial; Riveteers) · **Mezzio** (largest and liveliest; home of the Cabaretti, Obscura and Brokers) · **Park Heights** (glamorous top tier).
- **The five families (✅):**

### Phyrexia (Old Phyrexia) & New Phyrexia's spheres
- **Source caveat 🗣️:** the official guide is written in a Phyrexian propaganda voice ("Exult in the glory of Phyrexia!"); facts below are its plain historical statements.
- **Old Phyrexia (✅):** an artificial plane created by an unknown Planeswalker. **Yawgmoth**, a medical genius from Dominaria, planeswalked there and installed himself as ruler, the Father of Machines ([Yawgmoth, Thran Physician](cards/Y/ya.md#yawgmoth-thran-physician)). He perished with Urza — see [Characters Index](Characters%20Index.md#brothers-war--weatherlight).
- **Phyresis (✅):** Phyrexians reproduce in flesh-vats and birthing pods or by infecting others with **glistening oil**, a mutagen that twists minds; past a certain stage it cannot be reversed. Compleation surgeries follow.
- **From Mirrodin to New Phyrexia (✅):** Karn created Mirrodin but accidentally left a drop of glistening oil on its inner core. Under Mirrodin's five suns the Phyrexians split into **five factions under five praetors**, reshaped the plane with mycosynth, and enclosed the original surface in a new outer sphere. (Overview row: [Mirrodin → New Phyrexia](#planes-with-official-profiles).)
- **The Nine Spheres (✅):** the Monumental Facade (outer shell of monuments) · **Mirrex** (ruined former outer sphere of Mirrodin) · **Autonomous Furnace** (Quiet Furnace; [Urabrask](cards/U/ur.md#urabrask--the-great-work)) · **Hunter Maze** (Vicious Swarm; built from the Mirran Tangle by [Vorinclex](cards/V/vo.md#vorinclex-monstrous-raider) and the compleated [Glissa](cards/G/gl.md#glissa-sunslayer)) · **Surgical Bay** (Progress Engine; [Jin-Gitaxias](cards/J/ji.md#jin-gitaxias-progress-tyrant)) · **Dross Pits** (Seven Steel Thanes; [Sheoldred](cards/S/she.md#sheoldred-the-apocalypse) took the sphere from Thane Roxith) · **Fair Basilica** (Machine Orthodoxy; [Elesh Norn](cards/E/el.md#elesh-norn-mother-of-machines)) · **Mycosynth Gardens** (Mirrodin's original inner core, "mysteriously still and empty") · **Seedcore** (where [Memnarch](cards/M/me.md#memnarch) once lived in the Panopticon).
- **Prelude to the invasion (✅):** Elesh Norn promised [Tezzeret](cards/T/te.md#tezzeret-betrayer-of-flesh) an indestructible darksteel body; with the Planar Bridge he carried the praetors' parts to other planes. Vorinclex stole World Tree essence on [Kaldheim](#kaldheim); Jin-Gitaxias found a way to Phyrexianize Planeswalkers on Kamigawa. Planeswalkers built a new sylex and sent a strike team into New Phyrexia — see [Phyrexian Invasion of the Multiverse](Conflicts%20%26%20Arcs.md#phyrexian-invasion-of-the-multiverse) and [Master Timeline](Master%20Timeline.md#phyrexian-invasion).
- **Sets:** *Scars of Mirrodin* (2010-10-01) → *New Phyrexia* (2011-05-13); *Phyrexia: All Will Be One* (2023-02-10); *March of the Machine* (2023-04-21).
- **Sources:** [Planeswalker's Guide to Phyrexia: All Will Be One](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/planeswalkers-guide-to-phyrexia-all-will-be-one) · [New Phyrexia profile](https://magic.wizards.com/en/story/new-phyrexia-plane).

## Special realms & cosmology
- **Blind Eternities** — the chaotic void between planes, home of the Eldrazi titans ([Zendikar profile](https://magic.wizards.com/en/story/zendikar-plane)).
- **Omenpaths** — portals between planes that appeared after the Phyrexian Invasion, letting non-Planeswalkers travel (used throughout OTJ, Lorwyn Eclipsed and SOS stories; ✅ e.g. [OTJ Episode 1](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/episode-1-an-offer-of-revenge)). ⚠️ The exact in-story origin of the Omenpaths is not yet cited here — open question for next cycle.
- **Meditation Realm** — where Jace was "unmade" and from which he built the Echoverse (✅ [PW Guide: Reality Fracture](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/planeswalkers-guide-to-reality-fracture)).
- **Serra's Realm** — artificial plane created by Serra (✅ [Serra profile](https://magic.wizards.com/en/story/serra-planeswalker)).
