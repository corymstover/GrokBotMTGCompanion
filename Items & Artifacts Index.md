## Artifacts

### Golgothian Sylex
- **What:** Urza's world-ending bowl from the Brothers' War. Karn: "It was created by Urza to help defeat the Phyrexians. I intend to take it to New Phyrexia and detonate it there" ([RtD Ep. 9](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/return-dominaria-episode-9-2018-05-09)).
- **Use:** In the war's end, after the blast Tawnos "was greeted by the first flakes of snow" ([BRO Ep. 5](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/the-brothers-war-episode-5-as-cruel-as-necessary)). In 2022, "We're helping Teferi find information about how to use the sylex" ([BRO Ch. 5](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/the-brothers-war-chapter-5-exodus)). The strike team made "their own copy of the Golgothian sylex, an artifact of massive destructive potential," planning to "detonate it and destroy the Invasion Tree" ([PW Guide to MOM](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/planeswalkers-guide-to-march-of-the-machine)).
- **Arcs:** [The Brothers' War](Conflicts%20%26%20Arcs.md#the-brothers-war) · [BRO frame](Conflicts%20%26%20Arcs.md#the-brothers-war-frame-story-2022) · [MOM](Conflicts%20%26%20Arcs.md#march-of-the-machine-2023) · Retcon: [The sylex plan](Contradictions%20%26%20Retcons%20Log.md#the-sylex-plan-karn-2018-vs-the-invasion-copy) · **Card:** [Golgothian Sylex](cards/G/gol.md#golgothian-sylex)

### Blackblade
- **What:** An Elder-Dragon-slaying sword. Liliana hopes "when the Blackblade rips Belzenlok apart and drains his life, the pain is unimaginable and everlasting" ([RtD Ep. 11](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/return-dominaria-episode-11-2018-05-23)).
- **Use:** In WAR: "Now we need to finish this by using Blackblade on Bolas." Gideon swears his oath "raising Blackblade high" ([WAR: Ashes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/war-spark-ravnica-ashes-2019-06-12)).
- **Arc:** [Return to Dominaria & WAR](Conflicts%20%26%20Arcs.md#return-to-dominaria--war-of-the-spark) · **Card:** [Blackblade Reforged](cards/B/bla.md#blackblade-reforged)

### Immortal Sun
- **What:** "an artifact of mythic power that promises boundless wealth, the strength of empire, command over nature, and eternal life" ([Ixalan profile](https://magic.wizards.com/en/story/ixalan-plane)). The sphinx Azor made it by "severing his spark to create the artifact he called the Immortal Sun"; it was bait: "The Immortal Sun will seal you in your prison, Planeswalker" ([Sabotage](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/sabotage-2018-01-31)).
- **Use:** Taken to Ravnica by Bolas; in WAR it stops Planeswalkers leaving, until "The Izzet's Beacon, the Immortal Sun, the Planar Bridge, and the flow of Eternals from Amonkhet had all been shut down" ([WAR: Ashes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/war-spark-ravnica-ashes-2019-06-12)).
- **Arcs:** [Ixalan & the Immortal Sun](Conflicts%20%26%20Arcs.md#ixalan--the-immortal-sun) · [WAR](Conflicts%20%26%20Arcs.md#return-to-dominaria--war-of-the-spark) · **Card:** [The Immortal Sun](cards/T/thei.md#the-immortal-sun)

### Chain Veil
- **What:** Liliana's power-amplifying relic, home to "The Onakke spirits in the Chain Veil" ([RtD Ep. 11](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/return-dominaria-episode-11-2018-05-23)).
- **Use:** In SOI, Jace protests that she is "redirecting a whole world's mana into this...moon...thing, just to—just to be rid of the Chain Veil?" ([Liliana's Indignation](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/lilianas-indignation-2016-04-20)). Her Gatewatch oath: "If that means I can do what needs to be done without relying on the Chain Veil, then I'll keep watch" ([The Promised End](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/promised-end-2016-07-27)).
- **Arcs:** [Innistrad & Emrakul](Conflicts%20%26%20Arcs.md#innistrad--emrakul) · [DOM/WAR](Conflicts%20%26%20Arcs.md#return-to-dominaria--war-of-the-spark) · **Card:** [The Chain Veil](cards/T/thec.md#the-chain-veil)

### Godsend & Khrusor
- **What:** "To fight the sun god Heliod, the forge god Purphoros created a sword capable of damaging the divine." Heliod "reforged the sword into the spear Godsend" ([THB Story on Cards](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/theros-beyond-death-story-cards-2019-12-16)). Heliod's own weapon is "Khrusor, the Sun Spear" ([PW Guide to Theros Pt 1](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/planeswalkers-guide-theros-part-1-2013-08-21)).
- **Use:** Elspeth "stabbed Xenagos with Godsend, destroying him once and for all" ([THB Story on Cards](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/theros-beyond-death-story-cards-2019-12-16)). In THB, Heliod's Khrusor "promptly shattered in his hands" ([THB Story Summary](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/theros-beyond-death-story-summary-2020-01-10)).
- **Arcs:** [Theros (2013–14)](Conflicts%20%26%20Arcs.md#theros-xenagoss-ascension--elspeths-fall) · [THB](Conflicts%20%26%20Arcs.md#theros-beyond-death-2020) · **Card:** [Godsend](cards/G/god.md#godsend)

### Helvault
- **What:** A silver prison on Innistrad, "a massive, rough-hewn chunk of silver, forty feet tall at least" ([Stone and Blood](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/stone-and-blood-2016-06-15)).
- **Use:** Nahiri endured "the millennium spent trapped in the Helvault" ([Campaign of Vengeance](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/campaign-vengeance-2016-07-06)). "First the demon Griselbrand trapped Avacyn in the Helvault"; later "the Helvault was sundered and Avacyn was released" ([Innistrad profile](https://magic.wizards.com/en/story/innistrad-plane)).
- **Arc:** [Innistrad & Emrakul](Conflicts%20%26%20Arcs.md#innistrad--emrakul) · **Card:** [Helvault](cards/H/he.md#helvault)

### Hedrons
- **What:** "Massive stone formations known as hedrons, the ancient handiwork of the Planeswalkers Ugin, Sorin, and Nahiri the Lithomancer"; "the ancient Planeswalkers used the hedrons to lure, contain, and immobilize" the titans ([Zendikar profile](https://magic.wizards.com/en/story/zendikar-plane)).
- **Use:** In BFZ, "Nissa had heaved the last hedron into place, trapping Ulamog" in "an enormous hedron prison capable of holding an Eldrazi titan" ([Retaliation of Ob Nixilis](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/retaliation-ob-nixilis-2015-12-30)).
- **Arc:** [BFZ / Gatewatch](Conflicts%20%26%20Arcs.md#eldrazi-awaken--battle-for-zendikar--gatewatch) · **Card:** [Hedron Archive](cards/H/he.md#hedron-archive)

### Planar Bridge
- **What:** Tezzeret's plane-crossing device, "being assembled piece by piece" in the Aether Spire ([Breaking Points](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/breaking-points-2017-01-18)).
- **Use:** Sabotaged by the renegades: "its central mechanism utterly destroyed" (same). Later shut down in WAR ([WAR: Ashes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/war-spark-ravnica-ashes-2019-06-12)). By the invasion era it rests in Tezzeret's chest: "the resting place of the Planar Bridge" ([BRO Ch. 3](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/the-brothers-war-chapter-3-nemesis)).
- **Arcs:** [Kaladesh revolt](Conflicts%20%26%20Arcs.md#kaladesh-revolt) · [WAR](Conflicts%20%26%20Arcs.md#return-to-dominaria--war-of-the-spark) · **Card:** [Planar Bridge](cards/P/pl.md#planar-bridge)

### Spear of Hazoret
- **What / use:** Karn, Samut, Sarkhan and Jace "returned from Amonkhet with an impressively large spear, the property of a God named Hazoret" ([WAR: Ashes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/war-spark-ravnica-ashes-2019-06-12)); Niv-Mizzet drives it through Bolas (see the [WAR arc](Conflicts%20%26%20Arcs.md#return-to-dominaria--war-of-the-spark)). ❓ No card of this name in the KB.

### Temporal Anchor
- **What / use:** Saheeli's time device, its first iteration "based heavily on blueprints given to her by Jodah"; it "projected a person's spirit backward in time", with Kaya's ghostform making Teferi "entirely incorporeal" ([BRO Ch. 2](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/the-brothers-war-chapter-2-antiquities)). Arc: [BRO frame](Conflicts%20%26%20Arcs.md#the-brothers-war-frame-story-2022). ❓ No card of this name in the KB.

### Ozolith
- **What / use:** "a massive crystal formation" that Lukka uses to control mutated monsters; it "explodes in a flash of orange light" ([IKO Story on Cards](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/ikoria-lair-behemoths-story-cards-2020-04-10)). ⚠️ The story text is in images and was not machine-verified. Arc: [Ikoria](Conflicts%20%26%20Arcs.md#ikoria-lair-of-behemoths-2020) · **Card:** [The Ozolith](cards/T/theo.md#the-ozolith)

### Endstone
- **What / use:** The relic both faiths chase in Edge of Eternities: "He said there would be a battle over the Endstone" ([EOE Ep. 10](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/edge-of-eternities-episode-10)); Tezzeret delivers it ([EOE Ep. 11](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/edge-of-eternities-episode-11)). ❓ Who receives it is not stated. Arc: [Edge of Eternities](Conflicts%20%26%20Arcs.md#edge-of-eternities-2025) · **Card:** [The Endstone](cards/T/thee.md#the-endstone)

## Spells & world-scale magic

### Elderspell
- Bolas's spark-stealing spell on Ravnica: it "created a vast storm of magic, dropping all of Ravnica into an artificial night punctuated by the stolen Planeswalker Sparks" ([WAR: Ashes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/war-spark-ravnica-ashes-2019-06-12)). Arc: [WAR](Conflicts%20%26%20Arcs.md#return-to-dominaria--war-of-the-spark).

### Realmbreaker, the Invasion Tree
- Norn "sent Vorinclex to Kaldheim to steal some of the essence of the World Tree, which she then used to grow her own plane-traversing Invasion Tree"; it "breaches into countless planes" ([PW Guide to MOM](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/planeswalkers-guide-to-march-of-the-machine)). Its sapling: "It is the beginning of great things, Father" ([DMU Ep. 5](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/episode-5-whisper-wind-2022-08-18)). Arcs: [DMU](Conflicts%20%26%20Arcs.md#dominaria-united-2022) · [MOM](Conflicts%20%26%20Arcs.md#march-of-the-machine-2023) · **Card:** [Realmbreaker, the Invasion Tree](cards/R/rea.md#realmbreaker-the-invasion-tree)
