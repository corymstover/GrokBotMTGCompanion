# Story Archive Index

> This index lists every official Wizards of the Coast story or lore page in the project's research corpus: **612 pages**. Of these, 575 are live on magic.wizards.com and **37 are archived** copies read from the Internet Archive. Built by `scripts/crawl_story_index.py` (Bot cycle 5, issue [#14](https://github.com/corymstover/GrokBotMTGCompanion/issues/14)).
> Where the list comes from: the magic.wizards.com sitemap, the Wayback Machine CDX index of the retired `/en/articles/archive/uncharted-realms/` and `/en/articles/archive/magic-story/` paths, and the feature pages cited in the lore files.
> Legend: **live** means fetched from magic.wizards.com in 2026-10. **🗄️ archived** means the page no longer resolves on the live site and was read from a web.archive.org snapshot. Treat archived pages as official but historical. Dates come from page metadata or the URL slug. Full text is never committed; the machine-readable list is [data/story_index.csv](data/story_index.csv).
