# MTG Companion Refinement Sprint (user brief, 2026-10-15)

Phase 1 — EDH & Deckbuilding depth
- Mana-base construction guides by color identity (land counts, utility priorities, budget vs optimized).
- Reusable package libraries (ramp suites, card-advantage engines, aristocrats, +1/+1 counters, spellslinger, graveyard, etc.) with Commander-legal options and bracket notes.
- Win-condition taxonomy with example cards and supporting pieces.
- Interaction & hate hierarchies (removal, stack interaction, graveyard hate, stax, etc.).
- cEDH primer layer (fast combos, turbo lines, stax packages) kept clearly separated from casual material.
- Budget-to-optimized upgrade paths for popular commanders/archetypes.
- Mulligan & early-game priorities per major archetype.
- Partner / Background / Friends Forever support matrices.
- Additional themed or plane-restricted deck outlines.

Phase 2 — Lore & Story depth
- Narrative summaries of major story arcs (key beats, outcomes, lasting consequences) with direct citations.
- Fuller Planeswalker dossiers (personal timelines, spark moments, allegiances, current status).
- Explicit Omenpath / Multiverse connection notes.
- Art & flavor integration tying significant cards back to story moments.
- Side-media index (novels, webcomics, etc.) with relevance notes.

Phase 3 — Card & Rules enrichment
- Keyword & ability encyclopedia (evergreen and set-specific) with examples and ruling links.
- Reprint & set-history notes per card where useful.
- Format legality matrix beyond Commander.
- Structured token, emblem, and dungeon/adventure data.
- Artist / art-credit index.

Phase 4 — Structure & usability
- Consistent tagging or YAML front-matter on major entries for easier filtering.
- Central FAQ for common lore and rules questions with internal links.
- Documented lookup patterns or simple helper notes so entries are faster to retrieve.
- Additional knowledge-snapshot tags at meaningful milestones.

Rules: well-structured, cross-referenced, source-noted, no redundancy; official sources first (Scryfall, magic.wizards.com, Comprehensive Rules); flag non-canonical/uncertain; maintain README, Progress Tracker, issues/milestones; chain cycles continuously, stop only for genuine blockers.
